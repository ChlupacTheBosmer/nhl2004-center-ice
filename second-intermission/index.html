The Second Intermission

The week in the league, between the periods. Two episodes every in-game week, recorded from the week's coverage across every desk on Center Ice, hosted by Gord Tessier and Dana Whitlock: the main show is a long Deep Dive around the week's theme; the second is a Debate, a Critique or a Brief, drawn for the week.
