The Second Intermission

The week in the league, between the periods. Two episodes every in-game week, recorded from the week's coverage across every desk on Center Ice, hosted by Gord Tessier and Dana Whitlock: the main show is a long Deep Dive around the week's theme; the second is a Debate, a Critique or a Brief, drawn for the week.

RSS feed · every episode is built from the week's published pieces, linked in its show notes.

The hosts

Gord TessierHost, thirty years of play-by-play

Thirty years of radio play-by-play in the Ontario junior leagues. Thinks in streaks and seasons, loves a comeback, and has seen this movie before.

Fourteen years on the Northeast beat. Reads the Bureau's indices like the weather, keeps a notebook of who said what, and asks what the Wire has confirmed.
