Ales Hemsky, at 22: 99 Hands and the Jump from 13.1 Minutes to 18.8

Edmonton's right winger holds the league's top deking grade, a base of 75, and a contract with 0 years left on it.

Tomáš VránaProfile writer · The SweaterSaturday, 29 October 2005
ShareCopy link

Five men carry a deking grade of 99 in the Book. Todd Bertuzzi93, Marian Gaborik99, Peter Forsberg89, Jaromir Jagr90, and Ales Hemsky81, who is 22 and plays the right side for Edmonton Oilers.

The other four are finished articles. Bertuzzi reads 93, Gaborik 99, Forsberg 95, Jagr 91. Hemsky reads 77 against a base of 75, and he is the youngest of the group by a year. What puts him ahead of every player his own age is the ice time, not the hands.

Hemsky skated 13.1 minutes a night in 2003-04. Through 8 games of this season he skates 18.8. The Bureau's Development Index has him up 9, from his base of 75 to an overall of 77.

Edmonton has handed him the minutes a top-six winger gets. He is 22, the contract pays $1.10M, and it has 0 years left on it.

The hands

Hemsky learned the game in Pardubice, at HC Moeller Pardubice, where he played as a 16-year-old in the Czech Extraliga and split that season with the junior club. He judged the Canadian junior leagues the faster road to a scout's notebook, and Hull made him the first pick of the 2000 CHL Import Draft.

The hands were always the report. In 2000-01 he led all QMJHL rookies with 36 goals and 100 points, made the All-Rookie team, played the 2001 CHL Top Prospects Game, and took the Mike Bossy Trophy as the league's top pro prospect. He followed it with 27 goals and 97 points in 53 games, 19th in QMJHL scoring. Edmonton took him 2001 #13 (R1).

The NHL points came slower than the junior ones. His first point was 12 October 2002 against Nashville, his first goal 4 January 2003 against Montreal, in the 26th game of his rookie season.

Two seasons at 12.0 and 13.1 minutes produced 16 points and then 31. One month at 18.8 has produced 4 points in 8 games and a 9-point move on the Index.

The rest of the card

The reputation grades sit where you would put them. Deking 99, shot power 89, speed 87, puck control 85, passing 84. Accuracy and acceleration are both 81.

Three grades stop at 50, and one of them is endurance. The other two are fighting and the grade the Book keeps for the big moment. Endurance 50 while he plays 18.8 minutes is the gap Edmonton is running this season, and the Index has moved 9 in the months he's been running it.

His ceiling reads 91, so the Book still expects the rest of the card to fill in. He missed time last season with a neck, sometime between 2005-03-07 and 2005-03-09, and he was back 2005-03-13. He has not been on the injured list since.

The room

Edmonton is 1-4-2 for 6 points, one win in hand, with 20 goals scored and 26 allowed. The Oilers are 1-3 at home and 1-3 on the road. The club sits fourth in the Northwest, level on points with Minnesota and behind Vancouver, Colorado and Calgary.

The men around Hemsky are older and higher in the Book. Ryan Smyth86 is 82, Tommy Salo88 82, Mike Comrie84 80 and Mike York82 78. Jani Rita81 is 77 and up 8 on the Index himself, another young winger the club is pushing at the same time.

"You get the puck and the ice opens," Hemsky says of the extra minutes. "It did not open that way when I was sitting more."

What the calendar holds

His deal runs out after this season. What the league's calendar has as the trade deadline, 2006-03-19, is 141 days off, and what it has as the close of free agency, 2006-07-28, is 272 days out. A 22-year-old with a 99 in his hands and an Index up 9 is the kind of file a club tries to close before either date arrives, because the price only moves one way from here.

Through 8 games he has 4 points on 18.8 minutes. Edmonton has one win and 6 points. The hands are 99 and the contract has 0 years on it.
