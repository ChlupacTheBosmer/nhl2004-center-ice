The Bracket, May 17: A Cup Final from the start in the East, and a 3-win lead that holds in the West

Washington and Toronto meet in Game 7 tomorrow. Dallas has one foot in the Final with a 4-1 lead.

Howard BlaineSenior writer, The Blaine Rankings · The Hockey GazetteTuesday, 17 May 2005
ShareCopy link

Two conference finals are live. One has a Game 7 tomorrow in Toronto. The other has a 4-1 lead that should close on Wednesday in Dallas.

In the East, the fourth seed has beaten nobody on the road in this series, but the fourth seed has Jaromir Jagr91 [434] with 21 points in 14 games, the highest total in these playoffs, and Washington Capitals will be in Toronto's building tomorrow with the score tied at three. Jagr has 5 goals and 16 assists in these playoffs. Olaf Kolzig92 [502] has a 1.69 GAA and a .926 save percentage across 9 wins and 3 losses. WSH came in as the fourth seed and has outscored Tampa Bay and Carolina by a combined 31-9 in eight games before this series began. They are 9-2 through two completed rounds.

Toronto Maple Leafs has been slower. Ten wins and five losses in 18 games, with the last two games giving them back a series they trailed 1-3. Mats Sundin92 [961] has 8 goals and 6 assists in 18 games, the same point total as Gary Roberts87 [841] at 4+10. Neither has matched Jagr's pace. Roberto Luongo97 [593] is 11-7 with a 2.09 GAA and a .939 save percentage, and he is the reason Toronto is still playing.

The last result was Game 6 on Tuesday at Washington: Toronto won 2-1 to even the series. The next game is Wednesday, May 18, at Toronto. Four of the six games have been decided by one goal, and two went to overtime.

In the West, Dallas Stars holds a 4-1 lead over Mighty Ducks of Anaheim and the next game is Wednesday at Dallas. The Dallas Stars aggregate is 15 games, 44 goals for, 24 against. Marty Turco93 [1018] is 12-3 with a 1.48 GAA. Mike Modano84 [669] has 20 points in 15 games. Brenden Morrow82 [685] has 18.

Anaheim is 1-4 with the next game on the road. Sergei Fedorov90 [276] has 18 points in 17 games, the highest on a club that has won five games in this postseason and lost eight. Jean-Sebastien Giguere97 [317] is 8-7 with a 2.15 GAA and an .878 save percentage. The regular season record was Dallas Stars 7-1-2 over Mighty Ducks of Anaheim, and the playoff series has followed it with only one deviation: a 3-2 overtime win by Anaheim in Game 4.

What tomorrow decides

The East series has been a game of runs. Washington took the first two in Toronto by a combined score of 7-2, including a 4-0 shutout in Game 2. Toronto won the next two by 3-2 and the same score in overtime in Game 3. Washington won Game 4 by 3-1 at home. Toronto won Game 5 by 4-1 and Game 6 by 2-1. Neither club has won three in a row.

The West series has been the opposite: Dallas has won four of five and lost only once, in overtime in Game 4. The 4-1 lead has held across two games in each club's building. Game 6 is at Dallas on Wednesday.

Clubs eliminated: Boston Bruins, New York Islanders, Carolina Hurricanes, New Jersey Devils, Colorado Avalanche, Detroit Red Wings, Vancouver Canucks, Nashville Predators, Pittsburgh Penguins, Tampa Bay Lightning, Phoenix Coyotes, Edmonton Oilers.

Clubs alive: Toronto Maple Leafs, Washington Capitals, Dallas Stars, Mighty Ducks of Anaheim.
