The Bracket, May 19: Game 7 settles the East, and Dallas leads 4-1 in the West

Toronto came back from 0-2 down to close out Washington in a shutout. Anaheim, the 6th seed, faces elimination at home.

Howard BlaineSenior writer, The Blaine Rankings · The Hockey GazetteThursday, 19 May 2005
ShareCopy link

Roberto Luongo95 stopped everything Washington put on net and Toronto won 2-0 on Tuesday to take the Eastern Conference Final 4-3, closing out a series they trailed 0-2 and reaching the Cup Final for the second straight season. Dallas leads the Western Conference Final 4-1 with Game 6 at Anaheim, where the 6th-seeded Ducks are one loss from being out.

East: Toronto closes it in seven

Roberto Luongo finished 12-7 with a 1.98 GAA and a .942 save percentage across 19 playoff games, and Tuesday's shutout was his first of the spring. Toronto outscored Washington 8-3 over the final three games to turn a 1-3 deficit into a 4-3 series win.

Toronto came in as the 133-point regular-season champion, 29 points ahead of Washington. They lost the first two at home, 3-2 in overtime and then 4-0. A Game 4 loss in Washington dropped them to 1-3, and from there they won three straight, the 2-0 Game 7 margin the most comfortable of the three.

Mats Sundin92 had 9 goals and 6 assists in 19 games, 15 points that sit among the highest totals of these playoffs. Gary Roberts85, last spring's Conn Smythe, added 4 goals and 10 assists. Marian Gaborik99 appeared in only 8 games but posted 4 and 6 for 10 points, the highest per-game rate on the roster. On Washington's side, Jaromir Jagr90 led all players this spring with 5 goals and 16 assists for 21 points in 15 games. Pascal Dupuis79 had 9 and 6 for 15, Robert Lang 8 and 7 for 15, and Sergei Gonchar85 5 and 9 for 14. Olaf Kolzig89 went 9-4 with a 1.71 GAA and a .926 save percentage. Josh Harding83 was 2-0 with a 1.00 GAA in limited action.

Washington general manager Shawn Holliday spoke after the elimination. "The 104 points, I think that tells you the group competed," he told The Tunnel's Jan Kowalczyk on Tuesday. "We weren't good enough in the round after that, and that's on the roster I built. I own that part."

Washington swept Carolina 4-0 and Tampa Bay 4-0 to reach this round, outscoring both opponents 41-25 across 11 games. Toronto held them to 3 goals in the last three.

West: Dallas one game from the Final

Three of five games in this series have gone to overtime: a 3-2 Dallas win, a 2-3 Anaheim win, and a 2-1 Dallas win. The only game decided by more than a single goal was the 5-1 Dallas rout in Game 3 at Anaheim.

Anaheim came into the spring as the 6th seed at 97 points, a club that scored 229 and allowed 229 through 82 regular-season games, the evenest goal differential in hockey. Their 15 overtime losses led the league by 3. Jean-Sebastien Giguere97 grades 97 overall in the Book, the highest mark of any goaltender in the league. Dallas, the Presidents' Trophy winner at 109 points, has Marty Turco95 at 94. The Ducks scored 40 and allowed 43 across 17 playoff games. Dallas scored 44 and allowed 24 in 15.

Anaheim knocked out Nashville 4-2 and Edmonton 4-2 in the first two rounds, both lower-seed upsets. They now trail a series 1-4 with one game left at home.

Gone

The first round produced four lower-seed upsets and the second produced two more.

East. Toronto beat New York 4-1 in the first round and Pittsburgh 4-3 in the second. Pittsburgh, the 7th seed, beat New Jersey 4-2 in the first round, knocking out the 2nd seed, and pushed the top-seeded Maple Leafs to seven before falling 3-2 in Game 7. Washington swept Carolina 4-0. Tampa Bay beat Boston 4-2 before being swept by Washington in the second round.

West. Dallas swept Detroit 4-0. Phoenix beat Colorado 4-3 in Game 7, the 8th seed taking the top seed's series. Anaheim beat Nashville 4-2 and Edmonton 4-2 in the first two rounds. Edmonton beat Vancouver 4-2. Dallas beat Phoenix 4-2 in the second round.

Phoenix's run over Colorado was the spring's most improbable result. Colorado had 112 points, 310 goals for, and Peter Forsberg89 grading 95 overall in the Book, the highest-rated player in the league. Phoenix had 93 points and Sean Burke87 in net. They won Game 7 on the road, 4-3 in Colorado.

Out

Carolina, Boston, New York, New Jersey, Nashville, Colorado, Detroit, Vancouver, Pittsburgh, Tampa Bay, Edmonton and Phoenix are out. Twelve clubs eliminated. Four remain: Toronto, Washington, Dallas and Anaheim, with two series still on the sheet.
