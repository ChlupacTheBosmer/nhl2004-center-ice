ShareCopy link

Tonight's slate has 4 games and 2 chances to close. The Washington Capitals Capitals, up 3-0 on the Carolina Hurricanes Hurricanes, and the Dallas Stars Stars, up 3-0 on the Detroit Red Wings Red Wings, both play for the sweep on the road. The Pittsburgh Penguins Penguins, the 7th seed, lead the 2-seeded New Jersey Devils Devils 2-1 and host Game 4.
