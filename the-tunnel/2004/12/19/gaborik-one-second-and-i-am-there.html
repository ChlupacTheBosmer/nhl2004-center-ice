Gaborik: 'One Second And I Am There'

Marian Gaborik has 26 goals in 32 games on shorter shifts this season, and the Maple Leafs lead the league at 52 points.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelSunday, 19 December 2004

2:09 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Marian Gaborik95 scored twice for Toronto Maple Leafs in a 4-2 road win at Florida, lifting him to 26 goals in 32 games. The Bureau's Development Index has him at +6 on his base grade of 91, and his ice time has dropped to 19.2 minutes a night from 21.9 a season ago. Toronto leads the league at 52 points.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here with Marian Gaborik, post-game. Toronto wins 4-2 on the road tonight, Marian, and you had two goals. Twenty-six on the year, and the Bureau's Index has you up six on your base. What's changed about your game?

Marian Gaborik

Yeah. I feel, how you say, the game is slower for me now. Last year, maybe I need two, three shifts to feel the speed. This year, first shift, I am there. My legs, they are there. And the coach, he give me shorter shifts, so every one I go hard. I am at the net. I don't wait for the game. I bring it, you know.

Marcus Bell

Nineteen point two a night this year, down from 21.9. Is that the coach managing your load?

Marian Gaborik

The coach. He say, you go hard, you come off, you breathe, you go again. And I like that, you know. Because 21, 22 minutes, the last four, five, maybe I am not the same Marian. But 19, I go 30 seconds, I come off, I go again, and every time my speed is there. That is what I need.

Marcus Bell

Walk me through the two goals tonight.

Marian Gaborik

The first one, I get the puck in the slot and the D, he step back a little. So I just take it and I shoot. Quick. The second one, we break out of our zone and the ice, it is wide, you know? I have the room. I go, I go, and the goalie, he is not set. That is when I am the best. When nobody can catch me.

Marcus Bell

Toronto's 9 and 3 on the road. Does the road open things up for you specifically? Teams sit back a little at home, maybe give you more room in transition?

Marian Gaborik

Yeah. When they sit, they play a little tight, and I just find the gap. In our building, everybody know we go fast, they prepare for it. But on the road, some nights they, how you say, they forget. One second. One second is everything for me. One second and I am there.

Marcus Bell

Jan asked you about 12 goals last week and you didn't bite. You're at 26 now. Still don't want to talk about it?

Marian Gaborik

You guys don't quit, huh. No, but 26 is not the thing, you know. If we lose in the first round, nobody remember the goals. That is the number, the Cup. The goals, they come when you play right. I don't chase.

Marcus Bell

Last one, Marian. What's making this work right now, this group?

Marian Gaborik

We work. That is it. Mats, Gary, Alex, they make it easy for me. You play with guys like that, you just go, and the puck finds you. I don't have to be too smart. December is long. We take the next game.

Marcus Bell

Marian Gaborik, two goals tonight, 26 on the year. Thank you, Marian. Back to you.
