Turco points to the first, not the third, after PHI blows out Dallas

The Stars' goaltender says the 5-1 loss was gone before the final period, and that his team can't afford to hand points to 10-win clubs while chasing the top of the West.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelSunday, 19 December 2004

2:39 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Marty Turco96 had 24 saves on 29 shots and a 5-1 road loss to Philadelphia Flyers on 2004-12-17, his 27th start of a season in which he has 17 wins and 85 goals against. Dallas Stars sat on a 5-game win streak before Friday; they are now 19-10-1 and 5 points behind Colorado Avalanche in the Northwest. Jan Kowalczyk caught him in the tunnel at the Wachovia Center.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here with Marty Turco in the tunnel at Philadelphia. Marty, five goals on the board, road game. Where did it go for you tonight?

Marty Turco

Yeah, it's, uh, it's not one you want to break down. But the honest answer is the first period, not the third. We came out flat. They scored early, they got a second, and then we were chasing from the opening frame. By the time you're in the third, the game is basically, the game's over. The scoreboard just hasn't finished.

Jan Kowalczyk

But people are going to watch that third period and ask why it fell apart late. You gave up a couple in there. What happened?

Marty Turco

No, I mean, we were already down two, three, whatever it was. The third was, was just trying not to let it get ugly and it got ugly anyway. You pull, you empty the net, you put numbers up on the board and that's, that's desperation hockey. That's not a structure thing. We just didn't have enough of them in front of me early and you can't, you can't play from behind on the road against a team that's built to kill and grind. They're ten and fourteen for a reason but they don't care about that. They cared about tonight.

Jan Kowalczyk

Twenty-nine shots on your side of it. Five in. Did any of those catch you out?

Marty Turco

Yeah. The first one, I wasn't set. I knew it when it went in. I was a half second late across and the puck's, the puck's in there. The rest, screens and bodies. I'm not making excuses, but when you've got two guys in the paint and you can't track the puck, it's a different shot than the one I stop. I'll watch the video and I'll own whatever I need to own. But the first one, that one's mine.

Jan Kowalczyk

When we talked in Dallas after the fifth one in a row, you said you weren't counting. Does the table come back to you tonight? You're five back of Colorado with thirty-four games left in the schedule.

Marty Turco

Look, I'm not sitting in my room with a printout. I know where we are. But you lose 5-1 to a ten-win team on the road and the standings don't, they don't matter tonight. Tonight it's about how we played for sixty minutes and we didn't play well enough. If we do that, the standings take care of themselves. We've got 19 wins, we're not in trouble. We're in trouble when we play like that.

Jan Kowalczyk

What does the schedule ask from here? You've got games every other day, road and back, all the way through the break. What's the next one look like?

Marty Turco

I don't know if we get a day between these, do we? I mean, December's the same every year. You get in a city, you warm up, you play, you're on a bus. You don't get to feel good about a 4-1 road win because in thirty hours you're on another ice somewhere. The ask right now is just to show up sharp every time. No letdown games. We just played one. That's the ask.

Jan Kowalczyk

Bill Guerin got your only goal tonight. Mike Modano's back in the lineup. When you look at that room out there, what do you see?

Marty Turco

Yeah, Bill gets one. That's what Bill does, you know, he's there when the, when the puck bounces off the boards and nobody else wants to go get it. And Modano, Modano's, he's trying to get back into it. He's got one point the last game, that's, that's not Mike. We all know that. But that's, that's the thing. You need your top guys to take a period and tonight nobody did. Including me. I'm not, I'm not pointing fingers at anybody on that ice before I point at myself.

Jan Kowalczyk

Marty Turco, thank you. Back to you.
