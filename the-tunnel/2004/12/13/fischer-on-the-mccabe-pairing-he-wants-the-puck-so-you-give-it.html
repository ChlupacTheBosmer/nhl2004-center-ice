Fischer on the McCabe pairing: 'he wants the puck, so you give it'

The Flyers' 7-1 loss was Toronto's 14th straight win; Jiri Fischer talks about the pairing the coach put together and the night Bryan McCabe had.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelMonday, 13 December 2004

1:53 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Jiri Fischer85 spoke to [[player:kowalczyk|Jan Kowalczyk]] in the corridor at Philadelphia after Toronto Maple Leafs beat the Flyers 7-1 on 2004-12-12, the club's 14th straight win. The 24-year-old defenceman, who has 13 points in 24 games at 21.2 minutes a night, talked about the new partner the coach gave him and the two-goal, three-point night Bryan McCabe had.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here with Jiri Fischer, after a 7-1 win in Philadelphia, the 14th in a row for Toronto. Jiri, the coach shook up the pairings a little tonight. What changed for you out there?

Jiri Fischer

Yeah, he put me with Bryan. And I tell you, for me it is, you know, a different game. He wants the puck, he wants to skate with it, so you give it to him and you go. Simple. I don't have to think so much, you know. I like that.

Jan Kowalczyk

That was your first goal of the night for him. How does playing with a partner like that change the way you read the rush?

Jiri Fischer

It is easier. Because Bryan, he go, he go all the time. So I stay back, I watch the play, I am the, how you say, the last man. He create, and I just make sure we are safe. It feel good. We play the good game tonight. Both of us, I think.

Jan Kowalczyk

He had two goals and three points tonight. What's it like watching a defenceman have a night like that from the other end of the ice?

Jiri Fischer

You know what, it is the best place to watch. I am behind him, I see everything. Second goal, he just, he walk in and shoot. For me, I am happy. When your partner have a night like that, you don't have to do much. You just, you play simple and he take the game.

Jan Kowalczyk

Fourteen in a row now, 22 and 5. Does it feel like a team that's rolling, or is there something the coaches keep tightening?

Jiri Fischer

We don't talk about fourteen. Nobody in the room, you know, nobody count. We talk about the next game. Because tomorrow is different, every night is different. We play good now, but December, it is long. We just work. The coach, he changes the pairings, he changes, you know, little things. We listen. That is why we win.

Jan Kowalczyk

You've got 13 points in 24 games yourself, seven goals. Do you feel the coach's changes give you more room to join the rush?

Jiri Fischer

A little, yes. With Bryan, I know he stay, so sometimes I can go. But my job is, you know, first the defence. The goals, they come, they go. Tonight I don't score, we win seven-one. I take this every night. Every night.

Jan Kowalczyk

Jiri, appreciate the time. Enjoy the bus ride home.

Jiri Fischer

Yeah, thank you. Good bus, we take it, you know.
