Iginla: the game Calgary can play

Held off the scoresheet in an overtime loss to the league leader, Jarome Iginla says the night showed what the Flames room can still do with the season slipping.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelTuesday, 28 December 2004

1:08 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Calgary Flames came within a goal of the league's best record on home ice, falling 2-3 in overtime to Jarome Iginla94's former-benchmark Toronto Maple Leafs, the club that sits on 60 points to Calgary's 28. Iginla, scoreless in his 30th game despite 29 goals on the year, pointed at the two points that got away and the shift-by-shift work that doesn't stop for a 11-20-4 table.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here with Jarome Iginla in the tunnel in Calgary. Jarome, you had the best team in the league right to the wire. Overtime, 2-3. Does a night like that sting, or does it tell you something?

Jarome Iginla

Ah, it stings, Jan, you know. You're that close to the two. But we're not gonna stand here and pretend we got beat. We had 'em. Roman was sharp, we had our looks, and we took the best record in the league to the extra frame. That's, uh, that's the game we can play.

Jan Kowalczyk

You come off with the zero tonight. Twenty-nine goals in your thirty games, and against Toronto nothing. Did the chances come?

Jarome Iginla

Yeah, they came. I had one in the second, from the slot, and I, uh, I didn't beat the glove. Nights like that the puck finds Buzek instead. Petr, he had himself a night, a goal and a helper. You take that. It's not about me getting mine, it's about the two.

Jan Kowalczyk

You're sitting at 11 and 20, four points on either side of a lot of teams. The room's got the rest of the season ahead of it. What does that ask of a guy who's been where you've been?

Jarome Iginla

It asks that you keep coming, plain and simple. You don't fix a record in one night in December, you know? You fix it shift by shift. And tonight we showed that shift against the best of 'em. If we play that way, the table sorts itself out. I believe that.

Jan Kowalczyk

Jarome, thanks for the time.

Jarome Iginla

Thank you, Jan. Good building tonight, sorry we couldn't give 'em the extra one.
