Mogilny on 16.9 minutes: 'Forty-three points. What do you want from me'

The right wing shrugs off three short absences, a role cut by seven minutes, and a grade that has him below his base.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelWednesday, 22 December 2004

1:55 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Alexander Mogilny88 has 43 points in 30 games for Toronto Maple Leafs, but 16.9 minutes a night, down from 23.8 last season. The 35-year-old right wing spoke with The Tunnel's Marcus Bell in the corridor after a 9-2 win over St. Louis Blues on Tuesday.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here in the corridor, Alexander Mogilny has joined me. A 9-2 win over St. Louis tonight, third straight, you're 26 and 6. Marian put four in. You had the assist. Forty-three points in thirty games. But 16.9 minutes a night, that's almost seven less than last year. What's changed in your game?

Alexander Mogilny

The game tonight, it was... good. We score nine, we win, everybody is happy. The minutes, the coach, he decide. You play sixteen, you play twenty, you give what you have that night. I don't look at the clock. That is not how I work.

Marcus Bell

You told Jan Kowalczyk on the seventh that the coaching staff has your role set. You were scoreless in that 4-0 and you said the structure, the defence, was the thing. You still feel that way tonight with less ice? And you've been in and out of the lineup, a couple of short stints this month. Are you healthy?

Alexander Mogilny

You make it sound like I was out three weeks. I miss a day here, a day there. Maybe two days in the month. I come back, I play tonight, I have the assist. My body, it is thirty-five years, of course it talks to you in December. But I am here. That is the answer.

Marcus Bell

The Bureau's Development Index has you at minus three from your base today. Eighty-seven, you've been a ninety-grade player. Does that feel accurate?

Alexander Mogilny

Eighty-seven. You put me in sixteen minutes, I get forty-three points, and the number says I am below. What do you want from me. I can not run faster than the legs I have tonight. You want me ninety, you put me in twenty-two minutes. Maybe.

Marcus Bell

Let's talk about the road. Ten and three away, that's the best mark in the league. You've just come off a stretch in Atlanta, Florida, Tampa. What is the difference between road and home for a man who has played in how many buildings?

Alexander Mogilny

The ice is the same size. That is what I tell the young guys. The net is the same. Atlanta, we give up seven, that is not us, we look at it, we fix it. Florida, Tampa, clean. You do not carry the bad city to the next city. That is important.

Marcus Bell

Twenty-six and six, thirty-four games played. What do you see when you look at the calendar from here?

Alexander Mogilny

I see my team. We play sixty minutes. Every night, sixty minutes, that is the thing. The rest, June, we see in June. I don't look ahead.

Marcus Bell

Alexander Mogilny, thank you for your time. Back to you.
