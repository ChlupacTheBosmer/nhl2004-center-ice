Gary Roberts: a clean kill and an early goal carried a 4-0 shutout in Edmonton

On the second straight night of a road trip, the 38-year-old winger credits a clean penalty kill and an early goal for making a 4-0 shutout easy, his two assists carrying the league's best record to six straight wins.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelWednesday, 29 December 2004

1:45 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Marcus Bell caught Gary Roberts86 in the tunnel after Toronto Maple Leafs won 4-0 at Edmonton Oilers on the second straight night on the road, Ed Belfour stopping all 25 shots he faced. Roberts had two assists and spent the talk on the penalty kill in front of the netminder. At 28-6-0 with 62 points, TOR holds the league's best record, winning its sixth straight.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here with Gary Roberts after a 4-0 win in Edmonton, the second straight night on the road. Ed Belfour, 25 saves, the shutout. What does that do for the room?

Gary Roberts

Yeah, it's, it's everything tonight, you know . But it's, it's a team shutout. Ed's sharp when he needs to be, but we kept it clean in front of him and the kill, the kill was the thing. You give him that, he plays a big game.

Marcus Bell

You went down a man and kept it simple. Ed told us on this desk the kill's been his best friend this month. What was the difference in front of him tonight?

Gary Roberts

Ed's got a way of saying it, you know. But it's, it's details, honestly. Body in the lane, stick on the puck, get it out of the zone. Nobody on the kill tries to be the hero. You pressure, you get it deep, you change. That's, that's it.

Marcus Bell

You had two assists tonight. Take me to the one you'll remember.

Gary Roberts

The first goal, I think. I won the wall, got it to Mats in the slot and he, he didn't need a second look. You give a guy like that a clean look and the rest is his. I just get to the front after that.

Marcus Bell

It came early, and you never had to chase. How much does that change a second straight night with the legs heavy?

Gary Roberts

Everything, honestly. You get one early and you get to play the straight game, forecheck, get it deep, let them come. On a back-to-back the killer is chasing. We, we never had to, and that's all you can ask.

Marcus Bell

Six in a row for this group, and you're 12-3 on the road. Where's this team at today, December 29?

Gary Roberts

It's just a good room, Marcus. We get the lead, we defend it, and Ed's the backstop. Two nights, two wins, four points home. There's not much more you can ask in December.

Marcus Bell

You've got a couple days off for the new year. Enjoy them, Gary.

Gary Roberts

I'll sleep on the plane, you know . Thanks, Marcus. Good building tonight.

Marcus Bell

Good to see you, Gary. Back to you.
