Forsberg scores three as the Avalanche outlast the Flyers in overtime

The Swede had his best night of the season against the club that drafted him thirteen years ago, and afterward talked about nobody but the men around him.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelWednesday, 29 December 2004

1:41 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Peter Forsberg89 scored three goals and added an assist as Colorado Avalanche beat Philadelphia Flyers 6-5 in overtime on Monday at home. The 31-year-old left wing now has 33 goals and 73 points in 36 games, both career highs for a season pace. He was the sixth overall pick in the 1991 draft, selected by Philadelphia.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here in the corridor at Colorado Avalanche®. Three goals tonight, Peter, four points. You get to 33 on the season. What was different out there for you tonight?

Peter Forsberg

I think, uh, we played good in the second and third. The first period, they were faster to the puck. Then Teemu, he gets his goal, Serge gets his, and suddenly they cannot sit back. When they open up, you know, the ice, it is... for sure, it is different for me. I just go.

Jan Kowalczyk

You were the sixth pick in 1991, and Philadelphia took you. Thirteen years later, you put three past them. Did that one land different?

Peter Forsberg

Well. Of course I remember. Thirteen years, that is a long time. But tonight is about two points in December, not about 1991. I would rather have the two points and no hat trick, I think. I said that before.

Jan Kowalczyk

You did say that. On the seventh, you told me you do not watch the points, only the wins. Thirty-three goals, seventy-three points in thirty-six games. Is that still the answer?

Peter Forsberg

I think so. Yes. It is nice to score, I will not say it is not. But you do not win anything in November, you know? The list, it is there. I see it when someone shows me. But I do not go looking.

Jan Kowalczyk

Joe Sakic has been back since the seventeenth. You and him, what changes when he is out there?

Peter Forsberg

Joe, he reads. I cannot explain it better than that. He is already where the play will be, so the other team, they cannot double me. When Joe is not there, I have the man on me all night. With him, I get, uh, maybe one beat more. One beat is everything. For sure.

Jan Kowalczyk

You are 23 and 9, first in the Northwest, 52 points. Does this group in that locker room know what it is building?

Peter Forsberg

I think we play good hockey. I think the first period tonight, we were slow. We were slow and they scored, they scored. We cannot do that. The table is, it is fine. I do not look at the table much either, you know. But tonight I am happy. Two points.

Jan Kowalczyk

Peter Forsberg, thank you as always. Back to you in the studio.
