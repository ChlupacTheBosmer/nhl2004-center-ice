Joe Thornton on the new-look line: 30 goals in 30 games, and a loss that still stings

Thornton on the rebuilt top line that produced both Boston goals, the personal numbers climbing inside a losing season, and the one home win he wants before anything else.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelWednesday, 15 December 2004

1:44 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Joe Thornton90 set up both goals on the coach's new top line as Boston Bruins fell 2-4 at home to Ottawa. The 25-year-old center sits at 30 goals and 51 points through 30 games, a point a night, and stands 92 in the Bureau's Book, even as the home record sags to 5-11.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here with Joe Thornton, and it's two points for him tonight in a 2-4 night. Big Joe, the coach ran Sarge and Marty on your wings tonight. How did that line look from the middle?

Joe Thornton

Yeah, that's the first time he, uh, put that group out, I think. I like it. Sarge, he wants the puck, and he'll go find the soft ice, and Marty, he's a net guy, you know, he'll stand in there and take the beating. We got the two we scored, and honestly we left a couple more on the board.

Jan Kowalczyk

You outshot them 31-25 tonight, and you're 5-11 in this building. Those two numbers don't line up. What's going the wrong way at home?

Joe Thornton

Ah, it comes down to a couple minutes in the game, you know. We tie it, and then, we turn it over at our blue line and it's in our net. On the road you expect to chase. At home you can't give them the, the free one. That's what's been biting us.

Jan Kowalczyk

You're at 30 goals, 51 points in 30 games. The Book has you at 92 overall. On a night that ends in the loss column, does that number still land with you?

Joe Thornton

It means I'm doing my job, I guess. But, you know, I put up 141 last year, so the bar is mine and it's high. Thirty in thirty's nice. I'd trade half of it, honest, for where we sit. The points don't, they don't feel like much when the room's quiet.

Jan Kowalczyk

On the second goal you had it on the half-wall, waited, and laid it out front for Marty. Walk me through what you saw there.

Joe Thornton

Yeah, the D-man was cheating to the middle, so I just held it and waited for Marty to show. He got his stick down and I put it on there. With that group it's simple, you know. Get it to the net, let them sort it out. I'll take that feed every night.

Jan Kowalczyk

You keep that line together and the wins start coming, does it change the mood of the room heading into the new year?

Joe Thornton

It gives everybody a little air, sure. We got a couple lines that can score, and you can breathe. But we gotta close one out first, you know. Feel it once, and then you build off it. That's the plan. We'll take it from there.

Jan Kowalczyk

Joe, appreciate the time. Good luck this week.
