Brad Richards on an 8-1, scoreless: 'You take the two and you're happy'

A night after a 4-0 road shutout, TOR's center watched the depth do the work in a 12th straight win and walked off empty-handed without a gripe; the jobs nobody sees are what he wants to talk about.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelWednesday, 8 December 2004

1:48 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Brad Richards went home without a point in an 8-1 win and had no complaints. After Toronto Maple Leafs came back from a 4-0 road shutout in New York, the 12th straight win belonged to the depth in front of him: Marian Gaborik96 with 3 points, Mats Sundin87 taking the minutes nobody sees.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here with Brad Richards after an 8-1 win over New Jersey. Brad, you were in New York last night, 4-0 on the road, and tonight the building's on its feet all night. What does that feel like at center ice?

Brad Richards

Oh, it's the best part, honestly. You get in late, you sleep, and then you walk out for warm-up and they're already up. You don't feel the trip, you know, you don't feel any of it when the building's like that.

Jan Kowalczyk

You guys are 7-2 away. Does the back-to-back ever catch up, or do you just ride it?

Brad Richards

No, you ride it. You get home at two, three in the morning, but you win, you sleep good, and the legs show up. Tonight we had them right from the first shift, so. It's a good feeling.

Jan Kowalczyk

Eight goals tonight and Marian's got three points. A guy rolling like that, does the puck just follow him?

Brad Richards

It's what we're doing right now, you know? He's flying. Give him the puck in the middle and he makes things happen. It's fun to watch, honestly. You don't have to do much, just get it to him.

Jan Kowalczyk

On a night like this the show's easy. Who's the guy doing the work that doesn't show up on the sheet, the one the room notices?

Brad Richards

Matsy, always. He takes the hard matchups, he's out there on both ends, and he doesn't get loud about it. That's the stuff nobody sees and everybody rides on.

Jan Kowalczyk

And Dave behind you, you barely heard his name all night. Is that the best kind of night for a goalie?

Brad Richards

Yeah, he'll take it. You don't hear your goalie's name and that's a good night for a goalie. He was there when they pushed, and on a night like this that's all you ask.

Jan Kowalczyk

You put eight up and you're the one guy who goes home empty. Does that stick with you?

Brad Richards

You want to be on the sheet every night, obviously. But I've got 14, I'm not starving. Nights like this you take the two and you're happy. Somebody scores, everybody eats.

Jan Kowalczyk

You're sitting at 44 points, top of the league. What does the room carry into the next one?

Brad Richards

You don't look past it. We get back to work tomorrow, watch video, and go again. It's December, so you keep your head down. That's, that's all it is.

Jan Kowalczyk

Brad Richards, thanks for the time tonight. Take care of the ride home. Back to the studio.
