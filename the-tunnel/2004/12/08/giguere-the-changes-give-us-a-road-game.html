Giguere: the changes give us a road game

After ANA's 3-2 win in Chicago, Jean-Sebastien Giguere credits the coach's reshuffled look and a steady kill with steadying the club's road game.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelWednesday, 8 December 2004

1:58 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

After Mighty Ducks of Anaheim's 3-2 win in Chicago on 2004-12-06, Jean-Sebastien Giguere97 talked to The Tunnel about a kill that left him watching, and a lineup the coach changed. He held Chicago to 2 goals on 24 shots. The club's road record is 4-12; the win is a step, he said, and he has to be the man still. Giguere is 11-13 in 25 games this season.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here with Jean-Sebastien Giguere after the win in Chicago. Three-two, and they pushed you at the end. How does that one sit?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

Good. For sure, good. We play hard, we get the lead, and then, ha, we let them push a little. But the win, the win is the win. On the road, you take it, you don't ask how it, how it comes. You're happy to have the two points.

Jan Kowalczyk

Your coach reshuffled things tonight. Erik Cole to the middle, Joel Kwiatkowski in on the back end. From your crease, what did that look like?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

It's different, you know. Erik in the middle, he's fast, he creates, you see the puck come alive when he's, when he's there. And Joel, he's a guy who wants it. He makes the play, he's not afraid. I like it. The coach, he makes a change and the guys, they respond. They, they want to show him.

Jan Kowalczyk

And the kill tonight. What did you see from your crease?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

The guys, they give me nothing to do, you know? They block, they clear, they get, they get the sticks in the lanes. When it's like that, I just watch. I try to talk to them. 'Stay there, stay there.' And they do. That's, that's the special teams. That's the whole night, I think.

Jan Kowalczyk

Fedorov had two tonight. What's on that bench when he's going like that?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

Sergei, he's the same every night. He don't get low, he don't get high. He scores two and you think he's, he's made one pass. He's special. When he drives like that, I think, okay, we have a chance, every night, in any building.

Jan Kowalczyk

The club's road record is 4-12. Does a win like this mean a little more for that?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

Yeah. It means something. Because the road, it's hard. Every building, the fans, the ice, it's not your night, you have to make it yours. We have to be better on the road, that's it. Tonight was a step. But we cannot, we cannot stop there. One win, that's not, that's not the thing.

Jan Kowalczyk

The coach said it again this week, you're the number one. At 11 and 13, do you feel that?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

I don't think about the number. I go out, I play. The coach, he believes in me, and the guys in front, they believe, and that's enough. The record, it's not mine alone. We win, we lose, together. We have to be better, that's it. I'll, I'll take the next one.

Jan Kowalczyk

Thank you, Jean-Sebastien. Enjoy the flight home.

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

Thank you. Good to talk.
