Roberts and the 38-year-old kid in a room full of 20-somethings

Gary Roberts says the winning streak in Toronto runs on the young guys, not the one who brought home the Conn Smythe.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelTuesday, 7 December 2004

2:31 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Gary Roberts86 sat at 38 in a dressing room where the average age is 25.6, the youngest in the league. Toronto Maple Leafs won 4-0 at New York Islanders on 2004-12-06 to make it 11 straight, the longest run in the league. Roberts has 32 points in 26 games this season, down from 88 last year but close enough that nobody is complaining.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jenna Kowalczyk here with Gary Roberts. Eleven straight, a 4-0 road win, you're 19 and 5. What's the difference between this team and the one that won it last spring?

Gary Roberts

Well, obviously last spring we had the Conn Smythe on the mantle and we've still got the same hunger, you know. But look at this room. The average age is twenty-five. I'm the old man in here. I'm the old man.

Jan Kowalczyk

You're not an old man, you've got thirty-two points in twenty-six games. You're playing seventeen a night though, down from almost twenty-four last year. Does that bother you?

Gary Roberts

Nah. I mean, at thirty-eight, you know, you learn to take what they give you. The coach sits you, you take it. And the young guys are earning it. Gaborik's been unbelievable, Sundin's been unbelievable. I'm just, I'm just chipping in where I can. And, and thirty-two points in twenty-six, that's not bad for a guy who's supposed to be, you know, on his way out.

Jan Kowalczyk

Fourteen shots for, thirteen against tonight. Not a lot of ice time to work with in a game like that.

Gary Roberts

That's the thing about winning, you know? You don't need the shots. You get the lead, you protect it. And Belfour was, Belfour was standing on his head all night, you know. One and a half stars at least for him tonight.

Jan Kowalczyk

You walked into this room in December. What did you see when you first got here?

Gary Roberts

Well, I saw a bunch of kids. That's the honest answer. Spezza, Gaborik, Colaiacovo, Nash, Klesla, I'm going down the list and it's all, all twenty-year-olds or, or twenty-two. I'm the guy in the room going 'hey, where's your bag?' You know what I mean? The old guy telling the young guys to, to stretch. It's, it's funny. It's funny.

Jan Kowalczyk

You've got a year left on your contract. Is this the one where you ride it out and let these kids take over?

Gary Roberts

I don't know, man. I'm thirty-eight. I'm not thinking about next year. I'm thinking about tomorrow's practice and the next game and, and trying to stay off the bench and, and keeping this thing going. Eleven straight is great but, but you can't eat the last eleven wins tonight. You got to, you got to go out again.

Jan Kowalczyk

The league's best offence, the league's best defence, forty-two points in twenty-six games. Is there a ceiling here?

Gary Roberts

I don't think about ceilings. I think about the next shift. You know, that's, that's the cliché, right? One game at a time. But it's, it's the cliché for a reason. You look up at the standings in November you get comfortable. We've got a good group, a, a real good group of kids, and I'm just trying to hang on. Just trying to hang on.

Jan Kowalczyk

Gary Roberts, back to you.
