Forsberg, a point off the lead: 'When Joe is there, we are a good team'

The Avalanche's second-leading scorer in the league, at 55 points through 26 games, sits with a captain out until December 17, and the two of them talk about the run that got away and the small town that made two Swedes.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelTuesday, 7 December 2004

2:53 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Peter Forsberg89 has 55 points in 26 games, tied with Jaromir Jagr for second in the league and one behind Bill Guerin's 59. He sat for the studio table with Joe Sakic84 of Colorado Avalanche, out with a neck injury that has kept him since the start of the month and targeting a December 17 return; Sakic's 10 games hold 3 goals and 16 assists. Together they talked about last spring's final and the Swedish town that raised one of them.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here with Peter Forsberg and Joe Sakic of the Colorado Avalanche. Peter, you're at 55 points in 26 games, tied with Jagr for second, a point off Bill Guerin. Where are you with this season?

Peter Forsberg

Yeah, I think we play good. The line, they find me in the right places, you know. I don't look at the points. I look at the games we win.

Jan Kowalczyk

You've also got 87 penalty minutes. The league's second-leading scorer who hits like a fourth-liner. Is that in the plan?

Peter Forsberg

Somebody has to. I hate losing a battle, you know. It is, I take some penalties I should not take. But I do not want to stop.

Jan Kowalczyk

Joe, you're watching this from a chair. The neck's had you out since the start of the month, target December 17. Walk me through it.

Joe Sakic

It's a neck thing, you know, one of those. I'm skating this week, getting the strength back. The 17th's the date we're aiming at.

Jan Kowalczyk

Peter, you're carrying the thing without him. What's the room like when Joe's not there?

Peter Forsberg

It is different. Joe is the captain a long time. We have the guys who step up. But you want your leader, for sure.

Jan Kowalczyk

Joe, does the pace he's on surprise you at all?

Joe Sakic

No. I've sat beside that guy for a long time, you know. He makes everybody on the ice better. That's not news.

Jan Kowalczyk

Peter, you and Markus Näslund grew up ten days apart in the same Swedish town. He's at 49 points in Vancouver right now. How does one town make two of you?

Peter Forsberg

Hmm. It is small, you know, everybody knows everybody. We played the TV-pucken together in '88 and won it. And you think, maybe this can be something. It was nice to have him along.

Jan Kowalczyk

You went sixth overall in '91, and Philadelphia's papers didn't want you. Did that stick with you?

Peter Forsberg

They had me, I think, number 25. Yeah, I heard about it. And then, you know how that story ended. Ask Joe. He is the one who got me.

Joe Sakic

Best deal we never made, from our side. You know, Eric held out a year, and suddenly in comes this kid from Sweden and the room changed. I took it.

Jan Kowalczyk

That run in the spring, you beat the West, got to the final, and Toronto took it in the end. Does that one still sit with you?

Peter Forsberg

Yeah, of course. We were close. We played good, but they were better that week, you know. You do not get many chances like that.

Jan Kowalczyk

Joe, you had 13 goals and 30 assists in those playoffs. At 35, how many more of those runs have you got?

Joe Sakic

I think about the 17th. Get back in, help the guys. I don't count the runs, you know. You can't.

Jan Kowalczyk

Joe comes back mid-month, you're on this pace, the two of you together again. What's the ceiling for this team?

Peter Forsberg

Ceiling? I do not know ceilings. We play game by game. When Joe is there, we are a good team. It is simple.

Joe Sakic

Don't let him tell you he's simple. I've seen him stay after practice playing with the puck. He cares. He just won't say it.

Jan Kowalczyk

He cares enough to take 87 minutes in penalties. Does Joe ever get you to calm down?

Peter Forsberg

Every day. He tells me, yes, yes, I say, and then I go hit somebody. I cannot stop. It is who I am, you know.

Joe Sakic

Last year I'd've liked the 231 back, you know, but you take the guy. You take all of him.

Jan Kowalczyk

Peter, you're a point off the league lead, and the captain comes back on the 17th. What does the week ahead look like?

Peter Forsberg

We have games, you know. We play, we win, we see. Joe comes back. That is a good day.

Jan Kowalczyk

Peter Forsberg, Joe Sakic, on the Avalanche, thank you both. Back to you.
