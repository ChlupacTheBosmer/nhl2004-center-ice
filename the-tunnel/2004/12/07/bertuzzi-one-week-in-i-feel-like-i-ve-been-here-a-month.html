Bertuzzi, one week in: 'I feel like I've been here a month'

The newly acquired winger says Vancouver's young room has made settling in easy, even after a 6-4 loss at New Jersey on Saturday night.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelTuesday, 7 December 2004

2:02 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Todd Bertuzzi95 joined Vancouver Canucks on December 1 and has 1 goal and 2 points in his first 2 games with the club. The Canucks lost 4-6 on the road at New Jersey Devils on Saturday, falling to 14-8-2 on the season. Marcus Bell caught him in the tunnel after the final whistle.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here in the tunnel at New Jersey with Todd Bertuzzi. Todd, you've been with the club just under a week now. First read on the room?

Todd Bertuzzi

Well, it's been great, you know. Everyone's welcoming, the players, the coaches, the whole organization. I feel like I've been here a month, not a few days. The room's fun, man. That's the first thing I noticed. It's loose, it's not tight. I like that.

Marcus Bell

One goal, two points through two games. You feel like you've found your spot yet?

Todd Bertuzzi

I'm trying not to look at the stat sheet, honestly. I just want to be physical, make the right play, get the puck to Mark or Breezer when they've got a step. The numbers'll come. But yeah, two in two, I'll take it, you know?

Marcus Bell

This is a young group. The average age in that room is 26.9, league average's 28.1. You at 29, you feel like a veteran in there?

Todd Bertuzzi

Nah, I don't think of it that way. There's two guys in that room who are 33 or older, I'm nowhere near them. I mean, 12 of the 30 guys are 24 or under and they've got no fear, you know? They just go. I remember being like that. I like the energy they bring. It keeps you honest.

Marcus Bell

Tonight, 6-4, New Jersey wins. They're 14 and 2 and 3, they're a tough room on any night. Where did this one slip?

Todd Bertuzzi

They outworked us, especially the second period. We got caught coming out of our end a couple times and you can't do that against a team this good. They've got too many guys who can make you pay. We didn't play bad for stretches, we just didn't play bad for 60. That's the difference.

Marcus Bell

You're lined up with Naslund on the same wing. Two high-end wingers, how does that actually work from the inside?

Todd Bertuzzi

Mark's a great player, you know? Everyone knows that. But he's the first guy to make you feel comfortable. He'll look for you on the bench, he'll talk to you between shifts. You don't gotta figure it all out by yourself. And Breezer's in the middle feeding us, so we're not reinventing the wheel, you know? We're just trying to run the play.

Marcus Bell

Last one. You've got 21 minutes a night. Does that feel like what you want, or would you like a little more?

Todd Bertuzzi

I want more, obviously. You play this long, you always want more ice. But 21, 22 minutes against top lines, that's a job. That's respect. I'll take it. I just gotta make sure I'm making the most of every shift I get.

Marcus Bell

Todd Bertuzzi, thank you for the time. Marcus Bell, New Jersey. Back to you.
