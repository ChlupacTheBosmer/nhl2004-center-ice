Five days in, Gaborik watches the heater roll

The new Toronto winger went quiet in a 4-0 win at New York, the club's 11th straight, and talked about finding his legs in a Cup room.

Jenna KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelTuesday, 7 December 2004

2:19 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Marian Gaborik95 has been in Toronto for five days. The Toronto Maple Leafs winger, first seen on the club's roster December 1, did not register a point in a 4-0 road win at New York Islanders, the team's 11th consecutive victory. Jenna Kowalczyk caught him in the tunnel.

Transcript

Jenna Kowalczyk

Jenna Kowalczyk here in the tunnel at New York. Marian Gaborik, five days in a new room and an 11-game win streak. How does it feel to walk into that?

Marian Gaborik

It feels good. Everyone here, they already won it once, so there is a different energy. You feel it right away. The guys make it easy. You just try to keep up.

Jenna Kowalczyk

You came from Minnesota, a young club building something. Now you walk into a room with Belfour, Sundin, Mogilny, Gary Roberts. Different locker room.

Marian Gaborik

Very different. In Minnesota you are building, you are finding out who you are. Here, these guys, they know exactly who they are. Gary, Mats, Ed, they have been through everything. I just listen.

Jenna Kowalczyk

You have 34 points in 26 games this season, 20 goals. Tonight you did not point. Does that bother you, five days in?

Marian Gaborik

Yeah, a little. You want to contribute right away, you want the first goal in the new sweater. But the team won, clean game, nothing against us late. I will get mine.

Jenna Kowalczyk

A shutout in front of you. Belfour back there. What is it like skating back to the bench and seeing him calm like that?

Marian Gaborik

It is funny, right? You expect him to be screaming every save. He is just sitting there, catching pucks, barely moving. You think, is he even awake? Then you realize, he has seen this a thousand times. It calms you down.

Jenna Kowalczyk

Toronto is 19 and 5 now, 42 points. You are part of a run that does not feel like it is slowing. What do you see from the outside that the room sees every day?

Marian Gaborik

They just do not care about the record. That is the thing. You think, 11 wins, everyone outside is talking. In here, it is the next shift. The next game. They won it last year so they know how to be here.

Jenna Kowalczyk

Your contract, one year left, $1.10M. You are 22, you are on a Cup contender. Does this change how you think about the rest of the year?

Marian Gaborik

I think about tomorrow. That is all. You come here to play. This year is the only year right now.

Jenna Kowalczyk

Marian Gaborik, five days in the room. Thanks for stopping. Back to you.
