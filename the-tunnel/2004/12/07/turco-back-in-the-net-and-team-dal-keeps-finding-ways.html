Turco back in the net, and Dallas keeps finding ways

Marty Turco won his first game back from a short absence and called the win streak 'luck so far,' which nobody in that room believes.

Marty Turco92 won the first game back from a brief absence as Dallas Stars beat Montreal Canadiens 6-2 on 2004-12-04, extending the win streak to 5 and the home mark to 9-3. Turco, 29, is 13-8-1 with 1 shutout this season at 58.4 minutes a night. The Stars sit second in the Pacific at 37 points, six back of Colorado.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jenna Kowalczyk here, rinkside. Marty, five in a row, first night back, a 6-2 win. How does that sit?

Marty Turco

Well, it sits pretty good, I'll tell you that. You sit out a couple, you get a little antsy, and then you get out there and the guys put up six and it's, you know, easy work. Obviously nothing against the other guy, but, yeah, it's a nice night.

Jan Kowalczyk

You were out by the first of December, back the same day on paper. Was it that quick a turnaround, or was that a little smoother than it looked?

Marty Turco

Yeah, it was pretty, pretty quick. You know, you don't want to be out, but you don't want to be rushed back either. The trainers did their thing, I did my thing, and here we are. It's nice to be back, obviously.

Jan Kowalczyk

You look like a guy who's been here a while, and this room is the oldest in the league on paper. Average age just over 30. Does that come up in the room, the mileage?

Marty Turco

Yeah, it comes up, it comes up every morning, you know. But I don't know if, look, we've got six guys 24 or under, so it's not all grey hair in there. Obviously some of the guys have been around, they know how to handle the schedule, but I think people outside this building look at the average age and think we're, I mean, we're older than we are, you know?

Jan Kowalczyk

You came in as a fifth-rounder, 124th, so you've seen the other side of that argument.

Marty Turco

Yeah. Well, you know, I was undrafted in junior, played Jr. B in Cambridge, went to Michigan, played in Kalamazoo. So I, yeah, I know what it is to be the guy nobody expected. That's the whole room, I think. Nobody's expecting any of us to be at 37 points. And here we are, still playing.

Jan Kowalczyk

One year left on your deal. Does that kind of thing sit in the back of a guy's mind during a five-game streak, or does a streak make you forget about all that?

Marty Turco

I mean, you forget about a lot of things during a five-game streak. You know, you get home and you're tired, you watch maybe five minutes of whatever's on, and you're asleep. So no, it doesn't sit in the back of my mind. Not right now. Probably it will at some point, but not tonight.

Jan Kowalczyk

Marty Turco, thanks for your time. Enjoy the night. Back to you.
