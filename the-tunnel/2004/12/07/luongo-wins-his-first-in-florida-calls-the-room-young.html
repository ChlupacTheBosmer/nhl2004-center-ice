Luongo wins his first in Florida, calls the room young

The new goaltender won his debut, 2-1 over Carolina, and told Marcus Bell the Panthers are younger than people think.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelTuesday, 7 December 2004

1:44 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Roberto Luongo92 won his first game in a Florida Panthers sweater on 2004-12-04, a 2-1 decision over Carolina Hurricanes, who sit 10-6-6 with 36 points. Luongo arrived in Florida on 2004-12-01. He is 8-11 on the season with 0 shutouts in 19 games.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here in the tunnel with Roberto Luongo. Roberto, first game in a Florida sweater, and you get the two-one. How's that feel?

Roberto Luongo

Yeah, it feels good, for sure. The guys, you know, they played hard tonight. First one in this building, it's... yeah, it means something.

Marcus Bell

Only 17 pucks for Carolina. Did you have to keep yourself honest back there?

Roberto Luongo

Yeah, you know, it's the, it's the hardest part actually, when they don't shoot. You gotta stay, stay in it. And the guys, they blocked shots, they cleared the crease, so I thank them for that. You know, it's not all me back there.

Marcus Bell

You've got a veteran group in front of you. Nieuwendyk, Potvin, Kozlov. Does that experience make it easier to walk into a new room?

Roberto Luongo

No, no, I don't know where people get that, you know. We're a young team. Look, our average is 26.9, the league is 28.1. We got 11 guys 24 or under. Yeah, Joe's here, Felix is here, they're great guys, great leaders. But this is, this is a young room. That's just, that's what it is. He's young, this whole team is young.

Marcus Bell

Fair enough. Your own game, though: 8 and 11, no shutouts yet. You had 3 last year in 65 games. What's different?

Roberto Luongo

I think... the wins, you know, they take a minute. I know where my game is. I feel good, my game is there. It's just, some nights the, the team doesn't get me the one. And I had 3 last year, yeah, that's, that's fine, but I care about wins more than shutouts. The shutouts, they come when we play our game. That's, that's how I see it.

Marcus Bell

The Index has you at 93, up 2. Potential 98. You see that ceiling?

Roberto Luongo

Yeah, I see it, for sure. It's nice, the numbers, you know? But tonight I just care about the two-one. I can't, I don't care what the Index says on a night we win. The Index is tomorrow. Tonight is tonight.

Marcus Bell

Where does this group go? You're 9-14-1 right now.

Roberto Luongo

Yeah, I know the record. We have to be better, that's it. The young guys, they're getting better every night, and the vets, they're holding it together. We just need more of these, you know. More nights like tonight. That's all.

Marcus Bell

Roberto Luongo, first win in a Florida sweater. Thank you, Roberto.

Roberto Luongo

Thank you, Marcus. Good to see you.

Marcus Bell

Back to you.
