Mogilny: 'I am old man in here' on Toronto's 11th straight

The 35-year-old winger, six days in a new room, laughed off a quiet night and a dressing room he calls younger than he expected.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelTuesday, 7 December 2004

1:52 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Alexander Mogilny85 joined Toronto Maple Leafs on 2004-12-01 and skated in a 4-0 road win at New York Islanders on 2004-12-06, the 11th straight for a club that leads the Northeast with 42 points on a 19-5-0 record. He had no points tonight; he carries 39 in 26 games on the season at 17.1 minutes a night.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here in the tunnel with Alexander Mogilny. Eleven straight, four to nothing on the road, twenty-seven shots between both teams. Alexander, what did you see out there?

Alexander Mogilny

Team defense. Everybody played tonight. Every line, we defend, we block, we clear the front. We must play hard, every shift, every game. Tonight all of us, we did. That is why four and zero.

Jan Kowalczyk

You were held off the scoresheet yourself. That's not what people expect from a man with 39 points in 26 games this season.

Alexander Mogilny

I know what they think. Mogilny, no points, something wrong? No. Four to zero, shutout, on road, I take this every time. My points, they come, they go. Team wins, this is important.

Jan Kowalczyk

You've been in this room six days now. I think most people picture Toronto as a veteran group, the guys who brought the Cup home in June. What did you find?

Alexander Mogilny

Veteran? No, no. I walk in, first day, I look around. I think, 'I am too old for this.' I look again. These are boys! Average in here is twenty-five, twenty-six. I am thirty-five. I am old man. But energy, of course, is good. These young players, they skate, they go, they have no fear. I watch them and I remember. I was like this.

Jan Kowalczyk

You're clearly being used in specific spots. The coach sits you a lot but when you're out there you touch the puck a great deal. Does that role suit you?

Alexander Mogilny

Yes, of course. I am thirty-five, I cannot play like twenty-three. Nobody can. But in minutes I get, I must be dangerous. Puck is there, I must do something with it. Coach knows what he is doing. I know what I do. This is good.

Jan Kowalczyk

The Cup has been in Toronto a year. Eleven wins, the longest streak in the league. People are talking about another run. Do you hear that in there?

Alexander Mogilny

How do you not hear? Everyone talks, on TV, in the street. But Cup is in June. Tonight is December. We won tonight, that is good. Tomorrow, practice, next game. We must stay at work. Just work. Streak, it is nice. But it is December.

Jan Kowalczyk

Alexander Mogilny, thank you very much. Enjoy the win.
