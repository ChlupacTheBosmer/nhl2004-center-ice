Gaborik, five days in, says Toronto's room is not the one people picture

After a quiet night in a 4-0 win, the newest Leaf says the room is younger than its reputation.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelTuesday, 7 December 2004

1:21 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Marian Gaborik98 finished scoreless in Toronto Maple Leafs's 4-0 road win at NYI on 2004-12-06, the club's 11th straight. He carries 34 points in 26 games this season and joined the club on 2004-12-01. The Bureau's Development Index has him at +6, putting his overall at 95.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jenna Kowalczyk, rinkside here at Nassau Coliseum. Marian, 4-0, shutout, eleventh straight. What is it about this stretch?

Marian Gaborik

It's the whole group. We defense very good tonight, the D-men, Ed in net, everybody. You know when it's like this, zero on the board, the whole bench is... it's easy. Everybody is happy, everybody trusts. We play good game last eleven games.

Jan Kowalczyk

You yourself didn't put anything on the sheet tonight. Thirty-four points in twenty-six this season, that's a good pace. Does it nag when a night goes like that?

Marian Gaborik

A little, yeah. I want to score every night, that's just how it is in my head, you know? But when the team wins like this, 4-0 on the road, it's hard to be mad. Maybe tomorrow I watch video and I am not so happy. Tonight? I am fine.

Jan Kowalczyk

You've only been in this room five days. What's different about Toronto?

Marian Gaborik

Different guys, different way. Everybody talks about Belfour, Sundin, Mogilny, Gary, they think this is old team, a veteran room. No, no. You look around the locker? Many guys young like me. Very young. It's fast, everybody wants the puck, nobody is just standing. I like that. It's... how you say, hungry. Everybody hungry.

Jan Kowalczyk

The Bureau has you at 95 overall, up six on the Index. You feel that form out there tonight?

Marian Gaborik

I feel okay, I feel like me. Nine-five, it's a nice number in the paper, but it don't mean nothing if we not win cups in June. I just want to be ready every night. That is all.

Jan Kowalczyk

Marian Gaborik, thank you. Try to make it twelve.
