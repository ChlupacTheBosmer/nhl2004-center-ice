Vokoun: 7 wins, 0 blanks, and the 67.5 minutes that keep adding up

After the 4-3 win over Minnesota that gives Nashville its seventh straight, the 28-year-old Czech says the streak belongs to the room and the missing shutout is the one number he can't let go.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelFriday, 17 December 2004

1:40 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Tomas Vokoun91 has not recorded a shutout in 25 games this season, but Nashville Predators don't seem to mind. The 4-3 win over Minnesota on Tuesday night is the seventh straight, and the 28-year-old, who has played 67.5 minutes a night, came out of the tunnel still catching his breath.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here in the tunnel at the Predators' building, Tomas Vokoun just came out of a 4-3 win over Minnesota. That makes it seven straight for Nashville. Tomas, thank you for making time.

Tomas Vokoun

Yeah, thank you. It is close, you know. They come, we come back. Third period, they get the goal, and then the boys find one more. The thing with these wins, they never come easy. I see the puck, I make the save, and the boys in front, they clear it. That is all I can do, you know.

Marcus Bell

Seven in a row. What's it feel like from behind the net, stepping in knowing the whole building is feeding off it?

Tomas Vokoun

Ah, you know, when you are in the net, you see the bench, you see the guys. And they are calm. That is the thing. Seven games, and the feeling, it is the same. I step in, I play. The streak is the room's. I just hold the door, you know.

Marcus Bell

67.5 minutes a night this year. Last year it was 62.2. You're playing a lot more of the overtime stretches. Does it change how you manage a third period?

Tomas Vokoun

Ah, yes, it is different, you know. In the third, when you know the overtime is possible, you slow down a little. Let the boys kill, let them clear, and I just watch. I don't try to be the hero. I see the puck, I make the save, I wait. If it is overtime, it is overtime. My legs, they are okay. 28 years, the body, it is still there, you know.

Marcus Bell

You've gone 25 games without a blank. Zero shutouts. Last year you had two in 68. Is that the number that stays with you?

Tomas Vokoun

Yeah, that one, that one I think about, you know. 25 games, and the zero, it is not there. And this year, I try, I try, but the boys, they let one in, you know. First period, second period, one goal, and the game, it is different after. So I wait. It will come. But the win is more important, you know. 15 wins, that is the number I look at.

Marcus Bell

Tomas Vokoun, seventh straight win, still no blank. We'll catch you next week. Thanks for the time, Tomas. Back to you.

Tomas Vokoun

Thank you. Good building tonight. The fans are here, and that helps. Thank you, Marcus.
