Murray: Cechmanek's in Saturday, the kids have earned it

After a 3-2 road win at PHX, the Kings' head coach confirms his starter for Saturday and lays out why the young men are on the second line.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelFriday, 17 December 2004

2:59 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Andy Murray has been the head coach of Los Angeles Kings since 1999, and after a 3-2 win on the road at Phoenix Coyotes on 2004-12-15, he spoke with The Tunnel about the goaltending call for Saturday and the young players earning minutes. The Kings sit 8-14-5 in the Pacific, and the week ahead brings three games in five days.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here, outside the arena after a 3-2 win for the Kings. Andy, thanks for the time. You come in here to Phoenix, you know it's not an easy place to get it, and you do. How does that sit with you?

Andy Murray

Yeah, well, it's two points on the road and that's what we're after. The compete level was there, the details were there, and we managed the puck well enough to get the two. I'm happy with that. You know, you come in here and the building's loud and the ice is a little different, and you've got to find a way. We did tonight.

Marcus Bell

Okay, so Saturday you've got the Sharks at home. Roman Cechmanek's been in net 26 times this season, Cristobal Huet's got 8. How do you think about that call for Saturday?

Andy Murray

Well, obviously Roman's our starter. He's been out there all year, 26 games, and that's a lot. But the record's 10 and 15, and we've got to, we've got to be more consistent in front of him. We manage the puck, we block, we keep it out of his corners. That's the job on the ice. The rest is his. Saturday, I'd say Roman's in. He's our guy.

Marcus Bell

But Huet's 8 games is a decent chunk of the year. Is there a point where you're thinking about spreading that differently, or is it just, Roman's the guy and that's that?

Andy Murray

I think the 8 games come in situations where we need to, you know, manage a game or give a kid a look. That's fine. But I don't think we're at a point where we're trying to change the structure. Roman's the goalie, we build around him. The record's not where we want it to be, but the play is there. We've got to give him more of a cushion, and that's on the guys in front of him.

Marcus Bell

Let's talk about the kids. You've got Mike Cammalleri, 22 years old, 9 games in and 10 points, and you've put him on the second line as a left wing. And Jared Aulin, also 22, is up on the second line as center. What are you seeing that makes that the call?

Andy Murray

Yeah, well, Mike's been in the building, he's been with us in the minors, and he's earned it. You know, they say don't look at the numbers, but 10 points in 9 games is a good rate. And he's a competitor, he's got the shot, he's got the speed, and he plays with a little fearlessness about him that I like. And Jared, he's been in the system a long time, he's a tough guy to play against, he doesn't give you a lot. So yeah, both of them have earned the second line. Whether they're there all year, I don't know, but for now they're playing well and that's what matters.

Marcus Bell

And Alexander Frolov's 22 too, on the first line, 36 points in 30 games. When did you decide these kids were ready for this kind of stretch?

Andy Murray

Well, it's been a process, you know. Alexander's been developing, he's been in the system, and then the injuries came, you know, Jason Allison, Adam Deadmarsh, Zizka, and all of a sudden you've got to find minutes. And these kids, they've been ready. They've been ready for a while. We just had to find the space. And when you find the space, you put them in and you see what happens. They're responding.

Marcus Bell

Allison, when's he back?

Andy Murray

He's got a wrist, it's not a big deal, but we've got to let it heal. He's out until the 22nd. He's a good player, he's been here a while, and we miss him. But we play the guys in front of us.

Marcus Bell

Last one, Andy. You've got three games in a week, Saturday at home, Monday in Anaheim, Thursday at home again. How do you manage that with the group you've got? Back to you.

Andy Murray

Same as any other week. We practice hard, we rest when we need to, we take it one game at a time. The schedule's the schedule, you know. We've got to be ready for Saturday, we've got to be ready for Monday, and we've got to be ready for Thursday. Thanks, Marcus.
