Giguere's game is up three. The wins went the other way.

Seven straight losses, one overtime goal that ends the night, and a goalie whose Index is climbing with no one in front of him to show for it.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelSunday, 8 January 2006

Jean-Sebastien Giguere97 finished with 21 shots against and 3 goals allowed as Mighty Ducks of Anaheim fell 3-2 in overtime to Dallas at home on 2005-01-05, their seventh straight loss. His Development Index sits at +3, the best form he has shown all season, and Mighty Ducks of Anaheim are 11-16-5 with zero shutouts through 40 games.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here with Jean-Sebastien Giguere in the corridor at the Pond. Seven straight now. The overtime goal, what did you see?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

The screen. You know, the one in overtime, he had a look from the circle and there was a body in front of me and I didn't see it until, I think, it was too late. That's the thing. We work, we work, sixty minutes to get there, and then one second, that's it.

Marcus Bell

Your form is the best we have seen from you all season. Three points up on the Index. Did you feel that tonight in your game?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

Yeah, for sure. I felt good tonight. The legs were there, the first step, everything. I made the saves in regulation that I needed to make. But you know what, it doesn't matter if I feel great and we're sitting at zero points after tonight. That's the thing that eats at you.

Marcus Bell

This time last year we talked in this same corridor and you told me you wanted to talk about two points instead of the scoreboard. Here we are again.

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

Yeah, yeah. I remember. So here we are again. I still want the same thing. Maybe in February we get it. That's what I tell myself anyway.

Marcus Bell

Havelid had two points tonight, Fedorov got one. Against Dallas, who sit at the top of the division, you make it a one-goal game into overtime. What does that say about this group?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

It says the guys are there. Havelid tonight, what he did on the rush, getting there, driving the net, he's been getting there all year and the pucks are starting to go in. And Fedorov... you don't have to tell Fedorov anything. He shows up. But you know, a goal in a game we lose, it feels like nothing after ten minutes. Then you get on the bus and it's zero.

Marcus Bell

Jean-Sebastien Giguere, thank you as always. Back to you.
