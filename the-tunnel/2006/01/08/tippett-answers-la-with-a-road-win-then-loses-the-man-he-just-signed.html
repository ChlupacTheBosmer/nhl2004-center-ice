Tippett answers LA with a road win, then loses the man he just signed

The Stars coach addressed the 22-24 goal differential hiding under a 1st-place record, the Varada signing three days old, and the leg that put the winger out until February.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelSunday, 8 January 2006

3:47 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Dallas Stars won 3-2 in overtime at Mighty Ducks of Anaheim on 2006-01-05, one night after scoring zero in a 2-0 loss at Los Angeles Kings. Dallas sits 1st in the Pacific at 23-10-6, 60 points, but the last 10 games carry a minus-2 goal differential. The night before the game, the club signed winger Vaclav Varada for $468,040. He is out until 2006-02-13 with a leg injury.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Dave, Marcus Bell here, Center Ice, right after the game. You just beat Anaheim 3-2 in overtime on the second night of a back-to-back on the road. What did you see in that third period and the overtime that you didn't see the night before in Los Angeles?

Dave Tippett

Yeah, well, obviously Los Angeles wasn't, that wasn't the same team tonight. And you know what, I thought our compete level was there tonight. We managed the puck better, we didn't turn it over in our own end the way we did the night before. Those are the details. You win the hockey game because you don't beat yourself. Tonight we didn't beat ourselves enough times to lose.

Marcus Bell

That LA game, zero goals, 2-0 loss to a team that's eight and nineteen. You're scoring three tonight in overtime. Where's the line between, you know, a bad night and something that's actually a problem?

Dave Tippett

Uh, look, I don't think it's a problem. I think it's a night where, you know, things didn't go in. We had chances, we just, we managed to not convert and then you fall behind and the momentum goes. You get one, you get the second one tonight, the ice opens up a little. LA, the second one goes in and now you're chasing and you don't have the legs, second night, on the road. I don't lose any sleep over it.

Marcus Bell

I want to put something up on the screen for you, because I think your record hides it. Over the last ten games, you've scored twenty-two and given up twenty-four. You're winning these close ones but you're outscored. Is that something you look at in the room?

Dave Tippett

You look at it. Yeah. But here's the thing about being outscored in ten, you know, some of those are one-zero, two-one, overtime games. When you're in those games, you're in them because the structure is holding. I'd rather be in a one-zero game in the third than a five-zero game where nobody's backchecking. I know how that sounds. Every coach says it. But I'll take the one-zero.

Marcus Bell

Let me ask you about something that happened this week. You signed Vaclav Varada on January second. He's played forty-two games for you this year. Three days later, he's hurt, leg, back February thirteenth. What happened?

Dave Tippett

I don't know. Sometime, I mean, we're not sure exactly when, he's got a leg thing. We found out after the game last night, or, you know, it's been a few days. He's not going to be back until the thirteenth. Obviously we wanted him, obviously that's why we signed him, and now you lose that, that bottom-six forward who blocks shots, who kills penalties, who, you know, plays in the corners. So you make adjustments. You dress somebody else.

Marcus Bell

The room, how did they take the signing? Three days, you square a guy away, and then he goes down. Is there a feeling in there?

Dave Tippett

You know what, the guys were happy for him. You see a guy that's been, that plays forty-two out of forty-three games, that's not, that's not a guy who's collecting a cheque. He's in your face every night, he's blocking pucks. The guys respect that. And the signing was the right thing. The injury, that's hockey. That's hockey and you deal with it. You don't feel sorry for yourself.

Marcus Bell

You brought Richard Matvichuk back in tonight. He'd been sitting, twenty-three games this year. What made you put him in there?

Dave Tippett

He's been practising. I mean, that's, that's the, the answer I give everybody. But he's been practising well and he's a guy that, you know, he's played a lot of hockey, he's not going to make a mistake. He's not going to do something stupid on your blue line. We needed one of those right now with what we've got on the back end. He fits.

Marcus Bell

Marty Turco has started thirty-three of your forty-three games. You've got Jason Bacashihua who's played ten. Is there a conversation about managing that, or is it still Marty's net?

Dave Tippett

It's Marty's net. Marty's been great for us. And, you know, you get asked this every year, this time of year. Thirty-three starts. I think he can handle it. He's in there most nights and we need him in there most nights. Jason is ready. He's played ten games and he's played well. If Marty needs a night, Jason plays. But I don't, I don't plan on sitting Marty more than I have to.

Marcus Bell

You get Chicago at home on Tuesday, Colorado Thursday. Two teams fighting for different things. What's the plan for the week?

Dave Tippett

Play hockey. You know, Chicago, they've won three straight, they're desperate. Colorado's won four. You don't take a night off in this league, not this time of year, and we just proved that in Los Angeles. We just proved it. So you prepare the same way. You hope the details hold.

Marcus Bell

Dave Tippett, thank you again. Back to you.
