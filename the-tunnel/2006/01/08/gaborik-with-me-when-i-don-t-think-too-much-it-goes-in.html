Gaborik: 'With me, when I don't think too much, it goes in'

Gaborik scored his goal, liked the line his coach handed him, and had nothing personal to offer after Toronto's 4-3 overtime loss to Boston.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelSunday, 8 January 2006

1:22 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

Marian Gaborik99 scored 1 goal and 1 point and Toronto Maple Leafs still lost 4-3 in overtime to Boston at home on 2006-01-07. The 23-year-old winger, the Bureau's 99 and up 11 on the Development Index, sat with Jan Kowalczyk in the tunnel to talk about the line his coach changed, how it felt on the ice, and a special-teams night that stayed close. He gave the goal, and nothing else.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here with Marian Gaborik, a 4-3 overtime loss to Boston at home. Marian, you scored, and still Boston takes the extra point. What happened out there?

Marian Gaborik

Yeah. We play good, I mean, we had our chances, you know. 3 goals at home, most nights that is enough. Tonight it is not. Then overtime, and anything can happen.

Jan Kowalczyk

Your coach changed the lines in the third and put you out with Mats. What changed for you?

Marian Gaborik

Mats, he is, you know, he sees the ice. He finds you when you don't even think you are open. So it is easy for me, I just keep my legs moving and the puck comes.

Jan Kowalczyk

Take me through your goal.

Marian Gaborik

It is quick, uh, the puck comes and I don't think, I just shoot. With me, when I don't think too much, it goes in.

Jan Kowalczyk

The special teams. That is where these games get decided now.

Marian Gaborik

Yeah, special teams, you know, it is a big part. We work on it, every day we work on it. Tonight it is, how you say, close. The puck, it doesn't go in for us. Some nights it goes.

Jan Kowalczyk

Last time we talked after a night like this, you told me nobody in the room cares about your goal total after a loss.

Marian Gaborik

I remember. It is the same today, Jan. You score, okay, but we lose, so what is it? Nothing. I don't have the personal for you tonight.

Jan Kowalczyk

Then the week. What is next for this group?

Marian Gaborik

Rest, and then back to work, you know. We are a good team. One night is one night, we go again.

Jan Kowalczyk

Marian Gaborik, after a 4-3 overtime loss to Boston. Back to you.
