The Stars' goaltender faced 23 shots, gave up three to a Colorado team on eight straight, and said the one that won it belongs to him.

Marty Turco97 stopped 20 of 23 shots as Dallas Stars dropped a 3-2 overtime decision to Colorado Avalanche at home on Wednesday night. Colorado extended its streak to eight straight wins. Dallas sits at 24-11-7, 63 points, and a one-game losing streak.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here with Marty Turco at the boards, thanks for staying, man. Two-one going into overtime, you're out there again, and then it's over. What did that last 30 seconds look like from your side?

Marty Turco

Yeah, uh... I saw the pass coming to the left circle and I thought, okay, he's going to pull it back and shoot. And he did pull it back, he just got it across me and I was, you know, I was on the wrong foot. That one's on me. I should have challenged the shooter more instead of holding the gap.

Jan Kowalczyk

You're right, it was 23 shots tonight. Three on the board. Colorado's skating on eight wins straight, they came in with some gas. Did it feel like they were the one team that looked comfortable out there tonight?

Marty Turco

I mean, they're a good team, they've got eight in a row for a reason. They're not pressing, they're just, you know, playing their game and it's working. But I don't think they outplayed us tonight. Twenty-three shots against, we had twenty-five the other way. It's one bounce, one read, and the whole thing flips.

Jan Kowalczyk

You told me back in January that home ice for you is legs and sleep, not magic. You're thirteen and seven at home, which is a good number, but you've dropped two of the last three here. Is something wearing down in the building?

Marty Turco

No, I don't think so. I mean, yeah we lost one in overtime and that's, what, a coin flip? You're not going to win them all in overtime. The legs part is real, Jan, the schedule's real, but I don't think tonight was a legs night. I think I gave up the one I shouldn't have. You know what I mean? That's the answer.

Jan Kowalczyk

The other thing I wanted to ask about the room. You've got Jason behind you, and Ron. And there's been some movement in the back end, guys in and out of the lineup. How are you reading the depth right now, behind you specifically?

Marty Turco

Jase is good. He's, what, 91? I don't know what they grade him, I don't look at it. He's 91-something in my eyes. He's a young guy who's got some work to do but he's athletic, he moves well. And Ron, Ron's been here forever, he's the guy you want in your building. If something happens to me, and I'm not saying it will, but if, you've got two guys who can do it. That's not a worry for me.

Jan Kowalczyk

You're not looking at your own grade? You're 97 right now on the Bureau's Book. That's the highest in this league.

Marty Turco

Yeah, I've heard that. I don't read it, honestly. If I read it I'd start thinking about it and then I'd be trying to be a 97 instead of just playing. You know what I mean? You just go out and make the saves you can make and if you get beat on one, like tonight, you move to the next one.

Jan Kowalczyk

Alright, last one. Thursday you're back in it, you play again, and the schedule doesn't let up. Does a night like tonight stay with you or do you wash it off?

Marty Turco

It stays for about twenty minutes in the car. Then it doesn't. I'll watch the clip tonight, see what I should've done, and then I'm done. Thursday's Thursday. Back to the well.

Jan Kowalczyk

Marty Turco, thank you, back to you.
