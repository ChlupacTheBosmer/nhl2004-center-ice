Brodeur and the 3-2 life continue in Sunrise

The Devils' goaltender on why a Panthers team on a five-game skid is the kind of game that bites you, and why another one-goal win feels exactly the same as the last one.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelThursday, 12 January 2006

2:00 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Martin Brodeur stopped 21 of 23 shots as the New Jersey Devils Devils beat the Florida Panthers Panthers 3-2 in Sunrise on Monday, their second straight win and their fifteenth on the road this season. Colin White, Jamie Langenbrunner, and Erik Rasmussen scored for New Jersey, which sits at 24-9-3 and trails Toronto Maple Leafs by nine points in the overall race.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here with Martin Brodeur in the tunnel at the National Car Rental Center. Martin, Florida is eleven and twenty-four, they're five straight losses, they're playing at home. How do you walk into a building like that and keep your teeth sharp?

Martin Brodeur

You don't look at the standings, that's, that's the whole thing. They're at home, they're desperate, and a desperate team at home is the worst kind of team to play. You know, everybody wants to beat us right now. And when you're on a long streak of losses, you've got nothing to lose, so you play free, you shoot the puck, you get a little loose. That's the dangerous one.

Marcus Bell

Another 3-2. You told me back in January this is your identity, that this is what this team does. Still true after twenty-four wins?

Martin Brodeur

Yeah. We score three, they score two, we get two points. I don't know what you want me to say, it's, it's who we are right now. We don't blow people out. But we're twenty-four and nine so, you know, I'll take the 3-2. You take the two points and you go home.

Marcus Bell

You saw twenty-three shots tonight, so this wasn't a busy night by your standards, but late in the third it was still 3-2 and Florida had some momentum. Was there a stretch where you thought, okay, they might get one more?

Martin Brodeur

Yeah, the last, the last five, six minutes they started, they pulled a guy back and they just, they dumped it in and they went to the net. That's when you want the puck to stay in the other end. And our guys, I mean Colin with two points tonight from the blue line, that's, that's how you get a game done. The defensemen chip in, you hold the zone, you make them chase. It's not pretty but it's, it's hockey.

Marcus Bell

You mentioned Colin, and Langenbrunner scored tonight too, Rasmussen got one. Three different guys on the scoresheet.

Martin Brodeur

That's the way it has to be. We don't have a guy with fifty goals, you know what I mean? So you need Colin on the back end, you need Jamie, you need Erik, you need the third line to get one in there. Everybody scores four or five, six, that's how you win games in this league. That's, that's for sure how you win games in this league.

Marcus Bell

One more thing before you get on the bus. You're at thirty-three, you've been here your whole career, and you're still playing like a man with something to prove. What keeps the edge there?

Martin Brodeur

You just want to win, you know? It doesn't matter if you're thirty-three or twenty-three. You dress, you want the two points, you go home. And this group, the guys in that room, they make it easy to come in every day. They work, they don't quit, and that's what I need from them to be able to do my job back here. So, no, I'm not tired of it yet.

Marcus Bell

Martin Brodeur, thank you. Back to you.
