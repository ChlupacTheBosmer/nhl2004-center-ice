Gaborik: the building knows when we are rolling

After a 5-2 win over Calgary that made it four straight, Marian Gaborik talks about the crowd feeding the streak, what Petr Chlup brings to the line, and why 1 point in 2 games does not bother him.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelSunday, 15 January 2006

1:39 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Marian Gaborik99 and Toronto Maple Leafs won 5-2 at home over Calgary Flames on Saturday night, their fourth straight. He has 1 point in the last 2 games but the team is 18-7 at home and leads the Northeast by 20 points.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here with Marian Gaborik after a 5-2 win over Calgary Flames®, and that makes it four straight for Toronto Maple Leafs®. Marian, the building tonight, it was loud. Did you feel that shift in there?

Marian Gaborik

Yeah, you know, when we start, uh, when we score and we keep pushing, the building, it goes. It goes with us. Tonight was good, the crowd was with us from the first shift. And when you are winning, everybody is happy. The building feels different when you are on a streak.

Jan Kowalczyk

Calgary came in with a good road record, 15-8 on the year. Did they play a style tonight that tested you?

Marian Gaborik

They play good game, they are fast and they, uh, they keep coming. But tonight we, I think we were faster on the puck. They had some chances, of course, but the second period, we got the goals and after that they don't have, how you say, the legs to come back.

Jan Kowalczyk

Petr Chlup had 4 points in the last 2 games. What are you seeing from him on that line?

Marian Gaborik

Petr, he is everywhere. He is at the net, he is in the corner, he just, he wants it. And tonight he got the results. It is good for him, good for the line. He makes me, how you say, he makes my game easy because he is always there.

Jan Kowalczyk

You are sitting at 1 point in your last 2 yourself. Does that bother you when the team is winning?

Marian Gaborik

No. No, it doesn't. You know, when I was young I worried about the numbers. Now I look at the scoreboard and it says five, two. I don't care if I have one or zero tonight. The team wins, I sleep good. That is enough for me.

Jan Kowalczyk

Four straight, 18 wins at home. How do you keep the room from getting comfortable?

Marian Gaborik

We don't get comfortable. Well, maybe for one night we are happy. But tomorrow the coach, he puts the video on and he shows you everything we did wrong. And you forget the streak. You just want to fix the next game. That is how it works.

Jan Kowalczyk

Marian, good game. Thanks for your time. Jan Kowalczyk, back to you.
