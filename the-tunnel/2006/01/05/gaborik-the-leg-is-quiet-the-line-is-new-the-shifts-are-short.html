Gaborik: the leg is quiet, the line is new, the shifts are short

Back from a six-week leg injury, the winger says the coach has him on a different line and keeps him off the ice in short bursts.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelThursday, 5 January 2006

2:01 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Marian Gaborik [308] picked up an assist in his fourth game back from a leg injury that kept him out from mid-November to December 29, as Toronto Maple Leafs beat New Jersey Devils 3-0 at home on January 4. Roberto Luongo stopped all 31 shots. Gaborik skated on a line with Petr Chlup and Stanislav Chistov, both of whom scored.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here with Marian Gaborik in the corridor at the Air Canada Centre. Marian, three-nothing, Luongo takes all 31 shots New Jersey throws at him. You had the assist on one of them. How does the leg feel now, four games in?

Marian Gaborik

Yeah, it's good. I mean, uh, first two games I was thinking about it a little, you know? Like, I push off and I think, is it there. Now, no, I don't think about it. I just go.

Marcus Bell

You told me back in December, the shorter shifts keep your speed at full. Is this the same idea, or is the coach doing something different now with you back?

Marian Gaborik

No, no, it's, you know, different now. Before was, okay, let's keep speed for whole game. Now, uh, it's the leg. Coach says, go, four five shifts, then you sit, you breathe, you go again. So I go. I mean I go fast, you know? Fast is not problem. It's just, after six, seven shifts, the leg gets, uh, heavy. So now it's like, five shifts, you sit. Five shifts, you sit.

Marcus Bell

And you're on a new line tonight. Chlup centering you, Chistov on the left. That's not where you were before the injury?

Marian Gaborik

No. Before I was with Mats, you know, and, uh, different wing. But while I was out, they, uh, they found something, Petr and Staño, they play good, they move the puck. And coach says, why change? So, yeah. I just show up and, I try to, uh, not be stupid on their play.

Marcus Bell

Tell me about the assist. What did you see there?

Marian Gaborik

Uh, it was, okay, Staño wins it behind, behind their net, and Petr is going to, to the slot, and I'm on the wall, you know? And I just, I go to the net. I go hard, and the D-man, he looks at Petr, and I'm there, and the puck is there, and Staño is, how you say, in the right place. He's not my pass really. It's more like, uh, I'm just standing there and it comes to me. But a point is a point.

Marcus Bell

Thirty-one shots on the other side, though. New Jersey had looks. Did it ever feel like one was going to go in?

Marian Gaborik

Yeah, second period, maybe two, three times. They hit the post, I think, and then Luongo, uh, he makes, what, six, seven big ones? He's just, you know, he's in there laughing. You can see him laughing. So no. If you can't score on him tonight, that's, that's not our problem.

Marcus Bell

Marian Gaborik, thank you. Enjoy the win. Back to you.
