Lewis on Bunka, Uhlback, and the three wins nobody saw coming

The Detroit coach says the 19-year-old defenseman and the 20-year-old winger are earning shifts now, not next season.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelThursday, 5 January 2006

3:37 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

[team:DET] beat Phoenix Coyotes 3-2 on January 3, the third straight win for a club that had dropped three of four before it. Head coach Dave Lewis talked to The Tunnel after the game about Boris Bunka81, Samu Uhlback, and why the room's mood shifted faster than the standings suggest.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here, in the tunnel at Joe Louis Arena, with Dave Lewis. Three in a row, twenty-five shots against seventeen. How's the room after tonight?

Dave Lewis

Good. You know, we've been working at the same things for a while now and tonight it just came together a little bit cleaner. The details, the puck management, those things that we talk about every single day. You get three wins and everybody wants to know what changed. Nothing changed. We just executed.

Jan Kowalczyk

Seventeen shots for Phoenix. Is that a structure thing, did you have a plan to take away their entry?

Dave Lewis

I mean, look, Phoenix didn't press much tonight. I wouldn't say we smothered anybody. They had some looks. But we managed the puck well in our own end, we didn't turn it over in bad areas, so the game stayed in their zone longer than it might have. You know, a shot differential isn't always what you want to credit to your structure. Sometimes the other guy just has an off night.

Jan Kowalczyk

Let's talk about Boris Bunka. Nineteen years old, thirty-three games, eleven points on the year. Nine of them are assists. How do you manage a kid that young in the middle of this?

Dave Lewis

You don't manage him. You put him out there and he earns it or he doesn't. And Boris is earning it. That's the simple answer. He's got good hands, he's not afraid to make a play, and he's not making the same mistake twice, which is really all you can ask. You know, the numbers are the numbers but what I see is a kid who comes in every morning ready to work. That's what gets you shifts in this league.

Jan Kowalczyk

And Samu Uhlback, twenty years old, twenty-nine games, eight points. Are these guys the future in Detroit or are they part of the present?

Dave Lewis

I don't like the word future. You know what I mean? If I start thinking about next year I'm going to shortchange what these guys are doing right now. Samu is helping us win hockey games. Boris is helping us win hockey games. They're not here on a development plan. They're here because they're good enough to dress and they're good enough to play. And that's true for a lot of the young guys in our room.

Jan Kowalczyk

You've got seven guys twenty-four or under in that room. That's a lot of youth mixed with the veterans.

Dave Lewis

I've got a room full of players. That's how I see it. Niklas is thirty-five, he's playing thirty-nine games a night and he's never going to let you down. The young guys watch that. They don't need me to tell them what a professional looks like. They look at the ice, they look at Lidstrom, they look at Shanahan when he's out there, and they figure it out. My job is to not get in the way of that.

Jan Kowalczyk

Shanahan and Datsyuk, fifteen games each. Neither one's on the injured list now. Are they full-time men for the rest of this stretch?

Dave Lewis

They're available. That's all I can tell you. And when they're out there they make us better, obviously. Brendan's fifteen games and twenty-four points? You know, the production doesn't surprise me. What surprises me is the games he missed. But he's here, that's what matters.

Jan Kowalczyk

Manny Legace and Curtis Joseph, both twenty-four and thirteen games. How are you splitting that net?

Dave Lewis

You know, I get asked that every single week. Manny and Curtis are two guys who both want to start every game and I respect that. I'll tell you what, whoever's in there, the other guy backs him up and they're both ready. That's the answer. It's always the answer.

Jan Kowalczyk

You've got Tampa Bay Thursday, Florida Friday, Philadelphia next Tuesday. Three in four days on the road. What are you worried about?

Dave Lewis

I'm worried about Tampa Bay Thursday. You know, that's the cliché and I'll use it because it's true. You look too far ahead in this league, you get punched in the mouth. Tampa's got talent, they're home, they've got momentum too. I'm not thinking about Philadelphia. I'm thinking about Thursday.

Jan Kowalczyk

Dave Lewis, thank you. Back to you.

Dave Lewis

Thanks, Jan.
