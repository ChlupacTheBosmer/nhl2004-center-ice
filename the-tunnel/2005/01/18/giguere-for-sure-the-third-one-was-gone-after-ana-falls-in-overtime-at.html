Giguere: 'For Sure, the Third One Was Gone' After ANA Falls in Overtime at Ottawa

The Ducks' goaltender says Ottawa's speed in transition caught his group in the third period and overtime, and he is ready to stop talking about his shutout drought in 40 games.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelTuesday, 18 January 2005

2:50 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Jean-Sebastien Giguere93 watched the Ottawa Senators Senators take two points in overtime, 3-2, in front of a crowd that had come to watch a 15-22 team try not to lose. His own side fired 18 shots and left Ottawa with none. The shutout search, now at 40 games this season, goes on.

Yeah. Well. They, uh, they're fast, you know? Everybody says Ottawa is bad and okay, the record is bad.
