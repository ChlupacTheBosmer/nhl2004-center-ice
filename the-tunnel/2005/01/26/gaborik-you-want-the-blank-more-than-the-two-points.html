Gaborik: 'You want the blank more than the two points'

Marian Gaborik on protecting Roberto Luongo's shutout in the third and what a speed player does when the lead is on the line.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelWednesday, 26 January 2005

2:01 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Marian Gaborik98 had one assist as Toronto Maple Leafs beat Colorado Avalanche 3-0 on Tuesday night. Roberto Luongo94 stopped all 25 Colorado shots for the shutout, and the winger told The Tunnel's Marcus Bell what changes in the last twenty when your goaltender is standing on his head.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here, rinkside after the game. Three-nothing Toronto, Roberto Luongo with the blank, twenty-five saves on twenty-five shots. Marian, in the third period Colorado kept pressing, kept throwing pucks. How do you manage that as a forward when the lead is on the line?

Marian Gaborik

You know, when it is 2-nothing and the third is coming, you just... you stay simple. Get it deep, make them come to you, do not press. Gary is in the corners, Mats is winning the battles, so you just, you keep it out of our zone. That is the whole thing. You do not want to give them the rush, the open ice.

Marcus Bell

And you can tell from out there. When Roberto's having one of those nights. Does the shutout change what you do in the last twenty minutes?

Marian Gaborik

Yeah, yeah, you can feel it. The rebounds are not there. He freezes the second one, the third one, and you know he is there tonight. So you, you do not want to be the guy who lets one through. You get in the lane more, you block a little more. You know how you say... you want the blank more than the two points sometimes. You really do.

Marcus Bell

Colorado put twenty-five on the board. Did tonight feel like you took something away from them, or was it your game working?

Marian Gaborik

I think, the neutral zone. We are tight there, I think. They cannot get the speed going, so the shots are, uh, from the outside. They are desperation pucks maybe. And Roberto sees everything clean. So the 25, it is not, I do not know, 25 great chances. Some of them, the body is in the way. You block and you clear.

Marcus Bell

You told me in December that the short shifts keep your speed at full. Does protecting a lead tie you up? You're a speed guy.

Marian Gaborik

I think, you know, when it is 2-nothing you want to run. You want to take the ice and go. But if you press, if you force it, they take it and then it is 2-1 and the building, it goes quiet. So you stay home. You wait. It is hard for a speed guy to wait. But you do it. So maybe, 3-nothing, then I run. That is better.

Marcus Bell

Thirty-nine goals this year. You had the one assist tonight, no goals. After a three-nothing, does a quiet line sit different than it would after a loss?

Marian Gaborik

Tonight, no. Roberto has the blank, so, next game maybe I score two. Tonight is his night. That is fine. You do not worry about the point when the 3-nothing is on the board.

Marcus Bell

Marian, thank you for your time. Back to you.
