Marian Gaborik on the 7-2 win: a quiet night, an 83-point pace, and the Cup on his mind

Gaborik had no points in Toronto's 7-2 win at Atlanta and said it does not bother him, with the Maple Leafs at 83 points and the third period the part of the game he trusts most.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelMonday, 31 January 2005

2:07 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Marian Gaborik98 finished Toronto's 7-2 win at Atlanta Thrashers on 2005-01-30 with no points, and the idea did not trouble him. The win put the Toronto Maple Leafs at 83 points, 37-9-3 through 52 games, the best record in the league. Gaborik, at 40 goals and 72 points this season, talked about the third period that broke the game open and what a run like this feels like from inside.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk, The Tunnel, with Marian Gaborik in Atlanta. Seven-two on the road, 36 shots. Marian, where did that game open up for you?

Marian Gaborik

Yeah, you know... I think we just keep skating. All game, we skate. Chistov score two, Chlup score two, Mogilny, so when the puck go in for everybody... is easy, you know. Is fun.

Jan Kowalczyk

You've had nights where the shots don't go in. I asked you about one of them after Boston. Tonight they went in. What's different in the third period of a game like this?

Marian Gaborik

Yeah, I remember. And I don't think we play so different. Third period, we don't sit back. We keep forecheck, we keep the puck. When you sit back, that's when they come, and the game get hard. Tonight we don't do that.

Jan Kowalczyk

You're at 83 points now. Best record in the league, 37-9-3 through 52 games. From inside the room, how does that feel?

Marian Gaborik

We don't look at 83, you know. I know it is the number. But every game is the same. You win, you go home, you play again. You lose two, and everybody ask what is wrong with Toronto. We don't count the record. We just play.

Jan Kowalczyk

No points for you tonight in a 7-2 win. Does that bother you at all?

Marian Gaborik

Seven-two, zero points, I take that every night. You know, I have forty goals, that's nice. But no, if we win and I don't score, is better. Forty goals with no Cup is nothing. I want the Cup.

Jan Kowalczyk

The room after a win like this one, on the road, 36 shots. What's it like in there?

Marian Gaborik

Is good. Everybody smiling, you know. On the road, seven goals... but tomorrow you forget it. Next game is zero-zero again. That's how you have to think, or you lose.

Jan Kowalczyk

Coaches say you win games in the third period. Is it legs, or is it something you decide between the second and the third?

Marian Gaborik

Legs, yes. But also the head, how you say. When we are up, we don't change. Some team go back, they protect the lead. We keep coming. That is our game. That is why the third period is good for us.

Jan Kowalczyk

You've got the whole league chasing you. Does that change anything when you come to the rink?

Marian Gaborik

No, we like that, you know. When you are on top, everybody want to beat you. Every night is a big game. It is good. It is what you want.

Jan Kowalczyk

Marian, thanks for your time. Jan Kowalczyk, The Tunnel, in Atlanta.
