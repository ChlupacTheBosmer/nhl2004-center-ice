Forsberg: 'We get 3 out of 22 and they get 4 out of 18'

Two goals and a helper from the Avalanche captain were not enough at home, and he is honest about what went wrong: the back end, the screens, the tight ones that used to land his way.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelWednesday, 12 January 2005

2:16 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Peter Forsberg89 had 2 goals and 3 points for Colorado Avalanche on Sunday, but the Avalanche lost 4-3 at home to Mighty Ducks of Anaheim despite firing 22 shots to their 18. Forsberg sits at 82 points on the season, and Colorado now sits at 23-13-3 after starting the year 23-9 through the end of December.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here, Peter, in the tunnel at the Pepsi Center. You put up 2 goals, 3 points, the team put 22 shots on their net, they put 18 on yours, and you lose 4-3. How do those two goals sit tonight?

Peter Forsberg

They sit on the scoresheet, for sure. I mean, I scored, and of course that is... it is not nothing. But 4 goals against. At home. When you score 3 and lose, the 2 you put up don't feel like anything. Not tonight.

Jan Kowalczyk

What did the coach change tonight? Did the lines look different, a pairing, anything from the way you have been running lately?

Peter Forsberg

Not really. No. We are... the same lines. Joe, Milan, me. And the other units are the same. Maybe he wanted us to get more pucks to the net and we did, I think. 22 shots is not a low number. But we get 3 out of 22 and they get 4 out of 18. That is... of course, that is the game tonight.

Jan Kowalczyk

Sauve was in the net. Was that his turn or was it a change?

Peter Forsberg

His turn, for sure. He played... he made some good saves, of course. And the 4 goals, some of those he had no chance on. The screens were there. The puck comes off a stick and it is gone before he moves. We did not help him enough, the defense and me, the forwards, we did not clear the middle. That is on us, not on him.

Jan Kowalczyk

Four goals on 18. What did the special teams look like from your end, because the conversion on their side was... it was a lot.

Peter Forsberg

I think... we had bodies where they should be and the puck still gets through. And on our side, we get maybe... I do not know how many. A couple. And we do not bury them. You need to be, I think, you need to be sharper. You cannot give a team those looks at home and... no. You cannot.

Jan Kowalczyk

You told me after the Philadelphia game, three weeks ago, that you were 23-9. You are 23-13-3 now. What has happened?

Peter Forsberg

The legs, maybe. I think. We have played 42 games. And the games in November, December, the tight ones, the 2-1, the 3-2, the bounces... they go the other way now. We are not playing bad hockey. I do not think we are. But we are not winning those games anymore. And that is... that is the difference between 23-9 and what we have now.

Jan Kowalczyk

You told me you do not watch the points. You have 82, 36 goals. You have to see it by now.

Peter Forsberg

I see it, I see it. But I meant what I said. The goals... they come when the plays work. When Joe and Milan are there, the ice opens and I get the look. That is... of course, it is nice. But tonight we win the game and I do not need the 2. I would give both back for the 2 points.

Jan Kowalczyk

Peter Forsberg, thank you.

Peter Forsberg

Thank you, Jan.
