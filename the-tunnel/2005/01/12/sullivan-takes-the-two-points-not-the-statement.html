Sullivan takes the two points, not the statement

After Boston beat Toronto 3-2 on 21 shots, the Bruins' coach made the call on the goaltending, owned the 8-13 home record, and told the room not to build anything on one night.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelWednesday, 12 January 2005

3:03 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Mike Sullivan's Boston Bruins took 3-2 from Toronto Maple Leafs, the best record in the league at 71 points, on a night Boston managed 21 shots to Toronto's 32. Sullivan sat Steve Shields, went back to Andrew Raycroft after Raycroft had been healthy scratch, and afterward talked about what two wins fix, which he says is nothing. The Bruins are 16-19-3, 43 points, 8-13 at home.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here, on the boards at the Garden with Mike Sullivan after Boston takes it 3-2 over Toronto. Coach, you get outshot 32 to 21 and you win by one. What's your read?

Mike Sullivan

Well, when you're down in the shot column and you find a way, that's usually because the compete level was there and you managed the puck. We didn't give them a lot of second chances. Some, yeah, and they made you work. But the details were good. Our first gap, our first read, that's what keeps a game like this one out of overtime.

Jan Kowalczyk

You went back to Andrew Raycroft. Steve Shields was your starter and Chris Mason wasn't even dressed. What drove the switch?

Mike Sullivan

It's my call. Look, Stevie's done a nice job for us. Three and five in thirteen starts, that's a little thin, but he's not... he's not the reason we lost games. Andy's earned the look. He's been in here every day, sharp, square, and sometimes the crease needs a fresh set of eyes. You don't bench a guy because you're mad at him. You go with who's given you the best chance that night.

Jan Kowalczyk

Raycroft himself is 16 and 16. Thirty-three starts, even split. Is this really a hot hand?

Mike Sullivan

Well, yeah, Jan, I can see the standings. I'm not up here in a tent. But sixteen and sixteen in thirty-three starts, the guy's played well. The win-loss column is a blunt instrument. Thirty-three games he's been tested, and he's been up to it. I needed a guy who was ready tonight and Andy was ready. End of it.

Jan Kowalczyk

Two straight wins, one of them over the best team in the league. What's the room like right now?

Mike Sullivan

It's good, obviously. You take Toronto at home, two points, guys are happy, they've earned being happy. But I've been in enough of these to know the feeling fades by tomorrow morning. You watch the video, you see the first-period turnovers, the stuff that got you into trouble, and you get back at it. I'm not going to let the room build a house on one night.

Jan Kowalczyk

You're 8 and 13 in this building, 12 and 9 on the road. For a Boston club that's the wrong way around. What's happening here at home?

Mike Sullivan

Look, we'd all like it flipped. Eight and thirteen is not good enough. I don't have one clean answer. Maybe we press. Maybe the crowd, the building, there's a different expectation and the guys try to do too much instead of just playing their game. On the road, nobody expects anything, you just go out and compete and it works. I don't know yet, but we've got to own our building. And that's on us, the coaching staff, to find the setting that makes that happen.

Jan Kowalczyk

I'll be honest with you, coach. 16 and 19, 43 points, still under .500. You beat the league's best record, you win the next one in overtime against Buffalo. Two wins. What does that actually fix?

Mike Sullivan

Nothing. And that's the answer. Two wins in January don't fix anything. You look at our last ten, we're outscored seven goals, and that's not a team that's figured it out. But tonight you take the two, you get some confidence, and you go to work. I'm not going to stand here and tell you beating Toronto doesn't mean something. It does. It's just not everything, and I won't let anyone in that room pretend it is.

Jan Kowalczyk

Toronto's back here on the 14th, you've got Washington on the 17th, Montreal on the 18th. Are you looking ahead at all?

Mike Sullivan

No. You know, that sounds like a line out of a coach's presser, but I mean it. I look at tomorrow's practice, I look at who's played the heavy minutes tonight, where our first gap was bad in the second period, and that's my week. The 14th is Monday's problem, and right now Monday is about getting our legs back.

Jan Kowalczyk

Mike Sullivan, thank you, coach.
