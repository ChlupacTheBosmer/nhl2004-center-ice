Gaborik: 'Nobody Talk About My 37 In That Room'

The league's leading scorer found the net again, and the road, and the 32-shot number, still stung.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelWednesday, 12 January 2005

2:40 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Marian Gaborik98 scored for the 31st time this season in Toronto Maple Leafs's 3-2 loss at Boston Bruins on Tuesday night, a game Toronto outshot 32-21. Gaborik entered with 37 goals in 43 games and sits up six on the Bureau's Development Index. Toronto drops to 14-5 on the road, their fifth loss away from home.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here in Boston with Marian Gaborik. Marian, Boston came in eight and thirteen at their own building and they take it from you. What is the story in there tonight?

Marian Gaborik

Yeah. They play good game tonight, you know? They do not give us the rush, the open ice. They drop, they stay in front, and when we shoot, their guys block. And the building, yeah, when it is tight like that, they push and push and the home team, they play over their head. I feel it from the bench.

Jan Kowalczyk

Thirty-two to twenty-one on the shots. Gary Roberts after the Pittsburgh game said he was happy winning on 13 shots. Tonight you get 32 and you leave here with nothing.

Marian Gaborik

Yeah. That is the problem tonight, exactly. We have the puck, we have the chances, and the three they take, three go in. So what do you do with the 32? It means nothing. I know Gary said the 13, that is fine, but tonight is the other side of that. The number do not help you.

Jan Kowalczyk

You scored tonight. That was your 37th. How did that one work?

Marian Gaborik

Yeah, so, I get the puck, the D step up a little and there is the gap, I shoot, it goes in. You know, that is it. You do not think too much, the net is there and I put it there. But the goal, you know, it is one, the game is three, two. Nobody in that room talk about it.

Jan Kowalczyk

Boston's sixteen and nineteen. Not a good team by any measure. Did you see anything about how they set up tonight that was different?

Marian Gaborik

They are scared, how you say. The record is bad, they know, we know. But when a team come in scared, they play tight, they do not make the mistake, and the building help them. On the road you see this. A team that should be, what, 20 points from fourth place, tonight they play like they are in a playoff game. Every puck deep, every shot blocked. And we match them? No. We try to make the play, the nice play, and the night is, the night do not go.

Jan Kowalczyk

You told me in December, the first time we spoke, the grades do not matter without the Cup. The Development Index has you up six, 37 goals in 43 games. Tonight the speed is there, the goal is there, and it means nothing. How do you hold that?

Marian Gaborik

I do not hold anything. You write the number, that is your job. I scored tonight, fine, good. But we lose. In the room tonight nobody say 'Gaborik, good 37 goals.' Nobody. They say we cannot score on 32 and we cannot stop them on 21. That is it. That is the only thing I want to talk about.

Jan Kowalczyk

14 and 5 on the road. Boston is a building the table says you win. Does that one stink worse?

Marian Gaborik

Yeah, it stink. Because the game was there. We have 32 shots, that is the game right there. And we give them the second one, the third one, and we cannot answer. That is on us. Not on the ice, not on the crowd, not on them being scared. We had it and we do not close it.

Jan Kowalczyk

What does the room do now? Bus ride or tomorrow?

Marian Gaborik

Tomorrow. Bus you sleep, you forget. Tomorrow we watch what we did wrong and we fix it. That is all. Thank you, Jan.

Jan Kowalczyk

Marian Gaborik, thank you. Back to you.
