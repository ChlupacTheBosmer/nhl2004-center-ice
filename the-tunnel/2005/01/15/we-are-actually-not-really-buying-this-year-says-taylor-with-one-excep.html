"We are actually not really buying this year," says Taylor, with one exception

The Maple Leafs GM told The Tunnel that Toronto is content at the deadline but may look for toughness, and confirmed Ed Belfour is the number one goalie.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelSaturday, 15 January 2005

7:45 · the reporter's voice is generated; the general manager's answers are his own, recorded live and unedited · download

ShareCopy link

Toronto Maple Leafs general manager Ed Taylor told The Tunnel on Saturday that the club is not planning to be a buyer at the trade deadline, though he left the door open for a trade that brings in toughness. The Maple Leafs sit at 33 and 8, plus 131 on the season, with no players on the injured list. Taylor put the club's available cap room at roughly $20 million and said close to 17 contracts expire this summer, which sets up a long negotiation period whether or not anyone is moved before March.
