Wilson on three more on the road, and why the ice isn't different

San Jose lost its third straight at Chicago and heads to Dallas, Washington and Buffalo this week with no injuries and a 10-18 road record the coach refuses to dress up.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelTuesday, 25 January 2005

5:13 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

San Jose Sharks fell 1-2 at Chicago on 2005-01-23, their third straight loss. The Sharks are 16-23-7 and 10-18 on the road, and this week they play at Dallas Stars (home record: 18-3), at Washington Capitals, and at Buffalo Sabres. Ron Wilson spoke to The Tunnel after the game about the road, his young players, and the goaltending plan he promised us in January.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here at the United Center with Ron Wilson. Coach, 1-2 tonight, you lose your third in a row. What'd you see on the ice?

Ron Wilson

You know, we were, uh, the first period was even. We managed the puck OK. And then we, the second period we had a couple of, you know, soft goals. Two, two goals where, if you're square, you're not, you're not getting beat there. That's the game right there. You don't get those back on the road. You get 'em at home where the building lifts you. On the road it's, it's, the other team's building and you're chasing and it gets, it gets heavy.

Marcus Bell

You're 10-18 on the road. Is it the travel? Is it the preparation? What separates the 22-4 at home?

Ron Wilson

You know, every coach in this league gets that question. What's different on the road? And the honest answer is, nothing should be. You're wearing the same sweater. You're playing the same game. I don't think it's the ice, I don't think it's the travel. I think, look, some nights, when you're at home, and you're playing ahead, and the crowd's pushing, the guys get a step. They get a half-second on a first step and that's the difference in this league. On the road nobody gives you that. You've got to manufacture it yourself. And some of our guys, they've got to, I mean, they've got to be able to win that half-second without the building helping them.

Marcus Bell

Coach, you've got three more road games this week. Dallas tomorrow night, they're 18-3 at home. Washington, Buffalo after that. Is there anything you change in the preparation, or is it, you play whoever's in front of you?

Ron Wilson

You play the man in front of you. Obviously. I mean, Dallas is, they're good at home. They've got, what, eighteen and three? That's a statement. But you know what, we've got to go play. We don't get to choose. I've been in this league too long to, to start telling my guys, well, this one doesn't count, this one's too tough. You go, you compete, and you worry about the record later. What I'm watching for tonight is, the compete level was there. I don't think we were outworked. I think we were outplayed on two, two, two, you know, two bounces. And that's, that's a hockey game. Sometimes the bounce goes your way at home because, well, because you're at home and the puck likes you.

Marcus Bell

You've got seven guys on the roster who are 24 or younger. Roger Nylander, he's 20, he's played six games, scored twice. Are you going to lean on any of those young guys on this trip, or do you go with the same group?

Ron Wilson

Well, Roger, yeah, he's, he's done a nice job. Six games, two goals. That's, that's not nothing. He's got, he's got a good release, he's not, he's not afraid to shoot, and at twenty that's, that's something. You know, some twenty-year-olds, they're, they're looking for the pass, they're trying to be pretty. He's not. He shoots. And that's why he's in there. Whether he plays tomorrow or not, I don't, I don't want to, you know, I want him to earn it and keep earning it. I'm not going to throw a kid out there because, because we need, because we need bodies. That's not fair to him.

Marcus Bell

There's a question I've got from the January 4th conversation. You told Jan you'd talk through the goaltending plan once the road trip was done. The Nashville, St. Louis, LA trip is behind you. Evgeni Nabokov is at 42 starts, 84 percent of your games. Vesa Toskala's got 11. Do you want to give it to me now?

Ron Wilson

You know, I forgot you were going to hold me to that. No, fair enough. Look, Evgeni, he's our guy. Forty-two starts. He's, he's been, he's been up to it, most nights. The record doesn't show it, 17 and 22, that doesn't, that doesn't tell you the whole story. He's had some, some bad nights where the guys in front of him didn't, didn't give him the help he needed. But, but the workload, I'm not, I'm not worried about it. He's 29. He's in his prime. He can handle it. Toskala behind him, he's, he's ready. Eleven games, he's, he's been fine. He pushes Evgeni in practice and that, that's, that's what we need.

Marcus Bell

What about the young goalies? Nikolay Prokop, he's 19, Craig Chouinard, he's 20. Neither one's played a game this year. Are they part of the plan or are they just depth on the roster?

Ron Wilson

They're depth right now. Obviously. You don't, you don't put a nineteen-year-old in a 1-2 game in this league, not with, not with the season we've got. They're, they're watching. They're learning. And if, you know, God forbid something happens to Evgeni or Vesa, they're, they're in the call-up. But right now they're just, they're watching and they're getting stronger. And that's, that's enough for now.

Marcus Bell

Coach, Mike Rathje. He's 30, he's rated 77, and he's got zero games played. Ladislav Benysek too, 29, zero games. What's going on with those two? Do you just not trust them, is it the way the D corps has shaken out?

Ron Wilson

It's my call. That's the answer. Mike's, Mike's been here every day. He's, he's a pro. But you've got, Brad Stuart, Eric Brewer, Boris Mironov, Kyle McLaren, Jyrki Lumme, Rob Davison. That's, that's your six. And they've earned those spots. I'm not, I'm not sitting here going, well, maybe if I, you know, maybe if I shake it up... they're earning it. When they're not, they're not in. That's how I see it. And, and that's, that's the whole conversation. I'm not going to sit on TV and say a guy's not good enough. He's here, he's on the roster, he's a member of this team. Whether he's in the lineup or not is between me and him.

Marcus Bell

Last one, Coach. Wayne Primeau, three points in fifty games. Stephane Matteau's 35, zero points in sixteen. Is the bottom six productive enough for you, or is that something you're hoping to address at the deadline?

Ron Wilson

You know what, I'm, I'm happy with the bottom six. I am. Wayne Primeau is, he's in the corners, he's, he's killing penalties, he's not, he's not a scoring guy and he's never going to be a scoring guy and that's, that's what he's here to do. And Stephane, he's 35, yeah, he's, he's in the penalty kill, he's, he's giving us, you know, six, seven minutes a night, and he's doing it right. You want your fourth line to, to not give up a goal and, and give the top guys a short bench. That's the job. You don't, you don't ask them to score twenty-five. You ask them to do their job and they're, they're doing it.

Marcus Bell

Ron Wilson, thank you as always. Back to you.
