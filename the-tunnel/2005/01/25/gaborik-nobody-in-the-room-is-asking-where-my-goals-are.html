Gaborik: 'Nobody in the room is asking where my goals are'

With Svehla, Klesla, and Chlup carrying the scoring in a 3-1 win at Buffalo, Gaborik said the room does not ask where his points are when the board says two.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelTuesday, 25 January 2005

1:55 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Marian Gaborik97 had nothing on the scoresheet for Toronto Maple Leafs in their 3-1 road win at Buffalo on January 24. Robert Svehla82, Rostislav Klesla, and Petr Chlup carried the offense while David Aebischer86 started in net on a night the forwards went quiet. Gaborik said the room does not track individual production when the points go up.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here, rinkside, with Marian Gaborik. Three and one on the road, but thirty shots against you tonight, twenty-one for your side. Talk me through that third period. How do you hold a game when they're outshooting you like that?

Marian Gaborik

So the third, we know they push, you know. They're home, they shoot thirty times, they think they can get back in it. And we just put pucks deep. Make them shoot from outside. And the guys block, David is square, everything come to him. And the clock, the clock is your friend in a building like this one. That is the whole thing.

Jan Kowalczyk

Svehla scored, Klesla scored, Chlup scored. None of your forwards on the board tonight. What's it feel like, watching the blue line carry the offense?

Marian Gaborik

Yeah, Robert, Rostislav, Petr. Good for them. I don't care who put it in, you know. We get the two points, that is what matter. Nobody in the room look at the sheet and say, why no Gaborik tonight. That is not how it work.

Jan Kowalczyk

I asked you here after the Boston game, about how the room resets when the shots don't turn into goals. You said nobody in that room talks about your total after a loss. Tonight you don't have a point either, but the mood's different. What changes?

Marian Gaborik

Yeah, you remember. That was the Boston night. And I meant it, nobody talk about goals in there after a loss. Tonight, zero also. But everybody is, everybody is in a good mood. Because we win, because the board says two points. So the reset is easier. Nobody ask, where were your points tonight. They say good game, let's go. That is the difference, you know.

Jan Kowalczyk

David Aebischer in the crease tonight. Thirty shots against, one in. What does that say about the room when the man behind the starter steps up like that?

Marian Gaborik

David is ready. He is ready every night in here. When David play, we just play, because the net is covered. He made the saves he needed. That is all you ask from a goalie.

Jan Kowalczyk

Seventy-nine points, forty-nine games played. You're not looking at April yet?

Marian Gaborik

No, no. Forty-nine games, the legs are heavy. Everybody feel it now. But seven-nine, you just keep going. We don't look at the next twenty. Just the next game. Buffalo is done, now we go to the next one.

Jan Kowalczyk

Marian, thank you. Jan Kowalczyk, back to you.
