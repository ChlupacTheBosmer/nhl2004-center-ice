Fedorov on 22 Assists: 'Why Pass When Net Is Open?'

The Mighty Ducks® of Anaheim center told The Tunnel why his assists collapsed, why 82 games last season sit higher on his shelf than 76 goals, and why Alexander Mogilny is still the best he shared a line with.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelTuesday, 25 January 2005

3:03 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Sergei Fedorov88, 35, has 52 goals and 22 assists through 48 games for Mighty Ducks of Anaheim, a pace that would give him 89 goals in 82 but only 38 assists. His ice time is 23.4 minutes a night, up from 21.1 last season when he played 82 games and won the Lester B. Pearson Award. Marcus Bell sat with him in the studio.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here in the studio with Sergei Fedorov, the Mighty Ducks® of Anaheim center. Sergei, you are the man behind a profile this morning titled '52 Goals, 21 Assists: Fedorov Stopped Passing at 35.' Last year, 55 assists. This year, 22. What changed?

Sergei Fedorov

Nothing changed. I am in different position. Before, I start at my own blue line, I carry, I look for a boy. Now I start close. I am where I can shoot. Why pass when net is open?

Marcus Bell

Is that the coach putting you closer? Or is that how your legs tell you to play at 35?

Sergei Fedorov

It is both. At 25 you take the puck at your blue line, you carry through two men, you make a play. At 35, one man is on your hip and you shoot. Of course. My legs are... they are good, but they are not Detroit legs. I do not carry the same. So I am there, close, and when the look comes, I take it.

Marcus Bell

Your ice time is up though. 23.4 a night, from 21.1 last season. More ice, fewer passes.

Sergei Fedorov

Yes. More ice. And I want that. You write, 'stopped passing,' like it is a problem. I have 52 goals in 48 games. My team needs goals. I take 23 minutes because I want them. I do not feel sorry about that.

Marcus Bell

What on the record, this season or last, makes you proudest? Something that is not a stat line.

Sergei Fedorov

The 82 games. Last year. 82.

Marcus Bell

Not the 76 goals? Not the 131 points?

Sergei Fedorov

76 goals, that is nice. I am not a fool. But 82 games at 34 years old, after everything my body has been through... Nobody writes that. You open the paper, they write 131. They do not write, 'Fedorov, he played every single game.' Only I know. My body knows. Not one game missed. That is the number I care about.

Marcus Bell

You won the Pearson last season. The players' MVP, voted by the other locker rooms. What does that one mean?

Sergei Fedorov

The boys vote. That is... Every other trophy, a writer picks it, a committee picks it. The Pearson, your boys in every room, they put my name on a paper. 82 games, 131 points, they see it, they vote. That is... yes. That is the one I look at on the shelf.

Marcus Bell

Best player you have played with. Anybody, anywhere in your career.

Sergei Fedorov

Mogilny.

Marcus Bell

Alexander Mogilny. He came to North America before you did.

Sergei Fedorov

He come first. 1989. I come 1990 and I am 20 years old, I know nothing, the speed is different, the boards are different, everything is different. And Mogilny is already there. And he does not talk. Three words maybe, one whole day. But you skate with him and you are faster. You cannot be slow next to him. He make you better and he says nothing.

Marcus Bell

You won Cups together in Detroit, 1997 and 1998. What did he give the line on the ice?

Sergei Fedorov

The middle. When he takes the puck on the left wing, the whole other team has to watch him. The defense, they try to cover, they cannot. So you have the middle. You get the puck in the middle and it is open ice. He gives you the middle. That is what he does.

Marcus Bell

Sergei, thank you for your time tonight. Marcus Bell in the studio, back to you.

Sergei Fedorov

Thank you, Marcus. Good talk.
