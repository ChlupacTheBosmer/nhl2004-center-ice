Hartley calls the next three at home his window

After a 3-1 loss to Boston dropped them back under .250, Bob Hartley laid out what he needs from the room over the next week.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelWednesday, 19 January 2005

3:28 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Bob Hartley's Atlanta Thrashers sit 14 and 25 and 2, last in the Southeast at 38 points, after a 3-1 home loss to Boston Bruins on 2005-01-17. They won five straight in late December and early January, then lost three of four. Three home games follow: New York Islanders on 2005-01-19, San Jose on 2005-01-21, Ottawa on 2005-01-23.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here, outside the dressing room in Atlanta. Bob Hartley, the 3-1 loss to Boston, that's your fourth loss in five. What's the room like right now?

Bob Hartley

Quiet. Look, we won five, we feel good, we get back in the habit of winning, and then we lose three of four and we're back to this. The room knows what it is. They're not going to sit in there and pretend they didn't play down to the occasion tonight. Boston's a good team, we knew that, but 24 shots at home against a team like that, we have to be better.

Marcus Bell

That five-game run, December into January. You thought that was the turn, or was it just a patch?

Bob Hartley

I thought we were building something. You get five in a row with those two up front, with Ilya and Dany putting up what they're putting up, you start thinking the rest of the guys find the next gear. And then the rest of the guys don't. We score, what, three goals in the last four games as a group outside of the two? You can't win in this league with that. Obviously you can't.

Marcus Bell

You got 24 shots tonight, Boston had 29. You're not generating enough offensively to keep them honest?

Bob Hartley

It's not just the shots, Marcus. It's where. We're shooting from the outside, we're not getting bodies to the net. Boston's D, they drop back, they stay home, and if we're not going to push through the middle, what are we doing? We're giving them a Tuesday night. The compete level in the first period, it wasn't where it needed to be, and the whole game went that way.

Marcus Bell

Byron Dafoe's in there for all 60, 28 saves on 29 shots. Did you think about going to the other guy at any point?

Bob Hartley

No. Byron's been our man all year, 42 games. The goals against tonight, the second one especially, there's a screen, the angle gets taken. You lose one 3-1 when your goalie makes 28 saves on 29, the problem isn't the crease. It's the 24 shots in front of him.

Marcus Bell

Three home games coming up. Islanders, Sharks, Senators. Your home record is 10 and 12. What are you telling the guys tonight about this stretch?

Bob Hartley

I'm telling them this is the window. Ottawa's 15 and 22, they're in the same fight we're in. San Jose's below .500. The Islanders, they're a team we can push around if we're honest with ourselves. Three at home. We've been 10 and 12 here, that's not good enough, we know that. You look at the calendar, this is the week we either pass Florida or we don't. I'm not going to sugarcoat it.

Marcus Bell

You're 10 and 12 at home, and you're 8 and 15 on the road. What's different?

Bob Hartley

You'd think the home crowd's a gift. Here it's... they're with us, but they're restless. They've seen us lose enough that every turnover gets a groan, and the guys hear it. On the road, nobody expects much, you go out and compete, and we've actually had some better games. That's backwards. It's 10 and 12, that's what it says. We owe these fans more at home and we haven't given it.

Marcus Bell

Your top two, Kovalchuk and Heatley, they've got 157 points between them. Everyone else has... well, Savard's got 49, the rest are spread thin. How do you get the third line, the fourth line, producing?

Bob Hartley

You coach them. You put them in situations to score. I'm not going to stand in a hallway and tell you Patrik Stefan is going to become a 40-goal guy, or that Sami Kapanen at 31 is going to suddenly find 15 more. The third line gets a job. We ask them to manage the puck, to forecheck hard, to let the top two get the ice. That's the design. The problem is when we get to even strength and our third line is on the ice against their first, you get outmuscled and you chase, and you can't score when you're chasing.

Marcus Bell

No injuries on the IR for you tonight. The whole room's healthy. That's got to help going into this stretch.

Bob Hartley

It does. It does, and I'll give you that. Every other club's got bodies in the trainer's room, we don't. That's the one positive tonight, if you need one. We've got everyone. We've got four lines that can go. It's about putting it together for 60 minutes and not just 40, and not just on the nights where Ilya's got it going. That's the next three games. That's the whole conversation.

Marcus Bell

Bob Hartley, thank you. Back to you in the studio.
