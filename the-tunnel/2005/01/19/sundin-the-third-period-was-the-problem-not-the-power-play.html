Sundin: the Third Period Was the Problem, Not the Power Play

The captain on a quiet night, a quiet power play, and why the OT loss to Montreal lands differently than a regulation one.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelWednesday, 19 January 2005

2:02 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Mats Sundin89 had no points for the fourth straight game, and Toronto Maple Leafs lost 5-4 in overtime at Montreal Canadiens on 2005-01-18, dropping the 34-8-2 Maple Leafs to a one-game losing streak. The Brad Richards85 was out of the lineup and Sundin spoke after the game about the man who replaced him, the quiet power play, and the one fix he is working on now.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here in the tunnel in Montreal. Mats, 5-4 in overtime, outshot 30 to 28. Montreal's got forty points, you've got seventy-six. That's a game you feel you had.

Mats Sundin

Yeah. I think we had it. The first two periods, for sure, we are good. We move the puck, we are on top of it. Then the third period, they push and we... we get away from ourselves. Overtime is overtime. First team to score. Tonight it is not us.

Marcus Bell

The power play was quiet tonight. In a game this tight you need one goal from the man advantage and it didn't come.

Mats Sundin

The power play, we had looks. I think we did not shoot the puck enough. It is, you know, sometimes you want the perfect pass and you forget to just shoot. The puck is in the corner and it stays there and the clock runs. But I do not think the power play loses this game. Five-on-five, we had the chances. We just do not close.

Marcus Bell

Brad's not in the lineup tonight. Spacek moved up at center for some of your shifts. How did you manage without Brad on the ice?

Mats Sundin

Vaclav plays good. He wins the draws, he gets it deep, he is simple with the puck. For a young man, that is all you ask. You do not ask him to carry the game. He plays his game and he makes the right read. That is enough.

Marcus Bell

You told Jan in December you were fixing the first period. Has that clicked, or is tonight the same problem?

Mats Sundin

Tonight is not the first period. I think the first period is fine. We are clean, we are on top of the puck. The problem now is the other end. We get the lead, or we get the tie, and we... we try to hold. That is a different problem. December was the start. This is the end.

Marcus Bell

Two overtime losses in forty-four games. The record says thirty-four and eight. But an OT loss and a regulation loss, what's the difference to you?

Mats Sundin

In regulation you get outworked. You know they were better. In overtime... you had the game. You had it in your hands and you cannot close. That is harder to take, for sure. But you cannot build on it. You watch the video tomorrow, you fix what you saw, and you go again.

Marcus Bell

Zero points tonight. You've got twenty-eight goals and fifty-five points. How do you stay with it when the night goes like this?

Mats Sundin

You know, the puck does not come to me every night. That is... that is fine. I play eighteen minutes, I make the plays I can, and if the points come, of course, good. Tonight they did not come. But we win thirty-four games and lose eight. I can carry a zero-point night in a room like that.

Marcus Bell

Mats Sundin, thank you. Marcus Bell, rinkside in Montreal. Back to you.
