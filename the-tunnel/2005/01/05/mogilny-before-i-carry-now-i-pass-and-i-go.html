Mogilny: 'Before I carry. Now I pass and I go'

The 35-year-old's Development Index moved from minus 3 to minus 1 in two weeks; the minutes came down, the role changed shape, and tonight he scored none of seven in a blowout he watched from the ice.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelWednesday, 5 January 2005

2:00 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Alexander Mogilny88 finished with zero points in Toronto Maple Leafs's 7-0 win over New Jersey Devils on Tuesday night, his first quiet game since returning from a brief absence that ran between January 1 and January 4. The 35-year-old right winger is averaging 16.8 minutes a night this season, down from 23.8 last year, and 51 points in 36 games. The Development Index, which sat at minus 3 when Marcus Bell last spoke to him on December 22, now has him at minus 1.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Alexander, Marcus Bell here in the tunnel after a 7-nil night. New Jersey Devils® outshot you 19 to 16 and you scored seven. What happens in that third period?

Alexander Mogilny

When the game is four, five... they stop. They stop and the ice gets big. We are fast, you know that. Third period maybe they are tired, maybe they look at the clock. You play, you shoot, the puck goes in, you don't ask questions. Seven to zero, it is a good night to not ask questions.

Marcus Bell

You had none of those seven yourself. No points on the night. In a seven-goal game, what's that like on the ice?

Alexander Mogilny

Is a little strange. Yes. You are there, the boys are scoring, and the puck is not coming to you tonight. You move, you are open, they find somebody else. Nikita gets two. Vaclav gets three points. They are hungry. I am happy for them. But you know, I want the puck more than I show. That is the truth.

Marcus Bell

You're at 23.8 minutes a night last year, 16.8 this year. And the Index, when we talked on December 22, it had you at minus 3. Now it's at minus 1. What shifted?

Alexander Mogilny

OK. The Index. Last time is minus 3, now is minus 1. I take it. What shifted... Before, I carry. I drive through, I make the play with the puck on my stick, 23 minutes I own the shift. This year I play less and I play different. I open the ice, I make the first pass, and I go. I am not the man with the puck the whole time anymore. Maybe that is why the numbers say better, even though the minutes say less.

Marcus Bell

And the absence. You were out between the first and the fourth. You told me on December 22 the body was fine. Is it still fine?

Alexander Mogilny

I told you. Is nothing. You are the third man this week who asks. I am here tonight, seven to zero, I played, I was here. The answer is the same as the last time.

Marcus Bell

Fair enough. Toronto Maple Leafs® sits at 67 points, 30 and 6 and 1. Does this room look at that number and think about April?

Alexander Mogilny

You want me to say we look at the table. I will say: we do not. Tomorrow is practice. Then next game. That is how you get to April, one game. I am 35, I have seen the table mean nothing in February. We play the next one. That is the whole thing.

Marcus Bell

Thank you, Alexander. Marcus Bell, back to you.
