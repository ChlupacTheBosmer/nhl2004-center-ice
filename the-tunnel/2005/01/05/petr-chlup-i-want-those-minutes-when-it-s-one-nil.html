Petr Chlup: 'I want those minutes when it's one-nil'

The 18-year-old center scored in Toronto's 7-0 rout of New Jersey and said the blowout ice was welcome, but he is chasing the minutes that matter.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelWednesday, 5 January 2005

1:23 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Petr Chlup84 scored one of seven goals in Toronto Maple Leafs's 7-0 home win over New Jersey Devils on Tuesday. The 18-year-old center has 22 points in 29 games this season.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here on the boards with Petr Chlup. Petr, seven-nil, and you put one in. Take me to that goal.

Petr Chlup

Yeah, so, I'm, I'm on the back side and, uh, it just came to me. Clean look, I put it top shelf, and that's, that's all you ask for, right? One clean look.

Jan Kowalczyk

Sixteen shots tonight, seven on the board. From where you were, did it look like everything was finding a lane?

Petr Chlup

You know, it did. It's not easy, obviously, but when you're in the zone and you look up and Nikita's, he's already at the post before the defense knows where the play is going. Or Vaclav's on the back door and you don't even have to think, you just, you just put it where he's going. And then it's in. And the next guy's open. By the third period you're just looking for the open man and the puck just, you know, it goes.

Jan Kowalczyk

Third period, four-nil, five-nil. Protecting it or pushing?

Petr Chlup

Pushing. Obviously. You know the, the 'sixty minutes' thing? The poster on the wall? Yeah. Well, that's not a poster tonight. You stop, you give 'em energy, and I've seen what happens when a team gets a goal back when they're down big. The whole building changes. So no, we're not, we're not sitting on it.

Jan Kowalczyk

You looked more involved tonight. More carries, more time in the offensive zone. Did the coaching staff change something with your line?

Petr Chlup

I think so. I mean, I wasn't rolling with the same guys. And when the game's open like that, you, you get the puck earlier, you don't have to force it. I could carry, make the read. That's, that's my game. But I want those minutes when it's one-nil. That's, that's what I'm working toward. I don't want to be a blowout guy.

Jan Kowalczyk

You don't sound like one tonight, Petr. Good to see. Jan Kowalczyk, back to you.
