Sundin's 29th Stands Alone as Toronto Drops the Second

The captain scored the only goal in a 1-2 overtime loss to Philadelphia and said the problem was not the goaltending or the shot total but the read before the play.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelSaturday, 22 January 2005

1:42 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Mats Sundin89 scored Toronto Maple Leafs's lone goal in a 1-2 overtime loss to Philadelphia Flyers on Friday night, his 29th of the season. The Maple Leafs were outshot 21 to 32 and fell to 34-8-3 on a two-game skid, their first of the winter. Marcus Bell caught him in the corridor afterward.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here in the corridor after Toronto 1, Philadelphia 2, in overtime. Your 29th of the year, and the only one on the board tonight. What does a captain do with a night like that?

Mats Sundin

You are happy to put it away, for sure. That is never... you do not take that for granted. But we needed two. That is all I remember from the game. The two they had.

Marcus Bell

You were outshot 21 to 32. This club has been winning those games all year. What wasn't there tonight?

Mats Sundin

I do not think it is about the shots. We have been winning on 13, on 15, so 21 is not... the number is not the problem. It is the first read. Their forecheck comes and you already see the second man coming before the first one touches the puck. Tonight we were late. So you are always reacting, and when you react, you shoot the bad one.

Mats Sundin

And maybe, I think, they were a step faster tonight to the second puck. That is all.

Marcus Bell

David was in. 32 shots, two go in, one in overtime. How does the captain see it from the other end?

Mats Sundin

He had no chance on some of them. The second one, I think we have maybe one more body in the lane and he sees it. But we did not clear the middle enough. 32 shots is a lot for any goalie, and we did not help him. That is not a David question. That is us.

Marcus Bell

Back in December you told Jan Kowalczyk the first period was the thing you wanted to fix on video. Is it fixed now?

Mats Sundin

You remember that. Yes, I said the first period. Tonight the first period was fine, I think. The second period we lost the puck too easy in our own end. So now it is the second. It changes every time you watch the tape.

Marcus Bell

Two at home in a row. You're 22 and 4 there, and those two are the last two. Does it sting?

Mats Sundin

Of course it does. You lose, it stings. But we are still first, and the way we play I think is good. They were better tonight. I would rather have these two in January than in March.

Marcus Bell

Mats Sundin, thank you. Back to you.
