"Marian is where our future lies," but the 20 million waits for summer

Ed Taylor named Marian Gaborik his first extension priority, used the word dynasty for Ed Belfour and Gary Roberts, and said the roster still needs bodies if the Leafs want to survive a spring that hits back.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelSaturday, 22 January 2005

6:26 · the reporter's voice is generated; the general manager's answers are his own, recorded live and unedited · download

ShareCopy link

Ed Taylor, general manager of the Toronto Maple Leafs, told The Tunnel on Saturday that Marian Gaborik98 is the club's first extension priority, ahead of Brad Richards87, Ed Belfour93 and Gary Roberts88. Gaborik is 22, making $1.10M, and the Bureau's Book has him at 97 overall, the highest grade in the league. Taylor said the money for those conversations comes in the summer, after the playoffs, and that the club's 20 million of cap room competes directly with anything he might add at the deadline. He named physicality as the thing he is scanning for.
