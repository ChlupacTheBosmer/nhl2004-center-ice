Bertuzzi on the Shorthanded Back End: 'You Miss the Bodies, Plain and Simple'

The Canucks' right winger on a 6-3 home loss to Montreal, the defensemen who filled in, and why the room went quiet after.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelTuesday, 4 January 2005

1:57 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Todd Bertuzzi95 scored his 28th goal of the season in a 6-3 home loss to Montreal Canadiens on Sunday, a night that exposed the strain on Vancouver's depleted blue line. The Vancouver Canucks Canucks sit second in the Northwest with 51 points, but gave up six to a Canadiens club at 32. Ed Jovanovski and Mattias Ohlund were absent from the lineup.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here with Todd Bertuzzi after the 6-3 one to Montreal at home. 26 shots against, 6 in the net. What happened out there?

Todd Bertuzzi

They came out and hit, and we didn't match it. You give a team sitting 32 points two early goals at your own building, now they're playing on their heels and you're chasing. And when you chase, you take chances, and they score on the rush. That's 6 goals, Jan. That's the whole thing.

Jan Kowalczyk

You were short on the back end tonight. No Jovanovski, no Ohlund. Malik, Bergeron, the younger guys, they had to carry it. What's that look like from the bench?

Todd Bertuzzi

Marek's been playing great all year. He scored tonight, Marc got the assist. Those guys responded to it. But you're asking four guys to do what six normally do, and by the third period their legs are gone, and the screen's there, the body's there, and the puck doesn't get cleared. You lose the board battle and that's where it starts.

Jan Kowalczyk

What did Crawford change? Different matchup plan with those two out?

Todd Bertuzzi

Keep it simple. Get pucks deep, don't try to be fancy in your own end. And we tried, you know? We did try. But when you're down by two at home, you get, uh, you get anxious. You start trying to be the hero. And against a team that's desperate, that's exactly what they want.

Jan Kowalczyk

Your goal, early in it. Brendan Morrison finds you. How did that come?

Todd Bertuzzi

Brendan made the play. I was there, I put it in. Short version. Doesn't mean a whole lot when you give up 6. I'd trade the whole night for the two points.

Jan Kowalczyk

26 to 24 in shots. Two more looks, three more goals. Without your top pair, is it the zone time, the second chances?

Todd Bertuzzi

That's the thing. When your first and second D are both out there, they get it out, you change lines, you breathe. Tonight the puck stayed in our end, bodies in front, and Dan was making saves and then they got the rebound and you're, you're on your heels all night. Different game without those two guys. You can't fake it.

Jan Kowalczyk

What does the room look like right now, after that?

Todd Bertuzzi

Quiet. Angry. It should be. You lose, fine, it happens. You don't give up 6 at home. Tomorrow we watch tape, we get right for the next one. We've been good all year, one night doesn't change that. But tonight it stinks. Thanks, Jan.
