Wilson: 'You don't throw away cap room for a rental'

The Sharks coach said his young core is the move after a 6-4 win over Chicago broke four games without a regulation victory, and shrugged off the quiet front office.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelTuesday, 4 January 2005

3:10 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Ron Wilson watched San Jose Sharks beat Chicago Blackhawks 6-4 at home on Sunday, 24 shots to 21, to end a stretch of four games without a regulation win. The Sharks sit 15-17-4 through 39 games and Wilson told Jan Kowalczyk afterward that the young men in his room, seven of them 24 or under, are the reinforcements he wants.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here in the tunnel at San Jose with Sharks head coach Ron Wilson. Coach, six-four, twenty-four shots. That's the most goals you've scored in a month. What changed tonight?

Ron Wilson

The puck went in. That's the simple answer and it's the honest one. We've been, what, four games without a regulation win and in three of those we had the structure, we had the chances, we just didn't have the one extra bounce. Tonight we put one in early, got a lead, and their big defensemen had to turn around and chase. You get a good defenseman going backwards and the whole game opens up. So we scored. We needed that. I'm not going to pretend we fixed everything because we put six in one night.

Jan Kowalczyk

You've got seven players twenty-four or under in the room. Hossa at 23 has 16 goals. And Roger Nylander, he's 20, two goals in six games. Are those young guys in your plan tonight or in your plan for next year?

Ron Wilson

They're in my plan tonight. That's why they're playing. You watch Nylander, you watch the kid, and he's 20 years old and he doesn't look lost out there. He forechecks like he's got a habit of competing, not like he's trying to prove something to me or to you, he just plays that way. Two in six, that's encouraging. I'm not going to tell you he's the answer to anything yet. He's got a long way to go. But the habits are right and at twenty that's the thing you can't teach.

Jan Kowalczyk

And around them you've got Marleau at 25, 61 points. Sturm, Stuart, Brewer, all under 26. You built this around those guys and you didn't add a single piece this week while the rest of the league was out there signing players. That was the choice?

Ron Wilson

Well, hold on. Patrick and Marco aren't kids. They're not twenty. But yeah, we've got a core and I'll stand in front of you and say that and it'll sound like the same answer every coach gives. The difference is, I mean it. We've got a real core. The honest part is we've got depth issues. You know it, I know it. You look at our third and fourth lines and there's a step down. But you don't fix that by grabbing a thirty-four-year-old winger off the wire for forty games and spending cap room you'll need in July. You fix it by making Nylander better, by making Davison better, by finding two more goals from the Niko Dimitrakos of the world. That's the plan. And I don't make the phone calls upstairs, so don't ask me why we didn't sign anybody. I don't know. I coach the team I've got.

Jan Kowalczyk

Let's talk about the net then. Evgeni Nabokov has played 31 of 39 games. Seventy-nine percent of your starts. Does that number keep you up in January?

Ron Wilson

Every coach watches the mileage. I watch it every morning with my coffee. But you don't, you know, you don't start messing with a starting goalie in January because a number is getting high. Evgeni's our guy. Vesa Toskala gets his looks, he's got 11 starts. I'm not going to stand here and tell you I've solved February. Nobody has. But the plan is to be smarter, not to panic. And we just beat a team 6-4 with our goalie in net, so let's not go looking for a problem tonight.

Jan Kowalczyk

Tampa's here Tuesday. 21 and 11 and 3, five straight, best record in the Southeast division. What do you need to see?

Ron Wilson

I need to see sixty minutes. I know that sounds like the poster on the wall in the hallway, but that's the honest answer with Tampa. They've got speed, they've got skill, and Khabibulin's been sharp. You let up for one shift against them and they punish you. We just proved we can score on anybody when we get our chances, and we just showed what happens when you let a team hang around too long, Chicago put four on us. So. Tuesday's a good test. I'll take a good test over a soft one any night.

Jan Kowalczyk

Ron Wilson, thank you for your time tonight. Back to you.
