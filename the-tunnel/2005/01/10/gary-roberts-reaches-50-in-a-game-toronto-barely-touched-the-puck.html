Gary Roberts reaches 50 in a game Toronto barely touched the puck

The 38-year-old left wing picked up his 50th point of the season on a net-front battle in a 2-1 win where TOR managed 13 shots against 34.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelMonday, 10 January 2005

2:28 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Gary Roberts88 picked up an assist on one of Toronto Maple Leafs's two goals Sunday at Pittsburgh Penguins, bringing his season total to 50 points in 42 games. The win, a 2-1 road result in which Toronto was outshot 13 to 34, extended the streak to four and keeps Toronto Maple Leafs at 71 points atop the Northeast.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here in the tunnel at the Mellon with Gary Roberts. Gary, 13 shots for, 34 against. That's not how you drew it up this morning, is it?

Gary Roberts

No, you draw up something where you get the puck a little more than that. But two's two, you know? We had 13 shots and we put two in. That's... that's a good night. A lot of nights it doesn't work out that way.

Marcus Bell

Your assist on Chlup's goal. Walk me through that one, because it was about the only time we got set up in their end for more than a few seconds in the second period.

Gary Roberts

Yeah, so, Petr put it in deep to the corner, I went to get it, and there was a big defenseman already bearing down on me. I just... I just pinned it, held him off, turned him. And then I saw Petr come to the front and I popped it to him, top of the crease. One-timer. That's, that's the whole play. Win the puck, make a play, get out of the way. That's what they pay me for at this age.

Marcus Bell

You told Jan in December the role was different now, the minutes were different. But 50 points tonight, 18 goals. You're on pace for 43 of those. Does that number surprise you at 38?

Gary Roberts

No. I'll say no. I know what I can do. I just... you know, I spent the summer saying, I'm gonna be sharp when I'm on. I don't need the shift. I need, what, thirty seconds, forty seconds where I'm right. And the coaching staff's been good about that, putting me out there when it matters. The 50 is... it's nice. But the thing I'm happy about is the team's playing the way we need to. The 50's a side effect.

Marcus Bell

33 saves from Tellqvist tonight. When you've got a goalie like that, 17 minutes a night, what changes about how you play in front of him?

Gary Roberts

Well, you know, when your goalie's that sharp, you want to give him something. You don't want to be the guy in front who can't clear a rebound. So I'm just... I'm making it hard for the defense. I'm there, I'm screening, I'm making the defenseman look at me. And he makes the save, fine, but the D behind him has to help, and then you've got a second chance, a third. You give him a clean look, you're making his job easy. You don't do that to him.

Marcus Bell

Four straight. You're 32 and 6 with one loser. 71 points, nobody else in this league is even at 60. Do you feel it starting to feel different in there, the way the games are coming?

Gary Roberts

Marcus, I've been in this league since 1986. Every January feels like the one where you're gonna run away with it, and every March feels like the one where you wish you had. We're not running away with anything. We just won a game where we got outshot 34 to 13. That tells you everything about how we're doing it. We're doing it the hard way, and I like it. I like it because it means we're earning it.

Marcus Bell

Gary Roberts, 50 points tonight. Thanks for your time. Back to you.
