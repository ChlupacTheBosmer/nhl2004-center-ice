Gaborik: 'They shoot 40, we trust our net, that is the way'

The winger says the room leaned on Luongo through a second straight outshot game and never thought the result belonged to LA

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelSunday, 13 November 2005

1:58 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Marian Gaborik99 scored once and posted 2 points across Toronto Maple Leafs's two wins since last weekend, including Tuesday's 2-1 overtime decision over Los Angeles Kings at the Air Canada Centre. The Maple Leafs led the Northeast at 10-3-0 heading into the night and needed Roberto Luongo95 again to seal it, after being outshot 40-35 by a Los Angeles Kings club sitting at 3-7-4 on a three-game skid.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here in the corridor with Marian Gaborik. Marian, 2-1 in overtime, your second straight. LA came in at three wins and seven losses and they put forty shots on your net tonight. What did that feel like from inside?

Marian Gaborik

Yeah, they play good game. You know, forty shots, that is a lot. They have speed on their wing and they... they push, they shoot from everywhere. But we got the two. We win the game.

Marcus Bell

Sunday in San Jose they outshot you 28 to 19 and you won 5-2 anyway. Tonight 40 to 35. You haven't led in shots in either one. Does that bother the room?

Marian Gaborik

No, no. We know how we play. When the other team shoot and Luongo stop, you just... you trust it. The next shift you go and you make the chance. It is not a, how you say, a panic in there. We know one goal is enough.

Marcus Bell

Four months ago I asked you about an overtime loss to Vancouver and you told me you would not talk standings on a loss night. Tonight is the same game with the other ending. Does winning in OT feel different now than losing in OT felt in January?

Marian Gaborik

Very different. After Vancouver the room is quiet, nobody say nothing on the bus. Tonight is loud. OT is just... you play more hockey, that is all. The difference is who put the puck in last.

Marcus Bell

You missed the end of last spring with that ankle, back by June. You look like a man with nothing holding him back. How is the trust in it now?

Marian Gaborik

It is gone. I not think about it anymore, you know. First weeks, I was careful. Now I just go full speed. If I hit the wall I hit the wall. The ankle is fine. I feel good on the ice, every night I feel good.

Marcus Bell

Chistov has two goals in two games, Chlup three points in two. They are taking bigger minutes from you?

Marian Gaborik

They earn it. Chistov, he is fast and now the puck go in for him, so coach give him more. Chlup he work every day, he play two way, he is twenty-one but you not know. They are good for us. You cannot win with five guys, you know?

Marcus Bell

One goal, two points over these two games. You happy with where you sit tonight?

Marian Gaborik

I play good game. The goal comes when it come. We win two in a row, we got 26 points, that is... that is what matter. My number, nobody in that room ask me about it after a win.

Marcus Bell

Marian Gaborik, thank you again. Back to you.

Marian Gaborik

Thank you, Marcus.
