MacTavish defends the net, the top line, and the structure after Boston

The Oilers' coach says Tommy Salo stays in goal and Ethan Moreau stays on the top line, even at 2-8-2.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelSunday, 13 November 2005

3:45 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Edmonton picked up a 2-1 overtime win at Boston on Thursday, their second in 15 games. Edmonton Oilers head coach Craig MacTavish met the media in the corridor afterward with a record of 2-8-2 and a room that had just lost 5-1 at New York Islander and 4-2 at New Jersey before this one.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here at TD Garden after the game. Craig, 2-1 on the road in overtime, you're 2 and 8 and 2 on the season. What's this one worth to you tonight?

Craig MacTavish

It's two points. I'm not going to sit here and tell you the sun just came out. But the third period, the way we structured that thing... we managed the puck for stretches of it and we didn't give them a ton on the rush. You know? That's the way we have to play. We're not going to win 5-4 hockey games.

Jan Kowalczyk

Let me put this week in front of you. 5-1 at the Islander, 4-2 at New Jersey, tonight's win. What's the read on that stretch?

Craig MacTavish

The Islander game, that was poor. I won't dress it up, we gave up the first one quick and we sort of, I don't know, we drifted into the game instead of making them earn it. New Jersey was different. We outshot them there, 20 to 18. We made a couple of mistakes on their goals. So look, two of those three nights were fine. One wasn't. We won tonight, that's the third one you want.

Jan Kowalczyk

What's the room? You're 2 and 8. Is that group frustrated, is it starting to doubt?

Craig MacTavish

Nobody signs up for 2 and 8. You know what I mean. Nobody's comfortable. But I don't see a group that's checked out. The compete level's been there every night. I'll say this: we're losing games on the details. A coverage, a puck battle we don't finish. And the details, that's my job. That's on me.

Jan Kowalczyk

19 shots tonight against 21 for Boston. You didn't generate much offense, not by a long way. Is that the identity you want?

Craig MacTavish

Obviously not the identity I want. But it's the identity I've got, and I'll take the 2-1 and the two points. You put a structure on these guys that gives them a chance to win a hockey game. We weren't going to run with Boston tonight. We saw that the first time they scored, they're a dangerous team on the rush. So we tightened up and we got a guy to bury one in overtime. That's what wins hockey games when you don't have the edge in skill.

Jan Kowalczyk

Can I ask about Ethan Moreau on the top line? You've got Jani Rita at 81, Raffi Torres at 78, Brad Isbister at 77, all playing below him. Moreau's 73 in the Book. How do you make that call?

Craig MacTavish

Look, the numbers don't play the game, you know? Ethan's on that line because he wins puck battles, he backs things up behind the net, he's not going to turn it over at the blue line, and he makes the guys around him play simpler. You put Rita out there, fine, he's got more skill. But he doesn't finish his checks like Ethan does. You know what I'm saying. I want Ethan out there at 1-0. I want him at 2-1 in the third. I don't care what the number is.

Jan Kowalczyk

Fair enough. The goaltending. Tommy Salo has played 13 of 15 games, he's 5 and 8. You've got days off before you're at Los Angeles. Does he go, or do you give Ty Conklin the start?

Craig MacTavish

Tommy's our guy. He's been our guy since day one and I'm not going to change that because we're 2 and 8. He made the save when we needed it tonight. I don't lose faith in a goaltender because of the team in front of him. I'll make a change if I have to, and you'll be the first to know. But I don't have to make one right now.

Jan Kowalczyk

You've got Los Angeles next, then Chicago a few days later. What does the time off do for a group that's in a hole? You want rhythm, don't you?

Craig MacTavish

You want to play your way out of it, that's what every coach says, and every coach half-laughs at himself for saying it. Yeah, you want rhythm. But we've been running for a stretch and we're thin. So we'll use the days. Get the legs back, work on a couple of details, and we'll be right for Saturday. I hope.

Jan Kowalczyk

Last one. You mentioned the details costing you games. Is that something you see fixing in the next two weeks, or is this a longer thing?

Craig MacTavish

I see it fixing when we get healthy on the back end and we put the right guys together and they get comfortable. It's a process. The guys are buying in, I'll tell you that. We're not going to turn this around in one week, nobody at 2 and 8 does. But we're closer tonight than we were Tuesday. That's all I can ask for.

Jan Kowalczyk

Craig MacTavish, thank you for your time. Jan Kowalczyk at TD Garden, back to you.
