Martin on the road, the net, and the lines he hasn't moved

Florida's coach addresses the 2-10 record away from home, the Aebischer question ahead of tonight at Anaheim, and why nothing has shifted in his forward groups.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelThursday, 24 November 2005

2:42 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Florida Panthers lost 2-3 at Phoenix Coyotes on Tuesday, the Panthers' tenth road defeat this season against two wins. Head coach Jacques Martin spoke to The Tunnel's Jan Kowalczyk in the tunnel at America West Arena about the goaltending call for tonight at Mighty Ducks of Anaheim, the line combinations that haven't moved, and what he sees in the room.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here with Jacques Martin in the tunnel at America West Arena. Coach, you outshot them 20 to 17, you scored two goals on the road, and you still came away with nothing. What did you see out there tonight?

Jacques Martin

We had our chances. We managed the puck for stretches, we got to the net, and they found a way. Three goals against, two of them, I mean the second and third, they came off our own blue line. We didn't get it deep enough, we turned it over, and they scored. That's the details of it.

Jan Kowalczyk

Coach, you're 2 and 10 on the road. Two and ten. You win six of ten at home, you lose ten of twelve away. What's the difference between the team that dresses here and the team that dresses at home?

Jacques Martin

I don't think it's the dressing room. I think it's the start of the game. We come out here and we wait, you know? We wait for them to come to us, we let them establish, and then we're chasing. At home the first shift sets the tone for you. On the road you have to earn it first, every time, and we haven't earned it consistently.

Jan Kowalczyk

Aebischer is in for 17 of your 22 games this year, he's 7 and 10, and you go back on the ice tomorrow night at Anaheim. Is he your man again, or are you looking at something different?

Jacques Martin

That's a decision I'll make after morning skate. Obviously you look at the results, you look at the workload, and David has been our guy. He's been our guy all year. I'm not going to make that call on the phone at midnight.

Jan Kowalczyk

And Hurme's 0 and 4 in five games. Potvin's one and nothing in one. Does that tell you anything about the options?

Jacques Martin

It tells me David's played 17 games and our record is not good. It tells me nothing about the other two. I don't look at it as, oh, the backup hasn't won so he can't play. That's not how I see the net. You look at practice, you look at how they're tracking the puck, and you make a decision. Tomorrow's a decision.

Jan Kowalczyk

Coach, I looked at your lineup this week and I didn't find a single shift. Nobody moved up, nobody moved down, your four lines are the same four lines that started November. Is that by design or by default?

Jacques Martin

By design. And I'll tell you why. You move guys around every week and nobody knows where they're going. I want these guys comfortable. I want them to know where they're going on the forecheck, where they're going on the backcheck. Do I want more from some of them? Obviously. Yeah, obviously. But the fix isn't, oh, put Steve on the left wing and see if something happens.

Jan Kowalczyk

Nedorost has 15 points, 12 assists. He's playing on a line that hasn't scored consistently. Where is he in your mind going forward?

Jacques Martin

Vaclav is our best playmaker. He sees things, he puts pucks in the right spots. Twelve assists, he's carrying a good part of our offense right now, and he's doing it at twenty-three years old. That's what he does. I want the goals to come, sure. I want that line to score. But he's making the right plays and that's the hard thing to teach.

Jan Kowalczyk

You've got Anaheim tomorrow, Los Angeles Friday, and then home Sunday against Vancouver. Two on the road to start this week. Does the schedule change anything you do with the group?

Jacques Martin

You travel, you get there, you play. You don't overthink it. We'll look at film, we'll fix the second and third goal, we'll fix the transitions. And tomorrow night, you find a way to win, or you don't. That's the schedule. There's no shortcut on the road.

Jan Kowalczyk

Coach Martin, thank you. Jan Kowalczyk, The Tunnel, back to you.
