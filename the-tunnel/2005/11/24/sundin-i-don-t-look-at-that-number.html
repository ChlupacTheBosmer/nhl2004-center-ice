Sundin: 'I Don't Look at That Number'

After Toronto won 4-3 on 20 shots to Philadelphia's 33, the captain said he never checked the count and never will.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelThursday, 24 November 2005

1:31 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Mats Sundin92 had one assist and 4-3 victory to show for it after Toronto Maple Leafs held off Philadelphia Flyers on Thursday night at the Air Canada Centre. Toronto is 12-5-0, 30 points, first in the Northeast. Philadelphia fired 33 shots, Toronto managed 20.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here with Mats Sundin. Mats, twelve and five, thirty points, first place in the division, but you're looking at a thirty-three to twenty shot margin. How did that happen?

Mats Sundin

We played good, I think. First period, second period, we had control of it. The third, they push, of course. They had to push, they're behind. But Mikael was very good tonight. He made the saves when we needed him.

Marcus Bell

Walk me through your assist, the one on Nash's second goal. What did you see?

Mats Sundin

It was, uh, the transition. We won it in the neutral zone and Rick was there, open on the far side, and I just, you know, played it across. He buried it. Easy play for me.

Marcus Bell

Was there a stretch in that third period where you looked up and thought, this is getting tight?

Mats Sundin

No, I don't think so. When you're up by two and you have Mikael in front of you, you stay calm. You know, sometimes the puck goes in, it happens. But we didn't give them a lot of clean ones at the end. We did what we needed to do.

Marcus Bell

You told Jan back in January that shots and pace aren't things you watch. Thirty-three against, that's hard to miss from a bench though.

Mats Sundin

It's, of course, hard to not feel it. You're defending, you're defending all the time, and that takes something out of you. But the number, I don't look at it. I didn't look tonight. The score is what it is. Four and three, we win. That's all I need to see.

Marcus Bell

Even after the game? Nobody's put a sheet in front of you in the dressing room?

Mats Sundin

No, I'm not going to look. I know what I did out there. I know how it felt. Twenty shots or thirty, if you win it, it's fine. That's, that's how I see it.

Marcus Bell

Rick Nash with two goals tonight. You've been watching him all season. What does he bring to the room?

Mats Sundin

He's fast, of course. And when a young man scores two, you know, everybody feels it. It's good for us, for sure. I like watching him play.

Marcus Bell

Mats Sundin, thank you very much. Back to you.
