Forsberg on the form coming back: 'I feel the puck more'

He says the legs and the timing have returned, that he does not track the points or the Bureau's Index, and that the Avalanche room is whole.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelThursday, 24 November 2005

1:10 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

Peter Forsberg89 had 1 goal and 2 points and Colorado Avalanche beat Nashville Predators 3-2 at home on 2005-11-21, a second straight win for the Avalanche. The 32-year-old sits at 13 goals and 26 points in 20 games, at 23.0 minutes a night, and he met Marcus Bell in the corridor after the game.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell, Rinkside. Peter, 3-2 over Nashville, that is two in a row for you. Two points tonight. How did the game feel?

Peter Forsberg

Yeah. I think we played good. We scored three, and, uh, at the end we stayed with it. Two points, that is what we need.

Marcus Bell

You are at 26 points in 20 games now, playing 23 minutes a night. What has changed in your game the last while?

Peter Forsberg

I don't look at that so much. But the legs, uh, the legs feel better than a month ago. The timing comes back. I feel the puck more.

Marcus Bell

The Bureau's Index has you a touch below your base grade. Do you notice those things?

Peter Forsberg

No. Not that. I think about the next game. That is all, for sure.

Marcus Bell

And the room, Peter. You have had bodies in and out of that lineup. Who has filled the hole for you?

Peter Forsberg

No, I don't think we have anybody out. The room is full. That is how you want it, of course.

Marcus Bell

Fair enough. So who has been good around you?

Peter Forsberg

Kariya, Selanne, Hejduk. Sakic. When they are going, it is easy for me. They have been good all year.

Marcus Bell

Twenty games, 22 points, two in a row. What is different about this team from a month ago?

Peter Forsberg

I think we, uh, we believe more. Early we lost the tight ones. Now we, we find a way. That is the difference.

Marcus Bell

Good luck on the trip, Peter. Thanks for the time. Back to you.
