Sundin: 'The points matter now'

The Maple Leafs captain on a slow scoring start, a line he changed in December, and a third-period rush from the 3-2 loss to Boston that he cannot get out of his head.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelSunday, 20 November 2005

1:50 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Mats Sundin92 has 13 points in 19 games for Toronto Maple Leafs this season. The Bureau's Development Index has him at +1 on his base grade of 90, but the scoresheet tells a quieter story. After a 2-3 home loss to Boston Bruins on November 19, the second straight, he stopped with Marcus Bell at the boards.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Mats, Marcus Bell here, post-game. Twenty-six shots for you, twenty-three against, and it's two-three on the board. Second one straight. What is happening?

Mats Sundin

I think we played good, for long stretches. The second period, I think we controlled it. But they score on the chances they get and we don't score on ours. That is the game tonight.

Marcus Bell

Thirteen points through nineteen. That is a pace nobody expected from you. What's different?

Mats Sundin

I don't feel different. Of course, the points are not there. I think... the timing is a little late, maybe. Not my timing, but the team's. When I'm open, the pass is not coming. When I get it, the play has already... you know, it's moved. That's not one man. That's all five.

Marcus Bell

In December you told me the points you don't get don't matter if the team wins. Does that still hold?

Mats Sundin

Well. When you win, of course, you don't count. But when you lose two and you have no points yourself, you start to count. For sure. It is different when the losses are there. You can't be calm about your own game when the team is losing.

Marcus Bell

The Bureau has you plus one on the Index right now. Above your base. Do you feel that?

Mats Sundin

I feel good on the ice. My hands are good, my read is good. I'm not slow. I think... the game is fast right now. Not my speed, but everyone is fast. When I'm 25 I beat them to the spot. Now I have to be early. And sometimes I'm early and the play hasn't come there yet.

Marcus Bell

One shift tonight you will remember. Which one?

Mats Sundin

Their third goal. We have a rush, we're in their zone, maybe two on one and it's not finished. And then you turn around and they are on the other end and the puck is in. That is... you know, that's what you remember. The one you don't finish.

Marcus Bell

You are skating 23.4 minutes a night. That is back to your old numbers. Are you managing those minutes differently?

Mats Sundin

Of course. The shift is maybe a little shorter. But the ice time is there because the coach puts me out. I don't feel heavy at the end of the night. I think that says something. I'm 34, not 44.

Marcus Bell

What do you change?

Mats Sundin

I don't change my game. The team has to be better on the rush. Both ends. That is where the goals are tonight.

Marcus Bell

Thank you, Captain. Marcus Bell, back to you.
