Pascal Leclaire wants the feet and the body in the same conversation

The CBJ goaltender, 93 today on the Bureau's Book, talked family, the road, Geoff Sanderson, and the three attributes where his game still runs away from him.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelSunday, 20 November 2005

3:31 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Pascal Leclaire93 has been in Columbus Blue Jackets since before the record opened, and the Bureau's Development Index has him at +9 this edition, putting his overall at 93 with 24 games in at 11 wins and 10 losses. He sat with Marcus Bell in the studio to talk about the Sweater profile that ran this week, the road from Repentigny to Halifax to Columbus, and the best teammate he has.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here in the studio with Pascal Leclaire of Columbus. Pascal, the Sweater ran a piece on you this week. Headline: runs faster than anyone wearing a goaltender's mask. Ninety-nine agility, ninety-nine speed. Did you read it?

Pascal Leclaire

Yeah, I saw it. I saw it. My mother, he called me right away, she said, 'Pascal, they say you run faster than everybody, is that true?' And I said, Mom, I'm a goaltender. I don't run. I just move a little. But it's good, for sure. The skating is, you know, the skating is the thing I've worked on since I was small. The guys see it in the warmups, they, they kind of expect it now.

Marcus Bell

You're from Repentigny. You went to Halifax for junior, the Mooseheads, and you were about fifteen when you left.

Pascal Leclaire

Fifteen, yeah. Almost sixteen. You get on a plane, you're... I had never been that far from home, you know. Not that far. And the room, the guys in the room, they'd all been on the road already, some of them since fourteen, fifteen. And you're, you're a kid and you don't know how to do your own laundry, you don't know how to... you know. You learn. You have to. It's not, it's not optional.

Marcus Bell

And then you came back closer with the Rocket, Montreal. And then the draft, 2001, eighth overall, the first goaltender taken.

Pascal Leclaire

Montreal was... it was good to be closer. The language, the food, my family can come to a game, you know? From Repentigny it's not a big drive. That's different than Halifax. And the draft... people say 'you were the first goalie' and I say, thank you, that's it. You remember it, for sure. But it doesn't follow you onto the ice. Every night you're just trying to stop the puck, eighth pick or thirty-eighth.

Marcus Bell

The piece was about your feet, the 99s. In your own room, who moves in a way that makes you stop and watch?

Pascal Leclaire

Sanderson. Geoff. You watch him in practice, he's 33 and he moves like he's 23, you know? I see it from the crease every day. The first step, the edges. And he doesn't waste it. Every stride, it means something. Nothing is extra. That's what I try to do, I try to, every motion has to be there. He's got that down.

Marcus Bell

Best player you've played with?

Pascal Leclaire

I think... yeah. He's the guy that, when he's on the ice, you believe the game can go your way. You know? Even if it's 2 and 0 against. You look up and Geoff is there, he's calm, and you think, okay, one good break and we've got something. And he's, he's quiet in the room. He doesn't, he doesn't have to be loud. He just shows you on the ice. And at 33, you know, you see why.

Marcus Bell

Let's talk about your season. 24 games in, 11 wins, 10 losses, 1 shutout. Fifty-eight minutes a night. Where does that leave you?

Pascal Leclaire

It leaves me with work to do. I'm not satisfied. The guys, the room, we all know we're not where we want to be. Eleven and ten, that's... that's not the number. But you play the games you get. We have to be better, that's it.

Marcus Bell

The Development Index has you up nine this edition. Your overall sits at 93 today. That's the number the league puts next to your name.

Pascal Leclaire

Ninety-three. And I'm sitting here, 11 and 10. I don't know how they do the math back there. But, for sure, it's good. You want to feel like you're moving forward. You do the work in the morning, the practice, the skate, and if the Index says it's there, that means the work is counting. That's something.

Marcus Bell

Your contract is $1.10M and it's up after this season. Zero years left. You're 23. Does that sit in your head?

Pascal Leclaire

No. It's there, I know it's there. But you can't go into a game thinking about, about the money. The guys in the room, most of them have been through a contract year. They tell you: play the year. Take care of the rest after. And that's what I'm doing. I play the year.

Marcus Bell

Here's something the profile didn't dig into. The Book has you at 51 in fighting, 51 in pad stack, 51 in positioning. Those are the lowest numbers next to your name. Do you see them?

Pascal Leclaire

I see them. Every night. I see them on tape, my coach sees them, the guys behind me see them. Everybody talks about the feet. 'Oh, the feet, the speed.' But the positioning... if I'm out of position, my speed don't matter. You know what I mean? The feet can only take you so far. So that's the work. That's, that's where I have to get better. The body has to catch up to the feet. That's the road.

Marcus Bell

Pascal, thank you very much for your time tonight. Back to you in the studio.
