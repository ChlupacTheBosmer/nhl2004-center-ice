Brodeur on the 3-1 in Detroit: 'Two of Those I Want Back'

New Jersey's goaltender gave up 3 goals on 24 shots in a 3-1 road loss, said the late save is still the one he wants, and called six games in hand the room's comfort.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelWednesday, 30 November 2005

1:35 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Martin Brodeur93 gave up 3 goals on 24 shots as New Jersey Devils lost 3-1 in Detroit Red Wings on 2005-11-27, New Jersey's only defeat in 7 games away from home. The Devils have 29 points from 19 games, 6 fewer played than the Rangers, and with a dense stretch ahead Brodeur talked about the schedule as much as the goals he wanted back.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Martin, Marcus Bell at the rink in Detroit. 3-1, and you outshoot them 25-24. What did they do to you tonight?

Martin Brodeur

Yeah. They come at you, you know. Good team, and they win three in a row now. We get the 25 shots and one goal, and me, I give up three. Two of those I want back. That's the game, that's it.

Marcus Bell

Does it sit worse, losing a game you outshot them in?

Martin Brodeur

For sure it's worse, you know. You get 25 shots, you have to get more than one goal. So we lose, and I give up three. Everybody, we have to be better.

Marcus Bell

In January we talked about the third save. Is that the one again tonight?

Martin Brodeur

Yeah. In January I say the same thing, the third save, the late one. When it's 3-1, one more save and we still have the game. That's on me, that one.

Marcus Bell

Patrik Elias got the goal, Grant Marshall and Pavel Kubina the assists. Enough support for you?

Martin Brodeur

Patty, yeah. He score the big goals for us, he always does. Grant and Pavel they make the play, so the guys, they work. One goal is not enough, but it's there. We need more, for sure.

Marcus Bell

You've played 19 games, the Rangers 25. Six in hand. What does the schedule ahead ask of you?

Martin Brodeur

Six in hand is good for us, you know. We have 29 points and we have the games. A lot of hockey coming, and me, I like that. We play, we don't sit and think about the losses.

Marcus Bell

You've started 16 of them. Do you manage the load, or do you just want the net?

Martin Brodeur

I want the net. That's my job, I play. 16 games and the body is good, I feel strong. When the schedule is tight, you don't have time to be tired. You just go.

Marcus Bell

How's the room after a night like this?

Martin Brodeur

Quiet, you know. It's one game, we don't panic. Tomorrow we watch the video and we go again. The guys know. We have to be better, that's it.

Marcus Bell

Thanks, Marty. Back to you.
