Sundin Passes It In as Toronto Makes It Four Straight in Philadelphia

The captain scored none of the goals and owned none of the credit, and the Leafs sat at seven and one on the road before he got off the ice.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelWednesday, 30 November 2005

1:33 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Mats Sundin92 collected 2 assists in Toronto Maple Leafs's 4-2 road win at Philadelphia Flyers on Tuesday, his second multi-assist game of the season, as Toronto moved to 14-5-0 and 36 points. The Leafs now sit seven and one on the road, their 7-1 mark the quiet backbone of the best record in the league. Sundin, 34, turned the conversation toward Stanislav Chistov87, who scored twice and finished with 3 points.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Mats Sundin, thank you. Jan Kowalczyk, The Tunnel. Four in a row tonight, 4-2 in Philadelphia. What is it like in this building with this crowd, what did it feel like on the ice?

Mats Sundin

It is always good here. Philadelphia, the fans, they get going early, and I think in the first period the building was loud. But after the second goal it was quiet. I think we played good in the first period. That was the important part, because if you get out to a bad start here, it is hard.

Jan Kowalczyk

You are seven and one on the road right now. What is it about this group that travels so well?

Mats Sundin

I do not think about the number, for sure. But I think when you go on the road, you cannot feel sorry for yourself. You play your game, you do not look for the referee to help you. We just go out and we do the simple things. It does not matter which building you are in.

Jan Kowalczyk

Let me ask about the special teams tonight. How did they look from out there?

Mats Sundin

The PK did the job. We blocked shots, we were in the lanes. And on our power play, when you have Gaborik and Mogilny on the half-wall, you find the open man. That is the whole thing. You move the puck, you make the pass, you do not try to do too much. The open man is usually there.

Jan Kowalczyk

Chistov scored twice, three points on the night. You told me in January you had started playing more ahead of the play instead of chasing it. You had two assists tonight. How do those things connect?

Mats Sundin

He is a good player, Stanislav. I see it every day in practice. Two goals, three points, that is big for him. I just pass the puck, he puts it in. I told him on the bench, you are not allowed to sit quiet tonight. For the fans the goal is his. The assist is fine. I get a point, everybody is happy. That is how it works.

Jan Kowalczyk

Thank you, Mats. Four in a row, and the road record speaks. Enjoy it. Back to you.
