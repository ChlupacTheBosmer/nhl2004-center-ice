Bowness on the next crease: 'That's a question I'll answer after practice tomorrow'

The Coyotes coach won his first in three, kept Sean Burke in net for a 24th start this season, and then pointed at St. Louis and the streak across from them.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelWednesday, 30 November 2005

3:47 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Rick Bowness walked off the bench at Jobing.com Arena with a 4-3 overtime win over Chicago Blackhawks, the kind of game his room needed after dropping two straight. Phoenix Coyotes sits 5-16-1, 15 points, 47 goals for and 69 against in 24 games. Tomorrow night they play at St. Louis Blues, who have won eight straight.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here with Rick Bowness, and Rick, a 4-3 overtime win, the first one since the 21st. You've just gotten past Chicago. How does that one feel from where you were sitting?

Rick Bowness

It feels like a hockey game that went the way you wanted it to go for once, you know? We were, I mean, we got out to a start, they came back, and the third period we were, we just needed a break. And we got one in overtime. The boys competed tonight. I can't ask for more than that against a team that's fighting for its life like Chicago is.

Marcus Bell

You're five and sixteen and one now. Fifteen points. You're in last place in the Pacific. And I think you'd rather not hear that tonight after a win, but what does this group look like to you when you put the two together, the result and the record?

Rick Bowness

No, I hear it fine. I know what 15 points looks like. I've been in rooms where you claw out of it, and I've been in rooms where you don't. This one... we're close to a couple of those games. You look at the last ten, 21 goals for, 31 against. That's not a team that can't play. That's a team that's been a save, a bounce, a bad five minutes away. But I'm not going to stand here and pretend that number isn't what it is.

Marcus Bell

Sean Burke started tonight, that's 18 starts out of 24 games, 71 percent of your schedule. He's 6 and 11 with one shutout. You've got Brian Boucher who's 1 and 6. Do you go back to Sean tomorrow against St. Louis, or does this one land on a different pair of skates?

Rick Bowness

That's a question I'll answer after practice tomorrow. You know what Sean's given us, his numbers... look, six and eleven on this roster, it's not on him. I don't care what anyone writes. But I've got to look at the whole thing, who's played, what's coming at us. And what's coming at us tomorrow is a hockey team that's won eight in a row and scored 32 in their last ten. So yeah, it's a decision. And I'll make it tomorrow.

Marcus Bell

Let me push a little. You're allowing 69 goals in 24 games, that's almost three a night. Is that a goaltending problem, or is it what's in front of the goaltender?

Rick Bowness

It's both. And I'm not, I'm not going to stand here and point at a man's numbers. It's in front. We've got a puck management issue, we've got a problem where we give up a turnover in the neutral zone and now it's a two-on-one and your goalie's facing a grade-A chance he didn't need to face. That's a structure thing. That's on me. That's the details, and we've been too loose on the details.

Marcus Bell

You just said you'd be candid. If Sean sits tomorrow, does he come back in, or has the crease changed?

Rick Bowness

You're not getting that answer tonight, and you know it. Sean's 38 years old and he's started 18 of 24. He's a professional, he'll take care of himself, he'll be ready when I call on him. Whether that's tomorrow or Friday, I don't know yet. You want a straight answer, I'll give you a straight answer: I haven't made the call. And I'm not going to make a coach's decision for a reporter at 10:45 at night.

Marcus Bell

You lost Mike Sillinger to the back, he's out till the 31st. Brian Savage, the wrist, back December 18. That's two guys you'd want in your top six. How do you dress the middle of your forward group right now?

Rick Bowness

You dress it with guys who have to play up, right? Hrdina's been handling a lot of minutes, Nagy's carrying the offense, Doan's got a knee that isn't right but he's out there. You move people up and you pray the bottom six can keep it simple and not be a fourth line that gives up the first chance every shift. That's the challenge. Sillinger's the one who makes our top two lines look like two lines instead of one, so yeah, it hurts.

Marcus Bell

Tomorrow you're at St. Louis. Eight game win streak, 35 points, they've scored 32 in ten. Is there a plan for that, or is it just survive and see?

Rick Bowness

There's always a plan. Whether you execute it is the question. They're physical, they're fast, and they're playing with that confidence where everything goes in. You go into that building and if you make a mistake with the puck, they'll punish you. We have to be honest with ourselves about who we are and play to that. Play tight, play simple, make them earn it. And see where we are after 60.

Marcus Bell

One last one, Rick. You've coached in this league a long time. Does 5 and 16 feel different than it used to, or is it the same feeling?

Rick Bowness

It's the same feeling. The cold coffee at 6 AM, the feeling that you've got more on the board than you want, you've got four days between now and the weekend and every one of them has to be better. I've been 9 and 20. I've been 4 and 15. You either figure out the problem or the problem figures you out. We're going to figure out the problem. I believe that about this room.

Marcus Bell

Coach Bowness, thank you. Back to you.
