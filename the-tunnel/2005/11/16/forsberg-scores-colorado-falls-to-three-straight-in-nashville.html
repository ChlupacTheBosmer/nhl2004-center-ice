Forsberg Scores, Colorado Falls to Three Straight in Nashville

The alternate captain had the only Colorado goal Monday and gave Paul Kariya, Jamie Storr and the blue line more words than he spent on himself.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelWednesday, 16 November 2005

1:31 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Peter Forsberg95 scored the lone Colorado Avalanche goal in a 2-1 loss at Nashville Predators on Monday, the club's third straight defeat, and stopped in the tunnel afterward with his breathing still high. The goal gave him 1 goal and 2 points across 2 games since the last edition. The Bureau's Development Index has him a notch below his base grade of 97.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here with Peter Forsberg. Pete, 2-1 on the road, third straight, and you had the only one. What did you see on that goal?

Peter Forsberg

I think the play developed good. I was there and the puck came to me and I put it in. I think the whole game we had chances. We just could not put two in.

Jan Kowalczyk

Three in a row now. You told me back in January the close ones were turning against you. Still the same thing?

Peter Forsberg

Yes, I think so. The games are close and one puck goes in, the other does not. I think we played good in stretches. I think the goalie, Jamie, he was fine tonight. He made the save when we needed. It is just, we need one more. For sure.

Jan Kowalczyk

Joe Sakic not in the lineup tonight. How is the line finding each other without him?

Peter Forsberg

For sure Joe is important, of course. But with Paul and Milan we have three good hands out there. I think it takes time without him, but I think we can do good. Paul, he sees the play, and Milan is always open. I think we will be fine.

Jan Kowalczyk

Kariya had a goal and an assist in two games, Hejduk an assist in two. Are they getting you in the right spots?

Peter Forsberg

Yes, I think so. And you know, the back end, Derek, the boys, they move the puck good. I think when the defense is on we have quality everywhere. It is just, one more goal and we win this game. That is all.

Jan Kowalczyk

Two points in two games yourself. Does that number sit right when the team is losing?

Peter Forsberg

No. I would trade it. I do not look at the points. I look at the wins. Tonight I had the goal and we lost, so the goal means nothing. Of course I want the points and the win together. I think I have said that before.

Jan Kowalczyk

You have. What does it take to stop this?

Peter Forsberg

One more goal. I think we are good enough. The players are there. We just need to put sixty minutes together. I think that is all.

Jan Kowalczyk

Pete, thank you. From Nashville, Jan Kowalczyk.
