Gaborik watches five names score, wants his own

The winger went quiet in Toronto's 5-3 win at Buffalo but says the line with Nash and Sundin is close to something.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelWednesday, 16 November 2005

2:09 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Marian Gaborik [308] had no points in Toronto Maple Leafs's 5-3 road win over Buffalo Sabres on 2005-11-15, one night after the winger's Development Index reading reached +11 above his base of 91. Five different Toronto players scored in the win that made it three straight. Gaborik spoke to Marcus Bell in the tunnel after the game.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell, post-game in the Buffalo tunnel. Marian, five and three, third straight, and five different guys on the scoresheet tonight. What's it like on the bench when the goals spread across the room like that?

Marian Gaborik

Yeah, it's good, you know? You look down the bench and Alexeev's got one, Mogilny's got one, everybody's chasing it. It's... it makes the game feel easy when you see that. I'm happy for the guys, I really am. But you know, you're sitting there, you watch it, and you want to be in it too.

Marcus Bell

You told this desk back in January that nobody in the room cares about your goal total on a night you lose. Tonight you win, you still got zero in the column. Does it sit different when the two points come?

Marian Gaborik

Yeah. I mean... in January we lost, so it's easy, I say nobody cares. But tonight, you know, you're in the car, you're driving home, and you remember the shifts. I had, how you say... the looks. I had two looks in the second where I'm through the neutral zone and I have speed, I feel the speed, but I shoot and it's not... it's not the right one. The puck doesn't do what I want.

Marcus Bell

You're playing with Nash up left and Sundin in the middle. How's that line connecting? They each had a point tonight.

Marian Gaborik

Rick, he's fast, you know? He gets behind the defence and he pulls their guys with him, and that opens the slot for me. And Mats, he's just... he reads the play before it happens. I go to the net, he knows I'm going, and the pass comes on the backhand where only I can get it. Tonight it was close. Two, three times. It's one touch. Maybe next time it's there.

Marcus Bell

You're 99 overall right now, the Bureau's got you at the top of the Development Index. But tonight you've got nothing on the board. Does that gap between what the Book says and what the game says bother you?

Marian Gaborik

The Book, you know, I don't look at the Book. I'm 23, I'm still getting better, the Index says +11, okay, good. But in the game tonight, I'm passing up the open man, I'm making the right read, and the goal doesn't come. That's not... I don't know. I'm not worried. The speed is there. I feel it every shift. You know? It's coming.

Marcus Bell

Three straight now, 28 points through 17. You're at the top of the Northeast. Where does this win rank in the ones you've had this year?

Marian Gaborik

I don't rank them, Marcus. Five on the road, that's good. The whole team scored, five different names, the D men had points, Safronov, Colaiacovo. That's how you win in this league, you know? Everyone chips in. I'll get mine.

Marcus Bell

Marian Gaborik, Toronto number ten, 5 and 3 on the road. Thanks, Marian. Back to you.
