Lemaire on 3 and 8 at home: 'We know. We do not fix it by talking'

Jacques Lemaire after the Wild's 3-2 win over Washington, on the home record, the hole at center with Saku Koivu out, and what he wants from Pierre-Marc Bouchard.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelThursday, 17 November 2005

2:21 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Minnesota Wild won a second straight on 2005-11-16, 3-2 over WSH at the Xcel, but the record at home is 3 and 8 and Saku Koivu82 is out until November 29 with a shoulder. Head coach Jacques Lemaire spoke with The Tunnel's Marcus Bell after the game about the structure of his team, who centers in Koivu's absence, and the three games in four days that start Sunday.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here at the Xcel after the Wild beat Washington 3 and 2, your second in a row. Coach, the guys got it done tonight. What did you like about the compete level?

Jacques Lemaire

The second period. We managed the puck good, we were not taking chances, we were getting out of our zone clean. The compete level was there for sure. In the first period, uh, we were flat for maybe ten minutes. After that, it was good. That's it.

Marcus Bell

And what didn't you like? Because Washington got 22 shots on Manny Fernandez. You're 52 for, 50 against on the season. That's almost dead even.

Jacques Lemaire

Yeah. I want fewer shots against. That's the number. The details in the neutral zone, I don't think we were good enough there in the first period, and then in the third when they're pressing we were, we were backing up too much. But the guys competed. I don't have a problem with compete. I have a problem with, uh, when the puck is going the wrong way, we need to make the right play, not the fancy play. That's it.

Marcus Bell

Coach, you're 3 and 8 at home. That's the worst home record in the Northwest. How do you fix a thing like that?

Jacques Lemaire

We know. We do not fix it by talking about it. We are playing here, the guys, they want to play good in front of their fans, I'm sure of it. But, you know, sometimes the ice is different when you play here. We are trying to understand why. It is not a big thing, it is the details. Little things. We will find it. For sure we will find it.

Marcus Bell

Saku Koivu is out until November 29 with the shoulder. Nineteen points in thirteen games. Who centers?

Jacques Lemaire

Pierre-Marc. He is moving up. He played center all his life in junior, in the juniors, he knows the position. He is twenty-one, he is learning, he is going to make mistakes, I will not tell you different. But he has the hands, he has the head for it. I trust him there.

Marcus Bell

Seven points in nineteen games this year though. Is that enough production to play a 20-minute a night role?

Jacques Lemaire

You want him to score like Saku tonight. I cannot promise you that. The other guys, Richard, Tuomo, they score, that is fine. Pierre-Marc's job is to play good defense, to win the faceoffs, to make the play simple. The points, they come when the game comes easy. You will see.

Marcus Bell

Bill Muckalt's hand is healed, he's back Sunday against Los Angeles. Does that change what you're doing on the wing?

Jacques Lemaire

It gives me a body. Bill is not scoring tonight, he is, he's a fourth liner, but he plays hard, he blocks shots, he is good in the corner. When Saku is out I need the wings to help back, and Bill, he does that without being asked. That's good for me.

Marcus Bell

Last one. Three games in four days: Los Angeles Sunday, Washington Tuesday, Nashville Thursday. Manny's got 13 starts already. Are you thinking about the net, about giving Jean-Marc a turn?

Jacques Lemaire

Manny is my number one. He is 6 and 7, he is 1 and 7 in the last one, I'm sure. The record is not good, I know, but he gives us a chance every night. Jean-Marc, he's going to play. He will play in this stretch, for sure. But Manny starts. That's the call. Back to you.
