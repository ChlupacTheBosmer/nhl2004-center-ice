Iginla's Two Put Him At 16 In 18, And He Won't Chase The Pace

The Flames' alternate captain on McLennan's steady night, on Reinprecht finding him twice, and on why eighteen games is not a season

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelThursday, 17 November 2005

2:02 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Jarome Iginla86 scored twice and Calgary Flames beat San Jose 4-2 at home on November 16, moving to 7-7-1 with 21 points. The 28-year-old alternate captain now has 16 goals in 18 games, after winning back-to-back Maurice Richard Trophies the last two seasons. Jamie McLennan stopped 16 of 18 shots.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here in the tunnel, with Jarome Iginla. Four and two over San Jose tonight, two of those yours, your fifteenth and sixteenth of the season. Reinprecht had two assists, and you found the back of the net both times. What's it like when your centre keeps putting them right there?

Jarome Iginla

Yeah, it's a great feeling, obviously. Steve's been seeing things, you know? He's been finding the second guy all night. First one, I just backed into the slot and he put it right where I needed it. The second one, he caught the defender skating backwards and I had a half-second and I took it. So, you know, when your centre's on like that, you just try to bury them.

Jan Kowalczyk

McLennan tonight in the net. Sixteen saves on eighteen shots. You've got him and Turek, both capable. How does this room settle behind two goalies sharing the work?

Jarome Iginla

Yeah, look, both guys are good enough. Jamie's been solid, Turek's been solid. It's not like we're standing around debating it in the room. Obviously you want to give your goalie something, block a shot, get a body in a lane. I think we did that tonight. They only had eighteen shots, so we kept them to the outside for the most part. Jamie didn't have to do anything heroic and that's, that's fine by us.

Jan Kowalczyk

Sixteen in eighteen. You won the Richard Trophy last year and the year before. Seventy-six last season. People are doing the math on what this pace means. Are you aware of it?

Jarome Iginla

Yeah, obviously, you'd have to be dead not to hear about it. But eighteen games, that's nothing. I scored seventy-six last year and there were stretches where it wasn't going in and everybody figured I was done. So no, I'm not looking at the pace. I'm looking at the next shift. If the goals are there in April, great. If they're not, I'll worry about it then.

Jan Kowalczyk

Last time we talked, back in December after that one against Toronto, you said the looks were coming. Tonight Green got one, Conroy got one, you got two. Four different scorers. Are those looks there now?

Jarome Iginla

Yeah, I think they are. We're moving the puck a lot better than we were early on. And when you've got guys like Craig and Josh putting them in on other lines too, it takes some of the weight off, you know? Obviously I want to score, that's what I do, but when you've got four guys who can go tonight and four who can go tomorrow, that's, that's a hard team to guard.

Jan Kowalczyk

Two straight, twenty-one points. What does this room need to put together before the calendar flips to December?

Jarome Iginla

Consistency. You know, we can beat anybody on our night and then we'll give up four in a period and lose. And that's what we've got to get out of ourselves. You've got to play the full sixty. Tonight was good. But we've got to find a way to do it every night. That's the one thing.

Jan Kowalczyk

Jarome, thanks for your time. Appreciate it.
