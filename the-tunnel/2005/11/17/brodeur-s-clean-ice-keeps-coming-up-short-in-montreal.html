Brodeur's clean ice keeps coming up short in Montreal

The Devils' goaltender won his 11th of 13 starts but still has no shutout, and the one that keeps slipping away in the last twenty minutes.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelThursday, 17 November 2005

2:14 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Martin Brodeur93 won his 11th game in 13 starts this season for New Jersey Devils, a 2-1 road win at Montreal Canadiens that pushed the Devils to a 5-game streak and 31 goals against in 15 games. The shutout, though, did not come. Not in 13. Not this season.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here, post-game in Montreal. Martin, two to one, fifth straight. What did it take tonight?

Martin Brodeur

You know, it was tight the whole way. Two to one in this building, and they, they had their chances. Twenty shots, but some of them were good ones. We just, we held on at the end. For sure.

Marcus Bell

You grew up watching games in this building. Your father was behind that net here for 20 years. Does this place play different for you?

Martin Brodeur

Yeah, well. The ice is the same ice. The boards are the same boards. I know where the puck bounces, yeah, I knew that already. So no, it plays the same. Maybe the guys, some of them they look up in the stands and see a crowd. I just, I see a game.

Marcus Bell

Twenty shots against. Montreal's on a two-game slide, they're 4 and 8 and 3. Did they look like a team that's struggling, or did they make you work?

Martin Brodeur

No, no, you can't look at the record. Twenty shots but, you know, two of them hit the post I think. And the third one, the one we gave up, it was from the slot. They had their looks. You don't get twenty looks for free anywhere in this league. That's for sure.

Marcus Bell

You told me in January about the late save, the one in the third that wasn't coming. 13 starts this season, no shutout yet. Same problem?

Martin Brodeur

It's the same thing. One goal. It's always one goal, in the last twenty. Tonight it was that, that slot chance, and it goes in, and there it is, two to one, and now you're just trying to close. I don't know what changes when the third gets going. Maybe the legs. Maybe the traffic. I don't know.

Marcus Bell

31 goals against in 15 games. That's the best in the league. What's the room doing differently?

Martin Brodeur

They're blocking shots, that's what. The guys in front of me, Rafalski, Kubina, they eat pucks. And the forwards, Langenbrunner, Gomez, they backcheck all the way, they don't cheat. You get five guys in the zone and the other team, they just, they don't have space. It starts up top, not with me.

Marcus Bell

Josh Sim scored tonight. He's one of the youngest guys in your room. What does a kid like that need from a veteran in the blue paint?

Martin Brodeur

He just needs to play. That's it. I don't tell the young guys anything special. I yell a little, sure, but they know. Sim, he has quiet hands, he sees the play early. He scored on the road in Montreal, that's not nothing. The guys in that room, they were loud for him.

Marcus Bell

Five straight, 10 and 2 and 1. Do you look up at that record in November and think about what it means for April, or do you just take the next one?

Martin Brodeur

The next one. Always the next one. 10 and 2, that's good, for sure, but it's November. The guys know that. We have to be better in the third period, and that's it. That's where the games go. Thank you, Marcus.
