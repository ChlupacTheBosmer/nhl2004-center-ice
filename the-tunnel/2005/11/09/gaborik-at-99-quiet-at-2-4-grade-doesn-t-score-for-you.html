Gaborik at 99, quiet at 2-4: 'Grade doesn't score for you'

Sitting atop the Book's ceiling with a +11 on the Index, Gaborik watched Toronto fire 33 shots and lose 2-4 in Dallas, and says the room knows the difference between a number and a result.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelWednesday, 9 November 2005

2:33 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Marian Gaborik99 and Toronto Maple Leafs came to Dallas on a one-game losing streak after nine wins in twelve, outshot the hosts 33-23, and left with a 4-2 loss. Gaborik finished the night without a point, the first time the desk has caught him scoreless in a game where Toronto generated that much traffic in front of the net.
