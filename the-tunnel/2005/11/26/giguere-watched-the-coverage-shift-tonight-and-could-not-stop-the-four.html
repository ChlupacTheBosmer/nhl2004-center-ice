Giguere watched the coverage shift tonight and could not stop the four that came through

The goaltender says the defensive look in front of him changed against Florida, and that the special teams could not buy him a quiet night the way they did before.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelSaturday, 26 November 2005

2:41 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Jean-Sebastien Giguere93 gave up four goals on 21 shots as Mighty Ducks of Anaheim fell 2-4 at home to Florida Panthers on Thursday night, dropping them to 6-12-1 on the season. The goaltender said the coverage in front of him looked different tonight and that the special teams failed to protect him the way they did earlier in the year.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here with Jean-Sebastien Giguere. J-S, four and two, your own building, a Florida club that's only two points ahead of you. What changed out there tonight? I noticed the back end looked different from what I've seen the last few weeks.

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

Yeah, for sure. The guys, they were, uh... the first two periods the pairs were tighter to each other, closer to the net, and I could see it. I could see them, you know, reading the play a little earlier. But the third period they opened up again and the second goal, the fourth, they were too far from each other and you get, what do you call it, the seam opens and the guy just walks in.

Jan Kowalczyk

And that's not what you saw the last few weeks? You told me in December, when the coach shuffled things, you said the groups had speed. Is that gone?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

No, the speed is there. The speed is there, that's not the problem. It's the, how do you say, the trust between the two guys. Sergei is up there, Niclas is good tonight, he gets a point, but the other side, they didn't, they didn't come back the same way. I don't know if the coach said something at the intermission or what, but the third period looked different than the first two.

Jan Kowalczyk

Let me ask you about the specials. You had twenty-one shots all night and you gave up four. That's not a volume problem, that's a quality problem on the chances you did face. Were the penalty killers giving you clean looks?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

No. No, and that's, that's what gets me. In December I told you, the special teams quiet my night, they give me a break. Tonight was the opposite. Two of those goals, maybe more, I didn't see the puck until it was on me. The guys in front, they were late to the block, they were, I don't know, they were watching. And when you watch the puck, you have a screen and the shooter has time. I don't need a lot of shots, I need to see the puck, that's all.

Jan Kowalczyk

You've got zero shutouts this season. You're the Vezina winner from last year. What's the disconnect between the man who won that trophy and the man in that net right now?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

Yeah. I know. You know, I've been here since, since the record started and I know what the guys behind me give me. Some nights I'm the best player and some nights I'm... the consistency is my problem. I've always known that. People tell me, you're too good for that number, that consistency number, and yeah, maybe. But I'm standing there and I'm seeing four pucks go in and I'm not, I'm not making myself into something I'm not. We have to be better, the whole group, and I start with the next save, that's it.

Jan Kowalczyk

Six wins, twelve losses, one overtime. You're in front of Florida, you're in front of Columbus. What do you tell the guys in that room right now? Because you're six and a half hours ahead of where you need to be, not where they need to be, where you need to be.

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

I tell them we play again soon. That's it. I don't stand up and give a speech about the standings. I'm a goalie, I worry about the next game. But yeah, when you're six and twelve at home, in November, and you just gave up four to a team that's also six wins... it's not the time for speeches. It's the time for, for fixing what's wrong. The third period tonight, we fix that.

Jan Kowalczyk

J-S, thank you for your time. Jean-Sebastien Giguere, speaking to the media after the 4-2 loss to Florida. Back to you.
