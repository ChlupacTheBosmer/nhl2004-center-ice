Brent O'Connor: the money is here, the rooms are not, and Sanderson is still here

The Columbus general manager says his roster and his building are his chair, that the equipment room at zero has been zero the whole time, and that 25 points on $62.03M keeps them in it

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelSaturday, 26 November 2005

3:17 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Columbus Blue Jackets are 7-7-3 with 25 points, tied with Detroit Red Wings in the Central, and six games into a losing streak going into an open day. General manager Brent O'Connor sat down with The Tunnel's Marcus Bell on a Saturday morning to talk about what the coach has in the rooms, what the $22.59M profit means when the hockey is stalled, and what he does with Geoff Sanderson before March.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here in the studio with Brent O'Connor, general manager of Columbus Blue Jackets. Brent, six straight, 7-7-3, 25 points. You're tied with Detroit in the division, six back of St. Louis. How do you read that number from the other side of the glass?

Brent O'Connor

I read 21 games. That's the thing people forget when they say six straight. We've got 25 points, we're in the middle of the Central. The streak is loud because it's right now, it's this week, and I get that. But I'm looking at the whole ledger and we're in it.

Marcus Bell

Your coach. Does he have what he needs from you? Roster-wise, this week, right now.

Brent O'Connor

That's his chair. I don't call lines, I don't decide who sits, I don't dress the third pairing. I'll tell you what I see from where I sit though. Todd Marchant is out till January 30 with the knee. Joe Juneau till the 28th with the hand. That's two centers the coach doesn't have. I've got three guys on the shelf this week and that thins the middle. What he does with what's left, that's his call.

Marcus Bell

But you're asking the GM, so I'll ask you. The rooms. Your equipment room is at zero, your practice rink is at one. That's a live sum of negative 15 across the four tracks that move a rating. You've made one upgrade since May. Does the coach have what he needs in the building?

Brent O'Connor

No. No he doesn't. And I know that. The equipment room has been zero since I've been here. I moved travel and workout up in May, that's it. Everything else is sitting where it was. He's got a guy like Geoff Sanderson going 21 games and putting up 23 points on a zero equipment room. That's not what that number should look like with a four in there.

Marcus Bell

You're making $22.59M in profit. You're pulling 15049 a night at $83. The money is there.

Brent O'Connor

The money is there. That's the honest answer and it's not one I like giving because it makes me look slow. I know what the rooms should be. I know what they cost in terms of what the guys feel every morning. I just haven't moved on it and I should've.

Marcus Bell

Let me go to Sanderson. $6.34M, one year left, 33 years old, 84 overall, morale at 87. What the calendar has as the trade deadline is 113 days away. Do you move him?

Brent O'Connor

He's scoring. Twenty-three points in 21 games, that's real. He's giving us everything his number says he can give us. I'm not moving a guy producing like that unless I get back something that makes us better in 2006-07 than he makes us right now. That's the test. I haven't got that test met yet.

Marcus Bell

25 points on $62.03M. That's $2.48M a point. You're sitting eighth in payroll, ninth in per point cost. What do you think this club is worth in this league right now?

Brent O'Connor

The business is worth something. 15049 at $83, $46.08M in revenue, $22.59M profit. That's a healthy club on the books. The hockey side... we're a team that should be better than 25 with the money in it. The per point is too high. That tells me I'm not getting value out of the roster I've built or the rooms I'm running. Both need work and I know which one I control.

Marcus Bell

One year, six months out. What changes between now and what the calendar has as the deadline?

Brent O'Connor

I've got the money. I've got a building that's behind and I know it. I've got an expiring asset producing. And I've got a streak I can't fix by being the GM. I know what needs doing. I just haven't done it yet.

Marcus Bell

Brent O'Connor, thank you for your morning. Marcus Bell, The Tunnel, back to you.
