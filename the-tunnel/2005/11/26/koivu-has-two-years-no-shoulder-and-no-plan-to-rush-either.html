Koivu Has Two Years, No Shoulder, and No Plan to Rush Either

The Minnesota center, currently out with a shoulder that keeps him until November 29, says the Development Index has nothing to do with how he feels on a Tuesday morning.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelSaturday, 26 November 2005

3:41 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Saku Koivu86 sits on the studio couch with a shoulder that came back sometime between October 29 and November 6 and will not release him until November 29. The Bureau's Development Index has him at 82 overall against a base of 88. He has 2 years and $6.84M left on his contract with Minnesota Wild, and he says that is a comfortable number.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here in the studio with Saku Koivu, who is sitting down with us tonight because his shoulder came back in that window between late October and early November and will keep him out until the 29th. Saku, how are you?

Saku Koivu

I am good. Sitting is the hard part. I do not like sitting.

Jan Kowalczyk

Three days. You are back in the lineup on Friday.

Saku Koivu

They say three days. So. I will believe it on Friday.

Jan Kowalczyk

You watched from the bench the last few weeks. What do you see from out there?

Saku Koivu

I see Manny. You sit back there and you see Manny make saves that should not be saves. And you see Richard on the wing, he is open every time and nobody finds him. I used to find him. I cannot find him from the bench.

Jan Kowalczyk

Richard Park is sitting at 28 points through 22 games. That is fourth in the league.

Saku Koivu

I know. He should have more. Wes sets him up, I set him up, but from the bench I just watch him skate open and nobody pass. So I am not... I am not happy about it.

Jan Kowalczyk

Let me ask you about the Bureau's index. They have you at 82 overall, your base is 88. The Development Index has you six below where they think you can be. Does that number sit with you?

Saku Koivu

That number... I know it exists. I do not look at it in the morning. I look at my body in the morning. My body says what it says. The number, I see it maybe when a writer asks me like you are asking. That is fine. I answer. But I do not, I do not play to 88. I play to what my legs give me that night.

Jan Kowalczyk

You are 31. You came to Minnesota in February. You look settled here.

Saku Koivu

I am settled. Montreal was a long time. I was captain there. That is... that is a life. Minnesota is a hockey room. Manny is a good man. Wes is a good man. Pierre-Marc, he is young but he listens. I am here. It is fine.

Jan Kowalczyk

Let me ask about the contract. You have two years and $6.84M left. Some men in their 31st year with two years left, they play differently. They think about what comes after. Is that you?

Saku Koivu

I think about what comes after every night I sleep. But not when I skate. I cannot skate and think about money. It does not work. I tried once. I turned the wrong way.

Jan Kowalczyk

So the contract does not make you change your game? You are not going to take a hit along the boards and make them remember you.

Saku Koivu

You know, people look at my number and they say he is not tough. FIGH 50, TOUG 50. I know what it says. But I do not play that way. I never played that way. I pass. I skate. Two years left, I will still pass and skate. That is what I am good at. If I try to be somebody else, I am just bad.

Jan Kowalczyk

Fair enough. Who do you watch and think, that is the way to play at 31, 32, that is the way to keep your game?

Saku Koivu

Fozy.

Jan Kowalczyk

Peter Forsberg.

Saku Koivu

I played against him since we were... Since we were young men in Europe. He is 32 now. He has 29 points in 21 games for Colorado. He passes like he is seeing the play before it happens. And his body... his body has hurt him as much as mine. Maybe more. And he is still out there doing it. I respect that. That is the man I watch.

Jan Kowalczyk

You have been matched against each other for years. What is the thing about playing against Forsberg that you never got used to?

Saku Koivu

His eyes. He looks at you and he knows. He knows where you are going before you know. I used to think I could speed past him. You cannot. You pass around him or you do not. There is no speed past him.

Jan Kowalczyk

Colorado has him and Kariya and Hejduk. That is a line that puts up 85 points between three men right now. Do you think Minnesota can build something like that around you and Richard?

Saku Koivu

I do not know what they build around me. That is not my job. I just... I just try to be at my base. If I am at my base, Richard gets more points. That is the job.

Jan Kowalczyk

So the goal this year, simply, is to get the Index back to 88?

Saku Koivu

The goal is to be on the ice Friday. Then Saturday. Then the next game. The number, it will come. It always comes when the body comes.

Jan Kowalczyk

Saku Koivu, thank you very much. Good luck Friday.

Saku Koivu

Thank you.
