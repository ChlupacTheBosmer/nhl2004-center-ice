Forsberg: 'My one point is enough tonight'

The Avalanche's leading scorer takes a quiet night in a 5-1 road win and says he would take the five goals from anyone if the room keeps feeling like it does now.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelWednesday, 16 March 2005

1:55 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Peter Forsberg95 had 1 assist in Colorado Avalanche's 5-1 win on the road at Columbus Blue Jackets on 2005-03-13, a rare quiet night for a man sitting at 111 points in 70 games. Jan Kowalczyk caught him in the corridor afterward, where the topic was not his own line but the room around him.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here in the tunnel at Nationwide Arena, with Peter Forsberg. Peter, five different guys on the scoresheet tonight. Tanguay with two goals, Selanne three points. What does that kind of night look like from the bench?

Peter Forsberg

I think we played good. Yes. Alex, he was everywhere tonight, two goals. And Teemu, he is just, I do not know how he does it, three points and he never looks tired. The game was won by many people. That is the good thing.

Jan Kowalczyk

You had just the one point yourself tonight. For a man sitting at 111 in 70, that is a quiet night. Does it bother you?

Peter Forsberg

No. No, of course not. My point is there. I think... when I had 53 goals I was thinking, this is a lot of goals, but I do not care tonight. I care that we win 5-1 on the road. My points, they will be there in April.

Jan Kowalczyk

Jamie Storr only faced 18 shots. What does that feel like from a skater's view? You are out there, and you know the goalie is barely working.

Peter Forsberg

It is... good for him. Yes. You want to give him that. Eighteen pucks and he sees every one of them. The backcheck, the clear, it was simple tonight. And Storr, he is comfortable when he sees pucks clean. So I think we did the things he needs. It is his game too.

Jan Kowalczyk

Last time we talked, in January, you had just dropped from 23 and 9 to 23 and 13. You were frustrated about tight games turning. Now here you are, 38 and 21, 1st in the Northwest, 93 points.

Peter Forsberg

Yeah. You remember that, of course. It feels... different now. We lost some games, sure, in January I was... but this group, we just kept pushing. It is not a big run you can point to. It is just, every game from now is important. And we are in a good spot. For sure.

Jan Kowalczyk

You are 25 and 10 on the road. That is the best record on the road in the league. What makes this group travel well?

Peter Forsberg

I think... we like it, maybe. The room is good when we travel. Everyone knows their job. We get on the bus, we go, we play. It is not so complicated. And tonight... when you go on the road and win 5-1, the flight home is, how do you say, easy.

Jan Kowalczyk

Peter, thank you. Enjoy the flight. Jan Kowalczyk, back to you.
