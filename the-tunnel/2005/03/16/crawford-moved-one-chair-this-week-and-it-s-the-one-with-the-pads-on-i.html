Crawford moved one chair this week, and it's the one with the pads on it

After a 3-1 loss to the Maple Leafs, the Vancouver coach explains the goaltending change, says the forward lines are untouched on purpose, and puts the two home losses where he thinks they belong.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelWednesday, 16 March 2005

3:40 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

The Vancouver Canucks Canucks lost 3-1 at home to Toronto on Tuesday night, their second straight defeat against a club riding a 7-game win streak. Marc Crawford, in his first appearance on this desk since 1999 behind Vancouver's bench, changed his starting goaltender this week and touched nothing else in the lineup. He told Marcus Bell why, and what he wants out of the ten games left.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here, on the bench in Vancouver with head coach Marc Crawford, a few minutes after a 3-1 loss to Toronto. Twenty-seven shots to seventeen, and the Maple Leafs have now won seven in a row. Coach, you had your looks. What was it tonight?

Marc Crawford

They're good, Marcus. They're 51 and 12 and change, they're giving up a hundred and seventy goals over eighty-something... a hundred and seventy in 72 games, and they're on a roll. That's the premise of the night. And sure, we outshot them, but we didn't have the puck enough at the times that matter, and when you get one in on a team like that, they make you earn the next one. We didn't.

Marcus Bell

Both angles out of this one. The special teams: power play, penalty kill. Did they cost you anything tonight?

Marc Crawford

The kills have been, I mean, the effort's there. It's the jumps. You come out of the box against a club that scores 342 goals, your first three seconds are everything, and tonight we were a beat late more than once. On ours, we've got to move it quicker. Toronto sits in a 1-3 and eats the middle of the ice, they dare you to chip it in, and we chipped it in. That feeds their gameplan more than ours.

Marcus Bell

The one lineup move you made this week was in net. Dan Cloutier out, Alexander Auld in. Dan's made 54 starts for you this year, 31 wins. Walk me through that one.

Marc Crawford

Look, Dan knows, and I'll leave the rest with him. It's the freshest head. Goalies hate that phrase, I know, it's every cliché in the book. But I've got a game Thursday night in Dallas, and I want the guy between the pipes who's in the moment. Auld's done nothing wrong all year. Eight and seven. He's ready.

Marcus Bell

The Bureau's Book has Cloutier at 86 and Auld at 79. Does anything like that factor into a call like this?

Marc Crawford

No. No, I watch the ice, you know? The grades are what they are. I've got two guys who've won me games this year. Dan's got thirty-one of them. I'm not putting a number on a goaltender's forehead and asking him to chase it.

Marcus Bell

Everything else stayed put. Nobody moved up, nobody moved down. That's deliberate?

Marc Crawford

It is. You build around Markus and Todd, and Brendan's earned the middle chair with the points he's put up, you leave that alone. The kids, the Sedins, they know their roles and the roles are working, Daniel's 51 points. I'm not shuffling the deck because we lost a hockey game to the Stanley Cup champions. The lines are our lines.

Marcus Bell

The bodies, though. Trevor's groin keeps him out until after April 13. Cajanek the 16th, Lindgren the 29th, Allen late April. Linden's 19 goals and 55 assists. How down is this room without him?

Marc Crawford

It's down, sure. Linden's the heartbeat of that room, everybody in it will tell you. But here's the thing, and I'll use it: the next man plays. That's not a bumper sticker, it's just the job. Mats is back the 29th, that helps. We're not going to look at the list of who's not here and call it even before the puck drops. We're 35 and 23 and change. That's not luck.

Marcus Bell

You start a two-game swing Thursday in Dallas, a 92-point club that's 26-9 at home, then San Jose Tuesday. Vancouver's 18-18 on the road, and you've just dropped two straight at home.

Marc Crawford

Which is the pitch for Thursday. We're a better road team than a couple of nights make us look. Dallas's building is loud, their record at home is no accident, they make you earn every inch. Fine. I'd rather go find out there than feel sorry for ourselves here. We'll get the legs back, we always do.

Marcus Bell

You're at 92 points, Colorado sits a point ahead. Ten games left. What does a good March look like from here?

Marc Crawford

Getting through ten games without putting another name on the injured list, mostly. That's the honest answer. The points are there, we're in the hunt, but the standings are for April men. I want ten close games and our structure back, the details, the small stuff. It's a cliché. I'm happy to keep using it.

Marcus Bell

One thing you fix by morning?

Marc Crawford

Puck management through the middle of the rink. The room's going to roll its eyes at that answer, they hear it every day. We give up too many rush looks against, and nobody on our schedule forgives it. Fix the details, the goals against follows. Thanks, Marcus.

Marcus Bell

Marc Crawford, thank you, coach. Marcus Bell at the bench in Vancouver, back to you.
