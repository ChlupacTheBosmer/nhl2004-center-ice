Michael Burt on 90 points, a broken leg and 10 days

The Lightning GM sits down 10 days from what the calendar has as the trade deadline with his best player out and the cheapest 90-point team in the league.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelWednesday, 9 March 2005

3:47 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Tampa Bay Lightning sits first in the Southeast with 90 points on a payroll of $47.75M, which works out to $0.53M per point, the 5th best rate in the league. They have six players on the shelf, including Vincent Lecavalier89, out with a leg until April 1. Marcus Bell caught the general manager in the studio.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Michael Bell here, and with me tonight, Michael Burt of the Tampa Bay Lightning® Lightning. Thanks for sitting down with me. You're 90 points, first in the Southeast, and the math says you're spending $0.53M per point, fifth cheapest in the league. That's the kind of number that makes other general managers jealous. You feel that?

Michael Burt

I feel a two-game slide, Marcus. I feel six guys in the trainer's room. You can look at the spreadsheet all day, and sure, the per-point number is fine, I'll give you that. But I'm sitting here with 68 games in and my best player has been watching from the press box for three weeks. The number looks clean. It doesn't feel clean.

Marcus Bell

Lecavalier's been out since mid-February. A leg, back April 1. You're 90 points without him.

Michael Burt

90 points without him, yeah. And that's the thing I come back to. The guys have responded. John's been good in net, 15 games, 87 overall, the kid's carrying us. But you know what the April 1 date does to me? It tells me he gets back for maybe three or four games before the deadline if that. Maybe. I've been counting backwards, and the number keeps coming out wrong.

Marcus Bell

Which brings us to March 19. Ten days. Do you make a move?

Michael Burt

That's the question I'm in the office about every single day right now. And here's where it gets complicated. We've got $32.28M in profit this season. We can afford to buy. Most clubs can't. But what am I buying? I'm buying depth I already have. I'm buying for a run that starts in April with a healthy Vincent, or I'm buying for a run that might need me to win with the group I've got right now. Those are different trades. Those are different risk profiles.

Marcus Bell

The practice rink went up in February. Between the 21st and the 23rd. Is the next dollar heading toward travel or the workout room? Because the travel is at zero and that's costing you points on the road, 20 and 14.

Michael Burt

You're reading the building, Marcus. Yeah. Travel's next. I've told ownership that. You look at our road record, 20 wins 14 losses, that's respectable but we've been on bad sleep, bad meals, the whole thing. The practice rink, that was the priority because the guys see it every day. Travel is invisible until you're exhausted in the third period in Vancouver or Edmonton. I know what zero costs us. I know the number. And I'm telling you, I'd rather be up at the trade deadline than up in the travel line on a Tuesday night.

Marcus Bell

So you're not spending it before March 19?

Michael Burt

I didn't say that. I said the travel line is what I owe the room. The deadline is a different thing. If a piece falls into my lap that makes us harder to play against in a seven-game series, I can call ownership tonight and the money's there. Most clubs can't do that. That's the advantage of a payroll at $47.75M returning 90 points. I have flexibility most people in this league don't. I'm just not going to spend it chasing something I already have.

Marcus Bell

Last season your playoff run was zero games. You're sitting here now, 1st in the division, 110 points in Toronto, this is a different year for the organization.

Michael Burt

It is. And I'll be honest, that's what keeps me from pushing the restart button on the whole thing. When you're zero, when you're 20-something losses deep, you can blow it up. You can sell picks. We're not in that spot. 16,180 a night at $79, the building is full and they're coming to see something. The guys in that room know they're one step from putting this organization on the map. I'm not going to get cute with it. We're going to try to make the playoffs with what we have and get Vincent back in time.

Marcus Bell

One last one. Grahame. He's in the last year of his deal, 87 overall, 15 games, morale at 100. Are you talking extension?

Michael Burt

We're talking. He's happy here, the man's at 100 morale, you can see it in how he plays. Whether that becomes an extension before the deadline or in the summer, I don't know yet. That's his side of the table as much as mine. But yeah. We're talking.

Marcus Bell

Michael Burt, general manager of the Tampa Bay Lightning® Lightning. Thank you for your time.

Michael Burt

Thank you, Marcus.
