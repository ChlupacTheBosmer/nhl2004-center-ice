Trotz, after Dallas: three straight, a thin middle, and the details he wants back

Nashville's coach took the road win, held the structure he liked early, and admitted the hole at center will not fill itself between now and April.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelWednesday, 9 March 2005

2:34 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Barry Trotz brought Nashville Predators into Dallas on 2005-03-07 and left with a 2-1 win, the Predators' third in a row and their 18th on the road this season. They sit on 90 points, top of the Central, with Vernon Fiddler out to 2005-05-12 and the middle of the ice to patch. Trotz spoke with The Tunnel's Marcus Bell in the corridor after the game.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here with Barry Trotz, head coach of the Predators, after the 2-1 win in Dallas. Barry, that's a Dallas club that's 25-9 on its own ice, and that's your third win in a row.

Barry Trotz

Yeah, and that's a hard 60 minutes, Marcus, you know. That building, they're 25-9 for a reason. I thought we were on our toes early. Managed the puck, got it deep, made them come the length of the rink. That's the structure we want. Then we got away from it.

Marcus Bell

So where did you get away from it?

Barry Trotz

Second period. We turned it over at our line, two, three times, and Vokoun had to make the saves. We talk about the details every day. When we're a five-man unit we're hard to play against. When we're five guys, we're not. That's not a secret in this room.

Marcus Bell

Vokoun's played 52 of your 67 games. 33-19. Does Lasak get more of a look in March?

Barry Trotz

Tomas is our guy. 33-19, and the record doesn't show the nights he stole for us. Jan's been good, 8-5 in his 15, we trust him. But you know, March, you ride the horse. We'll look after him. The schedule gives us a day here and there.

Marcus Bell

Fiddler's out to May 12 with the groin. That's a center out of your bottom six.

Barry Trotz

Yeah, Vern, that's, uh. You hate it for the kid. 49 games for us, he's a worker. So somebody has to eat those minutes. Greg Johnson moves up. He's played 30 games, 27 points, he knows where to stand in our end. And Sharp gets a look. Sharp's been patient.

Marcus Bell

Sharp hasn't played a game yet this season. Is that a real look, or is he here to practice?

Barry Trotz

He's here to play, or he wouldn't be here. Look, we'll see. You don't hand a young center the middle of a playoff race and pat him on the back. He has to take it. But he's earned the chance to take it.

Marcus Bell

You've got 18 road wins. Does that travel game become who you are in April?

Barry Trotz

It has to. We're not going to out-skill you every night. We're going to out-structure you, out-compete you, and hope our best players are our best players. That's who we are, Marcus. It's not glamorous. It works.

Marcus Bell

Anything you'd take back from the week?

Barry Trotz

The start against Minnesota, maybe. 4-1 looks fine on the board. We were loose in our end early and Tommy had to stand tall. Against a better team that's 2-1 the other way.

Marcus Bell

Four straight now, and the Central's yours. What's the ask this week?

Barry Trotz

Three. Three straight. Let's not hand us one we didn't win. The ask is the same. Montreal comes in Thursday. Rest, manage the puck, don't give them free ice. Then Pittsburgh, then San Jose. Same 60 minutes every night.

Marcus Bell

Last one. Fiddler's back in April. Until then, is the middle good enough?

Barry Trotz

It has to be. Everybody in this league is missing somebody. Dallas is missing four, Detroit's missing six. You don't get to wait. You put the next man in and you play. That's the whole job.

Marcus Bell

Barry, thank you. Barry Trotz, Predators head coach, after the 2-1 in Dallas. Back to you.
