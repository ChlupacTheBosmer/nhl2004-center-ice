Gaborik: 'We don't dominate tonight. Ed is Ed, that's what happens'

The winger went pointless as Toronto won its fourth straight on only 11 shots, and he spent the night crediting the goaltender and a teammate's goal.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelWednesday, 9 March 2005

2:06 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Marian Gaborik96 recorded no points as Toronto Maple Leafs beat Vancouver Canucks 3-1 at home on March 8. Toronto won its fourth straight to reach 110 points but took just 11 shots against Vancouver's 29.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here, rinkside after the game. Marian, three-one, a fourth straight, but you were outshot eleven to twenty-nine tonight. How does that one end the way it does?

Marian Gaborik

Belfour. That's, that's who. You know, we don't play our game tonight. First period, second, we chase them a little, we lose the puck in bad spots. But Ed makes every save they need and we, we put the ones in that we get. Eleven shots, three in. How you say, we take it.

Marcus Bell

Three different Toronto names on the scoresheet tonight. Petr Chlup scored. What did you see from him?

Marian Gaborik

Petr, he's, uh, he's been around the net lately. Tonight he's there and he buries it. You know, for our bench it's good, yeah. Not every goal goes through Mats or me. When a guy like Petr gets one, everyone on the bench, they light up. That's, that's how you want it.

Marcus Bell

Last time we spoke, in January, you'd scored your thirty-seventh and the team lost anyway. You told Jan nobody in that room cares about your goal total after a loss. Tonight zero on the sheet, but a win. Different feeling?

Marian Gaborik

Yeah. Different. I'm not, I'm not going to sit here and say I love zero points, you know? I want the puck, I want to score. But the team wins tonight. Three-one against a good team. My goals, they're there. Forty-six is not nothing. So, yeah, it's fine tonight.

Marcus Bell

Twenty-nine shots. Vancouver pressed all night. They're on a four-game losing streak, they needed points in this race. Did it get uncomfortable?

Marian Gaborik

A little, yeah. Last ten, maybe fifteen minutes, they just, they keep coming. An extra man here, another rush there, the shots keep rolling in. But our guys, we block, we clear, and Ed, what can I tell you. He's not giving you two or three in a night like that. He's not.

Marcus Bell

Four straight, a hundred and ten points. The first round is in everybody's mind by now. Do you think about April at all in March?

Marian Gaborik

Everyone thinks about April. But tonight, no. Tonight I think about the bus, and I think about tomorrow's skate. You know, you don't win the Cup in March. We just want to keep this going. Four is good. Five is better.

Marcus Bell

One last one. They clearly had a plan for your line tonight, twenty-nine shots and you were off the sheet. Matchup thing, or just a night where nothing drops?

Marian Gaborik

I think a little of both. Every team, they have a plan for us. Tonight maybe they do it better than usual. We'll watch the tape, we find the ice, it'll be different next time. That's how it works.

Marcus Bell

Good man. Thank you, Marian. Marcus Bell, back to you.
