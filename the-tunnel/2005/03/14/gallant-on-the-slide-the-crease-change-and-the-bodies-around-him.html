Gallant on the slide, the crease change, and the bodies around him

After a 4-1 home loss to Dallas, the Blue Jackets coach addresses a two-game losing streak, explains why Pascal Leclaire took the G1 job from Marc Denis, and talks about who steps up when the dressing room is thin.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelMonday, 14 March 2005

3:01 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Columbus Blue Jackets dropped another one on Saturday night, 1-4 to Dallas Stars at Nationwide Arena, their second straight loss in a stretch that has left them at 23-32-6, 68 points. Head coach Gerard Gallant met the media after the game and addressed the room's mood, a goaltending switch that saw Pascal Leclaire [1202] take over as G1 from Marc Denis [221], and the physical element he tried to inject by dressing Dennis Bonvie [89] over Adam Mair [602].

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here in the tunnel at Nationwide Arena with head coach Gerard Gallant. Coach, 4-1, second straight, and you're outshot 21-16 by a Dallas club with 92 points. What did you see tonight?

Gerard Gallant

We were flat. Look, Dallas is a good team, they've got 92 points for a reason, they skate, they move the puck well. But we gave them too much in the first period, I mean, we just didn't manage the puck the way we can. You turn it over at the blue line twice in the first ten minutes against a club like that and you're digging. We didn't get out of it.

Marcus Bell

You've won six of your last ten but the last two have been rough. What's the mood in that room right now?

Gerard Gallant

You know, the guys are frustrated. They should be frustrated. We're not playing to our capability and everybody in that room knows it. But frustrated's not the same as quit, and I've seen this group too many times now to mix those two up. They're competing. Tonight wasn't a compete issue, it was details, and that's the one you can fix tomorrow.

Marcus Bell

Let me ask about the crease. Pascal Leclaire's your G1 now. Marc Denis started the year as your number one, put up 25 games. What changed?

Gerard Gallant

Pascal's 43 games in and he's been our best goalie. It's that simple. He's got 84 overall on the Bureau's Book and Marc's got 82, the numbers line up with what I see every day. Marc's a good goalie, don't get me wrong, I'll take him in any shootout. But Pascal's been the better man for us this year, so that's the call.

Marcus Bell

You dressed Dennis Bonvie tonight over Adam Mair, who'd been in your lineup 65 times. Mair's got zero points on the season. Was that a message about production or about something else?

Gerard Gallant

You're not gonna get me to sit here and say Adam's not earning his spot. Adam's been in our lineup 65 nights, he's a pro, he does everything we ask. This is about tonight. Dallas has big bodies, they forecheck hard, and I wanted a guy out there who's gonna finish a check and make them pay for going to the corners. Dennis gives us that. Adam's not a healthy scratch, he's just not the right fit for what we needed tonight.

Marcus Bell

You moved Tyler Wright from wing to center on the third line, Josh Holden up to the fourth. You're trying to add depth down the middle?

Gerard Gallant

Tyler played center before he got here, we know that. We've got Marchant at one, Todd's been fantastic, 78 points, but behind him we're thin. Espen Knutsen's got 14 in 38 games, he's been in and out. So yeah, we're trying to get a guy out there at three who can win a draw and play a reasonable shift. Tyler can do that.

Marcus Bell

Coach, you look at the league tonight and Dallas has five guys out, Detroit has five, Chicago has five. You've got nobody on the injured list. Is that something you count on, or do you just not look at it?

Gerard Gallant

I mean, I look at it. You'd be stupid not to. When you've got a club like Detroit without Holmstrom, without Draper, their top two wingers are gone and it changes the whole matchup. We've been lucky, you know? Bodies have held. But you can't count on it. You plan for it. And right now my job is to have a man ready to step up if we lose one tonight, not hope it doesn't happen.

Marcus Bell

You play Atlanta twice this week, on the 15th and the 17th. They've lost two straight themselves. Is that your chance to get back in column W?

Gerard Gallant

It's two games. Look, Atlanta's got 72 points, they're a real club, they're not going to lay down for us just because they dropped a couple. We've got to worry about our own game, our own structure. If we manage the puck the way we can, if we're honest in the defensive zone, we can score on anybody. Geoff's got 108, Todd's got 78, we've got offense in there. It's the other end that's been the problem.

Marcus Bell

Coach, thank you for your time.

Gerard Gallant

Thanks, Marcus.
