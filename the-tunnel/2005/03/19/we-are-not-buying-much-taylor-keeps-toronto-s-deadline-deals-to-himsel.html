"We are not buying much": Taylor keeps Toronto's deadline deals to himself

The champion's GM says a few offers are still on the table and no big purchase is needed, then sends the contract questions to Gaborik, to Richards, and to Belfour himself.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelSaturday, 19 March 2005

5:28 · the reporter's voice is generated; the general manager's answers are his own, recorded live and unedited · download

ShareCopy link

Ed Taylor took the call on deadline day with Toronto Maple Leafs on 118 points, winners of 8 straight, and $23.25M in profit on a $60.04M payroll. He said the club had been "buying and selling all over throughout February" and had a few deals left on the table, with a deadline of hours still to run. Then he put the building's three loudest questions back where he wanted them: Marian Gaborik98 extended, Brad Richards87 on the same track, and the future of Ed Belfour93, 39, a 1.98 and a .929, left to Belfour.

You've got the best per-point payroll in the league at $0.51M a point, you've got money to spend, and you're the Cup champions on an eight-game win. Does that make you a buyer today?
