Forsberg's half second: the 56-goal season, explained in overtime

The Avalanche left wing, who had 34 goals last year, has 56 now, and says the coach told him to stop looking for the pass and start looking for the net.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelSaturday, 19 March 2005

1:38 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Peter Forsberg95 has 3 goals in his last 2 games and 56 for the season, more than he scored across all 82 games last year. Colorado Avalanche beat Washington Capitals 4-3 in overtime on 2005-03-16, their third straight win, and lead the Northwest Division with 97 points.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here with Peter Forsberg, right off the ice. Peter, 4-3 overtime, on the road, third one in a row. How are you?

Peter Forsberg

I think we played good tonight. Washington makes it hard, for sure. But we stayed with them. The room is happy.

Marcus Bell

33 to 23 in shots, ten more pucks on their net than on yours, and it still goes to overtime. Tell me about that OT shift.

Peter Forsberg

The first change, you just want the puck. You want to end it. And the ice, you know, it is open but nobody wants to be the one who makes the mistake. So it is, simple. Play simple. Make the right play. And we got it.

Marcus Bell

You have 3 goals in 2 games, all goals, no assists. 56 this season. You finished last year with 34. What changed?

Peter Forsberg

The coach wants me on net, more than last year. He has told me. That is for sure. And Joe is back, Paul is good right now, so the ice opens up for me. I just try to get there.

Marcus Bell

I asked you in January, you said you would trade your goals for two points. You still feel that?

Peter Forsberg

I still feel it. The number is, you see it, I know that. But the games in January, the ones we lost, I remember those. Of course I remember those more.

Marcus Bell

Did the coach change your assignment tonight? Your line, where you played out there?

Peter Forsberg

Yeah, he switched me. First period it was the usual. Then he put me another wing, different side, and after that I had more room. That is what made the difference, for sure.

Marcus Bell

Teemu Selanne, 3 goals and 5 points in these 2 as well. What does it look like from inside when you and Teemu are both hot?

Peter Forsberg

They cannot cover both. When Teemu is like this, every defenseman watches him. And that means I get a half second. A half second is everything for me, you know.

Marcus Bell

97 points, first in the Northwest, two weeks to go. What is the feeling in the building?

Peter Forsberg

One game. Practice, next game. You do not look at the table today. But tonight the room is good. That is all I can say.

Marcus Bell

Peter Forsberg, thank you for your time. Back to you.
