Stirling holds the lines: no shuffle, and a 23-year-old in the net

After a 5-4 loss in Toronto, Steve Stirling said he will not touch his top six, called the power play a problem of entries and not personnel, and named Rick DiPietro his starter for a week of 3 games in 6 days.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelSaturday, 19 March 2005

2:26 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Steve Stirling took New York Islanders into Toronto and outshot Toronto Maple Leafs 30-20; the board still read 5-4 the wrong way. His club has 74 points through 73 games and 3 games in the next 6 days: Montreal, New Jersey, Chicago. He stopped in the tunnel for Marcus Bell to talk about the lines he has left alone, the special teams he has not fixed, and Rick DiPietro84.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Coach, Marcus Bell, in the tunnel at Toronto. 5-4 on the board, 30 shots on goal. What did you see out there?

Steve Stirling

What did I see. 30 shots on the road. Most nights that's enough, you know. We had our looks, we had the puck. And then, uh, we gave them 2 they don't have to work for. That's the whole game right there. You give up 5, you're not winning.

Marcus Bell

2 wins before this one, both 5-4. Ottawa, then the Rangers. What changed tonight?

Steve Stirling

Nothing changed, that's the thing. In both of those we found a way, and that's the room, you know. Nobody quit in either one. Tonight we get our 30 shots and we don't find it. I'd rather we learn that now than in April.

Marcus Bell

Your forward lines haven't moved in a while now. Who's gone up, who's gone down, and why?

Steve Stirling

We haven't moved much, no. And that's me. I'm not gonna shuffle a room for the sake of shuffling it, you know. The guy who earns it moves up. Mark's got 32 goals, he's been on the right side all year, he stays there. Alex, when he's going, we're a different hockey team. That's 56 games for him, and that's the number I look at, honestly.

Marcus Bell

So nothing changed at all this week?

Steve Stirling

The top six, no. We've had bodies in and out. But the lines, I haven't touched them. If you want me to tell you I moved a guy to spark us, I didn't. We've got 74 points, there's hockey left, and I'm not gonna panic over one night in Toronto.

Marcus Bell

Special teams. Where is the power play for you right now?

Steve Stirling

We get our looks. For me it's the entries. When we come in clean and we set up, now we're playing, now we're a power play. When we're standing at the line waiting for somebody, we're 5 guys watching. So it's the details, the puck movement, a shot and a body at the net. And the kill, the kill's been honest. The guys who kill for us, they block 1, they get up, they block the next 1. That's the compete level I want.

Marcus Bell

Rick DiPietro's at 55 games. Is that your net every night now?

Steve Stirling

Rick's our guy. 55 games, 28 wins, and he's 23 years old. That's a lot to put on a kid. But Garth's a pro, he's been around a long time, he gives us a chance every time he's in there, and on a week like this we're gonna need him. That's not a knock on Rick. That's the schedule talking.

Marcus Bell

Papineau's out for you. What do you do in that room without him?

Steve Stirling

Justin's back, they tell me April 11. He's working every day, you know. I'm not gonna put a date on when he's himself again, but we get him back in April and that's a body for us.

Marcus Bell

3 games in 6 days. Montreal, New Jersey, Chicago.

Steve Stirling

Montreal's been playing hard. New Jersey's got 101 points, they don't hand you anything, ever. Then Chicago. It's the league, you know. 3 in 6 days. We manage the puck, we compete, and we find out where we are. I like our room.

Marcus Bell

Coach, thanks for the time. Marcus Bell, back to you.
