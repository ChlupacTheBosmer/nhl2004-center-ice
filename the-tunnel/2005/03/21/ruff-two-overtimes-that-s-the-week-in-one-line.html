Ruff: 'Two overtimes, that's the week in one line'

The Sabres took a win and an overtime point from the week, and Lindy Ruff said the third period in New York is the part that sticks.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelMonday, 21 March 2005

2:58 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Buffalo Sabres took a 4-3 overtime win over Philadelphia and a 5-4 overtime loss at the Rangers from a two-game week. The Sabres sit at 25-31-10 and 74 points with three home games ahead, Colorado, Florida and Montreal, and Andrew Brunette75, signed on March 17, is out with a back until April 24. Coach Lindy Ruff talked through the week with Marcus Bell.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell, at the rink with Lindy Ruff of the Buffalo Sabres. Lindy, two games this week, both of them into overtime, a win and a point. What do you take from it?

Lindy Ruff

You know, two overtimes, that's, uh, that's the week in one line. We beat Philly in our building, we go into New York and we get the point out of it. So I'll take that part. What I won't take is the third period in New York. We sat back, and, you know, that one's on me.

Marcus Bell

The shot clock went against you both nights. 27 to 23 against Philly, 28 to 25 against the Rangers.

Lindy Ruff

Shot counts, I mean, you can feel good or bad about your night looking at them. Philly had 27, we had 23, and we won the game. So what do they tell you. For me it's the turnovers. When we manage the puck, we're hard to play against. When we give it away at their blue line, we spend the night in our own end. That's the detail I keep coming back to.

Marcus Bell

Marty Biron's at 59 starts, 24 and 30. Ryan Miller, 5 and 10 in 17 games. Mika Noronen, 3 and 1 in 8. Who gets the net the rest of the way?

Lindy Ruff

It's Marty's net. I don't love that question, and I'll tell you why. It puts a number on a man. Marty's played 59 games for us, he's given us a chance most nights. Ryan's 17 games into his career, he's going to be a good one. Mika, he's won three of his eight. I've got three goalies I trust. I'll pick the one who gives us the best chance that night.

Marcus Bell

You signed Andrew Brunette on the 17th, $111,150, and now he's out with a back. The date on him is April 24. What's the update?

Lindy Ruff

Bruner's back. That's the update, it's a back, and the date's April 24. He came in to help the room and the body didn't cooperate. He's still around the young guys, he's good that way, and that still counts for something.

Marcus Bell

You talk about compete level most weeks. What did you like this week, and what didn't you?

Lindy Ruff

What I liked was the start in New York. We were on our toes in the first, we forechecked, we made them move the puck fast. What I didn't like was after that. The legs stop, the puck starts going the other way. You can't watch a game in this league. You have to go get it.

Marcus Bell

Did the lines stay together this week?

Lindy Ruff

Mostly. I moved a couple of guys up for a shift or two, that's about it. You don't want to, uh, you don't want to shuffle everything when the guys are working. I'll change the lines when the lines tell me to.

Marcus Bell

Three at home now. Colorado Thursday, 99 points and four straight. Then Florida, then Montreal. What's the test?

Lindy Ruff

Colorado's the best the West has right now, that's a measuring stick, and you want that. Then Florida, then Montreal. We're 18 and 18 in our own building. That's not good enough for me. Here's three chances to fix it.

Marcus Bell

74 points, 25 and 31. The playoffs are out of reach. What are you playing for?

Lindy Ruff

The math is the math, you're right. I don't coach for April. I coach for that room. There's men in there playing for contracts, playing for jobs, playing because they've done it their whole life. And there's people paying to sit in our building. We owe them a compete level. Every night.

Marcus Bell

Ryan Miller, 5 and 10 in 17 games. What do you learn about a young goalie in a season like this?

Lindy Ruff

You learn plenty. 5 and 10, that's a team record, that's not on him. What I watch is the next shot. He gives one up, does he come back the next shift and stop the next one. Ryan does. That's what you want.

Marcus Bell

Lindy, thanks for the time. Lindy Ruff, head coach of the Buffalo Sabres. Back to you in the studio.
