Gaborik: You Hear Everything in Columbus, and That Helps

The right winger on a 9-game win streak says the quiet road buildings sharpen his line more than the loud ones do.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelMonday, 21 March 2005

2:29 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Marian Gaborik96 scored once as Toronto Maple Leafs won 3-1 on the road at Columbus Blue Jackets on 2005-03-20, extending their win streak to 9 and their road record to 28-9. He sits at 49 goals and 87 points in 74 games, with the Bureau's Development Index at +6 above his base.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here with Marian Gaborik, right after a 3-1 win on the road. Marian, this building, Columbus, you get a different feel here than at other road stops tonight. What did you hear out there?

Marian Gaborik

Yeah, it's quiet here, you know. Not quiet like nobody come, but quiet like you can, you can hear the bench when somebody call a name. In Toronto, in Boston, big place, you just play, you don't hear nothing. Here I hear Mats say, you know, he call for the puck and I know he's open. It's, how you say, it make you listen more.

Jan Kowalczyk

You're 28-9 on the road this year. Nine in a row. Is there something about the quieter buildings that suits your game specifically?

Marian Gaborik

Yeah, maybe, you know. When the building get loud you just go, you don't think about where your man is. But here, you hear Gary call the draw, you hear who switch, so I know before, I know the space is open before the puck come. So it help me, yeah. I get behind the defense easier when I hear what they do.

Jan Kowalczyk

Mats had two tonight. Gary had two assists. Talk to me about that line and how you've found each other over this stretch.

Marian Gaborik

Mats is, yeah, he is something else, you know. He is so smart with the puck, he wait, he wait for the last second and then, boom, he give it and the goal is there. Gary, Gary is, he just, he fight for everybody. He go in the corner and he come out with it, and then he look up and Mats is open, and I am open behind. It is, we trust each other now, you know. Early in the year we were, how you say, still learning the rhythm. Now we just go.

Jan Kowalczyk

Your goal tonight, walk me through it. You got behind the defense, one-on-one with the goalie.

Marian Gaborik

Yeah. Mats, he see me. I go, I just go, and he put it there, and I shoot, top corner, that's it. Nothink. I don't think when I go like that, I just go. Sometimes you think too much and the D-man read you. Tonight I just go and the puck is in.

Jan Kowalczyk

Marian, you've been here since before the season started, you've been here the whole year. You're 49 goals on the season, your line is humming, you're nine straight wins. How comfortable are you in that room right now?

Marian Gaborik

I am good, yeah. I am good here, you know. The room, the guys, we play together a lot now. It is not, it is not like September when you still find, who is who. Now I know Mats go here, Gary go there, I go here. We just, we just run. And we know we play good in April. That is what matter.

Jan Kowalczyk

Nine in a row, the Presidents' Trophy is done, you're sitting pretty. Does the room feel it, or is it business as usual?

Marian Gaborik

Business as usual, Jan. Nobody in there talk about the table, I promise you. We talk about the next shift, the next game. Columbus is done now, tomorrow we fly to, where we go, I don't know. But we just go. That is all.

Jan Kowalczyk

Marian Gaborik, thank you. Back to you.
