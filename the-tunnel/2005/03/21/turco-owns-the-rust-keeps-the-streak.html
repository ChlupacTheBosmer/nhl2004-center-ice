Turco Owns the Rust, Keeps the Streak

The Stars goaltender, back to a 96 overall after the December absence, talks about who covered his net and who's covering the room's.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelMonday, 21 March 2005

2:58 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Marty Turco93 finished with 20 saves on 23 shots as Dallas Stars beat Atlanta Thrashers 4-3 on the road Saturday. The Stars goaltender is 37-22-1 with 61.0 minutes a night this season, sitting one spot up on the Bureau's Development Index at 96 overall, and he has been candid all year about his own inconsistency. The win leaves Dallas at 97 points and a 4-game heater heading into the closing stretch in the Pacific.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk down here in the corridor with Marty Turco. 4-3, a two-game win streak, and it's your 37th. Twenty-three shots. Does it feel like the closer one on the ice?

Marty Turco

Yeah, it's close the whole night. Twenty-three doesn't sound like a lot, you know, but they were getting, uh, the second and third chances. And when they do that, three of them go in and it feels like you worked for forty. So yeah. Close.

Jan Kowalczyk

You mentioned back in December when you were coming back from the brief absence, you felt a little rusty at first. That was, what, eleven weeks ago now. Have you felt the rhythm come all the way back?

Marty Turco

Yeah. Yeah, honestly I have. You know, the couple starts when I came back, I was, I was late on some things. And Ron , you gotta give him credit, he'd been going and he was carrying us while I was out. He's 86 in the book, he's earned it. And then it kind of, uh, locks back in. I think I've been better since January. You know, the Index thing, the plus-one, I don't sit around looking at that, but I feel good in the crease. The legs are there.

Jan Kowalczyk

The room has been thin for stretches. Tonight you've got Mark Messier on the scoresheet, Pierre Turgeon, Sergei Zubov, all got their names on a goal. Four different scorers. How much of that is the next guy up when bodies are missing?

Marty Turco

It's everything. You know, you can't lean on Mike every night, even a guy like Mike. And when a guy goes down, you need the third and fourth lines to be a little more. And tonight they were. Mark, he scores, the guy's been doing that longer than I've been alive, basically. And Pierre, and Zubov. So yeah. You don't replace a guy one for one, Jan, you all just get a little bigger. That's the Stars way.

Jan Kowalczyk

You know your own number. The Bureau's got you at 51 on consistency. It's the lowest thing on your card by a mile. Do you think about that between periods?

Marty Turco

I think about my games between periods. And yeah, I know what it says. I've always been streaky. Everyone in this league who's been around me for a minute knows it. I can be, I don't know, 92 one week and 99 the next. And the 99s, those are the nights that win you games. So, uh, I'll take the criticism on the consistency number if we're 37 wins deep in March. That's, that's the trade I've made and I've made it honestly.

Jan Kowalczyk

Last time we talked, January 1st, you told me the home-road gap was legs and sleep. You're 28 and 9 on the road this year, which is, well, better than you were talking about back then. Did anything actually change in the travel line?

Marty Turco

Yeah. Yeah, actually it did, sort of. Not the travel itself, I mean, you still fly, you still wake up in somebody else's building. But, you know, we got a little more rest in the schedule, a few more back-to-backs came off, and guys stopped, uh, stopping in the hotel bar at midnight. I'm kidding. Mostly I'm kidding. But the room is, it's focused now. We're 97 points and we know we've got work to do in April.

Jan Kowalczyk

One last. You're a year left on the contract, $0.60M. I'm not asking about that. I'm asking: does that number bother you, or does it not exist in the building?

Marty Turco

You know, it doesn't exist in the building. I got a chance to be a starter at 26 and that's, that was the deal I wanted. The money's fine. It's, uh, it's my game and I'm playing it every night and the team's winning. You know what I mean? The contract's the last thing I think about at 10 p.m.

Jan Kowalczyk

Marty Turco, thanks for your time. Back to you.
