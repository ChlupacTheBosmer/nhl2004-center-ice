Gerard Gallant on the Detroit win and the homestand ahead

Columbus beat Detroit 2-1 and got outshot 23-20 doing it; Vancouver, Calgary and Dallas visit in the next six days.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelSaturday, 5 March 2005

2:31 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Gerard Gallant, head coach of Columbus Blue Jackets, met Marcus Bell after his club's 2-1 win over Detroit Red Wings on 2005-03-03. Columbus has 64 points, 189 goals for and 196 against, and three home games in the week ahead, against Vancouver, Calgary and Dallas.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell, Columbus. Coach, 2-1 over Detroit. That's the team ahead of you in this division and you got them at home.

Gerard Gallant

Yeah, Marcus. Good win, good hockey team over there, and we found a way. Two-one. You know, they got 23 shots on us, and 23 against is a number we can live with. When we're down there, we're in games.

Marcus Bell

You'd won two of nine before this one. What does a single night change?

Gerard Gallant

It changes the room for a night. That's all it changes. We're at 64 points, we've scored 189 and given up 196. That's a minus, and it's earned. One Thursday doesn't fix it.

Marcus Bell

Leclaire's played 41 games, Denis 23. Down the stretch, is that still the split?

Gerard Gallant

Pascal's earned it. He's 22 years old and he's playing like he belongs in there. Marc's a pro, he's won 10 games for us, he stays ready. We go game to game. I'm not handing out starts in March.

Marcus Bell

Marc's deal is up after this season. Do you want an answer on him before June?

Gerard Gallant

That's not mine, Marcus. That's upstairs. I coach the goalies I've got. Ask me in June.

Marcus Bell

Sanderson's got 57 goals and 105 points in 65 games. What's he doing that's different this year?

Gerard Gallant

Different? He's the same guy, he just gets there. Fifty-seven goals, that's a lot of nights in front of the net. Marchant's got 75 points feeding him, Vyborny's got 64. Those two don't get enough credit. A scorer still needs somebody to get him the puck, you know.

Marcus Bell

Kent McDonell is out. Is that the only body you're missing?

Gerard Gallant

Kent, yeah. Back on the 9th. That's it. Everybody else is dressed and available, so we're healthy enough.

Marcus Bell

Vancouver Sunday, Calgary Tuesday, Dallas Friday, all three in your building.

Gerard Gallant

All three here. And we're 16-16 at home. .500 in our own rink, that won't get us anywhere. Vancouver's got 90 points, Dallas has 85. These are the nights where you find out what you've got.

Marcus Bell

You're at 64 points. Phoenix is at 68 and has won seven in a row. Is that the club you're chasing?

Gerard Gallant

I don't chase anybody's scores, I chase ours. But I'd be lying if I said we don't look. Sixty-eight points, seven straight, that's a hell of a run they're on. All I can do is win our games.

Marcus Bell

Detroit was missing five regulars, Draper and Datsyuk and Holmstrom among them. Does that take something off the win for you?

Gerard Gallant

I played a lot of years in that sweater, Marcus. I know what that room is like when it's short. Nobody feels sorry for you in March. We played against who was in front of us.

Marcus Bell

Do you shuffle anything after a win like that? The lines, the power play?

Gerard Gallant

Why would I? You don't change a winning lineup. The lines hold. It's the details we work on, the little things that cost us in the ones we lost.

Marcus Bell

Coach, thanks for your time. Marcus Bell in Columbus, back to you.
