Tippett on a four-game homestand and the hole Boucher leaves on the blue line

The Dallas coach talks through a schedule that could seal the Pacific, and a defense corps that has to survive without its second-highest scorer until April.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelWednesday, 2 March 2005

3:23 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Dallas Stars beat Buffalo 3-1 on February 28, the second win in a row for a club that has surrendered 162 goals in 65 games, the fewest in the league. Head coach Dave Tippett spoke with The Tunnel's Marcus Bell before tonight's game against Pittsburgh, the first of four straight at home.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here at the arena with Dave Tippett. Coach, 3-1 on the road at Buffalo Monday night. They've lost eight straight so it wasn't the toughest test on the calendar, but how did you feel about the way your group handled it?

Dave Tippett

Well, you know, we got two points. That's what you're after on the road, especially when you're going into a building that's struggling. I thought our structure was there most of the night. We didn't give them a ton of grade-A stuff. You take the win, you move on.

Marcus Bell

And tonight you're back home. You've got four straight at home this week, starting with Pittsburgh tonight, then Chicago Friday, Nashville Saturday, St. Louis the following Wednesday. What's your read on this stretch?

Dave Tippett

Obviously it's a good stretch. We're 24 and 7 here, you know, this building's been good to us all year. Four games in front of our own crowd, that's, that's an opportunity. But you can't look past Pittsburgh. They've got speed, they've got guys who can change a game on any night. We'll worry about Saturday when Saturday gets here.

Marcus Bell

Nashville is the one people will be watching. 84 points for them, 85 for you. One point separates the top of the Pacific from the top of the Central, essentially, in the race for the higher seed. Do you think of it that way?

Dave Tippett

I think about winning the game in front of me. You know what I mean. But look, I'm not an idiot. Everybody in that dressing room knows where they are in the standings. You don't have to say it. When Nashville comes to town, you don't have to put up a whiteboard. They know.

Marcus Bell

Coach, let me ask you about Philippe Boucher. He went down with a back injury late in February, the league has him out until April 5. That's a defenseman who's put up 46 points, 37 assists. How do you fill that without him?

Dave Tippett

Well, it's a big one. You lose a guy who's second among our defensemen in points, who moves the puck well, who's good for us on the power play. So the answer is, everyone has to step up. Zubov's going to eat some more minutes, there's no question. But Stephane's been playing every game, 65 games. Richie's been every game too. Those two are going to have to carry a little more, and the young guys, Erskine, Berenzweig, they've got to be ready to chip in on the power play and on the penalty kill where we used Phil.

Marcus Bell

Sergei Zubov is 34. He's played all 65 games, 56 points. You're asking him to carry more. Is there a limit, do you have to manage his minutes?

Dave Tippett

You manage everybody's minutes. That's the job. And Zubov, he's a pro, he takes care of himself. But you know, we've got 17 games left, and I need him fresh in April. So yeah, we'll watch it. We'll get the others more reps on the first power-play unit. That's how you get through it. You don't replace a guy like Phil with one man. You do it by committee.

Marcus Bell

You've got the best goals-against in the league. 162 in 65 games. That's a real number. How much of that is the system and how much is the guys in front of you?

Dave Tippett

Oh, it's both, isn't it? You know, that's the old question. The structure gets us to the point where the goaltender's not facing a million grade-A chances. But then Marty's been, he's been outstanding. 53 games. And Ronny came in and did his job when we needed him. And the guys in front of us, the compete level, the backchecking, Guerin backchecks, Modano backchecks. That's how you get to 162.

Marcus Bell

Marty Turco's back in the number one spot now. You ran Tugnutt through the last stretch and now Turco's back in G1. How do you think about that call down the stretch?

Dave Tippett

Ronny's a good goalie. He won games for us. No disrespect, he did what we asked. But Marty's been our guy all year, 82 percent of our games. You don't go away from that with 17 to go unless something breaks. It's as simple as that.

Marcus Bell

One last one. You've got 17 games left, 85 points, you're in first in the Pacific. What's the target from here?

Dave Tippett

Win hockey games. That's what I say every year. But, you know, you want points. Every point you get now is one less you need in March. And we've got the home ice, we've got the health, mostly. We'll just keep pushing and see where the math lands us.

Marcus Bell

Dave Tippett, thank you for your time, coach. Back to you.
