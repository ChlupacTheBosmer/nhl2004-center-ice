Gaborik: 'I don't look at the board'

The Leafs' right wing talks about Belfour's calm, his quiet night, and why 102 points don't exist on a loss night.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelWednesday, 2 March 2005

1:59 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Marian Gaborik98 had no points in Toronto Maple Leafs's 1-2 home loss to Carolina on Tuesday. The Maple Leafs sit at 102 points with 17 games left, 28 ahead of Boston in the Northeast, but Gaborik wasn't interested in the table on a loss night.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here in the corridor with Marian Gaborik. Marian, one and two at home to Carolina. The league's best record and you drop this one. What's the room like right now?

Marian Gaborik

Yeah, it's not good. We play, I mean, the second period we lose the puck, how you say, easy, and, and two goals. Not enough. You know, normally we win these.

Jan Kowalczyk

Ed Belfour was in net tonight. Roberto wasn't in the lineup. What does it change for you up ice when it's Ed back there?

Marian Gaborik

Ed is... you know, he's calm. He won this whole thing last year, the Cup, so he, you know, he don't get nervous. I look back and it's the same like nothing, like a regular night. That helps the defense, maybe. Helps me too, I think. I just shoot and I forget, how you say, the goal, I go next play.

Jan Kowalczyk

You're held without a point. Nineteen shots your way, Mats gets the one goal, Gary the assist. When your name isn't on the board, does the game feel different out there?

Marian Gaborik

Well, you know, I try to play. I don't look at the board, because if I look I play bad. So I think, next shift, next one. Tonight maybe the puck didn't come, maybe I don't, I don't get there. Tomorrow we play, we go again.

Jan Kowalczyk

I asked you in January about how this room resets after a night where the shots don't come with points. You told me the loss is on Toronto, not on the ice. Same answer tonight?

Marian Gaborik

You remember that. Yeah, yeah, same. Nobody in the room is happy when we lose. Mats, Gary, Ed, nobody. So we, we come tomorrow and we do it again. We don't talk big. We go.

Jan Kowalczyk

Seventeen games left, 102 points, 28 ahead of Boston in the division. Does any of that sit on your mind this far in?

Marian Gaborik

I don't look at the table. Not after we lose one and two. Maybe you want me to talk about 102, I don't know. But tonight I care about Carolina. I care about those two goals. That's all.

Jan Kowalczyk

Fair enough. The hockey then, not the standings. What does the rest of this schedule ask of you, Marian?

Marian Gaborik

Yeah, okay. You know, more games, more road. We play good teams, hard teams, every night. So I need my, I need the legs, every night, the speed. That's all I can do. Seventeen games, seventeen games we go hard and then, then April.

Jan Kowalczyk

Marian Gaborik, thank you for your time. Back to you.
