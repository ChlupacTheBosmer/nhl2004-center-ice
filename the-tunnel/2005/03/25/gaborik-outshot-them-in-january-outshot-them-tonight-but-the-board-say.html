Gaborik: 'Outshot them in January, outshot them tonight, but the board says 5-1'

Down from 131 points last season to 91 this one, the winger says less ice and a different read have sharpened the game that has TOR eleven wins deep.

Marian Gaborik99 finished with 1 assist in Toronto Maple Leafs's 5-1 road win at Boston on 2005-03-24, the 11th straight for the Maple Leafs. His 91 points sit well below the 131 he put up last season, but the Bureau's Development Index has him +6 on his base grade, and his club leads the league at 124 points.

Marcus Bell here with Marian Gaborik. Marian, 5-1, your eleventh straight. Boston outshot you 27 to 25. That score looked a lot easier than the game must have felt.

Yeah. You saw it, right? They had more shots. The first period it was, how you say, a little bit tight. They came out, they were fast, the crowd was loud. But we, you know, we stayed patient. We didn't, uh, try to make the big play. Just, just first one, second one, and the goals come.

Marian Gaborik

Let me ask about the numbers. You're at 91 points tonight. Last season it was 131. A 40-point gap. The Index has you at 96, your best grade. So you feel better but you're not putting up what you used to?

Marian Gaborik

Yeah. It is different. Last year I shoot, I shoot a lot. Maybe too much. This year I look, you know, I look for Chlup, for Gary, for Mogilny. The pass first. And so the goals go down, a little. But the team, the team wins more. So I don't, I don't mind.

Marcus Bell

But 50 goals is still 50 goals. You haven't stopped being the guy who can score from nowhere.

Marian Gaborik

Well, that is, that is the one thing. I know I have it. Tonight I didn't score but, you know, I had the chance, maybe two, maybe. The puck is, it doesn't go tonight, it goes tomorrow. The game is different when you win eleven straight. You don't press. You just, you just make the right play and the board, the board takes care of you.

Marcus Bell

You've been asked in this building before about the crowd. You told Jan after the January game here, outshot 32, that the crowd lifts them. Tonight they had the shots again but you came out with five.

Marian Gaborik

Marcus Bell

Marian, five in a row on the road, 29 wins away from home this season. What does a team that is 29-9 on the road do differently in someone else's building?

Marian Gaborik

Well, we just, you know, we don't listen. I don't hear the crowd, I hear my, the bench, and that is all. If you listen to them, you make a bad play. And so we just play our game, simple, fast, first shift hard. That is the whole thing.
