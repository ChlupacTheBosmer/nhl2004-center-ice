"I want the coach to say my name when the game is on the line": Pierre-Marc Bouchard on 11.5 minutes and the 75 games nobody puts on a highlight

The Wild's 20-year-old center has the Bureau's second-highest potential among MIN's forwards at 88, but his proudest number this season is one the box score barely notices.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelFriday, 25 March 2005

4:48 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Pierre-Marc Bouchard79 came into this season with 36 games and 10 points behind him. In his third NHL year he has played 75 games for Minnesota Wild, scoring 14 goals and 29 points on 11.5 minutes a night, and the Bureau's Development Index has him +6 over a base grade of 74, overall 77. Marcus Bell sat down with him in the studio.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here, studio set, with Pierre-Marc Bouchard of the Minnesota Wild. Pierre-Marc, thanks for giving me the afternoon. I want to start where the Sweater started. Potential 88, Minutes 11.5. Third-center ice on a first-center game. Does that sit right with you?

Pierre-Marc Bouchard

Well... it sits, for sure. I want to play more, yeah. I'm not going to tell you I don't. But I look at who's in front of me. Saku's Saku, right? And Wes, he's... he's been around. He's a great hockey player. So I'm the third center. I know what that is. I'm 20.

Marcus Bell

You played 36 games last season. Half a season. What happened with that?

Pierre-Marc Bouchard

Well, I think... I was trying too much. First year I played 50 games, and I thought, okay, I'm here now, I'm the guy. And last year I wasn't the guy the coach wanted. So he sat me. And I was on the bench thinking, why not me, why not me, and the whole season goes by like that. And I was 19. And you're 19 and you don't know how to be patient.

Marcus Bell

What did you take from those 36 games?

Pierre-Marc Bouchard

That you can't think about it. You can't think about the minutes, the lines, the... you just get ready and when they call your name you go. That's it. We have to be better, that's it. And now I just want to be here. 75 games is good, I want 82. That's the whole thing.

Marcus Bell

75 games is the best number on your sheet this year. Is that the record you're proudest of?

Pierre-Marc Bouchard

Yeah. Yeah, I think so. Because everybody looks at the goals, the points. 14 and 15, 29. That's... that's not the number for a guy who went 8th overall. Everyone knows that. I know that. But 75 games? You gotta be there. Every night. And this year I was there. That's the number I'm proud of.

Marcus Bell

The Bureau has you +6 on the Development Index. 77 overall against a base of 74. Do you pay attention to that?

Pierre-Marc Bouchard

Not really. Some of the guys they check, you know, they want to see if they're up or down. I don't... I just want to score more. 29 points, I can do better. I know I can.

Marcus Bell

You've got speed 90, deking 88, passing 88. That's the front of the card. Then you flip it over and it's fighting 50, toughness 50, checking 52. You see those numbers too.

Pierre-Marc Bouchard

Yeah. I'm 165, you know? I can't be the guy... I can't be hitting defensemen into the boards. But I think you can be harder without hitting. You can be in the corner and keep the puck. You can... you can be there when they come at you and not just... not just slide by. That's what I work on. Not the fighting. The being there.

Marcus Bell

You play behind Saku Koivu every day. What do you take from him?

Pierre-Marc Bouchard

He's calm. That's the thing. Everybody's running around and Saku is just... calm. He knows where the puck is going before it goes there. And on the bench he's funny, he's always joking, the guys love him. But when he steps on the ice it's, it's different. I watch him and I try to be... to find that. That calm.

Marcus Bell

And Wes Walz. You share the minutes with him and he's ahead of you on the depth chart. What's it like playing with Wes?

Pierre-Marc Bouchard

Wes is good, man. He wins his faceoffs, he's always, always in the right spot. And he doesn't try to do too much. That's... that's a smart thing. He gets the puck to me in a good position and I can use it. For sure I learn from that. Don't try to be the guy who does everything. Just do your job and, and find your man.

Marcus Bell

You scored 5 goals last year. You've got 14 this year. What's different?

Pierre-Marc Bouchard

I play more, for sure. 75 games, 11.5 minutes, it's... it's more ice than 36 games at 11. But also I think I'm more comfortable. First year, second year, I was always thinking, should I pass, should I shoot, what's the right play? And now I just... I shoot. Some go in, some don't. But I shoot more. That's why I have 14.

Marcus Bell

Speed 90. Is that what people see first? Is that the first thing a fan says to you?

Pierre-Marc Bouchard

Yeah, the speed, the speed, always the speed. And okay, yeah, I have it. I won't say I don't. But I think the hands, the... the deking, the passing, that's where it matters. Speed gets you there. But then you gotta make the play. And that's, that's the next level for me. The next play. I think about that a lot.

Marcus Bell

One year left on your contract. $1.20M. You know what a contract year means in this league.

Pierre-Marc Bouchard

Yeah. I know. But I'm not thinking about it. I'm 20, Marcus. I'm thinking about getting to 82 games. That's my, that's my contract plan.

Marcus Bell

82 games and how many goals next year?

Pierre-Marc Bouchard

30? Yeah, I'd like 30. I know, I know. But if I don't say 30, I'm not going to try for it. And that's... that's what they think I can be, right? The potential, 88. So I say 30 and I try for 30.

Marcus Bell

You grew up in Sherbrooke. You played Pee-Wee in '98 with College-Français Rive-Sud. Does that kid and this guy feel like the same person?

Pierre-Marc Bouchard

No. No, that kid was so small. Like, tiny. And in Sherbrooke you had all the time in the world. You get the puck, you look, you find your buddy, you make the play. And here, here you don't have that. You get the puck and it's... gone. You gotta think before it gets to you. That took me, I don't know, 2 years maybe. Now I'm getting better at it.

Marcus Bell

Third season in this league. What does Pierre-Marc Bouchard want to be on this team next year?

Pierre-Marc Bouchard

I want to be important. That's what I want. I don't care, first line, third line, it doesn't matter to me. I just want... I want the coach to say my name when the game is on the line. That's the thing. And the rest, the rest I can wait for.

Marcus Bell

Pierre-Marc Bouchard of the Minnesota Wild. Thank you for the afternoon, Pierre-Marc. Back to you.
