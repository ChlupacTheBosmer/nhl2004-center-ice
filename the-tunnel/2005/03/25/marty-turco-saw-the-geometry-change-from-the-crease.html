Marty Turco saw the geometry change from the crease

The Stars' goaltender says a defensive pairing adjustment gave him cleaner looks in a 3-2 win, and the home-and-away gap hasn't closed because the road is the road

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelFriday, 25 March 2005

1:46 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Marty Turco94 finished with 14 saves on 16 shots as Dallas Stars edged San Jose Sharks 3-2 at home on 2005-03-23. The Stars sit 28-10 in their own building this season against 19-19 on the road. Turco, 29, is 38-23 with one shutout in 63 games.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here in the tunnel at Dallas. Marty, sixteen shots from San Jose, but they put two past you. From your crease, what's that kind of night?

Marty Turco

Yeah. Settled and lucky, is what it was. Both their goals were on rushes. The second one, I think, it deflected off a skate. I've probably got the rebound there but you know. You hate those. But we got the two. I've had games with thirty shots where I felt worse.

Jan Kowalczyk

You looked comfortable, though. Was something different in front of you tonight?

Marty Turco

Yeah. I think Dave moved, uh, he paired Zubov with Richards. Different side than what we've been running. And the geometry just, it's different. Sergei steps up, Richards covers the back door, and I've got fewer bodies in my sightline. You know, from where I'm sitting the gap between a clean catch and a scramble, it's that thin.

Jan Kowalczyk

Did he say anything to you about it?

Marty Turco

No, no. I just looked up, first shift, and I'm like, okay, Sergei's over there. And Richards is, he's a guy who wants to hit somebody. He's not backing in. So I trust the gap. You trust the body in front of you and the rest gets easier.

Jan Kowalczyk

Dave took the Jack Adams this year. Does he come up with one of those every week?

Marty Turco

You play twenty years and you still learn a new adjustment. The man earns his paycheck.

Jan Kowalczyk

Let me take you back to January. We talked about the split, you were thirteen and three at home, twelve and eleven on the road. You said it was legs and sleep. Now you're twenty-eight and ten at home, nineteen and nineteen on the road. Has anything changed?

Marty Turco

No. It hasn't. You go on the road, different bed, different time zone, you're playing back-to-backs, and your legs, they just don't come back the same. And here, twenty-eight wins in this building. I look up and I see guys who know the rink, know the crowd. Everything's familiar. I don't know how you fix that.

Jan Kowalczyk

One-goal game, late third. What does the building sound like from your crease?

Marty Turco

You know the cliché, I'm going to say it. The building gets loud and you feel it through the ice. It's a good cliché. I'll take it.

Jan Kowalczyk

Thanks, Marty. Good win. Enjoy it. Jan Kowalczyk, Dallas, back to you.
