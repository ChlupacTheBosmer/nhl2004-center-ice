MacTavish on the slide: one goal in two, and six left

The Oilers' coach after a third straight home loss says the goals are coming from the wrong players and that the work is on the top of his list, not the bottom.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelFriday, 25 March 2005

3:46 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Craig MacTavish stood at the boards after Edmonton Oilers's 1-2 loss to Tommy Salo84's Oilers fell again, their third in a row, all at home, one goal in the span. MacTavish has 6 games left to find the secondary scoring his 112-point captain cannot supply alone.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here in the tunnel at Rexall Place with Craig MacTavish. Coach, three straight, one goal across the last two games. What's the read?

Craig MacTavish

The read is we're not putting the puck in the net, and that's pretty straightforward. You know, WSH comes in, they defend the way we knew they would, they sit in the neutral zone, and we're one shot on the night. Zero. And tonight, NSH does something similar. Tight in the middle. We had looks. I don't think the chances were terrible. We just didn't bury them and they did.

Marcus Bell

You had twenty-two shots tonight to Nashville's twenty-one. That's not a team getting outworked.

Craig MacTavish

No, it's not. And that's the part that drives me a little. We're competing. I can watch this game and say the compete level was there, the urgency was there. We had the puck enough. We just, we had twenty-two shots and we got one in. Nashville had twenty-one and got two. That's the difference in the building tonight. Not effort. Execution.

Marcus Bell

Mike Comrie's got 112 points. That's the league's best total, I believe. And yet you're scoring at a level that loses you three straight. How does that happen?

Craig MacTavish

Because because if everybody in the building knows the puck's coming to Comrie, and then it's coming to Comrie again, well. You're not going to fool a lot of NHL teams on that, you know. We need the bottom two lines to be a factor. We need more than, what, two or three guys creating. That's what I keep hammering. You can't ask Mike and Ryan to do everything. Ryan's played 63 games and he's got 90 points, he's carrying a load, and he's getting doubled every night now. We need a third voice.

Marcus Bell

Does that third voice come from Radek Dvorak's line, or do you look down the roster?

Craig MacTavish

Obviously Dvorak's got 81 points, he's the guy next in line. And Mike York at 71 is doing his job. I think the answer is somewhere in between. You know, we've had the same lines out there for a while. I'm not, I'm not looking to blow something up with six games left. But I've looked at it. There's a question about who we dress and who plays. That's my job to sort out and it's my job to sort out fast.

Marcus Bell

Let me ask about Tommy Salo. Sixty-five games, that's eighty-six percent of your club's games. He's thirty-four. Does that number bother you at this point in a season?

Craig MacTavish

I think the question is fair from the outside. From the inside, Tommy's been our guy. Thirty-six wins, three shutouts, he's played well. Ty Conklin is a perfectly fine backup but I'm not going to start making changes at sixty-five games. The truth is we've been in every game because Tommy keeps us in. That's what I see from here.

Marcus Bell

You signed Brad May last week, forty-four games in, eight points. He's already back on the shelf, back coming April 10.

Craig MacTavish

Yeah. We signed him to add a body up front, a guy who could kill penalties, who could play a minute or two on the wing. And then, you know. That's hockey. We needed his experience on the PK and now we don't have it for the next two weeks. It's not ideal. I'll be honest with you, it's not ideal at all. Not with Petrov out for the year essentially, not with the depth chart as thin as it is.

Marcus Bell

Petrov's out until May 1. Oleg Petrov, seventy-three games, eighteen points, your fourth-line center for most of the year. That's a body you just don't get back this season.

Craig MacTavish

Right. And that's the part people don't see when they look at the top of our sheet. They see Comrie at 112, they see Smyth at 90. They don't see, well, we just lost the guy who ate all the minutes that nobody wants. So now who eats those minutes? That's the roster question and it's a bigger one than people give it credit for.

Marcus Bell

You're five points behind Colorado, and you've got Phoenix at home tomorrow. Then Columbus, then two on the road. Six games, the division lead on the table.

Craig MacTavish

Yeah. Six games. And I think, look, I'm not going to stand here and tell you we're not thinking about it. Colorado's won five straight, they're playing well, they've got one hundred and one points for a reason. But I'm not going to look at their schedule and count their losses. I need us to win tomorrow. That's what we control. Everything else is arithmetic that happens when the games get played.

Marcus Bell

Coach, thank you for your time. Back to you.
