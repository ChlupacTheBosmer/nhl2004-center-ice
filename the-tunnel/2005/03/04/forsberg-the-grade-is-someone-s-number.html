Forsberg: 'The Grade Is Someone's Number'

Peter Forsberg has 49 goals and 106 points in 65 games, a shift from the passer who had 34 last season, and he will not take credit for it.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelFriday, 4 March 2005

1:22 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Peter Forsberg95 sits at 49 goals and 106 points in 65 games for Colorado Avalanche, a pace well past the 101 he managed in 82 games in 2003-04. He added two points over the Avalanche's 5-3 road win at Chicago Blackhawks on March 2, their third straight, and Colorado sits two points ahead of Edmonton Oilers and three behind Vancouver Canucks in the Northwest.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here with Peter Forsberg, down in the tunnel after a 5-3 win in Chicago. Third straight for Colorado, Peter, two points over your last two games. How was this one?

Peter Forsberg

I think we played good. First period we were not sharp, they had some chances, but after that we had the game. When Joe and Paul are making plays like that, I am just finding them.

Marcus Bell

I want to stay on you for a second though. Forty-nine goals. You had thirty-four in eighty-two last year. And your assists are down, fifty-seven against sixty-seven. Your game looks different from the man I watched last season.

Peter Forsberg

I think I am shooting more. Before, I always looked to pass, and now I see the shot there and I take it. That is all, for sure. Forty-nine is... I see it.

Marcus Bell

And the Bureau's Development Index has you at ninety-five, a point below your base of ninety-seven. That is the lowest form reading the Index has shown on you all season. You are putting up 106 points and the Index says you are not quite yourself. Does that nag?

Peter Forsberg

I see it, of course. But I know how I feel in the third period. I know if my legs are there, if I am finding Joe, finding Paul. The grade is someone's number. I do not watch it.

Marcus Bell

Fair enough. Let me ask about the road. You are twenty-two and nine away from home, best on the team, and the Chicago crowd had some noise when it was 5-3. Does the travel wear on you this deep into a season?

Peter Forsberg

No. I mean... it is the same game. The ice is the same, the boards are the same. Chicago is loud, yes, and their fans cheer when they score, that is normal. But April is all loud places. So this is good. We get used to it now.

Marcus Bell

Peter Forsberg, thank you. Back to you.
