Kitchen puts Brent Johnson back between the pipes for Saturday

The Blues coach talks about the goaltending call, life without Keith Tkachuk, and what's left of the season.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelFriday, 11 March 2005

3:01 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

St. Louis Blues host San Jose Sharks tonight with Brent Johnson back as the starter after Chris Osgood held the G1 slot for the 3-1 loss at Dallas Stars on March 9. Mike Kitchen's club sits 22-32-5, 67 points, 4th in the Central, and Keith Tkachuk84 is out until April 23 with a hip. The Blues have scored 201 goals in 68 games without him.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here in the tunnel at the Savis Center, with Mike Kitchen. Coach, you lost that one in Dallas 3-1 on Thursday, 27 shots against, 21 for. Brent Johnson is back in net tonight after sitting that one out. Walk me through the decision.

Mike Kitchen

Well, you know, I looked at the whole thing and, uh, Brent's played 53 games for us this year. He's seen every team in this league at least twice. Osgood's been good in his stretch, I'm not taking anything from Chris, the two shutouts speak for themselves. But, uh, when you get to this time of year you want the guy who's been in the fire all winter. That's Brent.

Jan Kowalczyk

Twenty-three and twenty-five for Brent right now, no shutouts. Osgood's got two. You're going with volume and familiarity over recent results.

Mike Kitchen

I'm going with the guy who's carried us. Look, I know what the W-L column says. I see it every morning. But Chris came in and played well for us, that's the truth of it. And that competition is why we're still here at 67 points instead of, you know, wherever the bottom of this standings looks. Two guys pushing each other. That's healthy.

Jan Kowalczyk

One line of questioning I had: the lineup didn't move much beyond the goaltending this week. Just Brent sliding up. Was there anything you wanted to shake up with your forward groups after Dallas?

Mike Kitchen

We looked at it, yeah. We looked at it that night on the flight home. But here's the thing about losing TK. You don't get to reshuffle and pretend the hole isn't there. Doug's carrying 71 points. Pavol's got 63. These guys are going to have to produce and you can't just, you know, throw a fourth-line center onto the top unit and expect him to manufacture what Keith manufactures. You manage around it. You don't paper over it.

Jan Kowalczyk

TK's return date is April 23. How much do you think that means for what's left of this regular season?

Mike Kitchen

I think it means I don't plan for it. I'd be lying if I told you I haven't looked at the calendar. But you know, a hip, that's a tricky thing. The doctors say the 23rd, I trust the doctors. And if he's ready for the last handful, great, he adds something nobody else on this roster adds. If he's not, we play the men we have. Which is what we've been doing for a while now.

Jan Kowalczyk

You've scored 201 in 68 games, that's the fourth-lowest total in the league. Without him at even strength and on the power play, where does the next goal come from?

Mike Kitchen

Yeah, 201. I see it. It comes from, you know, getting to the dirty areas. Sergei Berezin's got 31 goals, he's been our goal guy. Brandon Reid's got 24 at 24 years old and he's still learning where to stand. We need more from Doug. That's not a criticism, that's just the arithmetic. He's got 19 goals and 52 assists and the goals have to go up if we want to score more than two a night. And Dallas Drake at 36, he's not going to suddenly find a gear. We need the 24-year-old to be a 30-goal guy and we need Berezin to keep finding pucks. That's the plan, for what it's worth.

Jan Kowalczyk

One last thing before you go. You've got San Jose tonight, then Calgary the 15th, Chicago the 17th. You're four points behind Columbus for that third spot in the division. Do you think there's still a run to be made here, or is this about getting men ready for something else?

Mike Kitchen

We're playing for the next game. That's what I tell them and that's what I believe. Four points behind Columbus with eleven games to go, that's not a death sentence in this league. Teams go cold. We've been cold. Look, I'll tell you this: nobody in that room is looking past San Jose tonight. Nobody. You watch. We'll play hard and we'll see where it lands.

Jan Kowalczyk

Coach Kitchen, thank you. Back to you.
