Marian Gaborik's numbers look smaller, and he says the game feels bigger

The Bureau's Development Index has him up six from his base, the highest he has been in a Toronto sweater, and he says the reason is not on the scoresheet.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelFriday, 11 March 2005

1:58 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Marian Gaborik98 finished with one assist in Toronto Maple Leafs's 6-2 win at Edmonton Oilers on Thursday, the quietest night of a five-game win streak that has Toronto at 112 points and 49 wins through 70 games. The Bureau's Development Index has him up six from his base grade of 91, putting him at 96 overall, the highest mark of his season. Gaborik has 46 goals and 84 points in 70 games, against 77 and 131 in 82 a year ago.

Marian Gaborik

Well, uh, I think, I think before, I was thinking too much about the, you know, the goal. The first thing, I want to shoot. Now, when I see Mats or Alex, I know they finish, so I pass. Marian Gaborik

No, I notice. I notice when I go home and I have zero. But when we win 6-2 like tonight, I don't care. I really don't. I look at the scoreboard and six is good. I don't look for my name. Maybe in April I will look. I don't know.
