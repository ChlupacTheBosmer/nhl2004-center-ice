Giguere Still Chasing His First Zero After 60 Games

The Ducks' goalie, rated 97 in the Bureau's Book with a 99 save grade, stopped 17 of 18 shots against Detroit but the shutout has not come once this season.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelFriday, 11 March 2005

1:34 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Jean-Sebastien Giguere97 picked up his 33rd win of the season, stopping 17 of 18 shots in Mighty Ducks of Anaheim's 4-1 decision over Detroit Red Wings on Wednesday night. The shutout, however, remains at zero in 60 appearances, a figure that sits oddly beside a 97 overall grade and a 99 save grade in the current Book.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here with Jean-Sebastien Giguere in the corridor after Anaheim's 4-1 win over Detroit. Eighteen shots tonight, one goal in. Jean-Sebastien, on a night that quiet, how do you stay locked in?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

It's, you know, it's harder. When you get thirty, thirty-five, you just go to work. Tonight, I'm looking at the guys, trying to, trying to find the rhythm. You don't want to be the cold one when the puck finally comes through.

Marcus Bell

And sixty games now without a shutout. You've got a 99 save grade, the highest in the league. Does that zero bother you?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

I mean, sure, everybody sees it. But the zero I want is in the win column. 33 wins, that's what I look at. The shutout is, it's decoration, for sure. I don't lose sleep over it.

Marcus Bell

We spoke in January after Philadelphia. You told me next time you wanted to come back here and talk about two points instead of the scoreboard. Well, you've got your two points.

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

You remember that? Okay. Yeah, it's nice. Two points. Sergei's been incredible, you know, two goals tonight, he just decides the game. It's easier to be a goalie when he's doing that every week. The guys in front of me, they just, they carry me some nights and that's the job.

Marcus Bell

That one goal Detroit got past you. Anything you wanted back on it?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

Yeah, for sure, I want that one back. But the boys answered right away. That's the thing with this room, if I let one in, they put it back in my hands with two, three more on the board. That's all I need.

Marcus Bell

Two straight wins now for Anaheim. Where does this group see itself when April comes?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

Hard to play. That's it. We're maybe not the best team in the West, no. But you play us seven games, you'll see. The guys believe. I believe. That's enough for me.

Marcus Bell

Jean-Sebastien Giguere, thanks for the time. I'll hold you to that zero next time. Back to you.
