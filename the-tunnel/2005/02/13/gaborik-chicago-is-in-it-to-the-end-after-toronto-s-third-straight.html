Gaborik: 'Chicago is in it to the end' after Toronto's third straight

The right winger hands the night to the kid who scored three, waves off 92 points and a contract year, and takes the win as the birthday present.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelSunday, 13 February 2005

1:56 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Marian Gaborik97 stopped at the microphone in the corridor after Toronto Maple Leafs made it three straight, 3-2 at Chicago Blackhawks on Saturday night, 25 shots a piece. His line reads 43 goals and 77 points through 57 games, and his club sits on 92 points, more than anybody in the league.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here, corridor level, with Marian Gaborik of Toronto. Three straight, 3-2 in Chicago, 25 shots a piece. Marian, how does that one feel?

Marian Gaborik

Good win. This building, it's, how you say, hard building. Chicago they fight all night for the points, you know? We take the two, that's the thing.

Marcus Bell

Petr Chlup had a goal on the night and three points on the week. What does the room say about the run he's putting together?

Marian Gaborik

What we say, we say every day already. Petry is first in the building, last one out, everyone know it. Tonight the puck go his way, the three points. We all happy for him. He's our guy, you know.

Marcus Bell

You added a helper of your own, your 77th point on the season. One of those nights the rest wasn't coming to you?

Marian Gaborik

Yeah. The night, it's not coming to me much, you know? I have the one play, my man he finish it. The rest is skating, chasing. Sometimes like this. 43 is there, it's okay.

Marcus Bell

Then the run. Third straight, 92 points through 57 games, nobody has more. Do you let yourselves look at that?

Marian Gaborik

We look, yes. The board is there, everybody see it. But tonight, 25-25 shots, Chicago is in it to the end. So when we start think the 92, we lose the game. We think next shift. That's why three straight.

Marcus Bell

You told me back in December that numbers don't mean anything in May. Still the line after 43 goals?

Marian Gaborik

Same thing. Last year is there the numbers, in May, they gone. Everybody in this room want the Cup, not the, how you say, one trophy for the stat line. We play good now, everybody feel it. This is the good time.

Marcus Bell

The contract year, then. One year left on your deal. It's February, do you think about it?

Marian Gaborik

That's for my agent and the team, you know? My job is the shift, the speed. Room is good, I play good. The rest is paper.

Marcus Bell

One more, the easy one. You turn 23 tomorrow, on the road. Any plans?

Marian Gaborik

You know what, tonight is the present, three wins. Tomorrow I don't know. I hope somebody bring cake in the room. Petry has three points, maybe Petry he buy the cake.

Marcus Bell

Marian Gaborik, Toronto's number 10, three straight and 23 tomorrow. Thank you, Marian. Back to you.
