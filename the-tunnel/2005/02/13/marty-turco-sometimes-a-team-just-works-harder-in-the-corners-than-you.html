Marty Turco: 'Sometimes a team just works harder in the corners than you do'

After Dallas fell 3-2 to Carolina on Sunday, the netminder owns a breakdown of his own, defends the home record, and says he is ready to sleep in a different bed.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelSunday, 13 February 2005

1:39 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Marty Turco94 gave up three goals on 22 shots as Dallas Stars dropped a 3-2 decision to Carolina Hurricanes at home on Sunday night, leaving the Stars on 74 points and a 21-6 home mark that has cracked six times this season. Both clubs now sit at the same point total in the standings. Marcus Bell caught him in the tunnel.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here with Marty Turco. Three-two to Carolina tonight, 22 shots against, 19 for your side. Marty, did this one get away from you in the third?

Marty Turco

Yeah, look, the first 40 we're, we're in it. We're tracking pucks, we're moving our feet, we're fine. And then, I don't know, the last twenty we start a half step late on the pinch and they're getting clean looks from the slot. You just, you can't give a team like Carolina that many clean looks.

Marcus Bell

You've got Zubov and Boucher out there with you. How's that group finding each other?

Marty Turco

Zubs is always two plays ahead. He sees things before they happen, you know. And Bouch is the opposite. He's physical, he's clearing the crease, he makes the job ugly. And it works, you know? The thing is tonight we had a couple transition breakdowns and I made a bad first pass on one of 'em. That's on me.

Marcus Bell

Carolina's been in 12 overtime games this year. They live in the close ones. Did you feel that tonight?

Marty Turco

Absolutely. That's who they are. They're not gonna get blown out, they're big, they're willing, and they'll grind it till the last ten minutes. We had our chances and we just couldn't, we couldn't get the second one to quiet the building down.

Marcus Bell

You told Jan on New Year's Day that the home edge is legs and sleep, nothing mystical. You're still 21 and 6 in here. Does a night like tonight change that?

Marty Turco

Yeah, I remember that one. And no, it doesn't. You look at 21 and 6, that's the legs talking. Tonight we had the legs, we had the crowd, we had everything. Sometimes a team just works harder in the corners than you do and you shake hands. That's hockey.

Marcus Bell

You and Carolina are sitting on 74 points right now. Are you looking over your shoulder at them?

Marty Turco

I don't know about looking over our shoulders. We've got 31 wins, they've got 20. You can do the math. We're not chasing them.

Marcus Bell

Fair enough. You've been home a while now. What's the road trip look like for you?

Marty Turco

Good, honestly. We know every square foot of this ice. You want a new view, you want to sleep in a different bed, you know? Gets the legs moving again.

Marcus Bell

Thanks, Marty. Marcus Bell, back to you.
