Giguere, 0 shutouts in 47: 'Maybe tonight. That's what I tell myself'

The Ducks' goaltender, who stopped 21 of 24 in a 3-2 overtime loss at Edmonton, still cannot find the blank page he found twice last season.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelMonday, 7 February 2005

2:40 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Jean-Sebastien Giguere97 was 21-of-24 in a 3-2 overtime loss at Edmonton Oilers on Saturday, the Ducks' 15 shots against Edmonton's 24 leaving him under constant pressure. Giguere now sits 24-22 with no shutout in 47 games this season, having posted two in 66 last year. The Ducks are 21-15-11, second in the Pacific.
