Gaborik's 41st, and a road loss in overtime at Tampa Bay

Gaborik scored his 41st of the season, Toronto lost 3-4 in overtime at Tampa Bay, and he said the 58 shots against are the part nobody in the room has to be told about.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelMonday, 7 February 2005

1:56 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Marian Gaborik scored his 41st goal of the season on 2005-02-06 and Toronto Maple Leafs left Tampa with nothing: a 3-4 overtime loss to Tampa Bay Lightning on a night the Lightning put 58 shots at Roberto Luongo93 and Toronto answered with 26. The Leafs lead the Northeast with 86 points. Jan Kowalczyk met Gaborik in the tunnel with the game still on him.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here at the rink in Tampa with Marian Gaborik. Marian, 3-4, overtime, and they threw 58 shots at you tonight. What kind of night was that?

Marian Gaborik

Yeah, it's, uh, you know, tough one. They shoot fifty-eight, we shoot twenty-six. Lui, he play, how you say, unbelievable. We give him too much work, you know. We can't do that and win.

Jan Kowalczyk

You got your 41st of the season. Take me through that one.

Marian Gaborik

Yeah, the puck come to me in the slot, you know, and I just, uh... I shoot. Quick. That's my game, I don't think too much, you know. It's nice, but, uh... after a loss, the goal, it doesn't feel... yeah.

Jan Kowalczyk

You were chasing most of the night. Where did the third period get away from you?

Marian Gaborik

No, I don't know if, uh, we, I mean... the third, we have chances too, you know. They have the puck more, sure. But we're there, 3-3, and, uh... in overtime, it's one bounce. One mistake. That's it.

Jan Kowalczyk

A bounce, or a mistake?

Marian Gaborik

Both, you know. In overtime you don't get many chance. They get one, they score. We go home, we, uh... yeah. That's hockey.

Jan Kowalczyk

You're on the road a bit longer, then back to your building. What does this week ask of you?

Marian Gaborik

Road is hard, you know, the travel, the... but we play pretty good on the road, 18-9, we like it. Home, 23-4. It's, uh... we take the rest, and then we go again. Every game now, everybody want the points.

Jan Kowalczyk

Last time we talked, I asked you how the room resets after a night where the shots don't turn into points. Is this that kind of night?

Marian Gaborik

Yeah, it is, you know. You lose, everybody, uh... we sit, we look at each other, and then tomorrow it's gone. New game. Mats, Robbie, they, uh, they've seen everything, you know. Me, I'm twenty-two, I'm... I learn from them. But we can't carry it. No.

Jan Kowalczyk

What does this team need before the next one?

Marian Gaborik

Rest, you know. No, we need, uh... we need to be better in our end, more simple. Fifty-eight shots, that's, uh, that's too many. We know it. Nobody has to tell us.

Jan Kowalczyk

Marian, thank you for your time.

Marian Gaborik

Thank you. Thanks, Jan.

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk, Tampa.
