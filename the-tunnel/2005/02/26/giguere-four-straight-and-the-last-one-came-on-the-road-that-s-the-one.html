Giguere: 'Four straight, and the last one came on the road, that's the one that counts'

The Ducks' goaltender says the four-game run means more because the win at Carolina was a road win, and credits Fedorov for making his night short.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelSaturday, 26 February 2005

1:44 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Jean-Sebastien Giguere Jean-Sebastien Giguere97 stopped 18 shots as Mighty Ducks of Anaheim beat Carolina 3-2 on the road on 2005-02-23, extending the club's win streak to four. Giguere is 30-24 this season with 0 shutouts, but the Ducks are 77 points and moving. The road has been the problem all year: 12 wins, 19 losses.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here with Jean-Sebastien Giguere. Four straight, J-S. You just won in Carolina. What's it feel like in that room right now?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

It feels good, for sure. You know, four games, and the last one, we did it on the road. That's, that's the one we've been looking for all year. Twelve wins at home... no, wait. Twelve and nineteen. Yeah. So winning here, it means something.

Jan Kowalczyk

You've been 12-19 on the road all season. What's different about these last wins away from home?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

I think we're, how do you say, we're not chasing the game. Tonight, Fed scores two early, and now I've got a cushion. I can, you know, I can settle. When you're down one on the road the whole night, every puck you touch is a problem. Tonight I touched maybe eighteen pucks. It was easy.

Jan Kowalczyk

Eighteen shots from Carolina, you're right. Fedorov two goals, two points. He's carried this offense the whole year. Does it change how you play behind him when he's the one scoring?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

You mean do I relax? A little. A little. But listen, the guys in front of me, they blocked shots all night. The, what's his name, Reirden was eating pucks in the slot. I don't know, maybe twenty blocks on the team. That's what wins on the road. Not me.

Jan Kowalczyk

Four wins. You're four points behind Dallas in the Pacific. What does the next stretch of this schedule ask of you?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

It asks us to win the next one. That's all it asks. I've been here enough years, when you look at the table too much you lose the next game. Fed scored two tonight and that's great, tomorrow he might not. So it's, it's the next practice, the next bus, the next puck drop. We'll take the next one.

Jan Kowalczyk

You said last time I caught you that one win is not the thing. Still feel that way after four?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

Yeah. Yeah, for sure. Four is good. But I've seen four become zero in a week. I don't want to get, you know, happy about it. I want to wake up tomorrow and the streak is five. Then we'll see.

Jan Kowalczyk

Thank you, J-S. Jean-Sebastien Giguere, from the tunnel in Carolina. Back to you.
