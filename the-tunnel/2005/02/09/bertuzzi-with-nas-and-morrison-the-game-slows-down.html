Bertuzzi: 'With Nas and Morrison, the game slows down'

The Canucks' right wing was held off the scoresheet at Buffalo for the first time in a while, and says the line has simply gotten used to finding the open man.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelWednesday, 9 February 2005

2:14 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Todd Bertuzzi93 came in tonight sitting on 96 points in 55 games, and Vancouver Canucks walked out of Buffalo with a 3-2 loss, a second straight defeat, and their top forward line held to a single goal between them. Bell caught him in the tunnel.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here in the tunnel at Buffalo after a 3-2 loss. Todd, you finish the night with no points. That hasn't happened a lot this season. What was it about tonight?

Todd Bertuzzi

Yeah, I mean, you look at the board and you think, come on, you know, we should've had more. But Buffalo, they, they collapsed the middle real tight. They're not, they're not a team that lets you run free through the hash marks and tonight they just, you know, they sat on the blue line and made us carry it in.

Marcus Bell

You've been rolling though. 96 points, 40 goals, the line with Naslund and Morrison. I mean, we talked about that first week in December, you said you were still learning where they liked to be. Where are you now?

Todd Bertuzzi

Yeah, December, man. I think I said something about not feeling like a veteran yet. Well I feel pretty settled now. You know, Nas, he's, he's gonna drive the net, he always drives the net, so you just learn, okay, I stay high, I get the return pass. And Morrison, he'll, he'll tilt it to the far post before you even ask. So you stop thinking. You just, you open your hips and you're there.

Marcus Bell

Naslund got one tonight. Sopel chipped in from the point. But the line only had that one. Is there a risk you start pressing when the numbers say you should be producing every night?

Todd Bertuzzi

No, I don't think so. I mean, obviously you want to go out and get four every night, that's, that's how it works. But, you know, we had looks. I had a look in the second where I, where I just, the puck hung on my tape a half beat too long and the D-man got a stick on it. Those go in 30 times this season out of 40. So I'm not, I'm not pressing. I know the next one's gonna go.

Marcus Bell

Buffalo outshot you 22 to 18 tonight. They're 20 and 21, they're not exactly the Bruins. Did it feel like they imposed themselves on the game?

Todd Bertuzzi

Well, they played good, yeah. You know, I'm not gonna take anything away from them. They're a hard-working team, they, they forechecked hard and, and we turned it over a couple times in our own zone late and that's, that's what kills you on the road. You get the two goals, you think you got a shot, and then you, you cough one up on the transition and it's three to two and suddenly you're chasing.

Marcus Bell

Two in a row now. You're still 28 and 17 though, second in the Northwest. Is this a wobble or something to worry about?

Todd Bertuzzi

I don't lose sleep over it. I mean, yeah, you don't like losing, nobody does, and we'd like to get back in the win column tomorrow or whenever we play next. But, you know, this is February. The games matter more now, sure, but one bad road game in Buffalo, I'm not, I'm not, we're not tearing the thing down. We got 96 points between the three of us up top. The thing's working.

Marcus Bell

Todd Bertuzzi, thanks again. Back to you.
