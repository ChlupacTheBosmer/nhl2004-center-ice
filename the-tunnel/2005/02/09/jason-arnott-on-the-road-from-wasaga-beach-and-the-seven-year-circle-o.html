Jason Arnott on the road from Wasaga Beach, and the seven-year circle of Bill Guerin

The Dallas alternate captain, up six on the Bureau's Development Index, says his story with Bill Guerin really starts on January 4, 1998, and he still hears one sentence from Mike Modano about earning the trade that sent Joe Nieuwendyk away.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelWednesday, 9 February 2005

4:15 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

Jason Arnott85 sits down with Marcus Bell in the studio this week, a week that found him up 6 on the Bureau's Development Index, with 16 games and 12 points behind him and Dallas Stars running at the front of the table. The Sweater ran its profile on him and Bill Guerin this fortnight, the seven-year circle that started with the January 1998 trade, and this is the other half of it: the rinks between Collingwood and Oshawa, the road, and the man who scored the Cup winner against the sweater he now wears.
