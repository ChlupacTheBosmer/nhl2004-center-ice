Sutter's lines don't move, even at 17 and 30

The Flames coach says he'll keep running the same groups through the stretch, and that four games in seven starting Tuesday will test whether that's the right call.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelWednesday, 9 February 2005

2:59 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Calgary Flames won 4-2 at Edmonton Oilers on Sunday night, the kind of game that barely dents the standing but gives a dressing room something to carry into Tuesday's date at Colorado Avalanche. Marcus Bell caught Calgary Flames head coach Darryl Sutter in the tunnel after the final horn to talk about why the lines haven't moved, the stretch coming, and whether Roman Turek86 starts again next week.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here with Darryl Sutter in the tunnel at Rexall Place. Coach, a 4-2 road win, your second in a row. Does this one feel any different from the ones before it?

Darryl Sutter

No, it doesn't. Two points is two points. We played a good period, we played a bad one, we played a good one again. You want all three but you get what you get. The compete level was there, and that's the one thing I'll take every night.

Marcus Bell

Coach, I've looked at the lines this week and you haven't moved anyone. Despite where you are in the standings, the groups are the same as they were two weeks ago. Is that deliberate?

Darryl Sutter

Yeah. It is. Look, I've got guys who've been together a while. Jarome, Craig, Dean. You don't break up something that competes every night just because the record isn't where you want it. You give them something to work at, you fix the details, you let them play. Churning it doesn't make a man play harder. It just makes him confused.

Marcus Bell

And the young guys? Chuck Kobasew's in and out, Mika Sulku's played forty games. Do they get more of it with the standings what they are?

Darryl Sutter

They get what they earn. Chuck's got a real shot, you know, he's a guy who competes and he's not going to stop. Mika, same thing. But I'm not putting a young man in a spot he's not ready for just because we've got nothing to play for in the points column. That's not how you build it. You build it right or you don't build it.

Marcus Bell

Talk me through the stretch. You've got Colorado Tuesday night, then Anaheim, then Los Angeles, then Vancouver home. Four in seven, three on the road.

Darryl Sutter

It's a hockey schedule. You manage it, you travel, you try to get your guys ready. Colorado's a good team, they're fast on the forecheck, they shoot the puck. We know that. Anaheim's tough at home, they clog things up. You just play the game. I'm not looking seven days out, I'm looking at tomorrow.

Marcus Bell

Back in December you told my colleague Jan that Roman Turek's numbers aren't there. He's 15 and 30 now with one shutout. Is there a conversation to be had about the net?

Darryl Sutter

The job is the job. Roman knows where he's at, I know where he's at. He's our starter tonight, he'll get the next one until I say otherwise. Jamie's back-upped all year, he's ready. But I'm not going to stand here and put a man up against his own teammate on the evening news. We handle it in the room.

Marcus Bell

You signed Jordan Leopold on Friday and he went out with a rib injury on Saturday. What's the plan there?

Darryl Sutter

He's out three weeks. That's what it says. The signing's not on hold because of an injury, we'll work with what we've got. Ken Klee and Denis Gauthier hold it down, Toni's been here all year. We'll be fine.

Marcus Bell

One last one. Forty-nine goals in forty-nine games from Iginla. He's got 82 points. What do you say to a man having a season like that, in a room that's 17 and 30?

Darryl Sutter

I say he's been our guy for years. He doesn't need anything from me, he's got his own thing. What I say is the other twenty-nine have got to make it easier on him. You know? He's going to score, that's what he does. But you can't put all of it on one man's shoulders and expect it to be enough every night. We've got to find the secondary offense. That's the detail we're chasing.

Marcus Bell

Coach Sutter, thank you. Back to you.
