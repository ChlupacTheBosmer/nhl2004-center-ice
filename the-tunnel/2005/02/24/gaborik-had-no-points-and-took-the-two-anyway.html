Gaborik Had No Points And Took The Two Anyway

After a 1-0 overtime win over Nashville, Gaborik said a goalless night is the same result he wanted in January, gave credit to Upshall's overtime winner, and called the room's mood a world away from the one after the Boston loss.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelThursday, 24 February 2005

2:18 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Marian Gaborik98 had no points. Toronto Maple Leafs had 22 shots against 33 from Nashville Predators. And he still walked off with a fourth straight win, 1-0 in overtime, and a minute with Jan Kowalczyk in the corridor.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here with Marian Gaborik, right after that overtime win. One-nil, thirty-three shots against, twenty-two for you guys. What does a game like that feel like from the ice?

Marian Gaborik

It feels good. Yeah. We not give them nothing, how you say, clean in our zone. The first period we were, I mean, we come out slow, but after everybody is back, everybody is in the play. So, a win is a win, you know. Twenty-two shots, that is, that is not us. But we get the two.

Jan Kowalczyk

The third period. Nashville had the puck, they were pressing, and you couldn't put one in through sixty minutes. How do you keep your feet under you in that stretch?

Marian Gaborik

The third is long when it is like that. They move the puck, we move our feet, and that is, that is the thing. We just hold the structure, you know? Belfour he make the save he need, two, three in a row, and you think, okay, we are still here. We block some, sure. But the structure is what we work on every day. That is where the shutout come from.

Jan Kowalczyk

We talked in January, the morning after the Boston loss. You told me the result was on Toronto, not on your goal total. Tonight: no points, twenty-two team shots, and a shutout in net. Same answer?

Marian Gaborik

Same answer. I score or I not score. The team win is what I want. Eighty points is nice on the paper, yeah. But tonight, zero, I don't, I don't feel bad. You know what I mean? We get the two points. Belfour have the zero. That is the night.

Jan Kowalczyk

Let me ask about Upshall. He was out there more tonight than you'd expect from a young winger, and McCabe and Abid find him on the overtime goal. He fills a hole in that lineup. What does Scottie bring when the room needs an extra body?

Marian Gaborik

Scottie. Yeah. When he go, he just, he go. Nobody catch him, almost. And the helpers, McCabe, Ramzi, they make the play, they make the pass and Scottie just finish it. But he, he work every day, you know. He want it so much. The kid is twenty-two and he play like, like he owe somebody something. That is good for the room.

Jan Kowalczyk

Four straight wins, a hundred points, twenty-seven and five at home. In January you described the room as heavy after a bad one. Is what you have now what you were working toward then?

Marian Gaborik

Yeah, it is. The room is different. The legs are good, the head is good. Everybody believe, you know? In January we know we can play, but it is hard when you lose three, four in a row like that. Now the win come and it is, how you say, easier to, to be in the room. But four wins is not enough. We not stop here.

Jan Kowalczyk

Twenty games left. Marian Gaborik, thank you for the time. For the Tunnel, Jan Kowalczyk.

Marian Gaborik

Thank you, Jan. We see.
