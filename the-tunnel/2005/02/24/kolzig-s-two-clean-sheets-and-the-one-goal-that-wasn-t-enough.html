Kolzig's Two Clean Sheets and the One Goal That Wasn't Enough

The Capitals' goaltender, who had no shutout across 70 regular season games and 9 playoff games last season, has two this year, and after another one-goal loss at home he says the building is still the hardest ice.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelThursday, 24 February 2005

1:35 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Olaf Kolzig92 faced 23 shots and came away with a 3-2 loss to Detroit Red Wings at the MCI Center on Monday night, his second straight defeat. He is 31-26 with 2 shutouts this season after going 70 games in the regular season and 9 in the playoffs last year without one, and Washington Capitals sit 14-17 at home with the season winding down.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here in the tunnel with Olaf Kolzig. Olaf, 3-2 to Detroit tonight, 23 shots you see, back to back losses. What happened?

Olaf Kolzig

Yeah, we started slow. They score early and after that, you know, it's a different game. We chase. Sergei gets one and I think, okay, now we're in it. But you can't, you can't give them the first one like that.

Marcus Bell

Sergei Gonchar's goal. Did you see it go in?

Olaf Kolzig

Yeah, he walks in, shoots through. I see it go top side and I'm thinking, this is the one, now the building is going. And it was. For a minute there it was. Then, you know, three is three. We needed one more.

Marcus Bell

Two shutouts this season, Olaf. Last year, 70 games, 9 in the playoffs, zero. Where does that number sit with you?

Olaf Kolzig

Well, you know, I think it means something. I didn't have one, not one, all last year. The Vezina, people say, how do you win without a shutout. And I didn't have one. This year, two. It's not a lot, but I feel, I feel different in some of them. The puck is, it's clean. I see it.

Marcus Bell

In January Jan asked you about the home record. You were 10-10 at home then. Washington is 14-17 in the building right now. Has it moved for you personally?

Olaf Kolzig

No. Not enough. I told Jan, the guys here, they press, they hold the puck one play too long. That hasn't changed. And on the road, you know, the other team's crowd, they cheer when the other guy shoots. It's loud. Here it's, it's quieter. I know I do better when it's loud. That's just how I am.

Marcus Bell

31 wins, 26 losses, the Vezina sitting at home from last spring. What are you looking at from here to the end of the season?

Olaf Kolzig

The next game. I've said this already, the trophy stops nothing. Every night starts from zero. We've got games left, the points matter, that's what I'm looking at. The next save, that's all.

Marcus Bell

Olaf Kolzig, thank you. Back to you.
