Giguere settles for the 3-2, and for the shutout he cannot get

The Anaheim goaltender took a close win over Columbus, owned his no-shutout streak, and pointed to Sergei Fedorov, Vaclav Prospal and his own back end for the 2 straight.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelMonday, 21 February 2005

2:18 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Jean-Sebastien Giguere [317] stood in the tunnel with his gear bag still on his shoulder after Mighty Ducks of Anaheim beat Columbus Blue Jackets 3-2 on 2005-02-19, a night of 17 shots and 2 goals against that left him with a win and no shutout. The Mighty Ducks of Anaheim goaltender has 28 wins and 0 shutouts this season, and his club has now won 2 straight to sit second in the Pacific division, 7 points behind Dallas Stars. Giguere spoke about closing a one-goal game, the goals in front of him, and the long streak of losses in overtime he is glad to be leaving behind.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here with Jean-Sebastien Giguere, in the tunnel after Anaheim beat Columbus 3-2. J.S., the scoreboard says a two-goal win, but Columbus put 17 on you and it stayed on the wire. How much of that late part did you have to win yourself?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

You know, when it's 3-2 at the end, every puck is a... is a problem for me. I don't like the last ten minutes like that. They score twice, and the guys in front are trying to protect a one-goal game with no time. That's it. But I'm happy. We get the two points, for sure.

Marcus Bell

Sergei Fedorov scores twice, three points. Vaclav Prospal another three-point night. When it's coming like that from your top two, what's left for you to do?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

The first period, the second, that's Fedorov, you know? He's the best, he's just the best. And Prospal three points tonight, yeah. When your top guys put it on the board, it's easy for me. I stay calm and let the guys do it. That's the quiet night for me.

Marcus Bell

You've won 2 straight now. You've also lost 11 in overtime this season, a lot of one-goal games. Has something changed in how you finish them?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

Well, I think... I think before we play close and we lose. Now we play close and we win. Is the difference, you know? Eleven, yeah, that's a lot to carry. But Havelid and the guys in the back, they're staying with it now, they're not giving up the second rush, the third, like October. That's the change.

Marcus Bell

You had 2 shutouts last season, 0 so far this season. Does that one get under your skin, even when you win 3-2?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

I hate it, I'll say that. The record says 2 last year, 0 now, and I know it. But the shutout is my game, not the team's. 3-2 is the same two points as 4-0. I told you before, Marcus, this room can win ugly. It's true. So I don't play for the zero. I play to win the game.

Marcus Bell

Two straight, and you're 7 points back of Dallas in the Pacific with a lot of road left. Is this the run where Anaheim starts believing it can chase them?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

I don't think about Dallas. Dallas is Dallas, they're winning, they get the two. We think about the next game. Columbus tonight, then the next one. I believe, I have to believe. But is one game at the time, that's it. You want the big word from me, I don't have it. I have the small one: the next game.

Marcus Bell

Last time we spoke, you said next time you wanted two points instead of the scoreboard. Tonight you got both. Did you beat me to it?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

Yeah, yeah I remember that. So I'm better, I'm better now. You keep me to the promise, good, good, that's how I like it. Next time I want the two points and the zero, for sure, I'm greedy that way. But tonight, 3-2, I take it, I'm done, I want my dinner.

Marcus Bell

Jean-Sebastien Giguere, the win on the night. Thanks for your time. Marcus Bell, back to you.
