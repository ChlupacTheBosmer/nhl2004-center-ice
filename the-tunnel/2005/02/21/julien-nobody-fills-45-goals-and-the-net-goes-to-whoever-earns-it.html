Julien: Nobody Fills 45 Goals, and the Net Goes to Whoever Earns It

After a 3-1 road win at Buffalo stopped a two-game slide, the Montreal coach liked the structure, hated the first ten minutes, and would not hand Zednik's minutes to one man.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelMonday, 21 February 2005

2:26 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Claude Julien spoke with The Tunnel after Montreal Canadiens's 3-1 win at Buffalo on 2005-02-19. The win stopped a two-game slide for a club that sits last in the Northeast with 50 points, and it came with Dwayne Roloson in the net and Richard Zednik out until 2005-03-03. Julien talked about the effort he got, the effort he did not, and who has to cover Zednik's 45 goals.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Claude, Jan Kowalczyk, The Tunnel. 3-1 on the road in Buffalo, back in the win column after two losses. What did you like in that?

Claude Julien

I liked the shape of it. We didn't give them much. 20 shots, that's a number we can live with. When we manage the puck and we're above it, we're a different team. For two periods tonight we were that team.

Jan Kowalczyk

And what did you not like?

Claude Julien

The first ten minutes. We were chasing it, turning pucks over in the middle, and that's a rush the other way every time. We got away with it tonight. Over a season, that's how you lose hockey games.

Jan Kowalczyk

Two losses coming in, and you go to Roloson in the net. Was tonight about the effort or about the call in goal?

Claude Julien

Both. Rollie was good. He was quiet back there, not fighting the puck, and the guys fed off it. That's what a start like that gives you.

Jan Kowalczyk

Theodore has played 50 games. Is this a rotation now, or is it one night?

Claude Julien

50 games is a load for anybody. Rollie's 35, he's a pro, he's waited his turn. I'll put the guy in who gives us the best chance, and I'll do that every night. That's the job.

Jan Kowalczyk

Zednik is out until March 3. He has 45 goals and 84 points on your club. Who fills that hole?

Claude Julien

Nobody fills 45 goals. You don't replace Richie with one man, you do it with five men doing a little more. Nylander, Bulis, Yanic, they all take a bigger piece. And Richie's back March 3, that part isn't a mystery. Until then, you know, it's the group.

Jan Kowalczyk

Perreault has 13 goals in 25 games for you. Has he earned the move up for good?

Claude Julien

You're writing my lines for me now. Yanic's given us 13 goals in 25 games, we need every one of them. Where he plays is between me and him. He'll play.

Jan Kowalczyk

Ricci and Kilger are out too, that's three bodies. Is the room thin enough that you go outside for help, or is there enough in there?

Claude Julien

There's enough for a week. When bodies go down, somebody gets a look he wouldn't have got, and that's how a room grows. I'm not going to sit here and cry about it. It's an opportunity.

Jan Kowalczyk

Toronto on Tuesday. 96 points, 42 and 10. What does that game need?

Claude Julien

Everything. That's the best team in the league walking into our building. If we turn pucks over the way we did in the first ten tonight, it's 5-1 the other way. If we're structured and disciplined, we can play with them. Tuesday, we show it.

Jan Kowalczyk

Last one. It has been a hard season. Do you see what you need to see from this group, night to night?

Claude Julien

Most nights. What I want is the details. Compete level, managing the puck, the small things. When we get those, we're in every game. When we don't, we're not. It isn't complicated. It's just hard.

Jan Kowalczyk

Claude, thank you. Jan Kowalczyk, Buffalo. Back to you.
