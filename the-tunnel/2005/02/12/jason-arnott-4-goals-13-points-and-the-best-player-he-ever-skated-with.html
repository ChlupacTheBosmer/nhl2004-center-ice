Jason Arnott: 4 goals, 13 points, and the best player he ever skated with

Dallas center Jason Arnott talks about a winter that has not gone his way, and about Patrik Elias, the best player he has shared a line with.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelSaturday, 12 February 2005

2:26 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Jason Arnott has 4 goals and 13 points in 18 games for Dallas Stars, after 33 goals and 64 points in 51 games a year ago. The man he was traded for in 1998, Bill Guerin, is at 105 points. Arnott sat with Marcus Bell this week to talk about the winter and the players who shaped his road.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here with Jason Arnott of the Dallas Stars. Jason, thanks for coming in. Eighteen games, 4 goals, 13 points. When you look at this season, what do you see?

Jason Arnott

Yeah. Well, the numbers are the first thing, right? Four goals. That's not who I am. I've been doing this a long time, and I know what a good year looks like and what this one looks like.

Marcus Bell

Last year you scored 33 goals in 51 games. Nineteen points in 17 playoff games. This year, 4 goals and 13 points in 18. What changed?

Jason Arnott

If I could put one finger on it, I'd have fixed it in November. You know, the puck doesn't go in, and one week turns into a month, and then you look up and it's 18 games. It's not one thing. It never is.

Marcus Bell

There was the groin, early December. A lot of people point at that.

Jason Arnott

The groin. I was back December 3. So, no. I'm not going to sit here and blame a groin. That's not the season.

Marcus Bell

Then what do you take from a year like this?

Jason Arnott

That you're the same player when it isn't going in. That sounds like a line, I know. But the work is the same. You show up, you take your 17.9 minutes, you do the job. What changes is you stop letting the puck decide whether it was a good day.

Marcus Bell

Let me ask you about Bill Guerin. There's a piece of history between you two.

Jason Arnott

There is. 1998. He goes one way, I come the other. I'm 23 years old, my phone rings, and I'm a New Jersey Devil. You don't forget the day. You remember where you were standing when it happened.

Marcus Bell

And now you're in the same room.

Jason Arnott

Same room. You know the funny part? We never played together back then. Not one shift. And now he's got 52 goals, 105 points, and I'm watching him every night. That's a strange circle to close.

Marcus Bell

Best player you've ever played with. Who, and why?

Jason Arnott

Patrik Elias. In Jersey. The A Line. Everything went through him, you know? You give him the puck and you don't see it for a second, and then it's on your tape and you're alone in front. That's the best I've been on the ice with.

Marcus Bell

Not Billy? He's at 105 points.

Jason Arnott

Billy's a different question. Best player and best teammate, those aren't the same guy. You want Billy on your side every time. First one in the room in the morning. But the best player I ever skated with, I'll keep Patrik.

Marcus Bell

Game six, 2000. Double overtime. Against the team you play for now.

Jason Arnott

8:20. Yeah. And now I dress in this room. You don't draw that up. First week I got here, a couple of the guys let me have it. They still do, once a year. That's hockey. That's the joke that doesn't die.

Marcus Bell

Last one. You're at 13 points, Billy's at 105, and there's a lot of season left. What does this room look like on a night like that?

Jason Arnott

It looks the same. That's a good room. Nobody's piling on a guy at 13 points. But you feel it. You want to earn your seat. I'm 30. I've been traded twice, I've won one. You don't forget 1998, you don't forget 2000, and you don't forget a year like this one. The puck goes in some years and it doesn't in others. Same job either way.

Marcus Bell

Jason Arnott, thanks for your time. Back to you.
