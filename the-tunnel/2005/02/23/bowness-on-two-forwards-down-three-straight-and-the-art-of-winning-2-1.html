Bowness on two forwards down, three straight, and the art of winning 2-1

The Coyotes coach explains how his group is winning low-event road games with Daymond Langkow and Brian Savage both out, and when they come back.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelWednesday, 23 February 2005

4:46 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Phoenix Coyotes beat Dallas Stars 2-1 on the road on 2005-02-21, their third straight win, with two top-nine forwards hurt and a shot differential against them in both games this week. Rick Bowness sat with Jan Kowalczyk after the final horn in Dallas to talk through the injury holes, the special teams, and a homestand where both men return.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here in the tunnel at Dallas with Phoenix head coach Rick Bowness. Rick, twenty-one shots against, seventeen for, a 2-1 win on the road against an eighty-point club. You're three straight now. What's the thread through these ones?

Rick Bowness

You know, the thread is we're managing the puck better than we were a month ago. We had the puck, uh, the first twenty minutes tonight and we didn't give up a grade-A until, I don't know, the second intermission maybe. So when you're playing a team that's got Turco in there and, and guys that can make you pay, that matters more than, than shot totals. The scoreboard says we outshot 'em, the scoreboard says they won the game, and they did.

Jan Kowalczyk

And you're doing it without Daymond Langkow and without Brian Savage. Daymond's neck, Savage's groin. Both out right now. Langkow back the 24th, Savage the 26th. Who's been carrying that load?

Rick Bowness

Yeah, look, uh, Daymond, his numbers don't jump off the page, five and three, but he's killing penalties and he's on our second unit. So when he's out we're asking was out too now, so you're asking guys like to, to take some of that, and Krystofer Kolanos has gotten an every-other-night look. He's twenty-three, he's still figuring out the league, but he's not hiding. And then Tyson Nash has had a bigger shift load, fifty-six games this year, he's used to that.

Jan Kowalczyk

You said Kolanos is not hiding. What do you see from him that maybe the four points in twenty-four games doesn't show?

Rick Bowness

You know the question, you read the stats before you walk up here. And look, four points, twenty-four games, I get it. But what he's doing is, he's winning his draws on the third line, he's getting us the puck in the defensive zone so our defensemen aren't trying to carry through the neutral zone with, with a team like Dallas on the forecheck. That's the detail. That's not a point on a sheet, but doesn't have to make the play under pressure because Kolanos won the draw and got us out clean. That's what I see.

Jan Kowalczyk

Let me talk about the special teams for a moment. You're giving up 193 goals this year, scoring 176. That's a minus-seventeen differential in sixty games. But you've won three straight. What are the special teams doing in this stretch that's different?

Rick Bowness

Well, obviously you want to score when you get the man advantage, and we've, we've been better on that. But the, the thing that's actually turned is the penalty kill. We're putting guys out there who want to be there, who are, who are blocking the lane and not reaching. Daymond was a big part of that and his absence shows, I'll say that. But Steve Konowalchuk has come back and he's only twenty-one games, five and eight, but he's a guy who kills penalties like it's a personal offense when someone scores against him. So the, the units have reshuffled, guys are moving up, and that's the kind of thing that, uh, the results take a while to show but the, the compete level is there.

Jan Kowalczyk

You mentioned Konowalchuk. Twenty-one games, five goals, eight assists. He's your second-highest rated player on the roster at 85, behind Johnson, Nagy, Doan, Sillinger. What's keeping him at twenty-one games?

Rick Bowness

A hip, a groin, I don't know, the, the record's got the groin. You know what it is, the training staff knows, I don't play him till he's right. And look, when he's in there he's, he's a different player than what people think of when they think of a fourth-liner. He's got hands, he's got size, he's, he should have more points than thirteen, obviously. But I'll take what I'm getting from him. Twenty-one games out of sixty is a, is a long time to be trying to build chemistry on a line.

Jan Kowalczyk

Sean Burke's in net tonight, fifty starts, twenty-three and twenty-seven. Not the cleanest record. What's the goaltending situation in front of this homestand?

Rick Bowness

You know what, has won two of three. He made the save we needed tonight in the last five minutes, the, the one where their guy got a backdoor look and Sean got his pads across. You don't win this game without that. So, no, I'm not going to talk about his record. His record is what it is, his team is winning, and he's, he's thirty-eight years old and he's giving us sixty games because nobody else is going to give us sixty games. That's not a complaint, that's the situation.

Jan Kowalczyk

Fair enough. You've got three at home this week: Calgary Monday, Carolina Friday, Anaheim Sunday. Langkow's back for the Calgary game. Savage back for Carolina. Does that change the lines, or do you stick with what's been working?

Rick Bowness

You know, I wish I had the luxury of just leaving it alone. When Daymond comes back he's going on the penalty kill, that's the first thing. That's where his value is right now. Savage, Savage comes back and he goes back on a wing, obviously, because we've got a spot open with his injury. The lines will, will settle themselves. The thing I care about is, is whoever's on the ice is playing with the same structure, the same, you know, the same commitment to the details in their own zone. That's not going to change because a guy comes off the IR. That would be a bad sign.

Jan Kowalczyk

One last one. You're sixty points in sixty games, third in the Pacific, twenty-three back of the lead in the division but three points out of the wild card spot in the West. You've got a four-day stretch before Anaheim. Is this room feeling like it's got something to play for?

Rick Bowness

Look, I've been in this league a long time, and the boys in that room, they know what the table says. They don't need me to tell 'em. But I'll tell you what I see: the last three games, the first guy out of the penalty box is the first guy on the ice for the next shift. Nobody's waiting for a change. That's, that's a room that's competing. You know, it's not always going to work out, we've got minus-seventeen in the goal differential, we know that. But three straight, back home now, and two guys coming back into the lineup. I'll take that as a, as a good week.

Jan Kowalczyk

Rick Bowness, thank you again. Back to you.
