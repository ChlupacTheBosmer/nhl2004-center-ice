Gaborik Knows What to Do When Montreal Gets Quiet

Toronto's speed winger scored the opener in a building he visited many times in a Wild jersey, and says the last 21 games are about getting sharper, not looking at the gap.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelWednesday, 23 February 2005

2:18 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Marian Gaborik99 scored the opener as Toronto Maple Leafs beat Montreal Canadiens 3-1 on February 22, extending their win streak to three and putting them at 21-9 on the road this season. He now has 44 goals and 80 points through 61 games. Toronto sits 29 points clear of Boston Bruins in the Northeast with 21 games remaining.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here with Marian Gaborik at Bell Centre. Marian, you've come to this building a lot, probably more than most guys on this ice tonight. What is it like for you in here when Montreal is where they are?

Marian Gaborik

Yeah, I been coming here a long time, you know, since Minnesota days. The building is, it's always loud the first ten minutes. These guys, they show up. But when you get to second period and the game is, it's not going their way, then it gets quiet. You can hear the bench. Tonight when we scored the second, how you say, it just, it was done. The building was done.

Jan Kowalczyk

You were outshot 27 to 22 tonight. I remember in January we talked about the nights where the shots don't go in, when you have 32 and you can't win. This is the opposite. Does it feel like a different kind of game?

Marian Gaborik

Yeah, it's a different game. Tonight we didn't need, you know, we didn't need the shots. Our defense was good, Luongo had the saves when we needed him. The first goal we scored fast, the second came quick, so the whole thing was different. In January, Boston was, they just had the puck more and we couldn't, we couldn't match. Tonight it's the other way.

Jan Kowalczyk

Walk me through your goal. You get the puck and you're gone.

Marian Gaborik

Yeah. Well, I got it in the neutral zone and I see the defenseman, he's, he's not good on his feet, he's backing up. So I just, I go. I take it wide and I open the hip and I shoot. I saw it go in. I didn't really watch, you know, I just, I skated around. You know how it is.

Jan Kowalczyk

You know how it is. Three straight wins, 98 points, 21 games left. You're 29 ahead of Boston. What does the schedule ask of this team now?

Marian Gaborik

You know, I don't look at the, the table. I don't. We just, we take the next game. The guys, the room, we talk about getting our legs back in the third period. That's what we talk about. Not the points. 98 is good, sure, but if we don't play good game in April, what is 98, you know?

Jan Kowalczyk

You said something like that in December about individual numbers. Is this the same thing, or is it bigger?

Marian Gaborik

Yeah, it's, it's the same. I said it, you remember, I said the numbers don't go on the Cup. So yeah. Same thing. We want to be ready when it matters. Not in February.

Jan Kowalczyk

Last one. You're 21-9 on the road, which is the kind of record that wins in April. Does the room feel it? Does it know what it has?

Marian Gaborik

You know, I think we know. We know when we're playing good and we know when we're not. Tonight the first period we were sharp, the second was, was a little flat, but we got the goals. That's, that's what you want on the road. You get the goals and the game ends and you go to the hotel. That's it.

Jan Kowalczyk

Marian Gaborik, 44 on the year, back to you.
