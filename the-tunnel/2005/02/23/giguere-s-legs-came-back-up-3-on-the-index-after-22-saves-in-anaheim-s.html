Giguere's legs came back: up 3 on the Index after 22 saves in Anaheim's third straight

The Ducks' goaltender says his game has found its rhythm since January, and credits the men who filled Cole's spot

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelWednesday, 23 February 2005

2:33 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Jean-Sebastien Giguere [317] stopped 22 shots as Mighty Ducks of Anaheim beat New York Rangers 3-1 at home on 2005-02-21, his 29th win of a season that has seen him climb 3 on the Bureau's Development Index to an overall 97. Erik Cole was out of the lineup; Curtis Brown scored twice.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here with Jean-Sebastien Giguere, who stopped 22 in a 3-1 win over New York tonight. That's number 29 on the season, third in a row for Anaheim. How did it feel out there?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

Yeah, good game. The guys in front of me were, you know, they blocked a few, they cleared their crease when they had to. Twenty-two is, it's a, a normal night for me. We played a good team and we won. That's, that's what I take.

Marcus Bell

You told me after the Philadelphia game in January that you felt your form was right but you couldn't fix the other end. The Bureau's Development Index has you up 3 now. What changed?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

Yeah, I said that, yeah. I don't know if one thing changed. In January I was feeling it in practice, I knew it was there, but you can't always see it in the scoreboard. Now, the legs, they, they feel easy. Tonight I got stretched out twice and I was back for the rebound without thinking about it. In December I was a half-beat late on a few of those. Now the half-beat is gone, you know? It's just, the body does it.

Marcus Bell

Zero shutouts in 54 games though. You're at 97 in the Book. Does that zero sit with you?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

Yeah, for sure it sits. You know I want it. But, you know, I'm not going to change what I do tonight because a column says zero. Maybe next week, maybe next month. I'm not going to start pressing in the third period to get the empty column. The team winning 3-1, that's, that's the thing I look at.

Marcus Bell

Erik Cole wasn't in the lineup tonight. Curtis Brown had 2 goals, Jason Krog 2 assists. When a guy like Cole's out, who steps up differently?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

Cole's a big piece, his speed on the wing, you know, it opens the lane for Sergei. Tonight Curtis, he got 2 and that's, yeah. Curtis does the dirty work every night and he doesn't get the, you know, he doesn't get the highlights. When he gets a clean look he buries it. And Jason, Jason's been good, 2 assists, he moves the puck. We need that. With Erik out, we all got to give a little more, the guys on the 3rd and 4th line.

Marcus Bell

Sergei scored tonight, a point. You said in December he carries too much. Did it feel more distributed tonight?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

Yeah, a little. Sergei is Sergei, he's going to do his thing, he's the best player on our team, you know? But tonight the 4 lines, they were, they were close. Curtis, Jason, Matthew, the younger guys, they created a little. So Sergei didn't have to do every shift by himself. That's what we want. Not, not every night, Sergei, Sergei, Sergei. You know what I mean?

Marcus Bell

Three straight. 75 points. Is there a feeling in that room that something's turning?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

Yeah, a little, for sure. The guys walk a little different, you know? After a win streak, the morning skate is a little, a little lighter. But it's February. We've got a lot of hockey left. We just got to, we got to keep this going. One game, then the next one. That's it.

Marcus Bell

Jean-Sebastien, thank you for your time. Back to you.
