Giguere sees it cleaner from the crease on another 3-2 night

The Anaheim goaltender says the coach's defensive adjustments have changed what he sees through traffic, and he is not worried about the shutout that has not come.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelSaturday, 19 February 2005

2:12 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Jean-Sebastien Giguere93 stopped 20 of 22 shots as Mighty Ducks of Anaheim beat New York Islanders 3-2 at home on Saturday, his 27th win of a season in which he has played 52 games and recorded no shutouts. Two nights earlier he gave up 3 in a 3-2 loss at Nashville Predators. The Ducks sit at 23-18-11, one game into a win.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here at the boards with Jean-Sebastien Giguere. J.S., two straight 3-2 games, a win tonight and a loss in Nashville. That's six goals against on 39 shots across two nights. How's the body?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

Yeah, it's good. For sure, 39 shots over two games, that's not a bad workload. The body feels fine. It's just, you know, the 3-2 in Nashville, that one stings. Tonight is a different feeling. You get the two points, everything looks a little cleaner.

Jan Kowalczyk

I want to go back to something you told me in December, when the coach moved Cole up to center. You said the changes gave you speed and quieted your evening. Looking at tonight, 22 shots against, does it look like what you saw then?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

It's, it's the same idea. We're not, we're not letting them walk through the middle anymore. Tonight I saw it, especially second period. They get the puck in the zone but they have to go around, not through. The slot is, is not open the way it was in December. So I'm seeing pucks, you know, more clean. Less traffic on me.

Jan Kowalczyk

And the guys in front of you, Fedorov had three points again tonight, Prospal three in two games, Brown two goals in two games. When the offense is giving you three, does that change how you settle into a one-goal game?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

Yeah, it changes things. You don't need a miracle. I just, I just need to do my one job and they give me the lead. Sergei, he makes plays that take the pressure off. He doesn't have to carry it every night. Tonight the defense did a good job too. Havelid blocked some shots I think nobody else saw. That's what wins you the 3-2 games.

Jan Kowalczyk

Marcus asked you about this in December, the shutout that hasn't come. You're at 52 games, 27 wins, and zero clean sheets. Does the streak sit with you or is it noise?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

No, it sits. For sure it sits. I don't like it. But I told Marcus the same thing I'll tell you. The guys are giving me 3-2 games. That means they're protecting me enough to keep it close but we're not, we're not putting up six or seven to make it easy. I'll get one. I'm not losing sleep over a line in the book. The wins matter more. 27 is 27.

Jan Kowalczyk

You've been in net 52 times this year. Does that tell you anything about how the coach sees the season?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

Yeah, it tells me he's not, he's not resting me much. No, I like it. I want to play. The guys, Gerber and Bryzgalov, they work hard in practice. I know they're ready. But I feel good. I want the 60. If he puts me in every night I'll be there every night.

Jan Kowalczyk

J.S., thank you. Enjoy the two points. Back to you.
