Bryan Murray liked the win, not his own February

The Ottawa coach was blunt about a four-game stretch that still leaves the Senators at 20 and 28, defended Patrick Lalime's workload, and said the week against New Jersey and Nashville is the one that tells them what they are.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelWednesday, 16 February 2005

2:49 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Bryan Murray's Ottawa Senators beat the best home team in the league tonight, 4-1 at Toronto Maple Leafs, on a night Ottawa outshot Toronto 34 to 10. The Senators sit 20-28-7 at 55 points and 11-21 on the road, and they had dropped two straight to Pittsburgh and Edmonton before this one. Marcus Bell caught the coach in the corridor after the game, his first visit to this desk.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here with Ottawa's head coach, Bryan Murray, in the tunnel at Toronto after a 4-1 road win. Coach, your club is eleven and twenty-one away, Toronto is twenty-five and five at home, and the road team won, outshooting the house team thirty-four to ten. What did you see?

Bryan Murray

Good. Honestly, good from the first shift. The compete level was there, we managed the puck, we came in deep, we got pucks to the net. Thirty-four shots on the road, that's the guys being there, that's what I wanted to see all week.

Marcus Bell

And what did you not like? Give me the other half.

Bryan Murray

The last three weeks, most of them. You're asking me about one period of one game and I'm sitting here at twenty and twenty-eight. Tonight gets cleaned up in two days if Sunday's the same club that played in Pittsburgh.

Marcus Bell

You lost those two in Pittsburgh and to Edmonton, one in overtime. What changed between Friday and tonight?

Bryan Murray

Nothing changed. That's the frustrating part, we've been playing mostly the same hockey. We're seven one-goal games in the building, go look at the standings, that's the whole difference. You win two of those, you're talking to me about fifty points, not whatever we're at. Hockey's cruel, that's it.

Marcus Bell

Structure question. Did you move anybody's spot this week, or is that room exactly where you left it?

Bryan Murray

Nothing moved. I don't move men when the work's right, and the work was mostly right, the results were wrong. Same four lines, and tonight the structure paid.

Marcus Bell

Patrick Lalime has started fifty of fifty-nine, eighty-five percent of the games, on a nineteen and twenty-eight record. Does Martin Prusek see the ice this week?

Bryan Murray

Paddy's played every game he's been fine, and you can put nineteen and twenty-eight out there if you want, I'll put the guys in front of him out there too. Martin's ready, he always is, but I'm not going to answer that on a microphone. We'll play hockey.

Marcus Bell

Marian Hossa's out to March 29, a fifty-six-point right winger. How do you carry six weeks without him?

Bryan Murray

It's a long stretch, no question about it. You slide people up. Radek's back on the left and he's got forty-one points for a reason, Fisher's there, Todd White's there. Zdeno's playing every situation, fifty-nine straight, he doesn't care who's next to him. We play it until we get him back.

Marcus Bell

The week ahead: New York Sunday at home, then New Jersey on the road, then Florida, then Nashville. Three of those four clubs have sixty-seven points or more.

Bryan Murray

And they're the games we'll learn something in. New Jersey's won three straight, seventy-seven points. Nashville's won four, same number. We show up there the way we showed up tonight, we know something. We don't, we're going to have a long spring.

Marcus Bell

The Rangers signed Eric Lindros Tuesday. What did you make of that?

Bryan Murray

I heard they signed him Monday, and I heard today he's out to April with a concussion. Four days. I'll leave that one for their doctor, I really will.

Marcus Bell

Florida's nineteen and twenty-nine. Is that the trap game of the week?

Bryan Murray

Don't give me trap games. We've lost to everybody this year, we're not in a position to look past one club, sure not the one that'll sit back and make us work for it. We play Florida like we played tonight or we lose to Florida.

Marcus Bell

Last one. What do you take out of tonight into Sunday?

Bryan Murray

The first shift. That's it, that's what I'll show them Sunday. Then we'll find out if it was us, or just a night.

Marcus Bell

Coach, thank you. Bryan Murray there after the win in Toronto. Marcus Bell, rinkside, back to you.
