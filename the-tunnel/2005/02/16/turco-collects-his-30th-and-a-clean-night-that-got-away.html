Turco collects his 30th, and a clean night that got away

The Dallas goaltender takes the win number in stride but bristles at one shutout in 48 starts.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelWednesday, 16 February 2005

2:07 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Marty Turco93 picked up his 30th win of the season on Monday night, stopping 1 goal against on 20 shots as Dallas Stars beat Mighty Ducks of Anaheim 3-1 at home. The Dallas goaltender now sits at 30-17 with 134 goals against in 48 starts, and the Stars lead the Pacific at 76 points. He has one shutout on the season.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here with Marty Turco in the corridor after the game. Thirty wins on the night, a 3-1 win over Anaheim, 19 saves on 20 shots. How does number thirty feel?

Marty Turco

I mean, yeah, it's a win. Thirty is a number. You know, Phil had two tonight and Stu got one, so the boys gave me a little cushion there. Thirty just means the guys in front of me played enough to get me the two points.

Jan Kowalczyk

Boucher's two goals came early, and the game never looked tight. But that one goal against in the second, how'd that get through?

Marty Turco

You know, I had him covered on the release, and then there was a body in front and I just, I think I saw it a beat late. You know what gets me though? Not the goal itself. One goal on twenty, I'll take it. What gets me is we've got one shutout in forty-eight games. Forty-eight. And tonight was right there and we couldn't close it out.

Jan Kowalczyk

One in forty-eight. You just told me in January that your road numbers, the legs and the sleep thing. You're fifteen and fifteen on the road now. Has it changed at all?

Marty Turco

Fifteen and fifteen. Yeah, that's what it says. And look, I'm not gonna sit here and tell you we went out and fixed it. We haven't. You play well on the road you win, you don't, you don't. But I'll tell you what, the sleep thing, it's real. You fly somewhere new, you get there at two in the afternoon, you sleep wrong, you're flat. It's not mystical. But we're fifteen and fifteen and I don't feel like a team that sleeps great.

Jan Kowalczyk

You're at 76 points, twelve ahead of Anaheim in the Pacific. What does the rest of the schedule ask of you? You've got twenty-two games left and you're a win away from forty in a season.

Marty Turco

Now you're gonna get me thinking about numbers, Jan. No, look, I'm not thinking about forty. I'm thinking about one more shutout. That's what bugs me. I don't want forty wins with one blank. I want, I don't know, three or four. The schedule doesn't matter to me right now. What matters is we lock one down tonight and we can't. So there's that.

Jan Kowalczyk

The Bureau has you at 96 tonight, up one from your base. You've been up all month. Is that a confidence thing or a work thing?

Marty Turco

I'll tell you, I don't know where the Bureau gets their numbers from and I don't care. What I'll say is, I feel good. I've felt good all year. The glove's there, the legs are there, and the guys in front of me, when they're locked in, they're really good. Modano's doing his thing, Arnott's doing his thing, and Morrow back there is a beast. You ride that.

Jan Kowalczyk

Last thing. You told me back in December that you own the first goal. You still carry it that way?

Marty Turco

Yeah, obviously. I mean, I'm the last guy. You're not gonna get me to say it's the defense. It's mine. Tonight was close to being a clean night and I let one through, so I own that too. But thirty is thirty and we won the game. I'll live with it.

Jan Kowalczyk

Marty Turco, thirty on the year, one night that got away. Thanks, Marty. Back to you.
