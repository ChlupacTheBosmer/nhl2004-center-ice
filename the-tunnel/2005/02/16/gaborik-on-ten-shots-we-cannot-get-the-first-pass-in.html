Gaborik on ten shots: 'We cannot get the first pass in'

The winger said the Senators' structure took his first step away, and that the building could not help a team that never got to its game.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelWednesday, 16 February 2005

1:50 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Marian Gaborik98 was held without a point as the Toronto Maple Leafs Maple Leafs lost 4-1 at home to the Ottawa Senators Senators on Tuesday night. Toronto managed 10 shots, against 34 for Ottawa. The league's highest-scoring team, 296 goals in 58 games, was held to one goal for the first time in a while.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here at the Air Canada Centre with Marian Gaborik. Ten shots tonight. You're a team that scores goals, and you had ten. What happened out there?

Marian Gaborik

Yeah, I mean, we were, I think the first period they come out and they already, how you say, they put the body there before I get there. And we cannot make the first pass clean. You know, it's not that we are tired. It's the first pass. We need one good pass to open it up and tonight we don't get it. Maybe three times, four times. Not more.

Jan Kowalczyk

I asked you in January, after Boston had 32 shots on us, how this room resets when the shots don't come. You said the result is on Toronto. Tonight you're down two in the second, the building is trying to lift you, and you get three shots in a period.

Marian Gaborik

Yeah, the building is good tonight, I think they are, you know, they keep pushing. But the crowd cannot make the first pass for you. In Boston, I think, we get chances, we shoot, the puck just does not go. Tonight is different. Tonight they take the speed from us before we even get it. That's harder, how you say, that's harder to fix in one game.

Jan Kowalczyk

So what does Ottawa do that nobody else has done this year? You've been playing well, 43 goals, you're not the problem.

Marian Gaborik

They play, I think, a little shorter. Their defensemen are not up at the blue line like other teams. So when I get the puck I think I have one step and I have... nothing. They already wait. You know? And Gary, he gets one through, his goal, and for a minute maybe, maybe we think we can, you know, one more and then. But no. They just, they keep the structure. All night.

Jan Kowalczyk

You lost now. First loss here in a while at home. Does the room talk about it tonight or is it tomorrow on the tape?

Marian Gaborik

We talk a little. We watch some, maybe one clip, two clips, to see what they do different. And tomorrow we see again. But I think, you know, this is one game. We play a lot of games left. If we play like tonight four times, then we have a problem. One night is... it happens.

Jan Kowalczyk

Marian Gaborik, thank you. Back to you.
