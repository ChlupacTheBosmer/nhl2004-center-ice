Peter Forsberg Gives Boston to Jamie Storr and Keeps His Own Quiet

After a 3-0 road shutout, Forsberg put the win on his goaltender, his winger, and his team's 17-8 road record, and left his 90-point season out of it.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelSunday, 6 February 2005

1:43 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Peter Forsberg95 scored once in Colorado Avalanche's two-game road trip, the quieter stretch for a man carrying 40 goals and 90 points through 51 games. Marcus Bell caught him in the tunnel after a 3-0 win at Boston on February 4, where the story was Jamie Storr84's shutout and a winger named Teemu Selanne.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here with Peter Forsberg in the tunnel after a 3-0 win on the road in Boston. Peter, Jamie Storr gives you a shutout tonight. What does that do for this room?

Peter Forsberg

I think it is good for everyone. Jamie was, uh, he was sharp. He gave us what we needed when they were pushing. You know, sometimes you win because you score a lot, sometimes you win because the other team just, they don't, the puck doesn't go in. Tonight it was the second one. For sure.

Marcus Bell

Teemu Selanne has 2 goals and 3 points across these two games. He looks locked in.

Peter Forsberg

Yeah. Teemu, he is, uh, he doesn't need to do much. He is in the right spot and the puck finds him. That's, you know, that's the skill. Good tonight, good the game before. That's what you want from a player like that.

Marcus Bell

You've got 1 point in 2 games on this trip. You're at 90 through 51, so by your standard, two quiet nights.

Peter Forsberg

Yeah, of course. But I told Jan once, I would trade my goals for the team's points. I still say it. We won 3-0 tonight, Jamie gave up nothing. I don't, uh, I don't need my point for that.

Marcus Bell

You said it in January. You're saying it again in February, so I'll take you at it.

Peter Forsberg

Yes. I don't say it for the camera, you know? It's just, 90 points, it means something if the wins come. If they don't, it's just, uh, numbers on a page.

Marcus Bell

You're on the road, New York then Boston. What does the rest of this trip ask of you?

Peter Forsberg

Well, for us, the road is, you know, it is not so bad this year. We are 17 and 8 away. That's not nothing. Maybe we play a little better away, I don't know why. But it's not the road that is a problem. We just need to keep it simple. Move it, shoot it. The things we do.

Marcus Bell

You sit at 65 points, chasing Vancouver and Edmonton and Nashville in the Northwest. Do you look at that race?

Peter Forsberg

I look. Of course. But when I look too much I start to, you know... it's better to just, uh, think about the next shift. The table will be there tomorrow.

Marcus Bell

Peter Forsberg, thank you. Enjoy the win. Marcus Bell, back to you.

Peter Forsberg

Thank you.
