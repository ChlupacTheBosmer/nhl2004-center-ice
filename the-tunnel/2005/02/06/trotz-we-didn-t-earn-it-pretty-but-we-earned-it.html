Trotz: 'We didn't earn it pretty, but we earned it'

The Predators coach on winning ugly in Detroit, filling Greg Johnson's hole on both special teams, and what the first place in the Central is actually worth in February

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelSunday, 6 February 2005

3:05 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Barry Trotz's Nashville Predators sit first in the Central Division at 29-15-5, 71 points, after a 3-2 overtime win on the road at Detroit Red Wings on 2005-02-04. They were outshot 27-22. Greg Johnson left the lineup with an ankle late in the week and is not expected back until 2005-02-11, which cost Trotz a man from both the power play and the penalty kill in the same stretch.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here with Barry Trotz in the tunnel at Joe Louis Arena. Barry, you just won 3-2 in overtime on the road, you're 29-15-5 and first in the Central, and you were outshot 27-22. How's the room tonight?

Barry Trotz

You know, two points is two points. I mean, I'd love to sit here and tell you we dominated, that we imposed our will, but we didn't. They had more chances, they had more bodies to the net, and we got two. That's the league, you know? Some nights you earn it pretty. Tonight we earned it the other way and I'll take it.

Jan Kowalczyk

Twenty-seven to twenty-two. That's a lot of shots. What did you see from your group in the third period when things got tight?

Barry Trotz

We got quiet, honestly. We stopped trying to make the right play and started making the safe play, and that's a win. Tomas was the one holding the rope for us. Forty-three games in, he's twenty-seven wins, and he just keeps doing it. I don't need him to do anything other than what he's doing. The guys in front of him had to block a few more than they liked and they did it. That's the compete level we talk about in practice, you know, that willingness to eat a puck when you're tired. We found it in the third.

Jan Kowalczyk

Let me take you to Greg Johnson. He went down this week, ankle, back February 11th. He's got fourteen goals in twenty-six games. That's a big hole. What happened with the lines?

Barry Trotz

Yeah, well, Greg's been a big part of what we do. Fourteen goals in twenty-six is not an accident, he finds soft spots in the zone and he attacks them. So when you lose him, you don't just lose a center, you lose a guy who was on the second power play and who was killing too. So I'm looking at coming in at the second center spot. Tom's not going to give you fourteen goals in twenty-six games, he's thirty-six and he's coming in to play a role. But he'll do the defensive stuff, he'll win his draws, he'll make the simple play. And moves up to third line, he's in there on the power play now.

Jan Kowalczyk

That's your special teams question right there. Greg was on P2 and on the penalty kill. Now Gratton's on the power play, and I see Martin Rucinsky picked up a shorthanded slot tonight. That's a reorganization on the fly.

Barry Trotz

It is. And look, has got thirty-eight points in forty-four games, he's not a natural penalty killer, but he's thirty-three, he's smart, he reads the play in front of him. I asked him if he could take a shift down there and he said yeah, and he did. That's the thing about a veteran, you know, you don't have to overthink it. You just tell him what you need. The power play's a little different without Greg. He was finding that fourth man on the half-wall and now we're sorting it out. Gratton's more of a net presence, he wants to drive to the front. It's a different look. We'll be fine, but it's not going to look the same for a week.

Jan Kowalczyk

You're first in the Central, 71 points, and the Predators' best finish has been a playoff appearance. What's the mood in the room when you're sitting where you're sitting?

Barry Trotz

I think the room knows what it is. They've played fifty-three games. They know it's a long season, they know you can go three and five and slide four spots. So nobody here is looking at the standings, you know? That's a March conversation. My job is to keep them in the next shift, the next game. We've got Columbus at home Thursday, that's a team that beat us last week. So the mood is, fine, we got two, we got a bad taste from Saturday, let's get those back. That's all it is.

Jan Kowalczyk

Last question. Greg back in seven days, what changes when he walks back in?

Barry Trotz

Everything, basically. No, I mean, the good news is will have had a week to settle and will have looked good on the power play, so I've got a choice to make when Greg comes back. That's a nice problem. I'll figure it out, I've got until Thursday to think about it. Bar to you, Jan.
