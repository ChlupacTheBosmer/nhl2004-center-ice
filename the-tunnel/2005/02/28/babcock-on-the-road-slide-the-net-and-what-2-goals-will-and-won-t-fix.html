Babcock on the road slide, the net, and what 2 goals will and won't fix

The Ducks coach defended his goaltending switch and called his team's effort inconsistent after a 2-4 loss at Florida that made it two straight.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelMonday, 28 February 2005

3:24 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Mighty Ducks of Anaheim dropped their second straight on the road trip, falling 2-4 at Florida Panthers on February 26 after an overtime loss in Tampa Bay the night before. Head coach Mike Babcock spoke with The Tunnel's Marcus Bell after the game about the effort in the loss, his decision to dress Martin Gerber over Jean-Sebastien Giguere, and what he expects from a room that has lost four of its last five.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here with Mike Babcock, thanks for sitting down. Two straight on the road, a 2-4 one tonight in a building where you were outshot 26 to 20. Give me the effort from your side.

Mike Babcock

You know, there were stretches where I liked it. The first twenty, I thought we moved the puck, we managed it well enough. And then the second period, we got, I don't know, we got loose with it. Turnovers in our own end, chips instead of a first pass. So you lose the structure, you lose the puck battles on the wall, and then you're chasing. And chasing costs you a goal. Costs you two.

Marcus Bell

You made a change in net tonight. Martin Gerber started, Jean-Sebastien Giguere sat. What went into that call?

Mike Babcock

Well. It's not a referendum on anything. He's played 55 games for us this year. Fifty-five. You don't undo that with one night. But I thought, look, we'd been on the road, we'd given up a late one the night before in Tampa in overtime, and sometimes a guy just needs to, you know, he needs to get his legs back, needs to be fresh. And Martin's a good goalie. He's in our room, he's in our group, and I wanted to see him tonight.

Marcus Bell

Back in December, Jan Kowalczyk was on this same ice and you said Giguere was your starter and that the losses were about the team playing around him, not a net problem. Is tonight a walk-back on that?

Mike Babcock

No. No, it's not. That's still true. The team around him, that hasn't changed. We're still a team that's giving up too many grade-two chances in our own zone and that's, you know, that's a coverage thing. That's a structure thing. And we'll fix that. But sometimes as a coach you feel like you try something, you give a different look, and maybe it wakes up a couple of guys in front. That's all it is.

Marcus Bell

Two goals. Two in the overtime one, two tonight. You've got Fedorov putting up 95 points and the team's only at 189 for the season. Where is the secondary scoring coming from?

Mike Babcock

It's coming. It's coming. You know, Andy McDonald's got 35, Tuomas Lydman's got 24 in 58 games, he's twenty years old, he's finding it. The problem is, we get our chances, I'll tell you that, we get three or four high-danger looks a night and we're not converting. That's not a structure thing, that's a puck going in the net thing. And obviously you want Sergei to get help. But we're not going to score five a night. We're not that team. We've got to win those 2-1, 3-2 ones.

Marcus Bell

What's the mood in the room after four in five, on the road, heading into a building like Phoenix that's on a five-game run?

Mike Babcock

You know, it's not a funeral in there. I'll give you that much. Nobody's happy. Nobody's happy losing two in two nights. But these guys, they've been together, they've been through worse stretches than this, and the compete level in practice tomorrow is going to be high. It's always high. That's not a concern. The concern is, can we play sixty minutes with the structure we talked about, can we manage the puck in the neutral zone, and can we keep the game tight? Because when we do those things, we're hard to beat.

Marcus Bell

One more thing. Alexei Smirnov's out with that leg, back around the 21st. Is he the only one you're waiting on?

Mike Babcock

He's the only one on the shelf, yeah. You know, it's not a huge hole, he's not 30 points for you a night, but he gives you a fourth line that can hold pucks, that can be physical in the corners. Without him we're short a body. So maybe you see somebody from the minors get called up, maybe you shuffle the bottom six. We'll figure it out. It's, you know, it's a Tuesday in February, you adapt or you die.

Marcus Bell

Phoenix on a five-game run, what do you key on for that one?

Mike Babcock

They're a fast team. They've got speed up the ice and they don't turn the puck over much, and they're on a run so everything goes in for them right now. You know how that goes. The details will matter. If we're turning pucks over at our own blue line against a team that has that much speed, you're going to be chasing all night. And I don't want to chase all night. I'm getting too old for that. Back to you, Marcus.

Marcus Bell

Thank you, coach.
