Bertuzzi's hat trick keeps Vancouver rolling, but he wants the four goals gone

The Canucks winger scored three in a 5-4 overtime win over Atlanta and walked straight to the part of the game his team wants erased.

Todd Bertuzzi89 scored 3 goals in Vancouver Canucks's 5-4 overtime win over Atlanta Thrashers on Saturday night, his third hat trick of a season that has him at 107 points through 63 games. The Canucks pushed their streak to 4 and sit first in the Northwest with 85 points. They were also outshot 28-22.
