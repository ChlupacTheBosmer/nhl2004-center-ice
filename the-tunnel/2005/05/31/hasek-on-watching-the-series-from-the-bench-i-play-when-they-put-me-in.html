Hasek on watching the series from the bench: 'I play when they put me in'

Swept in three, benched for Legace, the 40-year-old addresses his own 52-game season and the nine points the Index has taken from him.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelTuesday, 31 May 2005

2:18 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Dominik Hasek has watched the end of the season from the side, 0.9 minutes across three playoff losses, the net belonging to Manny Legace84. His 52 games and 0 shutouts bring him to a Development Index 9 points below his base grade. Jan Kowalczyk caught him in the tunnel.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here, in the tunnel, the arena emptying behind me. Dominik, three in a row, the season over in Game 3, and you from the side. Fifty-two games this year, no shutout. How do you sit with that, with watching it from outside?

Dominik Hasek

It is... not easy. You want to be there, in the net, when the thing is on the line. But the coach, he makes the call. I respect that. I am here, I am ready, and I play when they put me in. That is the job.

Jan Kowalczyk

Legace starts Games 1 and 2 and you never get the nod. You saw something in practice, something you thought, okay, this is coming?

Dominik Hasek

You know, I see practice every day. Manny, he is... good. He is in a good spot, how you say. But I don't look at the other side and think about my spot. I look at my own thing. My legs, they are here. That is what I can control.

Jan Kowalczyk

Marcus spoke with you in December about reading Lidstrom's back, your positioning off the D. Did you notice the pairings shifting in this series? Lidstrom and Hatcher, they looked more aggressive, more pressure coming up.

Dominik Hasek

Yes, yes. The first period they come up hard, they want to... how you say, put the pressure on the play before it reaches the blue line. I see it from the bench and I think, the puck is behind them, they are... committed. When I am in the net, I call for them. I can't call from the bench. That is the problem.

Jan Kowalczyk

Your grade on the Development Index is down nine from your base, 84 where your base is 93. Consistency at 50. Does that number bother you?

Dominik Hasek

I know the number. Everybody has the number, right? I'm not... I don't need anyone to tell me what I did this year. One win, thirty losses. Yes. I know. But the toughness is there. The agility, the body, they are here. It's the... the every night thing. The consistency. Yes. That one I lose. At my age, you know? You can be the best goalie in the world one night and...

Dominik Hasek

And then you are not. You know. That is the thing.

Jan Kowalczyk

You've got five years and $9.80M left on the contract. Forty years old. What does Dominik Hasek see when he looks at next season?

Dominik Hasek

I see next September. I see the ice. I see the net. I am forty, yes, but look, I am not... I am not finished. I can be the guy. I know I can be the guy. The body, the mind. You know. I just need, how you say, the luck with the consistency. That's all.

Jan Kowalczyk

Dominik Hasek, thank you for your time. Back to you.
