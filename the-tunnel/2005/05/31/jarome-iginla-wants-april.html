Jarome Iginla Wants April

The Richard Trophy winner and new Pearson winner on nine years in Calgary, a 76-goal season that went nowhere, and what he needs to see before July.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelTuesday, 31 May 2005

3:06 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Jarome Iginla86 scored 76 goals and 128 points for Calgary Flames in 2004-05, winning the Maurice Richard Trophy for the second straight year and taking the Lester B. Pearson Award as the players' choice. Calgary missed the postseason. His contract reads $6.50M with zero years left.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here with Jarome Iginla. Jarome, appreciate you sitting down.

Jarome Iginla

Sure thing, Jan. Glad to be here.

Jan Kowalczyk

So you're a St. Albert kid. Edmonton's your town. And here you are, nine years in Calgary, the wrong side of the province.

Jarome Iginla

Yeah. And that's what my family back home thinks, too. I grew up an Oiler, everybody there did, you know? I played goalie the first two years trying to be Grant Fuhr. Then Dallas drafts me in the summer of '95, ships me here in December for Nieuwendyk, and I'm 18 years old. So, wrong city? I don't know. Nine years later this is home. The city works for hockey. People here show up in January when it's minus thirty and the building is shaking. That counts.

Jan Kowalczyk

What does the building do for you? You've been in front of those people a long time.

Jarome Iginla

It's loud. When we get going, it's genuinely loud, and I'll take that over a half-building any night of the week. There's games where you can hear them before the puck drops. Gets under your skin. In a good way.

Jan Kowalczyk

76 goals. The Richard Trophy again, back to back. And now the Pearson, your peers voted you the best player in the league. That's a hell of a season. Does that soften missing the postseason?

Jarome Iginla

No. No, it doesn't. People say, well, you got the hardware, you should feel good. And I'm not kicking the trophy, it means something to me. But it made it harder, not easier. You look at it and you think, I did my part. I did it as good as I could. And we're still not there. That just highlights how far the team was, you know?

Jan Kowalczyk

Last year you had 92 goals and the same thing happened. Did this year show you something you didn't see last year?

Jarome Iginla

I think so. I think last year I was, I don't know, I was too hard on myself. Like if I just scored 10 more, we're in. And 76, same result. So I look around and I think, the guys next to you, that's everything. Conroy wins the draw and gets me the puck in a spot I can use, that's all I need. But some nights the whole thing just wasn't there, and you can be good enough by yourself and it still don't get you there.

Jan Kowalczyk

You said the word room earlier. Talk to me about what's in that room around you. Conroy, Turek, Gelinas.

Jarome Iginla

Craig is the guy, you know? He does everything nobody watches. Faceoffs, backchecks, the whole thing. And people look at the point total and think, well. He makes me better. That's all I need. Turek's been steady. Gelinas too. It's not that the guys don't try. I need to be clear on that. They try every night. It's more that we need more. You need another guy who can take the puck and go when the game's on the line.

Jan Kowalczyk

Your contract has zero years left. $6.50M comes off in July. You're free.

Jarome Iginla

That's the situation, yeah. And I'm not going to pretend I haven't thought about it. You're 27, you've got some good years left, and you get one decision that really matters. And it's not about the money. It never is at this level. It's about where you spend them.

Jan Kowalczyk

Does Calgary get you?

Jarome Iginla

I don't know. I really don't. I love this city. I love these fans. But I've been here nine years watching the same thing happen over and over. I need to believe the guys in charge want to build around me. I need to see a path to April. I can't do this again. I just can't.

Jan Kowalczyk

You didn't choose this city. Dallas chose it for you.

Jarome Iginla

No, I didn't. I was 18. My grandfather, he drove me to every game in St. Albert, every tryout, everything. He's the reason I'm even in this. And then I'm on a plane out here and he's not in the building. That's a memory. But you make it work. You learn the city. Nine years, you've got friends here, you've got routine. Leaving that is not nothing.

Jan Kowalczyk

December, after one of those Toronto games. You were leading the league on 60 points and you told me the shift Calgary could play. Do you still believe that?

Jarome Iginla

Yeah, I do. We played those games and we were right there. We were right there all year. That's not the point, is it? You can be right there and still not get it done. You need to be right there late, in the last ten minutes when the thing's on the line. And we weren't. Not enough. That's the whole story.

Jan Kowalczyk

Jarome Iginla, thank you for the time.

Jarome Iginla

Thanks, Jan. Appreciate it.
