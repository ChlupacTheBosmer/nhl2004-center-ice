Pierre-Marc Bouchard on the number everyone has circled: Checking 52

The twenty-one-year-old Wild center says he knows what the grade says about him, names the player he watches every time, and explains what changed between a 10-point season and a 29-point one.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelMonday, 23 May 2005

5:42 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Pierre-Marc Bouchard79 finished with 14 goals and 15 assists in 82 games for Minnesota Wild this season, up from 10 points in 36 games last year. The Sweater ran a profile Tuesday titled "Speed 90, Checking 52" and the kid took it with a shrug. Jan Kowalczyk sat with him in the studio after taping.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here with Pierre-Marc Bouchard, the center from Minnesota. Pierre-Marc, good to see you. I want to start where everyone's starting. The Sweater's piece on you Tuesday, the headline was Speed 90, Checking 52. Does that read fair to you?

Pierre-Marc Bouchard

Yeah. It's, it reads fair. That's what, that's what the numbers say, for sure. Speed is, is up there. Checking is down there. I know that. I, I don't, I don't read the piece and think that's wrong.

Jan Kowalczyk

So you've seen your Development Index too. The Bureau has you at plus six right now, overall 77 off a base of 74. You're going the right direction but people are still writing about the Checking 52.

Pierre-Marc Bouchard

Well, you know, that number it, it doesn't mean I go on the ice and, and check somebody into the boards. What it means is, is I'm small, I'm 165, and the, the guys are not small. That's, that's it. I can't change my body right now. I can be in position. I can be, I can be first to the puck. That's what I do.

Jan Kowalczyk

Does that frustrate you? That people look at a grade and decide what you are?

Pierre-Marc Bouchard

A little, yeah. A little. But look, I'm twenty-one. I'm not, I'm not going to, to pretend I'm not going to hear it. The guys here, Saku, he's been here ten years, he tells me the same thing. You get a number, people write a story about you. But, but inside the room, nobody cares about the number. They care if you're on the right side of the ice. They care if you're open for the pass.

Jan Kowalczyk

Let me ask about your coach then. The two angles I came with tonight, and one of them is the coach who changed you. Because last year you played 36 games, 10 points, 11 minutes a night. This year 82, 29, 11.6. What did he change?

Pierre-Marc Bouchard

He, he trusted me more. That's the honest answer. Last year, after the first, the first two months, I wasn't in the lineup some nights. He sat me. And that's, that's fair, I wasn't good enough. This year he, he told me in training camp, you're going to play every game, but I need you back on the, back on the defensive side. You can't just, just skate and look for the pass. You have to, you have to be there when we lose it.

Jan Kowalczyk

And you gave him that. 13 penalty minutes, not a brawler's number, but you were on the ice 82 times.

Pierre-Marc Bouchard

Yeah. I didn't miss a game and I, I wasn't hiding. That's, that's what he wanted. And I think, I think he saw that. You know, I'm not, I'm not a guy who's going to hit somebody hard, that's not me. But I'm there. My stick is there. That's what, what he asked me and I gave him that.

Jan Kowalczyk

You said Saku. Saku Koivu, he's 85 on the Book, he's been your captain. Is he the one who taught you the most about, about being a center here?

Pierre-Marc Bouchard

Oh, yeah, for sure. Saku is, is the, the guy. He's, he's 85 on the Book and he's, he's always 85, he's been 85 for, for years. He's so consistent. He doesn't do anything crazy. He's always in position, he's always, always on the same page. I watch him a lot. Before the game, in the meetings, I just, I just sit next to him and I, I ask him, why are you here? And he shows me. That's, that's the best way.

Jan Kowalczyk

Let me go to the other angle. A rival you respect. Who do you go after? When you're on the other side of the ice, who do you think is the toughest or the best?

Pierre-Marc Bouchard

Mike Modano. I think, I think Modano is the, is the guy. He's, he's 34 and he's still doing everything. 125 points, right? And he's not, he's not, he's not fast anymore, not like when he was young. But the positioning, the, the way he gets open, you know, you're watching him and you think, how is he, how is he there already? He's already there before you, before you turn around.

Jan Kowalczyk

Is that what you want to be? A 34-year-old Modano? Because you're a speed guy, and he's, by his own admission, not a speed guy anymore.

Pierre-Marc Bouchard

Well, I mean, I don't think about being 34 yet. But, but yeah. You know, the speed, it, it goes. Everybody's speed goes. What, what doesn't go is the brain, if, if you build it right. And, and Modano, he built it right. He's got the puck and he, he finds the open guy. He, he's not, he's not the fastest guy on the ice anymore but he's still 125. So yeah, I watch him. I watch how he moves before he gets the puck.

Jan Kowalczyk

That's the Development Index at plus six talking, by the way. The Bureau sees the form going up.

Pierre-Marc Bouchard

Maybe. I don't, I don't really read the, the, the reports. My agent reads them. But I feel better than last year. That's, that's the truth. I feel like I understand the game more. I'm not, I'm not guessing where to be as much. Last year I'd, I'd read it wrong two, three times a game. This year maybe once, maybe twice. That's improvement.

Jan Kowalczyk

Your potential on the Book is 88. You're at 77 now, base 74. That's eleven points of growth between you and what the Bureau thinks you can be. Does that number sit on you?

Pierre-Marc Bouchard

No. No, I don't, I don't think like that. I just, I just want to score more. I had 14 goals, that's not, that's not enough. I want to get to 20. That's, that's my, my number for next year. 20 goals. The rest, the rest is, is other people's math.

Jan Kowalczyk

One year left on your contract, $1.20 million. You're a restricted free agent after next season. Do you think about that?

Pierre-Marc Bouchard

A little. You, you think about it in the summer, maybe. But, but right now I'm just, I'm just playing. And look, if, if I score 20, 30, 40 points, whatever, then we'll talk. And if, if I don't, then it doesn't matter what the contract says, right? You have to, you have to play first. The, the money comes after. For sure.

Jan Kowalczyk

You mentioned Wes Walz earlier, in passing. He's 84 on the Book, a center here. What's his role in your development?

Pierre-Marc Bouchard

Wes is, is the, the guy who does the, the dirty work. He's, he's not, he's not a scorer like Saku, like, like me, but he's always in the, in the right spot in his own zone. He blocks shots. He, he hits people. And he's, he's good to me, you know. He's one of the guys who, who makes the young guys feel, feel part of it. He doesn't, he doesn't act like, like a big star. He just, just works. And I, I like that.

Jan Kowalczyk

Last one. The room is 34 guys, average age 28. You're 21. When do you stop being the young guy here?

Pierre-Marc Bouchard

I don't know. Maybe never, you know? Saku is 30, Wes is, is older, Manny is 30. I'm, I'm not going to be the guy for a while. And that's, that's fine. I, I have one year, maybe more, to just, just learn. We'll see what happens after that.

Jan Kowalczyk

Pierre-Marc Bouchard, thanks for your time tonight.

Pierre-Marc Bouchard

Thank you, Jan. Thank you.
