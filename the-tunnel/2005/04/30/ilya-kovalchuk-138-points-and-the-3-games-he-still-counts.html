Ilya Kovalchuk: 138 points, and the 3 games he still counts

The Atlanta Thrashers® left wing says the number he is proudest of is 79 games, not 138 points, and names Dany Heatley as the best player he has skated with.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelSaturday, 30 April 2005

2:59 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Ilya Kovalchuk91 finished the season with 63 goals and 138 points in 79 games, tied with Markus Naslund87 for second in the league behind Jaromir Jagr91's 145. The Bureau's Book has the 22-year-old at 90 overall, up five on the Development Index, with his skating at 99. He came into the studio this week to talk about the line, the shoulder he hurt in March, and the teammate he would take with him.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here, in the studio with Ilya Kovalchuk of the Atlanta Thrashers®. Ilya, thanks for coming in. Your season: 79 games, 63 goals, 75 assists, 138 points. When you look at that line, what do you see first?

Ilya Kovalchuk

The 3 games I don't play. That's the first thing. Of course the points is nice. But first I see the 3.

Marcus Bell

3 out of 82. Nobody plays all of them.

Ilya Kovalchuk

I want to. I want to be the guy who is there every night, you know? That is the number for me. 82.

Marcus Bell

And the shoulder, back in March. Sometime between the 25th and the 30th. You were back on the 30th.

Ilya Kovalchuk

Yeah. You feel it and everything goes quiet in your head, because it is the shoulder. Same one as when I am 18. So the doctor says wait. I wait 4 days, 5 days. Back on the 30th. That's it.

Marcus Bell

The first one, at 18, that cost you 17 games.

Ilya Kovalchuk

17. I sit and I watch and I hate it. You learn things on the couch you don't want to learn. That is why 79 is my number.

Marcus Bell

So of everything on your record, that's the one. 79 games.

Ilya Kovalchuk

Yes. 79. And 138 is close behind. But 79.

Marcus Bell

138 points. Second in the league, tied with Markus Naslund, behind Jaromir Jagr's 145. You would put the games ahead of that?

Ilya Kovalchuk

Yes. Look, the points is good for the team too, you know. If I have 75 assists, somebody is scoring. But if I am on the couch, I help nobody.

Marcus Bell

The Bureau's Book has you at 90 overall, up five on the Index. Skating at 99. Does any of that register with you?

Ilya Kovalchuk

My father, he reads the Book. He calls me, he says, ninety. I read the standings. Is nice, of course. But the Book doesn't play the games.

Marcus Bell

Who is the best player you have played with? Take your time.

Ilya Kovalchuk

Dany Heatley. Is not close, for me.

Marcus Bell

Why him?

Ilya Kovalchuk

In the offensive zone we don't talk. We just go. He knows where I go, I know where he goes, and the puck, it finds us. Two-on-one, he doesn't even look, he passes. He just knows.

Marcus Bell

He had 131 points this year, 65 goals, at 24.

Ilya Kovalchuk

Same line, most nights. But we are different players. He is bigger, he goes to the net, he shoots from anywhere. I am on the wing, I skate. People say, you two are the same, both young, both score. No. Is different.

Marcus Bell

Let's go back. Tver. Spartak Moscow. And the pee-wee tournament in Quebec in 1997.

Ilya Kovalchuk

Spartak sends us to the pee-wee in Quebec. 1997. I am a kid and I see the other kids and I think, okay. So this is the hockey. I remember it all.

Marcus Bell

Then 2 years with the men at Spartak, and 2001. First overall.

Ilya Kovalchuk

First overall. First Russian to go there. That is a big thing for my family. For me also, you know. But after that, is only hockey. Nobody gives you nothing for the draft.

Marcus Bell

And the 17 on your back.

Ilya Kovalchuk

Kharlamov. From the seventies. In Russia, every kid who plays knows that name. I take 17 because of him. Is a tribute. I keep it.

Marcus Bell

Last one. What's next for you?

Ilya Kovalchuk

Same thing. Summer, I skate, I get stronger. Then September, 60 minutes. We play the games and we see what happens.

Marcus Bell

Ilya, thank you for the time.

Ilya Kovalchuk

Thank you. Spasibo.

Marcus Bell

Ilya Kovalchuk of the Atlanta Thrashers®, from the studio. Back to you.
