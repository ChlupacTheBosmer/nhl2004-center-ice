Todd Bertuzzi, 128 points later, on the coach who once passed on him

In the studio, Bertuzzi says the letter on his sweater comes after the room, not before it, and that Marc Crawford, who passed on him in the OHL draft, is the man who broke his game down shift by shift.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelThursday, 28 April 2005

3:13 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Todd Bertuzzi89 closed the season with 128 points for the Vancouver Canucks, 56 of them goals, and the Bureau's Book has him at 93. He wears an A in a room that leans on Trevor Linden and Markus Naslund, and he sat down with Jan Kowalczyk this week to talk about the letter on his sweater, the coach who passed on him in the OHL draft, and the July date on his contract.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here in the studio with Todd Bertuzzi. Todd, 128 points, 56 goals. Last season you had 101 in 63 games. What changed?

Todd Bertuzzi

Yeah, you know, I don't really look at it like that, honestly. The number's the number, but 128, that's Markus and Brendan, mostly. You play with two guys like that, the puck finds your stick. You just try to get open.

Jan Kowalczyk

21.1 minutes a night. That's a big load.

Todd Bertuzzi

It's what you want. You want to be out there. Twenty-one minutes, that's the top line, that's the power play, and I'd rather be tired than sitting. Marc plays you if you're going, you know. It's that simple.

Jan Kowalczyk

That's a good place to start. You wear an A now. What does that letter mean in that room?

Todd Bertuzzi

Yeah. It means the guys around you, more than anything. You've got Trev in there, you've got Markus, you've got Jovo and Ohlie on the back end. The letter doesn't give you anything by itself. You've got to have the room first, and I think I, uh, I learned that here.

Jan Kowalczyk

Who taught it to you?

Todd Bertuzzi

Trevor, mainly. You watch a guy like that carry a room, the way he talks when it's going bad, and you take it in. And Markus is the same, quiet, but everybody knows where he stands. You don't get a letter and then turn into that. You learn it off the men in front of you.

Jan Kowalczyk

Let me take you back. Sudbury, then Guelph, fifth overall in the OHL. And a general manager up in Cornwall passes on you. That man is Marc Crawford.

Todd Bertuzzi

Yeah. He's told me. He told me to my face, you know. Says I was a big kid who hadn't grown into his body yet. That's the line he uses. And I laugh at it now, because he's not wrong. I was 16, 17. I was big and I didn't know what I was yet.

Jan Kowalczyk

He said maturity issues.

Todd Bertuzzi

He says that too. Look, I was a kid. I'd been suspended in junior, I'd done stuff. I'm not going to sit here and pretend I was easy. I wasn't easy.

Jan Kowalczyk

Then he ends up behind your bench. How does that work?

Todd Bertuzzi

Funny how it turns, eh. He passes on me as a kid and he's the guy who ends up coaching me. I think about that sometimes. He got the version of me he wanted, just, you know, later.

Jan Kowalczyk

What did he change in you, as a player?

Todd Bertuzzi

Everything, really. The way I play. He wants your feet moving, he wants pucks deep, he wants you hard to play against. When he got ahold of me, that wasn't me yet. He'd sit you down with the video and show you the shift, over and over, until you saw it the way he saw it. I owe him for that. I do.

Jan Kowalczyk

You've got 24.2 minutes a night in the playoffs, six games in. Is that a Crawford thing, leaning on you?

Todd Bertuzzi

Yeah, and I want it. Six games, you know, this is why you play 82. Nobody's tired now. You get to this part of the year and, uh, you'd play 30 if he asked. That's not a speech. That's just how it is.

Jan Kowalczyk

Your deal runs out in July. $2.60M, one year left. Have you thought about it?

Todd Bertuzzi

Not, uh... not right now. I know it's there. Everybody knows it's there. But I'm not going to sit in a room in April and think about July. I love it here. That's just true, no negotiation in it. The rest takes care of itself.

Jan Kowalczyk

Last thing. Back to Sudbury, the kid who played for two teams out of there before anybody knew his name. What would you tell him?

Todd Bertuzzi

Aw, jeez. I'd tell him, you know, keep your head up and your feet moving, and don't worry about the guys who pass on you. Somebody's going to want you. It just, uh, it works out different than you think it will. It did for me.

Jan Kowalczyk

Todd Bertuzzi, thanks for the time. Jan Kowalczyk, back to you.
