Gaborik's two goals carry Toronto over Ottawa, four to one

The winger scored twice despite a shot deficit and says the penalty kill gave Toronto the night

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelThursday, 7 April 2005

1:48 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Marian Gaborik98 scored twice as Toronto Maple Leafs beat Ottawa Senators 4-1 at home on Monday for their third straight win. Toronto was outshot 21-24. Gaborik has 55 goals and 99 points on the season.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here, in the corridor at the Air Canada Centre with Marian Gaborik. Marian, four and one, third straight, and two goals from you tonight. That first one, the D-man walked it in from the point and you had all the ice. What did you see?

Marian Gaborik

Yeah, so, he take the puck at the blue line and I just go, you know, I just go. The defense, they are slow, they turn and I see the ice, so I go. Good pass, good shot. How you say, simple play.

Marcus Bell

You put up 21 shots, Ottawa put up 24, but the score says four to one. Back in January you told me the shot count doesn't tell it. Tonight really doesn't.

Marian Gaborik

Yeah, you remember. Yeah, so tonight the puck go in, that is the thing. It doesn't matter how many you shoot. Twenty, thirty, the puck go in the net and we win. So yeah.

Marcus Bell

Your second one, in the second period, Alexander Mogilny worked it around the end boards and you buried it. Did you see him before he made the play?

Marian Gaborik

No, no, not really. He is behind me and I just, you know, I drive the net, and the puck is there and I put it in. He is always there, Mogilny, on the back door. You just, you know, you just give him the ice and he find it.

Marcus Bell

Ottawa managed one goal on all 24 shots. Your penalty kill was the story tonight. How much of this win belongs to that unit?

Marian Gaborik

Big part. Yeah, big part, you know. The PK, they play so hard, they block the shots, they win the faceoff, and, how you say, it give us the breath. When they keep it out, we can go up and do our thing. So yeah, the PK, they were big tonight.

Marcus Bell

Your power play didn't convert, though. You've had the chances all year. Does it bother you going into the stretch run?

Marian Gaborik

We have the chances, yeah. The puck not go in tonight, that is hockey. But the other side they score nothing and we score four. So, no, I don't complain about the PP tonight.

Marcus Bell

Third in a row, and the season is coming down. How does this room feel about where it sits?

Marian Gaborik

We feel good. We play good game, everybody, not just one line, everybody. Three wins, that is three wins, but we look forward, you know. Next game is the game.

Marcus Bell

Marian Gaborik, thank you. Back to you.
