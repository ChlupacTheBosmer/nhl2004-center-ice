Giguere keeps the overtime save, and takes the two points

Anaheim's goaltender held an 18-shot night close in Chicago, called the quiet games the hard ones, and said again that the scoreboard matters less than the two points.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelThursday, 8 December 2005

1:44 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Jean-Sebastien Giguere91 faced 18 shots and Mighty Ducks of Anaheim won 2-1 in overtime at Chicago Blackhawks, a second win in a row that puts Anaheim at 29 points. In the tunnel afterward he talked to Marcus Bell about the save he will keep and the two points he had been waiting for.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here with Jean-Sebastien Giguere, The Tunnel, after the 2-1 in Chicago. J.S., it goes to overtime, 18 shots come at you. Which one do you keep?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

Yeah... the one in the overtime, I think. They come across the middle, and, uh, I get a piece of it with the leg. That's a reaction, you know. Not much thinking in that one.

Marcus Bell

Is a night like that harder than 40 shots? You are almost idle and then it is on you.

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

For sure it's different. When it's busy, the puck keep you in the game, you know. When it's quiet... you have to keep yourself in it. I talk to myself a little bit. I stay with the play.

Marcus Bell

Two wins now, Buffalo and here. What has changed in your game these last few?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

I feel more settled in the net. Before, I was, uh, I chase a little bit, you know. Now I let the puck come to me. And the guys, they get the goal in the overtime, so. Two points.

Marcus Bell

The Bureau's Book has you at 97, up on the Development Index. Does that read right to you?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

I don't look at that, no. I don't. Maybe it's good. But you win or you lose, that's what I look at. Tonight we win.

Marcus Bell

Last season the Vezina. This year, no shutout yet. Does that sit with you at all?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

No. A shutout is nice, for sure. But I don't play for the shutout, I play for the two points. Some night the puck go in, some night it don't. You have to be the same man, you know.

Marcus Bell

Back in January you told me you wanted to talk about the two points instead of the scoreboard. You got them tonight.

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

Yeah, you remember that, uh. For sure. Tonight it's two points, so I'm happy. But we have to be better, that's it. Chicago, they work, they don't go away.

Marcus Bell

What got you this one on the road?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

Sergei, he's big for us right now. When he have the puck, you know, you feel like something is going to happen. You just try to do your part and let those guys work.

Marcus Bell

J.S., thanks. Back to you.

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

Thank you, Marcus.
