Morassutti: 'You don't sign them all, the math won't let you'

Colorado's general manager sits down with four expiring contracts at the top of a $66.38M payroll and tells Jan Kowalczyk which ones get priority.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelThursday, 8 December 2005

6:05 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Peter Forsberg95, Paul Kariya89, Milan Hejduk88, and Teemu Selanne86 all carry one year left on their deals at Colorado Avalanche. The four of them sit at the top of a payroll that runs to $66.38M, and together they've put up 32, 30, 32 and 20 points through 27 games. General manager Kerry Morassutti joins us in the studio to talk about which ones get priority.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here with Kerry Morassutti from the Colorado front office. Kerry, thanks for coming in. Let me start with the scoreboard. You're 10-10-5, 29 points, second in the Northwest behind Calgary at 34. Two losses in a row. Where does that sit with you?

Kerry Morassutti

It sits where it sits. We've got 29 points, we're two back of Calgary, and we've got games in hand on a couple of people. I don't think people outside this building understand how tight the Northwest is right now. You look at 34, 29, 28, 28, 27, that's one game between third and fifth. So I'm not in a panic. I'm in a position where every game matters and that's a good thing at this point in a season.

Jan Kowalczyk

But look at the per-point. Your payroll is $66.38M, that puts you at $2.29M a point. Dallas is at $1.45M. Nashville is at $1.42M. You've got $53.30M in revenue and $23.55M in profit, so the business side is fine. Are you getting what you should get out of the roster?

Kerry Morassutti

I'd like more. I would. You don't spend $66.38M and walk away happy at 29. I'd be lying if I sat here and told you that number doesn't bother me. But you have to look at where that money sits. It's concentrated. Four players account for a huge chunk of it and all four are playing well right now. Forsberg's got 32 in 27, Kariya's got 30 in 27, Hejduk's got 32 in 27. They're earning their money on the ice. The per-point tells me I need more from the bottom of the roster. That's where I look at this and say, we've got to get production from places other than those top lines.

Jan Kowalczyk

And that takes us to the contracts. All four of those names, Forsberg, Kariya, Hejduk, Selanne, they're in the contract-year class. Forsberg at $9.90M, Kariya at $10.11M, Hejduk at $2.50M, Selanne at $6.94M. You can't sign them all. Can you?

Kerry Morassutti

No. No you can't. That's the thing people don't see when they read those four names and say keep them all. You've got to pick. Hejduk at $2.50M for a 29-year-old putting up 32 points, that's the one that makes the math work. He's the cheapest piece and he's giving you top-six production. That's the first call I make, and it's not a close one. Then you look at the other three and the numbers get hard.

Jan Kowalczyk

So who's the second call? You've got a 32-year-old Forsberg making $9.90M, a 31-year-old Kariya making $10.11M, and a 35-year-old Selanne making $6.94M. They're at 95, 89, and 86. They're all at 100 morale. They want to be here.

Kerry Morassutti

They do. And I respect that. Every one of them is a pro and they're doing their jobs right now. But you're asking me to put those three salaries together and then build a roster around that. That's the entire problem. That's why we're at 29 and not at 35. Because that money is in four bodies and I've got to find a way to spread it. I don't have a clean answer for you today. I'm working on it. But I'd rather be honest with you than sit here and say everything's fine when I know it isn't.

Jan Kowalczyk

Let me ask about your rooms. Your workout facility is at zero and your practice rink is at zero. Those are the floor. Your travel room is at four, which is good, and you filed a scouting upgrade between December 1 and December 4. Why scouting and not workout?

Kerry Morassutti

The scouting room feeds the trade evaluation. That's what I need right now. If I'm going to move a piece this year, whether that's a deadline move or something before then, I need to get the most back for it. That's what the scouting room does. Workout at zero, practice at zero, yeah, those hurt. I know they hurt. I feel it every day I watch us play at home and then go on the road. But those are fixed costs. You build them once. The contracts are the thing that eats the budget alive year after year. So right now my money goes into the decision that frees up the budget, which is getting these contracts sorted.

Jan Kowalczyk

Your on-ice facility modifier is minus 1.78. That's players playing every night carrying that. The workout room moves strength, checking, endurance. The practice rink moves everything at home. You're asking them to fight through that.

Kerry Morassutti

I know what I'm asking. You saw the games. We've got a leg to stand on but we're not finishing checks the way I want to, we're not holding our zone the way a team with our talent should. That's the workout room. That's the practice room. I'd love to get both to three tonight. I can't. The payroll won't let me. That's the tradeoff and I'm making it with my eyes open. If I can free up one of those top salaries, the rooms get built and we've got a different team in 2006-07.

Jan Kowalczyk

What the calendar has as the trade deadline is 101 days away. Are you a seller before that if you can't sort this out with the contracts?

Kerry Morassutti

I'm not a seller. We're 29 points, two back of Calgary. Nobody's out of anything. If you're three games out in March I might think different, but right now? No. I need to be better, not thinner. And honestly the best deadline move I can make is one that helps me sort a contract or free a salary slot for next year. That's the deadline I'm playing. Not a rental guy for 20 games. I'm playing the one that lets me walk into July and sign the player I want without breaking the bank.

Jan Kowalczyk

Kerry Morassutti from Colorado, thank you for your time.

Kerry Morassutti

Thanks Jan.
