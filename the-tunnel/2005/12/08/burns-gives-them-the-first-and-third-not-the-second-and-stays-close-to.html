Burns gives them the first and third, not the second, and stays close to the chest on the net

After a 31-shot road win at Toronto, Pat Burns called out a five-minute stretch in the middle period, praised the compete level, and would not commit to Martin Brodeur in Columbus on Monday.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelThursday, 8 December 2005

2:34 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Pat Burns and the New Jersey Devils Devils beat Toronto Maple Leafs 3-1 on the road on 2005-12-07, outshooting Toronto 31 to 22. The win made it two straight for New Jersey, now 14-5-3, 37 points, second in the Atlantic Division. Burns spoke with Jan Kowalczyk in the corridor at the Air Canada Centre.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here with Pat Burns. Coach, you just beat a Toronto team that's 12 and 6 at home, on the road, 3-1, 31 shots to 22. What do you take away tonight?

Pat Burns

The compete level was there, for sure. 31 shots, that means we got to the net, we managed the puck through the neutral zone. I liked the first period, I liked the structure in the third. They didn't get a lot of quality in the third, we controlled the zone. That's what I take away.

Jan Kowalczyk

You said you liked the first and the third. So the second bothers you. What did you see in there?

Pat Burns

About eight minutes in the middle of the second, I think, we got lazy. Three, four entries in a row where we carried it in the middle and we just gave it up, no shot, nothing. That's not legs, I don't want to say it's legs because the guys just came off Pittsburgh it's the details. You make the first pass or you chip it deep and you chase, you don't carry it right into two defencemen. We had the chance to make it four, five-nothing in that stretch and instead we give them a little bit of life. We have to be better, that's it.

Jan Kowalczyk

You had 31 shots on a team that lets in 59 goals in 27 games. Was that the game plan, to get pucks to the net, or does it just come when the structure clicks?

Pat Burns

It comes when the structure clicks, yes. You don't design 31 shots. But when we're in their zone and we're cycling, we've got guys who can bury it. Rafalski, eight goals from the blue line, that's not a fluke, he's been doing it his whole career. When the cycle is right, the shots come. It's the same thing on the back end, if we're tight in the neutral zone, they shoot from the outside and Marty sees it. 22 shots for Toronto on a night they're at home, that tells me the structure was mostly there.

Jan Kowalczyk

Marty is 21 of 25. That's 84 percent of the workload. You go to Columbus on Monday. Is he in the building?

Pat Burns

You know, we've got four days. Four days is not nothing. I'll look at the guys in practice, I'll talk to the staff, and I'll decide. That's what the four days are for.

Jan Kowalczyk

Michael Leighton's dressed three, won one of those three. Is there a case for giving him a Monday, rest Marty a little?

Pat Burns

There's always a case for something. But you know, Marty's 33 and he's 21 and 6, he's our number one, he plays when he's healthy. I don't need to justify putting him in the net. If I sat him every fourth game he'd sit me.

Jan Kowalczyk

No lineup changes for you this week. Nobody moved up, nobody moved down. Are you happy with what you've got or are you waiting for something?

Pat Burns

I'm happy with the compete. That's what I look at first, can I trust the effort. The points, sure, I like Langenbrunner's 25, I like Gomez's 24, but I can't coach a point total. I coach the shift. Give me a guy who beats a checker to the puck every night and I'll find a way to make the rest work. Right now I trust the effort. That's enough to dress the same guys.

Jan Kowalczyk

Four days off before Columbus. Then you've got a stretch coming. What does the schedule look like from here?

Pat Burns

Four days, yeah, that's nice, the boys need it. We just went Pittsburgh, Toronto, that's a back to back on the road, you don't love it but you take the four points. Columbus Monday is not going to be easy, they've got speed, they just scored four on STL. We go there, we play our game, we don't get cute. Then we see what comes after. One at a time, that's it.

Jan Kowalczyk

Coach, thanks for your time tonight. Back to the desk.
