Giguere: Twenty Shots and a Win, That's Hockey

The Ducks goaltender watched his team manage 20 shots, still win in overtime, and told The Tunnel that this room knows how to be ugly.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelThursday, 1 December 2005

2:48 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Jean-Sebastien Giguere97 came up big again for Mighty Ducks of Anaheim on Tuesday night, stealing a 3-2 overtime win over Los Angeles Kings in front of the home crowd at the Honda Center. The Ducks were outshot 24-20 and sit at 7-12-1 through 24 games, 4th in the Pacific. Sergei Fedorov90 scored twice and Vyacheslav Kozlov78 picked up two assists.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here in the corridor at the Honda Center with Jean-Sebastien Giguere. Thirty-two saves tonight? Twenty-four shots faced, overtime, you get the two points. Twenty shots for your own side, twenty-four against you, and you still find a way. What's that game look like from the other end of the ice?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

Yeah, it's, uh, it's not pretty. It's not, you know, the best we've looked. We only get twenty shots, that's not a lot. Not a lot. But the guys in front of me, they stayed, they stayed with the game and at the end, for sure, the overtime is ours. So, I take it. I take it all day.

Jan Kowalczyk

You told Marcus Bell in December of last year that this room can win ugly. Does tonight feel like that conversation coming true, or is it different?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

It's, uh, it's the same thing. It's the same thing. When we're not, you know, when we're not the better team and we still get two points, that's what makes me say, okay, the guys are not quitting. They're working. They're working all sixty plus five minutes. And that's, that's important. More important than the pretty goals.

Jan Kowalczyk

Fedotov gets two tonight. You told Marcus back in January that you didn't think he should be carrying the whole thing every night. Two goals, four points for him in one game. Does that bother you, that he's the one doing it?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

You know, I say that and he hears it in the room, and he just looks at me. But tonight, yeah, he takes the game. Two goals, and Niko gets the other one. That's the thing, Niko is there, he's getting his. Kozzy has two assists, so he's finding them. I think, I think it's spreading a little. But Sergei is Sergei. You know what he gives you.

Jan Kowalczyk

Let me talk about the building. You're six and five at home this year. What does the crowd do when it goes to overtime on a night like this? Are you hearing it?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

Yeah, for sure. When it goes to the extra, the building, it wakes up. It wakes up. And I hear it, yeah, you can feel it behind you. The guys are flying in overtime, everybody's tired but nobody sits. And the crowd, they push. They push and you feed off that. It's, it's something special when the building is with you like that.

Jan Kowalczyk

You're seven and twelve and one. Twenty-three points. That's not the record a Vezina winner wants. You're two straight now after tonight. Does a second one change the way you walk into the room Thursday?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

No. No it doesn't change the walk, you know. I walk in and I see the record on the board and I know what it is. Seven and twelve. That's not, that's not where we are supposed to be. Not with this group. But, two in a row is, it's something. You build on it. You build on the two and you forget about the rest for a week and you just get the next one.

Jan Kowalczyk

One last thing. Your development is up. You're playing the best hockey of your career by every grade the Bureau prints. Do you feel that difference in the crease compared to last year when you were thirty-eight wins and no shutouts?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

Yeah, I feel it. I feel it in my legs, in the first period, I'm already there. Last year I was, uh, sometimes I was not there until the second. Now I'm there from the puck drop. And the guys know that too, they trust me more and I trust them. That's the difference. It's not one save, it's the whole game you're inside it.

Jan Kowalczyk

Jean-Sebastien Giguere, thank you for your time. Jan Kowalczyk at the Honda Center.
