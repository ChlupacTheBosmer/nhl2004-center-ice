Ruff says the 5-1 hides what he saw, and that being healthy makes the record harder to excuse

The Sabres coach acknowledges the three-game streak but calls it unfinished work, and says the injury-free roster makes last place in the Northeast impossible to explain away

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelThursday, 1 December 2005

2:51 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Lindy Ruff's Buffalo Sabres put five goals past the Presidents' Trophy leaders in Toronto Maple Leafs, a 5-1 road win that made it three straight. It did not move them off the bottom of the Northeast, where they sit at 22 points with nobody on the injured list.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here, in the corridor outside the visiting room at Toronto. Lindy Ruff, thank you for coming down. Three in a row, five goals on the road. You're 4 and 8 on the road this year and this was anything but a road game loss. How did it feel from the bench?

Lindy Ruff

Yeah, well, we scored some good ones and we didn't give them much to work with after the first one went in. The boys played a solid road game. You take what you can get.

Marcus Bell

Solid is one word for it. Five goals. But Toronto put 32 shots on the board, you had 30. That's not a team that was outplayed. That's a team that got one and you got five. What was the difference?

Lindy Ruff

I mean, we managed the puck better. We didn't turn it over at the blue line the way we have been lately, so you know, the chances go both ways, obviously. They had their looks, they just didn't get the one and we got ours. That's really all it is.

Marcus Bell

Three straight wins. You haven't done that all season. What changed in this stretch?

Lindy Ruff

Nothing changed. We just started playing to the level we need to play to every night. The compete level went up and the details came along with it. I don't think there's a switch you flip. We've just got guys who are starting to trust the structure a little more.

Marcus Bell

Lindy, I want to put something to you. You are one of the clubs in this league with nobody on the shelf. Nine teams have at least two out. The New York Islanders have six. And you are last in the Northeast, 22 points, 61 scored, 68 against. Does being healthy make that harder to explain?

Lindy Ruff

Look, I don't look at other rooms. I can't. If we had five guys out tomorrow I'd dress eleven forwards and seven D and we'd play somebody else's game. Health doesn't put the puck in the net. We've got guys who are playing hockey and they've got to do more. That's not a health thing. That's a us thing.

Marcus Bell

Chris Drury has 22 points, Daniel Briere 21, Owen Nolan 20. Your top three forwards are all producing. The gap below them is real. Do you expect someone else to step up or is that just what this roster looks like?

Lindy Ruff

You know, I don't think it's a gap the way you're framing it. Those three are producing, yeah, but the bottom six's job isn't to put up 15 points. It's to be a pain, to go in the dirty areas, to let those other guys have the easier minutes. I'm not asking them to be our top line. I'm asking them to do what we asked them to do. And lately they've been closer to that.

Marcus Bell

Martin Biron, 19 starts, 7 and 12. But he's been good in this stretch. Is he your guy going forward?

Lindy Ruff

He's our guy right now. That's as far as I'm going tonight. You want the goalie question in December, come see me in December.

Marcus Bell

The schedule ahead. Anaheim at home Friday, then New York Rangers on Sunday. Top of both divisions in a week. Chance to prove something?

Lindy Ruff

It's two more games. I've been here long enough to know that if you look at the Rangers before you've handled Anaheim, you get nothing on Friday. So we'll get ready for Anaheim and we'll talk about Sunday later.

Marcus Bell

You've been the head coach here since 1997. You've taken this group to a final. You've seen stretches like this. Does this group have something that can break through?

Lindy Ruff

You know, I've had groups that had more talent on paper and couldn't get it done, and I've had groups that everyone wrote off and they got deep. I think this group is still figuring out what it is. And three games isn't the answer. We're still looking. I'll know when we find it. Back to you, Marcus.

Marcus Bell

Lindy Ruff, thank you for your time.
