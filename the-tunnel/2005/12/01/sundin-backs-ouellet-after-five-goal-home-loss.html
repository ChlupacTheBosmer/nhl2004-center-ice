Sundin backs Ouellet after five-goal home loss

The captain pointed to the first period again and said the net was not his problem, after Toronto outshot Buffalo 32-30 and lost 5-1.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelThursday, 1 December 2005

1:50 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Mats Sundin92 went scoreless in the Toronto Maple Leafs 5-1 home loss to Buffalo Sabres on Wednesday. Maxime Ouellet started in net in place of Roberto Luongo and gave up five goals on 30 shots. Toronto fired 32 pucks at the other end and scored once, on a Alexander Mogilny89 setup for Maxim Afinogenov99.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here with Mats Sundin, in the corridor after that one. Thirty-two shots and one goal, five against at home. Walk me through what you saw.

Mats Sundin

They finished. That is the difference tonight. We had thirty-two shots and one went in, they had thirty and five went in. You cannot win that. They made the plays in the dirty area and we did not make ours.

Marcus Bell

Maxim got the lone one. What was that shift like, the moment where it finally clicked?

Mats Sundin

He finds a way. Of course you want more than one. But he has been good all year, he gets his chances and he puts them in. That is what you need from a winger.

Marcus Bell

Ouellet was in there tonight. Roberto did not play. Was that a change you felt on the ice, knowing who was behind you?

Mats Sundin

No. You play for whoever is in the net. Max was good earlier in the year and tonight the goals just came. That is not his fault. We did not give him enough in front. Five goals, you cannot put that on the goalie.

Marcus Bell

You told Jan last December that the first period was the piece you wanted to fix. Did you see it come back tonight?

Mats Sundin

Yes. We have had that conversation, I think maybe too many times now. Early goals against at home, they change the game. You start pressing, you lose structure, and then they are scoring on the counter. It is the same thing.

Marcus Bell

And your own night? Nothing on the board. How did it feel from your chair?

Mats Sundin

I had a chance in the second, maybe a third one late. Neither went in. That is a quiet night for me. You have to be better. I have to be better.

Marcus Bell

With Alexander and Brad on the wing, the first line got the one goal. Is there anything in how those shifts went that you can build from, even tonight?

Mats Sundin

The goal, yes. Alexander saw it and Maxim finished. That is one shift where everything was on time. The rest of the night we were, I think, half a step behind them. They were there before us. That is not a one-line thing, that is all five guys on the ice.

Marcus Bell

You are still first in the division. Does tonight feel like a blip, or is there something to worry about?

Mats Sundin

There is no blip. Five goals against, we have to fix it. You do not get to pick which nights you are sharp. Tomorrow is film. We come back and we play better.

Marcus Bell

Mats Sundin, thank you as always. Marcus Bell, back to you.
