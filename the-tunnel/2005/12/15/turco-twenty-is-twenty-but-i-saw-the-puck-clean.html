Turco: 'Twenty is twenty, but I saw the puck clean'

Dallas wins its fourth straight to sit first in the Western Conference, and the goaltender says tonight was about rhythm, not the penalty kill.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelThursday, 15 December 2005

1:58 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Marty Turco93 stopped 19 of 20 pucks as Dallas Stars beat Atlanta Thrashers 3-1 at home on Monday night, their fourth straight win. Dallas sits alone atop the Western Conference with 44 points, and the room is carrying it the way a room carries a hot stretch, loosely, with an easy silence in the dressing room afterward.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here rinkside with Marty Turco. Marty, fourth straight win, you're sitting on top of the West with 44 points. Walk me through what it feels like from the inside when you're in the middle of one of these stretches.

Marty Turco

Yeah, it's good. You know when you're winning you just... you don't feel the legs the same way. The room's loose, everybody's a little more patient with the puck, guys are making that second play instead of the safe one. I mean it's four games, we're not popping champagne in here, but it's better than the alternative.

Jan Kowalczyk

You only saw 20 pucks tonight. The penalty kill must have given you some room, or is it something else about how this game went?

Marty Turco

No, the box was fine, don't get me wrong. Lehtinen was killing one of them and he just, you know, he gets a stick in the lane and the guy can't get around him. But honestly I think the difference tonight was 5-on-5. We had 20 shots to look at and I saw them all clean. The bodies were, the bodies were in the right spots. And that starts in the neutral zone, we were clogging their entry and they had to, what, carry it in and then reset. That's a different kind of 20 shots.

Jan Kowalczyk

That's the first time you've pulled the PK out of the credits. You just said the box was fine but you gave the real answer to your defensemen and your forwards.

Marty Turco

Yeah, well, I get asked about the penalty kill every night and sometimes the answer is just, the PK didn't need to save me. Buehler and Zubov were, were, I don't know, they were just, they were just on the right side of the play tonight. That's not glamorous and nobody's gonna highlight-reel it but that's what wins you these games.

Jan Kowalczyk

Modano's got 4 points in his last 2 games. He looks like a different guy in January than he did a month ago. Is he back?

Marty Turco

He's always back. You know what he does, he... he waits. You're watching the play, you think, he should go there, and he just, he sits there for half a second longer than anyone expects and then the lane's open and it's gone. He's 35, he doesn't need to make you feel sorry for him. He just does it.

Jan Kowalczyk

You told me back in January that the home edge was legs and sleep. Are you saying a W4 is just that same thing turned on repeat?

Marty Turco

Pretty much. Yeah. You sleep better when you've won. You get on the bus and nobody's, nobody's thinking about tomorrow's opponent. You're still thinking about them but it's a, it's a lighter version. You're not grinding, you're just, you're just, you're just going. I know how that sounds. I know it's a cliché.

Jan Kowalczyk

It's a good cliché. Marty Turco, thank you. Back to you.
