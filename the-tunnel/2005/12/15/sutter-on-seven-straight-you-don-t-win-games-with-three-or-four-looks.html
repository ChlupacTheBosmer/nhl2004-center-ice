Sutter on seven straight: "You don't win games with three or four looks"

The Chicago coach, after a 2-3 loss at Buffalo on Tuesday night that made it seven in a row, talks about the special teams, a four-game homestand against his team's record at home, and why he sat Theoren Fleury.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelThursday, 15 December 2005

3:14 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Chicago Blackhawks lost 2-3 at Buffalo Sabres on 2005-12-13, their seventh straight defeat, and head coach Brian Sutter stood in the corridor at HSBC Arena afterward with a team holding 21 points and a 1-13 record at home. Four of the next five games are in Chicago, starting Thursday against first-place Dallas Stars.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here in the tunnel at Buffalo. Brian Sutter, seven in a row, twenty-one points, and you've got Dallas in your building Thursday. What was wrong tonight?

Brian Sutter

We didn't manage the puck well enough. You saw the shot count, nineteen to twenty-two, and when you don't have it you're changing on the fly, you're chasing, and we've been chasing the game for a while now. First period, we were flat. Came out slow, Buffalo took the lead, and after that we had looks, we had, I don't know, three or four real ones, but you don't win hockey games with three or four looks. You know what I mean. You've got to get traffic, you've got to get second chances, we didn't get enough of either tonight.

Jan Kowalczyk

Theoren Fleury is out of the lineup right now. Twenty-seven points in twenty-seven games, and he's been scratched. Walk me through that call.

Brian Sutter

I'll tell you what I'll tell you. Everybody in this room knows the rules. You play, you don't play, it's based on what you give us that week, and Theo knows exactly why. He's handled it the way he handles everything, which is like a professional. I'm not going to put it out there on television. He'll play again. That's where it sits.

Jan Kowalczyk

In net, Jocelyn Thibault is four and eleven, Ari Ahonen is three and twelve, and you've split them right down the middle. Are you sticking with that?

Brian Sutter

We play whoever we think gives us the best chance that night. Joc has been here, he's worked at it, Ari is young, he's getting his feet under him. I'm not going to put eighty-six goals on a position. That's on me and on the five in front of them. We've got to do a better job protecting the house, and until we do, nobody's win streak is going to be great. I don't care who's standing there.

Jan Kowalczyk

Let me ask about the special teams. You've moved Kyle Calder onto the power play at right wing, and Steve Sullivan's still up there too. What does Calder give you that you weren't getting before?

Brian Sutter

Size. He's a big body that goes to the front. Steve is a shooter, he wants to get open and let it fly, and we need somebody who's going to stand in the slot and screen the goaltender and get a rebound. That's the whole answer. We haven't scored enough on the power play to be picky. Kyle's a different look and we'll see if it works. You know, you try it, you watch the tape, and if it's not, you change it again.

Jan Kowalczyk

And the penalty kill. Steve Poapst is listed on both units on the depth chart but he hasn't dressed a game this year. Does that pairing actually exist on the ice?

Brian Sutter

It's a depth chart, it's a planning document. We dress who's ready. If a man isn't in the lineup, he's not on the ice, simple as that. The thing I'll say about our penalty kill is that we take too many. We're undisciplined, we're in the box too often, and the unit that's on the ice is tired. That's the issue. It's not the personnel on paper, it's the five guys who put us there in the first place.

Jan Kowalczyk

You're one and thirteen at home and the schedule hands you four of the next five at the United Center, starting Thursday with Dallas, who are four and a half points ahead of everyone else in the Pacific.

Brian Sutter

Yeah. And I know what that number looks like. One and thirteen. It's ugly, I don't need you to tell me that. But we've been playing better on the road than at home, and I can tell you I don't have a clean explanation for why. Maybe it's the pressure of the building, maybe it's something else. What I told the group this morning is, it's four games in your own rink, your own fans, you get back on the ice in front of your own people, and if you can't put a run together there, there's nowhere to put it. Dallas is the test right away. They're four on a win, they've got forty-four points, they're hard to score on. But we've got a shot this week. Whether we take it or not, that's what we'll find out.

Jan Kowalczyk

Coach Sutter, appreciate your time. Back to you.
