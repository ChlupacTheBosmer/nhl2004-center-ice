Turco Owns the Four, Won't Blame the Bus

The Dallas Stars goaltender says his 4-goal night in Nashville belongs to him, not the road, and brushed off his expiring contract.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelSunday, 4 December 2005

1:45 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Marty Turco97 finished with 4 goals on 21 shots in a 2-4 loss at Nashville Predators on 2005-11-30, the third straight defeat that keeps Dallas Stars at 36 points. Jan Kowalczyk caught him in the tunnel after the game.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here with Marty Turco in the tunnel at Nashville. Marty, 4 on 21, a 4-2 loss. Grade yourself tonight.

Marty Turco

I didn't make the save I needed to. That's, you know... that's the short version. Four on 21, that's not enough. Modano gets us one, DiMaio gets one, and I've got to keep us in it and I can't. That's me tonight.

Jan Kowalczyk

You told me in January the road was legs and sleep, not anything mystical. Nine and seven on the road this year. Tonight feels like the old explanation.

Marty Turco

No. No, tonight's not the road. Nine and seven is us, that's our number away from home and I'm fine with it. Tonight I wasn't there between the pipes and you can't blame a bus ride or a time zone for four goals. That's on me. The building was fine, the ice was fine. I wasn't.

Jan Kowalczyk

The Bureau's Development Index has you at 97 right now, three above your base. Does that number sting a little more when you come off a night like that?

Marty Turco

The Index. Yeah, I see the number. The Bureau puts it out, you read it, you nod. I don't put the pads on thinking I'm a 97 tonight. I put them on thinking save the puck. Tonight I saved 17 of 21 and we lost. The 97 stays where it is and I get to work tomorrow.

Jan Kowalczyk

Three straight now. Fourteen and seven and four, thirty-six points. You've been in enough seasons to know what a three-game skid in December means. Or doesn't.

Marty Turco

It means nothing in December, yeah. I've been here. You get a little skid, you shake it out, you move on. We're 36 points in six divisions, we're right there in the Pacific. It's not April yet and I'm not going to build a story out of three losses on a short trip.

Jan Kowalczyk

One year left. Zero after this season on the sheet. Are you thinking about that at all this year?

Marty Turco

You know what, I'm not. I've got a job tonight and tomorrow and the next one. The rest is... that's my agent's thing and I leave it alone. I just want to play and win and let the rest sort itself out.

Jan Kowalczyk

Fair enough. Marty Turco, thanks for your time. Back to you.
