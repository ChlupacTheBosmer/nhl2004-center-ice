Gallant knows a 3-2 win is hiding something

The Blue Jackets have won two straight and allowed 70 goals in 32 games, but their coach is not pretending the 17 shots in Chicago were a plan.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelThursday, 22 December 2005

3:29 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Gerard Gallant sat in the tunnel at the United Center after Columbus Blue Jackets beat the host club 3-2 on 2005-12-20, the Blue Jackets' second straight win. The club has allowed 70 goals in 32 games and scored 78, and Gallant told The Tunnel's Jan Kowalczyk that the structure is the identity while the offense is the problem nobody in the room can stop thinking about.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here in the tunnel at Chicago with the head coach of the Columbus Blue Jackets, Gerard Gallant. Coach, back to back, wins in Columbus and on the road. What does two straight tell you?

Gerard Gallant

It tells me the group is getting it, you know. We talked about the details all week, and the details are what won those two games. The compete level was there both nights. We didn't beat ourselves.

Jan Kowalczyk

You beat CGY 3-0 at home, and tonight you beat Chicago 3-2 on the road. 22 shots against Calgary, 17 tonight. Seventeen shots in Chicago. How do you win a game shooting 17?

Gerard Gallant

We manage the puck. That's the word I keep using in there. We manage the puck. We're not going to be a team that takes 30 or 35 shots a night, you know, that's not who we are right now. But we made the right play a lot tonight. Chicago's a bad team, obviously, but we didn't take their zone away from them until we had a real chance, and that's a discipline thing.

Jan Kowalczyk

Let's talk about the other end then, because 70 goals against in 32 games. That's a real number. Is that the penalty kill? Is that five-on-five? What's the backbone?

Gerard Gallant

It's every shift, it's every line. The PK's been good. The blocked shots, the shot blocks, those guys are putting their bodies in front all night. Our defensemen, Geoff's backchecking, everyone. You can't separate it, you know? You can't say oh that's the kill, oh that's five-on-five. It's the same 13 guys playing the same structure and when they buy in, the numbers show.

Jan Kowalczyk

Pascal Leclaire's started 23 of 32, Marc Denis eight. Fred Brathwaite three. Pascal's 13 and 10 with a shutout. When does that change?

Gerard Gallant

When he stops playing well, I guess. Pascal's earned it. You don't take a man who's giving you what he's giving you and pull him because of a number on a sheet. Denis is ready, Marc is ready, Fred's ready, all three of them. But Pascal's hot and he's in there.

Jan Kowalczyk

You just signed Henrik Tallinder on Saturday, and he's already out. Foot. Back January 4th. On top of Dimitri Kalinin and Todd Marchant. Three defensemen on the shelf. How thin are you?

Gerard Gallant

It's thin. Obviously it's thin. You lose a guy like Kalinin, 84 overall, that's not a guy you replace easy. And Todd, he was coming along, and the knee, that's a long one. January 30th. So yeah, Darryl's got to carry a lot more back there. The young guys are getting minutes they didn't expect, and they're good enough, but it's not ideal.

Jan Kowalczyk

And Tallinder, you sign him for $307,030, and he's hurt two days later. Is that a bad-luck thing or does that bother you as a coach?

Gerard Gallant

The signing's not my call, Jan, you know that. I don't make the contract decisions. But yeah, when you go out and get a body and the body goes down before you can use him, it's tough. It is. You need everybody right now with the way the injuries are stacking up.

Jan Kowalczyk

38 points, third in the Central. STL's at 48. That's ten points. You've won two, you're playing tight, and the gap hasn't moved. What do you say to a room that wins two and looks up and still sees ten?

Gerard Gallant

I don't look at the gap. I can't. The last 10 games, that's what I manage. You play the next game, you get the two points, and the standings take care of themselves. But yeah, we know where we are. We know we're not where we want to be. We've just got to keep stacking them.

Jan Kowalczyk

Geoff Sanderson's got 39 points in 32 games. The next man is David Vyborny at 27. Geoff's doing a lot of the lifting. Is that sustainable over 82?

Gerard Gallant

It's not. Obviously it's not. I need more from people, you know? Espen needs more goals. Tyler needs more points. I don't want to stand here and single anybody out, but the bottom line is you can't ask one guy to carry you in this league. You need three lines that can score, and right now we've got, what, one and a half?

Jan Kowalczyk

Last two before the break, the 23rd and you've got Detroit on the 26th. What do you want to walk away with?

Gerard Gallant

Four points. I want four points. We've got to close the half with something. And the details, Jan, we just got to keep the details. If we do the little things, the goals will come. I believe that.

Jan Kowalczyk

Coach Gallant from Columbus, thank you.

Gerard Gallant

Thanks, Jan. Appreciate it.
