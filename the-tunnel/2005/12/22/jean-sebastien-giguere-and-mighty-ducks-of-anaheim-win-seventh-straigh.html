Jean-Sebastien Giguere And Mighty Ducks® of Anaheim Win Seventh Straight At Los Angeles Kings®

The goaltender waves off the shutout question and hands the credit to the defensemen in front of him.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelThursday, 22 December 2005

2:25 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Jean-Sebastien Giguere95 faced 19 shots and gave up 2 as Mighty Ducks of Anaheim beat Los Angeles Kings 3-2 on the road December 19, the club's seventh straight win. The Bureau's Development Index has him up 3 from his base grade of 93, putting him at 97 overall in the Book. He is 14 wins and 11 losses with 0 shutouts in 25 games this season.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here with Jean-Sebastien Giguere. Jean, seven straight. Three and two on the road, 19 shots on your net. What is different about that room right now?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

Seven. For sure. You know, I don't, I don't think anything changed, from one day to the next. The guys, they just believe the system now, I think. Sergei tonight, two goals, and the guys in front of me, they're making me look good. That's the honest answer. It's not me up here. It's them.

Marcus Bell

Nineteen shots either way. Pretty tight affair. Was there a stretch in there where you felt Los Angeles Kings® might take the game by the throat?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

No. I mean, there were, uh, long stretches where their guys were cycling at the blue line but we weren't giving up the second chance. Niclas and the guys behind me, they were clearing the crease every single time. I'm happy with 19. That's a game you win 3-2 and you go home happy.

Marcus Bell

Talk to me about the penalty kill from where you sit. You don't see the whole ice, but you see what matters. What does the kill look like from the crease, and how much are you talking in there?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

Oh, you see everything. You see the forward who's cheating for the counter, you see if she's, if he's, if he's late coming back. And you just scream. 'Tie up! Tie up the stick!' That's it. And when they're in position, I see the release from the point and I'm already set, I know where it's going. When they're not, well, that's when I'm working and it's not pretty.

Marcus Bell

Back in January you told me you wanted to talk about two points instead of the scoreboard. Well, here you are, seventh straight, and the Bureau's Index has you up 3 from your base, 97 overall. Does that sit right?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

Yeah, I said that. I remember. And okay, seven straight, sure, I'll take it. The Index, the 97, it's nice. I'm not going to tell you I hate it. But I'm 97 because my defenseman isn't getting burned every shift. Because Sergei is tracking back, because Ilja is behind me and the room is good right now. It's not just me. I'm a part of it.

Marcus Bell

But 0 shutouts in 25 games. You won the Vezina last year. Does the blank sheet nag at you?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

Everybody asks me the zero. Okay. I hear you. But we won tonight. We won with 19 against and two in. Twenty-five games, no zeros, yeah. I'll take that over the blank sheet every night. Maybe it comes. Maybe it doesn't. I'm not losing sleep over it.

Marcus Bell

Seven straight, but you're 11 and 13 and 1 overall. When does this group start looking up the standings instead of just at the next game?

Jean-Sebastien Giguere

Not now. That's the answer. I don't look at it, I really don't. We have one game and we have to be better, that's it. Sergei, Slava, all of them, they know what to do. Simple game, pucks out, keep it in front of me. And then we see where we land. For sure we're not done.

Marcus Bell

Jean-Sebastien Giguere, seven straight. Thank you, Jean. Marcus Bell, back to you.
