Luongo sees it all and stops 23 of them

Seven different scorers, a perfect penalty kill, and a room that answered without its best player

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelThursday, 22 December 2005

2:19 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Roberto Luongo95 stopped 23 shots for his first shutout of the season as Toronto Maple Leafs beat St. Louis Blues 7-0 at home on Wednesday. Seven different Maple Leafs scored: Alexander Mogilny89, Rostislav Klesla87, Mats Sundin92, Brad Richards87, Maxim Afinogenov, Vaclav Spacek, and Petr Chlup. Toronto sits at 22-7-1, 53 points, and one win on a new streak.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here in the corridor with Roberto Luongo. Seven-nil, twenty-three saves, a shutout. What's it like watching that from your net?

Roberto Luongo

It's, uh, comfortable, for sure. No, look, the guys in front of me, they made it easy tonight. They cleared the slot all night, I barely saw the puck half the time. You know when you know, I could tell after the first period. The traffic was gone. I could see everything.

Marcus Bell

The penalty kill was perfect tonight. STL couldn't buy a look. What did you see from behind them?

Roberto Luongo

Yeah, yeah, I saw it. Klesla was everywhere, for sure. He was getting out to the point, he was back in the slot, he was, uh, he was two places at the same time. And the guys, the forwards, they were blocking. I counted four, five blocks in one shift. You can't teach that. You just, you have to want it and tonight they wanted it.

Marcus Bell

You're without Marian Gaborik tonight. He's not in the lineup. How does the structure change for you when your best player's not out there?

Roberto Luongo

Well, you know, it's different. Marian, when he's there, he's always open on the weak side, so I know the puck's going to him on the rush. Without him, I've got to read more. But tonight the guys, Mogilny took that role, and he's, he's 89 in the Book for a reason, for sure. He opened up and the defense didn't know where to look. So it's different but, it worked.

Marcus Bell

Seven different goal scorers. Mogilny, Eminger, Sundin, Richards, Afinogenov, Spacek, Chlup. Is that the plan, or is that just the way it fell?

Roberto Luongo

It's the plan, for sure. Mats says it every meeting, every, every morning skate. The guys have to find a way. We've got young guys in this room, Spacek, Chlup, they're playing and they're scoring. I've seen them all year in practice. They're not, they're not surprises to me. Not even a little bit.

Marcus Bell

In January you told Jan the team hadn't changed, the pucks were just going in. Is tonight what you meant by that?

Roberto Luongo

Yeah. Yeah, I think so. I mean, the boys played like this three weeks ago, five weeks ago, we just didn't get the, the one bounce. Tonight the pucks went in and the other side didn't. That's the game. But I stand by it. The guys, they've been playing the same way all year.

Marcus Bell

Development Index has you at plus-five right now, ninety-five overall. Does that feel like the number?

Roberto Luongo

I don't look at it, you know. I don't. The Index, it's, for sure, it's nice. But the number is the wins. I've got one tonight. That's what matters. Everything else is, is noise from the office.

Marcus Bell

One more quick one. Twenty-two wins now, first place in the Northeast by nine. Do you let yourself feel that or is it game-by-game?

Roberto Luongo

Game-by-game, for sure. You look at Boston, they've got four straight. New Jersey, four straight. You blink and you're one game instead of nine. I'm not thinking about January. I'm thinking about, about the next guy I have to face. Right now that's all I know.

Marcus Bell

Roberto Luongo, thanks for your time. Back to you.
