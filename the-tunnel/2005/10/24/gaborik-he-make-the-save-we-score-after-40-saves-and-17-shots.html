Gaborik: 'He Make the Save, We Score' After 40 Saves and 17 Shots

Toronto's speed winger said Roberto Luongo's 40-save night changed how the room played in a 3-1 win over Florida that saw the Panthers outshoot the Leafs 43 to 17.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelMonday, 24 October 2005

1:43 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Marian Gaborik99 had a goal and a point as Toronto Maple Leafs beat Florida Panthers 3-1 on 2005-10-23 in a game Florida outshot 43 to 17. Roberto Luongo95 stopped 40 of 43 shots to preserve the win. Gaborik spoke to The Tunnel's Marcus Bell at the boards after the game.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here, rinkside after the 3-1 win. Seventeen shots for Toronto tonight, forty-three against. That's not the script I expected from a team built the way you're built. What did that feel like out there?

Marian Gaborik

Yeah, you know, we play good game but Florida, they shoot a lot. They come in waves. So, uh, we stay in our zone, we block, we don't give them the second one. Forty-three, yeah, it's a lot. But on the bench you feel, I mean, you feel you are in the game the whole time.

Marcus Bell

Roberto stopped 40 tonight. What does that look like from the blue line, a skater watching a night like that?

Marian Gaborik

It looks like a win. He make the save, we score, he make another one. That's the game. And, you know, when you have him behind you, you play different. You take the shot knowing he's going to make it. So you don't panic. That's the whole thing.

Marcus Bell

You got the first one. Mats had two assists tonight. What was that play?

Marian Gaborik

Yeah, Mats, you know, he see the ice before everybody. He find me and I just, uh, I go. Fast. And the goalie, I try to make him move, and then I put it in. It's simple when Mats give you the puck like that.

Marcus Bell

And your two blue-liners got the other goals. Barret Jackman and Andreas Stahle. What do you see from your bench when your defense is on the scoresheet?

Marian Gaborik

It's good, you know? Barret, his point shot, it's live. And Stahle, young guy, he come up with confidence, he not scared. So the room, we see it and we like it. We need everybody. Tonight we needed it.

Marcus Bell

Jan Kowalczyk asked you in January about how the room resets after a game where the shots don't produce. Tonight you had seventeen shots and you won 3-1. Does that answer his question?

Marian Gaborik

Yeah, I remember. You know, every game is different. Some night the puck goes in, some night it doesn't. Tonight we score our three and Roberto does the rest. You take the two points. The room, we just want the Cup. So 17 shots, 43 shots, it doesn't... it's the same two points at the end.

Marcus Bell

Six and two, first in the Northeast. Good night, Marian. Back to you.
