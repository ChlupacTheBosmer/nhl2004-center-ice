Sutter on the Klee signing, the room, and the five days ahead

The Flames coach talks about a depth signing that went sideways before it started, and about a room that does not need telling it is good.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelMonday, 24 October 2005

2:56 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Darryl Sutter's Calgary Flames won 4-3 in overtime at Minnesota Wild on 2005-10-22, their 3rd straight win and 4th on the road in 4 tries. They sit at 7-2-0, 10 points, 3rd in the Northwest. The room is loose, the coach is not, and the front office signed a 34-year-old defenseman on October 20 who is already out with a rib.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here in the tunnel at the Xcel, Darryl. 4-3, overtime, on the road. Three straight wins. How's that room riding right now?

Darryl Sutter

It's one game, Marcus. We're seven in. The room's fine. The room was fine last week too when we weren't winning, and the room's not going to get too high tonight either. That's the job.

Marcus Bell

You said in December, to my colleague Jan, that morale is yours to manage and not the public's to discuss. Is it easier to manage when you're 4-0 on the road?

Darryl Sutter

Easier, sure. It's always easier to manage it when things are going. You don't earn anything for keeping a room level in October when you're winning. You earn it in February. We'll see about February.

Marcus Bell

Let me ask about the week. You signed Ken Klee on the 20th. $224,990. And he's got a rib already. That's a week of having him and he's back on the 27th. How did that land in here?

Darryl Sutter

It didn't land anywhere because nobody's thinking about a contract. We needed a body on the blue line. Ken gives us 6 minutes, he kills a penalty, he's been here. He's out five days, that's where we are. You don't build a conversation around a five-day scratch.

Marcus Bell

But you brought him in for depth and he's immediately on the shelf. Does that change what you're looking at on the back end?

Darryl Sutter

No. Toni Lydman is playing a third of the minutes for us already, he can slide to the left. Roman Turek's in the other end and we're not asking him to score goals. You adjust the pairings, you go to bed. The depth chart's not a science experiment.

Marcus Bell

Turek's started six of seven, 4-2. McLennan got one and won it. Are you settling in on the 1?

Darryl Sutter

I'm not settling in on anything. Roman's 35 and he's been good, that's what I know. Jamie's 34 and he won a game. You manage a goaltender the same way you manage everybody else, you play the guy that's ready tonight. That's not a mystery, Marcus, that's a schedule.

Marcus Bell

Next up is Dallas Stars tomorrow night, on the road. Then San Jose Sharks® on Saturday, Buffalo Sabres® on the 31st. That's four more away games on top of the four you've already won. Does a room get tired of that?

Darryl Sutter

It's a back-to-back tomorrow, that's what it is. You sleep, you travel, you play. Dallas is a good team, they're 4-2 and they're at home. You don't think about the fourth road game in a row, you think about tomorrow's details. If you start counting the bus rides you've got a problem you don't have yet.

Marcus Bell

Jarome Iginla's at 15 points in 7 games. 9 goals. Does that number surprise you?

Darryl Sutter

No. He scored 18 in 42 games last year in a lockout season that wasn't even a lockout yet. The guy's 94 overall, he's 28, he's in his prime. The question's not whether he's going to score, it's whether we're going to get him the puck in areas where it matters. That's our job. Not his.

Marcus Bell

Last one. Three straight wins, perfect on the road, the Cup's not gone long. Is the room relaxed, or is this the week you still ride them hard?

Darryl Sutter

We're seven games in. I'm going to ride them every single day until somebody tells me why that's a bad idea. A three-game win streak is not a message to the room. It's a message that we have to do it again tomorrow. And tomorrow's Dallas. Back to you, Marcus.
