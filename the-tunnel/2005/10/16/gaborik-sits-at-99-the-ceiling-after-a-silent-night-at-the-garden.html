Gaborik sits at 99, the ceiling, after a silent night at the Garden

The Bureau's Book puts Toronto's right winger at the top of the scale the same morning he finishes with no points in a 6-2 road win at New York

Marian Gaborik99 woke up at 99 overall on the Bureau's Book, eleven points above his base grade of 91 and the highest mark available. The grade came the morning after Toronto Maple Leafs won 6-2 on the road at New York Rangers, a game where Gaborik logged zero points in 1 game. Toronto now leads the Northeast at 6 points through 4 games.

My game, I mean, it's there. The legs are there. Just, you know, the puck didn't come to me at the right time. But the win is the win.

You're at 99 on the Bureau's Book tonight. That's the ceiling. Eleven points over your base grade. Does that number mean something on a night like this?

Marian Gaborik

You know, I don't look at it. I'll be honest. The grade, the 99, it's okay, it's nice. But I play 82 games. The grade comes up, the grade goes down. Tonight I have no points, the grade says ninety-nine. So it's not tonight. It's the whole thing.

But what does a quiet game feel like when everyone around you is scoring? Spacek gets one, Stahle gets one. You're on the wing, you see it all.

It's okay. It's okay because we win. When I have a quiet night and we lose, that's not good, you know? That sits with me. But tonight, Chistov scores, Andreas scores. Everyone's going. After the game, in the room, everybody's laughing. So yeah, I'm happy. My time comes, you know? It comes.

Toronto's first in the Northeast, 6 points through 4 games. Buffalo's one win behind you with a game in hand. What does the next stretch ask of this group?

Yeah, you know, Buffalo is a good team, they are. And everyone in the division, you play them twice, three times in the beginning, and every game is close. MTL has points already.
