Gaborik sits at 99, the ceiling, after a silent night at the Garden

The Bureau's Book puts Toronto's right winger at the top of the scale the same morning he finishes with no points in a 6-2 road win at New York

Marian Gaborik99 woke up at 99 overall on the Bureau's Book, eleven points above his base grade of 91 and the highest mark available. The grade came the morning after Toronto Maple Leafs won 6-2 on the road at New York Rangers, a game where Gaborik logged zero points in 1 game. Toronto now leads the Northeast at 6 points through 4 games.
