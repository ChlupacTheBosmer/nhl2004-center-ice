Gallant on Leclaire's three straight: 'He's fine, but we'll see Monday'

The Blue Jackets' coach says the goaltending decision at San Jose will be made Monday morning, and that having every man healthy changes how he manages the next four games.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelSunday, 16 October 2005

2:49 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Columbus Blue Jackets head coach Gerard Gallant spoke to The Tunnel after his club beat Phoenix Coyotes 2-1 on October 14, moving to 2-1-0 with Jean-Sebastien Giguere95's old Vezina season still fresh in the building's memory. Pascal Leclaire has started all 3 games; Marc Denis has not played. The Blue Jackets open a back-to-back Monday at San Jose Sharks.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here in the corridor at Nationwide, Gerard, thanks for stopping. Two to one, 18 shots to 16, a tight one against Phoenix. How do you see that game?

Gerard Gallant

Yeah, it was tight. They're a good team, they don't give you much, and we knew that going in. I thought our structure was good, I thought we managed the puck better than we have the last couple weeks. The details were there tonight.

Marcus Bell

Pascal Leclaire, third straight start, you've used him every single game so far. Twenty-two years old. What does it take to get Marc Denis in that net?

Gerard Gallant

Well, you know, Pascal's been good for us. Two and one, he's competing every night. Marc's a professional, he handles himself in practice the same way every day, and I don't think there's any issue there. But, uh, I'll make that call Monday morning. We'll see how the legs are after travel, we'll see how Pascal feels, and we'll go from there.

Marcus Bell

You've got San Jose Monday, Florida Friday, Tampa Tuesday. Four games in eight days. With no one on the injured list, does that change how you think about the lineup?

Gerard Gallant

Obviously it does. You'd rather have that problem than the other one, I'll tell you that right now. We're healthy, every man can go, and that means I've got choices. And that means guys like Mikey Keane and Andy Cassels and Jim Montgomery are sitting, and that's hard, you know? Those guys earned their spot. But we've got 34 guys and I can dress, what, twenty? I've got to pick the right twenty every night and that's my job.

Marcus Bell

Mike Keane hasn't played a single shift this year. Thirty-eight, an eighty overall player, zero games. How do you keep him ready?

Gerard Gallant

You talk to him. You tell him the truth. He knows, you know, he's been around a long time, he knows what the back-to-backs do to your legs. He's ready, I have no doubt. And when I need him, he'll be in there. But tonight wasn't the night, you know? I needed the legs of some other guys.

Marcus Bell

Dimitri Kalinin scored his first of the year tonight, one goal in three games. He's your second-highest rated D-man at eighty-four. What do you need from him going forward?

Gerard Gallant

The same thing. He's good, he's got that shot from the point, he sees the ice well. One goal's nice but I need him solid in his own end every night. He does that. The goals come. He's twenty-five, he's got his best years in front of him.

Marcus Bell

You're two and one, first place in the Central. What's the next step for this group?

Gerard Gallant

The next step is every game. We don't look at the standings this early, you know, that's, that's a trap. You win tonight, you go to San Jose, and they've got Nabokov and they're tough at home. You don't get anything for free in this league. We've got to compete every single night or we lose, it's that simple.

Marcus Bell

Tyler Wright and Todd Marchant are your top two centers on the score sheet right now. Is that who you want running against the other club's top line for the whole year?

Gerard Gallant

Well, you know, the lines, they're not set in stone. I'm going to match up. Some nights it's Espen Knutsen against their top guy, some nights it's Tyler. Yanic Perreault's been good for us too. I'm not going to put a label on it and say 'this is the first line forever.' It changes. It should change.

Marcus Bell

Last one. You said Monday morning you'll decide on the goaltender. Is there a plan now or is it truly that open?

Gerard Gallant

There's a plan. There's always a plan. But I'm not going to tell you what it is, you know? You'll find out Monday. Pascal's earned the right to hear it from me first, face to face, and that's what I'll do.

Marcus Bell

Gerard Gallant, head coach of the Columbus Blue Jackets, thank you, back to you.
