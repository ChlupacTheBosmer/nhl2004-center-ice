Forsberg lets the building do the work: 'That crowd, they carried us in the third'

COL's captain says the home crowd lifted the team through a third-period deficit and into overtime, and that early-season chemistry still needs time.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelSunday, 16 October 2005

1:46 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Peter Forsberg89 had 1 goal and 2 points for Colorado Avalanche in a 4-3 overtime win over Boston Bruins on 2005-10-14. The Avalanche sit at W1 with 4 points after two games, and Forsberg, 32, says the room is still finding its shape.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here at Pepsi Center with Peter Forsberg. Four, three, overtime. You had a goal and a helper tonight. What was it like on the bench when that overtime started? You could hear this building from up there.

Peter Forsberg

Yes. It was, I think the crowd, when we came out for overtime, they were already, you know, on their feet. And that helps. I think in the third period when they made it three, three, we felt them. They did not stop. For sure that was a big thing for us tonight.

Jan Kowalczyk

You are two games in. A win in overtime, a loss before that. What does it feel like from inside when the record is this early and the games are this close?

Peter Forsberg

I think we are still finding each other a little. When you lose the first one, like we did, you get the information. You see what is not working. And tonight we put some of it right. Kariya tonight, three points, and Foote with a goal from the blue line. Those are the guys, you know, they make the difference when the game is tight.

Jan Kowalczyk

We talked in January, remember? You were 23 and 9 and then you went through a stretch where nothing fell right. Are you worried this could feel like that, this back and forth?

Peter Forsberg

No. It is two games. I am not going to, you know, compare two games to forty. In January we had played a long time and the legs were, I would say, heavy. Right now, no. I think we played good tonight. The first period we were, I mean we were not sharp. But the second and third, yes, that was good. And overtime, you win the draw, you get the puck to Joe, and from there... it is just hockey.

Jan Kowalczyk

One more and you are on the bus. Anything about the crowd tonight that stood out to you personally, as a man who has been in this building a long time?

Peter Forsberg

When it goes overtime at home, that is a different sound. I have been here enough games to know that. The third period, when they score the third one, the building gets quiet for a moment, maybe two seconds. And then the next shift they come back. That is what I like. You can feel them want it. And the players, you want to give them something back for that.

Jan Kowalczyk

Peter Forsberg, thank you as always. Back to you.
