Tuomo Pihlman on 3 games, a 57 ceiling, and a plan that stops at tomorrow's practice

Minnesota's Tuomo Pihlman won the Calder two seasons ago and played 3 games the season after. He sat down with Marcus Bell to talk about both, and about a future he won't put a date on.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelThursday, 20 October 2005

2:57 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Tuomo Pihlman76 won the Calder Memorial Trophy two seasons ago, 23 goals and 34 points in 73 games for Minnesota Wild. Last season he played 3 games. The Bureau's Book now has him at 76 overall, up 4 on the Development Index, with a ceiling it sets at 57. He sat down with Marcus Bell this week.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell, The Tunnel. Tuomo, thanks for sitting down with us. No game tonight, no rush. How's the year treating you so far?

Tuomo Pihlman

Yeah, it's good. It's, you know, it's early and the room's good. I feel good. That's the main thing, right?

Marcus Bell

Let's go back one. Two seasons ago you win the Calder, 23 goals. Last season you play 3 games. I'll just ask it. What was that?

Tuomo Pihlman

Yeah. Three games. Honestly, there's no story there for you. It was 3 games. I was here, I was working, and that's what it was.

Marcus Bell

Did it feel like a lost year while it was happening, or only after?

Tuomo Pihlman

No, not while it's happening. While it's happening you're just thinking about tomorrow's practice. It's in the summer, when you're home, that you sit with it. That's when it lands.

Marcus Bell

What did you land on, when you sat with it?

Tuomo Pihlman

That you can't wait for it. The game, I mean. You can't wait for the game to come to you. When you're not playing, you think playing is the easy part. Then you get out there and you're a step behind. I was a step behind.

Marcus Bell

Behind on what? Reads, legs, timing?

Tuomo Pihlman

Legs were fine. It's the puck, and what you do before it gets to you. You don't get much time to decide in this league, and if you're late the puck's already gone. I was late.

Marcus Bell

You scored 23 the year before. Did you still feel like that player?

Tuomo Pihlman

He's in there. You don't forget how to play. But nobody in this league cares what you did the year before. You have to show it again, every night. That's the cliché, and, you know, it's the cliché for a reason.

Marcus Bell

The Bureau's Book has you at 76, up 4 on the Development Index. It's also got your ceiling at 57, 19 grades under where they've placed you. That one's been out there a while. Have you heard it?

Tuomo Pihlman

I've heard it. I don't read the Book. Somebody always tells you, though. 57. Okay. It's a number. I'm 21, I've got a lot of hockey left. They can write whatever number they want.

Marcus Bell

Does it bother you, though?

Tuomo Pihlman

Bother me? A little, maybe. You want them to think you can get better. Everybody wants that. But I'm not going to argue with a number in a magazine. I'll argue on the ice.

Marcus Bell

Alright, let me change lanes. When the skates come off for good, what then?

Tuomo Pihlman

I think about it, but not too much. I'm 21. I'm still figuring out how to play. But yeah, I'd want to stay around it. That's all I know.

Marcus Bell

Coaching, or upstairs, or something else?

Tuomo Pihlman

I don't know. Coaching, maybe. I watch what the coaches do here all day and it's a lot more than I thought. Maybe I'd be terrible at it. I don't want to sit here and say, yeah, coach, and then you play it back to me in ten years.

Marcus Bell

So no plan.

Tuomo Pihlman

No plan. I've got a plan for tomorrow's practice. That's about as far as I go.

Marcus Bell

Back to those 3 games. A kid walks into this room tomorrow and has a year like yours. What do you tell him?

Tuomo Pihlman

I'd tell him to keep working. That's what I got told. Wes Walz was on me every single day. He doesn't let you feel sorry for yourself, not for one shift. So I'd tell the kid, find yourself a Wes.

Marcus Bell

What would Wes Walz say to him?

Tuomo Pihlman

Not much. That's the thing with him. He doesn't give you a speech. He just skates with you until you get it right. Then he skates some more.

Marcus Bell

Does he still play that way?

Tuomo Pihlman

Every day. He's been around forever and he still practises like he's got something to prove. You watch that long enough and you quit feeling sorry for yourself. That's the lesson.

Marcus Bell

Where's the Calder these days?

Tuomo Pihlman

With my mom. She shows it to everybody who comes over. I told her, keep it. It's safer there than with me.

Marcus Bell

Last one. If this season goes the way you want, what does it look like?

Tuomo Pihlman

You know what it looks like? It looks like a lot of games. That's it. I want to be out there playing, and the rest, the goals, the numbers, that's for you guys. I just want the games.

Marcus Bell

Tuomo, thanks for your time. That's Tuomo Pihlman in Minnesota. Back to you.
