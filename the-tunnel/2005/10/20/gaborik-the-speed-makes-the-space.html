Gaborik: The speed makes the space

The TOR winger is quiet on the scoresheet with 1 goal in 5 games, but says the building tonight told him the team is playing the right way.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelThursday, 20 October 2005

2:35 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Marian Gaborik99 came off the ice with zero points after Toronto Maple Leafs beat Montreal Canadiens 2-0 on 2005-10-19, their third straight win and a 3-0 record on the road. Gaborik has 1 goal in 5 games this season against 55 last year, and the Bureau's Development Index has him at +11 with an overall grade of 99.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk in the tunnel at Montreal with Marian Gaborik. Two-nothing, third straight, three-nothing on the road this year. Thirty-one shots for the club, two on the board. What was it like in here tonight?

Marian Gaborik

The crowd, they're loud. Always. You know, when you come here the people, they want the game open, they want the run-and-gun. And tonight we say no. We keep it tight. And when this building gets quiet, you hear it all the way to the bench. That's... that's a good sound.

Jan Kowalczyk

In January I asked you after Boston about a game where the shots don't turn into goals, and you said the result is what matters. Tonight the result is the win. Does the zero sit any different when you're riding the bus with two points?

Marian Gaborik

Yeah, different. For sure it's different. But the zero is one game. I get chances, I push, I make the play. Maybe next game the puck finds me. That's how it works. In the room we talk about the 2-nothing, about Roberto, about the defense. Nobody sits there with the shot chart going, Marian why no point. That's not us.

Jan Kowalczyk

1 goal in 5 games. Last season you had 55. What's different out there for you right now?

Marian Gaborik

The spring, the ankle. It's good now, I can tell you that. But the first two games, I feel... how you say... I think with my feet. I push, I think, I push again. And in this league you lose half a step and it's nothing. Now I feel free. I feel like me. Tonight I feel like me. The goal, it's not there yet. It's coming.

Jan Kowalczyk

The Bureau's Index has you at +11 right now. Base 91, overall 99. That's the best you've been on the Index as long as I can remember. But 19.4 minutes a game, down from 20.5. You're 99 on the Index and the box says zero. Does that bother you?

Marian Gaborik

The Index, sure. I see it. But you know what, the Index doesn't put the puck in the net. The 19.4, that's the system. We play tight now. The coach, he wants the 2-nothing more than a 5-4. And the speed, you say it's wasted? No. 31 shots, that's the speed. We carry it, we push, we make the space. The goal is maybe one bounce. That's all.

Jan Kowalczyk

You're three-nothing away this season. Toronto plays differently on the road than at home. How do you see those games differently?

Marian Gaborik

Different buildings. Different noise. In Boston the crowd, they lift their team, you feel it the whole night. Here same. But tonight... tonight we take it from them. We make them play our game. And when Montreal plays Toronto's game, it's 2-nothing. Roberto doesn't let in one. I like that. I like it more than 5-4 where we run around all night.

Jan Kowalczyk

Five-one, ten points, first in the Northeast, W3. What's the room thinking about this?

Marian Gaborik

The Cup. Yeah, I say this, you know. But it's true. Every game, every road game, every 2-nothing, it's for that. 1 goal in 5, 55 goals, that's small. The Cup is the thing.

Jan Kowalczyk

Marian, thank you. Jan Kowalczyk in Montreal, back to you.
