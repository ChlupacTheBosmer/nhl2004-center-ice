Brodeur Wants the Overtime One Back

The Devils' goaltender lost his second at home in overtime, says the crease needs clearing, and wants that screened winner erased.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelThursday, 20 October 2005

1:55 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Martin Brodeur95 came off the ice at the Meadowlands with a 2-3 overtime loss to Nashville Predators on his hands, New Jersey Devils now 0-2 at home through 3 games. Brodeur played the full 60, stopped 21 of 24 shots, and his Devils outshot Nashville 26 to 24. The Patrik Elias88 and Jamie Langenbrunner88 goals weren't enough.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here with Martin Brodeur, right after that one. Martin, twenty-six shots for, twenty-four against, and you lose it two-three in overtime. What's the difference tonight?

Martin Brodeur

I don't know yet. Twenty-six, that's good. We had chances. But overtime, your legs are gone, and one shot gets by you. That's it, that's hockey. You're tired, the first man there gets the puck and I can't find it.

Jan Kowalczyk

The overtime winner. Walk me through what you saw.

Martin Brodeur

It was from the left circle, I think, or the point, I lose track of it. There's a body in front. I see the stick, I see the puck come off the stick, but by the time my eyes find it, it's past me. Like San Jose in December. I told Marcus, I saw it late. I saw it late tonight too. I want that one back.

Jan Kowalczyk

You told Marcus in January that the three-two, four-three win, that's your identity. Tonight was one of those games. But you didn't close it.

Martin Brodeur

Yeah. It's that kind of game, for sure. You're always in it, always one goal back, one goal up. But identity means you win those. We didn't win it. So tonight it's not the identity.

Jan Kowalczyk

You're zero and two at home through three games. That's where the points are sitting on the table. What does this stretch ask of you?

Martin Brodeur

It asks us to be better. We have to be better, that's it. The guys in front of me, they need to give me one more clean look. I'm tired of screened shots at our own building. We clear the crease, we get out of our zone with possession, we get the two-one lead instead of the two-two. That's all I'm asking.

Jan Kowalczyk

Two games played. Is the schedule heavy this early, does the legs come into it?

Martin Brodeur

I don't count them. Two, three, four, doesn't matter. You play, you rest, you go again. What matters is when I'm in the crease, I see the puck. Right now I don't see the puck enough. That's the whole thing.

Jan Kowalczyk

Patrick and Jamie both got one tonight. What do you need from them, from the guys up front?

Martin Brodeur

Patrick, he's there. He's always there, you know? The goal he gets tonight, good goal, he makes the play. But I need them to get me the cushion. Two-one. Not two-two. Twenty-six shots on their net, you should have the cushion, and I'd give up one less goal. Jamie too, same thing. They get their chance, they put it in, they get me the lead I need.

Jan Kowalczyk

Martin Brodeur, thank you, good luck. Back to you.
