Gaborik: 'We Can Connect More'

The Maple Leafs right winger talks about a line still finding its timing, and what the defensive structure has felt like through two games.

Jan Kowalczyk here at the Air Canada Centre with Marian Gaborik. Marian, two and oh, one goal allowed across two games. What has changed about the way this group is defending from the start?

Marian Gaborik

Well, you know, everyone comes back. Even me and Rick, we are back in our zone, first thing. And today, the first period, we were really tight, how you say, around the net. Did you feel the penalty killers give the team a lift tonight? Anything specific that you saw from the bench?

Marian Gaborik

Yeah, when you're short the guys are, uh, they are everywhere. Blocking, they don't care. And you feel that from the bench, you know, every shot that gets blocked it's, it's energy. The whole bench jumps. So yeah, that helps us, the guys up top, we get the puck and we can go.

You scored tonight. Your linemates did not put up a point. I know you've said before that the numbers don't matter to you, but how is it feeling up there, finding each other?

Marian Gaborik

Yeah, we play good, I think. But we can, we can connect more. Brad, he likes to go middle, and I, I like to go to the net, and sometimes we are, uh, how you say, we are on the same ice at the same time but the timing is not, is not there yet. Today I score and that's good. But we, we want to play more together. Give and go, one-timer. It will come.

Jan Kowalczyk

Last time we spoke, back in January, I asked you about a night where the shots weren't producing. Tonight, 36 shots, four goals. Does that change what you feel in the room, the confidence?

Marian Gaborik

Yeah, I remember that one. That was the, the 32 shots, no. Today is different. When the puck goes in you play with your head up, you know? You trust more. And, and everybody scored today, everyone. Rick, Mats, Chisty, me. When everyone scores, the next game you think, okay, my goal is coming. That's, that's important.

Marian Gaborik, thank you. Back to you.
