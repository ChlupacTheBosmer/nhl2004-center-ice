Alfredsson on a 0-2 start: 'They are the champions. We were there, but they showed it'

Ottawa's captain says Toronto's depth and speed broke open the third period, and he credits Rachunek and Schastlivy for filling gaps left by injuries in the lineup.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelThursday, 13 October 2005

2:13 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Daniel Alfredsson89 watched his Ottawa Senators fall 4-1 on the road at Toronto Maple Leafs on Tuesday night, leaving Ottawa at 0-2-0 with 2 goals for and 7 against through two games. Alfredsson, 32, the club captain, has no points in 2 games this season at 21.0 minutes a night. He spoke to Jan Kowalczyk in the corridor at the Air Canada Centre.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Daniel, Jan Kowalczyk here at the Air Canada Centre for The Tunnel. Four-one on the board. Your team is zero and two and oh, two goals for, seven against. And Toronto is back to back, eight scored, one allowed. How did you see it from out there?

Daniel Alfredsson

They are a good team, of course. They won the Cup. They have the players, the depth, all of it. We were in it, I think, for two periods. After that, they get the extra one, and then they get two more. That is a long way back against a team like this one.

Jan Kowalczyk

You said two periods. The third looked different. They outshot you 36 to 29 on the night, and you looked like the team chasing the play in the last period. What broke?

Daniel Alfredsson

I think we start to press, you know? You want the goal, and you open up the ice too much. And they are fast. They have the players who can go end to end. We get caught in transition. That is where they score the goals, I think. In the middle of our rush.

Jan Kowalczyk

I want to ask about the lineup. There were changes tonight. Rachunek with a point on the back end, Schaefer up higher, Schastlivy in the mix. When injuries thin your room, how do you keep those new combinations from looking a half step behind?

Daniel Alfredsson

It is never easy, no. You lose a guy, the lines change, you have to do it quick. But Karel, he is ready. He works every single day. He has been ready for a long time and tonight he shows it, for sure. And Petr, Schastlivy, he is young but he plays hard, he moves the puck. You just trust the guys who are in there. They are NHL players, every one of them.

Jan Kowalczyk

Marian Hossa scored tonight, his first of the season. But the group, two goals in two games. You put up 108 points last season. Do you feel the weight of where the offense comes from?

Daniel Alfredsson

Two games. It is nothing. We have the players. Marian, he is a goal scorer, you know that. Martin, he can score too. And me, I will get going, for sure. We just need to play minutes together, that is all. The goals come. The team is what I worry about.

Jan Kowalczyk

Thirty-six against, twenty-nine for. You spent a lot of time in your own zone. How tiring is it to chase a team like that?

Daniel Alfredsson

Very tiring. They cycle. They move the puck, their defensemen join, they dump and chase and come get it. You are in your own end for a long shift at a time. And then when you get out, your legs are gone. They have done it all year, probably. They are comfortable like that.

Jan Kowalczyk

Zero and two. You have one more in the Northeast before the schedule opens up. What do you tell the room in the morning?

Daniel Alfredsson

Nothing. I do not tell them anything. We know what happened. We watch the video tomorrow. We fix it. Next game, we play better. That is hockey. You cannot stay here and feel sorry.

Jan Kowalczyk

Daniel Alfredsson, thank you. Back to you.
