Gaborik watches six go in, wants his name on the next one

Toronto's top-graded winger, scoreless in a 6-4 win over Phoenix, talked about speed, special teams, and Petr Chlup's night.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelTuesday, 18 October 2005

2:06 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Marian Gaborik99 sat at zero points in Toronto Maple Leafs's 6-4 home win over Phoenix Coyotes on Saturday, a night Phoenix outshot 29 to 26. The 23-year-old winger carries a 99 overall in the Bureau's Book, up 11 on the Development Index from his base grade, the highest figure on Toronto's roster. Four different skaters scored, but Gaborik was not among them.

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here with Marian Gaborik after a 6-4 win for Toronto over Phoenix tonight. Marian, you were on the ice for all six but your name didn't go up on any of them. Start with the game itself. They had three more shots than you and you put six past them.

Marian Gaborik

Yeah. It is, uh, good win for us. They shoot, what, 29? And we put six in. The first period maybe we are not, not sharp, not so sharp. And then after the second goal, we get, how you say, going. We get our wheels. And six is six.

Marcus Bell

Back in December you told me the short shifts keep the speed at full. Were the legs there tonight?

Marian Gaborik

The legs, yes. The legs are good. I feel the speed, I get there before, before a lot of guys, I know that. But the puck goes to somebody else tonight. Petr, Mats, everybody get in. So I think, okay, good for the team, good. But also... I want more touches. I want to be in the play more, you know? I talk to coach maybe. More ice.

Marcus Bell

Special teams, the power play, the penalty kill. How did those feel from out there?

Marian Gaborik

The PK, I think we are solid. The structure, it is there. You see the guys block, they get in lanes, we kill what we take. And the PP, I was not on the unit tonight, I think, so I watch from the bench. And the next one they put me on, I find the space. I always find the space, how you say. I know where they are not. So. It comes.

Marcus Bell

Petr Chlup, two goals, three points. What did you see from him out there?

Marian Gaborik

Petr. Yeah. He is good, he is good. His first one, tonight, he get the puck, and he look up, and, uh, he go. And the goalie, he cannot move. Finished. And the second one... I think that is maybe a lucky one, you know? But lucky is fine. Petr score, Petr play good game, we win. Everybody happy.

Marcus Bell

Luongo faced 29 and had four go by. How much did that give you out there?

Marian Gaborik

Roberto, when he is good, everybody play different. You play a little more free, how you say? Because you know behind you, he take one, he take two, three. Tonight four, and we win. If he take five, bigger win. He is the wall.

Marcus Bell

Last one. Sundin, two goals tonight. How does he look to you right now?

Marian Gaborik

Mats is Mats, you know? He play like it is nothing, like it is easy. And tonight, two goals, and he is in the right place always. The right place. The captain, he just, he is there when you need him. And that is everything, I think.

Marcus Bell

Marian Gaborik, thank you. Back to you.
