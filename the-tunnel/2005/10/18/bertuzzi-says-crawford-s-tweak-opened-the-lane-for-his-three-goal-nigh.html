Bertuzzi says Crawford's tweak opened the lane for his three-goal night

The winger scored all three in a 3-2 win over Boston and pointed at a breakout change the coach installed before camp.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelTuesday, 18 October 2005

2:43 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Todd Bertuzzi89 scored all three goals for Vancouver Canucks in a 3-2 win over Boston on Monday night at home. The winger finished with 3 points, all of them set up by Markus Naslund87, and the Canucks sit at 2-1-1 through four games with a two-game winning streak.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here in the tunnel with Todd Bertuzzi. Todd, three goals, Naslund sets them all up, and you walk off with a 3-2 win. What did the coach change that let that happen?

Todd Bertuzzi

Yeah. Uh, the breakout. Marc has us doing a different first pass now. Before camp it was, you know, the D carries and looks for the winger deep. Now the center gets it at the blue line, we're going north-south immediately, and by the time their D realizes we're in there we're already, I mean, we're already set up. I just go get it.

Jan Kowalczyk

So it's not something that happened tonight, it's been in the building since September?

Todd Bertuzzi

Yeah, we've been running it in practice. Obviously it didn't click right away, nobody's, you know, it took a while. But tonight we had, what, three or four times where the puck came to me at the dot and I just, I didn't have to think about it. Shoot it. And the first one I put it top shelf, Nas found me, and the rest just went in.

Jan Kowalczyk

Naslund with three assists on the night. You two have been together for a while. Is the change about where you're picking him up, or is it something else?

Todd Bertuzzi

No, Nas is Nas. You know where he's going, he's always at the far post, he's always open. The thing is now, I'm getting there at the same time. Before I was a half-step behind him, maybe a step, and the lane was already clogged. Now I'm there and he's there and, you know, their D can't cover both of us. It's that simple.

Jan Kowalczyk

Ed Jovanovski and Mattias Ohlund both on the scoresheet too. Is the defense part of what changed?

Todd Bertuzzi

Yeah, and that's the other half of it. The breakout change means the D aren't stuck behind their own net trying to make a play with two guys on them. Ed and Oly, they're getting the puck clean, they're walking in, they've got options. Oly hit me on the second goal from the point, right? That's him having time. Last year that play doesn't exist, he's under pressure, he dumps it.

Jan Kowalczyk

You're 2-1-1 now, and you've got the division ahead of you. What does this stretch ask?

Todd Bertuzzi

Uh, it asks for four lines. That's what it asks. You can't just lean on the top two lines through November and December, we know that. Tonight was three goals from one line, that's, that's not how this works long-term. We've got to get the bottom six contributing, get Oly and Ed adding offensively like they did tonight. That's the next thing.

Jan Kowalczyk

Back in January we talked after a six-three loss here and you said the team was chasing too much. Does this look different to you from what you were running then?

Todd Bertuzzi

Yeah, it does. We're not chasing tonight, and that's the whole point. The system, whatever Marc's doing with it now, it, I mean it keeps us simple. We don't get pulled out of position trying to make a play that isn't there. Boston had twenty-four shots, we had twenty-three, that's a hockey game. That's not a track meet.

Jan Kowalczyk

Last year of the deal, Todd. You look comfortable out there.

Todd Bertuzzi

I've got three goals tonight, Jan, obviously I look comfortable. You know what I mean though. I like the way it's set up. I don't need to worry about the contract, I need to worry about tomorrow's practice and whether my legs are there. Three goals at thirty, you know what I mean, you're just happy the legs still work.

Jan Kowalczyk

Todd Bertuzzi, thanks for your time. Back to you.
