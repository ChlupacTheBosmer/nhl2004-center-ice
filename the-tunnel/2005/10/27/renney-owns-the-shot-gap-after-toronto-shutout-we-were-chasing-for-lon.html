Renney owns the shot gap after Toronto shutout: 'We were chasing for long stretches'

The head coach put his finger on the neutral zone and the young guys getting their first real run, and would not talk about goaltending after a 0-2 loss.

Marcus BellRinkside reporter · The TunnelThursday, 27 October 2005

3:26 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

New York Rangers managed 18 shots on the road at Toronto Maple Leafs on 2005-10-26, their fewest of the season, in a 0-2 loss that ended a two-game win streak. Head coach Tom Renney spoke to The Tunnel in the tunnel at the Air Canada Centre about the structure that broke down, the 20-year-olds in his lineup, and filling Bobby Holik's spot until November 12.

Transcript

Marcus Bell

Marcus Bell here, in the tunnel at the Air Canada Centre, with Tom Renney. Coach, 0-2, 18 shots for, 34 against. That's a long night.

Tom Renney

Yeah, obviously. When you're getting outshot like that you're chasing the game for long stretches, and we were chasing tonight. We didn't have enough structure through the neutral zone. They were getting through with speed and then we're turning pucks over trying to make the play and now we're backing. And it feeds on itself.

Marcus Bell

18 shots is your fewest of the season. Where did they go?

Tom Renney

You know, I think we got to a point where we were making individual plays instead of team plays. And against a club like Toronto, who are structured and patient, you just don't get pucks to the net. We had looks early, I thought the first ten or twelve minutes we were alright, and then we just stopped. And it's a long way back.

Marcus Bell

Jussi Nurminen, nine games now, still no points at 20. Are you asking him too much too soon?

Tom Renney

Well, Jussi is a young man. Nine games at this level, you're playing against men every night and you're going up against the other club's top pairing on a regular shift, and the fourth line isn't always the easy matchup people think it is. He's competitive, he's not afraid. That's the first thing I'm looking for. The points come when his game gets a little more confident. I'm not worried.

Marcus Bell

Craig Abid too, four games, zero. What do you need from those two right now?

Tom Renney

What I need is for them to be hard to play against. That's the job. You're not asking a 20-year-old to be your top-six forward. You're asking: can you win the puck, can you make the simple play, can you get through a shift without turning it over in the offensive zone. Those are the details. And at 20 you're going to have nights where you get those wrong. That's just the truth of it.

Marcus Bell

Bobby Holik, shoulder, back November 12. Seven assists in nine games before he went down. How big a hole is that for the middle of your lineup?

Tom Renney

It's a big one. He's been good for us, and it's not just the assists. He wins faceoffs, he's on the penalty kill, he's responsible. You lose that and you're reshuffling, and it takes time to find the combinations. So we're working through it.

Marcus Bell

Steve Kelly and Trent Whitfield are what's left at center by the record. Is that the answer, or are you looking to add?

Tom Renney

Steve's been in there, he's playing. And we've got guys who can center if we need to move someone. We're not in a position where we're panicking in late October. You work with what you've got, you get the next guy ready. If Bobby's back in a few weeks and we're in a good spot, great. You don't fix what isn't broken.

Marcus Bell

Goaltending. Mike Dunham six starts, Dan Blackburn five. Mike's 3-3, Dan's 3-1. Is that a competition?

Tom Renney

You get asked that every week. They're both good goaltenders. They're both giving us a chance, and I think that's the important thing. You want your goaltending to be a non-issue. Right now it kind of is, in a good way.

Marcus Bell

Who was in net tonight?

Tom Renney

Mike. And I thought he was fine. 34 shots against means the defense is getting pushed back for long stretches, and that's what we fix. Not the goaltender.

Marcus Bell

Phoenix on Friday, then Tampa, then Boston. What do you tell the room tonight?

Tom Renney

I tell them it's one game. You know, it's October. We're 4-2-2, nobody's panicking. But you lose a game like this, 0-2, 18 shots, you watch the tape, you're honest about what you saw, and then you move on. Friday's a new one.

Marcus Bell

Coach Renney, thank you for your time. Back to you.
