Forsberg: 'Not Concern. Not Happy.'

COL's alternate captain went scoreless in a 3-0 road loss to Phoenix, his third straight defeat, and stood by the two-points-over-personal-stats line he gave this desk in December.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelThursday, 27 October 2005

1:40 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Peter Forsberg95 and Colorado Avalanche lost 3-0 in Phoenix on Monday, their third straight loss. Both clubs finished with 20 shots on goal. COL sits at 2-2-2, eight points through seven games, three losses in a row.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here with Peter Forsberg. Three-nothing to Phoenix, third loss in a row, twenty shots each. Peter, the third period is where this one slipped. What happened out there?

Peter Forsberg

I think we were playing good. I think we were there. And they get one, and then the game changes, for sure. When you are behind on the road, everything gets faster. You know? We were not bad out there. But it did not go our way.

Jan Kowalczyk

You had 129 points last season. You came in here tonight and went scoreless. You told this desk back in December, after the hat trick in Philadelphia, that you would trade three goals for two points. Are you standing by that tonight?

Peter Forsberg

I am standing by it. I do not think you should say one thing when you score and something else when you do not. But yes. I would have liked to help the team a little more tonight. I did not. That is how it is.

Jan Kowalczyk

Two and two and two. Is there concern in that room?

Peter Forsberg

Concern? No, I do not think so. You are not happy when you lose. I am not happy. But concern means you think it is broken. We are not scoring. We had twenty shots and we could not get one over the line. That is not a broken thing. That is a thing that will come.

Jan Kowalczyk

Joe Sakic, Milan Hejduk, Paul Kariya, Teemu Selanne. That is a lot of skill up front, and the group went scoreless.

Peter Forsberg

Yeah. The players can score. I think we did not make it easy enough for each other. That is on us, and that is on me too. You look at the video and you find the thing, you fix it. It will come.

Jan Kowalczyk

Trevor Kidd gave up three on twenty shots.

Peter Forsberg

He was fine. I think he gave us a chance. We did not help him, in front of him. That is a forward's job and we did not do it. It is not on Trevor.

Jan Kowalczyk

Phoenix is one and six. Does that tell you anything about where you are?

Peter Forsberg

It says we have to be better. You lose to a team that is one and six, you cannot be happy. I am not happy. I am not worried. But I am not happy.

Jan Kowalczyk

Peter Forsberg, thank you. Back to you.
