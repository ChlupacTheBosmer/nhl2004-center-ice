Lewis on the net: Legace has played every one, and Lewis is not changing it

After a 3-2 road win at Mighty Ducks® of Anaheim that followed a 2-3 overtime loss in LA the night before, Dave Lewis talked about his goaltender, his quiet Norris winner, and the men behind him.

Jan KowalczykRinkside reporter and studio host · The TunnelSaturday, 15 October 2005

3:16 · synthetic voices, produced from the record · download

ShareCopy link

Detroit Red Wings beat Mighty Ducks of Anaheim 3-2 on the road on 2005-10-13, outshooting them 19 to 18, one night after dropping a 2-3 overtime decision in LA. The win leaves Detroit Red Wings at 1-1-1 with 3 points. Head coach Dave Lewis stayed after to talk to The Tunnel about Manny Legace's three straight starts, the man in the room who hasn't played yet, and the schedule that comes next.

Transcript

Jan Kowalczyk

Jan Kowalczyk here, post-game, the tunnel at Honda Center. Coach Lewis, thanks for stopping. You're one night removed from that overtime loss in LA, you go out and win 3-2 on the road. That's not the easiest bounce back.

Dave Lewis

Yeah, no, it wasn't. You know, the guys came out and competed. That's the thing. We weren't sharp, I don't think anybody was sharp after playing the night before, but the compete level was there and that's what wins you these games in October.

Jan Kowalczyk

You had 19 shots to their 18, so it wasn't like you dominated possession. Did you feel like you earned it or it was just one of those nights?

Dave Lewis

Well, look, I think we managed the puck better than we have the last two games. That's where it comes from. You turn it over on their blue line, now they're going the other way, now you're chasing. We did that less. That's the detail that mattered.

Jan Kowalczyk

Let me ask you about the crease. Manny's played all three. One and two, no shutouts. Curtis hasn't dressed yet. Is there a plan here, or is this a by-committee situation?

Dave Lewis

It's Manny's net. I'll put it that way. Look, one and two after three is, it's not what he wants, I'll tell you that. But the saves he's made have been the right saves. I'm not going to sit here and pull a guy because of a couple of bounces.

Jan Kowalczyk

And Curtis? You've got a 90 overall sitting on the bench. What do you tell him?

Dave Lewis

I tell him what I tell everybody. You're going to get your games. You've got to be ready. He's been a professional his whole career, he knows how this works. I'm not worried about Curtis Joseph.

Jan Kowalczyk

Nicklas Lidstrom, Norris Trophy last year, zero points through three. Does that show up on your radar at all?

Dave Lewis

You want me to be worried about Nicklas Lidstrom? Come on. He's 89 overall, he's playing 25 minutes, he's keeping everybody else honest. If he doesn't get the points I'm the last man losing sleep over that. You know what I mean. Some of those guys, the points come when they come.

Jan Kowalczyk

Well Phil Housley has 3 assists, Pavel Datsyuk has 3 assists. You're getting production from different places. Is that what you wanted?

Dave Lewis

It's what you need, yeah. Obviously we want our top guys driving the play. But Housley's been good. He's 41 and he's still making the first pass clean, still opening up the ice. Datsyuk, same thing, he's finding guys, he just hasn't had the one bounce that turns an assist into a goal. He'll get there.

Jan Kowalczyk

You're healthy right now, the cleanest ice in the league maybe. Five clubs have a guy out. Purinton just went down for New York, Ott got the concussion in Dallas. What's the plan if something happens here?

Dave Lewis

Well, you hope you don't find out. But you work with the guys who haven't played. Uhlback, Bunka, Pesonen, they're in practice every day. I can't put a 19-year-old in tonight, I just can't, the step up is too much. But they're going to play this year. You'd better believe it.

Jan Kowalczyk

And when they do play, does that come at the expense of a veteran's minutes, or does the structure just absorb it?

Dave Lewis

The structure absorbs it. That's what the structure's for. You shorten the bench a little, you trust the guys who are in, you simplify what you're asking them to do. It's not complicated. You just have to be willing to do it.

Jan Kowalczyk

Montreal's here on the 17th, then you go to Minnesota on the 19th, Calgary on the 21st. Three in five. Does that change the net plan?

Dave Lewis

It'll change it. I'm not going to tell you what it'll look like right now. But three in five, yeah, you'll see both of them at some point in there. I just haven't decided which one.

Jan Kowalczyk

Coach, thank you. Back to you.
