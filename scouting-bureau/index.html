The Book: Top 100

The Bureau's current grade for every player on a league roster, on the 50-99 scale, as it reads today: the base grade with form, morale, the club's facilities and the venue applied. Revised every edition.

The Development Index

How far a player's overall reads above his base grade today. The Index moves with form; a high figure is a man playing above himself, and it tends to run with youth. The hundred highest, revised every edition.

The Morale Scale

The Bureau's morale reading on a 0-100 scale, from the club's record. The fifty highest, then the fifty lowest. Ice time against a man's grade is the lever that moves it.
