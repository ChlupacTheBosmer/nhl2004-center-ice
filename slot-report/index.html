Goalie Timeshares League-Wide: True No. 1s vs. Committees

Twenty-eight clubs have a starter. Only two do not — and one of them is spending $67 million to split work between a 92 and an 85.

Doug Reimer
Analytics editor, ex-video coach · The Slot ReportSaturday, 4 December 2004

The method is simple. I ranked every club by its lead goaltender's share of games played through the December 4 snapshot, then classified each into one of three tiers: Workhorse (85%+ of starts), Clear Starter (70–84%), or Committee (below 70%). The finding: the league has consolidated. Twenty-six clubs carry a defined starter; four carry a workhorse who is carrying too much. Only two clubs — Toronto Maple Leafs and Columbus Blue Jackets — split their starts so evenly that no single goaltender owns more than 60% of the net.
