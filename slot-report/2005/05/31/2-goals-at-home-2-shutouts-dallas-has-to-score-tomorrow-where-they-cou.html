2 Goals at Home, 2 Shutouts: Dallas Has to Score Tomorrow Where They Couldn't All Series

The Cup Final is 4-2 Toronto with Game 7 in Dallas on June 1. The Stars have been held scoreless twice in their own building.

Doug ReimerAnalytics editor, ex-video coach · The Slot ReportTuesday, 31 May 2005
ShareCopy link

The piece

Toronto Maple Leafs leads Dallas Stars 4-2 in the Cup Final. Game 7 is tomorrow at Dallas. The Stars scored zero in Game 1 at home, two in Game 2, and zero again in Game 5.

Every home game this series, scored the way the sheet lists them:

One win, two shutout losses, two goals scored against Roberto Luongo95. Luongo has 3 shutouts in this postseason. Two of them came in Dallas.

The Round-by-Round Shape

Dallas went 7-4 at home this spring across four rounds:

RoundDAL at HomeDAL on Road
R1 v DET2-0 (7-3)2-0 (5-2)

Two rounds at home went against them. The Final is the worst home sample: the only round where Dallas scored fewer goals at home than anywhere else all spring.

Marty Turco97 has been the better goaltender on the raw line that matters most to his own team: 14-7 with a 1.65 GAA. Luongo is 16-9 with a 1.81 GAA. But Luongo's .943 save percentage against Turco's .921, and three clean sheets to none, tell you who has been in goal when his team needed a zero. Dallas has given up 19 goals at home in 11 games. Toronto has given up 26 at home in 14 games.

Who Has Scored, and Who Has Not

Dallas's spring offense runs through one line. Modano88 has 24 points in 21 games. Morrow86 has 19 in 21. Zubov84 has 15 in 21. Guerin has 14 in 12, missing time to injury.

Toronto spreads it across four men: Roberts85 19 in 25, Sundin92 18 in 25, Mogilny89 17 in 25, Fischer84 11 in 25. Every one of them played all 25 games. In Dallas's building, where the team has been quiet, four different guys can put one in.

Modano is still the best player on the ice. 24 points in 21 games. But his goal production (10 in 21) trails his assist total (14), and the two home games where Dallas scored nothing are the two games where his line produced the least offense.

The Money Behind the Rosters

Toronto carries $60.04M for 133 points, $0.45M per point. Dallas carries $67.99M for 109 points, $0.62M per point. Toronto generated $55.69M in profit against Dallas's $28.10M. Toronto's revenue is $128.15M; Dallas's is $102.84M.

If Dallas loses tomorrow, they re-sign most of this group and try again in October. Toronto re-signs Marian Gaborik99 at $1.10M on one year remaining, a 98 overall with 99 points, and still holds $60.04M committed.

The Stars have spent more per point all season and arrive at Game 7 needing to outscore a team that has held them to two goals in their own building.

The Regular Season Said This

The season series went 5-1-1 to Toronto, 20-11 in goals. Dallas won 2, lost 5, tied 1. The regular season predicted this outcome from the start. Tomorrow is the one game that can prove it wrong.

Luongo will start. Turco will start. Dallas has 2 goals in 3 games in this building in the Final.
