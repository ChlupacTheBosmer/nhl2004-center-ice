$0.87M a Point: The Cheapest Roster in Hockey

The Canucks spend $34.94M and get 40 points. The method is payroll divided by points. The top five cheapest rosters are all productive; the top five most expensive are not.

Doug ReimerAnalytics editor, ex-video coach · The Slot ReportFriday, 17 December 2004
ShareCopy link

I took each club's payroll from the Bureau's Contract Ledger, divided it by the club's points, and ranked the result. The Vancouver Canucks Canucks come in first at $0.87M per point. The next four are Edmonton Oilers at $0.94M, Tampa Bay Lightning at $1.09M, New Jersey Devils at $1.10M, and Nashville Predators at $1.17M. All five have 39 or more points.

The other end of the table: Philadelphia Flyers at $2.56M for 27 points, St. Louis Blues at $2.36M for 24, Montreal Canadiens at $2.31M for 23, Detroit Red Wings at $2.25M for 36, Calgary Flames at $1.97M for 22. Four of the five most expensive rosters have 27 points or fewer. The clubs that spend the least per point are the ones converting their dollars into standing.

The Canucks' value comes from three players doing the work of five. Markus Naslund89 grades 91 and has 61 points in 31 games at a 161 pace. Todd Bertuzzi93 grades 95, the third-highest mark in the Book, and has 53 points. Dan Cloutier86 grades 88 in net and has played 25 of 31 games. The defensive anchor is Ed Jovanovski85 at 87, the highest-graded defenseman on the club.

The four together account for the structure that produces 40 points. Naslund's 161 pace and Bertuzzi's 140 pace put two players on the top-10 scoring list. Cloutier's 88 grade means the team is not losing games it should win because of the goalie. Jovanovski's 87 means the team is not giving up goals it should not. The Book's grades and the scoring column agree on this one.

The payroll context matters. Vancouver Canucks's $34.94M is the second-lowest in the league, behind only Minnesota Wild at $29.97M. The Wild have 23 points. The Canucks have 40.

The room backs the structure. The Bureau's Morale Scale has Vancouver Canucks at 96.4, third in the league. Mattias Ohlund80 sits at 100, the highest individual morale mark in the club, playing 29.6 minutes a night at 82 overall. The room is the third-best in hockey and the payroll is the second-worst. The players are not being paid for their grades, and the grades are showing up in the standings.

The Canucks lost 114 days to 13 injuries this season, including two spells for Naslund and a knee for Jovanovski. The team is at 40 points with those absences. The roster is thin enough that a knee costs you a week, and the points held anyway.

Naslund has 61. Bertuzzi has 53. The payroll is $34.94M. The next 50 games will show whether $0.87M a point is a rate or a sample.
