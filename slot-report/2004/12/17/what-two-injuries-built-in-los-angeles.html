What Two Injuries Built in Los Angeles

Deadmarsh missed 12 games with a groin. Allison played 29 with a wrist. Two 22-year-olds filled the space and both read +6 on the Bureau's Development Index.

Doug ReimerAnalytics editor, ex-video coach · The Slot ReportFriday, 17 December 2004
ShareCopy link

I pulled the Development Index for every player on the Los Angeles Kings roster, cross-referenced it with the injury ledger, and looked for players whose form rating jumped while a veteran at the same position lost time. Two names cleared the threshold. Both are 22. Both are filling a slot that an 87-grade forward vacated.

PlayerAgeBaseFormCurrentGPPts
Mike Cammalleri792279+683910
Jared Aulin712271+675306

Cammalleri is the easier read. 10 points in 9 games. The Bureau's Book has his pass at 93, his deking at 93, his speed at 89. Andy Murray told The Tunnel's Marcus Bell after the 3-2 win in Phoenix on December 15: "10 points in 9 games is a good rate. And he's a competitor, he's got the shot, he's got the speed, and he plays with a little fearlessness about him that I like."

Aulin is the slower burn. 6 points in 30 games. The Index has him up 6 from a base of 71, which puts his current grade at 75. He has played in all 30 of the club's games. The coach is keeping him in the lineup even when the production is quiet. At 22, a base of 71 is a floor. The +6 says he is playing above that floor right now.

The anchor is Roman Cechmanek87, who grades 91 in the Book. His speed grade is 91, the same as his overall. The workload table puts him in 26 of the club's 30 games, with Cristobal Huet76 taking 8 and Tim Thomas73 one.

Adam Deadmarsh83 missed 12 games with a groin and has 11 points in 12. His speed is 90, his shot is 93. Jason Allison83 played 29 games with a wrist and finished with 43. His pass is 97, the highest mark among the club's forwards. The two are the club's top-6 forwards by Book grade, both at 87.

The club's injury ledger shows 11 spells and 112 days lost this season. Deadmarsh's groin, Allison's wrist, Cechmanek's unspecified spell, Cammalleri's elbow. The 112 days is the 9th-highest total in the league, more than 21 of the 30 clubs. The young players are filling the space those absences open up.

Zigmund Palffy83 is the constant. 87 in the Book, 59 points in 30 games at a 161 pace, 22.9 minutes a night. He is 32 years old and the highest-scoring forward on the team. His shot is 94, his puck handling 93, his deking 96. The 161 pace says what the Index would. What he needs is the two 22-year-olds to keep the defense honest on the other end, so he keeps getting the puck in the slot.

Murray on the replacement, told to Marcus Bell: "The injuries came, you know, Jason Allison, Adam Deadmarsh, Zizka, and all of a sudden you've got to find minutes. And these kids, they've been ready. They've been ready for a while. We just had to find the space."

The record is 27 points in 30 games. The last 6: a win at Phoenix, a win over Pittsburgh, then four losses. The Index says the two young players are playing above their base. The scoreboard says the team is losing more than it is winning. The replacement is working at the player level. The team level has not caught up.

A 22-year-old who converts his minutes into a +6 on the Index earns the next set. A 22-year-old who does not convert has a base that will pull him back down. Cammalleri is converting. Aulin's 6 points in 30 games is not there yet.

The Bureau's Morale Scale has Los Angeles Kings at 94.2, second in the league. The club spends $48.60M, which buys 27 points at $1.80M per point. The next 50 games will show whether the two 22-year-olds keep climbing or fade back to base.
