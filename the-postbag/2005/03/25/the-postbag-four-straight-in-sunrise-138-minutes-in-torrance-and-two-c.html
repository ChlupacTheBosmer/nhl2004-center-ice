The Postbag: four straight in Sunrise, 138 minutes in Torrance, and two clubs that keep meeting the same wall

Six readers and what five losses, 138 minutes, and eleven straight look like from the seats

Friday, 25 March 2005 · edited by Tunde Oyelaran

Six letters. A club in Sunrise that keeps reaching overtime and losing it, a man in Torrance who leads the league in the penalty box, eleven straight in Etobicoke, a pattern in Buffalo that has met the same opponent five times with the same result, the same thing on Long Island, and a club in Anaheim that is two teams depending on where it plays.

Four straight losses, and not one voice at this club that sounds like it knows what year it is. The building gets emptier every week and I keep going because I don't know what else to do. Is anyone watching or are we all just waiting for October?

Yolanda Espinosa, Sunrise

Florida Panthers is 25-31-14 with 74 points in 75 games. The 14 overtime losses tie the most in the league, and four of the last ten games went to overtime and came back as losses. The 222 goals for are the fewest in the Southeast division. That is what 14 overtime losses look like over a full season.

I have watched every game from here. Every time Laperriere gets the pen he takes something stupid, and we pay for it in front of our own people. Is he a player who helps this club or a penalty machine who happens to wear a sweater?

Marcus Delacroix, Torrance

Ian Laperriere70 has 138 penalty minutes in 75 games, more than any other man in the league. Los Angeles Kings sits at 23-35-9 with 71 points, and its 251 goals against are the most in the Pacific division. He leads the league in penalty minutes; that is not in dispute. Whether his 138 cause the goals against or come from the same roster that lets in 251, I don't think the record settles.

Eleven in a row and I have not read a word about it. What does this club have to do to get mentioned? Win another ten?

Winston Achebe, Etobicoke

The page printed the streak in January, and you are right to expect it again now that it has reached 11. Toronto Maple Leafs is 55-12-4 with 124 points in 76 games. The last ten have gone 45 goals for and 16 against. The next club in the Eastern Conference is New Jersey Devils at 103.

We have played Toronto five times and not beaten them once. Not once. I remember watching this as a kid and now I am watching it from a different seat. At what point is it a pattern and not bad luck?

Halina Sobczyk, Cheektowaga

Five meetings, five regulation losses. Buffalo Sabres is 0-5-0 against Toronto Maple Leafs this season: no wins, no overtime losses, no points at all. The Sabres finished the division schedule 4 wins, 1 overtime win, 10 losses, 3 overtime losses, 57 goals for, 86 against. Five of those thirteen division defeats came against one opponent. This is a pattern. You are not imagining it.

Five times against Pittsburgh and not a single win. I used to think that was coincidence. Now I think this club has an opponent. At what point do you stop calling it bad luck and start calling it a problem?

Rosalind Fairweather, Hicksville

New York Islanders is 0-4-1 against Pittsburgh Penguins: four regulation losses and one in overtime, five meetings, no wins. Pittsburgh Penguins went 4-0-0 with 1 overtime win against them. The Islanders have 76 points, the Penguins 77. Five meetings and every one of them went the same way. That is the pattern you are looking for.

Twenty-four and thirteen at home, fourteen and twenty-five on the road. That is not a bad stretch, that is the whole season. Everyone says it is the travel. I want to know if anyone at this club has looked at that number and asked whether it is more than planes and hotel beds.

Delphine Okonkwo, Anaheim

Mighty Ducks of Anaheim is 24-13 at home and 14-25 on the road. 213 goals for, 213 against, exactly even across 76 games. The club scores what it gives up, wins at home, loses on the road, and sits 6th in the Western Conference at 90 points with 6 games left. Travel might explain part of the split; the numbers only show it. But 24-13 and 14-25 are not a stretch, and your question is fair.
