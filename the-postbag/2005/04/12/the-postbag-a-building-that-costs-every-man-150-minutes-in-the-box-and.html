Letters to the editor

The Postbag: a building that costs every man, 150 minutes in the box, and what eleven losses sound like from the inside

Six readers wrote in the week the playoffs started. The page answers them with the record open.

Tuesday, 12 April 2005 · edited by Tunde Oyelaran

The regular season ended, the playoffs started, and the mail got pointed at the clubs nobody covered during the season. A building that costs every man on the roster, a man who spent more time in the penalty box than any other, and a coach saying what eleven straight losses sound like from the inside.

Does the state of this building actually cost us anything on the ice, or is that something people say?

Rosalind Fairweather, Hicksville

The Bureau puts the penalty at -3.64 on every man's overall grade from the facilities. That is the worst in the league. New York Islanders finished 30-38-4 with 84 points and made the postseason as the eighth seed in the East, which means they are playing Toronto Maple Leafs right now and losing 2-0 in the series. The club draws $79 a ticket with 12943 through the gate and carries a payroll of $52.47M against $70.41M in revenue. A $9.53M profit is not nothing, but it has not bought a new practice surface or a better training room. You asked whether the building costs you anything on the ice. The Bureau says it costs every man on the roster more than 3 full grades off his overall.

We are a different team on the road and nobody will say why. Is it the travel, or is it an excuse?

Kiyoshi Yamagata, Burnaby

Vancouver Canucks went 28-13 at home and 18-23 on the road. The split is real, a full 10 games between the two halves of the schedule. But the question you asked has a new shape as of this week. VAN opened the first round at home against Edmonton Oilers and lost 3-4 in overtime on April 9, then 2-3 the next night. Two home losses in the postseason, on the building that carried them all season. Their playoff scoring is Markus Naslund91 at 2+2=4 in 2 games and Todd Bertuzzi95 at 0+3=3 in 2. The series moves to Edmonton Oilers on April 13, and if VAN is the same road team the standings say they are, the series ends there. The travel question answers itself in the wrong direction.

Sean Avery77, is he helping this team, or is he just a man who sits down a lot?

Marcus Delacroix, Torrance

Sean Avery took 150 penalty minutes in 82 games, the most in the league. Los Angeles Kings finished 25-37-11 with 79 points and the 11th seed in the West. The record does not carry a line that tells you whether a man in the box helps or hurts. What it does carry: 150 minutes is the equivalent of two full games of five-on-four time, which means two full games of ice a teammate could have had instead. LA is home for the summer. Whether Avery helped is a question the record cannot answer. Whether he sat down a lot, it can.

11 straight losses. Is anybody at this club going to say something, or do we all just watch?

Yolanda Espinosa, Sunrise

Florida Panthers finished 25-36-16 with 76 points and 255 goals against. The eleven-game skid closed the season. Boris Van Ryswyk, the head coach, said on The Tunnel this week: "Not that long a streak. No. We've had rough patches, but eleven, that's, that's something else." He also pointed at the development structure underneath the roster and said it is not the players' fault or the coach's. FLA carries $51.07M in payroll against $72.88M in revenue and 14971 through the gate. The money is there, the room is better than New York Islanders's, and the result on the ice is 76 points and a 19-goal deficit. The record says the problem is not the building.

We have won 4 in a row and I have not read a word about it anywhere. What does this club have to do?

Rafael Villalobos, Tempe

Phoenix Coyotes finished 40-31-9 with 93 points, 241 for and 242 against, and the late-season streak put them in the postseason as the eighth seed in the West. They beat Colorado Avalanche 2-1 in game 1 on the road, lost 3-2 in overtime in game 2, and are tied 1-1 heading into game 3 tonight at PHX. Sean Burke84 is at 1.86 GAA and .926 save percentage through two playoff games. The club carries a payroll of $47.81M at $0.51M per point, the 6th-best rate in the league. Nobody printed the streak because the streak was in March. The streak is now a first-round series, and your readers get to watch it instead.

We have played TOR 5 times and not beaten them once. At what point is that a pattern and not bad luck?

Halina Sobczyk, Cheektowaga

Buffalo Sabres went 0-5 against Toronto Maple Leafs over 5 meetings, with no overtime marker on any of the five losses. BUF finished 27-34-12 with 84 points, 258 for and 298 against, and missed the postseason. TOR finished 59-13-5 with 133 points and a +191 differential, and is now up 2-0 on New York Islanders in the first round. Five meetings, five regulation losses, a 49-point gap across the season. Five is a pattern.
