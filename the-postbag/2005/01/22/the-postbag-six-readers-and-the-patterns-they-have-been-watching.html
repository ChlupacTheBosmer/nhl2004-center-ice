Letters to the editor

The Postbag: six readers and the patterns they have been watching

Three letters ask whether what they see is a pattern. One asks why the page ignored a winning streak. Two ask about a building and a man in the box.

Saturday, 22 January 2005 · edited by Tunde Oyelaran

Four letters this week ask whether something they keep watching is a pattern. Two more ask about a building and a man. The record answers all six.

Four straight losses. I do not ask for a miracle. I ask for somebody at this club to stand up and say something. Is the coaching staff holding meetings? Are the players talking in that room? Or do we all just watch the next one go wrong and call it a season?

Stanislaw Kubicki, Berwyn

Chicago Blackhawks is 12-26-7 with 37 points in 48 games and 179 against. The home record is 8-16. The road record is 7-17, the worst on the road in this league. Jocelyn Thibault90 is 12-27-0 with a 3.53 GAA and a .839 save percentage. Eric Daze87 has 84 points, Alexei Zhamnov85 has 71, and a team that gives up 179 goals is not going to be fixed by two forwards saying something in a room. The profit number is $25.22M. The points number is 37. Both are on the same line in the business table.

We have won 5 in a row and not a word about it on any desk in this league. Five games, five points earned, and Pittsburgh gets a paragraph at the bottom of a scoreboard page. Does this club have to win 15 in a row to get a sentence?

Mirek Hruby, Bethel Park

Pittsburgh Penguins is 15-21-5 with 47 points in 47 games, and yes, the desk sees the 5-game win streak. Here is the rest of it: 6 of their 15 wins have come in overtime. The goal differential is minus 20. They have scored 170 and allowed 190. A club on a 5-game streak with a minus-20 differential is winning the close ones and losing the rest. Mario Lemieux89 has 79 points in 41 games at age 39. The rest says this streak is fragile. At minus 20, the ceiling is where the 190 gets down to the 170 and not before.

We have played Ottawa four times and not beaten them once. Not once. I have sat in this seat and watched the same result four times. At what point do I stop saying bad luck and start saying they are better than us, and who at this club is going to say it first?

Philippe Deslauriers, Verdun

The season series has Montreal Canadiens 0-3-1 against Ottawa Senators over four meetings: three regulation losses and one in overtime. Four games, zero wins. It is a pattern at four. Ottawa is 5-4-2 in division play with 60 goals for and 55 against across the Northeast. The one thing in that MTL room moving in the right direction is Mike Komisarek80, up six on the Bureau's Development Index, but his base is 77 and he is 23 years old. He will not answer a four-game series by himself. You are right to name it.

We are a different team on the road and nobody will say why. Is it the travel, or is it an excuse? I want a real answer.

Ottilie Brandvold, Dearborn

Detroit Red Wings is 18-6 at home and 8-15 away. Eighteen wins in 24 games at Joe Louis Arena, eight in 23 everywhere else. Dominik Hasek85 is 23-17-0 with a 3.33 GAA and a .848 save percentage across 42 starts. The sheet does not carry a travel figure. It carries the 8 and the 15, and the 15 does not need an excuse. It needs a win.

I have asked at this building, the rink, the practice facility, the equipment room. People say it costs us, but nobody puts a number on it. Does the state of this building actually cost us anything on the ice, or is that something supporters tell each other at the bar?

Callum Fotheringay, Edmonton

The Bureau's buildings table carries Edmonton Oilers at −3.65 on every man's overall from its facilities. That is the worst in the league. The number is published and it is not an invention. Tommy Salo87 is 24-14-1 with a 3.29 GAA. Mike Comrie85 has 74 points. Raffi Torres75 is up five on the Development Index off a base of 74. EDM is 21-14-4 with 64 points in 48 games, first in the Northwest Division. So the building is costing something and these players are paying for it and still sitting first. It costs you, and you are winning anyway, and the difference between the two is what you would have been without the −3.65. The record gives you the number. It does not give you the counterfactual.

I am not asking you to hate him. I am asking you to be honest. Is Matthew Barnaby76 helping this team, or is he just a man who sits down a lot and the team pretends he is doing something useful?

Aaron Lindqvist, Yonkers

Matthew Barnaby has 66 penalty minutes in 45 games, the most in the league. He has played 45 of his club's 49 games. That is all the record says about him, and the record is the only thing I print. You asked two questions and I can only answer one of them. Whether he helps is a judgment about what a team does with a man in the box and what the other team does when he is not in it, and I do not have that on the sheet. Whether he sits down a lot is 66 in 45, and he is in the lineup.
