Letters to the editor

The Postbag: the 7-game streak nobody printed, and the building that costs 3.77 a man

Five letters this week, four of them from clubs the rest of the league has not mentioned since October.

Sunday, 6 November 2005 · edited by Tunde Oyelaran

Five letters came in this week. Four are about clubs that have not appeared on this page since the quiet-heater edition on 2005-10-27. The mail is still arriving from the same five cities because nobody else is writing about them.

We have won 7 in a row and I have not read a word about it anywhere. What does this club have to do to get a sentence printed?

Marguerite Oyelowo, Columbus

The Columbus Blue Jackets sit first in the Western Conference with 19 points in 11 games. Their goal differential of +8 (28 scored, 20 allowed) ties New Jersey for the best mark in the league behind Toronto's +22. The 7-game streak is the longest active run in the league; Pittsburgh and Toronto are next at 6.

Pascal Leclaire89 is 6-1 with a 1.37 GAA in 7 starts. That is the lowest GAA among every goaltender in the league with 5 or more games, ahead of Mikael Tellqvist73 at 1.48 and Roberto Luongo95 at 1.61. The Bureau's Development Index has him up 9 from his base of 83. Columbus pays $62.03M in salary for those 19 points, which works out to $3.26M per point, 4th cheapest in the league behind Pittsburgh ($2.72M), Vancouver ($2.73M) and Nashville ($3.02M).

What they have to do is keep doing it.

We are a different team on the road and nobody will say why. Is it the travel, or is it an excuse?

Marguerite Oyelowo, Columbus

The split on the sheet is 7-1 at home and 2-1 on the road. Three road games. One of them was an overtime win at San Jose. A sample of 3 does not carry a verdict about geography.

The buildings machinery does exist at other clubs. Anaheim carries −3.77 on every man's overall from its facilities, the worst in the league. The record gives me that number and gives me nothing next to Columbus. I will not invent one.

5 straight losses. Is anybody at this club going to say something, or do we all just watch?

Stanislaw Kubicki, Berwyn

Chicago Blackhawks is 2-5-3 with 9 points in 11 games. They have allowed 37 goals in those 11 games. The home record reads 1-7. That is the number you are watching.

The Development Index has Igor Radulov80 at +11 form and Steve McCarthy81 at +8, so the young men are working. Jay Pandolfo68 sits at −4, and Steve Sullivan84 is out with an arm through 2005-11-11. The 37 goals against is not a lack of effort. It is a goaltending and structure problem, and the record says so in the column headed GA.

Does the state of this building actually cost us anything on the ice, or is that something people say?

Delphine Okonkwo, Anaheim

It is on the Bureau's page as a number: −3.77 on every man's overall. That is the worst facilities penalty in the league. The number says each player on that roster is graded 3.77 below his true Book grade when he wears the sweater.

Jean-Sebastien Giguere97 posted a 2.17 GAA and a 0.881 save percentage in 10 starts this season. The Book carries him at 95. If the −3.77 is real, the true Giguere is not the one in the crease. Anaheim is 3-6-1 with 11 points, and the next game is tonight against Nashville.

I cannot tell you how many points it costs. The penalty is on the sheet.

We pay more than anybody and I would like somebody to tell me what for.

Aaron Lindqvist, Yonkers

The New York Rangers payroll is $92.95M, the largest in the league. The per-point cost is $4.65M, which places them 8th best: Pittsburgh, Vancouver, Nashville, Columbus, Tampa Bay, Detroit and Carolina all buy a point more cheaply. New York sits 2nd in the Eastern Conference with 20 points in 14 games.

The seat costs $82, the 6th highest price in the league. Attendance is 14,489 a night. The 7-1 home record is doing the work; the 2-4 road record is not. What the money buys is 20 points and a comfortable profit of $16.90M. What it does not buy is a lead in the conference, or a road team that wins.

The streak is the story this week. Leclaire started 7 of 11 games and has not been beaten more than twice in any of them. Columbus earns at least one point on the road at San Jose on 2005-11-07, and the mail will be different then.
