The Postbag: 11-3 on the road, 1-9 away, and a man with 62 penalty minutes

Six clubs the wire left off its page this week: a streak, two patterns, a skid, a split, and the most penalized man in the league

Sunday, 27 November 2005 · edited by Tunde Oyelaran

Six clubs have answers the wire has not printed this week. St. Louis has won 7 straight, two clubs cannot beat the same opponent, one team cannot win on the road, Columbus is on a six-game skid, and the most penalized man in the league draws a question about whether he helps.

We have won 7 in a row and I have not read a word about it anywhere. What does this club have to do to get a sentence?

Verna Kaltenbrunner, Kirkwood

St. Louis Blues sits 14-4-3 with 33 points through 22 games, 66 goals for and 55 against. Their road record reads 11-3, the most road wins any club in the league has. The home record is 4-4. The per-point cost is $2.11M on a $69.76M payroll. They sit 2 points behind Dallas Stars at the top of the Western Conference. Tonight they travel to Nashville Predators, who are 6-0 at home. The season series between STL and NSH reads 5-6 in goals over 2 meetings.

We are a different team on the road and nobody will say why. Is it the travel, or is it an excuse?

Royce Vandermeer, Atlanta

Atlanta Thrashers is 6-4 at home and 1-9 away through 20 games. 45 goals for, 56 against, 16 points. That 1 road win is the fewest in the Eastern Conference. Four men sit on the injured list, tied with Vancouver Canucks for the second-highest count in the league behind New York Islanders at 6. Patrik Stefan78's ankle keeps him out until 2005-12-03. The payroll is $40.34M, the smallest in the league. What the travel room looks like is on the buildings page. The record tells you the roster, the injuries, and what it costs.

We have played DAL 3 times and not beaten them once. At what point is that a pattern and not bad luck?

Rafael Villalobos, Tempe

Phoenix Coyotes has met Dallas Stars 3 times and lost all 3. The season series reads 7-4 in goals, with all three meetings decided by a single goal. Their 13 points in 22 games are the lowest total in the league. Two meetings remain. The next is on 2006-02-08.

We have played PIT 3 times and not beaten them once. At what point is that a pattern and not bad luck?

Isadora Fontaine, Brandon

Pittsburgh Penguins has beaten Tampa Bay Lightning 3 times this season: 1 in regulation, 2 in overtime. 13-9 in goals across 3 meetings, with 2 decided by one goal. Next meeting on 2006-01-17.

6 straight losses. Is anybody at this club going to say something, or do we all just watch?

Marguerite Oyelowo, Columbus

Columbus Blue Jackets is 7-7-3 with 25 points in 21 games, 51 goals for, 48 against. The 6-game slide sits inside a record that keeps them in the middle of the Central division. Pascal Leclaire89 has gone 7-7 with a 2.00 GAA and a 0.904 save percentage. Two centers are on the injured list: Todd Marchant83 (knee, back 2006-01-30) and Joe Juneau71 (hand, back 2005-11-28).

Brent O'Connor told The Tunnel's Marcus Bell after the game on 2005-11-26: "The streak is loud because it's right now, it's this week, and I get that. But I'm looking at the whole ledger and we're in it."

Tie Domi74 — is he helping this team, or is he just a man who sits down a lot?

Delphine Okonkwo, Anaheim

Tie Domi carries 62 penalty minutes in 22 games, the most in the league. Mighty Ducks of Anaheim is 6-12-1 through 22 games, 44 goals for, 54 against, 19 points. The payroll is $54.64M, the per-point cost $2.88M. Tonight Mighty Ducks of Anaheim hosts Phoenix Coyotes, their first meeting of the season.
