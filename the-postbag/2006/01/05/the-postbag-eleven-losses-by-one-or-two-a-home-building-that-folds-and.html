Letters to the editor

The Postbag: eleven losses by one or two, a home building that folds, and the man sitting down more than anyone

Three clubs the wire has not touched in weeks, one penalty total that doubled since November, and a building that is still costing every man on the roster.

Thursday, 5 January 2006 · edited by Tunde Oyelaran

Five letters this edition. Three come from clubs the wire has left alone since late November: Edmonton Oilers, San Jose Sharks, Detroit Red Wings. One comes from the same Anaheim correspondent who asked about the building in November and now wants to know about the man in front of it. The patterns were printed in October. The readers arrived in January.

We have played DAL 3 times and not beaten them once. At what point is that a pattern and not bad luck?

Callum Fotheringay, Edmonton

The record says three games, three one-goal margins, three Dallas Stars wins. On October 15 you lost 2-3 in overtime on the road. On October 28 you lost 2-3 at home. On December 31 you lost 1-2 on the road. Five goals scored, eight allowed. The Edmonton Oilers goaltender, Tommy Salo88, carries a 2.32 GAA and a 0.883 save percentage across 31 games. Marty Turco95 answers with 1.77 and 0.908 in 32. That gap shows up in the margins you keep losing.

Dallas Stars is 23-9-6, 58 points, 14-10 on the road. They have beaten Mighty Ducks of Anaheim to a split and Phoenix Coyotes 3-0 in the Pacific. You are not unlucky against them. You are outmanned at the crease.

11 straight losses. Is anybody at this club going to say something, or do we all just watch?

Priya Ramanathan, Campbell

I went through all eleven games. Here is the shape of the damage. You lost 1-3 to Tampa Bay Lightning on January 4, 2-3 to Chicago Blackhawks on January 2, 3-4 in overtime at Detroit Red Wings on December 31, 2-3 to Columbus Blue Jackets on December 29, 2-3 at Columbus Blue Jackets on December 28, 1-3 to Colorado Avalanche on December 26, 3-4 in overtime to Los Angeles Kings on December 23, 2-3 at Phoenix Coyotes on December 20, 3-4 in overtime at Mighty Ducks of Anaheim on December 18, 3-4 at Los Angeles Kings on December 17, 3-4 in overtime to Atlanta Thrashers on December 14. Eight of the eleven were decided by exactly one goal. Four went to overtime. You scored three goals and lost five times.

The problem is not that you cannot play anybody. The problem is the Honda Center: 3 wins, 16 losses, the worst home record in the league. You are 9-11 on the road. The San Jose Sharks payroll is $66.24M, the fifth-largest in the league, and it buys 32 points. That is $2.07M per point, the worst conversion rate in the league. Evgeni Nabokov87 has a 2.79 GAA and a 0.859 save percentage in 31 games. No goaltender in this league has allowed a higher percentage on the shots he has faced.

San Jose Sharks plays at Nashville Predators on January 7. You went 0-2 against them already. You scored 3 and allowed 7.

We are a different team on the road and nobody will say why. Is it the travel, or is it an excuse?

Ottilie Brandvold, Dearborn

The numbers are not vague. At home Detroit Red Wings has played 22 games, scored 62 and allowed 50, good for a 15-7 record. On the road: 18 games, 36 scored, 44 allowed, a 6-11 record. The travel room sits in the buildings table, and Detroit Red Wings pays for it, but let me put the division record beside it first. You are 2-1-6 in the Central. Against Nashville Predators: 0-3. Against St. Louis Blues: 0-1 with one overtime. Against Columbus Blue Jackets: 1-2. Against Chicago Blackhawks: 1-0. You are 43 points, 8th in the West, one game from the line. Your last three games were wins. The road is where you need them.

Tie Domi74 -- is he helping this team, or is he just a man who sits down a lot?

Delphine Okonkwo, Anaheim

122 penalty minutes in 39 games, the most in the league. When I printed a man at 62 in late November, he had already sat down a good deal of that total. He has done it again since, doubling the number in five weeks.

Mighty Ducks of Anaheim is 11-16-4, 42 points, on a 6-game losing streak. Your top scorer, Vyacheslav Kozlov78 with 11 goals and 38 assists, has a knee and is out until January 24. You are 8-13 on the road. The penalty box is one of a dozen things costing you points.

Does the state of this building actually cost us anything on the ice, or is that something people say?

Delphine Okonkwo, Anaheim

I printed the number in November: the Bureau's facilities index puts -3.77 on every man's overall at Anaheim, the worst in the league. Six weeks later the number has not moved and the losing streak has. You are 8-13 on the road and 11-7 at home, and the road games are where a depleted lineup hurts most. Jean-Sebastien Giguere97 has a 2.21 GAA and a 0.883 save percentage, the same Vezina-caliber numbers he posted last year. He cannot fix the rooms around him. Your 6-game slide has cost you two points below the playoff line. The building costs you goals every night. It did not cause this.
