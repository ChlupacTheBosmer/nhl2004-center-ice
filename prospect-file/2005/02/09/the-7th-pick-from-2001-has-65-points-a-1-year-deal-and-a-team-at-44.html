The 7th Pick From 2001 Has 65 Points, a 1-Year Deal, and a Team at 44

Mike Komisarek leads all defensemen under 24 in scoring and plays for the last-place team in the Northeast on $1.10M with one year left

Pete LarocqueScouting editor · The Prospect FileWednesday, 9 February 2005
ShareCopy link

Mike Komisarek86 has 65 points in 56 games. He is 23 years old, plays for a team with 44 points, and carries a contract with one year left at $1.10M. The trade deadline is 38 days away.

Mike Komisarek was the 7th pick in 2001. The Montreal Canadiens took a defenseman from the University of Michigan who had 46 points in 80 college games and a CCHA title on his resume. Four years later he leads every defenseman in this league age 23 and under in scoring. The next one on the list is Rostislav Klesla87 at Toronto Maple Leafs, 23 points in 55 games.

What the Development Index says: he is up six. The Book has his base at 77, his overall reads 74 today, his potential sits at 90. The riser list carries him alongside Marian Gaborik99 and Mike Cammalleri81.

The 2001 first round, four years in

Among every first-rounder from 2001 who still plays in this league, one man has more points than Komisarek. That man is Ilya Kovalchuk91, who leads the league at 93 points in 53 games and was the 1st pick.

The body and the numbers

Komisarek stands 6'5", 225 pounds, shoots right. His check grade is 84. His speed is 81. Aggression reads 80, puck handling 80, shot power 79, passing 77. These are heavy grades for a man who plays 29.4 minutes a night and skates like that.

His fight grade is 51. His faceoff grade is 52. He doesn't win draws and he doesn't drop the gloves. He has 10 goals and 55 assists in 56 games. The 55 assists fit a 77 passing grade and a 79 shot power used mostly to feed wingers.

The contract and what comes next

One year. $1.10M. He turns 24 in January, which puts his next restricted-free-agency window at 24.

Montreal sits at 15 wins, 32 losses, 4 overtime losses, 44 points. The Canadiens score 178 goals and allow 222. They are 13-14 at home and 7-22 on the road. A top defenseman playing 29.4 minutes on a 44-point team at $1.10M will show up in general managers' spreadsheets over the next 38 days.

Saku Koivu86 leads the club in scoring with 103 points. Richard Zednik86 has 114. Komisarek sits third at 65. A top-3 scorer at 65 points on a one-year, $1.10M deal with a 44-point team.

The road to here

Komisarek grew up on Long Island, played varsity at St. Anthony's in Suffolk County, then went to USA Hockey's National Team Development Program before two seasons at the University of Michigan. He was named a First Team Division I All-American in 2002. Montreal signed him on July 24, 2002. He played 24 games in 2003-04, 3 points, 11.8 minutes a night. This season he plays 29.4.

He went from 3 points in 24 games to 65 in 56 in one offseason. The Index tracks that. The Book noticed too: his overall moved from 77 at base to 74 now, and the +6 form means the Bureau sees him climbing.

I saw him at Michigan, CCHA tournament, where he cleared a forward off the boards and then made a tape-to-tape pass from his own blue line. The hands were always there. The 84 check told you the rest was coming.
