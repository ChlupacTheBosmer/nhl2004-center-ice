The 212th Pick Just Came Back From a Head Injury and Scores at 1.39 a Night on $0.40M

Radim Vrbata's contract expires in 1 year. Carolina pays $0.0045M per point for a 23-year-old at 1.39 a game.

Pete LarocqueScouting editor · The Prospect FileSaturday, 12 February 2005
ShareCopy link

Radim Vrbata79 returned to the Carolina Hurricanes lineup on February 10 after a head injury that kept him out 5 weeks. He has 53 points in 38 games this season, a 1.39 pace that would produce 114 points over 82 games. He is 23 years old, on $0.40M with 1 year left on the deal, and he was the 212th pick of the 1999 draft.

The 8th round is where the draft stops being a scouting exercise and starts being a numbers game. You run the list until you run out of roster spots. Carolina picked Vrbata 212th, and 6 seasons later he is the highest-scoring player from that class's late rounds still in the league this season. The Book has his potential at 82. His base grade sits at 81. The 1-point gap between base and potential is the narrowest margin of upside on the Development Index for any player under 24 with 50 or more points this season.

The Bureau sees a player who has almost developed. At 1.39 a night on 20.9 minutes, he is performing at what his 81 base suggests he should be capable of, and the Index puts him right at base (form 0). He is not overperforming the Book. He is scoring the way the Book already rated him capable of, and he is doing it on $0.40M.

His $0.40M bought 89 points last season, $0.0045M per point. This season at 53 points in 38 games, the cost sits at $0.0075M per point. Both figures come from a salary that expires in 1 year.

Where he ranks among late-rounders

The 212th pick sits in a line with some good ones. Richard Zednik went 249th in the 9th round and scored 84 points in 50 games for Montreal Canadiens this season, a 1.68 pace. Peter Bondra, 156th in the 6th, has 80 points in 52 games for Washington Capitals at 1.54. Steve Sullivan, 233rd in the 8th, has 105 points in 74 games for Chicago Blackhawks at 1.42. Vrbata's current 1.39 ties Zednik's full-season 1.39 from last year (82 games, 114 points). Zednik did it over 82 games. Vrbata is matching that pace across 38 with a head injury in the middle of it.

The contract window

One year at $0.40M. Free agency closes what the calendar has as July 28, which is 166 days away. If he stays at 1.39 for the rest of this season he finishes with 114 points. A player who scores 114 points does not sign another deal at $0.40M. Carolina knows this. The Hurricanes sit at 19-14-12, 72 points, third in the Southeast. They are in the playoff race with their cheapest good player about to leave.

His shot power sits at 87 and his puck handling at 83. Those are the numbers of a winger who creates offense from the slot and protects the puck in traffic. His speed is 79. His 2 PIM in 38 games tells you he does not go to the dirty areas to make things happen. He scores from the slot, on 20.9 minutes a night, without taking a hit to do it.

The head injury is the only question mark left. The onset falls between January 1 and January 4. The return date of February 10 is firm. He missed roughly 15 games at a stretch where Carolina needed offense. The Book calls him a player who is nearly done growing. The ice says 1.39 a night, on a deal that expires, at a price no team can match. The 212th pick just came off a head injury and he is scoring like a top-4 winger who forgot to read his own draft position.
