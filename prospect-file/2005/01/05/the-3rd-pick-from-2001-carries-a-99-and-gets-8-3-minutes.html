The 3rd Pick From 2001 Carries a 99 and Gets 8.3 Minutes

Alexander Svitov's potential figure ties the highest number in the league among under-23s. The coach gives him 8.3 minutes a night and the season line reads 2 points in 27 games.

Pete LarocqueScouting editor · The Prospect FileWednesday, 5 January 2005
ShareCopy link

Alexander Svitov67 has a potential figure of 99. That ties Rick DiPietro84 for the top spot among every under-23 in the league. Svitov averages 8.3 minutes a night for Tampa Bay Lightning.

The 3rd pick from the 2001 draft is 22 years old. He has 1 goal, 1 assist, 2 points and 20 penalty minutes in 27 games this season. His overall sits at 67, which puts him below 10 of the 14 first-rounders from his own draft class who are still on league rosters.

I saw him the summer he was taken. Everyone did. The kid came out of Omsk at 6'3", 198 pounds, and Tampa Bay took him 3rd overall. Two years later he had 8 points in 63 games. Then a midseason trade sent him to Columbus Blue Jackets for Darryl Sydor. He's back in Tampa now, second season, same production problem, smaller role.

The 99 means something different here

Svitov's 99 puts him in a group with three other under-23s: DiPietro (99, 52.4 minutes in New York Islanders), Steve Eminger76 (99, 16.1 minutes in Toronto Maple Leafs), and Scottie Upshall78 (99, 12.2 minutes in Toronto Maple Leafs). Svitov gets 8.3 minutes. That is the lowest ice time of any player carrying a 99.

His grade sheet is the odd part. The strongest numbers: speed 79, balance 77, toughness 77, acceleration 76, agility 76, aggression 74. Those are the tools of a top-six center. The weakest: endurance 50, fighting 50, accuracy 52. The 50 endurance is the one that bothers me. A center who gasses out after two shifts is a bottom-six center, and a bottom-six center does not develop at 8.3 minutes a night.

The 2001 draft, six months after the 49th pick

Yesterday this desk noted that Mike Cammalleri83, the 49th pick of the 2001 draft, carries an 83 overall that outrates 11 of that draft's first-rounders. The gap between the 49th pick and the 3rd is 12 points of overall. Cammalleri gets 20.2 minutes and has 19 points in 17 games. Svitov gets 8.3 minutes and has 2 points in 27.

Two years and one million dollars

The contract reads $1.00M with 2 years left. Tampa Bay holds a 99-potential center at one million dollars. Vincent Lecavalier84 carries 84 overall and the first-center job. The Lightning have 53 points and sit second in the Southeast. They are not rebuilding, which means there is no organizational patience for a project.

The Development Index does not list Svitov. He is not on the risers and he is not on the fallers. That means the Index reads his current form at his base.

The 99 is a likelihood of development, not a ceiling. But a 99 likelihood sitting at 67 overall in year two, getting 8.3 minutes, tells you the development is not happening in this lineup. The question Tampa Bay will face in 2005-06 is whether a 99 potential on a cheap deal is worth a roster spot that Lecavalier's backups and the bottom-six forwards need more than Svitov needs minutes.

I have the 3rd pick on my board because of the tools. I keep him there because of the price tag. At one million dollars, a team can wait one more season for the 99 to show up in the point column. At two million, they can't.
