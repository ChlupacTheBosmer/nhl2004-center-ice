Atlanta's 138-Point Player Is a Free Agent. He Costs $1.10M.

Ilya Kovalchuk won the Lady Byng at 22, scored 138 points, and has zero years left on a contract built for a bottom-six winger.

Pete LarocqueScouting editor · The Prospect FileTuesday, 31 May 2005
ShareCopy link

Ilya Kovalchuk91 has 138 points, the Lady Byng, an 89 base grade in the Book, and 0 years left on a contract that pays him $1.10M. He is 22. The draft is 8 days away. Free agency closes in 58 days. Nobody in Atlanta Thrashers has answered: does he stay?

Kovalchuk finished this season with 63 goals and 75 assists in 79 games at 20.0 minutes a night. He scored 134 last year in 82 games. The Development Index has him +5 above his base. His potential is 85, which means the Bureau still believes there is a floor between him and the men above him, but the gap between 89 overall and 85 potential is the narrowest in the top 40.

His age and his linemate are what make this a prospect story. Dany Heatley90 is 89 overall, one point below Kovalchuk in the Book, and they are the top two scorers on a team that won 31 games. Heatley had 131 points this season (65 goals, 66 assists). No other young core in the league matches what those two put up, and neither man is older than 23.

The problem is the roster around them. Atlanta Thrashers scored 274 goals this season. Kovalchuk scored 63 and Heatley scored 65 between them, which leaves the rest of the lineup carrying almost nothing. The next-highest scorer is Sami Kapanen81 at 104 points, a 22-year-old on a declining trajectory (overall 80, form not among the top 40 under-23s). Behind Kapanen, the drop is steep: Vyacheslav Kozlov78 at 91 points carries a 77 overall. Patrik Stefan79, the center, has 47 points and an overall of 78 at 23.

Atlanta carries $37.20M against the cap and generated $70.29M in revenue. The math is not tight. Kovalchuk at $1.10M with 138 points is a market problem. Atlanta Thrashers has 58 days to find out whether he will stay at $1.10M or look around the league for a contender.

Kovalchuk finished the season 31-37-6 in the Southeast division, 84 points, outside the playoff field. He has never been in a playoff series. The Lady Byng is for sportsmanship and production. Atlanta's prestige is 547 in the league's measure. The building seats 14,430 at $78 a ticket.

The 2001 draft gave Atlanta Thrashers a franchise player at #1. They drafted #3, #5 and #6 in the four years since and none of those picks appear on the under-23 list the Bureau will write home about. Their draft capital going into June: 5 picks, 1 first-rounder. They will not fix the roster around Kovalchuk in one draft.

A 22-year-old with 138 points and a 5-point gap between his current grade and his potential is rare. A 22-year-old in that situation with no contract is rarer. Atlanta Thrashers has 58 days.
