The Rental Market: LA and Chicago Are the Deadline's Supply Side

Eight expiring contracts, 2 clubs with nothing to play for, and the deepest pool of available talent in the league

E.J. "Ears" CallahanInsider · The Hot StoveSaturday, 4 December 2004
ShareCopy link

The phone hasn't stopped since the league filed the CGY-to-NJ swap on Tuesday, confirmed by the league record. Four players, no picks, small potatoes. The real supply is somewhere else, and it's bigger than Atlanta.

Start in Hollywood. Los Angeles Kings sits at 23 points, dead last in the Pacific, and carries 4 names from the contract-year class. Jason Allison87 — 37 points in 24 games at $6.70M — is the crown jewel and the albatross at once. A man who would know says Allison's morale of 98 means the club isn't forcing him out the door. But a center who doesn't want to leave is still a rental on someone else's timeline, and $6.70M buys a lot of draft capital if the price is right.

Roman Cechmanek91 is 91 overall on a one-year, $3.30M deal. Thirty-three years old, 21 starts, and Los Angeles Kings has no business in a goaltending arms race. The kind of backup that wins series for someone else.

Adam Deadmarsh87 adds a top-six right wing who's out until 2005-01-21, but his $2.80M is expiring and he only played 12 games this season, collecting 11 points. A late-February proposition, not a January one. The quietest name is Mike Cammalleri83: 22, $1.10M, out until 2004-12-11, 7 points in 7 games. A pennies-contract-year wing is exactly what a contender wants at the deadline.

Now cross the country. Chicago Blackhawks has 21 points, 6-11-5, and the same problem with a different roster. Eric Daze85 has 45 points in 24 games at $3.00M. Forty-five. If Chicago Blackhawks keeps him, they're wasting a first-line scorer on a club that will miss the postseason for the third straight year.

Alexei Zhamnov83 is 34, but 44 points at $4.20M is still a center of the first line. Steve Sullivan83 tacks on 37 points at $3.00M on the right wing. And Jocelyn Thibault88 — 90 overall, $2.80M, 20 starts — is the backup every contender is missing.

Add the 2 kids in Atlanta — Ilya Kovalchuk86 at 39 points and $1.10M, Dany Heatley84 at 34 points and $1.10M — and you have the 3 clubs that will define the next 2 months. The Thrashers are the loudest story. The real supply is in Los Angeles and Chicago.

League sources say at least 1 of these clubs will make a move by mid-January. The ones holding the picks should be holding their phones.
