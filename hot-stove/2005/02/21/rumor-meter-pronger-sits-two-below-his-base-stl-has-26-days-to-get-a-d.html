RUMOR METER: Pronger Sits Two Below His Base. STL Has 26 Days to Get a Defenseman's Price. 🔥🔥

The Blues' captain carries the biggest salary on the contract-year market. His grade has been sliding since October. The deadline is March 19.

E.J. "Ears" CallahanInsider · The Hot StoveMonday, 21 February 2005
ShareCopy link

The Bureau's Development Index has Chris Pronger80 two points below his base of 90. He prints an 86 overall on St. Louis Blues's blue line, 60 games in, 35 points, 32.9 minutes a night. Two months ago this desk wrote that he would not move because he loved the room. His grade has not held still since then.

St. Louis Blues sit 20-27-5, 61 points, third in the Central. Nashville Predators is 20 points ahead. Detroit Red Wings is 6 points ahead on a W1 streak. The Blues are neither tanking nor climbing. They are carrying $9.50M at the position that decides playoff series, on a club that will miss the dance.

The January read was morale. Pronger's sits at 100, the same reading in the league's highest bracket. The room is fine. The city is fine. That read was correct then. It matters less now, because the market prices a defenseman's grade, not his feelings, and his grade is walking toward the deadline at 86.

What the calendar has as the trade deadline sits 26 days out. A club shopping a defenseman at 86 collects a different package than one that moved him at 90 in October. The timing problem for St. Louis Blues is simple: the return today probably beats the return in June, when the captain walks and they collect nothing.

The shelf is deeper than one name

St. Louis Blues carry $9.97M in non-Pronger expirings on top of the captain's deal. Pavol Demitra79 at $3.80M and 85 overall sits second on that shelf, also at 100 morale, also unsigned past this year. The Blues hold 5 draft picks, 1 first rounder. Not a stockpile. Not a club that has committed to the slow road either.

The business case: St. Louis Blues cleared $11.12M in profit at a payroll of $56.63M. They do not need to cut salary to stay solvent. They need to decide whether the product they are selling at 61 points justifies carrying the biggest contract among all players with one year left.

The flame: Pronger's morale has not budged since this desk flagged it in January. The desk called him unmoved, and he has been unmoved since. But a man who is happy in a room going nowhere is a man whose club can move without his objection. The buyers are watching the Index, not the mood.
