RUMOR METER: 3-for-3 Again. NSH, MTL and SJ Land Their February 9 Signings on IR. 🔥🔥

The streak hits 68. The cheapest man on the run costs $21,990, plays for the league's second-happiest room, and is out until February 19.

E.J. "Ears" CallahanInsider · The Hot StoveSunday, 13 February 2005
ShareCopy link

Three clubs signed a player on February 9. Every one of them is on the injured list as of today. The run that the desk called dead twice is at 68.

Rem Murray74 to Nashville Predators for $21,990. Arm, out until February 19. Mike Ricci75 to Montreal Canadiens for $77,110. Groin, out until March 20. Marcel Hossa74 to San Jose Sharks for $241,300. Elbow, out until March 8.

The pattern used to favor high-dollar names. Big contracts, long seasons, accumulated wear. These three contracts total $340,400 between them. The pattern doesn't care about the money. It cares that a pen touched paper and a body reported.

The likeliest club in the league to survive, and it didn't

Nashville Predators sits first in the Central at 75 points, on a three-game win streak, with a club-average morale of 95.3, second only to Vancouver. Their profit margin of $18.33M on a $46.77M payroll is the kind of number that makes a GM sleep. The Predators signed a depth winger for $21,990, the lowest salary on the entire 68-man run, and he went down within four days of the signing.

Nashville has no excuse left. No payroll squeeze, no locker-room rot, no travel budget cutting corners. The Predators did the thing every contender does in February: call up a body from the press-box list, sign him for the minimum, hope he's a fourth-liner. The league said no.

The other two clubs fit the pattern's usual shape. Montreal is last in the Northeast at 46 points and 15-33-4. San Jose is last in the Pacific at 51 points, on a five-game slide, and Marcel Hossa's elbow keeps him past the trade deadline, whatever the calendar has as that date.

What this says about the remaining 34 days

What the calendar has as the trade deadline falls March 19. Every club in this league will sign at least one body between now and then: call-ups, tryout deals, emergency depth. The pattern says they'll all land.

The desk scored the streak at 57 in a piece that read "Shawn Thornton Survives." Shawn Thornton's hand went on the IR. The streak hit 65 the next edition. Nobody in this league is immune.

The only way to break the run is for a club with nothing to lose to stop signing depth entirely, which means accepting they have no fourth line, which means the last 34 games get a lot shorter. Nobody's done that. Nobody's braver than a GM who needs a center for one night and finds one on the street.
