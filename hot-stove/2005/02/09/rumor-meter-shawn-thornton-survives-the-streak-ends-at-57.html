RUMOR METER: Shawn Thornton Survives. The Streak Ends at 57. 🔥🔥🔥

Five of six men signed February 4-5 land on IR. The Chicago enforcer is the first man in the run who did not.

E.J. "Ears" CallahanInsider · The Hot StoveWednesday, 9 February 2005
ShareCopy link

The piece

The pattern reaches 57. Five of the six men signed between February 4 and February 5 are on the injured list. The sixth, Shawn Thornton in Chicago, is not.

The desk has tracked every league signing since the January window opened. The count reached 52 on February 7, when Marian Hossa83 went to the Ottawa Senators injured list within 4 days of his deal. The next batch filed on February 4 and 5 produced six more names. Five landed. One survived.

Poapst's hand keeps him out past the trade deadline window. Belanger's ankle keeps him out through March 4. The other three return inside two weeks, but every one of them cost their club at least a week of whatever depth they were brought in to provide.

Chicago took two very different deals that day. Thornton at $549,650, Poapst at $430,300. One stayed standing. The other broke a hand within the three-day window the league logged between February 7 and February 9. The Chicago Blackhawks now carry 3 men on IR with Poapst's addition.

The clubs in play

Washington Capitals signed Pilar and lost him to a hip on a return date of February 18, one day after his onset window opened. The Capitals hold 69 points and sit 3rd in the Southeast with 2 men out. A defenseman at 75 overall who misses a week is not catastrophic.

Calgary Flames sent a $231,950 deal to Leopold and got a rib injury back. Leopold's overall sits at 71. He was depth. He is now not depth.

Mighty Ducks of Anaheim gave Gosselin $493,790. He played zero games before the ankle landed him on the list with a February 21 return. ANA now carries 3 men out at 65 points and L2.

Los Angeles Kings took Belanger at $257,220, a center at 78 overall. He is out until March 4. LA sits at 54 points, W3, and holds 5 expiring contracts the desk flagged in January. The club that needs bodies in February is the one that just lost one to its own transaction.

What the pattern says about March

The trade deadline the calendar has as March 19 sits 38 days away. Every club that shops will sign replacements. The desk has logged 57 signings who landed on IR since January.

If the next window produces four more signings and the pattern holds, the count reaches 61. If it goes perfect again, that is 6 of 6 and the streak resumes from 52.

The pattern has not missed a single club since January 28. Chicago escaped one of two. The desk expects the count to climb again before the deadline. League sources in three front offices describe the same internal conversation: sign a body, hope he holds, move on if he doesn't.
