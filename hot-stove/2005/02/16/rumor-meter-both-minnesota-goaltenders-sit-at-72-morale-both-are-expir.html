RUMOR METER: Both Minnesota Goaltenders Sit at 72 Morale. Both Are Expiring. 🔥🔥

The league's cheapest club runs its most profitable business. Its two netminders are among the 15 unhappiest men in hockey. Minnesota has 52 points and 31 days.

E.J. "Ears" CallahanInsider · The Hot StoveWednesday, 16 February 2005
ShareCopy link

Minnesota Wild holds the league's lowest payroll at $29.97M and its highest profit at $37.86M. It also holds two goaltenders at 72 on the Bureau's Morale Scale, both on contracts with 0 years remaining. Minnesota sits at 52 points. Vancouver Canucks and Edmonton Oilers hold 75 in the Northwest. The deadline is 31 days away, and both men are on the most rentable shelf in hockey.

Manny Fernandez90 started 50 games at 81 overall on a $1.50M expiring deal. Dwayne Roloson85 has 14 starts at 80 overall on $0.60M, also expiring. Both appear on the Bureau's lowest-15 morale list. No other club carries two goaltenders there.

The business works. The room does not.

The per-point spend at MIN is $0.58M, tied with New Jersey Devils for the league's best rate. Attendance is 13,547 at $80 a ticket. The club's average morale is 87.9, the 4th-lowest of 30. Prestige points are 590, 3rd behind Nashville Predators and NJ.

Top-3 prestige. 4th-lowest morale. Lowest payroll. Highest profit. The two men in net, both at the Bureau's lowest-15 morale mark, are expiring in July.

Nobody has rung this shelf yet

This desk flagged Los Angeles Kings's Roman Cechmanek90 in January as the rentable goaltender circling every Pacific buyer. Cechmanek sits at 78 morale on $3.30M. Fernandez is cheaper, lower on the Morale Scale, and a 50-game starter. Roloson is a $0.60M backup on the same shelf.

League sources say clubs are calling. At least one buyer needs a bridge goaltender through a playoff run. Another needs a starter it can plug in for 2005-06 without touching its cap situation.

MIN holds 5 picks including 1 first-rounder. It has 31 days. Two men at 72 morale are not waiting for July.
