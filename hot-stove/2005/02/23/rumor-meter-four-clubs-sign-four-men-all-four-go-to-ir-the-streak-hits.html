RUMOR METER: Four Clubs Sign Four Men. All Four Go to IR. The Streak Hits 72. 🔥🔥

DAL, PIT, CHI and PHX add bodies on February 18-19. Three of the four sit out past what the calendar has as the trade deadline.

E.J. "Ears" CallahanInsider · The Hot StoveWednesday, 23 February 2005
ShareCopy link

Four signings, four injured lists, no survivors. Chicago Blackhawks, Pittsburgh Penguins, Dallas Stars and Phoenix Coyotes each put pen to paper between February 18 and 19, and all four new arrivals are on the IR with return dates that, in three cases, land past the trade deadline on March 19. The run reaches 72.

Pittsburgh Penguins holds 6 picks and 1 first-rounder, including Toronto Maple Leafs's 3rd this year. They signed Guillaume Lefebvre, a 72-overall left winger, on February 18. He returns May 15, which means he won't play a shift in a Pittsburgh Penguins sweater this season. The club sits at 58 points, $44.95M in payroll, $0.78M per point. They are hoarding draft capital and spending it on bodies who break on arrival.

Dallas Stars has 80 points and is fighting for the second wild-card spot in the West. Philippe Boucher was depth. He is out until April 5. The Stars get nothing from a $149,550 signing, not one game before the deadline.

Chicago Blackhawks is the one with a story. Kyle Calder cost $5,440 and won't be back until March 21, two days past what the calendar has as the trade deadline. The club has 51 points, the third-lowest total in the league. Their payroll is $39.27M, the third-cheapest. They are $0.77M per point.

GM Jason Hughes told The Tunnel's Jan Kowalczyk on February 16 that the club is listening on Eric Daze87, who carries 99 points in 61 games at $3.00M with a contract that expires this summer. "He's playing at a level right now, 96 points, the highest he's ever been," Hughes said, before adding: "If somebody puts something in front of me..." The sentence went unfinished.

Daze sits at 91 on the morale scale. So does Thibault. The club average is 90.6, twelfth in the league. The room is not unhappy. The table is the problem.

Phoenix Coyotes is the only club that gets a body back before the deadline. Brian Savage returns February 26, three days from now. He cost $164,230 and missed 2 games. The Coyotes are at 60 points with $47.81M in payroll, and they hold 4 picks, the fewest in the league. They also hold New York Rangers's 4th-round choice, while the Rangers hold their 2nd and 3rd. The pick math says Phoenix is building. The Savage signing says Phoenix is trying to stay alive until March 19.

The streak has now hit 72 consecutive signings that landed on the injured list. Three of the four new arrivals will cost their clubs a full season of nothing. The desk will be watching for number 73.
