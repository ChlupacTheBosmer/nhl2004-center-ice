RUMOR METER: Ottawa Signs Hossa on February 3, Sends Him to IR Within 4 Days. The Pattern Reaches 52. 🔥🔥

The back injury keeps him out 10 days past what the calendar has as the trade deadline. The Senators get zero this season.

E.J. "Ears" CallahanInsider · The Hot StoveMonday, 7 February 2005
ShareCopy link

Marian Hossa86 signed with Ottawa Senators on February 3 at $341,600. The league's injured list places him out with a back injury, onset between February 6 and February 7, return March 29. The pattern that started in December reaches 52 instances. No club has signed a player since January 28 and escaped with a healthy body. The window between signing and injury is now as short as 3 days.

Hossa's return date puts him past what the calendar has as the trade deadline by 10 days. The deadline falls March 19. Hossa does not come back until March 29. Ottawa spent $341,600 on a player with an 83 overall grade who won't be available to trade and won't factor into any playoff push the Senators are 34 points back of making.

The Senators sit at 19-25-6, 52 points, in the Northeast. Their club morale average is 88.7, sixth-lowest in the league. They own 5 draft picks. Their payroll is $42.80M. None of that changes because of a signing that lasted between 3 and 4 days before the training staff logged him out.

The pattern started in December. The desk logged 33 instances by January 22, 44 by January 30, 51 by February 6. Hossa is 52. Seven clubs signed between January 28 and February 2 and all seven landed their man on IR. Ottawa is the eighth club in that stretch, and the window from signature to the trainer's report keeps shrinking.

The pattern hasn't answered whether this is systemic or a run that has gone too long to call a fluke. 52 consecutive signings ending on the injured list is not a small sample. No league source has offered an explanation. No club has publicly acknowledged the run. The desk asked around. Nobody in the Meadowlands, nobody in Ottawa's front office, nobody at any of the 15 clubs caught in this since December would say the words "injury prevention" without laughing.

The $341,600 is a minimum. That sets it apart from the Joe Thornton91 situation in Boston or the Michael Rupp72 signing in New Jersey. Ottawa took a flyer on a young man with an 83 overall and lost him before he suited up. No one got hurt playing. He arrived and went out.

The Senators hold 5 picks and $42.80M in payroll against 52 points. They are not buyers at what the calendar has as the trade deadline. They are sellers who cannot sell the one asset they just added.

The desk has been wrong before on this pattern. Deadmarsh was supposed to be traded by January 21 and he wasn't. Columbus was supposed to shed Denis, Wright, or Spacek by February 1 and all three remain. The desk owns those misses. It will not own a miss on this one: the pattern is what the league's own records show, and the records say 52.
