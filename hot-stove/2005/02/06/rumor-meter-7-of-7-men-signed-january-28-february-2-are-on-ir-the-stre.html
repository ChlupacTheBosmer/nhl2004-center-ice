RUMOR METER: 7 of 7 Men Signed January 28-February 2 Are on IR. The Streak Hits 51. 🔥🔥🔥

No club signed a player in the past ten days and escaped with a healthy body. Seven clubs, seven signings, zero survivors.

E.J. "Ears" CallahanInsider · The Hot StoveSunday, 6 February 2005
ShareCopy link

Seven signings were recorded between January 28 and February 2. Every single one is on the injured list. The pattern the desk has tracked since December reaches 51 instances, and this window is the first where no front office escaped.

Seven clubs. One signing each. A perfect sweep.

Detroit pays $80.89M and signs a rookie to a $24,860 contract. He goes straight to IR.

Pavel Datsyuk76 carries a 76 overall and an ankle that keeps him out until March 13. The Detroit Red Wings sit at 63 points with the highest payroll in the league at $80.89M, and they now have a man on their books who hasn't dressed. Michael Zigomanis75, 74 overall, is already out with a back until March 13. That's Detroit's second signing-injury match in this stretch.

Nashville Predators moved for Greg Johnson82, 82 overall, on February 2. His ankle lands him on the shelf with a February 11 return, the earliest in this batch. Nashville, at 71 points and a 93.4 club-average morale, can absorb three weeks from a depth center. Turner Stevenson72 to New Jersey Devils is worse timing. He was signed February 2 and his knee was already recorded before the ink dried. Stevenson returns February 22, meaning New Jersey Devils will have gotten perhaps two or three games of him between signing and the next trade window.

This is no longer a coincidence at any club

Boston Bruins signed Sean O&#x27;Donnell73 on February 2 and he landed on IR with a wrist, out until March 19. That joins Joe Thornton91, 91 overall, a hand, back March 5, and Jonathan Girard77, a neck, out until April 7. Boston carries 56 points, $43.97M in payroll, and the 3rd-highest signing-injury count in this stretch. At 92.0 club morale, they're 16th in the league. Two more days of this and nobody in the building can pretend the calendar is a coincidence.

The trade deadline is 41 days out. Every club buying at the deadline is signing a man whose medical file the desk has been watching turn red. The pattern at 51 instances says that man is a good bet to hit the shelf. The pattern at 7-for-7 in this window says he's more than a good bet.
