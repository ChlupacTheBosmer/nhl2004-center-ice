CONFIRMED vs. DENIED: I Said San Jose Would Trade Brad Stuart. I Was Wrong.

The deadline passed with him in green and black. Now the pool is empty, the draft is 18 days away, and the top-pairing defenseman market is two players deep.

E.J. "Ears" CallahanInsider · The Hot StoveSaturday, 21 May 2005
ShareCopy link

I called it on March 14. The piece said San Jose Sharks would move Brad Stuart80 before the trade deadline on March 19. The deadline came and went. Stuart finished the season with the Sharks: 77 games, 52 points, 87 overall. I got it wrong. I'm scoring it against myself because the lesson matters more than the embarrassment.

The pool is not thin. The pool is empty.

The League Scouting Bureau's Contract Ledger carries zero unsigned players in its top 25 by overall. Every team that loses a contract-year player this summer has to replace him by trade, not by signing. That is the market Stuart is walking into, and it changes the math on his price.

The Contract Ledger lists two defensemen among the top 30 contract-year names. Stuart, 25, $1.80M, 87 overall, 52 points. Chris Pronger80, 30, $9.50M, 86 overall, 43 points. That is the entire top-pairing market for clubs entering July with a hole at the blue line.

Pronger is the best defenseman available and costs $9.50M a year. St. Louis Blues carries 8 picks and 2 firsts, so the Blues can buy him back with draft capital if they want to keep him. For every club that cannot spend $9.50M on a single blue-liner, Stuart is the only option. He is 5 years younger, 75 pounds of value cheaper, and his 52 points in 77 games sit above Pronger's 43 in 82.

Who actually needs him

Minnesota Wild carries $37.91M in payroll, the second cheapest in the league, and went 22-38-9. They hold 5 picks, 1 first, and can afford $1.80M for a top-pairing defenseman.

Florida Panthers went 25-36-16 on $51.07M and sit 7th in picks at 6, holding a first plus what the ledger has as Toronto's round 0 selection. They also face a goaltender decision on David Aebischer86 (86, $0.50M, contract year). Two moves to make before October, and only 18 days of calm before the draft.

Columbus Blue Jackets carry Pascal Leclaire91 at $1.10M and 88 overall, also a contract year. A defenseman for a pick or two, and they keep their goaltender cheap.

The window

The draft sits 18 days out. The free-agent window closes July 28. Between now and June 8, clubs with first-round picks and no top-pairing defenseman have one question: do I draft for the future or deal for Stuart now? I was wrong that San Jose Sharks would move him by March. I'm less sure they keep him past July. A 25-year-old defenseman at $1.80M with 52 points is not something a 76-point team holds onto twice.
