49 Trades in a Day: MIN, OTT and PIT Each Moved 8 Times. Only 1 Deal Is CONFIRMED. 🔥🔥

29 of 30 clubs filed trade windows on May 31. The rookie draft is 8 days away and the free-agent pool holds 0 names.

E.J. "Ears" CallahanInsider · The Hot StoveTuesday, 31 May 2005
ShareCopy link

49 trade windows. 29 of 30 clubs. 1 deal the league confirms at news level.

The rookie draft is 8 days away. The free-agent pool has 0 unsigned names. Any club that wants a new player before October has to take him off another team's roster. The reshuffle started three days ago.

The only CONFIRMED trade

Columbus Blue Jackets and Montreal Canadiens swapped 6 players with 0 picks attached. Columbus had 0 mentions in this desk's file before today. Every other window on the list is "believed done" until the league files it as news.

Minnesota Wild sits 4th in the Northwest with 79 points and a payroll of $49.16M, the 7th lowest in the league. Their club morale of 93.2 is mid-pack. Eight trades in a day, holding 5 picks and 1 first-rounder, tells you a front office is moving its asset mix before the draft rather than after it. Minnesota scored 245 goals and allowed 281. That gap is the problem.

Ottawa Senators finished 4th in the Northeast at 77 points and missed the playoffs. Three of their contract-year players rank in the top 30 of the expiring class by overall: Daniel Alfredsson89 at 93 and $11.40M, Ed Belfour at 91 and $6.50M, Marian Hossa84 at 88 and $2.30M. Ottawa's payroll of $58.72M runs $0.76M per point. Eight trades with that much salary walking into unrestricted free agency means the phones did not stop.

Pittsburgh Penguins went 85 points, took Toronto Maple Leafs to 7 games in Round 2, and carries a payroll of $48.15M, the 4th lowest in the league. Their cost per point is $0.57M, their club morale is 94.7, 2nd highest behind only Toronto. They hold 6 picks and 1 first-rounder. Eight moves after a Game 7 playoff run is a front office trying to keep a window open without spending like Toronto or New York.

What today's volume says

Philadelphia Flyers is the only club with no trade window filed today. Every other team moved. The one window pairing Florida Panthers with Ottawa lines up with what the desk reported yesterday: league sources in Sunrise believed the Panthers were moving a goaltender before the draft. The pairing fits, but the league hasn't filed it as news.

The draft is 8 days away. Whatever is not done by June 8 is likely not getting done before camp.
