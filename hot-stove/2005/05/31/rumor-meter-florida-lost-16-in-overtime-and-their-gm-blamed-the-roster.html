RUMOR METER: Florida Lost 16 in Overtime and Their GM Blamed the Roster. He Has 6 Picks and 8 Days. 🔥🔥

League sources say the Panthers are moving David Aebischer before the draft. Nobody in Sunrise is unhappy. That's the problem.

E.J. "Ears" CallahanInsider · The Hot StoveTuesday, 31 May 2005
ShareCopy link

Florida lost 16 games in overtime. No other club lost more than 12. Their general manager spent Saturday telling anyone who would listen that the roster is the reason, not the system, and not the coach. Then he reminded everyone the free-agent pool has no names. The rookie draft is 8 days away.

David Aebischer86 is the only thing Florida owns that another club clearly wants. He carries 86 overall at $0.50M, played 33 games, has 80 morale and one year left. League sources say his name is moving. At $0.50M, any club that lost a backup or needs insurance can absorb him without touching its top roster.

Boris Van Ryswyk told The Tunnel's Marcus Bell after the final game: "The roster is not the excuse, it's the reason... That's a talent problem. You give a coach a team that can't kill a five-on-five late, you're asking him to coach..."

The recording cuts off there. The logic doesn't. The Panthers finished 25-36-16. They carry $51.07M in payroll for 76 points, $0.67M per point. They lost their final 11 games straight. And their club average morale sits at 95.4, tied with Pittsburgh Penguins.

The happy-team problem

That's the detail nobody can fix with a coaching change. Nobody in Florida is asking to be traded. Nobody is on a cold streak. The players like it in Sunrise and reach the third period and lose anyway. The GM's own numbers say the roster lacks the closing ability, not the commitment.

The pick count is 6, with 1 first-rounder. Florida walks into the draft with those selections and nothing in the pool to supplement them. Van Ryswyk's stated target is specific: "Somebody who makes the other team's life miserable in the last ten minutes." He then added, "I don't know if that's in the draft. Honestly, I don't know."

A man like that exists on another club's roster. The pool is empty. A trade is the only path.

Aebischer goes before June 8. The return is a depth piece and a future look. The six picks get spent, not banked. Nobody on this roster can win a game in overtime, and the general manager told the whole league what he thinks.
