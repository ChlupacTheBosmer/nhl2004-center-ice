LA Walks $12.80M. Holds 0 Firsts. The Pool Has 0 Names. 🔥🔥

Three contracts expire, the Blues own the first-rounder, and nobody in the league is unsigned. The Kings enter the draft with no lever.

E.J. "Ears" CallahanInsider · The Hot StoveMonday, 23 May 2005
ShareCopy link

The Los Angeles Kings Kings have $12.80M in salary walking this summer, zero first-round picks, and a free-agent pool with zero names in it. The draft is 16 days away.

Three contracts expire: Jason Allison80 at $6.70M, Roman Cechmanek84 at $3.30M, Adam Deadmarsh81 at $2.80M. Allison turns 30 next month, scored 91 points in 77 games at 87 overall on the Bureau's Book. Cechmanek is 34, rated 91 overall, played 66 games. Deadmarsh logged 31 games and 25 points before the league put him on IR with a knee; his return date is 2005-06-04.

All three belong to a club that finished 25-37-11, 79 points, out of the postseason, holding 4 picks across 5 rounds and 0 first-rounders. The Kings' first belongs to St. Louis Blues. The Blues took it in a trade that left Los Angeles with nothing above round 2 to call its own.

The Bureau's Contract Ledger lists no unsigned players at any grade. A club that sheds $12.80M cannot replenish through free agency because the open market is a parking lot. Every available body costs a pick, a prospect, or a contract swap. LA has four picks and three contracts about to come off the books.

League sources say the Kings' front office is open to moving multiple picks before 2005-06-08 to land a top-pairing defenseman. The market is two players deep: Brad Stuart76 at San Jose Sharks ($1.80M, 87 overall, 77 games) and Chris Pronger84 at St. Louis Blues ($9.50M, 86 overall, 82 games).

A man who would know in the Pacific says the club's preference is Stuart, not Pronger. The price, according to the same voice, is "more than two picks." LA has four total, none of them firsts.

After the three walk, LA's payroll drops to $39.63M, among the smallest in the league, with nobody unsigned to fill it. The only path forward is trade, and the only currency left is 4 picks in rounds 2 through 5 and the salary space that opens in July.

St. Louis Blues sits at the other end: 8 picks, 2 firsts, a $9.50M blue-liner about to walk, and the happiest locker room in the league at 97.4 morale.

The draft is 2005-06-08. The Kings will pick four times, none in the first round, then spend a summer in trade talks.
