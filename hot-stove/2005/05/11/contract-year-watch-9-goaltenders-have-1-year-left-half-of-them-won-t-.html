Contract-Year Watch: 9 Goaltenders Have 1 Year Left. Half of Them Won't Come Back.

The deepest position in the expiring class is between the pipes. MTL, CHI, OTT and CBJ each face a decision before the season starts.

E.J. "Ears" CallahanInsider · The Hot StoveWednesday, 11 May 2005
ShareCopy link

Nine of the top 30 contract-year players by overall are goaltenders. That is 30% of the class, the deepest position in the league at the expiring mark. Eight of the nine posted 49 games or more this season. They are starters whose deals run out after the coming season.

Four clubs that did not make the playoffs this spring face the same choice: sign them an extension before the season starts or trade them to someone who will.

The four who probably walk

Montreal Canadiens carries Jose Theodore93 at $5.50M with a 91 overall. Montreal's payroll sits at $45.26M on 66 points. The per-point cost is $0.69M, the 2nd highest in the league. A club paying $5.50M for a goaltender while finishing 66 points is a club that needs that money elsewhere. Theodore's morale is 94, which tells you he wants to stay. The front office has to decide whether wanting it is enough.

Chicago Blackhawks carries 3 veterans on expiring deals, starting with Jocelyn Thibault87 at $2.80M and 70 games played. Thibault sits at 96 morale, happy and productive at 30. But Chicago also has Eric Daze87 at $3.00M and 127 points, Alexei Zhamnov84 at $4.20M and 111 points, and Steve Sullivan84 at $3.00M and 100 points. Four expiring veterans on $13.00M combined in a $39.27M payroll. The club cannot keep them all. It probably cannot keep two of them.

Ottawa Senators has Patrick Lalime92 at $1.90M, 90 overall, 68 games played. His morale is 82, which is the lowest of any goaltender in the class and the 4th lowest among all contract-year players. Ottawa finished 77 points and holds 5 picks, 1 first. They have Martin Havlat92 on an expiring deal at 88 overall and $0.90M. Lalime is 30, the price is not the problem, and the morale says the fit is.

Columbus Blue Jackets has the cheapest answer: Pascal Leclaire89, 22 years old, 88 overall, $1.10M, 49 games, 92 morale. This is the one case where the club should re-sign immediately. A 22-year-old at 88 overall on $1.10M is a franchise piece. If Columbus lets him walk, nobody else in this league will understand why.

The ones who stay

Turco at 94 overall on $0.60M is the best value in the class. He will re-sign in Dallas before the season starts. Belfour at 40, on $6.50M, in a Cup year, will either retire or take a cheap one-year. Neither outcome moves the market. Aebischer at $0.50M and 80 morale is the kind of asset that gets moved for a 4th-round pick and nobody complains.

The draft is 28 days away. The trade window reopens the moment a GM calls another GM about a goaltender who knows his time is short.
