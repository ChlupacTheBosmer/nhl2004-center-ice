RUMOR METER: Phoenix Holds 4 Picks, 5 Expiring Names, and a 4-Point Gap to the Last Playoff Spot 🔥🔥

The league's thinnest pick inventory collides with its most rentable shelf. The decision comes before March 19.

E.J. "Ears" CallahanInsider · The Hot StoveWednesday, 26 January 2005
ShareCopy link

Phoenix Coyotes sit 4 points behind Columbus Blue Jackets for the final playoff berth in the West, with roughly 33 games left. They hold 4 draft picks, the fewest of any club in the league. The trade deadline is 52 days away.

Phoenix have 5 players in the contract-year class and a sixth (Daymond Langkow75) whose deal is also expiring and who is presently on injured reserve with a neck through February 24. If the club is not in the standings by March 19, the shelf clears itself. If the club is in it, they are fielding a playoff run with 4 picks and no future capital to trade away.

Here's the table:

Sillinger is the name a contending club wants at this deadline. 30 points in 29 games at $1.80M, 94 morale, an 88 overall grade. He has missed half the season and still sits on a point-per-game pace. A first or second-round pick plus a prospect is what that looks like in March.

Mara at $1.20M with 35 points from the blue line is a cheap offensive defenseman any playoff team would add for a third-rounder. Doan at 84 overall and 43 points is the captain-type, the one whose name moves the needle in the trade market the most. He is also the one Phoenix Coyotes most want to re-sign.

Phoenix hold just 4 picks and 1 first-rounder. New York Rangers own Phoenix's 2nd and 3rd rounders. Phoenix own New York Rangers's 4th. The clubs are tangled. The Phoenix Coyotes front office cannot trade forward picks it does not hold, which means every piece they want at the deadline has to come from a package built around these 5 expiring names.

Langkow doesn't fit. 74 morale, 8.4 minutes a night, $2.10M, and a neck that keeps him out until February 24. He is on the wrong side of 30 with an expiring deal and a role that has evaporated. A club looking for a middle-six center at a deadline discount could take a flyer on him at a fraction of that salary.

A 4-point gap means Phoenix have to decide what they are before March 19. If they lose two straight and fall 8 back, Sillinger, Mara, and possibly Doan become available. If they climb over Columbus Blue Jackets, the shelf stays put, the 4 picks stay put, and 5 contracts walk in July.

League sources say the club is monitoring the gap game by game and has not yet made the call either way.
