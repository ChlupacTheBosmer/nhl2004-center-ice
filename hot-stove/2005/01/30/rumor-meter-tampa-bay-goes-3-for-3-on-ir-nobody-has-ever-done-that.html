RUMOR METER: Tampa Bay Goes 3-for-3 on IR. Nobody Has Ever Done That. 🔥🔥

Three signings, three injured lists, zero survivors. And their locker room is the happiest in the league.

E.J. "Ears" CallahanInsider · The Hot StoveSunday, 30 January 2005
ShareCopy link

The Tampa Bay Lightning signed 3 free agents between January 23 and January 26. All 3 are on the injured list.

Francis Bouillon76, a defenseman, carries a leg designation with a return date of April 25. That is a season-ending absence. Cory Stillman84, an 84-overall winger, has a wrist that keeps him out until March 5. Tim Taylor70, signed January 26, has a hip issue and returns March 13.

Three men in, three men out. That is the worst result any single club has produced in this pattern since the desk began logging it in early January. Philadelphia went 2-for-2 on January 11. Boston matched it across the weekend of January 16-18. No one had signed three and lost three until now.

The League's Happiest Room Has the Most Men on the Shelf

Tampa Bay Lightning holds 67 points and the highest club morale in the league at 96.6 on the Bureau's Morale Scale. They also have 7 players on the injured list, the most in the league.

The 7 include the 3 new arrivals plus Dan Boyle81 (head, out until February 8), Bill Lindsay74 (concussion, March 11), Randy Robitaille74 (wrist, February 25), and Nolan Pratt73 (head, February 23). The Lightning are on a 3-game losing streak, and Carolina Hurricanes sits even with them at 67 points in the Southeast.

11 of 14 Signings This Week Land on IR

The 3-for-3 at Tampa is the worst outcome by club, but it sits inside a league-wide pattern that has not slowed. Between January 23 and January 26, 14 players were signed across the league. Eleven appear on the injured list as of today. That includes 2-for-2 at the Detroit Red Wings (Michael Zigomanis74, back, out until March 13; Ray Whitney78, abdomen, out until February 3) and 2-for-2 at Montreal Canadiens (Richard Zednik83, hip, March 3; Francois Beauchemin76, back, February 6).

Three men survived the week across the league. All 3 signed for clubs outside the top 4 in their division. No player signed by a club in first place is on the injured list as of today, but the sample of first-division signings this week is too thin to read anything from that.

The desk logged 33 instances through January 22. This batch adds 11, bringing the running total to 44.

What Tampa Bay Does Next Is the Rumor

Tampa Bay Lightning has 48 days to what the calendar has as the trade deadline. They carry $45.79M in payroll, $0.68M per point, and $27.66M in profit. They are in a playoff position, their locker room feels good, and they are missing 7 men.

The question league sources are asking is whether Tampa Bay adds a 4th body before March 19 or trusts that Taylor, Stillman, Boyle, Lindsay, Robitaille, and Pratt cycle back in sequence through February and early March. The desk cannot name a January signing from this month that a team could count on immediately. Every club that has gone shopping has come home with a medical form.

Detroit going 2-for-2 on the same weekend is the secondary story. They hold 6 draft picks, 2 first-rounders, and the highest payroll in the league at $80.89M. They signed for depth and got two more men in the training room. Their per-point cost of $1.35M is 2nd worst in the league. The money is already spent and the bodies keep landing on the wrong side of the glass.
