RUMOR METER: 6 of 9 Men Signed January 9-10 Are on IR. The Pattern Reaches 21. 🔥🔥🔥

Nashville and San Jose sign for depth and get injured bodies instead. Calgary returns to the list.

E.J. "Ears" CallahanInsider · The Hot StoveFriday, 14 January 2005
ShareCopy link

The January 9-10 depth sweep produced 9 contracts. Six are on injured reserve. The signing-then-IR pattern that started in mid-December now sits at 21 instances across the league.

Two clubs are new to this column. Mark Eaton72 signed with Nashville Predators on January 9 for $12,150. Eight days later he is out with a hand injury through January 21. Alyn McCauley72 signed with San Jose Sharks on January 10 for $406,280. His ankle went between January 12 and January 14, out until January 20. Neither man cost a team enough to notice. Both cost a team a roster spot.

Ron Wilson told The Tunnel's Jan Kowalczyk on January 4 that San Jose had "depth issues" and pointed at the third and fourth lines. Nine days later, the man his club signed to patch that hole is out with an ankle.

The sweep keeps collecting clubs

Dave Lowry73 signed with Calgary Flames on January 9 for $80,220. A foot sent him to IR between January 12 and January 14, out until February 5. Calgary was already on this list through Dean McAmmond83, who landed on IR with a knee between December 29 and January 1. Two depth signings, two injuries, 2 weeks apart.

The other three casualties belong to clubs this column has visited before. Dan Boyle78 and Bill Lindsay70 signed with Tampa Bay Lightning on January 9. Boyle, at 81 overall, went down with a head injury between January 10 and January 12, out until February 8. Lindsay went down with a concussion between January 12 and January 14, out until March 11. Matt Cooke72 signed with Vancouver Canucks on January 9 and landed on IR with a rib injury, out until February 25.

Six contracts, six IR entries, none costing more than $406,280. The three survivors carry $605,420, $596,250 and $461,370 between them. They wear Philadelphia, New York and Washington sweaters. The survivors cost more money and didn't break. The cheap ones did. Luck, not a spreadsheet.

The return dates tell you how rough the damage is. McCauley, the ankle, back January 20: 10 days. Eaton, the hand, back January 21: 12 days. But Lindsay, the concussion, is out until March 11. Cooke's rib keeps him off until February 25. Ten and twelve days is a scratch. March 11 and February 25 are roster holes.

Nashville Predators has 2 men on IR now. Calgary Flames has 3. Tampa Bay Lightning has 4, including both of its January signings. The clubs that survived this window have 2 each. The pattern hit the cheap contracts first and the expensive ones second, and three weeks in it's still finding new clubs.
