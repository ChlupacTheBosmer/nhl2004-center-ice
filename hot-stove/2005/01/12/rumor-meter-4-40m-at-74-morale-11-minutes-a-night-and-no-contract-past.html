RUMOR METER: $4.40M at 74 Morale, 11 Minutes a Night, and No Contract Past June 🔥🔥

Ottawa has the league's most unhappy room. Its three unhappiest men are all free agents in waiting on a team that just lost its sixth straight.

E.J. "Ears" CallahanInsider · The Hot StoveWednesday, 12 January 2005
ShareCopy link

Ottawa Senators carries the lowest club morale in the league at 85.8. Three men in that building sit at 74: Bryan Smolinski76, Vaclav Varada74 and Peter Schaefer74. All three are unsigned past June. Combined, they carry $4.40M in salary for roughly 11 minutes of ice each night.

Smolinski makes $2.40M at 76 overall. He has played 42 games at 11.5 minutes a night. Varada earns $1.40M at 73 overall, 42 games at 11.9. Schaefer is the cheapest of the three at $0.60M, 74 overall, 37 games at 11.1. None of them is hurt. None of them has spoken to a reporter this fortnight.

The club sits at 40 points, 15-20-4, second-last in the Northeast ahead of Montreal's 34. They just dropped their sixth straight. Their road record reads 7-15. Their per-point payroll sits at $1.07M, which buys them 40 points in a division where Toronto has 71.

A man who would know, talking from the east end of the building, said the room has stopped pretending. Not a line he offered for print. A feeling.

Here is the market read. Three bottom-six forwards with expiring deals, no no-trade clauses on record, combined under $5M, playing for a team with nothing to play for and the league's worst morale. They are trade bait by definition. The question is whether anyone wants 11-minute depth in a year when the trade deadline is still three weeks away and every GM is shopping his own.

Smolinski at $2.40M is the hard one. At 76 overall he fills a checking role, but no contender pays that much for a third-liner who plays 11 a night. Varada at $1.40M is closer to a fit. Schaefer at $0.60M is almost a freebie, the kind of piece a cup team adds for February insurance.

The number that catches a GM's eye is 74. Eleven players in the league sit at 74 morale. Ottawa owns three of them. The other eight are spread across five clubs. Three at once in one building, all expiring, all bottom-six, tells you the front office has already stopped selling these men on staying.

League sources say Ottawa has taken calls. No calls have come close. The three sit at 74, the lowest morale in the league outside four men at 72 in Columbus and Chicago. That is the kind of number a GM in June reads as a discount waiting to open.
