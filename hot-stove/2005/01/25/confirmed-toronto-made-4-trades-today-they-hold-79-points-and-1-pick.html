CONFIRMED: Toronto Made 4 Trades Today. They Hold 79 Points and 1 Pick.

The one-day sweep brought Roberto Luongo to the Air Canada Centre. Five clubs own more of Toronto's draft future than Toronto does.

E.J. "Ears" CallahanInsider · The Hot StoveTuesday, 25 January 2005
ShareCopy link

The league filed four trades on Tuesday. Every one involves Toronto. Every one is CONFIRMED.

Toronto Maple Leafs acquired Roberto Luongo from Florida, Ramzi Abid from Pittsburgh, Andreas Stahle from Columbus, and Maxime Ouellet from Washington. They sent Ivan Majesky and Alexei Smirnov to three of those clubs. The pick table shows the cost: the Maple Leafs hold 1 selection across 5 rounds. Zero first-rounders. Nobody in the league has fewer.

Toronto sits at 79 points in the Northeast, 27 ahead of second-place Boston Bruins. Detroit Red Wings hold 6 picks and 2 firsts. Washington Capitals, Pittsburgh Penguins, and Florida Panthers each hold 6 picks. Five clubs carry more draft capital than the league leader.

Ed Taylor told The Tunnel's Jan Kowalczyk on January 22 that extensions for Marian Gaborik96 and Brad Richards85 are the priority and that he has $20M to work with. The GM offered no timeline. Tuesday answered the question.

The goaltending chart has a $6.50M question

Ed Belfour91 is 39, earns $6.50M, and is a free agent in July. Taylor named Belfour the starter over the backup when he spoke to The Tunnel on January 22: "today, which is what I'm more interested in talking about than next season, I think that Ed would start." Four days later the backup is Roberto Luongo.

Florida finished last in the Southeast at 42 points. Columbus Blue Jackets sit at 50 in the Central. Pittsburgh Penguins are at 47 in the Atlantic. All three clubs that traded with Toronto on Tuesday are on the sell side. The contender is buying.

I expect Belfour in a different sweater before the trade deadline on 2005-03-19. That's 53 days. A 39-year-old at $6.50M with a new backup who happens to be Roberto Luongo has one use left: somebody else pays it in February, or Toronto starts October without it.

The Maple Leafs have $25.51M in profit, the best in hockey, and every dollar is going toward winning now. The 2005 draft belongs to Detroit, Washington, Pittsburgh, and Florida. Toronto's single remaining selection will be the 30th name called. Give or take.
