RUMOR METER: Two Depth Forwards Signed This Week. Both Are on the IR Before January 2. 🔥🔥

Jason Blake and Dean McAmmond join a pattern that now spans five clubs and six contracts in twenty days.

E.J. "Ears" CallahanInsider · The Hot StoveSaturday, 1 January 2005
ShareCopy link

New York Islanders signed Jason Blake80 on December 28 for $294,160. The league's injury list has him out with a wrist, onset between December 29 and January 1, back February 12. Six weeks he was not expected to miss. He has not played a shift in a New York sweater.

Three hundred miles west, Calgary Flames signed Dean McAmmond76 on December 27 for $590,480. Knee, onset between December 29 and January 1, back January 18. Three weeks. McAmmond played one game for Calgary before the league put him down.

Those are the fifth and sixth entries in a pattern that started in early December and now touches five clubs: Nashville Predators, Washington Capitals, New York Islanders, Calgary Flames, and the one that kicked this thread off in the Meadowlands. Six signings, six IR designations, twenty days. No club has answered for it.

The pattern is not about stars. Every contract in this group sits under $600,000. Every one is a depth fill signed by a club that needed a body. And every single player landed on the injury report within four days of signing. League sources at two of the affected clubs declined to discuss the timing.

The Islanders are the worst-affected

Blake is not New York Islanders's first signing to vanish. The club already has Mike Peca85 (wrist, back January 13), Roman Hamrlik81 (neck, back February 6), and Mariusz Czerkawski75 (neck, back January 16) on the list. Czerkawski also sits at 76 on the Morale Scale, one of the fifteen lowest marks in the league.

New York carries 36 points, 15-17-2, one game out of the final playoff spot in the Atlantic. Their five-man IR is the joint-most in the league alongside Nashville Predators and Pittsburgh Penguins. The club that signed Blake for a fifth forward at $294,160 now has a hole where that body should be.

Scott Walker82 is not here; he's in Nashville Predators. But the Islanders' room is not warm either. Garth Snow78 sits at 74, and the club average of 89.0 puts them ninth-lowest in the league.

What the pattern costs

Calgary Flames is in worse shape than New York. Twenty-eight points, the second-lowest club morale in the league at 86.9, and now McAmmond's knee for three weeks. Darryl Sutter told The Tunnel's Jan Kowalczyk after a December 27 loss that the room needed to compete harder. Two days later the club's newest depth forward was out with a knee.

The six contracts total roughly $2.1M in salary. No club will lose a season to this, but in a 30-club league where the 8th and 12th seed in any division are separated by single digits, three or four weeks of a $300K forward is a point. Maybe two.

I score my own calls in a CONFIRMED vs. DENIED column at season's end. The signing-injury cluster is not yet CONFIRMED as anything more than coincidence. But the sample is six, and the window keeps shrinking.
