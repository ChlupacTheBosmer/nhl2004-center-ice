RUMOR METER: 3 of 4 Men Signed January 2 Are Already on IR. The Only Survivor Walked Into 7 Vacancies in New York 🔥🔥

The signing-injury pattern reaches its 15th instance. Greg Hawgood is the sole survivor of the January 2 sweep, and his new club has more men on IR than anyone else in the league.

E.J. "Ears" CallahanInsider · The Hot StoveSaturday, 8 January 2005
ShareCopy link

Four contracts filed January 2. Three of the four men who signed them are on injured reserve before the week is out.

Artem Chubarov75, signed by Vancouver Canucks for $20,470, is out with a leg through February 3. Alexei Zhitnik83, signed by Buffalo Sabres for $190,190, carries a groin and returns tomorrow, January 9, if the window holds. Brenden Morrow86, signed by Dallas Stars for $159,500, has an ankle and a return date of January 16. All three onsets fall between January 5 and January 8, three to six days after the ink.

The fourth name, Greg Hawgood, went to New York Islanders for $361,270. He is the only one standing.

That makes 15 instances in roughly three weeks. A depth signing, a body part designated by the league, a return date set, and the contract becomes a dead cap line before the player suits up. The pattern now touches at least 7 clubs across both conferences.

Hawgood signed with the league's medical ward

New York Islanders carries 7 players on injured reserve, the most of any club in the league. Mike Peca85 (wrist) returns January 13. Roman Hamrlik81 (neck) returns February 6. Jason Blake80 (wrist) returns February 12. Mariusz Czerkawski75 (neck) returns January 16. Three more names round out the count.

New York sits at 40 points in the Atlantic, one point behind New Jersey Devils with a game in hand, and is on a one-game losing streak. They need bodies. They signed one.

The pattern does not care who needs bodies. Fifteen men have signed and landed on the shelf since mid-December, across clubs with 31 points and clubs with 56. The common thread is depth, cheap deals, and a January calendar that punishes anyone who takes a hit in a lineup shuffled to cover gaps.

The dollar figures tell the story

No contract in the January 2 group clears $400,000. Chubarov at $20,470 is the cheapest signing filed this window. These are emergency patches. Teams are filling holes with minimum-salary bodies because the injury list is eating their depth, and the new bodies are getting hurt before they can fill anything.

Dallas Stars is the sharpest irony. Morrow carries an $1.30M cap hit on the contract-year class at 84 overall and 55 points in 42 games. He was signed for $159,500 on January 2, an ankle came between January 5 and January 8, and Dallas is down one more body on a team that already lost a game and a half since the Presidents' Trophy run in December.

A man who would know says the league's trainers are filing bodies faster than GMs can clear waivers. He did not say who is next. He did not need to.
