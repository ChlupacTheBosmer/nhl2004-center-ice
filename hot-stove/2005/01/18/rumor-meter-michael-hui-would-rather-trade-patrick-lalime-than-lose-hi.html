RUMOR METER: Michael Hui Would Rather Trade Patrick Lalime Than Lose Him in July 🔥🔥

Ottawa's general manager said on the record this week that he has decided to move his 90-overall goaltender at the deadline. He hasn't told the goaltender yet.

E.J. "Ears" CallahanInsider · The Hot StoveTuesday, 18 January 2005
ShareCopy link

The piece

Patrick Lalime88 sat through this week's practices with no signal from the front office. Michael Hui told The Tunnel's Marcus Bell after Ottawa's latest loss that he would trade the goaltender rather than let him walk in July.

"That's the problem with where we are. Patrick's done a good job, he's been our guy, and if we're not in a position to re-sign him at a number that makes sense, then I'd rather trade him in March and get something back for the pipeline than lose him for nothing in July."

Lalime is 30, sits 90 in the Bureau's Book, carries a $1.90M salary with one year left, and has started 37 games for a club that is 15-22-4 with 42 points. Buffalo holds the 8th East playoff spot at 49.

The Ottawa Senators room has the lowest average morale in the league at 84.7. Hui told Bell he does not have a player to build around.

"I don't have him. And that's the honest answer. Everybody wants me to point at a kid and say, there's our man, that's the core. We've got young guys, they're in the room, they're working. But I don't have a player I can look at and say, there's the top-end talent we build the next five years around."

That is a general manager shopping his assets before they walk, not one planning for April.

What Lalime is worth on the open market

The trade deadline the calendar carries is 60 days away. Ottawa holds 5 picks in the 2005 draft, 1 of them a first-rounder, and $42.80M on payroll returning 42 points. Lalime's morale is 85, which is not the number of a man who suspects he is on a plane to somebody else's building by March.

Six goaltenders with one year left sit 86 or better in the Book. Lalime is not the most talented on the list. He may be the most movable.

Turco at 96 and $0.60M is the best value on that board, but he is 95 morale in Dallas, a club at 62 points and $66.88M. He re-signs. Theodore at $5.50M is a payroll problem for a 38-point club in Montreal. Lalime at $1.90M is the one a contender can actually absorb for 40 games.

The conversation that hasn't happened

Hui said it himself: "I haven't had it with him yet. But yeah, he's 30."

The goaltender has not been told. If Hui moves him before March 19, the message lands in a practice room, not a contract meeting. That is not a negotiation. That is an announcement.
