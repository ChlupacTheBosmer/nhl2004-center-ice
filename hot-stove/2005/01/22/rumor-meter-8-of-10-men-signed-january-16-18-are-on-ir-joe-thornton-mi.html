RUMOR METER: 8 of 10 Men Signed January 16-18 Are on IR. Joe Thornton Misses Until March. 🔥🔥🔥

Boston went 2-for-2 across the weekend. The pattern reaches 33 instances and the biggest name yet.

E.J. "Ears" CallahanInsider · The Hot StoveSaturday, 22 January 2005
ShareCopy link

The Boston Bruins signed Joe Thornton84 on Tuesday for $465,030. By Thursday he was on injured reserve with a hand and a March 5 return date. Boston had already placed Jonathan Girard70 on IR on Monday, a neck, out until April 7. Two signings, two IR slots, two days apart.

That makes 8 of the 10 men signed between January 16 and January 18 who are now on the injured list. The pattern, which the desk has tracked since late December, reaches 33 instances. The biggest name yet is Thornton, a 91-overall center.

The previous heaviest signing window, January 9-10, produced 6 of 9 on IR. This one went 8 of 10. The clubs hit are spread thin: Boston twice, Pittsburgh, Nashville, Phoenix, Tampa Bay, Ottawa, Columbus. Only the New York Rangers and the Vancouver Canucks walked away clean, and they signed the same day, January 17.

Mario Lemieux85 cost Pittsburgh a $497,380 cap hit and 7 vacancies on the IR before his rib heals on January 23. Scott Walker84 gave Nashville a concussion and a February 4 date. Landon Wilson72 walked into Phoenix with a concussion and left January 24. Nolan Pratt71 landed in Tampa Bay with a head injury and a February 23 return. Chris Phillips77 gave Ottawa a head injury and a January 24 date. Dimitri Kalinin82 arrived in Columbus with an elbow and left January 25.

None of these injuries predates the signing. The onset windows on the league's IR list all fall after the transaction date. Girard, Lemieux, Wilson, Thornton, Pratt, Phillips and Kalinin all carry onset dates between January 19 and January 22. Walker's window is January 15-18, one day after Nashville filed his contract. The desk has no explanation.

The names set this apart from the January 9-10 sweep. Thornton at 91 overall is the highest-rated player to fall into the sequence. Girard at 75 is the longest absence: 75 days from his signing date to his return. Boston now has 3 men on IR, including both of its January additions, and sits 50 points out of first in the Northeast with 47 games played.

Tyler Wright78 at Columbus has watched the club sign a defenseman on Tuesday and put him on IR by Thursday. The Columbus Blue Jackets already carry the league's second-worst club morale at 86.3, with Marc Denis90 at 72 and Espen Knutsen80 at 72 on the bottom of the league's Morale Scale. They just spent $392,750 on a man who will miss one game.

The survivors are [[player:xxx|Paul Healey]] at New York ($646,460) and [[player:xxx|Brandon Reid]] at Vancouver ($113,230). Both signed January 17. Neither has a line on the injured list as of this morning. The desk gives them until the end of the month.
