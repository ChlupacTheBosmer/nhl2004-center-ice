RUMOR METER: Five of the Twelve Men Signed Since December 29 Are Already on Injured Reserve 🔥🔥🔥

A 3-day depth sweep produced 12 contracts and 5 IR entries. The pattern that started in mid-December keeps landing bodies teams cannot use.

E.J. "Ears" CallahanInsider · The Hot StoveTuesday, 4 January 2005
ShareCopy link

Twelve contracts were filed between December 29 and December 31. Five of those players are on the injured list as of Tuesday. The pattern that The Hot Stove has followed for three consecutive editions has widened across five new clubs in three days.

Chad Kilger75 is the far end. He signed in Montreal on December 31 for $416,300. His elbow keeps him out until March 22, which is 81 days from the day the contract was filed. Montreal Canadiens sits 11 and 21 and 4 in the Northeast. The Canadiens now carry Yanic Perreault74, a 78 overall center out until February 3, and Joe Juneau70, a concussion case through January 26.

The previous edition counted seven instances of this across five clubs and six contracts in twenty days. This batch adds five contracts in three days and touches Carolina Hurricanes, Colorado Avalanche, New Jersey Devils, Phoenix Coyotes, and Montreal Canadiens. The positions vary and the body parts run head, shoulder, shoulder, concussion, elbow. The only thread is the signature.

Michael Rupp74 signed with New Jersey Devils for depth on the wing. He's rated 74. The shoulder keeps him out until February 18. The Devils carry 2 names on the injured list. Rupp is one. Ari Ahonen84, whose return is January 5, is the other. The team that built its roster on payroll discipline, $44.98M for 49 points at $0.92M per point, signed a forward for 6 weeks of nothing.

Phoenix Coyotes has its own problem layered on top of the expiring class the desk ran last week. David Tanabe80, rated 74, arrived on December 30 for $399,420 and went down with a concussion between January 1 and January 4. The Coyotes had Steve Konowalchuk83 and his back on the list before Tanabe landed. Tanabe's return is February 12.

The other seven signings held up. Brooks Orpik, Justin Papineau, Kyle Wanvig, Nikos Tselios, Pavel Trnka, Ville Nieminen, and Daymond Langkow79 are all available. Five of twelve went to the trainer. The price of a $400,000 contract just went up.
