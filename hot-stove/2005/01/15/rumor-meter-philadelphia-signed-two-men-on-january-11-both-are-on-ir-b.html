RUMOR METER: Philadelphia Signed Two Men on January 11. Both Are on IR. Both Miss the Playoffs Push. 🔥🔥🔥

The signing-injury pattern hits its 25th instance, and for the first time one club went 2-for-2 in a single night.

E.J. "Ears" CallahanInsider · The Hot StoveSaturday, 15 January 2005
ShareCopy link

Philadelphia signed Keith Primeau84 and Justin Williams77 on January 11. Four days later, both carry named injuries and both are on the shelf through the end of January. Primeau's arm keeps him out until January 21. Williams's hip runs to January 20. That is the 24th and 25th instances of a pattern that has now swallowed 25 contracts across 28 days.

The club went 2-for-2. No team has matched that in a single signing night during this stretch, which runs from mid-December through today.

Shawn Bates80 went down with an abdomen injury the same window, return date January 19. He landed in New York Islanders, which already carried 7 men on IR before Bates arrived, more than any other club. Kristian Kudroc74 to Florida Panthers, shoulder, out until January 20. That makes 4 of the 5 January 11 signings on the injured list.

The lone survivor is Kris Beech74 to Pittsburgh Penguins at $4,340. Beech is not on IR. He is, however, a 19-year-old signed for pennies to a club carrying 6 men on IR, including Radim Vrbata79 (head, February 10) and Brian Holzinger77 (concussion, January 30).

The payroll of pain

The five contracts signed on January 11 carry individual deals of $147,170 (Primeau), $205,680 (Williams), $621,470 (Bates), $99,080 (Kudroc), and $4,340 (Beech). Four of those five are out; only Beech's $4,340 is delivering a usable body right now.

Philadelphia Flyers sits at 42 points, 14 back of a playoff spot in the Atlantic. The Flyers needed forward depth. They got two injured forwards instead. Primeau was rated 84 before the arm. He is the highest-overall man on the injury list for Philadelphia, which now has 4 names on IR total.

Twenty-five contracts, at least 14 different clubs involved, spanning three weeks. A man signs, a body part is designated, and the return date is never sooner than 5 days away. The desks keep calling it bad luck. Twenty-five instances is a pattern.

The 7-deep crease in Long Island

New York Islanders has 46 points and 7 men on IR. Bates's abdomen is the newest entry on a list that includes Roman Hamrlik82 (neck, February 6), Jason Blake81 (wrist, February 12), and Mariusz Czerkawski76 (neck, January 16). The Islanders signed Bates for $621,470 because they needed scoring depth. They will not have it for 4 days. After that, they will need their 7 other injured men back faster than their return dates allow.

A league source says the pattern has clubs looking twice at depth signings with fewer than 5 days of workout clearance. Whether that is policy or superstition, the 5 January 11 contracts tell the same story every other day's transactions have told since December 29: the bodies arrive, and then they leave.
