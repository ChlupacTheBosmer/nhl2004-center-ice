RUMOR METER: Los Angeles Holds 3 Expirings. Cechmanek's Morale Says He Goes First. 🔥🔥

The desk was wrong about Deadmarsh. The goaltender is the name every buyer in the Pacific is circling.

E.J. "Ears" CallahanInsider · The Hot StoveMonday, 31 January 2005
ShareCopy link

The Los Angeles Kings carry 3 of the top 30 contract-year players in the league, all expiring in July, at a club trailing the next team up in the Pacific by 1 point with 47 days to what the calendar has as the trade deadline: Roman Cechmanek86, Jason Allison82 and Adam Deadmarsh83.

The desk said on January 5 that Deadmarsh would be gone by January 21. He wasn't. The other 2 are still there too.

The goaltender is the liquid one

Cechmanek, 33, carries a $3.30M cap hit with 42 games and a 76 on the Morale Scale. That's the lowest morale of any goaltender in the contract-year class and the 13th-lowest mark in the league. Patrick Lalime88 at 90 and Marty Turco93 at 92 are both in trade conversations already at other clubs, and their morale reads like men who plan to stay.

Goalies move at deadlines, and league sources around the division say the club is fielding calls on Cechmanek now. The return question is first-rounder or second and a prospect.

The center nobody wants to absorb

Allison at $6.70M is the biggest salary among all 30 contract-year names in the league. His 66 points in 46 games are real, and his 91 morale means he isn't forcing a change. But $6.70M for 25 games of rental hockey narrows the buyer list to clubs already spending above $66M. That's Detroit Red Wings, Colorado Avalanche, Toronto Maple Leafs and Dallas Stars. The first two already hold 6 picks each and have no reason to pay for a half-season of center.

Nobody is calling Los Angeles about a center.

Deadmarsh is still here

The desk owns the miss. Deadmarsh played 12 of LA's 51 games, carries 11 points on a $2.80M deal, and sits at 93 morale. Whatever kept him out for 39 games, the club hasn't moved him and he's not in a position to force one. With 12 games of tape, nobody's paying a deadline price for him.

The younger core is waiting

Alexander Frolov75, 22, overall 74, returns from a neck injury on February 1. At 74 with club control, he's the building block a rebuild actually needs. LA has 5 picks and $15.41M in profit, which means they can stand pat or sell. With 48 points and 47 days to the deadline, the 76 morale on Cechmanek's sheet is what tells you the club is leaning sell.
