RUMOR METER: Sign and Injure. 4 Contracts Filed Oct 7-8. All 4 on IR by Oct 12. 🔥🔥

The pattern the desk flagged in May is running again. The pool that fed these signings is empty.

E.J. "Ears" CallahanInsider · The Hot StoveWednesday, 12 October 2005
ShareCopy link

Four players signed contracts between October 7 and October 8. Every one of them is on the injured list as of today.

Carolina Hurricanes took Sandis Ozolinsh89 [749] at $216,510 on October 7. He went down with a hand and is out until October 16. Phoenix Coyotes signed Robert Reichel77 [824] for $593,030 the same day. Wrist, back October 14. New Jersey Devils filed for Pavel Kubina79 [524] at $241,470 on October 8. Ankle, October 14. Florida Panthers grabbed Branislav Mezei73 [658] for $65,710 that same day. Leg, November 13.

I printed the exact same finding on May 4, back when the free-agent pool had names in it. Four signings, four IR entries, the same 5-day window. Nobody on the desk thought it would repeat. It repeated.

The pool that fed them is gone

The league entered October with nobody unsigned. The free-agent pool reads "none." These four players were the last bodies available, and the moment they were filed they were shelved. There are no replacements for the replacements.

The onset window for all four reads "between 2005-05-31 and 2005-10-12," which tells you nothing about whether the injury pre-existed the signing or arrived after. A hand, a wrist, an ankle, a leg. The link is the timing.

What this costs

The combined salary of these four is small money by this league's standards. The cost is a month of a fourth defenseman in Sunrise, a backup center in Phoenix who will not play until the calendar flips, a depth blue-liner in Newark, and a veteran defenseman in Carolina who could not log a single shift since signing.

Ozolinsh, 85 overall, is the name that matters. Reichel is 73 overall at $593,030, and PHX has zero points through one game already. Kubina at 77 was depth for a New Jersey club that won its opener 3-1. Mezei, 72, is out 32 more days, the longest stretch of the four.

The Bertuzzi score

While I'm settling accounts: I called Vancouver trading Todd Bertuzzi before the 2005-06-08 rookie draft. He is still on Vancouver Canucks. The club leads its division with 2 points and 99.5 club morale. I was wrong. He is still there, still happy, and nobody in the building looks ready to let him go.

The next signing filed by any club in this league is the test. Four out of four is a pattern. Five out of five makes it a curse. The pool is empty again, so whoever files next is pulling from the waiver wire, a minor-league callup, or a club shopping a healthy scratch. Those are different pools. They may behave differently.

They may not.
