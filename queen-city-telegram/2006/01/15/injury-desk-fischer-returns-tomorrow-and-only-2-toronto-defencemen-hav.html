Injury Desk: Fischer returns tomorrow, and only 2 Toronto defencemen have played every game

Five different blue-line pairings since December 22, a 25-minute nightly load on Klesla and Jackman, and a 5th body who missed 27 games

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramSunday, 15 January 2006
ShareCopy link

Jiri Fischer84 is back in the lineup tomorrow night at Washington Capitals. The league-listed date is 2006-01-16. He has played 18 games this season at 17.4 minutes a night, carrying 1 goal and 6 assists. League lists it as a knee.

Toronto's back end has carried the season on two men. Rostislav Klesla93 is at 45 games, 25.6 minutes, 20 points on 6 goals and 14 assists. Barret Jackman88 is at 45, 25.4 minutes, 28 points on 10 goals and 18 assists. The third-highest total on the blue line belongs to Tomas Kaberle85 at 26 games and 17 points. No other Toronto defenceman has played more than that.

The shuffle that started on 2005-12-22, when Eminger and Fischer came in and Kirill Safronov71 and Bruno St. Jacques66 came out, produced a 23-shots-against game on its first night. Both went back to the bench shortly after. Fischer returns now. Eminger sits at 19 games with 9 points and 15.7 minutes.

The five who have dressed and played through the current stretch are Klesla, Jackman, Kaberle, McCabe, and Eminger. Ron Hainsey77 at 19, Carlo Colaiacovo76 at 25, Andreas Stahle76 at 18, Safronov at 20, and St. Jacques at 15 have all been healthy scratches.

Back-to-back with storylines attached

Tuesday at Washington gives Maxime Ouellet86 a meeting with the club that traded him on 2005-01-25. Karel Pilar68 lines up for the Capitals from the other side. Washington has 12 wins, 22 losses, 7 overtime losses, 37 points, and a 1-game win streak after beating New York Islanders 3-1 on Thursday.

Wednesday at Montreal Canadiens brings Hainsey to the building where he spent last season before Toronto claimed him on 2005-05-31. Toronto leads the season series against Montreal 1-0-0 with one additional overtime win. Against Washington, the single meeting went 4-2 Toronto.

The Capitals allow 123 goals in 44 games. Toronto has scored 165 in 45. The math favours the visitor on Tuesday and again on Wednesday.
