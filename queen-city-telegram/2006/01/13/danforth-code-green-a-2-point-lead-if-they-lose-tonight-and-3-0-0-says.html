DANFORTH: Code Green, a 2-point lead if they lose tonight, and 3-0-0 says they won't

The Rangers own 8 straight. Toronto owns this series 3-0-0. Tonight decides whether the lead holds at 4 or compresses to 2.

Frankie DanforthColumnist · Queen City TelegramFriday, 13 January 2006
ShareCopy link

A regulation loss tonight in New York cuts the lead to 2 points. Two. With 2 games in hand for Toronto Maple Leafs, which means the real gap is maybe 5 points depending on who wins those. That is a draw with extra steps, not a lead. I'm not proposing we panic. I'm proposing we look at the math before the first faceoff.

The Rangers sit at 64 points in 46 games. Toronto sits at 68 in 43. The gap is 4 points and 3 games in hand. Tonight the Rangers close the game count to 47 and Toronto to 44. If New York wins in regulation, both numbers tighten to 2. If Toronto wins, the gap goes to 6. If someone loses in overtime, somewhere in between.

Now. Toronto has beaten these people three times this season. 15 goals for, 7 against. Not close. The New York Rangers streak is 8 games, which is real, and I'm not going to pretend an 8-game heater is nothing. But the streak has been built on clubs outside the top 6 in the East. The one club in this league that has made the Rangers look like a .500 team in January is the one skating into Madison Square Garden tonight.

Pavel Bure83 is out with a concussion through 2006-02-28. He's their second-best winger by a mile. The line that was supposed to make the third unit dangerous is gone for 6 weeks, and the Rangers are running that streak without him. I don't know what their coach told them after the win on Monday, but the sheet says the man behind Bure has 1 point in 1 game since Bure went down. That's a small sample, and I'm a man who knows what a small sample looks like. Still.

New York Rangers has scored 136 in 46 games. Toronto has allowed 90 in 43. Those numbers have been sitting next to each other for 3 games and Toronto won all three.

I want the lead to stay at 4 or push to 6. I want Mats Sundin92 to have 34 minutes and be invisible by the second period because Toronto is up 4-1 and the Garden is empty by the intermission. I will not get that. I'll probably get a 1-0 game where Roberto Luongo95 makes 38 saves and my blood pressure gets to Green by a hair.

But I'll take it. Code Green. The 3-0-0 says the math never gets printed tonight.
