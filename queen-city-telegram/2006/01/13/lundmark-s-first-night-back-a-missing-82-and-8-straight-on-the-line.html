Lundmark's first night back, a missing 82, and 8 straight on the line

The Rangers' streak hits 8 without their best winger. Jamie Lundmark meets the club that gave him 35 games and 8 assists.

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramFriday, 13 January 2006
ShareCopy link

Toronto visits New York tonight with a 3-0-0 season series (15 goals for, 7 against) and the chance to widen the Northeast lead from 4 points to 6. The Rangers arrive on an 8-game heater missing Pavel Bure83, who has a concussion and a return date of 2006-02-28.

Bure, rated 82 by the Bureau, is the Rangers' second-highest scorer this season by any measure that matters. His absence has forced depth re-draws on the right side and left the fourth line without a man who can finish. The Rangers have scored 136 goals in 46 games against 124 allowed. Toronto has scored 157 in 43 and allowed 90.

Two men meet the club they left

Jamie Lundmark83 dresses for the first road game in New York since he left the Rangers on 2005-05-31. He has 9 points in 35 games on the season at 8.0 minutes a night, earning $0.80M. The Bureau's Development Index has him at +8, tied with Stanislav Chistov92 and Rostislav Klesla93 for second on the roster behind Marian Gaborik99 at +11.

On the other bench, Kenny Jonsson77 meets Toronto for the first time since the Cup Final ended on 2005-05-31. He left as a champion and returns as a Ranger trying to close a 4-point gap.

What the streak has been

The 8-game run includes wins over Pittsburgh Penguins, Washington Capitals, New Jersey Devils, and other clubs currently sitting between 34 and 54 points. Toronto is the first opponent on the schedule with 68. The New York Rangers defense has been tested in stretches, giving up 124 goals in 46 games, and Toronto's top line of Mats Sundin92, Alexander Mogilny89, and Marian Gaborik carries 81 points between them. Gaborik alone has 14 in 23 games at 22.2 minutes a night, the highest per-game production on the roster.

The goaltending question

Roberto Luongo95 dressed for the Boston game on 2006-01-11 and stopped 44 shots in a 4-2 win. His workload sits at 23 starts in 43 games. Maxime Ouellet86 has 10 starts. The committee hasn't used Mikael Tellqvist73 since the two-deep experiment, and his Index sits at -5, the lowest mark on the roster.

The Rangers have 2 players on the injured list: Bure (concussion) and Mike Wilson64 (on IR, 1 game this season). Toronto has none.
