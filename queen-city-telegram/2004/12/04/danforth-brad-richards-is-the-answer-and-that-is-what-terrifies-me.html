DANFORTH: Brad Richards is the answer, and that is what terrifies me

The second-centre problem I have written about every day since October might be solved, and nobody on this roster looks like they want to talk about what happens in July.

Frankie DanforthColumnist · Queen City TelegramSaturday, 4 December 2004
ShareCopy link

I have been asking this desk for a second centre every day since October. I have written the same column in four different moods: pleading, furious, resigned, and — the most dangerous one of all — hopeful. I did not think I would find myself here on a Saturday in early December, writing a sentence that begins with his name and ends with the word answer.

Brad Richards83 is the answer.

Not the complete answer. I want to be precise before the fan mail arrives. The second-centre problem is not solved because one man puts up 27 points in 25 games at 16.8 minutes a night. It is solved — tentatively, grudgingly, in a way that makes my jaw ache — because the way those points are being produced suggests there is more coming.

The Development Index has Richards up 4 on his base, sitting at 85 overall. That is not the number that stops me. The number that stops me is the slot sheet. Richards is carrying L2C_ and P2C_ — second-line center, second power-play unit. He is producing like a top-six forward from a secondary deployment. There is a ceiling here and we have not found it.

At 24, with an 88 potential, he is the cheapest productive forward on the roster. $1.00M. 1 year left. I am now saying the words I swore I would never type in this building: the Leafs need to sign Brad Richards to an extension before this season is over.

Not before next season. Not before the trade deadline. Before this season is over, when there is still no reason to panic.

Mats Sundin87 is 33 and carries $8.40M and 35 points in 25 games. That contract has 5 years on it and a body that will not survive all of them. Richards at 85 and $1.00M is the bridge between Sundin's twilight and whoever comes next. The arithmetic is not complicated, which is what makes it so terrifying to watch a front office stare at it without flinching.

For context on the value: Alexander Mogilny86 has 39 points at $5.50M. Marian Gaborik94 has 34 points at $1.10M. Bryan McCabe80 has 35 points at $3.00M. Richards has 27 points at $1.00M. Nobody on this roster produces at a lower cost.

The team has 40 points in 25 games and leads the Northeast by 18. The Panic Meter reads Green and I resent it. But I will say this, once, quietly, without the usual caveats: Richards is good enough. He is here. He is ours. And his contract expires before the season ends.

The second-centre problem is solved. God help us with the salary-cap problem that follows it.
