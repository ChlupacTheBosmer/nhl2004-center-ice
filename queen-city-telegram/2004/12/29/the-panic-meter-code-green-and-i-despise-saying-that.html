THE PANIC METER: code Green, and I despise saying that

28-6-0, 217 goals scored, 103 against, 24 points clear in the division, and all I can grip about is a 4-0 win where we took 17 shots

Frankie DanforthColumnist · Queen City TelegramWednesday, 29 December 2004
ShareCopy link

The piece

Seventeen shots. That's what Toronto put on net in Edmonton, a team on a 3-game losing streak, and the score was 4-0. The Edmonton boys took 25 at Belfour. We won by four on fewer looks than the other team. I watched 60 minutes and I cannot tell you whether that's dominance or a mirage, and the fact that it ended in a shutout means nobody outside this desk is going to ask.

I am going to ask. Not because I think we lose to Vancouver on Friday. I think we beat Vancouver on Friday. I think we beat New Jersey twice next weekend. I think the 6-game streak becomes a 9-game streak and the table doesn't move. I am asking because 4 goals on 17 shots is a hot start that cools off, and when it cools off against a team that shoots 30 a night, the 4-0 becomes a 2-3.

Ed Belfour92 told The Tunnel's Jan Kowalczyk after the Calgary game that "the PK is my best friend right now," and that's the most honest sentence a 39-year-old goaltender has written. He doesn't need us to control the puck. He needs us to kill it clean so he isn't throwing himself across the crease for 60 minutes. He got 25 saves in Edmonton and walked off with a shutout. The hips can wait.

The numbers I cannot argue with

217 goals for. 103 against. A differential of +114 through 37 games. The Northeast is Ottawa at 38 points and we are at 62. That is 24 points of daylight in late December. I have wanted a second-centre answer since I was a kid with a pencil and a score sheet, and Brad Richards85 has 43 points at 24 years old for $1.00M with one year left on the contract. He is up 4 on the Bureau's Development Index. He is the answer. I am done asking.

Marian Gaborik96 is 96 on the Book, up 6 on the Index, with 55 points in 37 games at $1.10M. Rostislav Klesla82 is up 5 with 20 points out of the third pairing. The Development Index has four Leafs on the risers list this edition. The farm is not the future. It is the present.

Code Green

I am coding this Green. Not because the margins feel safe. Three of the last six wins came by one goal or went to overtime. The 17 shots bother me. The fact that I need a shutout to feel clean about a road win bothers me more.

But the division is not close, the crease has an answer tonight, and the second-centre problem I have logged since 2004 is solved by a 24-year-old making a million dollars. I cannot manufacture panic out of a 28-6-0 record and a +114 differential. Not honestly. Not this week.

The shooting dries up eventually. The back-to-back with New Jersey will test the legs. The January schedule has Boston, Pittsburgh, and whoever is left standing in the Northeast by then. There are games ahead where 17 shots loses.

Friday is not one of them.
