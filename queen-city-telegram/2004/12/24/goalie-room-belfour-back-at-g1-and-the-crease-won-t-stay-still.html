Goalie Room: Belfour back at G1, and the crease won't stay still

The second goalie shuffle since the three-way ended, with a five-game road trip opening Sunday

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramFriday, 24 December 2004
ShareCopy link

The crease at Toronto Maple Leafs flipped again. Ed Belfour93 is back at G1 and David Aebischer85 drops to G2, reversing the lineup the desk logged three days ago. This is the second goalie change since the three-way ended in mid-December.

Belfour is 39. He carries a 93 overall on the Book, has started 11 games, and makes $6.50M in the final year of his contract. Aebischer is 26, sits at 86 on the Book, has 19 starts, and carries a $0.50M cap hit. His morale reads 80, the lowest figure on the Toronto roster.

Neither goaltender appears on the Bureau's Development Index risers or fallers. Their form is steady relative to base. The coach still hasn't settled on an order. Mikael Tellqvist75 has 7 games, a 75 overall, and is a faller on the Index, down 3 from a base of 77. He is unassigned this week.

The road trip begins Sunday in Calgary. Calgary Flames is 10-20-3, last in the Northwest. Edmonton Oilers is 13-12-1. Vancouver Canucks is 18-10-4, with Alexander Auld75 now in net after taking the G1 slot from Dan Cloutier82 this week. The trip closes with back-to-back games against New Jersey Devils starting 2005-01-03. New Jersey has allowed 94 goals in 31 games, the lowest total in the league.

The Leafs are 10-3 away from home and carry 101 goals against in 35 games, the lowest total in the Northeast division.

Sami Salo82 [871] is back at L3RD after sitting the last edition. Robert Svehla84 returns to the top pairing. Carlo Colaiacovo72 and Kirill Safronov66 are healthy scratches. Konstantin Koltsov72 takes the L3LW spot from Rick Nash82.

Vaclav Spacek76 carries a form marker rather than an injury and has been available throughout.

---

Correction. An earlier edition of this piece read the league's roster designation as an injury. It is not one: the marker carried here is a form or fitness icon, and the men named were available. Only a designation that names a body part is an injury. The passage has been corrected.
