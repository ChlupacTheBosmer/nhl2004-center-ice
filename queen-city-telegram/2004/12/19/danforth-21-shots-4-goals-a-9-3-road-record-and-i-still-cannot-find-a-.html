DANFORTH: 21 shots, 4 goals, a 9-3 road record, and I still cannot find a centre

Gaborik is up 6 on the Index, the division lead is 19, and tonight Tampa Bay is on 4 straight wins.

Frankie DanforthColumnist · Queen City TelegramSunday, 19 December 2004
ShareCopy link

We won 4-2 in Florida on Saturday. We took 21 shots. They took 33. I do not know how to feel about that, and I have been watching this team for eleven years.

The road record reads 9-3. Mats Sundin88 [961] and Bryan McCabe83 [637] are tied for the team lead in points at 44. Marian Gaborik97 [308] leads the club with 26 goals and carries a 95 overall, up 6 from his base on the Development Index, the highest single jump on the Leafs' side of the ledger. He is 22 and he is the best player on this roster right now. Rick Nash82 [364] is up 5, Rostislav Klesla83 [491] is up 5, Brad Richards86 [832] is up 4. The young core is climbing in every column the Bureau publishes.

And I cannot enjoy it because the second centre is still Brad Richards [832] and the third centre is Petr Chlup83 [1000815], who is 18 and getting 11.8 minutes a night, and the fourth centre is Jason Spezza71 [227] at 8.3 minutes, and somewhere between Gaborik's 95 and Spezza's 8 minutes there is a gap shaped like a playoff series.

Tonight we go to Tampa Bay, who are on a 4-game win streak, 42 points in 31 games, and 11-7 on the road. They are not Atlanta. They are not Florida. They are a team that has found something in December and is riding it into a matchup with the best team in the East.

The schedule knows what it is doing

After Tampa Bay it is St. Louis at home, then Atlanta at home, then Calgary and Edmonton on the road. Two of those four teams have more points than the third-best squad in our division. The division lead is 19 points over Ottawa, which sounds enormous until you remember that Ottawa has played 3 fewer games and the calendar does not care about point differentials in December.

Klesla told The Tunnel's Marcus Bell after the Atlanta loss that "8-3 is good. We need to keep winning there." It is 9-3 now. Keep it going.

I am not worried tonight. The Index says the team is getting better, not worse. The crease question is answered. The division is 19 points wide. The only thing I am worried about is the thing I have been worried about since December 2004 began: who takes the draw when Sundin sits the box, and whether the answer is good enough in April.

The answer tonight is probably Richards. The answer in April needs to be better than Richards. That is the whole column.
