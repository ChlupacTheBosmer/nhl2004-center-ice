Goalie Room: Aebischer takes G1, and the Index does the arguing for him

Tellqvist drops to G2 after the Bureau puts him down 3. Belfour is undressed again.

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramSunday, 19 December 2004
ShareCopy link

David Aebischer83 [4] starts in net tonight at Tampa Bay Lightning. The move was made between Friday's loss in Atlanta and Saturday's win in Florida: Aebischer went from undressed to G1, Mikael Tellqvist72 [270] slid to G2, and Ed Belfour90 [55], undressed for the first time since returning from his knee, went back to the press box.

The numbers explain the change without much editorializing. Aebischer carries an 84 overall from the Bureau's Book, 17 games played, 57.6 minutes a night when he is in there, and a $0.50M cap hit with 1 year left. He is the cheapest reliable body on the roster.

Tellqvist started the season as G1 and held it through the Belfour injury. His overall now reads 73, and the Development Index has him 3 points below his base of 77. He has played 7 games at 54.3 minutes a night, the lowest average on the roster, which suggests partial games or pulls. His morale is 95. He is 25, and his potential sits at 91, so the long view in Toronto is that this is a setback, not a verdict.

Belfour is 39, carries a $6.50M cap hit, and has appeared in 10 games at 56.0 minutes a night. His overall of 91 is the highest on the roster. He is the third goalie on a team that leads the Northeast by 19 points, and the desk will keep asking whether that makes sense. The coach has answered by sitting him, which is one answer.

The three-way is over. The room has a starter, a backup, and a man in the press box earning the second-highest salary on the club. Whether Aebischer holds the job through a playoff push is a different question than who plays tonight, and tonight is the question the sheet answers.
