DANFORTH: the road starts Friday and I am not worried, and that scares me more

The away record is 8-3. The division lead is 19 points. The streak is dead. None of that is the problem. The problem is that I should be worried and I am not.

Frankie DanforthColumnist · Queen City TelegramFriday, 17 December 2004
ShareCopy link

Fifteen games and I was writing columns about the margin. 13-0, 9-2, 8-1. The margin was the story. Then 3-7 in Atlanta and the margin is the story again, just in the other direction. 19 shots to 30. A team with 7 wins. I have said what I think about that. I will not say it again in this column.

The road trip is Florida Panthers Friday, TBL Saturday. Florida has 11 wins in 30 games and is 5-10 on the road. Tampa has 16 and is on a 4-game win streak, 11-7 on the road. The away record is 8-3. I looked at that number and felt nothing. That is what scares me.

What I care about

I care about Mats Sundin91 at 33, 17.7 minutes a night, 5 years on his contract, $8.40M a year. I care that he has 42 points and that Marian Gaborik98 has 42 and costs $1.10M. I care that Brad Richards87 has 32 in 31 games on a 1-year deal and the Index has him at +4.

I do not care about the streak. The streak was a number. The number is gone. The record is 23-6 and the division lead is 50 to 31 over Ottawa Senators and I am not worried, and a columnist who is not worried is a columnist who is about to be wrong.

I care about the fourth line. I have said it for three weeks. The fourth line is a tax. Jason Spezza72 has 14 points at 8.6 minutes. Konstantin Koltsov73 has 14 at 8.0. Nikita Alexeev78 has 19 at 10.3. That is not a fourth line. That is a collection of names on a sheet. And when the other three lines go quiet, as they did in Atlanta, the fourth line is what is left, and 8.6 minutes is not enough to carry a team against a team that has 7 wins.

The crease

Mikael Tellqvist75 is back Friday. The Index has him at -3, below his base of 77. He is 25. He is supposed to be the answer. The answer has a -3 on it and I am not convinced. But the crease is settled. That is one thing. One.

The road trip is two games. The next five are FLA, TB, STL, ATL, CGY. Three of those five are on the road. The away record is 8-3. I am not worried. I should be. I am not.
