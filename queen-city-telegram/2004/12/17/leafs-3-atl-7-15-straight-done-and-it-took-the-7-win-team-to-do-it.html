LEAFS 3, ATL 7: 15 straight done, and it took the 7-win team to do it

Toronto lost its 15-game winning streak in Atlanta on Thursday, outshot 30-19 and outplayed for 60 minutes.

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramFriday, 17 December 2004
ShareCopy link

The Toronto Maple Leafs lost 7-3 in Atlanta on Thursday, ending a 15-game winning streak that had run since November 26. The shot count was 19 to 30, and the game was never in question after the first period.

Atlanta Thrashers has 7 wins in 30 games. Chicago Blackhawks has 6 in 31. That is the number that stings more than the scoreline: the league's second-worst team took the thing, and the margin was 4.

The streak had been 15 straight. The last ten before Thursday carried a 13-0 on Buffalo Sabres, a 9-2 on Pittsburgh Penguins, an 8-1 on New Jersey Devils, a 4-0 in New York Islanders, a 7-1 in Philadelphia Flyers, and an 8-6 at home against New York Rangers. The margin was the story for three weeks; every column and every beat note was about it.

Thursday, the margin went the other way. 19 shots for Toronto, 30 against. The score was 3-7. It was not a close game; Toronto was outplayed by a team with 7 wins.

The road starts Friday

Toronto travels to Florida Panthers on Friday and TBL on Saturday. The away record stands at 8-3. Florida has 11 wins in 30 games and is 5-10 on the road. Tampa has 16 wins and is on a 4-game win streak of its own, 11-7 on the road.

The standings do not move on a single loss. Toronto sits at 50 points in 31 games. Ottawa Senators has 31. The home record is 17-3. The goal differential is 186 for, 93 against.

The streak was a number, and the number is gone. The record, the division lead, the depth chart, the goal differential: none of those moved. What moved is the column: three weeks of "the streak hits 13" and "the streak is 15," and now "the streak is over" and the thing that stopped it has 7 wins.
