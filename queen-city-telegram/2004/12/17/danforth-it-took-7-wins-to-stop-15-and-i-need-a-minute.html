DANFORTH: it took 7 wins to stop 15, and I need a minute

The panic meter moves to Yellow. Not because of the loss, but because of who won.

Frankie DanforthColumnist · Queen City TelegramFriday, 17 December 2004
ShareCopy link

I have been writing about this streak for three weeks and I am tired. Fifteen games. The league's second-worst team walked into the Georgia Dome and took the thing. Atlanta Thrashers has 7 wins. Seven. I keep looking at that number like it is a typo.

The shot count was 19 to 30. No one can call that close. I do not have the goal-by-goal. I do not need it.

What I am mad about

I am not mad about the loss. I am mad about the pattern. We won 13-0 on Buffalo, 9-2 on Pittsburgh, 8-1 on New Jersey, and the column was about the margin.

This is the second-centre problem wearing a different hat. Mats Sundin88 has 42 points in 31 games. Marian Gaborik95 has 42 and costs $1.10M. Brad Richards84 has 32 on a 1-year deal. The top three are doing the work. When the other lines go quiet, a team that should not be able to win does, and they win ugly, and the shot count is 19 to 30 and the score is 3-7 and the thing that stopped you has 7 wins.

I want a draw-winning centre behind Sundin. I have said it for three weeks. The loss makes it a little louder.

The road

Friday is Florida Panthers, Saturday is TBL. The away record is 8-3. Florida has 11 wins. Tampa has 16 and is on a 4-game streak. The cushion is real, 50 points to 31 in the division, and one loss in December does not change the shape of this season.

But I am writing this column on a Friday morning and I am still staring at 7.

Panic Meter: Yellow. The streak is dead. The record is not. The margin was the story for three weeks and now the margin is the story again, just in the other direction, and I do not know which one I hate more.
