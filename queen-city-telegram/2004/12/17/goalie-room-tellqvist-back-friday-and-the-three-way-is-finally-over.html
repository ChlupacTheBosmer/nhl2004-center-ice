Goalie Room: Tellqvist back Friday, and the three-way is finally over

Mikael Tellqvist returns to the crease on Friday against Florida. The question that has run since Belfour's injury is settled.

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramFriday, 17 December 2004
ShareCopy link

Mikael Tellqvist73 is back. His return date is 2004-12-18, he is dressed, and he carries the G1 slot. The three-goalie experiment, which started when Ed Belfour went down and David Aebischer88 took over the crease, is over.

Belfour played 10 games before his injury, 56.0 minutes a night, $6.50M on a 1-year deal. Aebischer played 16, 57.5 a night, $0.50M. Tellqvist played 7 before going out, 54.3 a night, $0.80M. Three goalies, three workloads, one crease.

What the Index says

The Bureau's Development Index has Tellqvist at -3, below his base of 77. That is a fall. He is the only Toronto player on the fallers list, and the number matters: a 25-year-old who is supposed to be the long-term answer is reading below his overall. The Index tracks form, the distance between a player's current overall and his base. A -3 means the Bureau's Book has him 3 points below where he was at the start of the season.

Belfour, at 39, reads 91 on the Book. He is the better goaltender by a wide margin. The question was whether his body would let him. He has 10 starts this year. The contract is 1 year, $6.50M. The Jennings from last season is on the shelf.

Friday

Tellqvist starts against Florida Panthers on Friday. Belfour is G2. Aebischer, 16 games this season and a shutout against the Islanders on December 6, is not dressed. He has no slot. He is not on the injured list.

The crease has a name on it again. Tellqvist, 25, G1, 54.3 minutes a night last time out, -3 on the Index.

The next five games: FLA on the road, TB on the road, STL at home, ATL at home, CGY on the road. Tellqvist will start at least three of those five. The Index will tell you whether the -3 was a blip or a trend. The next five will tell you whether the three-way is really over or just paused.
