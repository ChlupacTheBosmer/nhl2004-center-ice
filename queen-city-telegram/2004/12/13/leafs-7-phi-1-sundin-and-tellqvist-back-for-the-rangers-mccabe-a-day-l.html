LEAFS 7, PHI 1: Sundin and Tellqvist back for the Rangers; McCabe a day later

The Rangers bring 44 points without Bure, Carter or Poti, and the only open question is the crease

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramMonday, 13 December 2004
ShareCopy link

The Leafs ran the streak to 14 with a 7-1 win in Philadelphia on Sunday, and the lineup that faces the Rangers at the Air Canada Centre tomorrow night is a fuller one. Mats Sundin86, listed with an unspecified injury in the 12-11 to 12-13 window, has a firm return date of tomorrow. Mikael Tellqvist73 is back tomorrow as well. Bryan McCabe81 is set for the day after, in time for the trip to Atlanta on 12-16.

Sundin rejoins the league's most productive attack with 40 points in 29 games, 17 of them goals, at 17.2 minutes a night. The Leafs have scored at least 7 in each of their last 3 games and stand at 175 goals for, 80 against. Brad Richards84 told The Tunnel's Jan Kowalczyk after the Devils game that Sundin "takes the hard matchups, he's out there on both ends, and he doesn't get loud about it. That's the stuff nobody sees and everybody rides on."

The Rangers are the closest the East has to a rival on the board. They hold first in the Atlantic at 44 points on a 2-game win streak, the only club within 4 of the Leafs' 48. They arrive thin: Pavel Bure81 (ankle, set for 2005-01-06), Anson Carter82 (knee, 12-26) and Tom Poti79 (rib, 12-17) are all out.

The crease resolves in part tomorrow. David Aebischer84, who carried 11 straight starts before the unspecified injury, still has no return date on the board. Tellqvist is back from three below his Book base at 52.0 minutes a night, and Ed Belfour91, 39, holds 10 starts at 56.0 minutes. The three-goalie rotation is the one open question on a club with none.

After tomorrow comes a road swing through the Southeast: Atlanta on 12-16, Florida 12-18, Tampa Bay 12-19, then St. Louis at home on 12-21. McCabe's 40 points in 29 games from the back line, at 23.2 minutes, return to the group for it.
