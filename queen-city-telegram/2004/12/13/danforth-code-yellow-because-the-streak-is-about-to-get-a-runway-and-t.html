DANFORTH: code Yellow, because the streak is about to get a runway and the crease still has three men in it

The Rangers are wounded, the Southeast is waiting, and the goalie who won 11 straight still has no return date

Frankie DanforthColumnist · Queen City TelegramMonday, 13 December 2004
ShareCopy link

The streak is 14, the Leafs sit atop the East at 48 points, and I cannot look away from a goal crease with three men in it and a schedule with one real test on it. David Aebischer85, the man who won 11 straight starts, has been rested. Mikael Tellqvist74 is available and three below his Book base. Ed Belfour92 is 39, with 10 starts and a $6.50M deal, and he is the closest thing this crease has to a certain hand.

One of those is a hockey game, and even that one shows up short-handed, no Pavel Bure89, no Anson Carter88, no Tom Poti85. The other four are a runway, and a streak this town has refused to stop announcing is about to get all the runway a club with 22 wins could want.

That is where I get nervous, and the man who knows best said it. After the 4-0 shutout of the Islanders, David Aebischer told The Tunnel's Jan Kowalczyk: "You win in December and nobody cares in April if you stop."

He was describing the room, and he was describing the calendar. The offence will score. The crease is the thing that decides Aprils, and the crease is the only thing on this club that is not decided.

Correction. An earlier edition dated a player's return from a roster marker that is not an injury. That date is when a form or fitness marker lapses, which no reporter could know, and it has been removed.
