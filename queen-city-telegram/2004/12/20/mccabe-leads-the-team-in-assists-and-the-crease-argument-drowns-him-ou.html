McCabe leads the team in assists and the crease argument drowns him out

32 assists from the blue line, 4 years left at $3.00M, and the only defenseman the coach trusts every single night

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramMonday, 20 December 2004
ShareCopy link

Bryan McCabe82 has 32 assists, more than any other Toronto Maple Leafs player. He is a defenseman. He is tied with Mats Sundin89 at 44 points for the team lead, and McCabe got there with 12 goals and 32 assists while Sundin got there with 20 goals and 24. The assist column is where McCabe's numbers separate from everyone else's on this roster, including Sundin's.

McCabe has played all 33 games and logs 23.1 minutes a night, the most of any Toronto player this season. The Toronto Maple Leafs blue line has been thin all month. Robert Svehla82, who won the Norris Trophy last season, has 14 points in 18 games and is not dressed. Sami Salo82 has 6 points in 15 and is not dressed either. Neither appears on the injured list. The coach is sitting them, and McCabe is the only defenseman who is dressed every night and playing top minutes against the other team's best line.

Klesla has 19 points and is up 5 on the Bureau's Development Index. Fischer, McCabe's partner, has 7 goals, which is more than any other defenseman on the roster. Kaberle has 17 on a $1.90M contract that expires after this season. Every one of those men has a legitimate case for the second-best defenseman on this team.

But McCabe is 44 points ahead of the second defenseman. He is 29, graded 81 overall in the Bureau's Book, and the Book's potential grade on him reads 78. He is playing above the ceiling the Bureau projected, signed for 4 more years at $3.00M. You do not find many defensemen with 44 points on that number.

Jiri Fischer told The Tunnel's Jan Kowalczyk after the Philadelphia game what it is like playing beside him: "Bryan, he go, he go all the time. So I stay back, I watch the play, I am the last man. He creates, and I just make sure we are safe."

When St. Louis Blues visits on Tuesday, the Blues bring a 26-point season, 101 goals for, and 133 against. Barret Jackman84 has 17 points in 31 games. McCabe has 44.
