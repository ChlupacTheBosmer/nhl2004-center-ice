LEAFS 9, PIT 2: the streak hits 13, and the Penguins never had a shift in it

Toronto pours in nine at the Air Canada Centre and runs the home mark to 16-3 ahead of Sunday's trip to Philadelphia.

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramSaturday, 11 December 2004
ShareCopy link

Toronto Maple Leafs beat Pittsburgh Penguins 9-2 at the Air Canada Centre on Friday night, a 13th straight win that leaves the Leafs at 21-5 on the season with 46 points.

The Penguins came in 8-12-4 and left the way most visitors leave this building. Toronto outshot them 32-27 and turned the game into a formality early.

It is the fourth game of this run in which the Leafs have scored eight or more. The count now reads: 12-3 over Philadelphia, 13-0 over Buffalo, 8-1 over New Jersey, and 9-2 over Pittsburgh. The season totals sit at 168 goals for and 79 against in 28 games.

Mats Sundin90 leads the club with 37 points, Alexander Mogilny89 sits on 42, and Marian Gaborik97 has 38, with 23 of them goals. The Book has Gaborik at 97 overall, up six on the Bureau's Development Index, the biggest riser in hockey.

The home record is 16-3. The road record is 7-2. Neither number has a soft patch in it.

Petr Chlup85, the 18-year-old centre, has 14 points in 17 games at 11.6 minutes a night, and Rostislav Klesla83 has climbed five on the Index to an 83 grade with 18 points from the back end.

The crease question

David Aebischer80, whose 16 appearances have come at 57.5 minutes a night, was rested this week, with the club pencilling him back in for Dec 12.

Mikael Tellqvist75, who has 4 appearances at 50.0 minutes, is the fallback behind Ed Belfour93, who has 10 starts at 56.0 minutes a night.

The Leafs visit Philadelphia Flyers on Sunday. The Flyers have lost five straight and sit last in the Atlantic at 9-12-3. Keith Primeau79 is out for Philadelphia with a rib injury until Dec 19. Then come the Rangers at home on Tuesday, with New York on a 16-8-4 mark that leads the Atlantic at 42 points.

The streak began Nov 19 with a 2-0 win over Boston. Every game since has been a win, and eight of the 13 have come by three goals or more.
