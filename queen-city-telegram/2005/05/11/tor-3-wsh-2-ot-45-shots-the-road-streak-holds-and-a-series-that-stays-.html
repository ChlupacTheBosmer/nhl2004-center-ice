TOR 3, WSH 2 (OT): 45 shots, the road streak holds, and a series that stays alive

Toronto wins its first overtime game of this spring on the road, with 45 shots and a goaltender playing his 15th straight playoff night.

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramWednesday, 11 May 2005
ShareCopy link

Roberto Luongo95 made 33 saves and Mats Sundin92's club found a way in the extra period. The Toronto Maple Leafs won 3-2 in overtime at Washington Capitals on 2005-05-10, cutting the series deficit to 2-1 with Game 4 on the same ice Tuesday.

Forty-five shots on goal against 33 for Washington, the most by either team in this series. The Toronto Maple Leafs outshot Washington Capitals for the second straight game and finally won one.

This was Toronto's first overtime victory in 3 extra-period games this spring. They lost Game 6 to Pittsburgh Penguins 2-3 in OT, lost Game 1 to Washington 2-3 at home in OT, and won tonight. That makes them 1-2 in overtime through 15 playoff games.

The series swings back to Washington's building. The Toronto Maple Leafs road record in this postseason reads 4-1. They beat New York Islanders at home, beat Pittsburgh Penguins twice on the road, lost one in OT at Pittsburgh Penguins, and won tonight in OT. Their only loss on the road came in overtime.

Washington's record at home this spring had been 4-0 before tonight. They beat Carolina Hurricanes twice and Tampa Bay Lightning twice in their own building. Toronto became the first team to win there in this postseason.

What the goals cost

Gary Roberts85 carries 13 points in 15 games this spring. Sundin has 12. Those two are well clear of the rest of Toronto's lineup. Alexander Mogilny89 sits at 9, and Marian Gaborik99 at 10 in only 8 games before his ankle shut him down.

Washington answers with Jaromir Jagr90 at 18 points in 11, Pascal Dupuis79 at 14, Robert Lang at 13, and Sergei Gonchar85 at 13. Four Washington players have more points than anyone on Toronto.

The regular season series went to Toronto, 4-2-1, with a 25-19 goal differential. In three playoff games, Washington leads 9-5 on goals.

Game 4 is 2005-05-12 at Washington. The Toronto Maple Leafs can even the series. The Washington Capitals can push it to 3-1 and put the season on the edge.
