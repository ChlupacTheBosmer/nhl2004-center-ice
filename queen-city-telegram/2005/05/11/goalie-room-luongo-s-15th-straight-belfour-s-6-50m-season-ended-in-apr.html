Goalie Room: Luongo's 15th straight, Belfour's $6.50M season ended in April

Fifteen starts, 2.17 goals against, .937 save rate. The backup on the bench is 23 and just left Washington.

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramWednesday, 11 May 2005
ShareCopy link

Roberto Luongo94 has started every playoff game for Toronto Maple Leafs. Fifteen starts, nine wins, six losses, 2.17 goals against, a .937 save percentage. The numbers are the best of any goaltender in this bracket who is still playing.

He played 45 games in the regular season at 60.9 minutes a night. He added 15 in the postseason. Sixty starts, plus preseason, plus the games he dressed for but did not play. At 26, the workload is what the desk watches.

Ed Belfour93 is not in the building. He is 40, carries a $6.50M salary for a one-year contract that is ending, grades 91 overall in the Bureau's Book, and his dressed status reads false. He played 37 regular-season games at 58.8 minutes a night. The last one was before the bracket opened.

The backup who left Washington

Maxime Ouellet82 is the man on the bench. Twenty-three, 80 overall, a 97 potential. He played 13 games for Toronto Maple Leafs in the regular season at 40.2 minutes a night. He carries the G2 slot and he has been on the bench for all 15 playoff games.

Ouellet left Washington on 2005-01-25. He faces his old club starting Tuesday. Karel Pilar69 left Toronto Maple Leafs on the same date and plays for Washington Capitals now. Two men traded on the same day, one in each direction, meeting in a conference final.

The 0-4 and what it means

Luongo gave up four goals on how many shots in Game 2 is not on the record. The team was shut out 0-4. No Toronto Maple Leafs goaltender has been pulled in this postseason. The record carries no goaltender changes in any of the 15 games.

Washington's two goaltenders have been the answer the Toronto Maple Leafs forwards have not found. Olaf Kolzig87 is 8-1 with a 1.48 and a .937 and one shutout. Josh Harding74 is 2-0 with a 1.00 and a .952. They have given up 5 goals in 11 games between them. Toronto has scored on them for 5 goals in 3 games.

Luongo's 2.17 is the only goaltending number in this series that looks like a fair fight. Everything else leans Washington's way.

The contract question is simple. Luongo has 2 years left at $1.60M. Belfour's deal is up. The cap math writes itself. The dressing-room math does not always.
