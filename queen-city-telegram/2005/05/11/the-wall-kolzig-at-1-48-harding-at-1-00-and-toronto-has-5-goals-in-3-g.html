The Wall: Kolzig at 1.48, Harding at 1.00, and Toronto has 5 goals in 3 games

Washington's two goaltenders have combined for a 10-1 record and nearly 15 shutout minutes per game. The Leafs need to find a way past one of them Tuesday.

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramWednesday, 11 May 2005
ShareCopy link

The piece

Olaf Kolzig89 entered this spring with a Vezina on the shelf. He has been better since. Eight wins, one loss, 1.48 goals against, a .937 save rate, one shutout, 11 games played.

The loss came in overtime to Toronto Maple Leafs on 2005-05-10. That is Washington's only game lost since the regular season ended.

Josh Harding83 is 20 years old, 2-0 with a 1.00 goals against and a .952 save rate. He sits +5 on the Bureau's Development Index, the second-highest rating for any Washington player. The shutout on Kolzig's line is the 4-0 in Game 2 against Toronto.

What 5 goals in 3 games looks like

The Toronto Maple Leafs scored twice in Game 1 (a 3-2 OT loss), zero in Game 2 (a 4-0 loss), and three in Game 3 (a 3-2 OT win). Five goals across three games against a tandem that has given up 7 total in 11 games.

Washington's forwards do not need volume. Jaromir Jagr90 has 18 points in 11. Pascal Dupuis79 has 14. Robert Lang has 13. Sergei Gonchar85 has 13. Four players at 13 or above. Toronto has two: Gary Roberts85 at 13 and Mats Sundin92 at 12.

The regular season says otherwise

The Toronto Maple Leafs beat Washington Capitals 4 times in 7 regular-season meetings, 25-19 on goals. Three of those seven games were decided by one goal. Toronto scored at will against this same Washington defence in October through April.

What changed: the ice is smaller, the pace is faster, and Washington's goaltending went from good to something else. Kolzig's regular-season numbers are not on this sheet. His playoff numbers are 1.48. That jump is the difference between a conference title and watching from home.

The path through

Mats Sundin needs 20.3 minutes and the power play. Gary Roberts at 20.0 has done most of the heavy lifting. Alexander Mogilny89 at 18.4 has 9 points in 15, fewer than expected from a man with 84 points in the regular season.

The third and fourth lines cannot outscore this Washington team. They have to keep the puck out of their own end and trust the top two units to find a seam. The 45 shots in Game 3 suggest the seams are there. The 5 goals in 3 games suggest Toronto is still looking for them.

Game 4 is 2005-05-12 at Washington. If Toronto scores more than two, the series holds. If the shutout happens again, this ends Thursday.
