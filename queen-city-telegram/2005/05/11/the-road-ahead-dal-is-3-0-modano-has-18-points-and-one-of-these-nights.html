The Road Ahead: DAL is 3-0, Modano has 18 points, and one of these nights someone plays in June

The Western Conference Final is one win from a sweep. The Presidents' Trophy team has outscored Anaheim 12-4 and its captain is having the spring of his career.

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramWednesday, 11 May 2005
ShareCopy link

Dallas Stars leads Mighty Ducks of Anaheim 3-0 in the Western Conference Final. Game 4 is 2005-05-12 in Anaheim. Dallas needs one win to reach the Stanley Cup Final for the second consecutive spring.

The series scoreline understates it. Dallas outscored Anaheim 4-1, 3-2 in overtime, and 5-1 across three games. Twelve goals for, four against. The Presidents' Trophy winner, 42-24-7 and 109 points, is playing like the best team left in the bracket.

Modano's spring

Mike Modano88 has 9 goals and 9 assists for 18 points in 13 playoff games. He has matched his point total against his games played. At 34, this is the postseason he has been chasing.

Brenden Morrow84 carries 16 points (3+13) in 13. Sergei Zubov83 has 13 (5+8). Jere Lehtinen83 has 13 (5+8) in only 9 games, the highest per-game rate of the four. Marty Turco94 is 11-2 with a 1.49 goals against and a .913 save rate.

Four Dallas players with 13 points or more. The Toronto Maple Leafs have two. Washington has four. In a Cup Final, the point distribution matters as much as the top man.

What the regular season says

Dallas and Anaheim met 8 times in the regular season. Dallas went 6-1-1 (+2 overtime losses), outscoring Anaheim 29-19. In the postseason, Dallas has made it look easier: 3-0, 12-4.

Anaheim's scoring: Sergei Fedorov90 has 16 points (11+5) in 15 games. Vaclav Prospal80 has 13 (4+9). Andy McDonald76 has 9 (7+2) in 12. The team that beat Colorado Avalanche and Edmonton Oilers to get here looks like a different club against Dallas. Jean-Sebastien Giguere97 is 7-6 with a 2.29 and a .872. The save rate is the worst of any goaltender still playing.

The matchup no one wants

If Dallas closes this out and the East takes its time, the Cup Final starts on short rest for one of these clubs. The Toronto Maple Leafs and Washington Capitals are 2-1 with four more games possible. Dallas could be resting for a week.

The Dallas Stars regular-season record against Eastern teams is not on this sheet. The record carries only divisional play. Dallas is 30-11 at home, 21-20 on the road. Whatever the rest looks like, they play Game 4 on the road tonight and then wait.

Modano has 18 points in 13 games. Toronto has been 13-7 in two series and needs at least two more wins to reach him. Washington has been 10-1 and needs at least one more win to reach him. Someone plays for the Cup in two weeks. The Presidents' Trophy team, with its captain at 18 points and its goaltender at 1.49, is the one with the most rest and the most goals against anyone in this bracket.
