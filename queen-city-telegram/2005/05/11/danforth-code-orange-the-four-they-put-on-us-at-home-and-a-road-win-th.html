DANFORTH: Code Orange, the four they put on us at home, and a road win that buys one night

Washington leads 2-1. The zero on the scoreboard at home is the number I cannot get past.

Frankie DanforthColumnist · Queen City TelegramWednesday, 11 May 2005
ShareCopy link

I want to talk about the four.

Not four points. Not four wins. Four goals. In our building. Against our goaltender. A zero on the other side. That was Game 2 of this series, and I have been staring at it for two days now and it still looks like a punishment.

The Toronto Maple Leafs won Game 3 in overtime at Washington Capitals and I am supposed to feel relief. Forty-five shots. A win in the extra period, the first of three tries this spring. The road record says 4-1 and I hear that, I do, but I also see 0-2 at home in this series and a 2-1 deficit against the best team in the East right now.

Code Orange. Not Red, because the season is alive and because Roberto Luongo97 gave us the third period and the overtime. Not Yellow, because you do not lose 0-4 at home against a 104-point team and get treated gently.

The centre I keep asking for

This is where I go. It is always where I go. Brad Richards89 has 66 points and a +4 on the Bureau's form measure and I am grateful for him the way I am grateful for a good windshield in a storm. It does not change what is driving.

Petr Chlup87 is 19 years old. Fifty-nine points in 71 games. He carries a second-centre slot and he has earned it. I will not take that from the kid. But I will say that Jaromir Jagr84 is collecting 18 points in 11 playoff games against our blue line while we ask a 19-year-old to slow him down, and I do not love the arithmetic.

Richards is up +4 on the Index. Chlup sits at base. Rick Nash85 is up +5, Barret Jackman86 up +5, Rostislav Klesla87 up +5. Five guys above their base, all under 25. That is the roster. That is what we have.

What 45 shots means

Forty-five shots means the engine is working. It means the possession numbers, the thing I have been writing about since the Pittsburgh Penguins series, are doing what they are supposed to do. It means Mats Sundin92 is driving and Gary Roberts87 is finding him and Alexander Mogilny89 is still getting his chances at 36.

It also means one game. One out of three. One out of a series where the other team scored four on us at our building and held us to zero. The 0-4 is the game that matters. Everything else is noise until we stop that from happening again.

Game 4 is Tuesday at their building. We are 4-1 on the road this spring. I know. I wrote it above. I also wrote 0-2 at home and I meant it more.

Code Orange. I am not moving off it until someone wins two in a row against this Washington team.
