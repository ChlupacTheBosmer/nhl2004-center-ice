DANFORTH: Code Green, the winger I want back on June 2, and the second centre I've been filing about since December

The Orange is gone. The meter is clear. I still have complaints.

Frankie DanforthColumnist · Queen City TelegramThursday, 19 May 2005
ShareCopy link

The piece

Green. The Panic Meter is Green for the first time since the first week of May. I've written Code Orange four times and Code Yellow three. Wednesday night, Roberto Luongo95 faced 37 pucks and did not let one cross the line, and the series ended 4-3, and I have nothing to complain about tonight except that I'm writing this in the past tense about a Game 7 I spent nine hours unable to watch from the press box.

The two centres

I logged the second-centre problem in December. I said we needed a draw-winning centre behind Mats Sundin92. Brad Richards87 finished the year with 66 points in 80 games at 25, a 16.1 minutes-a-night job that asked him to be the first centre on a cup-contender and the second on ours. Petr Chlup87 is 19 years old, scored 59 points in 71 games, and the Bureau's form measure put him six above his base when they last published it. That kid has 95 potential. If he keeps climbing, the problem I filed about in December solves itself without a trade.

Richards is a restricted free agent after this season. $1.00M in his pocket, one year of control left on the paper. I will say this until someone in a front office says it back to me: he is too good to lose for nothing and too expensive to keep at 32 minutes a night behind Sundin. Find him a second spot or find him a trade partner. Chlup deserves the minutes Richards takes.

The winger

99 points. 55 goals. A 98 overall. The second-best player on this roster, by any measure the Bureau keeps, sat home in a dress shirt during Games 5, 6 and 7 of the Eastern Conference Final. Marian Gaborik99 comes back on 2005-06-02. The winger who scored 10 points in 8 playoff games before an ankle ended his spring will be available for the Cup Final, and I don't care what anyone says about timing or conditioning or rhythm. He's here. Put him on the ice.

What we play for

Dallas Stars leads Mighty Ducks of Anaheim 4-1 in the West. The Presidents' Trophy winner has the deepest forward group in the bracket and a captain who scored 18 points before the second round. If the series holds, we play a club that won 109 points and buried four different opponents this spring. If Anaheim steals another game or two, we play a club that has survived three overtime games in this bracket and has nothing left to lose.

Either way, the Leafs are the team that played 19 playoff games, erased a 0-2 deficit and a 3-2 deficit, and closed a conference with a goaltender who stopped everything on the night that mattered. The winger comes home in 14 days. The draft is in 20. For once, I don't need the meter to tell me where we are.

It's Green. Let me enjoy the night.
