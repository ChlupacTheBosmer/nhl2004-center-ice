TOR 2, WSH 0: A shutout ends it at 4-3, and Toronto plays in June

Luongo stopped all 37 shots he faced in Game 7. The Leafs erase a 0-2 deficit and a 3-2 deficit to close the series.

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramThursday, 19 May 2005
ShareCopy link

The piece

Roberto Luongo95 faced 37 shots and did not yield one. The Maple Leafs beat Washington Capitals 2-0 at the Air Canada Centre on Wednesday night to win the Eastern Conference Final 4-3, and they will play for the Cup.

The game had no drama after the first period. Toronto scored twice, Luongo cleared the rest, and the building emptied its nervous energy into a 25-shot offensive output that was never enough to make this a rout but always enough to make it feel safe.

Toronto fell behind 0-2, then trailed 3-2 after Game 4, and won the last three by a combined score of 8-2. The road swing from 2005-05-10 through 2005-05-16 carried this club back from a 3-1 series hole, and the Game 7 shutout closed it without the tension the series had earned.

Luongo's spring

Luongo finished the playoffs 12-7 with a 1.98 GAA, a .942 save percentage and the one shutout. He played all 19 games across three series. The clean sheet on Wednesday was the first of his spring.

Olaf Kolzig89 finished 9-4 at 1.71 with a .926, and Josh Harding82 was 2-0 at 1.00 with a .952 across 7 games. Neither could hold when Toronto scored first in the final three games.

Scoring leaders

Mats Sundin92 led all Toronto scorers this spring with 9 goals and 6 assists for 15 points in 19 games. Gary Roberts85 followed with 4 goals and 10 assists for 14 in 19. Alexander Mogilny89 had 4 and 7 for 11. Marian Gaborik99, who played 8 games before an ankle ended his spring, posted 4 goals and 6 assists for 10 in that span.

For Washington Capitals, Jaromir Jagr89 finished with 5 goals and 16 assists for 21 points in 15 games. Pascal Dupuis78 and Robert Lang each scored 15 points.

What's next

The Western Conference Final is not yet settled. Dallas Stars leads Mighty Ducks of Anaheim 4-1 in the Western Conference Final with one game remaining on the bracket. The Presidents' Trophy winner outscored Anaheim 18-7 across the first four games before the lone Anaheim win in Game 4.

Toronto's 133 points and 59 wins this season sit behind only one Cup run the last two years. The draft is 20 days away. The winger comes home in 14.
