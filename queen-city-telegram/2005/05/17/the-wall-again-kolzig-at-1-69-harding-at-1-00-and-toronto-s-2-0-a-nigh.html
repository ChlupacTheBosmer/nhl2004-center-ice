The Wall, again: Kolzig at 1.69, Harding at 1.00, and Toronto's 2.0 a night without 99 points

The goaltending math of this series and the one save that made Tuesday's win possible.

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramTuesday, 17 May 2005
ShareCopy link

Olaf Kolzig87 entered the spring with a Vezina Trophy. He has a 1.69 goals-against average in 12 starts this postseason, a .926 save rate, and one shutout. Josh Harding74, 20 years old, is 2-0 with a 1.00 and a .952 in two appearances. Two extra starts don't account for the difference between them.

Roberto Luongo94 has 18 starts, 11 wins, a 2.09 goals-against average, and a .939 save rate. He is 26. Ed Belfour93 is 40 and his season ended in April with a 37-start log. The workload question that followed Belfour all February and March is now moot. It is Luongo or Maxime Ouellet82, who was Washington's until January.

The shot disparity

Tuesday's game: Toronto 18 shots, Washington 38. That is a gap of 20 shots and the score was 2-1 Toronto. Luongo stopped 37 of 38.

In this series, Toronto has 12 goals in 6 games against a Washington Capitals defence and goaltending pair that has allowed 14 in the same stretch.

Three games in this series were decided by one goal: Game 1 (2-3 OT), Game 3 (3-2 OT), and Game 6 (2-1). The others had margins of two goals or more.

What Game 7 requires

Jaromir Jagr84 has 21 points in 14 games this spring. Sixteen of them are assists. That means Washington's goals come from the second and third men, from behind the net, from the point, from the slot. Luongo has been a .939 goaltender for 18 nights and Kolzig has been a .926 one for 12.

Toronto shot 18 on Tuesday. The regular season gave them 381 goals in 82 games, which means the offense exists without Marian Gaborik98 and his 99 points on the shelf until June 2. The problem is the shot total. Toronto has managed fewer than 20 shots twice in this series. They have not lost either time. Game 7 at Toronto Maple Leafs on May 18 is the night that decides whether the repeat runs or the draft is 22 days away and nothing else matters.
