DANFORTH: Code Yellow, the road that saved this season, and a Game 7 I cannot explain with a 2.0 a night

Two one-goal wins on the road to force Game 7. The winger is home until June. I am allowed to be afraid and hopeful at the same time.

Frankie DanforthColumnist · Queen City TelegramTuesday, 17 May 2005
ShareCopy link

I wrote Code Orange four times in ten days. I am writing Code Yellow now. Not because I feel safe. Because I feel what a man feels when the building is full for Game 7 and the winger who should be on the ice is in a rehab room with a date of 2005-06-02 on his file.

I have been saying this since March. The Mats Sundin92 slot behind him was never filled. Brad Richards87 has 66 points in 80 games and he is 25 years old and this is the most he has carried. Petr Chlup87 is 19 and has 59 points in 71 games. Neither of them is the man I wanted. The winger who was supposed to close this out, Marian Gaborik99, has 99 points on the year and his ankle says June.

But here is what I got wrong and what I did not.

I was wrong that this team folds when it has no answers. Game 6, Tuesday, Washington: 18 shots. Eighteen. Against a Washington club that put 38 on the other side. We scored 2. They scored 1. Roberto Luongo95 is 11-7 this spring with a .939 save rate and he is 26 years old and Belfour is home with his $6.50M and his 40 years and his season is over and that is fine. I want Luongo in the net for as long as this goes.

Game 3 in Washington was 3-2 in overtime. Game 6 was 2-1. One-goal wins on the road. The season series says 6-3-1 to us, 32-24 on goals. In the bracket we have managed 12 goals in 6 games. The winger accounts for the difference. He had 55 goals in 82 games. I do not have 55 goals from anywhere else.

What I did not get wrong: I said the second centre was dead. I said the fourth line was a tax. I said the team that wins the Cup needs a player who can take draws behind Sundin and score when the first line is smothered. That is still true. Gary Roberts85 is 38 and has 14 points in 18 games. He is carrying this club and I love him and I do not know how much longer that holds.

What Game 7 asks

Washington Capitals has Jagr84 with 21 points in 14 games. They have Dupuis with 9 goals. They have Lang with 8. They have Gonchar with 14 points from the back end. They have Kolzig at 1.69 and Harding at 1.00 in 2 starts. We have had 4 goals from four different men in one game once this series. That was Game 5 at home, the 4-1. Before that, we lost 0-4 at home. Two games ago at home, the same building, the same crowd: four goals against.

I cannot explain why Wednesday will be different. I can tell you that 133 points in the regular season means something. I can tell you that we went 33-8 at home. I can tell you that in this series at home we are 1-2, and the one win was the 4-1, which was the best night Toronto has had all spring.

Code Yellow means I believe it can happen. It does not mean I have slept. The draft is 22 days away. If we lose this, I spend June drafting a second centre. If we win it, I spend June drafting a second centre because I still will not have one. Either way I am right. That is not consolation.
