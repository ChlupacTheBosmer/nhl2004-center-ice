Game 7 at home: 133 points, no winger, and the two wins that bought this night

The Leafs forced a Game 7 with consecutive one-goal road wins. Gaborik's return date is 2005-06-02. The season series says 6-3-1.

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramTuesday, 17 May 2005
ShareCopy link

Mats Sundin91 has 14 points in 18 playoff games. Jaromir Jagr84 has 21 in 14. That is the top of each scoring column heading into Game 7 of the East Conference Final on Wednesday night at Toronto Maple Leafs.

The Leafs won 2-1 on Tuesday in Washington on 18 shots against 38. That is the third straight one-goal result in this series and Toronto's second consecutive road win. The first came in overtime in Game 3. Neither was supposed to be possible with the 99-point winger watching from home.

Marian Gaborik98 has not played since Game 3 against Pittsburgh Penguins on April 26. His ankle, designated by the league between April 26 and April 28, carries a return date of 2005-06-02. He is not coming back for this series. In 8 playoff games he logged 4 goals and 6 assists. Without him, Toronto has produced 2 goals in Game 1, none in Game 2, 3 in Game 3, 1 in Game 4, 4 in Game 5, and 2 in Game 6. That is 12 in 6 games, a pace of 2.0 a night against a team whose goaltender has a 1.69 goals-against average this spring.

The regular-season record between these clubs reads 6-3-1 to Toronto, 32 goals for and 24 against. In the bracket it reads 3-3, 12 for and 14 against.

The goaltending matchup

Roberto Luongo94 has started 18 games this spring, 11-7, 2.09 goals against, .939 save rate. Olaf Kolzig87 has started 12 for Washington Capitals at 9-3, 1.69, .926, with one shutout. Behind him Josh Harding74 is 2-0, 1.00, .952 in 2 appearances. Harding was the last man Toronto scored against in Game 4 (3-1 Washington on May 12). In Game 6 on Tuesday, Kolzig gave up 2 on 18 shots.

Toronto had 18 shots on Tuesday. It had 38 against. Luongo was the reason the scoreline reads 2-1 and not wider. The Leafs have been better on the road than at home this spring: 5-3 away against 6-4 at home. In this series specifically, they are 2-1 on the road and 1-2 at home. Game 5, the 4-1 blowout, was the home win. Games 1 and 2 were losses.

Who faces whom

Maxime Ouellet82, the backup goaltender dressed for Toronto, was traded from Washington on 2005-01-25. Karel Pilar69 is on the Washington side and was traded from Toronto on the same date. Neither has appeared in this series.

What the sheet says a win requires

Jagr has 21 points, including 16 assists. Pascal Dupuis74 has 9 goals. Robert Lang75 has 8 goals. Sergei Gonchar78 has 14 points from the blue line. Washington has four players in double-digit points in this bracket; Toronto has three. The 4-1 win in Game 5 came with 4 goals from four different men. The 2-1 in Game 6 came from fewer. The pattern since the sweep of Carolina: Washington Capitals has scored 14 goals in 6 games against a Toronto defence that allowed 190 all season. Toronto has scored 12.

The puck drops Wednesday. Sundin is still the first centre. The winger who plays next to him is not Gaborik.
