Game 7 at Dallas: two missing Stars, three forwards on fire, and 2 goals in 3 road games

The head-to-head this season reads 5-1-1 in the regular series and 4-2 in the Final. The defence has allowed 8 goals in 6 games.

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramTuesday, 31 May 2005
ShareCopy link

The Toronto Maple Leafs season against Dallas Stars closes tomorrow night. The next game of the Stanley Cup Final is 2005-06-01 in Dallas, with the Toronto Maple Leafs leading 4-2 after a 4-2 win in Game 6 on 2005-05-30.

Roberto Luongo97 has appeared in every Toronto Maple Leafs playoff game this spring. 16-9, a 1.81 GAA, a .943 save percentage, 3 shutouts. Two came on the Dallas ice. Game 1 produced a 2-0 road win; Game 5 produced another. The home side has 2 goals total in 3 Final games played in Dallas.

Marian Gaborik99, the club's highest-paid player at $11.90M over 5 years, returns from his ankle injury on 2005-06-02, the day after Game 7. He went down in the second round against Pittsburgh Penguins, between 2005-04-26 and 2005-04-28.

Dallas Stars will be without Claude Lemieux74 (wrist, back 2005-06-06) and Todd Simpson63 (groin, back 2005-06-18). Neither man returns before the Final is over. The Dallas Stars season home record was 30-11; the Final has trimmed it to 29-12.

Radek Dvorak80, 28, has filled the winger role through the Gaborik absence. 11 goals, 7 assists, 18 points in 12 playoff games, on top of 36 goals and 53 assists in 69 regular-season games. He carries a 2-year deal at $3.20M.

Three forwards carrying the spring

Gary Roberts85, 39, leads the club with 19 playoff points in 25 games. Mats Sundin92 has 18 points. Alexander Mogilny89 has 17.

The regular-season record against Dallas reads 5-1-1, 20-11 in goals. The Final has added 4-2, 14-8. The Toronto Maple Leafs defence has allowed 8 goals across 6 games.
