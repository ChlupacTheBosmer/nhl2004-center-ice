LEAFS 4, DAL 2, series 4-2: 4 goals, 33 shots, and Game 7 goes to Dallas

Toronto's highest goal total of the Final closes Game 6. One win left on the road.

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramTuesday, 31 May 2005
ShareCopy link

Gary Roberts85 put his stick on one early and the Toronto Maple Leafs scored 4 goals for the first time in this Final. A 4-2 win over Dallas Stars on 2005-05-30 put Toronto ahead 4-2 in the series. Game 7 is 2005-06-01 at Dallas Stars.

The 33 shots Toronto took, against 18 for Dallas Stars, matched the 4-goal output. Their three previous wins came by scores of 2-0, 3-1 and 2-0.

The margin pattern held through all 6 games. Every Toronto Maple Leafs win has come by 2 goals. Every Dallas Stars win, including a 3-2 overtime game on 2005-05-24, has come by 1.

Roberto Luongo94 sits at 16-9 with a 1.81 goals-against average, a .943 save percentage and 3 shutouts in 25 playoff starts. He faced 18 shots in Game 6.

Mats Sundin92 leads Toronto with 11 playoff goals and 18 points. Roberts is first in points at 19. Alexander Mogilny89 has 17 and Jiri Fischer84 has 11 points from the blue line.

The Toronto Maple Leafs finished 5-1-1 against Dallas Stars in the regular season, outscoring them 20-11. The Final has been a different contest, but Toronto has not trailed in a game since 2005-05-24.

Marian Gaborik99 scored 55 goals and 44 assists this season. He is out with an ankle injury and not expected back until 2005-06-02, which means Toronto's best forward in this series will watch Game 7 from the bench.
