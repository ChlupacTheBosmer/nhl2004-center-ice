DANFORTH: Code Green, the $1.10M winger watching Game 7, and the centre nobody is fixing

One win from a repeat. The column still has problems it cannot put down.

Frankie DanforthColumnist · Queen City TelegramTuesday, 31 May 2005
ShareCopy link

Toronto is one win from the Stanley Cup and I will allow myself about 18 hours of it.

The Toronto Maple Leafs lead the Final 4-2. Game 7 is 2005-06-01 at Dallas Stars. Roberto Luongo95 is 16-9 with a 1.81 goals-against average, a .943 save percentage and 3 shutouts in 25 playoff starts. Gary Roberts85 has 19 playoff points. Mats Sundin92 has 11 goals in 25 games at 34 years old. I will give this group what it has earned.

What I cannot let pass is the view of Game 7 from the dressing room. Marian Gaborik99 scored 55 goals and 44 assists this season for $1.10M on a contract with 1 year left. His ankle keeps him out until 2005-06-02. He is 23, and he is not on the ice for the most important game this club has played in two years. The same $1.10M buys a player who dresses fourth-line minutes and kills a shift on the third power-play unit.

Brad Richards87 scored 66 points at $1.00M and has 1 year left. He is 25. He is good. I have been filing about this since December: 66 points behind a captain carrying 20.3 minutes a night and 91 points of his own is not a second-center situation I trust in June. Richards on an expiring deal at $1.00M means you are looking at a restricted-free-agent conversation in the fall, or you are looking at someone else.

The draft is 8 days away, on 2005-06-08. Petr Chlup87 is 19, sits at 86 on the Bureau's Book, and plays center. He is already in the lineup. Good sign, but not the answer to a question that has been open on this desk since the middle of the last calendar year.

The Panic Meter sits at Code Green. Luongo gives them 18 saves, Roberts finds one more loose puck, and Toronto lifts the Cup on 2005-06-01. Then the season starts on 2005-10-03 and every problem I have been filing about since December is back on the first page.
