DANFORTH: one day too late, and a goalie worth more than $1.60M

Gaborik's ankle clears June 2. The Final is on the line June 1. I have made my peace with that gap, and I don't like that I have.

Frankie DanforthColumnist · Queen City TelegramTuesday, 31 May 2005
ShareCopy link

Marian Gaborik99's return date is 2005-06-02. Game 7 is 2005-06-01. One day. Five years at $11.90M, the largest contract on this roster, and the night this franchise has waited for is the night he can't dress.

He's been out since late April. An ankle, somewhere between 2005-04-26 and 2005-04-28. The third round and this entire Final have been played without the best player on the team, and the Leafs are up 4-2.

Roberto Luongo95 has been in goal for all 25 Toronto Maple Leafs playoff games. 16 wins, 9 losses, a 1.81 GAA, a .943 save percentage, 3 shutouts. Two came in Dallas, on the road, in games the Leafs had to win to steal the series. He plays on a 1-year deal at $1.60M and a .943 playoff save percentage is not a number that survives October.

Gary Roberts85 is 39. 19 points in 25 games. I have written all season that the fourth line is a tax and I need to sit down because that man is not a fourth-liner. He's the second line's engine and the power play's backbone and the reason I reread my own December columns and feel stupid.

Radek Dvorak76 has been the third man in this stretch. 11 goals, 7 assists, 18 points in 12 games after 36 and 53 and 89 in 69 regular-season games. His morale reads 87, the lowest figure on the roster, which tells you what it feels like to play a supporting role after putting up 89 points. The winger slot next to Mats Sundin92 is a problem I've been filing about since December. Dvorak, 28 and on a 2-year deal at $3.20M, has answered it with the quiet competence of a man who knows he's a solution for tonight and not for next October. That's a July problem. Tonight is a May problem.

The regular-season record against Dallas Stars reads 5-1-1, 20 goals for, 11 against. The Final adds 4-2, 14-8. You can read this season any way you want and Toronto has been the better team against this club, and Dallas hasn't scored more than 3 in a single game in this Final.

I want it. I want it the way I haven't wanted anything since before the last Cup, which means I'm scared, which means when the clock hits zero tomorrow I will feel something I haven't felt in years. And then Marian Gaborik arrives at the rink on June 2 with a ring and four years left at $11.90M and I'll be right back here, wondering whether the team that won without him is the team that has to keep winning without him.

The draft is in 8 days. Petr Chlup87 is 19, scored 59 points in 71 games this season, and his contract expires. That's a June problem. Tonight is a May problem, and the May problem is Roberto Luongo and a 1.81 GAA and one more win in a building that has managed 2 total goals in 3 home games.

Code Green.
