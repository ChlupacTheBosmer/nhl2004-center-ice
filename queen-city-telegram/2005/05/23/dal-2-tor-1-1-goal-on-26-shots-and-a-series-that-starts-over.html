DAL 2, TOR 1: 1 goal on 26 shots, and a series that starts over

Dallas ties the Final at 1-1 on a night Toronto outshot them and scored once

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramMonday, 23 May 2005
ShareCopy link

Dallas took Game 2 of the Stanley Cup Final 2-1 on Saturday night at the American Airlines Center, and the series is tied at 1-1.

Toronto fired 26 shots. Dallas answered with 25. Dallas put 2 of theirs past Roberto Luongo97. Toronto put 1 past Marty Turco95. The Leafs have 3 goals through 2 games in this Final, against a regular season that produced 381 in 82.

Same as the last two rounds. Luongo has started every game this spring: 13 wins, 8 losses, a 1.89 goals-against average, a .943 save percentage, and 2 shutouts. He faces every shot in this series and gave up 2 on 25 Saturday.

Mike Modano86 leads all Dallas skaters with 20 playoff points in 17 games. Brenden Morrow84 has 18 in 17, and Sergei Zubov82 has 15 in 17. That trio has been the deepest forward group in these playoffs. Game 2 produced their goals on a smaller shot total than Toronto managed.

Toronto's offense rests on Mats Sundin92 at 16 points in 21 playoff games, Gary Roberts87 at 15, and Alexander Mogilny89 at 11. Marian Gaborik99, the club's leading scorer with 99 points in 82 regular-season games, has 10 in 8 playoff games before his ankle ended his spring. His return date is 2005-06-02. The latest this series can reach is 2005-06-01.

Game 3 goes Tuesday at the Air Canada Centre, where Toronto went 33-8 in the regular season. The next two are on this ice before the series shifts back to Dallas on 2005-05-28.
