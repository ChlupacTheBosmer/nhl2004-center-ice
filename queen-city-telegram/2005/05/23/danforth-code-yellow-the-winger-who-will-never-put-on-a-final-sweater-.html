DANFORTH: Code Yellow, the winger who will never put on a Final sweater, and 1 goal on 26 shots

The winger's ankle gets him back the day after the season ends, and I called Game 2 wrong

Frankie DanforthColumnist · Queen City TelegramMonday, 23 May 2005
ShareCopy link

I called Game 2 on Saturday. I said Toronto wins. Dallas won 2-1. The prediction desk logged it. 52 right, 13 wrong, and Saturday goes in the wrong column.

The loss does not bother me. What produced it does. One goal on 26 shots. Dallas had 25 and scored twice. Roberto Luongo94 has been in every crease this spring: 13-8, 1.89 goals against, .943, 2 shutouts. He deserves more than 1 goal of protection on a night like that.

Here is the sentence I did not want to write in May of a Cup Final. Marian Gaborik98 scored 55 goals this season, 99 points in 82 games, 20.5 minutes a night, $1.10M. His ankle gets him back on 2005-06-02. The last game this series can reach is 2005-06-01.

He will not play in the Final. Not if it goes 5 games. Not if it goes 7. The Bureau's Development Index has him at +6, the highest riser on our roster. The man with more goals than anyone else in the league is in a boot, and the boot comes off after the season ends.

What we have instead. Mats Sundin91 has 16 playoff points in 21 games. Gary Roberts88 has 15. Alexander Mogilny90 has 11. Brad Richards87 scored 66 in the regular season and sits 4 above his base on the Index, the second-highest form reading on this club after Gaborik. Good numbers from good players. None of them scored 55.

Petr Chlup86 is 19 and has 59 points. I have been filing about the second-centre vacancy since December 2004. He filled it. That thread is closed. Tonight's problem is a different forward.

The road and the building

The series is 1-1. Two games at the Air Canada Centre next, where this team went 33-8 between October and April. Luongo was the difference in Game 1. He made Game 2 a 1-goal loss instead of something uglier. The forwards gave him 1 goal on 26 shots. The winger is in a boot.

Code Yellow.
