DANFORTH: Code Green, the Cup I still don't believe in, and the centre who will be missing in October

Toronto is one win from a repeat, and nothing about how this team was built changes on the morning after

Frankie DanforthColumnist · Queen City TelegramSunday, 29 May 2005
ShareCopy link

Code Green. I am writing that sentence without gripping the underside of my desk. Toronto leads the Final 3 games to 2, plays at home, has won 3 straight, and has allowed 6 goals in 5 games against the best offense in the Western Conference.

I was wrong about Game 2. I was wrong about Game 3 at home in overtime. I called those the way I call everything: with certainty and a bad read on the goaltender on the ice. I have been wrong about this team for three months and the team has been too busy winning to notice.

The road that saved everything

Toronto lost Game 3 at the Air Canada Centre in overtime and the building was silent. Then Roberto Luongo97 shut out Dallas Stars twice in 9 days on the road, both 2-0, both while the Leafs held the puck for stretches that should not have worked against Mike Modano84 and Brenden Morrow82. Toronto won Game 5 on 36 shots to 15. That is a team that stopped looking at the scoreboard.

I have been writing about the second-centre problem since December 2004. Petr Chlup87 is 19 years old with 10 points in 24 playoff games and a potential of 95 on the Bureau's Book. Jason Spezza73 is 21 and hasn't dressed. Brad Richards89 has 66 points on a $1.00M salary and a one-year contract. Whether Toronto lifts a Cup tomorrow or Tuesday, Richards's contract still expires. The second centre still isn't a long-term answer. The fourth line is still a tax.

What I want

I want a draw-winning centre behind Mats Sundin92 and I have wanted one since I could hold a pencil. Sundin is 34, carries 20.3 minutes a night, and is 18 points through 24 games this spring. His contract runs 5 more years. None of those facts change because a banner goes up.

I also want Marian Gaborik99 in the lineup. His return date is June 2. The rookie draft is June 8. Free agency closes July 28. If the Leafs close this out tomorrow, Gaborik watches it from a training-room table in a suit, same as me.

Code Green means I think they win tomorrow. I have not been comfortable since April, and I suspect comfort is not what this desk is for.
