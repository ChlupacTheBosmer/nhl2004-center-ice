DANFORTH: Code Yellow, the one-goal wall, and the fact that I keep being wrong about this

Toronto has lost 2 straight one-goal games at home in the Final. I called Game 2. I got it wrong. I'm still not panicking, but I can explain the feeling.

Frankie DanforthColumnist · Queen City TelegramWednesday, 25 May 2005
ShareCopy link

I predicted Toronto wins Game 2 on May 22. Dallas won it 2-1. The desk logged the miss and the miss is mine. A single wrong call on a team that has been losing one-goal games all week is still a wrong call.

The Maple Leafs have lost 2 straight games in which the margin was one goal. They are 5 goals for, 5 against in this Final. Sundin has 17 points in 22 games and cannot make the scoreboard move by himself on nights when the other team's captain has 22.

Panic Meter: Code Yellow. I'll tell you what it would take to go Orange.

A loss tomorrow makes it 3-1 Dallas. Two on the road. You are then watching a club that beat Washington in seven and survived Pittsburgh in seven, with the best record in the league all season, trying to dig out of a 3-1 hole against a team with the best goaltender in the postseason by win-loss record. Turco is 14-4. Fourteen and four. I cannot write those digits without wincing.

The winger question again

I have filed about Marian Gaborik99's absence since December. He sits at 99 points, 55 goals, 44 assists in 82 games and he is in a walking boot until June 2. The Final ends June 1 at the latest. He will not play.

I could deal with the injury. The problem is a team that built its season around a top two in scoring on the same roster, and one of them is watching from the press box while the other carries 17 points through 22 games. I logged the second-centre problem in December because the sheet told me to. I log this now because the sheet tells me again.

What I want to see tomorrow

Fifty-eight shots like Washington faced in Game 7 would be a start. Toronto put up 40 and lost. Forty is not bad. Forty-two is better. Forty-five is what a team does when it knows the margin is one and wants more than one shot's worth of cushion.

I will not tell you to relax. I will not tell you to burn the sweater. The team that won 133 games and outscored people 381-190 is one game from tying the Final at home with a building that went 33-8. That is enough reason to sit in Code Yellow and not higher.

But if Turco wins again and the scoreboard reads 3-1, we have a different conversation. And I will have a different number on the Meter.
