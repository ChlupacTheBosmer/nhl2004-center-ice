Final, Game 3: 43 shots, 2 goals, and the series flips

Dallas outshot Toronto in overtime at the Air Canada Centre. The Final is 2-1 Dallas heading into Game 4 tomorrow night.

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramWednesday, 25 May 2005
ShareCopy link

The piece

The Dallas Stars took Game 3 of the Stanley Cup Final 3-2 in overtime on Thursday night at the Air Canada Centre. Dallas fired 43 shots. Toronto Maple Leafs fired 40.

That makes the series 2-1 Dallas. The goal total sits even at 5-5. Every game so far has been one goal.

The goaltending math

Roberto Luongo97 carries 13 wins, 9 losses, a 1.92 GAA and a .943 save percentage through 22 games this spring. Marty Turco93 sits at 14-4, a 1.49 GAA, .921. Toronto has the GAA and the save percentage. Dallas has the wins, and Turco has lost 4 games to Luongo's 9.

Toronto's defense has kept games tight. Dallas's wins are 2-1 and 3-2 in overtime. Neither goaltender has been pulled.

What the shot table says

Toronto outshot Dallas 40-43 in Game 3 and lost. That reverses the pattern from Game 2, where the Maple Leafs generated fewer shots and scored once. In this series Toronto has fired 40 or more in each game. Dallas has scored 0, 2, and 3.

Mats Sundin92 leads all scorers this spring with 17 points (11 goals, 6 assists) in 22 games. Mike Modano84 has 22 points (10, 12) in 18. The gap is 5 points across 4 fewer games for Dallas's captain. Brenden Morrow82 adds 18 for Dallas. Toronto's second-highest point total belongs to Gary Roberts85 at 15.

Marian Gaborik99 will not play in this Final. His ankle keeps him out until 2005-06-02. The Final ends no later than 2005-06-01. He finished with 99 points in 82 regular-season games and 10 in 8 before the injury.

Game 4

Toronto gets another home date tomorrow. A win ties the Final at 2-2. A loss puts Dallas 3-1 with two on the road and one back at home. The Maple Leafs went 33-8 at home this regular season. They are 2-1 in this Final at home.
