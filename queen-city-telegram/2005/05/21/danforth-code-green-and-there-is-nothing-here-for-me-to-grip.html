DANFORTH: Code Green, and there is nothing here for me to grip

The winger is in a boot, the second centre is 19, the fourth line is a tax, and the club just shut out the Presidents' Trophy team on the road. The Meter is clear. I hate it.

Frankie DanforthColumnist · Queen City TelegramSaturday, 21 May 2005
ShareCopy link

The Panic Meter is Green

The Panic Meter is Green. I want to say that up front because I know you're waiting for the catch. There isn't one tonight. Toronto won 2-0 at Dallas. Roberto Luongo94 stopped all 21 shots. The Modano line, 53 playoff points between three forwards, scored zero. The Toronto Maple Leafs outshot a 30-11 home club 31-21. Everything worked. I am filing a Green and I am filing it with a jaw that doesn't want to unclench.

What the club is missing

Marian Gaborik98 has 99 points this season. Fifty-five of them are goals. He is out with an ankle until June 2. He has 10 points in 8 playoff games because the ankle arrived in late April. He plays on a $1.10M contract. Three Toronto fourth-liners carry the same figure. I wrote about that in April and nothing about the arithmetic has changed.

The second centre behind Mats Sundin91 is Petr Chlup86, 19 years old, 59 points, $2.34M, 2 more years on the deal. He is 19 and he is in the Cup Final and he is having a fine season and I have been saying since December that this club needs a draw-winning centre and he is not that man yet. The Index has him +6, which means the form reads six above the Book's base. They start above their base at 19 and the rest of the league finds the seam.

The fourth line is still a tax. Rick Nash83, 20, 13.1 minutes, $1.20M. Scottie Upshall79, 21, 7.9 minutes, $1.20M. Stanislav Chistov82, 22, 13.1 minutes, $1.10M. Four players at the bottom of the roster eating first-round minutes against a club whose first line has 53 points. I said the fourth line is a tax. It is still a tax. Tonight it just didn't cost anything.

The men carrying it

Mats Sundin is 34 with 5 years and $8.40M a year left. He has 16 points in 20 playoff games. He has not missed a shift. The club is up 1-0 in the Cup Final because he carried it here and he will carry it to the next building. I trust him completely. That sentence does not mean I trust the rest.

Gary Roberts88 is 38. He has 15 points in 20. He is a free agent after this season. The Toronto Maple Leafs won the Conn Smythe last spring on his back and tonight he was the second man on the scoresheet by production. He will not be here in October at 20 minutes a night. He is 38.

The gap I can't stop counting

Toronto has played 20 playoff games. Dallas has played 16. Four extra nights. Sundin at 34, Roberts at 38, Mogilny90 at 36. The Dallas Stars core is younger and more rested and has been sitting on its hands for two days while we played a seven-game war in Washington.

Game 2 is at Dallas on May 22. The Meter is Green. I'll be back with a complaint the moment the score isn't 2-0 in our favour.
