TOR 2, DAL 0: Luongo's 21 saves and 53 points go quiet

The Leafs take Game 1 on the road. Dallas enters the Final with 53 playoff points from three forwards and scores none.

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramSaturday, 21 May 2005
ShareCopy link

The piece

Toronto took Game 1 of the Cup Final 2-0 at Dallas on May 20, shutting out the Presidents' Trophy club on its own ice and opening a 1-0 lead in the series. Game 2 is at Dallas Stars on May 22.

Roberto Luongo95 stopped all 21 shots Dallas directed at him, his second shutout of these playoffs. The Toronto Maple Leafs fired 31 the other way. Toronto went 30-11 on the road all season, and the margin tonight was two goals against a club that lost only 12 games all year in front of its own crowd.

The line that didn't score

Dallas entered this Final with three forwards carrying it. Mike Modano86 has 20 points in 16 playoff games. Brenden Morrow84 has 18. Sergei Zubov82 has 15. That's 53 points from three men through four rounds. None of them touched the scoresheet on May 20.

Bill Guerin has 13 points in 7 appearances this spring, the highest per-game rate on either roster. His absence from the line card takes a body off the Dallas forward line. The sheet does not record why he has appeared in only 7 of 16 playoff games.

The Leafs' own top two through 20 games: Mats Sundin92, 10 goals and 6 assists for 16 points. Gary Roberts85, 5 and 10 for 15. Both men are on the wrong side of 34 and both are eating 20 minutes a night. Sundin's contract runs 5 more years at $8.40M. Roberts is a free agent after this season at $2.70M.

The game gap

Toronto has played 20 playoff games. Dallas has played 16. Four extra games, roughly a week and a half of hockey on legs that are older. Alexander Mogilny89, 36, logged 18.4 minutes a night in the regular season and 11 points across 20 games this spring. Roberts, 38, has 15 points in 20. The extra games land on the men who cannot pay them back.

Luongo has started all 20. He is 13-7 with a 1.88 GAA and a .944 save percentage. The second choice, Maxime Ouellet86, has 13 regular-season appearances and zero playoff ones.

The winger in the boot

Marian Gaborik99 has 99 points on the season, 55 goals, 20.5 minutes a night, $1.10M against a back that pays more for fourth-liners. He is out with an ankle until June 2. Toronto scored 2 goals in Game 1 without him. The club went 2-0-0 against Dallas in the regular season, outscoring them 8-3. With tonight, the season series reads 3-0-0.

Game 2 tips at the American Airlines Center on May 22.
