LEAFS 4, OTT 1: 3 of 4 first-game scorers were 22 or under, and Chlup scored in his first NHL shift

Toronto opens 2-0-0 with 8 goals for and 1 against. The scoring is distributed across four men, three of them 22 or under.

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramWednesday, 12 October 2005
ShareCopy link

Toronto Maple Leafs opened the season 2-0-0 with a 4-1 win over Ottawa Senators at home on October 12, following a 4-0 road shutout at Pittsburgh Penguins two nights earlier. The first four goals of the season came from four different players, and three of those scorers were 22 or under.

In the PIT game, Petr Chlup87 scored at 19 years old in 6.0 minutes of ice time. Vaclav Spacek83, 20, added a goal in 17.0 minutes. Stanislav Chistov87, 22, had a goal and an assist in 6.0 minutes. Maxim Afinogenov86, 26, was the fourth scorer with a goal in 16.0 minutes.

Chlup sits on the second line centre spot (K1C_) and carries an 87 overall with a 95 potential at a $2.34M cap hit. He is the youngest regular forward on the roster and the only player drafted in the 2004 class who dressed for game one.

Roberto Luongo97 played the full 60 minutes against PIT and stopped all 29 shots he faced. Maxime Ouellet86 took the OTT start; Luongo has 1 game played on the season, not 2.

The defence log tells a different story on ice allocation. Rostislav Klesla87 skated 42.0 minutes and Barret Jackman86 41.0, both carrying the top pair against a Pittsburgh Penguins club that managed 0 goals on 25 shots. Tomas Kaberle85 logged 11.0 minutes and Jiri Fischer84 10.0, a workload split that suggests the coach is sheltering the second pair early in the season.

Up next: PIT comes to Toronto on October 14, then the Leafs travel to New York Rangers on October 15. Jamie Lundmark83 will face the club he left on May 31, and former Toronto defender Kenny Jonsson78 will be on the other side.

The five-game stretch from October 14 to October 21, 8 days with 4 divisional opponents, brings PIT, NYR, PHX, MTL, and BOS.
