Young Guns Watch: 5 Maple Leafs in the Bureau's top 20 form risers, and no other club has more than 2

Gaborik's +11 is the single highest form reading in the league. The four behind him are the reason this team looks different than it did in May.

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramWednesday, 12 October 2005
ShareCopy link

The Bureau's Development Index lists 20 players with the highest positive form readings after opening week. Five of them play for Toronto Maple Leafs. No other club has more than 2. Chicago Blackhawks has 2 (Igor Radulov80 at +11 and Steve McCarthy81 at +8). Boston Bruins and Florida Panthers have 1 each.

The five Toronto risers:

Gaborik's +11 is the highest single reading on the Index. His base of 91 puts him among the top 5 players in the Book, and the form boost to 99 is the Bureau's read on where his game stands right now. He has not played a game. The reading comes from skill evaluation and offseason projection rather than October production.

Chistov's jump from 78 to 87 is the most consequential for the lineup. He is 22, carries a $5.00M cap hit, and posted a goal and an assist in 6.0 minutes on October 10. If the form holds, he is a first-line winger by February.

Klesla at +8 means his overall now reads 87 at 23 years old, on a $0.90M contract with 1 year remaining. He played 42.0 minutes in game one. The Norris Trophy he won last season (637 overall in the Book, the highest-rated defenceman in the league that year) came the year before the Index would have pegged him at this base.

Nash at +7 is the least surprising entry. The 21-year-old has a 95 potential and was already at 85 in the Book's base. The Index is reading what was already there.

Lundmark is the oldest at 24, and the only one with a prior club connection this week: he visits New York Rangers on October 15, where he played before leaving on May 31.

The one Toronto name on the fallers side is Mikael Tellqvist73 at -5, dropping his overall to 73 from a base of 77. He is the third goaltender and has not played.
