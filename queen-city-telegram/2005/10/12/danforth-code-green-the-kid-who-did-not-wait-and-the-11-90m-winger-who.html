DANFORTH: Code Green, the kid who did not wait, and the $11.90M winger who has not skated

The second-centre problem had a name, a number, and a $2.34M cap hit. It turns out the answer was sitting in the building since last June.

Frankie DanforthColumnist · Queen City TelegramWednesday, 12 October 2005
ShareCopy link

Code Green. Two games, 8 goals for, 1 against, and I am writing the words I have been waiting since December 2004 to write: the second centre is here.

Petr Chlup87 is 19. He scored in his first NHL game. He plays 6.0 minutes a night, which means someone is being careful with him, which is correct. His overall reads 87 with a 95 potential and a $2.34M salary. He is cheaper than Rick Nash85 and better than anything the front office signed in the offseason.

I have logged the second-centre problem for a year and a half. I said it in December. I said it in April. I said it during the Final. The Bureau's Development Index had five Toronto players above their base on opening weekend, and four of them are under 25. The answer was never a trade or a signing. The answer was the kid in the building.

Except Gaborik is dressed. Marian Gaborik99 is listed at L2RW with 0 games played. The Bureau's Development Index has him at +11 form, the highest single-player form boost in the league, putting his overall at 99. He is the highest-rated player on the sheet, both teams combined. And he has not skated.

$11.90M. 5 years. The highest salary on the roster, against Mats Sundin92's $8.40M. Dressed and watching.

I do not have an explanation. The injured list does not carry his name. The body-part designation is absent. He is simply not playing, and I do not know what to do with a man who has the form of a 99-rated winger and the season production of a healthy scratch.

The Toronto Maple Leafs defence logged 42.0 minutes from Rostislav Klesla87 in game one. The second pair got 11.0 from Tomas Kaberle85. That gap is a coaching choice, not a talent gap, and it will narrow by November.

Five Maple Leafs sit in the Bureau's top 20 form risers. No other club has more than 2. The Development Index tells you what this team looks like in March. It does not tell you whether Marian Gaborik skates in October.

Green. For now.
