LEAFS 3, FLA 1: 17 shots, 43 against, and 2 points that still count

Toronto won at home on Saturday despite the Panthers putting 43 shots on goal. The Leafs managed 17 of their own.

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramMonday, 24 October 2005
ShareCopy link

Toronto beat Florida Panthers 3-1 at home on Saturday night on a 17-43 shot count, the widest differential either side has had all season. The Leafs outshot them by twenty-six and won by two. You get one of those, you take it.

The Leafs are 6-2-0 with 12 points, first in the Northeast. Florida is 1-4-1, 4th in the Southeast.

Toronto scored 3 on 17 attempts. Florida scored once on 43. The starter that night is not broken out on the sheet, but the team has allowed 14 in 8, which is where the workload sits.

Mats Sundin92 leads the club with 8 points (3 goals, 5 assists) in 8 games at 20.8 minutes a night. Petr Chlup87 has 8 points of his own (5 goals, 3 assists) in 19.6. Same point total, same number of games, minutes within a breath of each other.

Marian Gaborik99 has 2 points (2 goals, 0 assists) in 7 games at 20.0 a night, a +11 on the Development Index behind only Florida's Vaclav Nedorost83 at +12. Overall reads 99 on a base of 91.

On the blue line, Barret Jackman86 has 7 points (2 goals, 5 assists) in 8 games at 27.0 minutes. Rostislav Klesla87 has 2 points in 26.3 and sits at +8 on the Index, tied with Stanislav Chistov87 among the Leafs' risers.

The Leafs close the week at home against New York Rangers on 2005-10-26, then host Mighty Ducks of Anaheim on 2005-10-28 and Atlanta Thrashers on 2005-10-31.
