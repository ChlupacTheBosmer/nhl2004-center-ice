Northeast Race: 3 points, 2 games in hand, and Boston's 3-game win streak

Toronto leads the division with 12 points, but Boston has 9 in 6 games and a W3 running.

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramMonday, 24 October 2005
ShareCopy link

The Maple Leafs hold first place in the Northeast with 12 points in 8 games. Boston Bruins sits second at 9 points in 6 games, which leaves the Bruins with 2 games in hand and a 3-game win streak. If Boston wins both, they reach 13 and pass Toronto.

Toronto is 3-2-0 at home, 3-0-0 on the road. Boston is 0-0-0 at home (no home games played yet) and 4-1-1 on the road. Every Boston game so far has been away.

Sergei Samsonov82, Boston's top left wing with an 86 overall, is on the injured list with a neck injury between 2005-10-15 and 2005-10-16, and is not expected back until 2005-11-09. He's not playing through the current run.

The head-to-head this season reads 0-1-0 Toronto against Boston. The Bruins won 3-2 at the Air Canada Centre on 2005-10-21, the only meeting so far. Toronto scored 2 goals that night.

The rest of the division

Buffalo Sabres is 1-2-1 with 7 points in 6 games, on a 2-game losing streak. Montreal Canadiens sits 4th at 1-4-1 with 3 points and a 4-game losing streak, the worst record in the division. Ottawa Senators has 2 points in 3 games and faces Boston Bruins tonight on the road.

Toronto's 14 goals against in 8 games is the lowest total in the division. Boston has allowed 15 in 6. The Bruins' run of 3 wins has come entirely on the road, with 20 goals for and 15 against across those 6 games.
