Goalie Room: Luongo returns tomorrow, Tellqvist starts against Detroit

The Cup-winning netminder is one day from a return. Mikael Tellqvist takes the crease in his absence tonight.

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramWednesday, 16 November 2005
ShareCopy link

Roberto Luongo95 carries a return date of 2005-11-17. Tonight Mikael Tellqvist73 starts against Detroit Red Wings at the Air Canada Centre.

Luongo sits at 95 in the Bureau's Book. Tellqvist sits at 73. The Development Index has Tellqvist at -5, the deepest fall on the Toronto roster this month. He has started 6 games at 60.7 minutes a night.

Detroit visits for the first meeting of the season. The Red Wings are 8-5-1 (19 points) on a 2-game losing streak, with 3 players on the injured list: defenseman Derian Hatcher86 (a concussion, back 2005-12-02), left wing Brendan Shanahan85 (a back, back 2005-11-29), and center Pavel Datsyuk77 (a hip, back 2005-11-17). Datsyuk's return falls the same day as Luongo's.

The gap and the game in hand

Toronto leads the Northeast by 6 points. Boston Bruins sits at 22 points in 16 games, one game in hand against Toronto's 17. The two meet Saturday.

The head-to-head this season reads 0-1-0 from Toronto's side. Boston won the first meeting in regulation.

Toronto's 32 goals against in 17 games is the lowest total in the division. Detroit has scored 39 in 15 games.

The homestand runs through 2005-11-23: Detroit tonight, Boston on Saturday, Philadelphia Flyers on Tuesday. None of the three visitors carries a full roster. Toronto has no player on the injured list.

Tellqvist's last start came on 2005-11-12, a 2-1 overtime win over Los Angeles Kings. Toronto has won its last 3.
