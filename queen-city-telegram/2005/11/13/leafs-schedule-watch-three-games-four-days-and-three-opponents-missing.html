Leafs Schedule Watch: three games, four days, and three opponents missing bodies

Buffalo, Detroit and Boston arrive this week without a combined six players. Toronto has none on the shelf.

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramSunday, 13 November 2005
ShareCopy link

Toronto plays Buffalo Sabres on 2005-11-15, Detroit Red Wings on 2005-11-16, and Boston Bruins on 2005-11-19. The three opponents arrive with a combined six players on the injured list. Toronto has none.

Buffalo, Tuesday

Buffalo Sabres is 3-6-2, 14 points, W2. They have lost Tim Connolly80 (rib, out until 2005-11-30) and Janne Laukkanen74 (foot, out until 2005-11-16). Their season record at home is 3-3.

First meeting of the season. Buffalo's current 2-game streak is all they have of substance: 14 points in 14 games, a 38-42 goal differential. They are 4th in the division.

Detroit, Thursday

Detroit Red Wings visits the Air Canada Centre on 2005-11-16. The Red Wings are 8-4-1, 19 points, L1. They are missing three players: Derian Hatcher79 (concussion, out until 2005-12-02), Brendan Shanahan78 (back, out until 2005-11-29), and Pavel Datsyuk70 (hip, out until 2005-11-17).

On the road Detroit is 3-3, respectable, but Datsyuk's absence alone takes 77 points of scoring depth. Their 37 goals in 14 games sit 3rd in the Central, and losing Shanahan on top of Datsyuk thins that depth considerably.

Boston, Saturday

Boston Bruins comes to Toronto on 2005-11-19 for what is functionally the divisional game of the week. The Bruins are 8-3-2, 20 points, L1. They are missing Marty McInnis66 (rib, out until 2005-11-19), meaning he may return for this game or miss it by a day depending on how the designation falls.

The season series is 0-1-0 against Boston. The 6-point lead is the cushion, but the head-to-head is against them.

The other side of the ledger

Toronto Maple Leafs has no player on the injured list. The last Toronto name to appear there was Mikael Tellqvist73, who returned on 2005-11-07 and has 6 starts since. Petr Chlup87 returned from his own absence and has not missed a shift since.

Three games in 4 days tests a healthy roster. Toronto has one. The question is whether freshness converts to points or whether the shot gap keeps biting.
