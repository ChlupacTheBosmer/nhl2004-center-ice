Injury Desk: Tellqvist returns tomorrow, Chistov on Wednesday, and Modano sits out the Cup Final rematch

Two Toronto players carry return dates this week. Dallas plays Tuesday without its captain.

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramSunday, 6 November 2005
ShareCopy link

Mikael Tellqvist73 has a return date of 2005-11-07, which makes him available for tomorrow's lineup. The goaltender has not dressed since late October. His 6 starts came in the opening stretch of the season, and Roberto Luongo95 has taken over the net since, making 5 starts in that window with Maxime Ouellet86 filling in twice.

The Toronto Maple Leafs rotation heading into the Dallas trip will be the first in two weeks with all three goaltenders available. Luongo carries a 97 overall in the Book. Tellqvist sits at 73, down five spots on the Bureau's Development Index from a base of 77. The Index has him the 4th-biggest faller in the league behind four names with negative-4 ratings; his -5 is the steepest single decline on the list.

Stanislav Chistov87 returns 2005-11-08, which is the Tuesday game at Dallas. The left winger has 7 points in 13 games and skates line 2 with Chlup and Afinogenov. His Development Index carries a +8, one of 3 Toronto players on the risers list alongside Klesla (+8) and Lundmark (+8). Line 2 has run without a natural left winger in his absence.

Dallas, without Modano

Dallas Stars visits the Air Canada Centre on Tuesday for the first meeting between the 2005 Cup Finalists this season. The Stars are 7-3-2 with 18 points, 1st in the Pacific, on a 2-game losing streak. They allowed 2 goals in regulation to Columbus on 11/01 and 3 to Detroit on 11/03.

Mike Modano86, their centre with an 88 overall, has a shoulder designation on the League's injured list with a return date of 2005-11-09. He will miss Tuesday. The onset window runs between 2005-10-29 and 2005-11-06.

Dallas plays Vancouver at home today. Toronto has never faced them this season. The 2005 Final went 6 games; Toronto won 4-2.

The rest of the week

Toronto also plays at San Jose on 2005-11-09 and hosts Los Angeles on 2005-11-12. San Jose Sharks is 4-3-2 with 12 points. Los Angeles Kings is 2-5-3 with 11.
