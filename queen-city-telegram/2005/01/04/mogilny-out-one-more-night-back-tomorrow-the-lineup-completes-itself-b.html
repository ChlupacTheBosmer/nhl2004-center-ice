Mogilny out one more night, back tomorrow: the lineup completes itself before the Boston test

The 35-year-old winger returns January 5 and will be available for Friday's home game against Boston

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramTuesday, 4 January 2005
ShareCopy link

Alexander Mogilny87 is out tonight against New Jersey Devils. The league lists his return as January 5, which means he practises tomorrow and should dress Friday when Boston Bruins comes to town.

He sat out the 4-1 win in Newark on January 3 with an unspecified injury that began sometime between January 1 and January 4. The absence cost the Leafs a man who has 51 points in 35 games, 36 of them assists, and the top power-play unit ran through Mats Sundin88 and Marian Gaborik95 instead.

Tonight is the second half of a home-and-home against the Devils, who entered January 3 on a two-game losing streak and carry a 12-5 road record. They are 19-11-3 through 37 games, sitting 16 points behind Toronto in the overall East standings. The two clubs met three times last season and the Leafs won all three.

Without Mogilny, the forward grouping that skated in Newark likely repeats tonight: Sundin centering Gaborik and Gary Roberts85 on the left wing, Brad Richards84 with Maxim Afinogenov83 and Konstantin Koltsov70, Petr Chlup83 centering Rick Nash80 and Stanislav Chistov79. The fourth line is Vaclav Spacek74, Nikita Alexeev75, and Scottie Upshall76.

The second line is where the absence hurts most. Richards has 43 points in 39 games at 18.0 minutes a night, but the Bureau's Development Index has him at +4 over his 82 base, which means the overall of 85 is not where his form is trending. Put Mogilny on his wing and Richards gets a partner with 36 assists in 35 games who draws attention and opens seams. Remove him and Richards is the primary playmaker on that line with no one else on the roster carrying comparable scoring threat from centre.

"My body, it is thirty-five years, of course it talks to you in December. But I am here. That is the answer," Mogilny told The Tunnel's Marcus Bell after the 9-2 win over St. Louis on December 21, his last game before the injury window opened.

The crease is settled. Ed Belfour91 started in Newark and carries a 15-game workload at 57.3 minutes per game. David Aebischer80 has played 19 games at 57.9. Tonight is Belfour's turn on the back half of the back-to-back.

On the blueline, Bryan McCabe81 leads all defencemen with 46 points (12 goals, 34 assists) at 22.0 minutes a night. Rostislav Klesla81 sits at +5 on the Development Index, a 78 base pushing to 82. He played 15.1 minutes in Newark and scored none of the four goals, but his 20 points on the season at a $0.90M cap hit make him the cheapest piece of offence on the roster from the back end.

One note for tomorrow's practice sheet: Mogilny's return clears the fourth forward slot. Vaclav Spacek, the 19-year-old with 11 points in 22 games at $1.13M, drops to a healthy scratch or a call-down depending on what the medical staff signs off.
