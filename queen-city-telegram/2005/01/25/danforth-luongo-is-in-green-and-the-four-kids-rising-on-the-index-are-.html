DANFORTH: Luongo is in Green, and the four kids rising on the Index are not who I want

The trade fixes today. The Index shows four risers. Three of them score goals from the wing or the blue line. I asked for a centre in September and nobody has answered.

Frankie DanforthColumnist · Queen City TelegramTuesday, 25 January 2005
ShareCopy link

The piece

I am in Green. Luongo is here. The crease has an answer that is 25 years old and 91 on the Book, and I am not going to pretend otherwise. Write it down. Danforth, Code Green, January 25.

The Bureau's Development Index lists four Toronto Maple Leafs players as risers. Marian Gaborik96, +6 on the form term, 70 points in 49 games, making $1.10M. Rostislav Klesla82, +5, 23 points in 49 games, $0.90M. Rick Nash81, +5, 30 points in 36 games, $1.20M. Brad Richards85, +4, 51 points in 47 games, $1.00M. None of those four makes more than $1.20M. Ed Taylor said on The Tunnel that the club has $20M to work with. The money was never the problem.

Here is the problem. Three of those four risers play a position I already have covered. Gaborik plays right wing. Nash plays left wing. Klesla is a defenceman, and a fine one, and he is not what I am asking about. The fourth, Richards, is a centre. He plays the L3C_ slot. The third line. Not the second.

Mats Sundin89 carries the 41C_ and H2__ slots, 56 points, 18.4 minutes a night, 33 years old, $8.40M, five years left. Behind him: Petr Chlup84, 18 years old, 26 points in 38 games, 13.8 minutes a night. Not on the Index risers. Not close to it. His overall reads 82 and the potential says 95, which is what every 18-year-old's potential says until the league proves otherwise.

I have been asking for a draw-winning centre behind Sundin since before training camp. The Index does not help me. It tells me the wings are climbing, the blue line is climbing, and the man who is a centre by trade sits on the third line because the coaching staff likes what he does there. That may be correct. It does not answer the question I asked.

Luongo arrives and fixes the crease. The Index risers confirm the talent in the building. I believe both of those things. I also believe that in the second round of the playoffs, whoever we face will line a checking centre up against Sundin, and the second centre will get the puck in the offensive zone with the game on the line. That centre is not Chlup this year. He might be Chlup when the team needs it most, but that is a hope, not a roster.

The Panic Meter reads Green. I wrote it. I stand by it. Green with a note: the note says the centre question does not go away because a goaltender arrives, and none of the four rising players I just listed will draw a faceoff on the road in May with the series tied at two.
