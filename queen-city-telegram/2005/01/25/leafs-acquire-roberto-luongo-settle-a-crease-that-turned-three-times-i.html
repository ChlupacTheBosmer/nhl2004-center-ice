Leafs acquire Roberto Luongo, settle a crease that turned three times in six days

The Panthers get David Aebischer back. Luongo takes G1 tonight, and his old club visits the Air Canada Centre in eleven days.

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramTuesday, 25 January 2005
ShareCopy link

Toronto traded for Roberto Luongo92 today and sent David Aebischer80 to Florida Panthers in return. Luongo steps straight into the G1 slot, displacing Mikael Tellqvist73, who took the January 19 start and is no longer dressed.

Ed Belfour91 moves to G2, where he has logged 19 starts this season. Luongo arrives with 35 games on his ledger and a 91 overall. He carries a $1.60M cap hit with two years left, making him cheaper than Belfour's final-season salary of $6.50M and younger by 14 years.

The trade closes the crease question that started on January 15, when Ed Taylor told The Tunnel's Jan Kowalczyk that Belfour was "definitely" the number one. Four days later, Tellqvist started. Six days after that, the front office moved for a different answer entirely. The depth chart now reads Luongo, Belfour, with Maxime Ouellet80 and Tellqvist as healthy scratches.

Tonight's first test comes against Colorado Avalanche, the league's second-highest-scoring club at 200 goals in 47 games. Toronto's defence has allowed 129 in 49, the best total in the league.

The road trip begins Saturday: Carolina Hurricanes on January 29, Atlanta Thrashers on January 30, Florida Panthers on February 5, and Tampa Bay Lightning on February 6. Luongo meets his former club in 11 days. Aebischer meets his at the Air Canada Centre on March 3.
