COL at TOR tonight: first meeting between the league's two highest-scoring clubs

The Leafs have 265 goals in 49 games. Colorado has 200 in 47. They have not played yet.

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramTuesday, 25 January 2005
ShareCopy link

The Toronto Maple Leafs host Colorado Avalanche at the Air Canada Centre tonight, the first meeting between the league's top two goal-scoring clubs this season. Toronto has 265 goals in 49 games. Colorado has 200 in 47.

The Avalanche enter 26-15-3, 61 points, on a two-game winning streak that includes a 3-2 home victory over Columbus Blue Jackets on January 23. Colorado is 15-6 on the road, the best road record in the Northwest Division.

Toronto sits at 35-8-3, 79 points, 27 clear of Boston at the Northeast summit. The Leafs are 22-4 at home. They outscored Buffalo Sabres 3-1 on January 24, winning despite being outshot 21 to 30.

Peter Forsberg88 told The Tunnel's Jan Kowalczyk after a 3-4 loss to Mighty Ducks of Anaheim on January 9 that Colorado is not playing badly but is not closing tight games. The Avalanche have since answered that. They have 200 goals in 47 games, and a two-game run puts them within 4 points of the division lead.

The lineup tonight

The slot chart lists David Aebischer86 as G1 and Ed Belfour91 as G2. Ed Taylor told The Tunnel's Jan Kowalczyk after the January 21 loss to Philadelphia Flyers that he thought Belfour would start, adding that Aebischer is "still growing." The coach has not publicly reversed that since.

Brad Richards84 carries the L3C_ slot, sitting third-line centre behind the 18-year-old Petr Chlup83 on the second line. Marian Gaborik95 continues on the top line with Mats Sundin88 and Gary Roberts85, producing 70 points in 49 games at 19.1 minutes a night.

Sami Salo82 remains healthy but undressed, as he has been in recent games. Karel Pilar75 and Carlo Colaiacovo70 are also scratched.

The road trip begins January 29

After tonight, the Leafs leave for four consecutive road games: Carolina Hurricanes on January 29, Atlanta Thrashers on January 30, Florida Panthers on February 5, and Tampa Bay Lightning on February 6. Toronto is 16-7 on the road this season, including a 3-1 win at Buffalo on January 24.

The Philadelphia Flyers game on January 21 was the last time the Leafs lost by a single goal at home. They are 22-4 at the Air Canada Centre, and the only two teams within reach of their home win total this season are Detroit Red Wings at 18-6 and New York Rangers at 18-9.
