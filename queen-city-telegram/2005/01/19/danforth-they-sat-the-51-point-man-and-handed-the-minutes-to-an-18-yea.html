DANFORTH: They sat the 51-point man and handed the minutes to an 18-year-old

Brad Richards is undressed, Petr Chlup is on the power play, and the coach just did what I have been asking for since December

Frankie DanforthColumnist · Queen City TelegramWednesday, 19 January 2005
ShareCopy link

The piece

Brad Richards85 is not in the lineup. The depth chart has him undressed. Petr Chlup82, 18 years old, moved into the 42 centre slot, the K2 faceoff spot, the L3C line, and P2 on the power play. Vaclav Spacek75, 19, is dressed for the first time in this read.

I have been writing about the second-centre problem since December 2004. Twelve years of asking for a draw-winning centre behind Mats Sundin89. The Bureau's Development Index had Chlup at plus 4 in December, same as Richards. Ed Taylor told The Tunnel on January 15 that he considers the kid a top-six forward and that he took a huge leap in the last few months. "I think it can only get better from here," Taylor said.

The sheet says Richards, 24, 85 overall, +4 on the Index, 51 points in 46 games, 24 goals, a $1.00M cap hit, one year left on his deal. Chlup, 18, 82 overall, potential 95, 25 points in 36 games, 13.5 minutes a night, $2.34M over two more years. One of them sits. One of them is on the second power-play unit. I know which one I would have guessed three weeks ago.

The contradiction is real. Richards has more points, more games, a higher overall, and a rising Index. He is 51 points better than Chlup in 10 more games, and his points-per-game rate is higher. Every instinct I have built in twelve years of watching this club throw away established centres tells me you do not sit a 24-year-old with 51 points in January while you are 34 and 8 with a 26-point division lead.

But here is the thing that makes me uncertain: Taylor's "two first lines" comment. He said it about Gaborik and Richards, that the club basically has two first lines because of those two men. Now the coach has undressed one of those two and replaced him with an 18-year-old from the OHL. The GM says top six. The coach says undressed. One of those two people is about to look wrong.

Ed Belfour92, asked after the Calgary game on January 14 about what the young guys bring, said: "Petr's making the first pass clean." That was before this lineup card. I wonder if it still holds at P2 in a playoff race, or against the Philadelphia centre group when Keith Primeau80 is back (arm, expected January 21, the same day PHI visits the Air Canada Centre).

The risk is the message. If you sit a 51-point man in a January with 26 points of cushion, you are telling every player in that room that production does not protect you. You are telling Mikael Tellqvist74, who just fell 3 spots on the Index, that form can swing you out. And maybe that is fine. Maybe that is how you build a roster with six 80-plus players and no one comfortable. Or maybe you are sitting a man who can carry a second unit through April, and you find out in April that you wanted him.

The test comes in two days. PHI is 15-21-4, 42 points, on a one-game losing streak. They have Primeau (arm, return date January 21) and Justin Williams73 (hip, return date January 20) both coming back into a lineup that has already lost to Toronto three times this season by a combined 23-6. This is not the test that tells you whether Chlup can play 18 minutes in April. But it is the next one, and the sheet will record who dressed and who did not.

My Panic Meter says Green. Twenty-six points of lead, a seven-win homestand, 261 goals for, 126 against. You do not panic over a lineup card. But I am writing this down, and if March comes and the second unit is flat and Richards is watching from the press box, I will not pretend I did not ask.
