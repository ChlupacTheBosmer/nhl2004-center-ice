DANFORTH: Four risers on the Index, and none of them is the centre

The 7-2 win at Atlanta settles nothing I care about. The second centre spot settles everything.

Frankie DanforthColumnist · Queen City TelegramMonday, 31 January 2005
ShareCopy link

Toronto scored 7 on 36 shots at Atlanta Thrashers on Sunday night, holding Atlanta to 2 on 25. The Panic Meter is Green. The Toronto Maple Leafs lead the Northeast at 37-9-3, 83 points, 277 goals for, 137 against. Boston Bruins is next at 56. There is nothing in the standings tonight that gives me a reason to worry.

What gives me a reason to worry is the Bureau's Development Index, published today. It lists 4 Toronto players on the riser column: Marian Gaborik99 at +6, sitting at 96 overall with 72 points in 52 games. Rostislav Klesla87 at +5, 82 overall, 23 points in 52 games from the blue line. Rick Nash85 at +5, 81 overall, 31 points in 39 games. Brad Richards86 at +4, 85 overall, 53 points in 50 games.

A right winger. A defenseman. A left winger. A winger playing the third-line centre. Four risers, and none of them is a second centre.

I asked for one in September. It is the end of January and the sheet still shows Mats Sundin92 with 60 points, 31 goals, 18.5 minutes a night, and no one behind him who is not a winger wearing the centre ice dot by necessity or a teenager still finding his legs.

Petr Chlup87 is the closest thing to an answer. He is 18, dressed and centered the fourth line Sunday, 28 points in 41 games, a Book grade of 82 against 95 potential. The kid will grow. He may become the man. But 28 points in 41 games at 18 is a projection, not an indictment, and not an answer either.

Jason Spezza73 is 70 overall, not dressed, 15 points in 37 games. The Index has him nowhere near the risers. He sits on the roster as a reminder of a question nobody in the front office has answered in public.

The crease has flipped again: Ed Belfour at G1, Roberto Luongo97 at G2. Fine. I have 83 points and no argument about that call. What I want is a line that centers behind Sundin in Game 7, whoever is in the net.

February 5 in Florida starts the next stretch. February 8, Montreal Canadiens visits the Air Canada Centre. The trade deadline the calendar has is 47 days away. I want the lineup that plays both games to include a real second centre. The Index does not have one. The sheet says I am on my own.
