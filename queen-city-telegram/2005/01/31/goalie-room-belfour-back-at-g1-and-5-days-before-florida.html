Goalie Room: Belfour back at G1, and 5 days before Florida

The lineup shift reverses a crease order that held for one game, and the road win at Atlanta gives Toronto a full week to live with it

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramMonday, 31 January 2005
ShareCopy link

Ed Belfour91 is back at G1. The lineup change filed since Saturday puts him ahead of Roberto Luongo92, who held the slot for one game, the 7-2 road win at Atlanta Thrashers on Sunday night.

Belfour, 39, has played in 20 games this season, averaging 57.9 minutes a night. He carries a $6.50M salary in the final year of his contract. Luongo, 25, is at 37 games and 61.1 minutes a night on $1.60M, with 2 seasons remaining after this one. Neither figure moved with the switch, so the crease now sits in the order Ed Taylor outlined on The Tunnel after the January 21 overtime loss to Philadelphia Flyers: "today, which is what I'm more interested in talking about than next season, I think that Ed would start."

Toronto scored 7 on 36 shots at Atlanta Thrashers, holding Atlanta to 2 goals on 25 attempts. The Toronto Maple Leafs lead the Northeast at 37-9-3, 83 points, with 277 goals for and 137 against through 52 games. Boston Bruins is second at 56. Atlanta dropped to 17-26-2, 48 points, 9-15 on the road.

The next game is 5 days out, February 5 on the road at Florida Panthers, a building where Luongo played before the January 25 trade. The season series sits at 2-0 to Toronto, 8-4 in goals, with 2 games remaining. Toronto has not lost to Florida this season.

Behind the two starters, Maxime Ouellet80 has appeared in 8 games at 27.9 minutes a night and Mikael Tellqvist73 in 11 at 56.4. Neither is in the active lineup. The Development Index has Tellqvist at -3 on form, his overall grade now 74 against a base of 77.

Ed Taylor has 5 days and one full practice week before the Florida date to decide whether the crease holds or shifts again.
