DANFORTH: A 39-year-old wants his sixth shutout and the crease just moved under him

Belfour said he wanted six on The Tunnel last week. Luongo is 94 overall at $1.60M, and the starts are no longer automatic.

Frankie DanforthColumnist · Queen City TelegramWednesday, 26 January 2005
ShareCopy link

The piece

Ed Belfour93 told The Tunnel's Marcus Bell after the Calgary game on January 14: "Five shutouts, I want six. That's not about the record. That's between me and the net." He was 39 then and he's 39 now, and between those two dates the club acquired a 25-year-old goaltender rated 94 with 97 potential at $1.60M for two years.

The crease question is settled on paper. Luongo is G1. Belfour is G2. The sheet says what it says. But the workload question is open, and it determines how this ends.

Belfour has 19 starts this season, 57.8 minutes a night. Luongo carried 61.2 minutes a night across 36 starts in Florida before the trade. Belfour's contract runs one year at $6.50M. Luongo's runs two at $1.60M. Ed Taylor told The Tunnel's Jan Kowalczyk on January 15 that "today, which is what I'm more interested in talking about than next season, I think that Ed would start." Four days later Ed Taylor's club traded for Luongo and put the new man in G1.

The Leafs lead the Northeast by 26 points over Boston Bruins. There is no race to manage. The question in front of the coach now is how many games a 39-year-old gets before the minutes belong to the 25-year-old. If Ed Belfour wants his sixth, he needs the crease. If Ed Taylor wants the future, he needs the crease for Luongo.

I am not against Belfour. He won the Jennings last season. He has been square and tracked and durable all year. But $6.50M buys one more year of a 39-year-old's legs, and $1.60M buys two years of a 25-year-old's ceiling. The arithmetic does not need a column to tell it what it already knows. It needs the lineup card to match.

Ed Belfour has five shutouts and one year left. He wants a sixth. Whether he gets the starts to chase it is the last real decision this roster makes before April.

The Panic Meter is Green. The team is 36-8-3, on a two-game win streak, and the crease is deep. I am just noting that a man who said he wanted something has to play for it, and the path just got narrower.
