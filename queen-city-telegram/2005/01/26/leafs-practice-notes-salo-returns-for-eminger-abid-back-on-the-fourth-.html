Leafs Practice Notes: Salo returns for Eminger, Abid back on the fourth line

Two lineup changes ahead of Saturday's road game at Carolina. Luongo is available tomorrow.

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramWednesday, 26 January 2005
ShareCopy link

Toronto Maple Leafs made two changes to the lineup that took the ice against Colorado Avalanche on Monday, moving Sami Salo81 onto the third defensive pair and recalling Ramzi Abid80 to the fourth wing. Steve Eminger82 and Scottie Upshall77 were scratched.

Salo returns after logging 8 points in 22 appearances this season, averaging 18.9 minutes a night. He replaces Eminger, who had 19 points in 39 games at 16.0 minutes a game on the left side. The swap puts the heavier minutes back on Salo's stick, with his 4-on-4 and power-play time restored.

Abid's the bigger move. He carries 63 points in 50 games (20 goals, 43 assists) and 20.2 minutes a night. His 43 assists are second on the club behind Bryan McCabe81's 35. Upshall had 17 points in 45 games at 10.7 minutes a night before the scratch.

Roberto Luongo94 returns from his post-trade clearance on January 27. The Leafs' road trip begins Saturday at Carolina Hurricanes (January 29, first meeting of the season between these clubs), then Atlanta Thrashers on January 30, Florida Panthers on February 5 and Tampa Bay Lightning on February 6. Toronto is 16-7 on the road.

Ed Taylor told The Tunnel's Marcus Bell on January 15 that Luongo's arrival strengthened the crease depth, saying the club's interest was in boosting endurance across a long season. With Luongo available, that split begins Saturday.
