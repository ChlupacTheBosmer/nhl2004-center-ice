Leafs 2, Penguins 1: 13 shots, 34 saves, and the crease question that answers itself tomorrow

Toronto won in Pittsburgh with 13 shots on goal against 34. Ed Belfour made every save that mattered, and Mikael Tellqvist returns January 11.

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramMonday, 10 January 2005
ShareCopy link

The piece

The Toronto Maple Leafs Maple Leafs won in Pittsburgh with 13 shots on goal. The Pittsburgh Penguins Penguins fired 34 at Ed Belfour92 and came home with 2 goals against on the scoresheet. A 2-1 road win built on 21 fewer scoring chances than the opponent, which is not the kind of thing you can schedule twice in a week.

Belfour finished 2004-05 with 16 games played, 14-1-0, and now this 34-save win. He is 39 years old, carries a $6.50M cap hit with 1 contract year remaining, and sits at 92 overall on the Bureau's Book. The Development Index does not carry goaltenders among its risers or fallers this edition, but the body of work is there: 16 starts through 42 games, and 4 straight wins with the third period of the Boston game the last time the Leafs allowed a goal in regulation.

Mikael Tellqvist74 carries a form marker rather than an injury and is available. His return date is firm. He has 9 games played this season, 55.6 minutes a night when on the ice, and the Bureau's Development Index has him at minus 3 form, reading 74 overall against a base of 77. David Aebischer87 has 19 games played, a $0.50M salary, and 85 overall with form carrying him there. The three-goalie math is straightforward: 16 plus 19 plus 9 equals 44 appearances across 42 games, which means at least two games saw a change in net mid-game.

The coaching staff has not spoken on the crease plan for tomorrow. Belfour played Pittsburgh. Tellqvist's return date lands the morning of the Boston game. Whether the staff rolls Belfour again on short rest at 39 or hands the net to a 25-year-old coming off an absence, the answer comes before puck drop.

Toronto's four-game win streak puts the club at 32-6-1, 71 points, with a 26-point lead over Buffalo Sabres in the Northeast division. The 4 goals against across those four wins is the number that matters more than 13 shots on any given road night. A defence that allows 110 goals in 42 games can lose a game with 13 shots. It has just done that and won anyway.

The next road stop is Boston on January 11, then New York on January 13, then home to Calgary on January 14.

---

Correction. An earlier edition dated a player's return from a roster marker that is not an injury. That date is when a form or fitness marker lapses, which no reporter could know, and it has been removed.
