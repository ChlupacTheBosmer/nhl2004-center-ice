Leafs 7, Flames 1: the home formula holds, and the road question carries into Washington

Gaborik's heater keeps climbing, Belfour stays in the crease, and the next two games are on the road.

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramFriday, 14 January 2005
ShareCopy link

The piece

Toronto Maple Leafs beat Calgary Flames 7-1 at the Air Canada Centre on Friday night, the third straight home game decided by four goals or more. The Maple Leafs are 32-8-1, 71 points in 44 games, still 24 ahead of Buffalo Sabres in the Northeast.

Marian Gaborik99 carried 64 points into the night, 37 goals, 27 assists, and sits at plus 6 on the Bureau's Development Index, the top riser in the league. Mats Sundin92 has 52 points in 44 games at age 33. Brad Richards86 matched Sundin's point total within five, 47 points at 24 years old and $1.00M against the cap.

Ed Belfour has started 17 games. Mikael Tellqvist73 has started 10 and sits at minus 3 on the Index, below his Book base. The crease order has not changed since the second shutout streak in early January.

The pattern the standings cannot capture

At home: 21-3. On the road: 14-6. The last two road games produced losses, a 3-2 defeat in Boston Bruins and a 6-3 loss to New York Rangers on Thursday. Toronto had 29 shots in that game, 25 for the Rangers, and the difference was six goals against.

Brad Richards told The Tunnel's Jan Kowalczyk after the 5-0 win over Boston: "It asks you to not coast. That's the honest one. You win five, six straight, 69 points, you're the best record in the league, and every room you walk into, the other guy's game plan is you."

Two points from tonight and 71 on the board, with the home record doing most of the lifting.

What is next

Washington Capitals on the road Tuesday, Montreal Canadiens on the road Wednesday. Washington is 23-13-5, 55 points, riding a 5-game win streak. The Capitals are the closest team in the East to Toronto Maple Leafs's point total outside the division. Montreal is 12-23-4 and 36 points, last in the Northeast, and coming off a 4-3 overtime win over Buffalo Sabres on the 11th.

Paul Kariya89 is out for Colorado Avalanche with a leg injury through 1/23. No Toronto player is on the injured list. The full crew skated.
