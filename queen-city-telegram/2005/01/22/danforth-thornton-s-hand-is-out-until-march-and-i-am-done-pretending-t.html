DANFORTH: Thornton's hand is out until March, and I am done pretending there is a division race

Boston loses its center for six weeks with 27 points to make up. The only thing left to decide in the Northeast is who finishes second.

Frankie DanforthColumnist · Queen City TelegramSaturday, 22 January 2005
ShareCopy link

Joe Thornton89 is out with a hand. The return date is March 5. The Boston Bruins center, 91 on the Bureau's overall grades, will not lace them up for six weeks. Boston sat at 18 wins, 20 losses, 4 overtime losses, and 50 points before that happened. They sit 27 points behind the Toronto Maple Leafs.

I have been writing about a second-centre problem in Toronto for twelve years. The Boston Bruins second-centre problem is no longer hypothetical. Their first centre is in a hospital. Their second centre, whoever that is, is not coming either.

The Buffalo Sabres Sabres are one point behind Boston at 49. Buffalo has its own problems, 5 overtime losses and 187 goals against in 45 games, but one point is one point. Without Thornton, the Boston Bruins offense thins. Glen Murray83 is back in the lineup after a stretch on the outside, which helps. The center who feeds him does not exist right now.

The overtime thing is real

The Maple Leafs are 0 for 3 in the extra period. Three games after 60 minutes. Three losses. The shot gap against Philadelphia Flyers on January 21 was 21 to 32, and the Philadelphia Flyers record is 16-21-5. A team that should not beat the Maple Leafs found the extra man anyway.

I do not need a panic. Toronto leads the Northeast by 27 points with a 262-128 goal differential. I need those three extra-period losses in the back of my head when April arrives and every game is a one-goal game. Gary Roberts88 said it plain on The Tunnel this season: every January feels like the one where you run away with it, and every March feels like the one where you wish you had. We are not running away with anything. We are 34 and 8 with a problem in overtime that has not been solved because it has not had to be solved yet.

What I keep asking

Brad Richards87 sits at 51 points, 86 on the overall, +4 on the Index, and a million dollars a year. He has not dressed in three games. Petr Chlup86 plays 13.5 minutes and has 25 points in 37 games at 18. The coach made his choice. I made mine, and it is the same one I have been making since December: put the 51-point man in and see what happens.

The one thing I will not complain about is what happened to Marian Gaborik98. +6 on the Development Index, 97 overall, 70 points in 48 games, 39 of them goals. The best player on the best team in the division is 22 years old and climbing. That is the part I like.
