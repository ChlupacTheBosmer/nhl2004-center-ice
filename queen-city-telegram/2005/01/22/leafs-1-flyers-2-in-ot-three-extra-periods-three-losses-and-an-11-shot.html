Leafs 1, Flyers 2 in OT: three extra periods, three losses, and an 11-shot gap nobody answered

Aebischer starts in his first turn since the slot chart flipped on Tellqvist, and Richards sits for the third straight game

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramSaturday, 22 January 2005
ShareCopy link

The piece

The Toronto Maple Leafs dropped a 1-2 overtime decision to the Philadelphia Flyers on January 21, their third loss in the extra period this season and their second in a row. The Flyers fired 32 shots. The Maple Leafs managed 21.

Philadelphia held the puck through regulation and found the extra man in overtime at the Air Canada Centre. The Maple Leafs led the Northeast by 27 points heading into the night. They still do. The three overtime losses have not moved the standings.

David Aebischer86 started in net, his first turn in the crease since the slot chart handed G1 to Mikael Tellqvist74 on January 19. Ed Belfour92, the Vezina Trophy winner last season, slides back to G2. Three goaltenders have held the G1 slot since the calendar turned to January.

Brad Richards85, 24 goals and 27 assists through 46 games, did not dress for the third consecutive game. He sits at 86 on the Bureau's overall grades and +4 on the Development Index. Petr Chlup84 skated on the second power-play unit and logged 13.5 minutes a night. Chlup has 25 points in 37 games at 18 years old. Richards has 51.

Toronto has 34 wins, 8 regulation losses, and 3 overtime losses through 48 games. The 77-point total and the 262-128 goal differential are fine. The problem is the extra period. In three games decided after 60 minutes, the Maple Leafs are 0-3.

The Maple Leafs open a road swing Tuesday at the Buffalo Sabres, the club Toronto beat 6-3 on October 13 and 5-4 on December 13. The Maple Leafs have outscored Buffalo 21-1 across two meetings. Colorado Avalanche visits the Air Canada Centre Wednesday.

Marian Gaborik96 carries 70 points through 48 games and sits +6 on the Development Index, the highest form reading in the league. He is 22. Mats Sundin89 has 56. Gary Roberts86 has 57. The scoring is there.
