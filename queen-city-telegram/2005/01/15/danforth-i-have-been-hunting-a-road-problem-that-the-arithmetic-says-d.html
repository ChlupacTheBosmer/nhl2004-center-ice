DANFORTH: I have been hunting a road problem that the arithmetic says does not exist

The Panic Meter goes Green, the second-centre answer arrives on a Tuesday, and the only question left in January is who stands in the crease come April

Frankie DanforthColumnist · Queen City TelegramSaturday, 15 January 2005
ShareCopy link

The piece

The Toronto Maple Leafs Maple Leafs are 14-6 on the road. That is the best road record in the Northeast. I have been writing about a road problem since December because they dropped two in a row in January, and two losses in 20 games is not a problem. It is Tuesday.

I am lowering the Panic Meter to Green, and I want you to know how rare that sentence is from me.

Petr Chlup86 returns on January 16. He has 23 points in 34 games at 13.3 minutes a night, a $2.34M cap hit, and an overall of 83 with a 95 potential. That is the second-centre answer I have been shouting into the void since 2004-12. He is not ready to play 20 minutes against the Washington Capitals Capitals' first line, and nobody in the building expects him to. But he is here, and the minutes distribution shifts tomorrow. Brad Richards87 moves up a role, and the top-six has an answer to the question I logged in December: who plays the minutes Mats can't cover, and the answer is a 24-year-old at a million dollars with 50 points.

The Index has Richards at +4, Marian Gaborik98 at +6, Rostislav Klesla84 at +5, Rick Nash83 at +5. Four young players whose overall grades read above their base. That is not a roster in decline. That is a roster climbing into its window.

The crease is the only January question worth asking

Ed Belfour93 has started 18 games in 45. David Aebischer86 has 19. Mikael Tellqvist75 has 10. The three of them split 45 starts almost evenly. Belfour is 39. He played 57.8 percent of Toronto's minutes last season in a Conn Smythe run, and this year he is at 40 percent through 45 games.

That is not a workload problem yet. That is a 37-game problem. The Toronto Maple Leafs finish with 37 games left, and whoever starts 25 of them will carry the postseason. The Division crown is 26 points wide. There is no race to win in January. The only race is the one in the crease, and the only person who can decide it is the coach, game by game, with the medical staff's numbers on the whiteboard.

Gary Roberts88 told The Tunnel's Marcus Bell after the Pittsburgh game in January that the team is "doing it the hard way," and he was right about the method. You win with 13 shots when the other team has 34. You win 5-0 on the road at Boston. You win 7-1 against Calgary at home after losing 6-3 to New York. The hard way is the way that works in April because it doesn't require the bounces.

I wanted a second-centre. I wanted him on the wing next to Gaborik and I wanted him to break the fourth line open. Chlup is the closest thing to that wish this roster has ever handed me, and he is 18 years old, and he costs $2.34M for two more years, and he arrives on a Sunday so he can play on Tuesday. The answer came. The problem I was solving is now someone else's problem.

Green. The crease is the only thing that matters from here to April. Everything else is arithmetic, and the arithmetic is fine.
