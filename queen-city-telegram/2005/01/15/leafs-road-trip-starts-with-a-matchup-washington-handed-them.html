Leafs road trip starts with a matchup Washington handed them

Jagr moves to first centre, Chlup is expected to dress tomorrow, and Richards gets the assignment he has been waiting for all season

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramSaturday, 15 January 2005
ShareCopy link

Petr Chlup82 is expected to be available when the Toronto Maple Leafs Maple Leafs open their two-game road trip at Washington Capitals on Tuesday, January 17. The 18-year-old centre, out since late December with 23 points in 34 games, has a return date of January 16. He will practise with the group tomorrow.

The Washington line chart shifted over the last two days. Jaromir Jagr89 moved from second-centre to first-centre, sliding Robert Lang80 down a slot. That puts the Capitals' leading scorer, now the first man off the bench, squarely across from Brad Richards85. Richards has 50 points in 45 games at 17.9 minutes a night, a $1.00M cap hit, and a +4 on the Bureau's Development Index.

The Toronto Maple Leafs are 14-6 on the road this season, the best mark in the Northeast division. Their two January road losses came at New York Rangers (6-3 on January 13) and Boston Bruins (3-2 on January 11), games the Leafs were outshot in both. At home they are 22-3. Tuesday's test comes at a Capitals building where Washington is 10-10.

Coach Pat Quinn has not confirmed the lines. Richards skated on the second-centre pair throughout Monday's optional.

The other road game is Thursday at Montreal Canadiens, where Montreal carries a 36-point record and a 7-game losing streak. Both contests precede a home date with Philadelphia Flyers on January 21, and the Philadelphia Flyers lineup changes tell you something about how their roster is reshuffling: Keith Primeau80 is back in the lineup after an arm injury that carried a January 21 return date, but he skated as a fourth-liner with 9.0 minutes of ice time in three games before sitting out again. Justin Williams73, listed as dressing for Philadelphia, has a hip issue with a January 20 return date. Neither man may be at full strength Thursday.

For Toronto, the question on this trip is narrower than the standings suggest. With 26 points between first and second in the division, the Toronto Maple Leafs are playing for minutes and a health check, not position. Chlup's ice time will tell you whether the coaching staff trusts him in the road-structure role or keeps him in the blowout slot he has occupied for 13.3 minutes a night.
