Leafs 3, Bruins 2: outshot by 11, and the conversion problem the shot clock cannot solve

Toronto put 32 pucks on Raycroft and scored twice, dropping its second road game of the week while Tellqvist slides further below his base

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramWednesday, 12 January 2005
ShareCopy link

The Toronto Maple Leafs scored twice on 32 shots in Boston, lost 3-2, and walked out of the FleetCenter on the wrong side of a shot differential that used to go the other way all season.

Toronto's 32-21 advantage in shots came without three of its top nine forwards in any scoring role tonight. The Boston Bruins home record sits at 8-13. This is a team with 43 points that just beat a team with 71. Boston went to overtime Tuesday night against Buffalo and came back to win this one inside regulation.

The crease and the Index

Mikael Tellqvist73 carries the G1 slot and 10 starts. The Bureau's Development Index puts him at minus 3, a base of 77 read down to 74. Ed Belfour sits G2 with 16 starts and an overall of 92. The coaching staff has not changed the pecking order. Tellqvist faced 21 shots and gave up three. The game was not decided by goaltending in any single sequence, but the gap between what the Index says a starter is carrying and what the backup grades at keeps widening by the week.

Toronto is 14-5 on the road, second in the Northeast's away column. Two games remain on this trip: New York Rangers tomorrow and Calgary Flames Friday at the Air Canada Centre. After that it is four days off before Washington on the 17th.

The numbers that did not turn

The Toronto Maple Leafs power play went scoreless. Marian Gaborik99 carries 62 points in 43 games and Brad Richards87 46, but tonight the third man did not appear. Mats Sundin92 logged 17.7 minutes per game this season and had nothing on the scoresheet in Boston.

Toronto has 241 goals for in 43 games. The road record does not care about one night's shot differential. But 32 shots and 2 goals is the lowest conversion of any game this season where the shot total crossed 30. The formula that built a 32-7-1 record was defense first, goaltending second, opportunism third. Only defense held tonight.

Gary Roberts85 carries a form marker rather than an injury and is available. He told The Tunnel's Marcus Bell after the Pittsburgh game that the team is "doing it the hard way." Tonight looked like the hard way without the payoff.

Correction. An earlier edition dated a player's return from a roster marker that is not an injury. That date is when a form or fitness marker lapses, which no reporter could know, and it has been removed.
