Leafs 2, Hurricanes 6: 8 goals surrendered in the last 2 away games, and ATL waits tonight with a 5-game streak

Toronto managed 22 shots against Carolina and gave up 6. Tonight the schedule sends them to a team that hasn't lost since January 15.

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramSunday, 30 January 2005
ShareCopy link

Toronto scored 2 goals on 22 shots at Carolina Hurricanes on January 29 and lost 6-2. It was the second straight road game in which the Leafs' output dropped below 3, and the worst loss by goal differential on the season.

Roberto Luongo93 was in net. He faces 30 shots, stops 24. Carolina scored 3 in the second period and 2 more in the third. The Leafs managed 1 power-play goal, which came on the game's final power play with the score already out of reach.

Ed Taylor addressed the result after the game on January 22 against Philadelphia Flyers, and the pattern he named then held again in Raleigh. "We played well losing some moments in the defense especially, there were a few lapses which I think turned out into great goals, and we also struggled with offense which is something that we were not used to," Taylor told The Tunnel's Jan Kowalczyk after the Flyers game. He also pointed to the physicality of the play as a problem. Carolina's 30 shots and 6 goals answered both points.

The shot gap sums up the last two road dates. At Buffalo Sabres on January 24 the Leafs won 3-1. At Carolina on January 29 they lost 2-6, outshot 30 to 22. The only other road game in the last seven produced more than 25 shots: at Washington Capitals on January 17, where Toronto scored 6 in a 6-1 win.

Tonight: a streak, a 90-point winger, and a new goalie

Atlanta Thrashers enters tonight on a 5-game winning streak, the longest active run in the Atlanta Thrashers building. They have won consecutive games against New York Islanders, San Jose Sharks, Ottawa Senators, New York Rangers twice. The wins came by margins of 1 and 2 goals: 4-3 twice in overtime, 4-2, 4-3, 2-1.

Ilya Kovalchuk91 carries 90 points through 50 games for Atlanta Thrashers, sitting at +5 on the Bureau's Development Index. He is the highest-scoring player on the ice tonight.

The Thrashers made a goaltender change this week. Byron Dafoe81 takes the G1 slot; Pasi Nurminen79 moves to the bench. Dafoe has 62 career games on the season line, though the sheet does not list his 2004-05 appearances before tonight.

The season series between Toronto and Atlanta is 2-1-0 Toronto Maple Leafs with the goals tied at 14 apiece. The three meetings have produced 4, 5, and 5 goals respectively. No game in the series has been decided by more than 2.

Toronto is 16-8 on the road. Atlanta is 9-15 at home, the 4th-worst home record in the Eastern Conference. The Thrashers sit at 48 points through 50 games, 7th in the Southeast. The Maple Leafs lead the Northeast by 25 points over Boston Bruins.

The two teams meet at 7:30 Eastern at Philips Arena. Toronto has no new injuries. Atlanta is without Andy Sutton75 (leg, out to 2005-04-12).
