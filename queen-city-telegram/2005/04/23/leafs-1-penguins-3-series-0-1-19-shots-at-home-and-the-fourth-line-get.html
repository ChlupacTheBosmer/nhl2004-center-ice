LEAFS 1, PENGUINS 3, series 0-1: 19 shots at home, and the fourth line gets a new centre

Pittsburgh takes the opener at the Air Canada Centre; Game 2 is tomorrow night.

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramSaturday, 23 April 2005
ShareCopy link

Pittsburgh beat Toronto 3-1 at the Air Canada Centre on 2005-04-22 and leads the second-round series 1-0. Game 2 is 2005-04-24 in the same building.

The number to hold is 19. That is what Toronto Maple Leafs put on the Pittsburgh goal at home in Game 1. The Pittsburgh Penguins put 28 on Toronto. In the regular season this matchup read 4-1-0 for the Leafs, 21-12 on goals, with two one-goal games. None of that showed up Friday.

The card changed in two places

Maxim Afinogenov86 dressed for Game 1, and Rick Nash85 moved down a line to make room. Konstantin Koltsov75 came out. On the fourth line, Jason Spezza73 gave way to Vaclav Spacek83, 19, who has 50 games and 17 points this season at 7.6 minutes a night. Spezza's line is 46 games and 15 points at 6.7.

The goalie room turned over its backup slot: Maxime Ouellet86 dressed, Mikael Tellqvist73 did not. Ouellet is 23 and the Book grades him 82, with a 97 potential. Tellqvist is 25, graded 75, and the Bureau's Development Index has him down three.

What Pittsburgh is bringing

Mario Lemieux88 has 8 points in 7 games this spring, 2 goals and 6 assists. Eric Meloche78, who took the Calder last season, has 5 goals and 2 assists, 7 points in 7. Shane Willis72 and Martin Straka86 have 4 points each. Pittsburgh got here by beating New Jersey Devils in six games, the last of them in overtime.

Toronto's spring scoring runs through Marian Gaborik99 at 3-5-8 in 6 games, Gary Roberts87 at 1-6-7, Mats Sundin92 at 3-1-4 and Alexander Mogilny89 at 2-1-3. The Bureau's Index has Gaborik up six, and the Book reads him at 98.
