Series 2-0: Luongo's 1.01 and a 2-goal Islanders offense head into Game 3

Toronto carries a two-goal margin in each game and a goaltender who has not been pulled once, on the road tonight for the third meeting

ShareCopy link

The Toronto Maple Leafs Maple Leafs lead New York Islanders 2-0 in this first-round series and take a 1.01 GAA to Long Island tonight for Game 3. Roberto Luongo95 has started both games and posted a .971 save percentage.

New York has scored 2 goals in two games. Rick DiPietro86 faces a .841 save percentage in the series after the first two meetings. The Islanders finished 30-38-4, 84 points, with 243 goals for through the regular season. In the series, that production has evaporated: Chris Simon72's goal in Game 1 and Adrian Aucoin79's in Game 2 are the only tallies.

Toronto's scoring in this series has spread across four names. Marian Gaborik99 carries 2 goals and 2 assists. Gary Roberts85 has 4 assists in 2 games. Mats Sundin92 sits at 2 goals and 1 assist. Petr Chlup87 has 1 goal and 1 assist across the two games.

The regular season produced a 4-1-1 record for Toronto against New York, with an 18-11 goal differential. Two of those seven games went to overtime. Tonight's meeting is the first on Long Island.

No new injuries
