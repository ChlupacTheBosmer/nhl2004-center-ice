LEAFS 4, PENGUINS 2, series 2-1: Gaborik reaches 10, Luongo holds at 1.73

A 4-2 road win puts Toronto ahead in the second round. Game 4 goes Saturday at the same building.

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramTuesday, 26 April 2005
ShareCopy link

The Toronto Maple Leafs Maple Leafs won Game 3 in Pittsburgh, 4-2, on Tuesday night to lead the second-round series 2-1. Game 4 goes at Pittsburgh Penguins on 2005-04-28.

Marian Gaborik98 has 4 goals and 6 assists through 7 playoff games. Gary Roberts88 carries 2 and 6. Mats Sundin91 sits at 3 and 3. Alexander Mogilny90 has 2 goals and an assist.

Roberto Luongo94 is 5-2 this spring with a 1.73 GAA and a .953 save percentage. He has started 7 games while Ed Belfour93, 40 years old on a $6.50M contract, watches from the bench.

From 19 shots to seven goals in two nights

Toronto scored 3 in Game 2 at the Air Canada Centre and 4 on the road tonight, a 7-goal swing over two games after the 19-shot, 1-3 loss on 2005-04-22 that put this series in doubt after one period.

Ramzi Abid81 returns to Pittsburgh on Saturday. He was dealt from the Penguins on 2005-01-25. Mikael Renberg77 suits up for Pittsburgh against the club that traded him on the same date.
