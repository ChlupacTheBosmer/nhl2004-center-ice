DANFORTH: The Index has Richards at +4. That is progress. That is not April.

The Panic Meter sits Green. The Bureau finally moved a centre. The only questions left are contractual.

Frankie DanforthColumnist · Queen City TelegramWednesday, 9 February 2005
ShareCopy link

The Bureau's Development Index has Brad Richards87 at +4. He was not on the risers list in January. I noted that at the time. He is on it now. I want this in the record so that nobody claims I saw it coming.

I did not see it coming. I called for a trade in September, then October, then again on this page when the Index showed four risers and none of them played centre. Richards answered by rising. He is 24, with 54 points in 53 games, an overall of 84, and the Bureau's form measure puts him four above his base. He is also earning $1.00M with 1 contract year left.

That last sentence is what bothers me about the whole thing.

One year is a window, not a foundation

Richards becomes a free agent after this season unless Toronto extends him first. The Book says his potential is 88. If he finishes this season close to his pace now, he collects whatever a 25-year-old centre with that production commands on the open market. I do not have that number. I have $1.00M and a single remaining year and no guarantee he wears blue next October.

The other centre is the one that keeps me at the kitchen table at midnight. Mats Sundin92 is 33 years old, earning $8.40M, with 5 contract years left. His overall is 88. His potential is 60. The gap between those two numbers is what a declining body looks like in the Bureau's system. 63 points in 55 games at 88 overall is not a problem tonight. At 84 next year, at 79 the year after, against $8.40M with no exit, it becomes a cap structure that has no answer until the money runs out.

I have no plan for either number. I have the numbers.

Code Green because February is over

The Panic Meter sits at Green. Toronto has 88 points in 55 games. Boston is 30 points back. There are 7 division games left and the only one that carries any meaning is Boston, and even that is academic. February is a coronation and everyone in the building knows it.

Edmonton Oilers arrives Tuesday at 72 points, the first opponent above 70 to visit the Air Canada Centre since at least January 25. Ryan Smyth86 will generate a close shot total the way the Canadiens did Saturday. Toronto will either score 5 on 32 again or the game will be 2-1 heading into the third period and everyone will remember why the Panic Meter exists.

I will take the +4 on the Index. I will take the 54 points on a million-dollar salary and the 88 potential and the 24 years old and I will take all of it. Then I will sit here next week and ask for the faceoff draw that wins Game 7 until somebody wins one for me or I retire this column. The Index moved and that's not the same as being fixed.
