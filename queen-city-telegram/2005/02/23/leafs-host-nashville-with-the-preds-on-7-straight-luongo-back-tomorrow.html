Leafs host Nashville with the Preds on 7 straight; Luongo back tomorrow

First meeting of the season between the Northeast leader and the Central's top club

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramWednesday, 23 February 2005
ShareCopy link

Toronto Maple Leafs and Nashville Predators meet for the first time tonight at the Air Canada Centre. The Preds sit at 35-15-5, 83 points, and carry a 7-game winning streak into the game. Toronto holds 98 points at 43-10-4, the Northeast's top seat by 29 points over Boston Bruins.

Nashville's division record tells the story of the season: 9-2-2 in the Central, with a 3-0-1 mark against St. Louis Blues and a 3-0-1 against Detroit Red Wings. The Preds have scored 210 goals in 59 games and allowed 172. Their last loss came before the calendar turned to February.

Toronto enters on a 3-game run in which the club has been outshot every night. At Montreal Canadiens on Monday, the shots read 22 for, 27 against, and the final score 3-1. The WSH game on Saturday saw 33 for and 47 against. The CAR game on Thursday: 26 for, 39 against. The goaltending and defensive structure have carried the margin in all three.

Roberto Luongo92 carries a return date of 2005-02-24 on the roster, meaning he is available for Tuesday's meeting with New York Islanders. Maxime Ouellet80 has dressed as G2 and started the last stretch; his save log reads 11 appearances, 36.6 minutes a night. Luongo sits at 42 games played and 61.0 minutes.

The Preds have 7 clubs on the injured list, including Vincent Lecavalier84 (leg, out until April 1) and Cory Stillman78 (wrist, back March 5). Toronto has none of its own on the injured list today.

After tonight, Toronto hosts New York Islanders on Tuesday, travels to Detroit Red Wings on Thursday, and gets Carolina Hurricanes on March 1. The trade deadline is 24 days away.
