Leafs at TB tonight: 7 injuries, a 5-game skid, and a series Toronto already leads

The Lightning carry the most injured players in the league. Toronto has won 2 straight and leads the head-to-head 2-1-0.

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramSunday, 6 February 2005
ShareCopy link

Toronto Maple Leafs (38-9-3, 85 points) visit Tampa Bay Lightning (28-18-3, 67 points) tonight in St. Petersburg with a 2-game win streak and the best record in the league.

TB enters on a 5-game losing streak and with 7 men on the injured list, the most of any club in the league. Pittsburgh Penguins is next with 4.

The Lightning's casualties: Cory Stillman78 (wrist, returns March 5), Dan Boyle75 (head, February 8), Francis Bouillon70 (leg, April 25), Bill Lindsay68 (concussion, March 11), Randy Robitaille68 (wrist, February 25), Nolan Pratt67 (head, February 23), and Tim Taylor64 (hip, March 13). Stillman, with an 84 overall on the Book, is the highest-rated man on the list.

TB has 186 goals for and 169 against in 53 games, good for 2nd in the Southeast Division behind Carolina Hurricanes's 69 points. Toronto's 85 are the most in the league.

The season series favors the visitors. Through 3 meetings, Toronto Maple Leafs lead 2-1-0 against TB and have outscored them 18-7.

Toronto's last 2 games were both road wins: 7-2 at Atlanta Thrashers on January 30 and 3-1 at Florida Panthers on February 5. The team is 18-8 on the road this season.

Ed Belfour91 has appeared in 21 games this season. Roberto Luongo92, acquired on January 25, has 37. Belfour is 39. Luongo is 25.

After tonight, Toronto hosts Montreal Canadiens on Saturday and Edmonton Oilers on February 11 at the Air Canada Centre, travels to Chicago Blackhawks on February 12, and hosts Ottawa Senators on February 15.
