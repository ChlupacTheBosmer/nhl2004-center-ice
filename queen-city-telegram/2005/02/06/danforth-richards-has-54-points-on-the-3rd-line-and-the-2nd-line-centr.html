DANFORTH: Richards has 54 points on the 3rd line and the 2nd-line centre is 18. I want someone to explain the plan in April.

Toronto sits at 85 points. Boston has 56. The division is a formality. The slot behind Sundin is not.

Frankie DanforthColumnist · Queen City TelegramSunday, 6 February 2005
ShareCopy link

The Panic Meter reads Green. 38 wins, 9 losses, 3 overtime defeats, 85 points. Boston Bruins has 56. The Northeast is over, and not in the way that matters to me.

I want to talk about the 2nd-line centre.

Petr Chlup87 is 18 years old. He has 19 goals and 29 points in 42 games at 14.3 minutes a night. His overall on the Book reads 82. The potential column says 95. He is on the second power play unit. He takes draws against the other team's second line every night.

I want to be fair here. Chlup is not a bad player. A teenager putting 19 goals in 42 games in a top-9 assignment is something. He is not the problem. He is the answer the coaching staff picked, and on a development timeline that answer may turn out to be the right one.

My problem is Brad Richards86. He has 54 points in 51 games. 17.4 minutes a night on the 3rd line. A $1.00M contract. He appears on no power play unit. The Bureau's Development Index has him up 4, which means his form is running ahead of his base, and still the lineup card puts him behind an 18-year-old.

54 points from a 3rd-line centre. I have watched this team for 30 years. I know what a 2nd line looks like. It does not look like this.

Mats Sundin92 has 60 points in 53 games, 18.5 minutes a night, $8.40M, 33 years old. He is not going to get younger. He is not going to get a centre who makes the 2nd line a threat unless that centre already has the hands and the head. Richards has them. He has them right now, this season, at 24 years old, for $1.00M.

The Index risers: Marian Gaborik99 up 6 with 73 points, Rostislav Klesla87 up 5, Rick Nash85 up 5, Richards up 4. Three of those four are wingers or a defenceman. The one centre who is rising is buried on the 3rd line. I asked for a 2nd-line centre in September. Nobody has answered.

The 4th line has Ramzi Abid80 with 63 points and 43 assists. That is a depth problem too, but it is not the slot I lose sleep over. I lose sleep over the draw behind Sundin.

Montreal Canadiens come to the Air Canada Centre on Saturday. They are 15-30-4 with 44 points and a 7-20 road record. That is a tune-up. Edmonton Oilers on February 11 are 22-14-6 with 70 points. That is the game I am thinking about.

The calendar has as the trade deadline 41 days away. The phone calls are what matter.

Code Green. I am writing this because the team is winning and I still have to say it.
