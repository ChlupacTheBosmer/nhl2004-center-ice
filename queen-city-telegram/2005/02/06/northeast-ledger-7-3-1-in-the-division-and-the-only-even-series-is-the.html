Northeast Ledger: 7-3-1 in the division, and the only even series is the one against Boston

Toronto's division record carries a 57-32 goal differential. Buffalo has been swept. Only Boston has split the season series.

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramSunday, 6 February 2005
ShareCopy link

Toronto Maple Leafs have played 12 games inside the Northeast division: 7 regulation wins, 1 overtime win, 3 regulation losses, 1 overtime loss. 57 goals for, 32 against. 8 division games remain.

Mats Sundin89's club is 2-2 against Boston Bruins through 4 meetings. Boston entered February at 21-22-4 with 56 points, 2nd in the division. The head-to-head is the only series where Toronto does not hold a winning record, and the only one with regulation games left to decide it.

Against Buffalo Sabres, the record is 3-0-0. Toronto has won all 3 meetings. Buffalo sits at 19-21-5 with 55 points, 3rd in the division. The Sabres have managed 52 goals for and 73 against in 15 division games, the worst goal differential in the Northeast at minus 21.

Montreal Canadiens present the only overtime loss in the division set. The series stands at 1-1-1 through 3 games. Montreal is last at 15-30-4 with 44 points, and their road record is 7-20 in 27 games. They visit the Air Canada Centre on Saturday.

The Ottawa Senators series has 2 meetings: a regulation win and an overtime win for Toronto. The next meeting is February 15 at home.

Toronto's 57-32 in division play is the best in the Northeast by a wide margin. Ottawa Senators are next at 62-56, and the gap between those two differentials is 27 goals across 12 or 14 games depending on how many each club has played.

The next 2 division games come at home: Montreal Canadiens on February 8 and Ottawa Senators on February 15. Both clubs are in the bottom half of the standings. Toronto has beaten Montreal in regulation once this season. The other 2 meetings produced 1 loss and 1 overtime loss.
