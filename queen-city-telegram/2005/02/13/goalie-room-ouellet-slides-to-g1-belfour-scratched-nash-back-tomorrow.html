Goalie Room: Ouellet slides to G1, Belfour scratched, Nash back tomorrow

A goaltending reshuffle ahead of Saturday's Ottawa game, and a 20-year-old returning from injury

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramSunday, 13 February 2005
ShareCopy link

Maxime Ouellet82 will start on Saturday against Ottawa Senators. The shuffle came between Thursday's win and Saturday's game: Ouellet moved from the press box to G1, Roberto Luongo94 dropped from G1 to G2, and Ed Belfour93 was scratched from the lineup.

Ouellet, 23, has 9 appearances this season at 31.4 minutes per game. His overall reads 80 on the Book. Luongo carries a 92 and 40 starts at 61.1 minutes a night. Belfour, 39, has logged 21 games at 58.0 minutes per game on a $6.50M salary with one year remaining.

Toronto Maple Leafs beat Chicago Blackhawks 3-2 on Thursday on 25 shots from each side, its third straight win. The Maple Leafs sit at 40-9-4, 92 points in 57 games, first in the Northeast.

Nash returns for the Ottawa series

Rick Nash82 is expected back tomorrow. His injury return date reads 2005-02-14. The 20-year-old left wing has 32 points in 44 games at 13.2 minutes a night, and the Bureau's Development Index has him up five spots on the risers.

Four Toronto names sit on the risers: Marian Gaborik97 at +6, Rostislav Klesla83 at +5, Nash at +5, and Brad Richards86 at +4.

Nash comes back ahead of Ottawa Senators's visit to the Air Canada Centre on Saturday. The Senators sit at 19-27-6, 52 points, on a two-game losing streak. Marian Hossa83, Ottawa's top forward at 85 overall, is out until March 29 with a back injury.

Toronto leads the season series 1-0-0 with an overtime win, 10 goals for and 7 against across two meetings.

The next five games test the shuffle. Ottawa at home Saturday, Carolina Hurricanes at home on February 18, Washington Capitals on the road on February 20, Montreal Canadiens on the road on February 22, Nashville Predators at home on February 23. Two of the five are division rivals.

If Ouellet holds the net through that stretch, the staff gets rest for Belfour's legs and a real look at Luongo's reserves. If the goals come, the answer points the other way.
