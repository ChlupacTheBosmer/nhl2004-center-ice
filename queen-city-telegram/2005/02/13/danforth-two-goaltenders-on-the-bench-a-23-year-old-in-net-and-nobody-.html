DANFORTH: Two goaltenders on the bench, a 23-year-old in net, and nobody has said why

The Bureau's Development Index carries four Toronto risers. None of them is the new starter.

Frankie DanforthColumnist · Queen City TelegramSunday, 13 February 2005
ShareCopy link

Maxime Ouellet82 starts Saturday. Roberto Luongo94 sits. Ed Belfour93 was not dressed Thursday and will not be dressed Saturday. The man ahead of him carries a 92 on the Book. Ouellet carries an 80.

The Development Index carries Marian Gaborik97 (+6), Rostislav Klesla83 (+5), Rick Nash82 (+5), and Brad Richards86 (+4) among the Toronto risers. Ouellet does not appear on the page. Nine appearances at 31.4 minutes a game is not a sample the Index recognizes, and maybe the Index is wrong. Maybe the staff saw something in practice that the Bureau's weekly form measure missed.

I want Belfour rested. I have been writing about his 58.0 minutes a night for months. Thirty-nine years old, $6.50M, one year left. You protect that man. I understand the instinct completely.

What I do not understand is the choice underneath the protection. Luongo is 25 years old with a 92 and 40 starts. He is in his prime. You have a 92 in your net and you slide him to the second string in February, with the season still in front of you? That is not protecting Belfour. That is replacing Luongo.

The case for the move is fatigue. Forty starts at 61.1 minutes for a 25-year-old over 57 games is a real number. The legs may not recover the way they did at 22. The staff may have counted Luongo's minutes the way I have counted Belfour's and arrived at a different conclusion: that 40 starts is enough to fill the tank and too much to empty it. That is a reasonable position. It is one the coaching staff has not made in public.

I want Belfour protected. I want Luongo in the lineup when the games matter. I want Ouellet to walk in cold and steal one. What I do not get is why the man with the 92 gets benched while a 23-year-old with 9 appearances handles the rest of the regular season.

Marian Hossa85 is out until March 29. Saturday will not test Ouellet. The next five games will.
