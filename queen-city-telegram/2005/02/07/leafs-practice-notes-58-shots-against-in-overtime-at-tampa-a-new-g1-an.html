Leafs Practice Notes: 58 shots against in overtime at Tampa, a new G1, and Montreal at home tomorrow

The 32-shot gap is the worst of Toronto's season, and the crease order reversed the morning after

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramMonday, 7 February 2005
ShareCopy link

The Toronto Maple Leafs were outshot 26 to 58 in a 4-3 overtime loss at Tampa Bay Lightning on Tuesday night. The 32-shot gap is the largest margin Toronto has conceded this season. Tampa Bay carried seven players on injured reserve, the most of any club, and still generated more than double Toronto's shot volume.

Roberto Luongo94 is now listed G1, with Ed Belfour93 at G2. Luongo has appeared in 38 games this season; Belfour in 21. The reversal comes after Toronto's road record dropped to 18 and 9, including 1 regulation loss and 2 overtime defeats across the last 7 visits to opponent arenas.

Montreal Canadiens arrives at the Air Canada Centre tomorrow for the first of 4 games before February 15. Montreal is 15-30-4, last in the Northeast with 44 points, and carries a 7-20 road record. The season series sits at 1-1-1 with Toronto, the Canadiens outscoring the Leafs 13 to 12 across 3 meetings.

Montreal Canadiens coach Stefaan Bullard addressed the gap between payroll and standing when he spoke to The Tunnel's Marcus Bell on January 25. "Our travel budget is zero. Not low. Zero," Bullard said. "The worst in the league. And the modifier on that is minus six on every attribute when we're on the road."

The Canadiens have Richard Zednik79 (hip, back March 3) and Chad Kilger75 (elbow, back March 22) out.

Toronto is 23-4 at home, and the next 3 games after Montreal put 2 of 3 back at the Air Canada Centre.
