DANFORTH: Twenty-six days to the deadline and the centre behind Sundin still is not here

The record says 42-10-4. The calendar says 26 days, and the second line has no centre settled for April.

Frankie DanforthColumnist · Queen City TelegramMonday, 21 February 2005
ShareCopy link

What the calendar has as the trade deadline is 2005-03-19, and the hole behind Mats Sundin90 has not moved since December.

Toronto Maple Leafs is 42-10-4 for 96 points, and the Northeast is Toronto's to lose. Next in the division: Boston Bruins 67, Buffalo Sabres 61, Ottawa Senators 58, Montreal Canadiens 50. That is not the question. The question is who centres the second line in April.

Sundin is 34. He has 70 points in 60 games at 19.3 minutes a night, $8.40M, and 5 years on his contract. Nobody is asking about him.

Behind him the record reads: Brad Richards86, 24, 55 points in 58 games at 16.6 minutes, 1 year left on a $1.00M deal. Petr Chlup85, 18, 38 points in 49 games at 15.6 minutes, 2 years at $2.34M. Jason Spezza71, 21, 15 points in 37 games at 7.5 minutes, $1.00M, 2 years, not dressed.

I wrote in this space on 2005-02-09 that the Index had Richards at +4, and that it was progress and not April. It is still not April. One of those three is the answer and two of them are the reason I keep writing this.

The Panic Meter: Code Orange

The record says calm. Toronto has 304 goals for and 155 against, and it has won 2 straight. I am at Orange because the second centre isn't signed, and 26 days is not a long time.

Richards is a second centre on most clubs in this league. On this club he is either the second centre signed past this season or he is the reason the deadline matters. The record has him with 1 year on the deal.

The division race will not force a decision. The deadline will, on 2005-03-19.
