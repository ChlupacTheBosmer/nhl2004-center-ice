Leafs 5, Capitals 1: 33 shots for, 47 against, and Montreal next

Toronto scored 5 on 33 shots and gave up 47, and the Northeast's last-place club hosts Tuesday.

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramMonday, 21 February 2005
ShareCopy link

Toronto beat Washington 5-1 on Sunday night, its second straight win, on 33 shots to the Capitals' 47.

Toronto Maple Leafs scored 5 times on 33 shots. Washington Capitals took 47 and got 1. The two points lifted Toronto to 42-10-4 and 96 points, best in the East, and dropped Washington to 30-20-7 and 77 points.

Toronto has won 2 in a row since the 4-1 home loss to Ottawa on 2005-02-15. The shots went the other way all night.

The box score, and the box

The penalty record from Sunday is one of the season's heavier nights: Toronto served 11 minutes on 4 penalties, Washington 9 minutes on 3. Both clubs served a five-minute major in the same game. The record names no fighter and marks no penalty as a fighting major.

Montreal, Tuesday

Tuesday sends Toronto to Montreal, last in the Northeast at 50 points and 16-35-4. The series is closer than the standings: Toronto is 2-1-1 in the 4 meetings, with a 17-15 goal edge, and the most recent one was the 5-2 Toronto win at home on 2005-02-08.

Montreal arrives on a 1-game win streak after the 3-1 result at Buffalo on 2005-02-19, and it has changed its goaltending since the last look. Dwayne Roloson86 is dressed as G1 and Eric Fichaud72 as G2.
