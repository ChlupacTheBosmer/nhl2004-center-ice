Leafs 3, Hurricanes 1: Ouellet shuts the door, then meets the club that sent him away

26 shots for, 39 against, a 3-1 win. Sunday the goaltender who left Washington on January 25 starts at the other end.

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramSaturday, 19 February 2005
ShareCopy link

Maxime Ouellet82 allowed 1 goal on 39 shots and the Toronto Maple Leafs beat the Carolina Hurricanes 3-1 on Saturday at the Air Canada Centre. The win moved Toronto to 41-10-4 and 94 points. Boston Bruins, second in the division, sits at 65.

The shot gap did not close. Carolina put 39 on net. Toronto managed 26. Three straight games now where the Leafs could not control possession, and they won two of them.

Ouellet, 23, is in his first season with Toronto. He was sent from Washington on January 25. He is the G1 on the depth chart, an overall of 82 with a 97 potential. In 11 appearances his minutes per game read 36.6. He has not carried a full schedule yet.

The stretch that tests whether this holds

Sunday, Ouellet plays at Washington, where Karel Pilar75 plays at the other end. Pilar went the same direction on the same January 25 date. Toronto leads the season series 2-0 with a 12-5 goal differential. Washington are 30-19-7, 77 points, on a 3-game winning streak.

Tuesday is Montreal Canadiens on the road. Toronto leads that series 2-1-1. The Canadiens are 15-34-4.

Thursday brings Nashville Predators. Nashville have won 6 straight, sit at 34-15-5 and 81 points, and carry the best record in the Central. They outscored opponents 207 to 170. Between now and what the calendar has as the trade deadline on March 19, no opponent on this stretch presents a harder test.

The possession problem is not new. What is new is that a 23-year-old goaltender has been good enough to cover it for three games. Whether that holds against teams with real offense is what the next five answer. Toronto has no injured players, which means the depth is available if the coaching staff wants to adjust.
