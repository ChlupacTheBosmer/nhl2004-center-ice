DANFORTH: Gaborik is at +6, Richards is at +4 for a second straight check, and you are one bad goaltending night from a slide nobody will explain

The shot gap hasn't closed. The second centre hasn't arrived. The two best teams in the East outside Toronto come this week.

Frankie DanforthColumnist · Queen City TelegramSaturday, 19 February 2005
ShareCopy link

26 shots for, 39 against, a 3-1 win. Maxime Ouellet80 gave up 1 goal on 39 shots on Saturday. The win is real. The goaltender is real. The possession problem is the same one it has been since the season opened, and the only difference between a 3-1 win and a 1-4 loss is who gets the puck through to the net and how often.

The Bureau has not moved

Marian Gaborik96 is at +6 on the Development Index. 97 overall. 77 points in 59 games. The #1 riser in the league. I love him. He is not the reason we are outshot. He is not the reason we cannot sustain time in the offensive zone without a turnover that produces a rush the other way. When your hottest player is your best player, the Index is telling you what you already know about one man and nothing about the system around him.

Brad Richards85 is at +4. Same as February 9 when I wrote the number down. 55 points in 57 games at 16.8 minutes a night. That is good production. That is not 21 minutes of center ice against the other team's top pair in the third period of a playoff game. I want someone to explain the plan in April and I will keep asking.

Konstantin Koltsov71 has 18 points in 35 games at 9.9 minutes a night. He is not the answer. Nobody who looks at the sheet thinks he is the answer.

Petr Chlup84 has 37 points in 48 games at 18 years old, 15.4 minutes a night. I think about what that means in two years. I do not think about what it means in April because he is 18 and I am not insane.

The week ahead is a test of whether a goaltender is a strategy

Washington: 30-19-7, 77 points, 3-game winning streak, the top team in the Southeast. Toronto leads the season series 2-0, 12 goals to 5. That was at home. This is at theirs. Washington are healthy (1 man out, the fewest in the league) and they are generating.

Thursday: Nashville Predators. 34-15-5, 81 points, 6 straight wins. They have outscored opponents 207 to 170. They will generate shots at a volume that requires goaltending and defensive structure working together, not a 23-year-old papering over a gap that has been open for months.

What happens when Ouellet faces a team that puts as many on net as Carolina did and their own goaltender has the better night? That is the game I worry about. That is the game where the second center I have been asking for since I had hair would matter. You need a man who wins draws, who keeps the puck in deep, who lets your defensemen breathe. I do not have him. I do not think anyone in that building has him.

The Panic Meter goes to Yellow. The trade deadline, what the calendar calls it, is 28 days away. Gaborik at +6 is not a plan. Richards at +4 for a second straight reading is not progress. The goaltender is real and I hope he keeps stopping things. The center is not. I will keep asking until somebody answers.
