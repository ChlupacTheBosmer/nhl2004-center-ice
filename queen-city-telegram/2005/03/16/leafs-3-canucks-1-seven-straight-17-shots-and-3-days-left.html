LEAFS 3, CANUCKS 1: Seven straight, 17 shots, and 3 days left

Toronto finishes its western swing 3 for 3 and turns toward New York Friday with what the calendar has as the trade deadline on Saturday

Sam OkaforLeafs beat reporter · Queen City TelegramWednesday, 16 March 2005
ShareCopy link

Mats Sundin91's Leafs beat Vancouver Canucks 3-1 on Tuesday night in Vancouver, closing a 3-game western trip with 3 wins, 9 goals for, and 4 against. Toronto put 17 shots on goal against 27 by Vancouver. The win extends the streak to 7 games and lifts the record to 51-12-4, 116 points, 30 clear of Boston Bruins in the [[Northeast]].

The road record reads 27-9. Toronto's away win total is 4 games above Washington Capitals and New Jersey Devils, the next clubs in the East.

New York comes to town

The New York Islanders arrive Friday at 26-33-4, 70 points, on a 2-game slide. The season series is split 1-1-0 with Toronto outscoring New York 4-2. The Islanders have Justin Papineau72 out with a shoulder through 2005-04-11. Mattias Weinhandl71 carries a wrist designation with a return date of 2005-03-18, the same day as this game.

Marian Gaborik98 leads the club at 85 points in 72 games, sitting atop the Bureau's Development Index risers at +6. Alexander Mogilny90 has 76 points in 68 games. Gary Roberts88, 38, carries 70 points in 72 games.

The next stretch

Toronto's five-game run after New York: at Columbus Blue Jackets on 2005-03-20, home to Buffalo Sabres on 2005-03-22, at Boston Bruins on 2005-03-24, at Carolina Hurricanes on 2005-03-25. The Columbus date carries two reacquaintances: Josh Holden74 left Toronto for Columbus on 2005-01-25, and Andreas Stahle71 went the other way the same day.

Boston Bruins returns Joe Thornton89 to its lineup for the first time in this window, slotting him at first-line centre. Thornton is not on Toronto's schedule before 2005-03-24, and the gap is 30 points.

The deadline falls on 2005-03-19, between Friday's game and the Columbus trip.
