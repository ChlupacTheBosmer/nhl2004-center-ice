RECAP: Dallas 6, Chicago 2 -- nine straight, and the road record is the number that counts

The Stars' last 10: 55 for, 29 against. The Blackhawks' last 10: 24 for, 45 against. That is the entire story.

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkFriday, 17 December 2004
ShareCopy link

The Dallas Stars Stars extended their win streak to 9 on Wednesday, beating the Chicago Blackhawks Blackhawks 6-2. Dallas outshot Chicago 30-25.

The road record is 10-7. That is what separates this streak from a December fluke. The building is loud and the ice is wrong on those nights. Dallas is winning them.

In their last 10 games, the Stars have outscored opponents 55-29. The Blackhawks are 24-45 over the same span.

Chicago Blackhawks is 6-17-6 with 22 points and a 10-game losing streak. They sit last in the Central. The skid stretches back to early December. Twenty-four goals for and 45 against over the last 10.

Marty Turco92 has started 26 of 32 games for Dallas, an 81% share. Ron Tugnutt82 has 8. The crease is stable. Last season's Presidents' Trophy winners are 19-9-1 with 45 points, first in the Pacific and second in the West behind Colorado Avalanche.

The schedule ahead is manageable: Philadelphia Flyers today, Detroit Red Wings Sunday, New Jersey Devils Tuesday. None of the three is in a position to challenge a team that is 55-for, 29-against over its last 10.

The streak ends somewhere. Whether 45 points becomes 50, 55, or more is the next thing to see.
