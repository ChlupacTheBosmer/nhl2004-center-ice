Notes from the West: Nashville's streak deepens to seven, and the infirmary keeps filling

The Predators are 15-8-4 and 10-7 on the road. They are also losing two more players to injury this week.

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkFriday, 17 December 2004
ShareCopy link

The Nashville Predators Predators won a seventh straight on Tuesday, 4-3 over the Minnesota Wild Wild. They are 15-8-4 with 40 points and third in the Central. The road record is 10-7.

Four days ago, the streak was at 4. The Predators have been playing the league's best hockey in December, and they are doing it without Oleg Tverdovsky79, whose arm injury keeps him out until January 10. He was signed on December 12 for $181,620.

Vladimir Orszagh77 went down with a leg injury between December 15 and 17. Return: January 8. He was signed on December 12 for $181,620 and had been dressed and in the lineup before the injury. He is now undressed. Greg Johnson80 (neck, return January 11) is also out. The Predators carry 4 men on the injury list.

Jonas Andersson70 and Bill Houlder71 are dressed, both right wingers, filling the spots the departures left open. Tomas Kloucek70, a defenseman, is undressed. The depth chart is thinner than it was two weeks ago. The January return dates are what keep Nashville from fielding a shorter roster in the second half.

Nashville Predators plays Calgary Flames today. Calgary is 9-19-2 with 22 points and a 6-game losing streak. This should be a win. The question is whether it is an eighth, and whether the streak outlasts the return dates for Tverdovsky and Orszagh.
