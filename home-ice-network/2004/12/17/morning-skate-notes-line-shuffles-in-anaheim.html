Morning Skate Notes: line shuffles in Anaheim

Havelid and Krog into the lineup, Lydman and Kwiatkowski out, Smirnov up to the second line. The Ducks won 3-2 in OT in Pittsburgh.

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkFriday, 17 December 2004
ShareCopy link

Mighty Ducks of Anaheim put Niclas Havelid73 on the top pair and Jason Krog65 on the third line, and dropped Tuomas Lydman70 and Joel Kwiatkowski63 from the dress list. The Ducks won 3-2 in overtime at Pittsburgh Penguins on Tuesday.

The shakeup is deeper than the dress list suggests. Havelid, who was not in uniform before this week, now carries the top pair, the first kill, and the first power play unit. That displaces Ruslan Salei72 from the first unit; he drops to the second pair and picks up a fifth power play slot. Krog comes in on the third line at left wing, a spot Lydman vacates.

Lydman had been a second-line left winger on the second power play unit and the first kill before the change. He does not dress. Kwiatkowski, who carried the third pair right side and the second kill, is also out of the lineup.

The center shift is quieter. Erik Cole75 takes the first kill, up from the second. Alexei Smirnov67 steps from the third line to the second line and the second power play unit, a promotion that puts a faster skater in front of Jean-Sebastien Giguere93 on the odd man.

Giguere talked about this exact dynamic last week. "The coach, he makes a change and the guys, they respond. They, they want to show him," he told The Tunnel's Jan Kowalczyk after the 3-2 win at Chicago.

The road record is 4-12. The overtime win in Pittsburgh is the fourth road victory in 16 road games.
