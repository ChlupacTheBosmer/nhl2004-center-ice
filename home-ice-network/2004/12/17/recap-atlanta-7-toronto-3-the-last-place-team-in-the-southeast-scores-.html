RECAP: Atlanta 7, Toronto 3 -- the last-place team in the Southeast scores 7

Two days after shutting out San Jose, the Thrashers outshot the league's best club 30-19 and won by 4.

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkFriday, 17 December 2004
ShareCopy link

The Atlanta Thrashers Thrashers beat the Toronto Maple Leafs Maple Leafs 7-3 on Thursday. Two days earlier, they shut out San Jose Sharks 2-0. The team that is 7-18-2 with 22 points, last in the Southeast, has now won two straight.

The Toronto result is the one that will get picked up. The Maple Leafs had won 9 straight going into the Thrashers' building. They left with 3 goals and 19 shots. Atlanta had 30.

Byron Dafoe82 has started 28 of 30 games for Atlanta, a 93% share. Milan Hnilicka80 has 9. The crease is stable. The story is on the ice in front of it: a team with 22 points outshooting the league's best club 30-19 and scoring 7.

The Thrashers are 5-9 at home and 5-11 on the road. The 7-3 win does not change either of those records. It changes how the team carries itself, which is all a 22-point team can point to.

111 for, 132 against in 30 games. The 7-3 is a blip in those numbers. The 2-0 shutout over San Jose is the more telling result. The back end can keep a team out when the job is that simple.

The schedule through the weekend is a test: Philadelphia Flyers on Saturday, then Carolina Hurricanes on Monday. Philadelphia is 9-14-3 with 27 points. Carolina is 12-7-7 with 43 points. Two games that will tell you whether the Atlanta surge is two wins or a pattern, and whether the 22-point total starts to look like 24, 26, or something else by the end of December.
