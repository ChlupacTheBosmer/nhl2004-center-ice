Notes from the West: two 40-point clubs meet Wednesday, and the road split is the number

Vancouver and Nashville are tied at 40. The game is in Nashville, where the Predators are 8-5 and the Canucks are 7-8 on the road.

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkFriday, 17 December 2004
ShareCopy link

Vancouver and Nashville are both at 40 points, and they play each other Wednesday night in Nashville. Colorado sits alone at 50, so this is a fight for the second spot in the West.

The split that matters is the road one. Vancouver Canucks is 7-8 away from home. Nashville Predators is 8-5 in front of its own crowd. The Canucks have the scoring edge in the last 10 (46 for, 41 against); the Predators have the defensive edge (40 for, 28 against). One team wins pretty, the other wins ugly.

The streak is the louder number. Nashville has won seven straight, and Tomas Vokoun90 has played 25 of the club's 30 games. After the 4-3 win over Minnesota on Monday, he told The Tunnel's Marcus Bell: "The streak is the room's. I just hold the door, you know."

The infirmary complicates the road trip

Nashville is managing a four-player absence list. Oleg Tverdovsky80 (arm, back 2005-01-10) has been out since before the last edition. Vladimir Orszagh77 (leg, back 2005-01-08) is new, designated between 2004-12-15 and 2004-12-17. Greg Johnson80 (neck, back 2005-01-11) has been out the longest. The fourth is not on the designated list but the club carries him as out.

On the defense, Tomas Kloucek71 dropped from the lineup this week. Jason York steps onto the top pair, Andrew Ference73 shifts to the second pair, and Curtis Murphy66 takes a second-pair role with a power play assignment.

Vancouver's gaps are longer but narrower: Ed Jovanovski84 (knee, back 2005-02-19) and Brent Sopel76 (shoulder, back 2004-12-23) are both out, but the Canucks have absorbed those losses in a 16-9-4 record.

Wednesday in Nashville. The loser drops to 40 with the division title and the seeding both slipping, and a week of games still to play.
