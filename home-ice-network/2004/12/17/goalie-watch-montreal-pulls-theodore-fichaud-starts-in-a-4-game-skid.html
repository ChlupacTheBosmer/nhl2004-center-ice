Goalie Watch: Montreal pulls Theodore, Fichaud starts in a 4-game skid

The Canadiens' 87% starter is out of the lineup. The guy with 5 games this season is now the number one.

Luc PelletierMontreal correspondent · Home Ice NetworkFriday, 17 December 2004
ShareCopy link

The Montreal Canadiens Canadiens pulled Jose Theodore83 from the lineup on Tuesday. Eric Fichaud66, who has 5 games this season, is now the starter. J.F. Damphousse65, 4 games, is the backup. Theodore has 26 appearances, an 87% share of the club's 30 games.

The change comes after a 4-game losing skid and a 3-10 road record. Montreal is 9-18-1 with 23 points, last in the Northeast. They lost 6-2 to Boston Bruins on Tuesday, the fourth straight defeat.

Fichaud's 5 games came while Theodore was the established starter. His 17% share tells you he was a spot guy. Damphousse's 4 games and 13% share put him in the same category. The crease that was one man's is now two players who have combined for 9 starts in 30 games.

The schedule is not kind. Montreal hosts St. Louis Blues today, then travels to New York Rangers on Sunday. New York is 16-9-4 with 44 points. That is a team you do not want to face on the road with a new starter in his third game of a four-game trip.

The question is whether Fichaud can hold a crease for a team that is 3-10 on the road, 102 goals for in 30 games, and 131 against. Theodore's return is a separate question, and the schedule will answer it before the room does.

The Canadiens carry 3 men on the injury list, the same number as a week ago. The crease change is a response to results. Nobody in that locker room is surprised, but nobody is celebrating either. Fichaud gets the start, and the road record is the scoreboard he is trying to move.
