Notes from the West: Nashville wins four straight, loses Tverdovsky to an arm injury

The Predators are 12-8-4 and 10-7 on the road, but their newly signed defenseman is out until January.

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkSaturday, 11 December 2004
ShareCopy link

Nashville Predators ran its win streak to 4 with a 4-3 overtime decision in St. Louis on December 7 and a 4-2 win in Anaheim on December 8, and it will spend the next month doing it without Oleg Tverdovsky81.

The league designated the defenseman's injury an arm problem, suffered sometime between December 8 and December 11. His return date is firm: 2005-01-10. That is roughly 12 games, minimum, for a club that already has Greg Johnson81 out with a neck until 2005-01-11.

The timing stings because Nashville signed Tverdovsky on 2004-12-05 for $296,310. The ink was three days old.

The road record is the real story

Strip the injury out and the Predators are the quietest good team in the West. They sit 12-8-4 for 34 points, and the split that matters is away from home: 10-7 on the road against 5-5 in their own building. They have scored 108 and allowed 98, and the last ten read 40 for, 33 against.

Tomas Vokoun91 has started 22 of 27, an 81% share, and the crease behind him has been tidy all fall. Nashville's injury ledger shows 12 spells but only 87 days lost, the kind of season where the trainer's room empties as fast as it fills.

Tverdovsky's month changes that math. The Blues, last in the Central at 7-15-2, come to town 2004-12-14 the same night San Jose Sharks visits, a homestand chance to push the streak past 4 with or without him.

Special teams decide Novembers. Depth on the blue line decides these middle weeks, and Nashville is about to find out how much of it has.
