Notes from the West: Colorado is 14-2 on the road, and Kariya has been watching

The Avalanche lead the West with the league's best away record, missing their top winger since early December.

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkMonday, 20 December 2004
ShareCopy link

Colorado Avalanche is 14-2 on the road, the best away record in the league through 31 games. The Avalanche lead the West at 50 points, 23-5-2, with 154 goals for and 92 against. Dallas has 45 in 33. Nashville sits at 42 in 31.

The split is the story: 10-5 in Denver, 14-2 everywhere else.

Paul Kariya90 has been on the shelf with a leg injury, the league's designation, since at least Dec 1, with a return date of Jan 23. The road wins have come without him.

Storr carries the net on every road trip

Jamie Storr84 has 23 starts in 31 games, 74 percent of the workload. Trevor Kidd81 has 7, Philippe Sauve80 has 3. The depth behind Storr is thin.

The Colorado injury ledger has 11 spells and 70 days lost. Kariya is the longest current absence. The Avalanche entered this week on a 1-game losing streak and host Minnesota Wild on Tuesday before Vancouver Canucks visits Thursday. A road date at San Jose Sharks follows on Dec 26.

Minnesota is 9-21-1 with 23 points. Vancouver is 17-9-4 with 42. Colorado has a favorable home pair this week, then heads to San Jose, a club at 11-15-3 and 31 points that will be looking to snap its own 4-game slide.
