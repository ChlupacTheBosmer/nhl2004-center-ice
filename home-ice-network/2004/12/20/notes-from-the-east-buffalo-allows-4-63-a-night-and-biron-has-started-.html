Notes from the East: Buffalo allows 4.63 a night, and Biron has started 26 of 30

The Sabres gave up 7 to Boston on Saturday and sit last in the league in goals against per game.

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkMonday, 20 December 2004
ShareCopy link

Buffalo Sabres lost 7-6 to Boston Bruins on Saturday, adding to a 10-game stretch that has produced 54 goals against. The Sabres sit last in the league at 4.63 goals against per game across 30 contests.

Buffalo is 11-14-2, 30 points, fourth in the Northeast. Boston scored 7 on 32 shots. Buffalo's 6 would have been enough in most buildings this season.

Martin Biron84 has started 26 of Buffalo's 30 games, 87 percent of the schedule. Ryan Miller77 has 7 starts, Mika Noronen76 has 5. The coaching staff has returned to Biron after every injury spell and the workload has never come down.

The defensive rotation has never been whole

The Buffalo injury ledger carries 10 spells and 62 days lost this season. Janne Laukkanen71 is out with a concussion until Jan 5. Chris Drury80 has missed time in 4 separate spells, the latest opening this week. The defense has cycled bodies all November and into December.

Buffalo's home record is 8-8. On the road they are 6-8. The goals against number is a whole-roster problem.

Buffalo hosts Florida Panthers tonight and visits Montreal Canadiens Tuesday. Florida has 99 goals in 31 games, Montreal 109 in 32. Those are the thinnest offenses on this two-game stretch, and the Sabres will need the low-event games to slow the number down before it gets further from reach.
