Correction: Visnovsky was never hurt; the Kings' designation was a form marker

LA beat San Jose 4-3 in overtime Saturday; Cechmanek has started 26 of 31, 84 percent of the workload

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkSunday, 19 December 2004
ShareCopy link

Lubomir Visnovsky80 picked up the league's form marker between Dec 17 and Dec 19, which is not an injury and did not keep him out. It is the second spell of his season.

The Los Angeles Kings defenseman, rated 80 by the Bureau, is the 4th name currently in the room. Adam Deadmarsh87 (87, groin) is out until Jan 21. Jason Allison87 (87, wrist) is out until Dec 22. Tomas Zizka76 (76, foot) is out until Jan 6.

Los Angeles Kings beat the San Jose Sharks Sharks 4-3 in overtime on Saturday, outshot 23-21. They are 8-14-5, 29 points, 3rd in the Pacific, outscored 122-108 over 31 games. Their last 10 are tighter: 36 for, 37 against.

Roman Cechmanek91 has started 26 of 31, an 84 percent workload. Cristobal Huet80 has 9 starts. Tim Thomas75 has 1.

Los Angeles Kings have absorbed 14 injury spells this season, 113 days lost. The ledger runs across the roster: wrists, elbows, groins, feet, heads. Cechmanek and Huet both appear on it. Visnovsky has been on it twice.

Andy Murray told The Tunnel's Marcus Bell after a 3-2 win over Phoenix Coyotes on Dec 15 that the fix is defensive, not a goalie change.

"We've got to be more consistent in front of him. We manage the puck, we block, we keep it out of his corners. That's the job on the ice. The rest is his."

The Kings are 5-8 at home, 7-11 on the road. The Mighty Ducks of Anaheim Ducks visit the Staples Center on Sunday. Jean-Sebastien Giguere93 has 28 starts in 31 games for Anaheim, a 90 percent share.

---

Correction. An earlier edition of this piece read the league's roster designation as an injury. It is not one: the marker carried here is a form or fitness icon, and the men named were available. Only a designation that names a body part is an injury. The passage has been corrected.
