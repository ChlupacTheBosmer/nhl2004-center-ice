RECAP: Philadelphia 5, Dallas 1 -- a 7-game skid ends behind Hackett's return

The Flyers outshot the Stars 29-22; Hitchcock benched Esche, scratched Kallio, and installed new signing Seidenberg

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkSunday, 19 December 2004
ShareCopy link

The Philadelphia Flyers Flyers beat the Dallas Stars Stars 5-1 on Saturday at the Wachovia Center, ending a 7-game losing streak and stopping a 9-game win streak by the Pacific's first-place club.

Philadelphia Flyers outshot them 29-22 at home. The Flyers are 10-14-3, 29 points, 4th in the Atlantic, 9-8 on the season in front of their own crowd.

Their last 10, newest first: W, W, L, L, L, L, L, L, L, W. The 7-game slide was the longest stretch of the season for the club.

8 slots moved before the puck dropped

Ken Hitchcock shuffled the lineup in bulk. Jeff Hackett83 returned between the pipes; Robert Esche73 was a healthy scratch. Tomi Kallio69 was dropped from the dressing room entirely.

Dennis Seidenberg74, signed Dec 15 for $208,390, went straight onto the blue line. Topi Lehto62 joined the 4th line. Per-Johan Axelsson74 shifted from left wing on the 3rd unit to right wing on the 2nd. Donald Brashear72 moved from right wing to left wing on the same line. Adam Graves73 slid up from the 4th line to the 3rd.

Hackett now has 24 starts in 30 games, an 80 percent workload. Esche had 9 starts before the scratch. Neil Little68 has 3.

Philadelphia Flyers's season totals read 114 goals for, 132 against in 30 games. Two wins do not fix that gap.

The Flyers host the Ottawa Senators Senators on Dec 21.
