Goalie Watch: New Jersey benches Brodeur after 20 starts in 22 games

Ari Ahonen moves into the crease for the league's lowest goals-against total

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkSaturday, 4 December 2004
ShareCopy link

Martin Brodeur92 is not in New Jersey's lineup. The coach moved Ari Ahonen85 to starter, added Corey Schwab78 as backup, and dropped Brodeur from the dressing list entirely. The change landed sometime between December 1 and December 4.

New Jersey Devils sits at 13-2-3 with 37 points after 22 games. They have allowed 63 goals, the fewest of any club in the league. Brodeur played 20 of those 22 games, a 91% share. No other starter carries that kind of workload on a winning team.

The Devils are on a 4-game win streak. They beat San Jose Sharks 7-1 on November 30 and Philadelphia Flyers 5-4 on December 2. The shuffle came in the middle of that run.

Brodeur does not appear on injured reserve. He is healthy and available. His entry in the lineup change reads: G1 before, nothing after. No explanation on the sheet.

Ari Ahonen has played 3 games this season, a 14% share. Corey Schwab has played zero. The Devils switched on the fewest-goals-allowed team in the league, mid-streak.

New Jersey Devils hosts Vancouver Canucks tonight and Pittsburgh Penguins on December 6, then travels to Toronto on December 7. Ed Belfour91 returns from injury on December 5, the first chance a Toronto coach will have to sort its own crease. Both creases get a Saturday test.

New Jersey's remaining schedule through December 10 includes Pittsburgh Penguins (7-11-3, L4) and San Jose Sharks (8-10-2, L1) at home. The next road date after Toronto comes December 10 against San Jose Sharks again. If Ahonen holds through this stretch, the coach will have navigated the easiest window of the month with the 91% starter watching from the bench.
