Numbers Game: Colorado has 42 points without Sakic or Kariya

The Avalanche are 20-4-0 with the league's best road record and two 84-overall forwards out

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkSaturday, 4 December 2004
ShareCopy link

Colorado is 20-4-0 after 25 games with 42 points, the most in the league. Joe Sakic84 (overall grade 84, return targeted December 17) and Paul Kariya84 (overall grade 84, return targeted January 23) have both been out since before December 1.

The Avalanche have scored 50 goals and allowed 21 over their last 10 games. They are 12-1 on the road, the best away record in the league by total wins. On November 30, they beat Edmonton Oilers 5-3 in Edmonton. Their 125 goals for and 67 against over 25 games sit behind no one in this column.

Sakic's absence runs through at least 8 more games after tonight. Kariya's runs through roughly 30. Colorado will play nearly half its remaining schedule without both centers before the line looks the way anyone drew it in September.

The goalie position shifted too. Philippe Sauve74 moved to starter, Jamie Storr78 dropped to G2, and Trevor Kidd75 came off the dressing list. Sauve has played 3 games, a 12% share. Storr has 18 starts and a 72% share. The coach made the move with the team at 20-4-0.

Colorado Avalanche hosts Montreal Canadiens at home on December 6, then visits Vancouver Canucks on December 11. The road record gives them a real shot at 13-2 in Vancouver against a club that has allowed 79 goals in 24 games and is on a 4-game win streak. That is the kind of game depth players win by stepping into larger minutes.

The question for November is whether this holds once opponents adjust to the lineup. The question for January is whether the health arrives before the point total becomes irrelevant to the race. Right now Colorado leads the Northwest by 9 points over Vancouver Canucks, and the Avalanche have played one fewer game.
