Goalie Watch: Islanders pull DiPietro after a 4-0 loss; Snow starts in Pittsburgh

Three netminders, one crease, and a team that scored zero on 13 shots.

Luc PelletierMontreal correspondent · Home Ice NetworkTuesday, 7 December 2004
ShareCopy link

Rick DiPietro83 is out. Garth Snow78 is in. Stephen Valiquette75 was added to the roster this week. The New York Islanders goaltending change is done, and Snow starts on the road tonight in Pittsburgh.

The move followed a 4-0 loss to Toronto on 2004-12-06 in which New York Islanders was outshot 14-13. DiPietro had carried 20 starts in 26 games, a 77% share. Snow had made 11 appearances (42%). Valiquette was not on the active list before the move.

The Islanders are 12-11-1 with 29 points, third in the Atlantic Division behind New York Rangers (40 in 29) and New Jersey Devils (39 in 23). Their goal differential is +1: 98 scored, 97 allowed.

Over the last 10, New York Islanders is 4-6 with 22 goals for and 33 against. A five-game losing streak sits in the middle of that stretch, the kind of slide that turns a .500 season into a 29-point slog. Their home record reads 9-6; on the road they are 5-6. The problem travels about as well as they do.

Tonight's opponent, Pittsburgh Penguins, is 7-11-4 with 22 points and on a 5-game losing streak. Johan Hedberg82 has started 20 of 24 games at 83% and takes the crease.

For New York Islanders, the bigger absence remains Mike Peca85 (84 overall), out until 2005-01-13. Kenny Jonsson79 (78 overall, defense) is also out through 2004-12-16. Without their top two shutdown pieces, the Islanders have managed just 98 goals in 26 games.

The schedule ahead offers no rest. New York Islanders visits Pittsburgh (12/07), hosts Chicago Blackhawks (12/10, a team on a 4-game losing streak at 6-12-5), then goes on the road at Florida Panthers (12/13) and Tampa Bay Lightning (12/14). Four games in seven days will test whether a new starter can hold.
