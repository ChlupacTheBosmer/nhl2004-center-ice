Notes from the West: Nashville's streak hits 9, and 25 goals against in 10 is the number

The Predators lead the Central by 4 points and the run is built on the back end.

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkWednesday, 22 December 2004
ShareCopy link

Nashville Predators 6, Vancouver Canucks 3 on Saturday. Nine straight wins, 44 points in 32 games, 4 clear at the top of the Central. Detroit Red Wings sits 4 points back after beating Dallas Stars 5-3 the same night.

The Predators scored 41 and allowed 25 across their last 10. Through 32 games, Nashville has given up 110 total.

Tomas Vokoun90 has started 26 of 32 games, 81 percent of the workload. "You know, when you are in the net, you see the bench, you see the guys. And they are calm. That is the thing. Seven games, and the feeling, it is the same," Vokoun told The Tunnel's Marcus Bell after the seventh win on Dec 14. Nine later, nothing's changed.

Vokoun has no shutout this season. "25 games, and the zero, it is not there," he said on The Tunnel. "It will come. But the win is more important."

Three absences and a lineup rotation

The Predators have 4 players on the injured list. Greg Johnson80 (neck, return Jan 11) and Oleg Tverdovsky80 (arm, return Jan 10) are the two longest. Vladimir Orszagh77 (leg, return Jan 8) is the third named on the current shelf.

The lineup rotated over the weekend. Jonas Andersson71 was dropped after a concussion logged earlier in the season. Mark Eaton71 entered on the blue line. Aaron Gavey70 took the fourth line center spot. Kirk Vas74 was scratched.

Two road teams come to Nashville this week

Nashville visits Florida Panthers on Tuesday, hosts Dallas Stars on Thursday and Detroit Red Wings on Saturday. Dallas is 19-11-1 and leads the Pacific. Detroit is on a 5-game win streak, 40 points in 32, the closest hunter in the Central.

Nashville is 10-7 on the road this season. That number carried the first five wins. The Tuesday game at Florida, a club that is 11-17-2 and has lost 3 straight, is the easiest of the three. The homestand against Dallas and Detroit is where nine becomes twelve or stalls at ten.
