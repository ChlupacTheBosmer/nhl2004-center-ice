Goalie Watch: San Jose sits Nabokov after five straight, Toskala starts tonight

Nabokov has started 48 of 57 games and is scratched for the first time in three weeks; the Sharks have scored 16 goals in their last 10

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkSunday, 13 February 2005
ShareCopy link

Evgeni Nabokov90 is scratched tonight against Philadelphia Flyers for the first time since his January 22 start. The decision follows five consecutive defeats, during which San Jose Sharks scored 16 goals and allowed 26 across 10 games.

Evgeni Nabokov has started 48 of 57 San Jose games this season, an 84 percent share of the workload. He is the Vezina-winning goaltender from last season and the most-used netminder on the roster by a wide margin. Tonight, Vesa Toskala79 goes in goal. Toskala has 12 starts in 57 games, a 21 percent share.

The Sharks sit 17-27-9 and 51 points, 12th in the Western Conference. Their five-game skid includes four regulation losses and one overtime defeat. The offense has not been the only problem: they allowed 26 goals in that stretch against a group that has 159 goals for in 57 games across the whole season.

What the next 72 hours tell you

San Jose hosts three games in four nights: Philadelphia tonight, Phoenix Coyotes on Sunday, and Calgary Flames on Tuesday. All three at home, where the Sharks are 10-16 this season. They face Vancouver Canucks on Friday. A goaltending change during a home stand against four clubs that are all above .500 does not fix a scoring problem, but it resets the room.

The first meeting between San Jose and Philadelphia went 6-4 to the Sharks, with Nabokov in net. Tonight is the second of four scheduled meetings this season.

Toskala's 12 starts have come mostly in relief or spot duty. His record in those games is not on the sheet I have tonight. Benching an 84 percent workhorse after five losses says something about the group playing in front of the netminder.

The Sharks have allowed 187 goals in 57 games, which puts them 2nd-fewest in the Pacific Division, ahead of LA (196), PHX (178) and CGY (208), behind only Dallas Stars (177). Those numbers say the defensive structure is there. The losing streak is about goals scored.
