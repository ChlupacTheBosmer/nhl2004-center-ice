Injury Report: Ottawa signs Hossa, and the back designation arrives within 3 days

The Senators paid $341,600 for an 83-overall right wing who will miss roughly 50 days

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkMonday, 7 February 2005
ShareCopy link

Ottawa Senators signed Marian Hossa88 on February 3 for $341,600. The right wing the Bureau's Book grades at 83 is on the injured list with a back designation, return targeted for March 29.

The league's onset window runs February 6 to February 7. The gap between the signing and the shelf is 3 days at most.

Ottawa sits 19-25-6, 52 points, 10th in the East, 3 behind 8th-place New York Islanders. The club has 28 games left and the season's last game falls April 6. Hossa's return date places him in the final week at best, assuming no setback.

This is Hossa's third trip to the shelf this season. Ottawa's injury ledger carries two earlier spells for him, neither with a named body part.

The Senators have scored 181 goals in 54 games, 9th among the 15 East clubs. Hossa would have addressed a top-six right-wing vacancy the day he signed.

A goaltender shuffle on the same night

Ottawa also swapped its goaltending pairing for the February 5 game at New York Rangers, a 3-2 regulation loss. Patrick Lalime90 moved ahead of Martin Prusek83 in the depth chart. Lalime has made 45 appearances, 83% of Ottawa's games. Prusek has 11.

The Senators' total lost days sit at 77, the second-lowest in the league behind San Jose Sharks's 70. Hossa's back bumps that number within the week. Ottawa had banked on staying that clean through a February that now sits a top-six winger short.

Ottawa Senators hosts Atlanta Thrashers on February 8. Ottawa carries a 0-1-1 mark in that series this season, one overtime win and one regulation loss.
