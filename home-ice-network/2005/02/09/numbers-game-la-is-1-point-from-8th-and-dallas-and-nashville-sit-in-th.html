Numbers Game: LA is 1 point from 8th, and Dallas and Nashville sit in the next 72 hours

The Kings have won 3 straight. Their next two opponents have combined for 5 regulation wins and 2 overtime wins against them this season, with 0 losses.

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkWednesday, 9 February 2005
ShareCopy link

Los Angeles Kings sits at 54 points, 1 behind Columbus Blue Jackets (55) for the 8th and final playoff spot in the West. The Kings are riding a 3-game win streak heading into tonight's road game at Dallas Stars (70 points) and Thursday's matchup at Nashville Predators (71), neither of which they have beaten this season.

Seven meetings, zero wins, 21 goals for and 28 against. The Dallas series was close: 3 of 4 games were decided by 1 goal. The Nashville series was not. NSH has outscored LA by 6 in 3 games.

The 3-game streak that brought LA here includes a 3-2 overtime win at Carolina Hurricanes on February 7, where Roman Cechmanek91 stopped 20 of 22 shots against his former club. The run moved LA from 2 points back of 8th to 1 point back. They have played 54 games; Columbus has played 53. LA has 28 games left on the schedule.

Dallas is 20-5 at home, the best home record in the conference. Nashville is 17-8 at home, second in that column. LA is 10-17 on the road, the 4th-worst away record in the West.

Cechmanek has started 45 of 54 (83% share), Cristobal Huet80 has 14, so no workload issue. The one body on the shelf is center Eric Belanger78, a 78-overall pivot out with an ankle designation through March 4.

After the Dallas and Nashville games, LA comes home Tuesday to host Calgary Flames, the West's 44-point team with a 9-20 home record and a 10-16 road record. CGY beat Edmonton Oilers 4-2 on February 7, which tells you nothing useful about what Tuesday looks like. It tells you the streaks in this league end on random nights.

The arithmetic: LA needs points from at least one of these top-4 clubs to stay within range while Columbus (29 games left) continues to put them up. If LA goes 0-2 this week and Columbus wins 2 of its next 4, the gap widens to 4. If LA steals a point in either game, the Tuesday matchup against Calgary becomes the one that decides whether this club is still in the conversation on February 16.
