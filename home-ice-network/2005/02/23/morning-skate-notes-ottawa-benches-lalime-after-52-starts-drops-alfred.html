Morning Skate Notes: Ottawa benches Lalime after 52 starts, drops Alfredsson to the second line

Thirteen lineup changes after a 3-2 loss to Florida, including the first demotion of the captain this season

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkWednesday, 23 February 2005
ShareCopy link

Patrick Lalime88 started 52 of 62 games for Ottawa Senators this season, 84% of the work. He is out. Ray Emery78, who has appeared in 3 games all year, gets the start Feb 25 against Minnesota Wild.

The change is one of 13 made after a 3-2 loss to Florida Panthers on Feb 20, the second in a row for a club that sits 12th in the East at 58 points, 3 behind Buffalo Sabres for the last playoff berth.

Daniel Alfredsson86 moved from the first half-ice role to the second. He still dresses on the opening power-play unit. That is the captain's first demotion this season, and it came the same night the staff benched the goaltender who has carried the team all year.

Shaun Van Allen74 jumped to the first line centre spot. Bryan Smolinski76 dropped from the opening faceoff unit to the third. Denis Hamel72 was added to the top-half penalty-killing and the second line right wing. Petr Schastlivy70 moved from left wing to centre on the fourth line. Vaclav Varada73 lost his power-play slot. Radek Bonk79 swapped special-teams assignments. Mike Fisher78 was added to the second-line faceoff duty and the penalty-kill second unit. Steffen Prommersberger63 enters the lineup on the fourth-line right wing.

Todd White81 is gone from the roster entirely. He is on the injured list with a hand designation, return date March 29. Before the injury he carried special-teams roles on all four units, which is why his absence triggers so many shuffles. Marian Hossa85 is also out, a back designation, same return date.

Ottawa's last 10 games: 2 wins, 7 regulation losses, 1 overtime loss. 24 goals scored, 28 allowed. The 2-game slide dropped them from 9th to 12th.

The Senators have not played Minnesota this season. The Minnesota Wild Wild are on a 3-game losing streak, 16-31-6, and sit 13th in the West. Ottawa is at home. The staff will hope the shake-up starts with the easiest opponent on the remaining schedule.
