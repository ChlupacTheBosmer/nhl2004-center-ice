Numbers Game: STL holds 8th by 3, and the two clubs chasing it are both winning

The gap between the last playoff spot and the first club out shrank from 5 to 3 over the last two game days

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkWednesday, 23 February 2005
ShareCopy link

St. Louis Blues sits in the West's last playoff spot at 63 points, 21 games left, on a 3-game win streak. Phoenix Coyotes and Los Angeles Kings are 3 points back, and both added 4 points since Monday. The gap that stood at 5 before the last edition is now 3.

STL's run of 6 straight wins broke on an overtime loss in that window, but the Blues have won 3 since, including a 3-2 overtime win over Columbus Blue Jackets on Feb 21.

PHX has won 3 straight after losing 5 of 6 before that stretch. They added points at Chicago (4-2 on Feb 20) and Dallas (2-1 on Feb 21), two wins against teams ahead of them in the table. Tonight they host Calgary Flames, which they beat twice already this season, with a chance to close the gap to 1.

LA's two wins came at Philadelphia (4-3) and Buffalo (3-2). The club has scored 29 goals in its last 10 games, second only to STL (30) among the five clubs jostling near the 8th-place line. LA also changed goaltenders this week: Cristobal Huet80 had been listed as G1 and is now second behind Roman Cechmanek90, who has played 49 of 61 games this season at an 80% share. Tim Thomas75 drops from G2 to out of the rotation.

Columbus is the club falling back. The Columbus Blue Jackets Blue Jackets have lost 3 straight, including the overtime defeat at STL on Feb 21, and moved Pascal Leclaire93 from G1 to the press box in favour of Marc Denis89, who started that game. Leclaire has 36 starts this season; Denis, 23.

STL plays Columbus in St. Louis on Feb 24. A regulation win puts the Blues 5 clear of Columbus. A loss keeps the race at 3 points and puts pressure back on a team whose point differential is minus-34, the worst of any club still alive in the playoff picture.

The five-club race has 4 points separating first through fourth. STL's schedule gives them 21 games left with 7 against the bottom three in the division. PHX has 22 left and is on the longest active winning streak in the Pacific.
