Numbers Game: Buffalo and New York sit at 55 points for 8th, and they play each other Sunday

Two losses for the Islanders, two wins for the Sabres, and a game in hand that could close a 4-win gap

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkSunday, 6 February 2005
ShareCopy link

The piece

Two clubs, 55 points, one playoff spot between them. Buffalo Sabres and New York Islanders meet Sunday on Long Island with the tie at the bottom of the Eastern Conference bracket up for grabs.

Buffalo is 19-9-5 through 51 games. New York is 23-3-3 through 52. Same points, 4 wins apart. The Sabres carry a game in hand, one fewer result required to overtake on the column that breaks the tie.

Buffalo has won 2 straight, both on the road: 4-2 at New Jersey on February 4, 3-2 in overtime at San Jose on January 30. New York has lost 3 straight, all at home: 1-4 to Philadelphia, 2-3 to Montreal, 0-2 to Pittsburgh. The Islanders were outscored 9-3 across that stretch on their own ice, where they sit 15-14 this season.

Buffalo's goal differential is -23. New York's is -9. The Sabres have been outscored by 14 more goals than their rival for the same standing. The tiebreaker column reads wins, and Buffalo sits four back there. Buffalo's 5 wins over the last 10 came with some margin; New York's 5 over that same window came before the 3-game slide.

A Buffalo win on Feb 8 moves the Sabres to 57 points and leaves New York at 55 with the game in hand now a full 2-point gap. A New York win puts the Islanders at 57 with the 4-win cushion intact and a game played that Buffalo cannot undo.

Buffalo's path after Sunday: San Jose Sharks February 11, Detroit Red Wings February 13. New York's path: Washington Capitals February 7, Toronto Maple Leafs February 11. The Islanders play the league's hottest team in Washington Capitals before they play Buffalo, and Washington has won 2 straight with the conference's second-best goal differential at +28.
