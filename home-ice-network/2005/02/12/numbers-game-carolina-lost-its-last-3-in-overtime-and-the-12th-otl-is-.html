Numbers Game: Carolina lost its last 3 in overtime, and the 12th OTL is only the beginning

The Hurricanes have gone past regulation 23 times this season, 5 more than any other club. Their overtime record is now 11-12.

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkSaturday, 12 February 2005
ShareCopy link

Carolina Hurricanes dropped its 3rd consecutive game in overtime on February 9, a 4-3 loss at Mighty Ducks of Anaheim. The Hurricanes now have 12 overtime losses, the most in the league. They have gone to overtime 23 times this season, 5 more than the next club.

This follows the pattern I tracked three days ago: no team plays past regulation more than Carolina, and the question was whether it helps or hurts. The answer so far is a wash. Their overtime record is 11-12. They earn 11 points from overtime wins and give up only 12 from overtime losses, a net deficit of 1 point across 23 games. The Hurricanes remain 72 points, 3rd in the East.

LA is +2 from overtime. Carolina is -1. The Hurricanes carry 12 OTLs against 11 OTWs, so the extra periods are netting against them.

Kevin Weekes90 has started 46 of 56 games. His 82% share is among the league's highest. The 23 extra periods mean 23 extra chances to lose one shot more than the other side.

Carolina plays Washington Capitals at home on February 14. The season series between them is 2-1-0 in Carolina's favor, 7-7 in goals. Washington is 71 points, 1 behind. If Carolina goes to overtime again, they will have done it in 4 consecutive games. If they win in regulation, the streak ends.
