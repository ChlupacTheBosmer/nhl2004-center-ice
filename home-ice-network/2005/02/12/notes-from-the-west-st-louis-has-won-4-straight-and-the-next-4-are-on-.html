Notes from the West: St. Louis has won 4 straight, and the next 4 are on the road

The Blues sit 3 points from 8th and their heater was built on the road, where the next four games take them

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkSaturday, 12 February 2005
ShareCopy link

St. Louis Blues has won 4 in a row. The last two came on the road: 3-1 at San Jose Sharks on February 8, and 3-2 at Detroit Red Wings on February 10. The Blues sit at 52 points, 10th in the West, 3 behind Columbus Blue Jackets for 8th.

The next four games are all on the road. They open at Columbus tonight, then travel to Chicago on February 14, Dallas on February 16, and Los Angeles on February 17. Columbus and St. Louis have met twice this season, split 1-1-0, with Columbus holding a 9-7 goal advantage.
