Goalie Watch: Buffalo benches Biron after 48 starts and puts Miller in tonight against Montreal

The Sabres sit 8th in the East by 1 point. The goaltender they leaned on for 84% of the season is on the bench

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkSaturday, 19 February 2005
ShareCopy link

Buffalo Sabres has promoted Ryan Miller83 to the No. 1 spot with the game tonight against Montreal Canadiens at HSBC Arena. Martin Biron88, who started 48 of 57 games (84%), is not dressed. Mika Noronen82 was activated as the backup.

The timing could not be worse for a goalie change. Buffalo Sabres sits 8th with 61 points. Atlanta Thrashers is 9th with 60. One point separates them, with 25 games left for Buffalo and 24 for Atlanta. The New York Islanders at 59 points trail by 2, and they have played the same number of games.

Buffalo's last 10 read LLWWLWWWLL. They scored 25 and gave up 24 in that stretch. A +1 differential over 10 games is not what a team holding an 8th seed looks like.

Miller has 12 starts this season, a 21% share. Tonight he starts. He was drafted, he has NHL games under his belt, and his 7-game-ago cup of coffee against the Islanders went as well as anyone could have asked. The opponent is the worst team in the East.

Montreal Canadiens is 15-34-4 at 48 points, last in the conference by 9 points over Florida Panthers. They come into tonight on a 1-game losing streak and 7-23 on the road. Their last two games produced 4 goals for and 6 against. The Montreal Canadiens goaltending shuffle mirrors Buffalo's own: Jose Theodore91 was the starter for 50 of 60, but Eric Fichaud72 has been moved up from G2.

The season series favors Buffalo Sabres. Three games met, Buffalo is 1-0-2 (one regulation win, two overtime losses). The goal differential is 14-12 in Buffalo's favor. The Buffalo Sabres row in the division table reads 4-1-7-3 across 15 games (4 regulation wins, 1 OT win, 7 regulation losses, 3 OT losses), a 9th-place division finish. But the conference standings put them 8th, and that is the only table that counts right now.

Four division games remain on the calendar beyond tonight: Boston Bruins, Ottawa Senators, and one more against Montreal Canadiens. Outside the division, Buffalo Sabres visits Tampa Bay Lightning on Feb 23, Florida Panthers on Feb 24, and Washington Capitals on Feb 26. Three of those four clubs are ahead of Buffalo in the East.

Biron started 48 games and carried this team's playoff hopes for 84% of the season. Miller has 12 starts and gets the node tonight against the worst team in the conference. If Buffalo wins, the 8th seed holds. If they lose and Atlanta Thrashers wins somewhere, the gap is gone.
