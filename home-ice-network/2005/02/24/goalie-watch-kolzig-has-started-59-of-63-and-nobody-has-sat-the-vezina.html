Goalie Watch: Kolzig has started 59 of 63, and nobody has sat the Vezina winner

No goaltender in the league carries a heavier workload. The Capitals' starter faces last-place Montreal tonight.

ShareCopy link

The next closest starter is Dominik Hasek at 90% (55 of 61), and he was scratched tonight in Detroit's game against Los Angeles. After that, three goaltenders sit at 85%: Evgeni Nabokov87 of San Jose Sharks (53 starts), Martin Biron87 of Buffalo Sabres (50), and Roman Turek79 of Calgary Flames (53).
