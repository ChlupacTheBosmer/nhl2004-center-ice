Goalie Watch: Kolzig has started 59 of 63, and nobody has sat the Vezina winner

No goaltender in the league carries a heavier workload. The Capitals' starter faces last-place Montreal tonight.

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkThursday, 24 February 2005
ShareCopy link

Olaf Kolzig89 has started 59 of 63 games. That is 94%, the highest workload share in the league by 4 percentage points over the next-highest. The Washington Capitals Capitals' Vezina Trophy winner from last season takes the ice tonight against the Montreal Canadiens Canadiens.

The next closest starter is Dominik Hasek at 90% (55 of 61), and he was scratched tonight in Detroit's game against Los Angeles. After that, three goaltenders sit at 85%: Evgeni Nabokov83 of San Jose Sharks (53 starts), Martin Biron86 of Buffalo Sabres (50), and Roman Turek79 of Calgary Flames (53).

Washington sits 4th in the East at 77 points, on a two-game losing streak. The Capitals fell 3-2 to Detroit on February 22 and lost their previous game before that. Montreal has 16 wins in 63 games and sits last in the Northeast division at 50 points. Washington has beaten Montreal twice this season, outscoring the Canadiens 6-4 across those meetings.

Three games in six days

Buffalo Sabres visits Washington on February 26. Then the Capitals travel to New Jersey Devils on March 1. If Kolzig starts all three, he reaches 62 of 66.

He has been through 3 injury-designated spells this season. None of them carried a named body part in the league's record. He is not on Washington's injured list heading into tonight.

Montreal's Jose Theodore89 has started 51 of 63 (81%) and is the projected starter. The Canadiens are 8-23 on the road this season.

Among the league's other top starters, Tomas Vokoun91 of Nashville Predators carries 80% (49 of 61), Martin Brodeur95 of New Jersey Devils sits at 82% (49 of 60), and Marty Turco97 of Dallas Stars carries 82% (50 of 61).
