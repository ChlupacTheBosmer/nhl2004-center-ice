Morning Skate Notes: Pittsburgh moves 18 players ahead of Los Angeles

Three in, three out, a Calder winner at centre. The Penguins rebuilt the lineup for tomorrow.

Luc PelletierMontreal correspondent · Home Ice NetworkThursday, 24 February 2005
ShareCopy link

The Pittsburgh Penguins changed 18 of their 20 dressed players ahead of tomorrow's home game against Los Angeles Kings. Three enter the lineup, three sit out, and twelve shifted lines, pairings, or positions.

Martin Straka85 returns after a wrist injury and slots in at fourth-line centre. Brian Holzinger76 enters on the third line. Tomas Surovy74 takes a wing on the bottom unit. Guillaume Lefebvre75, Steve McKenna68, and Mike Eastwood69 are scratched.

Eric Meloche77, last season's Calder Trophy winner, moves from the top line's right wing to centre. Aleksey Morozov79 shifts from left wing to the fourth centre spot. Kelly Buchberger73 drops from the third line to the fourth. Rico Fata73 rises from fourth-line centre to the third wing.

On defence, Michal Rozsival76 switches from left to right on the top pairing, Richard Lintner73 moves from top-pair right to second-pair left, Jamie Pushor67 flips to the left side of the third pairing, and Shane Hnidy66 takes the third-pair right.

The reason: five clubs chasing one playoff spot

Mikael Renberg77 drops from the first line to the second. Shane Willis74 rises from the second to the third. Vitali Yachmenev76 slides from the second wing to the third. Milan Kraft69 loses a spot in the lineup entirely.

Pittsburgh sits 13th in the East at 60 points. One point separates them from 8th-place Buffalo Sabres and 61 points. Five clubs share that 60-point mark: Pittsburgh, New York Islanders, Atlanta, Ottawa, and Philadelphia.

The Penguins have carried 40 injury spells and 510 days lost this season. Straka's wrist had him out; Lefebvre's foot, designated between February 21 and 23, keeps him out until May 15. He was in the lineup before that designation and now carries a second injury on his season.

Pittsburgh beat San Jose 3-2 on February 22, ending a two-game losing stretch. The win brought the Penguins level with the five-club pack chasing Buffalo.

Los Angeles enters with 2 straight wins and 60 points, 10th in the West. Their goaltender Roman Cechmanek91 has started 49 of 61 games (80%). Pittsburgh's Johan Hedberg87 has gone 45 of 61 (74%), and tonight's starter has not yet been named.
