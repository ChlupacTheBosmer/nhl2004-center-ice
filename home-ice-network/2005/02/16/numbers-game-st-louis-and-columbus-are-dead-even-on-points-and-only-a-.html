Numbers Game: St. Louis and Columbus are dead even on points, and only a game separates them

The Blues have won 6 straight, sit at 56 points with 25 to play, and visit the 2nd-place Stars tonight

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkWednesday, 16 February 2005
ShareCopy link

St. Louis Blues and Columbus Blue Jackets sit at 56 points for 8th in the West. Columbus has played 56 games; St. Louis Blues has played 57.

The Blues have won 6 in a row. They scored 3 in regulation at Columbus on February 12 and 3 again at Chicago on February 14. Both road wins, both 3-1. Over the last 10 games the club has outscored opponents 31-22.

Tonight St. Louis Blues visits Dallas Stars, 2nd in the conference at 76 points in 58 games. The Stars have a 22-6 home record. In 2 meetings this season the clubs split 1-1, with Dallas Stars outscoring St. Louis Blues 6-5.

St. Louis Blues carries a 12-16 home record and a 14-15 road mark, and 3 of the 6 consecutive wins came away from the Scottrade Center. They visit Los Angeles Saturday, New York on Monday and Columbus Wednesday. Three of the next four on the road.

Brent Johnson84 has started 45 of 57 games at 79% of the workload. His backup Chris Osgood87 has appeared in 17. The starter's share is the league's 5th-highest among qualifying goaltenders.

At 56 points with 25 games left, the 8th-place line sits at the same number tonight. Columbus has one fewer game played and one more remaining, which means Columbus Blue Jackets needs to stumble for St. Louis Blues to pass on pure points. They play each other February 21 at St. Louis.

The conference standings show Phoenix Coyotes and Los Angeles Kings at 54, one point back of St. Louis Blues and Columbus Blue Jackets.
