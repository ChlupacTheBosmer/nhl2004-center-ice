Roster Move Roundup: 8 of 9 players signed since February 10 are on the injured list

The pattern from last week extends: Lindros joins the list within 4 days of signing, and Morrisonn's elbow keeps him out until late April

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkWednesday, 16 February 2005
ShareCopy link

New York Rangers signed Eric Lindros87 on February 12. Concussion designation hit February 16, return targeted April 13. The 83-overall right wing misses the final 6 weeks of the season.

8 of the 9 players signed in that 3-day window are now on IR. The exception is Kristian Kudroc74, signed by Florida Panthers on February 10. He was removed from the lineup and has not dressed since the transaction.

The previous edition covered Rem Murray73, Mike Ricci79 and Marcel Hossa76, all signed February 9, all hurt before suiting up. The league designated 9 new spells in 7 days across 9 different clubs.

Boston Bruins took the hardest double: Andrew Raycroft81 was pulled from the starting slot after his 5th unexplained absence and replaced by Steve Shields80, while Morrisonn's elbow designation keeps a 73-overall defenseman out for 67 days. The club already sits 4th in the league with 40 injury spells.

Four of the eight designations carry a return date after March 20. The clubs paying these salaries will not see the bodies until the stretch run or later.
