Morning Skate Notes: Tampa Bay puts St. Louis at center, moves 4 defenders

Lecavalier's leg designation arrives on game day, and Tampa Bay's coaching staff reorganizes the entire lineup for tonight's trip to Carolina

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkMonday, 21 February 2005
ShareCopy link

Vincent Lecavalier84 is out until April 1 with a leg injury. The Tampa Bay coaching staff moved Martin St. Louis81 from right wing to center on the top line, shifted Dave Andreychuk73 up from the fourth line to the second center spot, and inserted Dan Boyle75 into the top defensive pairing. The reshuffle hits tonight when Tampa Bay Lightning visits Carolina Hurricanes.

Tampa Bay Lightning now carries 8 players on the injured list, the most in the league by 3. The Predators and Red Wings have 5. Tampa Bay's season injury ledger reads 39 designated spells and 550 days lost, both league highs.

St. Louis slots into the 42C_ role before moving up to H2__ and S1__ during the game. Patrik Svedberg67 takes the 32C_ slot. Ruslan Fedotenko75 slides from the second center to the first. Daniel Tkaczuk65 and Alexander Svitov67 shift one line forward.

On the back end, Boyle joins the first pairing alongside a right-side partner. Jassen Cullimore69 returns to the top unit after the league designated his wrist in a separate spell. Chris O&#x27;Sullivan66 drops to the third pairing only. Cory Sarich74 and Hans Jonsson65 are both repositioned. Pascal Trepanier70 and Jimmie Olvestad63 are healthy scratches.

Between the pipes, the staff added Scott Clemmensen71 as backup in place of John Grahame78.

The rest of the list

Cory Stillman78 (wrist, returns March 5), Francis Bouillon70 (leg, April 25), Bill Lindsay68 (concussion, March 11), Randy Robitaille68 (wrist, February 25), Nolan Pratt67 (head, February 23), and Tim Taylor64 (hip, March 13) round out the injured list alongside Lecavalier.

Tonight is the 5th meeting against Carolina Hurricanes this season; the four-game series is dead even at 15 goals each, with 3 of those games decided in overtime.
