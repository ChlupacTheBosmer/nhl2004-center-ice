Morning Skate Notes: Tampa Bay puts St. Louis at center, moves 4 defenders

Lecavalier's leg designation arrives on game day, and Tampa Bay's coaching staff reorganizes the entire lineup for tonight's trip to Carolina

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkMonday, 21 February 2005
ShareCopy link

Vincent Lecavalier89 is out until April 1 with a leg injury. The Tampa Bay coaching staff moved Martin St. Louis87 from right wing to center on the top line, shifted Dave Andreychuk up from the fourth line to the second center spot, and inserted Dan Boyle81 into the top defensive pairing. The reshuffle hits tonight when Tampa Bay Lightning visits Carolina Hurricanes.

Tampa Bay Lightning now carries 8 players on the injured list, the most in the league by 3. The Predators and Red Wings have 5. Tampa Bay's season injury ledger reads 39 designated spells and 550 days lost, both league highs.

St. Louis slots into the 42C_ role before moving up to H2__ and S1__ during the game. Patrik Svedberg73 takes the 32C_ slot. Ruslan Fedotenko81 slides from the second center to the first. Daniel Tkaczuk71 and Alexander Svitov76 shift one line forward.

On the back end, Boyle joins the first pairing alongside a right-side partner. Jassen Cullimore75 returns to the top unit after the league designated his wrist in a separate spell. Chris O&#x27;Sullivan71 drops to the third pairing only. Cory Sarich68 and Hans Jonsson71 are both repositioned. Pascal Trepanier64 and Jimmie Olvestad69 are healthy scratches.

Between the pipes, the staff added Scott Clemmensen75 as backup in place of John Grahame82.

The rest of the list

Cory Stillman85 (wrist, returns March 5), Francis Bouillon73 (leg, April 25), Bill Lindsay73 (concussion, March 11), Randy Robitaille74 (wrist, February 25), Nolan Pratt71 (head, February 23), and Tim Taylor69 (hip, March 13) round out the injured list alongside Lecavalier.

Tampa Bay Lightning sits 3rd in the East at 78 points through 60 games. Their last 10 games carry 4 regulation wins, 3 overtime losses, and 3 regulation losses. Tonight is the 5th meeting against Carolina Hurricanes this season; the four-game series is dead even at 15 goals each, with 3 of those games decided in overtime.

The Tampa Bay Lightning bench will lean on what it has. The reshuffle puts 33-year-old Andreychuk at the second center alongside players half his age, and the defensive corps now has two men who were healthy scratches as recently as February 17.
