Numbers Game: New Jersey has won 6 straight, hosts Boston tonight, and is 19-9 on the road

The Devils trail Toronto by 13 points for 1st in the East but have the second-best record in the conference, and almost nobody on the beat has said so

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkMonday, 21 February 2005
ShareCopy link

New Jersey Devils has won 6 in a row. The Devils sit 2nd in the Eastern Conference at 83 points through 59 games, trailing only Toronto Maple Leafs (96). Their 6-game streak is the longest in the league right now, and no other desk has named them.

Tonight Boston Bruins visits the Continental Airlines Arena. Boston is 24-23-5 for 67 points and 7th in the East. The two met once before this season: New Jersey Devils won 4-2.

The Devils have lost 115 days to injuries this season, 4th fewest in the league behind Dallas (51), Colorado (81), and Minnesota (114). Their only current injury is Turner Stevenson72, out with a knee until February 22.

Martin Brodeur91 has started 48 of 59 games, an 81% share. His club is 28-15-5 with 172 goals allowed. They give up fewer than their opponents by 24.

The road splits

New Jersey Devils is 19-9 on the road this season. That beats their home record (20-11) by winning percentage. A club that wins more easily away than at home tends to be built around speed and transition, not a system that demands defensive structure in a tight rink. Their 35 goals-for and 26 goals-against in the Atlantic Division series tell you they are getting to the net first.

The Devils entered the season off a Presidents' Trophy race run by Dallas in the West. In the East, Toronto owns the top spot with 42 regulation wins, 14 more than New Jersey. But the Devils have 11 overtime wins (the OTW column) compared to Toronto's 4. They drag you deep and then beat you there.

Tonight is the 2nd of 11 scheduled meetings between these clubs this season. The series is 1-0 New Jersey Devils. Boston's last 10 reads: W, L, W, W, OTL, W, W, L, L, W. They won 4-3 at Carolina on February 19 and carry a 1-game win streak into tonight.

New Jersey Devils closes February with games at Pittsburgh (Feb 23), at New York Rangers (Feb 25), and against Boston (Feb 23 and 28). Two more points on those four nights puts them at 85 and within reach of a bye if Toronto stumbles.
