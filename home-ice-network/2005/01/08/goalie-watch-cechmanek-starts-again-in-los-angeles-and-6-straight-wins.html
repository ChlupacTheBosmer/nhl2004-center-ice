Goalie Watch: Cechmanek starts again in Los Angeles, and 6 straight wins follow

The Kings reversed their crease decision within the same week they promoted Huet, and 40 goals in 10 games have followed.

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkSaturday, 8 January 2005
ShareCopy link

Los Angeles Kings has won 6 straight, the longest active win streak in the league. The Kings beat Minnesota Wild 6-4 on January 6 and edged Dallas Stars 4-3 in overtime on January 4.

The run overlaps a crease decision that got reversed. On January 4 the desk reported Los Angeles Kings promoting Cristobal Huet80 to the starting role and adding Tim Thomas77 to the roster. The lineup changes filed since January 5 show Roman Cechmanek91 back as G1, Huet shifted to backup, and Thomas removed from the dressed lineup. Cechmanek has started 33 of the Kings' 40 games, 82 percent of the workload.

The streak began before the goalie reversal. Los Angeles Kings has scored 40 goals and allowed 30 in its last 10 games. The Kings sit at 13-16-5 with 43 points, third in the Pacific Division behind Dallas Stars and Mighty Ducks of Anaheim.

Still missing bodies

Adam Deadmarsh87 carries a groin designation with a January 21 return. Alexander Frolov78 has a neck and is out until February 1. Los Angeles Kings has logged 20 injury spells and 167 days lost this season, among the heavier totals in the Western Conference.

The next 3 games are at home: Edmonton Oilers on January 9, San Jose Sharks on January 11, and St. Louis Blues on January 13. The Kings are 10-8 at home and 9-13 on the road, so the schedule tilts their way. The Oilers enter that first game on a 3-game win streak at 16-14-2 and 52 points in the Northwest.

Cechmanek's 33 starts and Huet's 11 tell you the coaching staff leaned on the veteran before the brief Huet experiment. With two top-nine forwards out and a road record that drags, Los Angeles Kings needs the crease to hold while the rest of the group stays patchwork.
