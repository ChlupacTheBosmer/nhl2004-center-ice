The gap widens: Buffalo is 3 losses in a row and further from 8th than it was

The Sabres sit 9th with 51 points while New York holds 8th with 55, and tonight's trip to San Jose is the latest chance to close it

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkSunday, 30 January 2005
ShareCopy link

The piece

Five days ago, Buffalo and 8th place were separated by a tiebreaker. Buffalo Sabres is now 9th in the Eastern Conference with 51 points. New York Islanders holds 8th with 55. The gap opened during a 3-game losing streak that includes a 2-1 loss to Ottawa Senators on January 25 and a 4-3 loss to Nashville Predators on January 27.

The Sabres have allowed 31 goals in their last 10 games against 22 for.

Buffalo has played 49 games. New York has played 50. One of those games is tonight's contest between these two clubs, a matchup where neither has a divisional claim on the other. Buffalo has beaten the Islanders twice this season; New York has beaten Buffalo once. The season series sits at 2-1, and the winner tonight extends that lead.

The road gets harder before it gets easier

Buffalo plays San Jose Sharks tonight, its first meeting of the season. San Jose is 17-24-7 with 49 points, 10th in the Western Conference, one position below Buffalo's standing. The Sharks have gone to overtime 7 times, more than any club in the Pacific. They are on a 1-game win streak after beating Washington Capitals 2-1 on January 28.

After tonight, Buffalo's schedule reads New Jersey Devils on February 4, then Boston Bruins and Chicago Blackhawks in a short window through February 6. Three of those four opponents are above .500. The Sabres do not face a team with a losing record again until mid-February.

Taylor Pyatt78 (foot, return February 23) is the one man on Buffalo's shelf. The 18 wins on the season sit 4 games behind what the Sabres need to clear New York in the wins column, and the 5 remaining meetings against divisional opponents include 2 against Ottawa Senators, a club on a 2-game heater that just beat Buffalo.

Buffalo needs roughly 8 more wins to reach where the Islanders are now, and the Islanders still have 32 games to play. The Sabres have 33.
