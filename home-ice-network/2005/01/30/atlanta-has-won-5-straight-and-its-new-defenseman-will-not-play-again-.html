Atlanta has won 5 straight, and its new defenseman will not play again this season

Andy Sutton signed January 26 for $85,520 and landed on the shelf with a leg designation that runs past the last game

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkSunday, 30 January 2005
ShareCopy link

The piece

Atlanta Thrashers has won 5 straight, the longest active streak in the Eastern Conference. The Thrashers signed a defenseman on January 26 to help them climb out of 13th place. That defenseman will not play another game this season.

Andy Sutton76 signed for $85,520. His designation is a leg, onset between January 26 and January 30, return targeted April 12. The season's last game is April 6.

Atlanta is 17-25-2 with 48 points, 13th in the East, 7 points behind 8th-place New York Islanders. The heater came during a stretch where the offense matched its own defense: 29 goals for and 29 against across the last 10. In the 2 games visible since January 25, the Thrashers beat the Rangers twice by a combined score of 6-4.

The Thrashers also moved Byron Dafoe83 back into the starting crease this week. Dafoe carries 45 appearances and a 90% share of Atlanta's games. Pasi Nurminen80 dropped from the active roster entirely.

Atlanta hosts Toronto Maple Leafs tonight, the league's top club at 36-9-3 with 81 points. The season series between these two clubs is 3 games deep: Toronto leads 2-1, but the goal totals are dead even at 14-14.

Atlanta's 14-12 home record is better than its 9-15 road mark, and the heater has been built at home. Sutton's return date makes him a problem for next October.
