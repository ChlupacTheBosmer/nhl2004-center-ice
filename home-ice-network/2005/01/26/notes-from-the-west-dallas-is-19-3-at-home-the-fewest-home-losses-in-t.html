Notes from the West: Dallas is 19-3 at home, the fewest home losses in the league

The Stars have lost 3 games in front of their own crowd all season. The next closest club has 4.

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkWednesday, 26 January 2005
ShareCopy link

Dallas beat Tampa Bay 2-1 on Sunday without Marty Turco94 in the lineup. Ron Tugnutt84 started, Jason Bacashihua83 dressed for the first time, and the home record moved to 19-3. That's the best home mark in the league by fewest defeats.

The road numbers tell a different story. Dallas is 13-15 away from American Airlines Center, 33 points behind its home pace. The gap between 19-3 and 13-15 is the widest split in the league, and it puts the Stars 2nd in the West at 66 points, 1 behind Edmonton Oilers. Their home and road records look like two different teams.

Marty Turco has carried 41 of 50 games, 82% of the workload. Tugnutt has 12. Bacashihua's dress on Sunday gave the club a third body for the first time this season, which could matter more over the next month than at the trade deadline.

The health story helps. Dallas has lost 49 days to injury this season across 26 designated spells. That's the lowest total in the league, ahead of Ottawa Senators at 27 days from 18 spells. Mike Modano88 has been listed 5 times but is not currently on the shelf.

Ottawa comes calling Tuesday

Ottawa Senators visits on January 27, a first meeting. Ottawa is 16-24-5, on a 1-game losing streak, 12 points back of Boston for the 8th seed. The Stars haven't dropped more than 1 game in a row at home since November.
