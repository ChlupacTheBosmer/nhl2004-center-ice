Notes from the Northeast: Ottawa ends an 8-game skid, and Washington visits tonight

The Senators needed 65 minutes to stop the slide and now stare at a 7-point gap to the playoff line

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkTuesday, 18 January 2005
ShareCopy link

The Ottawa Senators beat the Mighty Ducks of Anaheim 3-2 in overtime on Tuesday night, ending an eight-game losing streak that had buried the Senators at 15-22-4. Ottawa sits in 11th place in the Eastern Conference with 42 points. Buffalo Sabres holds the eighth and final playoff spot with 49 points from 44 games.

Patrick Lalime88 has started 37 of Ottawa's 45 games. Martin Prusek79 has 10 starts and Ray Emery78 has 2. The workload never wavered through the streak.

"It's been heavy. You look at the standings every morning, same thing, another loss, and you think, all right, here we go again," general manager Michael Hui told The Tunnel's Marcus Bell this week. "It doesn't hit different on the seventh night than the first. It just gets heavier."

The Senators' home record sits at 12-10, including the overtime win over Anaheim. On the road they are 7-16. Tonight, Washington Capitals arrives in Ottawa. The Capitals are 24-15-5, 57 points, 3rd in the East, and 15-9 on the road. In the one meeting this season, Washington won 4-2.

The overtime win was Ottawa's fourth this season in eight extra-time games. The Senators are 4-4 in those matchups, a coin-flip record that has kept them hovering just outside the line without sinking further. Olaf Kolzig93 has started 43 of Washington's 46 games, a 93 percent share that leads the league.

What the path looks like

Ottawa has 37 games remaining. To crack eighth, the Senators must outperform Buffalo by the gap that currently sits between 42 and 49 points. Of Ottawa's remaining schedule, 19 come at home.

Washington has played through 189 lost days to injury across 27 spells, yet Kolzig has missed only 3 games all year. The Capitals' 15-9 road record is tied for the league lead.

If Ottawa wins tonight, the win total moves to 16 and the point total to 44. If they lose in regulation, the gap to eighth widens further and the losing streak restarts at one. The Senators have not won consecutive games in their last 10.
