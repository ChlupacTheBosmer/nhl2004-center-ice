The Infirmary: 6 Islanders are on the shelf, and 17 men changed lines Tuesday night

New York has the league's most players simultaneously out, the most lineup changes in a single day, and somehow sits at 17-17-2

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkWednesday, 5 January 2005
ShareCopy link

The piece

New York Islanders sent 17 different players through different lineup slots between Monday's morning skate and Tuesday's 5-3 win over Boston Bruins. No other club made more than 5 changes in the same window. The Islanders did it while carrying 6 players on the injured list, the most of any club in the league.

Mike Peca86 is out with a wrist, targeted for January 13. Roman Hamrlik82 has a neck injury that keeps him out until February 6. Jason Blake81 (wrist) is out until February 12. Mariusz Czerkawski76 (neck) returns January 16. Two more names sit on the shelf without a designated body part on the record.

The 27 injury spells and 223 days lost put New York second in the league's injury ledger, behind only Pittsburgh's 305. Yet the Islanders are 17-17-2 with 40 points, one point under .500 and four points out of the fourth seed in the Atlantic.

Adrian Aucoin80 moved from left shot on the top pair to right shot on the second pair. Janne Niinimaa82 flipped from left to right on the third pair. Kenny Jonsson80 slid from the left side of the third pair to the right side of the top pair. Radek Martinek72 moved from right shot on the top pair to left shot on the second.

On the forward side, Chris Simon73 jumped from the fourth line left wing to the second. Trent Hunter66 was recalled from the press box and slotted between centres. Pierre Dagenais69 and Justin Papineau72 were scratched entirely.

Alexei Yashin84 dropped from the top centre position to the second line, where he scored on a backhand from the half-wall in the second period.

Steve Stirling had no quote after the game. The line score will have to do: 5 goals, 20 shots on a Andrew Raycroft83 who has faced 44 in his last ten, and a second straight win. New York hosts Pittsburgh Penguins Thursday, the first of three meetings in five nights.
