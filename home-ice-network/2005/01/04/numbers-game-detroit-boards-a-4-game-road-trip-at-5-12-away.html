Numbers Game: Detroit boards a 4-game road trip at 5-12 away

The Red Wings have won 16 at home and 5 on the road, and the road schedule starts Tuesday in Chicago

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkTuesday, 4 January 2005
ShareCopy link

Detroit Red Wings boards a plane for Chicago Blackhawks on Jan 5 with a road record that reads 5-12 and a home record that reads 16-5. Four consecutive road games follow: Chicago on Tuesday, Tampa Bay Lightning on Thursday, Florida Panthers on Friday, Philadelphia Flyers on Monday Jan 11. The Red Wings sit 46 points, 4 behind first-place Nashville Predators in the Central Division.

The Red Wings have scored 145 goals and allowed 144 across 38 games, essentially even. But the wins have come almost entirely in one building. Joe Louis Arena is 16-5. Every other arena in the league is 5-12.

Dominik Hasek91 has started 33 of 38 games, 87 percent of the workload. Steve Yzerman85 (shoulder) is out until Jan 25. Detroit has lost 134 days to injury this season across 21 spells, a middling figure in a league where Pittsburgh carries 305.

The Red Wings' only game since the previous edition was a 4-3 overtime win over San Jose Sharks on Dec 31. In their last 10, Detroit has gone 7-3 with 39 goals for and 30 against. The Dec 31 win extended a 1-game streak that follows a stretch where 4 of 5 games were victories.

The next 4

Chicago Blackhawks sits at 28 points, 19 behind Detroit, on a 2-game losing streak. Tampa Bay Lightning leads the Southeast at 53 points and is riding a 5-game win streak. Florida Panthers has won 3 in a row, including a 5-2 road win over New Jersey Devils on Jan 1 and a 5-2 win over Colorado Avalanche on Jan 2. Philadelphia Flyers is 10-8 at home.

Detroit is 5-12 on the road without its top center and with a 40-year-old goaltender in net 87 percent of the time. At Joe Louis, they look like a division winner. Four games to sort out which one they are.
