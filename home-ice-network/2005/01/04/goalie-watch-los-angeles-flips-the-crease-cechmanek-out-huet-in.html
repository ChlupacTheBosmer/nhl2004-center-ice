Goalie Watch: Los Angeles flips the crease, Cechmanek out, Huet in

After 31 starts from Cechmanek, the Kings promote Huet and add Tim Thomas to the roster

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkTuesday, 4 January 2005
ShareCopy link

Los Angeles Kings moved Cristobal Huet76 from backup to starter and removed Roman Cechmanek86 from the active roster between editions. Tim Thomas73 joined as the new backup, dressed for the first time this season. The Kings beat Philadelphia Flyers 4-3 on Jan 2, the first game under the new setup.

Cechmanek had started 31 of 38 games, 82 percent of the workload. The Kings entered the season 12-16-5 with him as the clear workhorse. Huet carried 11 starts before the promotion, 29 percent. Thomas appeared in 1 game.

The timing follows a 4-game win streak. Los Angeles Kings is 38 goals for, 29 against across its last 10. The Kings sit 39 points, against 42 for Mighty Ducks of Anaheim, 2nd in the Pacific Division, and face Dallas Stars on Jan 4 at home before a road stretch.

The numbers behind the switch

The Kings allowed 142 goals in 38 games. Their penalty kill has held. Even-strength goaltending was the issue with Cechmanek, who entered the switch on a 2-game losing streak.

Huet's 11 starts came in relief and spot duty through December. Thomas, the new backup, has 1 appearance on the season. The Kings' remaining schedule through Jan 11 has 5 games: 3 at home against Dallas Stars, Edmonton Oilers, and San Jose Sharks, and 2 on the road at Minnesota Wild and Dallas Stars. Whether Huet carries the full load or splits with Thomas is the next question.
