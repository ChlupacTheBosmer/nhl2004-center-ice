Notes from the East: Pittsburgh has won 8 of 10 and sits 4 points from the playoff line

The club that has lost 425 days to injuries this season is the one making the 8th-spot race uncomfortable

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkMonday, 31 January 2005
ShareCopy link

Pittsburgh Penguins has won 8 of its last 10 and sits 10th in the East with 51 points. The 8th spot belongs to the Islanders at 55. Four points, 31 games. Pittsburgh is not out of anything.

The heater is built on a defensive turnaround. Pittsburgh has allowed 2.10 goals per game over its last 10 after giving up 3.90 for the season. The goal differential flips the same way: minus-20 across 51 games, plus-7 in the last 10.

Notable in any January. The injuries make it remarkable. Pittsburgh has logged 34 injury spells this season, second only to Nashville's 35 in the league, and 425 days lost, second only to Tampa Bay's 497. Four men are on the shelf right now: Tomas Surovy72 carries a back designation with a February 9 return, Kris Beech70 has a groin with a March 3 return, Pasi Salminen71 a hip with a March 7 return.

Mario Lemieux88 has been through 5 spells this season, the most recent a rib designation that closed on January 23. He is back in the lineup and the wins followed.

The schedule gives them 2 home dates in the next 6 days. Vancouver visits on February 4, Florida on February 6. Vancouver is 27-15-5, on a 3-game win streak, carrying 5 men on the shelf including Ed Jovanovski81 (knee, out until February 19). Florida is 17-26-6, on a 4-game losing streak, minus-3 on the season with 158 goals for in 51 games. The Florida game is the one to take.

Pittsburgh's home record this season is 11-13, 12-15 on the road. Neither number suggests a club capable of winning 8 of 10. The defensive structure has been there long enough now for the wins to pile up, and the 8th spot is close enough that a February run changes the table.

The Islanders have 32 games left and a 4-point lead. The Penguins have 31. If Pittsburgh takes the Florida game and splits with Vancouver, the gap is 2 points with 29 games still to go for each club. That is a race.
