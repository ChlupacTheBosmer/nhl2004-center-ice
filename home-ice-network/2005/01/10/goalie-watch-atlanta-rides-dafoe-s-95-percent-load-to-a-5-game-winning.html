Goalie Watch: Atlanta rides Dafoe's 95 percent load to a 5-game winning streak

The Thrashers' goaltender has started 38 of 40, topping Kolzig by 2 points, and the streak has moved them to within 4 points of last place in the division

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkMonday, 10 January 2005
ShareCopy link

Byron Dafoe77 has started 38 of Atlanta Thrashers's 40 games. That is 95 percent, the highest share in the league. Olaf Kolzig92 in Washington Capitals has started 38 of 41, a 93 percent share. Dafoe now carries a heavier load than the reigning Vezina winner, and Atlanta has 36 points where Washington has 51.

Atlanta sits at 13-21-2, 9-11 at home, 8-12 on the road, 4 points ahead of Montreal Canadiens in the Southeast. Five straight wins, including a 5-2 road victory at Carolina Hurricanes on January 7, moved the record from 8-21-2 to where it stands now. The streak has not lifted the point total out of the bottom third.

44 goals in the last 10 games against 36 allowed. That is the 3rd-highest goal total in the league over that stretch, behind Toronto Maple Leafs's 49 and Washington Capitals's 45. The 5-2 win at Carolina produced 30 shots on goal to 23.

Milan Hnilicka74 has 10 games played in 40 team-games, a 25 percent share. The next test comes at New York Islanders on January 11. The Islanders have 7 players on the injured list, the most in the league, and sit at 17-19-2 after 40 games. Atlanta will lean on its goaltender again.
