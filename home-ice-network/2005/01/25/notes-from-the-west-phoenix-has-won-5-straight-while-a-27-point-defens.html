Notes from the West: Phoenix has won 5 straight while a 27-point defenseman sits out

The Coyotes' heater arrived after 2 key men went down in the same window, and the structure has held

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkTuesday, 25 January 2005
ShareCopy link

Phoenix Coyotes has won 5 games in a row while 2 players who anchor its scoring depth are on the shelf. David Tanabe76 (a concussion, out through 2005-02-12) and center Daymond Langkow74 (a neck, out through 2005-02-24) went down in the same window, between 2005-01-01 and 2005-01-04. The heater opened 7 days later.

Phoenix sits at 46 points, 4 behind Columbus Blue Jackets for 8th in the Western Conference, and has 34 games left to close the gap.

The streak, game by game

The 5-game run reads: 4-1 at Nashville Predators, 3-2 over San Jose Sharks, 3-1 over Buffalo Sabres, 3-0 over St. Louis Blues, 3-2 at Calgary Flames. Four wins by 2 goals or fewer, one by 3. Goaltending carried most of those four tight ones. Sean Burke82 has 38 games this season at a 79% share, carrying a 17-21-0 record with 1 shutout. He has a losing season between the pipes while his team wins five straight.

Tanabe has 27 points this season, 4 goals and 23 assists, in 39 games. He is the team's leading scorer among defensemen by a wide margin, and without him the blue line has little secondary offense. Brian Boucher75 and Zac Bierk have combined for just 4 wins in 15 starts off the bench, so there is no reliable relief behind Burke.

What comes next

Phoenix Coyotes visits Vancouver Canucks on 2005-01-26 and hosts Calgary Flames on 2005-01-28. The single meeting between Phoenix and Vancouver this season ended 6-3 in the Canucks' favor. Phoenix scored 3 per game over the streak and allowed barely more than 1, so the head-to-head loss is not the whole story.

Langkow's neck keeps him out through 2005-02-24. Tanabe returns 2005-02-12. Until then, the Coyotes are finding a way to win on their top 3 forwards and a goaltender with a losing record.
