The Infirmary: Vancouver carries 4 out to a date with the league's hottest team

The Canucks are on a 2-game slide, have lost 214 days to injury this season, and host Phoenix tomorrow on a 5-game run

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkTuesday, 25 January 2005
ShareCopy link

Vancouver Canucks hosts Phoenix Coyotes at Rogers Arena on 2005-01-26 with 4 men on the injured list, a 2-game losing streak, and a visitor carrying the longest active run in the league.

Ed Jovanovski81 [456] (a knee, out through 2005-02-19) is the biggest absence. Matt Cooke70 [174] (a rib, out through 2005-02-25) and Artem Chubarov69 [156] (a leg, out through 2005-02-03) remove two bodies from the center depth. Brandon Reid71 [1211] (an abdomen, out through 2005-02-07) is the fourth. Vancouver has had 28 designated injury spells this season, costing 214 days.

Standing their ground

The Canucks hold 61 points and sit 4th in the Western Conference, tied on points with Colorado Avalanche who have played 1 game fewer. They are 11 points ahead of 8th-place Columbus Blue Jackets at 50. At 48 games played, they have 34 left.

Dan Cloutier82 [166] has carried 77% of the workload this season across 37 starts. Alexander Auld75 [1175] has 11 games, Tyler Moss70 [686] has 5.

The visitor

Phoenix Coyotes enters on 5 straight wins, allowing 2, 0, 1, 2, and 1 goal across that stretch. Phoenix is 46 points, 4 back of 8th, and playing its best hockey of the season without a 27-point defenseman and a top-nine center. The one head-to-head meeting between these two this season ended 6-3 in Vancouver's favor.

The Canucks' next 2 games after Phoenix: Minnesota Wild visits 2005-01-28. Minnesota is 45 points, 12th in the West, and on a 1-game win. A chance to stop the slide before February and the 2005-01-30 slate, when Edmonton Oilers arrives.

The 4 men out are all projected back before the league's what-the-calendar-has-as-the-trade deadline on 2005-03-19.
