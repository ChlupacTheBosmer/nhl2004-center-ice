Numbers Game: Anaheim has gone to overtime 14 times, the most of any club that cannot win them

The Ducks are 5-9 in the extra period, the league's worst record among teams that keep ending up there

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkTuesday, 25 January 2005
ShareCopy link

Jean-Sebastien Giguere97 has started 43 of 48 games for Mighty Ducks of Anaheim, and nine of those starts have ended with the Ducks on the losing side of an extra period. Anaheim is 5-9 in overtime this season, the worst record in extras among any club that has played more than 10 of them.

No team reaches overtime more often than Carolina (18 games) or Edmonton (14). But the Hurricanes are 9-9 in extras and the Oilers are 9-5. The Ducks are 5-9. Anaheim sits 7th in the Western Conference at 57 points, four behind Detroit for 6th and eight behind Nashville for the division lead.

The workload

Giguere's 43 starts carry the league's highest share for a goaltender on a team that keeps going to overtime. Martin Gerber79 has 5 starts and Ilja Bryzgalov81 3. The Ducks are 19-15-9 with 151 goals for and 149 against. A two-goal margin over 43 games means they have been in tight ones all year and lost the late ones.

The 9 overtime losses are tied with Carolina for the league lead. Anaheim gets to the extra period more than anyone. It just keeps losing when it gets there.

What it costs

Nine losses in overtime are nine games where the Ducks collected 1 point instead of 2. In a division where 6th and 7th are separated by 1 point (Detroit at 58, Anaheim at 57), those margins stack up fast. A 9-5 overtime record instead of 5-9 would put Anaheim at 61, past Detroit and into a first-round bye rather than a first-round date.

The Ducks' last 10 games read WWLOTLWWWWWL, five regulation wins in the stretch and one overtime loss to Colorado Avalanche on January 9. They beat Los Angeles Kings 3-2 on January 22 in regulation.

Mighty Ducks of Anaheim visits Ottawa Senators on January 29 and hosts St. Louis Blues on January 30, two games where the Ducks will look for regulation answers. They have not played Detroit since October.
