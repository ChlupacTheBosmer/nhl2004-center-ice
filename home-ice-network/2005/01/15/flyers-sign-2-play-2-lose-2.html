Flyers sign 2, play 2, lose 2

Keith Primeau and Justin Williams suited up for a 5-2 win over Atlanta on Tuesday night. Both are on the injured list by Thursday morning.

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkSaturday, 15 January 2005
ShareCopy link

The Philadelphia Flyers Flyers signed Keith Primeau82 for $147,170 on Sunday and Justin Williams77 for $205,680 the same day. Both were in the lineup Tuesday night against Atlanta Thrashers in a 5-2 win. Both are now on the injured list.

Primeau carries an arm injury, onset somewhere between Jan 14 and Jan 15, return targeted for Jan 21. Williams has a hip, same onset window, return set for Jan 20. The Flyers paid $147,170 and $205,680 respectively and got exactly one game from each.

Neither man is new to the shelf this season. Primeau missed time earlier with a rib injury. Williams had a head spell. Both are on their second trip to the injured list in under six weeks.

Head coach Ken Hitchcock slotted Primeau as his third-line center with penalty-killing duties (L3C_, K2C_), and put Williams on the second line right wing alongside a power-play unit. The lineup changes show Williams filling L2RW and P2RW after being undressed the previous game. Primeau came in cold after being undressed before the signing.

Philadelphia Flyers sits at 15-20-4, 42 points, fifth in the Atlantic division. The Flyers have won 2 straight and are 13-8 at home but 6-16 on the road. They have lost 171 days to 25 injury spells this season, already among the league's worst.

The schedule does not wait. Philadelphia hosts Montreal Canadiens on Jan 16, then Tampa Bay Lightning Jan 18, New York Rangers Jan 19 and Toronto Maple Leafs Jan 21. Neither Primeau nor Williams will be available for any of those four games. Primeau's earliest possible return lands on the Toronto night. Williams would be a day late.

The Flyers will lean on Primeau's replacement on the third line for the next week. Michal Handzus81 moved down from second-line center in the same lineup shuffle that brought Primeau in, dropping to L2C_ and K2C_. The hope in Philadelphia is that the 5-2 showing against Atlanta was a sign of health in the lineup rather than a one-game spike before the next wave.
