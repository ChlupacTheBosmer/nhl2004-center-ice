RECAP: Blue Jackets 7, Sharks 2 -- 12 goals in two nights against San Jose

Columbus scored 5 at home Tuesday and 7 on the road Wednesday, and the road record now reads 10-8

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkSaturday, 1 January 2005
ShareCopy link

Columbus Blue Jackets went to San Jose on Wednesday night and scored 7 goals in a 7-2 win, one night after putting 5 past the same opponent at home. Twelve goals, four against, four points banked.

The Blue Jackets entered Wednesday on a 2-game win streak. They leave on a 2-game win streak with the goals-against differential in their favor. Their last 10 reads 44 goals for, 33 against, which is 4.40 per game scored against 3.30 allowed.

Columbus is 10-8 on the road and 10-8 at home. Same either way.

San Jose Sharks is now on a 2-game losing streak and sits at 14-17-3, 37 points, fourth in the Pacific. The Sharks allowed 12 goals in two games to a Columbus club scoring 3.47 per game on the season. San Jose's goaltending has been shuffled this week: the lineup changes show Evgeni Nabokov82 out, Miikka Kiprusoff72 in as G1, and Mathieu Garon73 dressed as backup.

The goaltending change on Columbus' side was even more drastic. Fred Brathwaite81 was removed from the roster, Marc Denis86 moved to G2, and Pascal Leclaire86 promoted to starter. Denis and Leclaire have split 34 starts almost exactly: 17 each. The decision to hand Leclaire the crease came in the 48 hours before this two-game set.

Columbus travels to Washington on Jan 3, then hosts Nashville on Jan 6. Those two games will test whether the 7-goal outburst was a hot night or a pattern. The season average is 3.47 goals per game. Wednesday night's 7 is not a sustainable pace, but 4.40 over 10 is closer to what this group has been lately.
