Goalie Watch: Kolzig has started 36 of 38, the highest share in the league

No other goaltender is above 89 percent. Washington leans on its Vezina winner every night and he keeps delivering

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkSaturday, 1 January 2005
ShareCopy link

Olaf Kolzig92 has started 36 of Washington Capitals's 38 games, 95 percent of the workload and the highest share of any goaltender in the league. The next closest is a cluster at 89 percent: Nikolai Khabibulin86 in Tampa, Manny Fernandez81 in Minnesota, Martin Brodeur92 in New Jersey, and Jose Theodore83 in Montreal.

Kolzig won last year's Vezina Trophy on the same pattern. Washington did not lean on him then at 95 percent, but the usage is close enough that the coaching staff runs on the assumption that Kolzig plays unless he cannot.

Washington sits 3rd in the Southeast with 45 points, 2 back of New Jersey Devils for the final playoff spot in the East. They are 13-9 on the road, better than their 7-9 home mark, and that road strength has kept them afloat through a 24-injury spell that has cost the club 182 days across the season.

Maxime Ouellet81 has 7 starts, an 18 percent share. Josh Harding79 has 2.

The risk in this pattern is February. Goaltenders who carry 90-plus percent workload through 40 games tend to fade as the schedule tightens. Washington is already showing signs: they are 7-9 at home and 13-9 on the road, and their last 10 has them outscoring opponents 43-36.

Kolzig told The Tunnel's Jan Kowalczyk this week about the grind: "You prep the same, you trust the work. I don't change my routine because I miss a day." Except he has not missed a day, or rather, he has missed two in 38. The routine is the only thing that matters when you carry 95 percent of the load.
