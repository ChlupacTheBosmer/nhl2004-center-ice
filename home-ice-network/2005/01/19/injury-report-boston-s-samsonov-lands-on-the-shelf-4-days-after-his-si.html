Injury Report: Boston's Samsonov lands on the shelf 4 days after his signing; the 4th spell of his season

BOS paid $468,080 for the winger on January 15; a groin keeps him out through January 30

Luc PelletierMontreal correspondent · Home Ice NetworkWednesday, 19 January 2005
ShareCopy link

The Boston Bruins signed Sergei Samsonov81 for $468,080 on January 15. Four days later, the league placed him on the injured list with a groin, onset between January 18 and January 19, return targeted for January 30.

This is Samsonov's 4th injury spell this season. The league's repeat-case log carries him on the shelf twice in December and once in early January before the groin. His overall grade is 81 and he skates left wing. The signing was Boston's only transaction this week.

Boston sits 18-19-4, 48 points, 9th in the Eastern conference, 1 point behind Buffalo Sabres for the 8th and final playoff position. The Bruins are on a 2-game winning streak, the latest a 3-1 win over Atlanta Thrashers on January 17. They are 8-14 at home, tied with Florida Panthers for the worst home mark among Eastern clubs. On the road they are 14-9. Samsonov joins a Boston forward group that has 163 goals in 45 games.

A club that keeps getting nicked

With Samsonov out, Boston carries just 1 player on the shelf, tied with Buffalo Sabres and New Jersey Devils for the fewest among Eastern clubs. The Bruins have generated 30 injury spells this season, 4th-most in the league. Those spells have cost only 110 lost days. Short absences still leave a hole on the wing for the stretch run.

Head coach Mike Sullivan told The Tunnel's Jan Kowalczyk this month about the crease change from Steve Shields76 to Andrew Raycroft77: "Andy's earned the look. He's been in here every day, sharp, square, and sometimes the crease needs a fresh set of eyes." Raycroft has started 36 of 45 games, carrying 80 percent of the workload. Shields sits at 13 starts.

Boston Bruins hosts Washington Capitals on January 20, visits Pittsburgh Penguins on January 23, and hosts Philadelphia Flyers on January 25.
