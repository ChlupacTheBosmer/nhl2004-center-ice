Injury Report: Vancouver carries 5 unavailable men to Detroit, which is without Yzerman

The Canucks have shifted 15 lineup slots in 48 hours and still sit tied for 2nd in the West at 61 points

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkWednesday, 19 January 2005
ShareCopy link

The Vancouver Canucks lineup shifted in 15 slots between Sunday and Tuesday's morning skate, and tonight the Canucks arrive at Detroit Red Wings with 5 men on the injured list.

Ed Jovanovski85 (knee, return February 19), Matt Cooke74 (rib, February 25), Artem Chubarov73 (leg, February 3), Henrik Sedin72 (shoulder, January 22), and Bryan Allen75 (foot, January 23) are all unavailable. Vancouver has logged 26 injury spells and 198 lost days this season.

The morning-skate log shows Todd Bertuzzi93 moved to the left wing on the second line. Jason King71 slid up from a wing role to center the fourth line, and Trevor Linden83 added a center assignment. Ken Belanger66 and Scott Ferguson67 joined the active roster. Brent Sopel79 and Zenith Komarniski68 swapped defensive pairings, and Marek Malik78 moved up to the top unit. Marc-Andre Bergeron73 and Darren Langdon66 both shifted lines.

Vancouver sits 25-13-5, 61 points, tied with Nashville Predators for 2nd in the Western conference. Their 13-9 road record is 3rd in the conference. They have put up 186 goals, 2nd in the West behind Colorado Avalanche at 193. Dan Cloutier86 has started 35 of 46 games, carrying 76 percent of the workload.

Detroit holds the home ice and the head-to-head

Detroit Red Wings is 20-17-4, 54 points, 6th in the conference, riding a 2-game winning streak. Detroit is 17-6 at home, 2nd in the conference behind Dallas Stars at 17-3. The Red Wings are without Steve Yzerman86 (shoulder, return January 25) and have logged 26 injury spells and 138 lost days. Dominik Hasek92 has started 41 of 46 games, holding 89 percent of the crease.

The only meeting between these two clubs this season ended 4-1 for Detroit. Tonight is the second.
