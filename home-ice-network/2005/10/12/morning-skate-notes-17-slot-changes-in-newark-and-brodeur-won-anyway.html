Morning Skate Notes: 17 slot changes in Newark, and Brodeur won anyway

No club reshuffled more than New Jersey in Week 1; the Devils beat Ottawa 3-1 on the opening weekend

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkWednesday, 12 October 2005
ShareCopy link

Seventeen of New Jersey Devils's players carry different lineup assignments than the depth chart the club filed before the season. That is the most of any club in the 30-team league. Montreal Canadiens is second at 11, Carolina Hurricanes third at 16.

Martin Brodeur95 Martin Brodeur faced 18 shots and gave up 1 in a 3-1 win at Ottawa Senators on October 10. The Devils sit at 2 points after 1 game, 3 goals for and 1 against. The reshuffling did not cost them the opener.

The deepest changes sit at centre. Jeff Friesen88 Jeff Friesen moved from second-line centre to first. John Madden81 John Madden dropped from first-line centre to the fourth line. Sergei Brylin78 Sergei Brylin shifted from a fourth-line winger spot to second-line centre and picked up a penalty-killing role on the second unit. Three of four lines changed hands.

On special teams, Patrik Elias88 Patrik Elias climbed from the fifth man on the top power-play unit to third. Pavel Kubina79 Pavel Kubina was added to the first power-play unit. Kubina is now out with an ankle injury and returns October 14.

Scott Gomez84 Scott Gomez dropped from the third man on the top unit to the fourth. Brian Gionta75 Brian Gionta slid from third-line winger to fourth. Turner Stevenson74 Turner Stevenson moved from fourth-line centre to third-line right wing.

Vernon Fiddler72 Vernon Fiddler dressed for the first time after not appearing in the pre-season chart. Jason Strudwick67 Jason Strudwick and Christian Berglund73 Christian Berglund went the other way: dressed before, out now.

Brodeur played 1 of 1. New Jersey has not dressed a second goaltender.

The Devils host Columbus Blue Jackets Wednesday. Columbus lost 2-1 to Chicago Blackhawks and sits at 0 points after 1 game.
