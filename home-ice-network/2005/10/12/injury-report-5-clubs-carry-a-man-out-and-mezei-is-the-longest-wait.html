Injury Report: 5 clubs carry a man out, and Mezei is the longest wait

Kubina and Reichel return Tuesday, Ozolinsh Thursday, Jagr Friday. Florida waits until November

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkWednesday, 12 October 2005
ShareCopy link

Five clubs enter the week with a player on the injured list. The league has designated a body part for all 5.

Pavel Kubina79 of New Jersey Devils and Robert Reichel71 of Phoenix Coyotes are the closest to a return. Kubina carries an ankle designation and is out until October 14. Reichel has a wrist and the same return date. New Jersey Devils hosts Columbus Blue Jackets on the 12th and the 14th gives them a full day to clear.

Sandis Ozolinsh87 of Carolina Hurricanes has a hand designation, out until October 16. His absence opened a spot on the blue line that Tomas Malec70 Tomas Malec filled in the opener against Atlanta Thrashers. Sean Hill77 Sean Hill moved up a pair.

Jaromir Jagr91 of Washington Capitals carries an elbow and returns October 17. Washington lost 3-2 in overtime to Nashville Predators on October 11, playing without their Art Ross winner.

Branislav Mezei72 of Florida Panthers has a leg designation and is out until November 13. The 32 days are the longest single spell the league has recorded this season.

Six more clubs logged a spell that is already over: Mighty Ducks of Anaheim, Dallas Stars, Buffalo Sabres, Boston Bruins, Atlanta Thrashers, Toronto Maple Leafs, New York Rangers, and Nashville Predators each had 1 spell with 0 or 1 day lost. Those players are in their clubs' lineups.
