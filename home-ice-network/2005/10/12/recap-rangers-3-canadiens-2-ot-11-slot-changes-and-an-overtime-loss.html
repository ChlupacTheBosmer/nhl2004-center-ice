RECAP: Rangers 3, Canadiens 2 (OT) — 11 slot changes and an overtime loss

Montreal dressed Stephane Quintal for the first time, moved Kilger to first-line centre, and lost in the extra frame on opening night

Luc PelletierMontreal correspondent · Home Ice NetworkWednesday, 12 October 2005
ShareCopy link

Jose Theodore89 Jose Theodore faced 27 shots and gave up 3. The Canadiens lost 3-2 to New York Rangers at Madison Square Garden on October 11, their first game of the season and their first overtime defeat.

The Rangers outshot Montreal 27 to 22. The Canadiens scored twice in regulation to force the extra frame, and New York finished it there.

The lineup card is the story. Eleven Montreal skaters carry different assignments than the pre-season chart. Chad Kilger78 Chad Kilger moved from second-line centre to first. Jason Ward71 Jason Ward jumped from fourth-line centre to second, adding a penalty-killing role on the second unit. Shayne Corson74 Shayne Corson dropped from third-line centre to fourth.

Benoit Gratton66 Benoit Gratton went from dressing to sitting out entirely.

On the back end, Stephane Quintal72 Stephane Quintal dressed after not appearing in the pre-season configuration. Francois Beauchemin72 Francois Beauchemin slid from the top pair to the second. Andrei Markov77 Andrei Markov, Mike Komisarek83 Mike Komisarek and Richard Zednik83 Richard Zednik each lost a power-play slot.

The Rangers sit at 4 points after 2 games, the most of any Eastern Conference club. Mike Dunham84 Mike Dunham played both contests, 2 of 2.

Montreal opens Northeast Division play Tuesday at home against Buffalo Sabres, who beat New York Islanders 5-3 on October 10. Buffalo carries 2 points. Montreal has 1, from the overtime loss. The Sabres sit 8th in the conference. The Canadiens sit 9th.
