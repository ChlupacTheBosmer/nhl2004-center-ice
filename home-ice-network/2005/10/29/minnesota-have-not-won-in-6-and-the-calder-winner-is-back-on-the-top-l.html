Minnesota Have Not Won in 6, and the Calder Winner Is Back on the Top Line

The Wild carry 6 points from 9 games into tonight's home game against Colorado, with Tuomo Pihlman back on the first line and the crease turned over.

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkSaturday, 29 October 2005
ShareCopy link

The piece

Minnesota have not won in 6 games and host Colorado tonight, with Tuomo Pihlman76 back on the top line and a new man listed first in the crease.

The Wild carry 6 points from 9 games. They have scored 23 goals and conceded 28, and their home column reads 1-4.

The Calder winner returns

Tuomo Pihlman won the Calder Trophy last season as the league's top rookie. He did not dress in the early going. Now he goes straight onto the first line and the first power-play unit, the left side of both.

The Wild have shuffled their lines and their defense pairs all month. Pihlman's return is the one that adds.

A crease turned over

Manny Fernandez90 started 6 of Minnesota's 9 games, a 67% share of the work. He is out of the rotation. Jean-Marc Pelletier77, who has 3 games and a 33% share, is listed first, and Johan Holmqvist70 has been added as the second man.

Two Wild forwards are on the injured list. Wes Walz82 is out with an ankle injury, return targeted for 2005-11-04. Bill Muckalt73 is out with a hand injury until 2005-11-19. Walz had been on the second penalty-kill unit before he went out.
