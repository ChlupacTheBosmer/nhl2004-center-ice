Numbers Game: Boston Has Played 6 Games and Not Once at Home

4-1-1 on the road, 0-0 at home, and the first home date is Monday against Ottawa

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkSaturday, 22 October 2005
ShareCopy link

The piece

The Boston Bruins Bruins have played 6 games this season and have not played one at home. Their first home date is Monday, October 24, when the Ottawa Senators Senators visit. It opens a 2-game homestand that closes Thursday against Atlanta Thrashers.

The standings carry Boston at 4-1-1 with 9 points, 2nd in the Eastern Conference. The home/away split reads 0-0 at home and 4-2 on the road.

The most recent win came October 21: a 3-2 road victory at Toronto Maple Leafs, the defending Stanley Cup champions. The Bruins outshot Toronto 30 to 23. That result pushed Boston past both New York Rangers and Pittsburgh Penguins, who sit at 8 points each.

The goaltending shifted before the weekend. Steve Shields84 moved from the backup slot to the starter's role. Andrew Raycroft85, who had started 5 of the first 6 games, was dropped from the dressed list. Chris Mason71 was elevated to the backup position.

Sergei Samsonov87 carries a neck injury with a November 9 return date. He is the only Boston player on the active injured list. The Bruins scored 20 and allowed 15 across those 6 road games.

Ottawa visits at 1-2-0, 2 points, riding a 1-game win streak after beating New Jersey Devils 3-1. It is the first meeting between these clubs this season.
