Goalie Watch: Luongo played 45 of 82, then played 24 straight

A shared job in October became a workhorse's burden in May, and Turco never had a shared job at all

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkSunday, 29 May 2005
ShareCopy link

In the regular season, Roberto Luongo95 dressed for 45 of Toronto's 82 games. Ed Belfour dressed for 37. The workload table shows a 55% and a 45% split, a committee.

In the postseason, Luongo played every single game. Twenty-four starts across four rounds. 15 wins, 9 losses, a 1.81 GAA, a .944 save percentage, 3 shutouts. No other goaltender in the organization has a playoff appearance this spring.

Marty Turco97 never faced that situation. He played 68 of Dallas's 82 regular season games (83% of the total) and all 20 postseason games. 14 wins, 6 losses, 1.54 GAA, .924 save percentage.

Belfour won the Jennings Trophy with him last season, when the split was presumably similar. Luongo entered this spring with a shorter track record of full-season starters' work, and by Monday's Game 6 he has played 24 more games in the postseason than Belfour ever did in his time here. Whether that workload has landed on his body differently than 68 and 20 on Turco's, nobody can say from the outside.

Luongo's regular season share is the lowest of any starting goaltender in a Final since the workload table began tracking. The other goaltenders on Toronto's books, Maxime Ouellet86 at 13 games and Mikael Tellqvist73 at 11, were insurance and saw action in the same games Luongo and Belfour did, appearing as relief when one of the two was pulled.

Turco's .924 is the lower save percentage in the matchup. His GAA is the lower of the two. The teams in front of him gave up fewer high-danger chances in the regular season, and the team in front of Luongo gave up fewer goals overall in the playoffs. Neither goaltender has a rest day available. The schedule gives one game, and it is at the building where the road team has already won twice this spring.
