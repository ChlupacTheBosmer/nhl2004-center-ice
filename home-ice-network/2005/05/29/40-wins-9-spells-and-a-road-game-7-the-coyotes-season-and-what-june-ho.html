40 Wins, 9 Spells, and a Road Game 7: the Coyotes' season and what June holds

Phoenix beat the 1st seed and pushed the 2nd seed to six, then went home to a 10-day countdown and a defenseman still hurt

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkSunday, 29 May 2005
ShareCopy link

Phoenix Coyotes finished 40-31-9 for 93 points and the 8th seed in the West. In Round 1 they eliminated Colorado Avalanche, the Presidents' Trophy winner at 43-23-6 and 112 points, in 7 games. Game 7 came on the road, a 4-3 win in Colorado. In Round 2 they took Dallas Stars to 6 before the run ended with a 4-1 loss in Game 6.

Their starting goaltender went on the injured list 9 separate times this season.

Sean Burke84 played 63 of 82 games, a 77% share. The repeat-cases section lists all 9 spells: December 11, December 27, January 10, January 18, January 30, February 28, March 7, March 16, and March 30. The league did not designate a body part for any of them. He played through gaps short enough to keep his season intact but long enough that Phoenix Coyotes dressed 3 goaltenders across the year (Burke 63 games, Brian Boucher77 18, Zac Bierk75 6).

The defense that carried him: Todd Simpson63 is still on the injured list with a groin that the league placed between April 9 and April 12. His return date is June 18, so he will not be available for the draft on June 8. Simpson's 3 separate groin-related spells this season are among the more persistent injury patterns the league recorded.

Phoenix enters the draft with the core from this run intact. Game 5 in the second round was close; Game 6 was a blowout. The front office has 10 days to decide what 93 points means going forward, and a goaltender landing on the injured list nine times a year is part of that call.
