The Infirmary: 7 return before the draft, 6 don't, and Yzerman won't be back until June 26

Thirteen men are still on the injured list. Six will miss the draft, and one will miss July as well

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkSunday, 29 May 2005
ShareCopy link

The rookie draft is 10 days away. As of today, 13 players remain on the league's injured list. Seven of them have return dates before June 8. Six do not.

The biggest name is Steve Yzerman79. The Detroit Red Wings center has a groin the league designated as an injury sometime between April 15 and April 21. His return date is June 26, 18 days after the draft, when summer programming is already underway.

Marty Reasoner74 of Edmonton Oilers has the longest window. His elbow goes back to between April 15 and April 21, and the return date is July 5. He'll miss the draft and the start of summer development.

The two men still out for clubs that are playing right now: Marian Gaborik98 of Toronto (ankle, returns June 2) and Claude Lemieux77 of Dallas (wrist, returns June 6). Gaborik could be available if the Final goes to Game 7 on June 1. Lemieux's timeline puts him back after the Final is scheduled to conclude.

Before the draft, the return dates cluster tightly. Micki Dupont64 (shoulder, June 1), Tom Fitzgerald73 (wrist, May 31), Dennis Seidenberg78 (foot, June 3), Adam Deadmarsh87 (knee, June 4), Matthew Stajan67 (back, June 4), and Steve Moore69 (groin, June 12) are all back within 14 days. Deadmarsh's June 4 date leaves just 4 days before the draft.

After the draft: Lynn Loyns75 (hand, June 11), Brad Norton71 (hip, June 13), Rob Valicevic73 (leg, June 16), and Todd Simpson69 (groin, June 18) will all still be on the shelf when the draft happens on June 8. Their clubs may have already made plans without them.

The season's full injury ledger logged 1,213 spells where the league did not name the body part. Among the named categories, the wrist led at 29 spells with an average of 28 days. The hand averaged the most time lost at 32 days across 17 spells.
