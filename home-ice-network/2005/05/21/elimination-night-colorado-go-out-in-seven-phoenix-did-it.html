ELIMINATION NIGHT: Colorado go out in seven; Phoenix did it

The 1st seed, last spring's Cup finalist, lost Game 7 at home to the 8th seed on 2005-04-20

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkSaturday, 21 May 2005
ShareCopy link

The Colorado Avalanche Avalanche finished the regular season with 112 points, 310 goals scored and a plus-64 differential, the best in the Western Conference. They went to the Cup Final last spring. They lost Round 1 to the Phoenix Coyotes Coyotes, the 8th seed, in seven games.

Game 7 fell on 2005-04-20 at Pepsi Center. Phoenix won it 4-3. Colorado had been 24-17 at home. They finished 0-1 at home in this series.

The series had five one-goal games and one overtime. Phoenix won Game 1 on the road, split Game 2, went up 2-1, lost Game 4 by three, won Game 5, lost Game 6, and closed it out on the Avalanche's ice. At no point did either club hold a lead greater than one game.

The season said it was close

Colorado went 6-5-0 against Phoenix across 11 regular-season meetings. The two clubs combined for 88 goals for and 66 against from Colorado's perspective. That is a 7-4 run rate over 11 games. The 1st seed won the season series, and still fell in seven.

The toll

The Avalanche logged 49 injury spells and 262 days lost this season. Steve Moore68 (groin) will not be available until 2005-06-12. Jamie Storr85 started 68 of 82 games, an 83 percent share. The wear was visible by April: five losses in their last ten regular-season games, and they went 1-4-0 over that stretch's first half before closing 3-1-0.

Phoenix, by contrast, played 40-31-9 hockey, 93 points, minus-1 on goals for and against. They eliminated the top seed in the conference on the road in Game 7.

The Coyotes pushed to Round 2 before Dallas ended their run 4-2. The draft is 18 days away.
