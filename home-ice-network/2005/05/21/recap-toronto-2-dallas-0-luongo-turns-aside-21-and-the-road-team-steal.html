RECAP: Toronto 2, Dallas 0 — Luongo turns aside 21, and the road team steals Game 1

The Maple Leafs outshot the Stars 31-21 on the road and now lead the Final one game to none

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkSaturday, 21 May 2005
ShareCopy link

Roberto Luongo97 stopped 21 shots on 2005-05-20 at the Reunion Arena, and the Toronto Maple Leafs Maple Leafs took Game 1 of the Cup Final 2-0 over the Dallas Stars Stars.

Luongo finished the night with his second shutout of this spring. Across 20 playoff games he sits at 13-7, 1.88 GAA and a .944 save percentage. Toronto generated 31 shots against a Dallas team that managed only 21 of its own, the lowest output the Stars have produced in any game this postseason.

The result fits the season's pattern. Toronto went 2-0-0 against Dallas in the regular season, outscoring them 8-3 with zero one-goal games. Now the Maple Leafs are 3-0-0 against their Final opponent, and every meeting has been decided by two goals or more.

Marty Turco93 drops to 12-4 on the spring with a 1.51 GAA and a .913 save percentage across 16 games. The Stars' top line carries the weight: Mike Modano84 at 20 points and Brenden Morrow82 at 18 both went quiet.

What's next

Dallas gets one day before Game 2 at the same building on 2005-05-22. The series then shifts to Toronto for Games 3 and 4 on 2005-05-24 and 2005-05-26, with a Game 5, if required, returning to Dallas on 2005-05-28.

Series: Toronto leads 1-0.

The Maple Leafs have played 20 playoff games, four more than Dallas at 16. The schedule gives both clubs a rest day before Thursday, then back-to-back starts in Toronto over the weekend.
