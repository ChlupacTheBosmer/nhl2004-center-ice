Injury Report: Dallas lose Lemieux (wrist) and Erskine (elbow) on series-clinching night

The Stars have 4 players on IR with 56 designated spells; Turgeon returns May 12, Lehtinen May 10

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkWednesday, 4 May 2005
ShareCopy link

Two Dallas Stars players went down during the Game 6 series-clincher against Phoenix on 2005-05-02, both with the onset window between 2005-05-02 and 2005-05-04.

Claude Lemieux76, right wing, overall 77: wrist. Return date 2005-06-06. That is a season-ending injury. Lemieux finished the regular season with 77 grade on the Bureau's Book and dressed for the entire spring until Monday. He logged 4 playoff games without a goal or assist in a top-six role.

John Erskine70, defenseman, overall 68: elbow. Return date 2005-05-07. Erskine was signed on 2005-04-30 for $55,890 and dressed for the first time in the Phoenix series. He was already listed on the lineup-change sheet moving from a second-pairing slot to a third-pairing slot before the injury. He returns before Game 2 of the next series, assuming the Stars advance past their first game on 2005-05-04.

The full list

Dallas has 4 players out past today:

Lehtinen's arm injury landed between 2005-04-26 and 2005-04-28, which means he missed Game 3 and onward. He had 5 goals and 8 assists for 13 points in 9 playoff games before going down, the highest points-per-game on the team. Bill Guerin took his roster spot starting with the 2005-04-30 game and is now a healthy scratch on the dressing list.

Turgeon's shoulder goes back to a window between 2005-04-09 and 2005-04-12. He returned briefly in the Detroit series and went down again. The league has now designated him 2 separate spells.

Context for Dallas

The Stars' 56 injury spells and 329 days lost are the fewest among the 8 clubs that finished in a playoff position. The 4 currently out include a first-round center (Turgeon, overall 75), a 13-point winger (Lehtinen, 83), a fourth-liner going out for the year (Lemieux, 77), and a depth defenseman signed for $55,890. Marty Turco97, at 68 games and 83% share, has not been among them. Neither has Mike Modano88, at 13 points in 10 games.
