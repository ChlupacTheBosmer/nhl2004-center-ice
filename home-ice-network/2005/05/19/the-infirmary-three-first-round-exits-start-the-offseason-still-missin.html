The Infirmary: three first-round exits start the offseason still missing their men

Detroit, Nashville and Vancouver carried some of the heaviest injury loads in the league. Their key players are still on the shelf with the draft 20 days away.

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkThursday, 19 May 2005
ShareCopy link

Detroit Red Wings lost 603 days to injury across 56 separate spells. Nashville Predators logged 65 spells, the most of any club, across 425 days. Vancouver Canucks lost 456 days over 60 spells. All three were eliminated in the first round. All three still have players on the injured list.

Detroit: 38 more days without the captain

Steve Yzerman carries a groin designation with a return date of 2005-06-26. The onset window runs between 2005-04-15 and 2005-04-21, the same window in which Dallas closed the first-round series against Detroit in four games. The Wings' postseason ended, and their captain has been out ever since.

Detroit's injury ledger lists Dominik Hasek, Curtis Joseph91, and Nicklas Lidstrom among its repeat entries. The team finished 35-31-6 with 96 points and a 7th seed in the West. They scored 257 and allowed 250, neither figure screaming roster depletion. But Yzerman's 79 overall sat in the press box for every one of those four playoff nights.

Nashville: 65 spells and a wrist that cost the spring

Scott Walker85 appeared on the Predators' injury report 7 separate times. The club's 65 spells ranked first in the league, though its 425 days lost sat 6th. The absences were short. Nashville finished 42-28-7 for 101 points and the third seed. They lost to Anaheim in 6.

Tom Fitzgerald73 (wrist) has a return date of 2005-05-31, 12 days from now. His absence spans the entire postseason. Nashville scored 253 goals in 82 games.

Vancouver: seven and seven

Dan Cloutier90 played 63 games despite the interruptions. Markus Naslund91 played through a season that landed him on the list 7 times. Both sit in the league's repeat-injury table at 7 spells, alongside Mike Modano88 and Mats Sundin92.

Vancouver Canucks finished 38-29-7 with 99 points. They scored 277 and allowed 256, a better goal differential than either Detroit or Nashville. They lost to Edmonton in 6.
