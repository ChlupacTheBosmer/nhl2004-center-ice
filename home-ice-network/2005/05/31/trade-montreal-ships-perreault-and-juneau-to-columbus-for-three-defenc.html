Trade: Montreal Ships Perreault and Juneau to Columbus for Three Defencemen

The Canadiens acquired a blue line after allowing 295 goals in the worst season in the Eastern Conference

Luc PelletierMontreal correspondent · Home Ice NetworkTuesday, 31 May 2005
ShareCopy link

The Montreal Canadiens send Yanic Perreault and Joe Juneau to the Columbus Blue Jackets for Scott Lachance, Darrel Scoville and Jaroslav Spacek. The league confirmed the deal.

Perreault and Juneau both spent time on Columbus's injured list this season. Perreault carries a back designation on the CBJ ledger. Juneau carries a concussion. Both men return to a club that needs forward depth.

Montreal finished 21-46-6 with 66 points, last in the Northeast, last in the Eastern Conference. The club scored 238 goals and allowed 295, a gap of 57 goals. Buffalo's 40-goal deficit is next worst.

The crease was the season's problem. Jose Theodore87 played 66 of 82 games. Dwayne Roloson82 played 21. Eric Fichaud64 played 14. J.F. Damphousse66 played 5. Theodore landed on the injured list 7 separate times this season. Four goalies and no answer.

Montreal's front office thinks the next hole is on the blue line. The blue line allowed 295 goals. Goaltending can be addressed in the draft. The back end needs bodies. Montreal got three.

Columbus finished 26-38-7 with 81 points, 10th in the Western Conference. Pascal Leclaire89 went on injured reserve 6 times. The Blue Jackets lost 395 man-days to injury this season, the 10th-highest total in the league. Perreault and Juneau go back into a room that had 54 separate injury spells this year.

The rookie draft is 8 days away. Both clubs are starting over. Montreal has to hope that three defencemen who couldn't stop goals in Columbus are better than the men who couldn't stop goals in Montreal.
