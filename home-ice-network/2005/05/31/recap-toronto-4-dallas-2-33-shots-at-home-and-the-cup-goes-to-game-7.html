RECAP: Toronto 4, Dallas 2 — 33 shots at home, and the Cup goes to Game 7

The Maple Leafs outshot the Stars 33-18 to take a 4-2 series lead, and the next game is Wednesday in Dallas

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkTuesday, 31 May 2005
ShareCopy link

Toronto Maple Leafs scored four, the Dallas Stars Stars scored two, and 10 days of back-and-forth will be settled in one game on Wednesday.

Roberto Luongo95 turned aside everything Dallas put at him early, and the shot chart told the rest of the story: 33 for the home club, 18 for the visitor. No goaltender can hold a crease against 33 shots and keep the game at one. Toronto pulled away in the third period and won 4-2.

The series is 4-2 to Toronto. Game 7 is at Dallas on 2005-06-01.

Mats Sundin92 sits at 11 goals and 7 assists for 18 points in 25 playoff games. Gary Roberts85 has 7 goals and 12 assists for 19 in the same 25 games. Dallas answers with Mike Modano86 at 10 and 14 for 24 points in 21 games. The scoring race between those three men has carried both clubs through four rounds.

The crease tilts toward Toronto

Luongo enters Game 7 with a postseason line of 16-9, a 1.81 goals-against average, a .943 save percentage and 3 shutouts. Marty Turco95 is 14-7 with a 1.65 GAA and a .921 save percentage. The numbers sit close on paper, but Toronto's depth forwards generated 15 more chances than Dallas in Game 6, and a .921 against 33 shots lets 3 or 4 through.

Dallas was 30-11 at home in the regular season. In this postseason they are 7-4 in their own building, which includes two losses to Toronto in this Final.

What separates them

Toronto outscored Dallas 14-8 across six games. That is a 6-goal margin over 6 contests. One team converts late in close games, the other does not. The 4-2 score in Game 6 is the widest winning margin in this Final.

The regular season said the same thing: Toronto went 5-1-1 against Dallas with a 20-11 goal differential.

Game 7. Wednesday. Dallas' building. One win for the Maple Leafs' second consecutive Cup, one win for the Stars to win the series.
