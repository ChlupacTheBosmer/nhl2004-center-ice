Sixteen Overtimes, Five Wins: Florida went there more than anyone and came home last

The Panthers' 16 overtime losses were the most in the league, and the club behind them has no answer for why

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkTuesday, 31 May 2005
ShareCopy link

Florida went 5-16 in overtime. Five wins in 21 chances past 60 minutes. A coin flips heads at .500. The Panthers flipped heads at .238.

What it cost them

Each overtime loss is 1 point. Each overtime win is 2. Florida finished with 76 points, 14th of 15 clubs in the East, ahead of only Ottawa Senators at 77 and Montreal Canadiens at 66. The gap between 76 and the 8th-place Atlanta Thrashers is 8 points. Florida needed 4 more OT wins and 4 fewer OT losses to close that gap. They converted 5 of 21.

The club was on an 11-game losing streak at the end of the regular season, the longest active run in the league on the final day. Home record 15-26, road record 15-26. Equally bad everywhere.

The voice from the room

"Losing in overtime means you got to the promised land and you couldn't cash the check," Boris Van Ryswyk told The Tunnel's Marcus Bell after the season. "Every single one of those games was winnable. Sixteen games that should have been sixteen points, or thirty-two points. That's the difference between 76 and 92, 94."

He did not blame the system. "The structure was fine. We had enough structure to get to the third period of every game, and we didn't have enough structure to close them. That's a talent problem."

The crease, again

Florida used 4 goaltenders on the season: Felix Potvin86 in 37 games, David Aebischer87 in 33, Jani Hurme85 in 2 and Mathieu Landry73 in 1. Neither Potvin nor Aebischer held more than 45% of the games. No starter emerged, and the committee lost 16 one-goal overtime games.

The draft is 8 days away. The Panthers will pick high. Van Ryswyk said on The Tunnel that he was looking for "somebody who makes the other team's life miserable in the last ten minutes," and that he was not certain that player exists in this class.
