Roster Move Roundup: Atlanta Swaps Two Defencemen, Moves Heatley to Centre on the Power Play

Boileau and Tjarnqvist out, Foster and Sutton in as the Thrashers try to fix the division's worst defence

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkTuesday, 31 May 2005
ShareCopy link

The Atlanta Thrashers moved four defencemen in and out of the dressed lineup. Patrick Boileau70 and Daniel Tjarnqvist68 come out. Kurtis Foster68 and Andy Sutton70 go in. The forward group shifts too.

Atlanta finished 31-37-6 with 84 points. The club allowed 284 goals, the most in the Southeast Division. In the last 10 games, Atlanta scored 27 and gave up 34. The group could not close. Atlanta was 22-19 at home and 17-24 on the road.

Boileau carried slots on the top defensive pairing, the second penalty-kill unit and the second power-play unit. Tjarnqvist sat on the third pair. In their place, Foster enters at the left side of the top pairing, the first penalty-kill unit and the first power-play unit. Sutton mirrors him on the right side.

The swap says Atlanta's front office blames the back end for the 84-point finish. Byron Dafoe79 played 68 of 82 games. Milan Hnilicka75 played 20. Pasi Nurminen71 played 6. Dafoe was the clear starter.

Dany Heatley85 shifts from a left-wing slot on the second power-play unit to centre. Ilya Kovalchuk86 moves from centre to left wing on the first power-play unit. Marc Savard70 stays at centre on the top unit.

Derek MacKenzie68 enters the lineup at the second penalty-kill centre slot and the third-line centre spot. Vyacheslav Sbruev63 gets the third-line left wing. Clarke Wilm63 comes out entirely. Patrik Stefan74 loses his special-teams assignments and drops to a checking-line role.

Atlanta finished with 84 points, the same total as New York Islanders, New York Rangers and Buffalo Sabres. The Islanders held the final Eastern playoff berth. The rookie draft is 8 days away.
