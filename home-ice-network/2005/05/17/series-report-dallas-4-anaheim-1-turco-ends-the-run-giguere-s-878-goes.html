SERIES REPORT: Dallas 4, Anaheim 1 — Turco ends the run, Giguere's .878 goes with it

The Stars close out the West in overtime at home; Modano finishes with 20 points and Turco at 1.48

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkTuesday, 17 May 2005
ShareCopy link

Dallas Stars beat Mighty Ducks of Anaheim 2-1 in overtime Saturday to take the Western Conference Final 4 games to 1. The Stars advance to the Stanley Cup Final against the winner of the Toronto-Washington series.

Marty Turco97 finished the postseason 12-3 with a 1.48 goals-against average and a .910 save percentage. Three of the five games in this series went to overtime, and Turco was in net for all three. He held the Ducks to 1 goal in Game 5 and 1 goal in Game 2.

Mike Modano88 tallied 9 goals and 11 assists for 20 points across 15 games this spring. Brenden Morrow86 added 18 points (4+14). The Dallas top pair finished the series outscoring Anaheim 14-9.

The Ducks exit with a goaltender's number nobody wanted

Jean-Sebastien Giguere97 closed the postseason at 8-7 with a .878 save percentage. He entered this series at .872 in 12 starts after two rounds. Three one-goal losses here dropped it further. He played 72 games in the regular season, 88 percent of Anaheim's schedule.

Mighty Ducks of Anaheim came into this series as the 6th seed, having beaten Nashville Predators in 6 and Edmonton Oilers in 6. They scored 1 goal in Games 1 and 3 in Dallas, the two worst nights of their postseason. Game 4, a 3-2 overtime win at home, was their only multi-goal performance of the five-game series.

Sergei Fedorov90 finished with 12 goals and 6 assists (18 points) in 17 games. He was the only Anaheim player above 14 points.

Rob Valicevic73 went down with a leg injury during this series, listed between May 13 and May 15 with a return date of June 16. Andy McDonald74 was promoted into the lineup for the final games.

Dallas Stars has 3 days before the Cup Final opens. Toronto Maple Leafs and Washington Capitals still have Game 7 on Wednesday.
