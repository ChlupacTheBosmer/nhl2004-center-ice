RECAP: Toronto 2, Washington 1 — 18 shots on the road, and the series goes seven

Luongo faces 38 and the Maple Leafs steal one in Washington; Game 7 is Wednesday at home

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkTuesday, 17 May 2005
ShareCopy link

Toronto Maple Leafs took Game 6, 2-1, at Washington Capitals on Monday night on 18 shots, tying the Eastern Conference Final at 3 games apiece. Washington fired 38 pucks, a 20-shot margin that came up one goal short. Roberto Luongo95 stood in the Toronto crease and kept a lead built on very few chances. He entered the night with an 11-7 record and a .939 save percentage across these playoffs.

The series turned after Washington Capitals took a 3-1 lead through four games. Toronto Maple Leafs has won the last two on the road, both by a single goal: 3-2 in overtime in Game 3, and 2-1 on Monday.

Jaromir Jagr91 carries the most points in this postseason at 21 (5 goals, 16 assists in 14 games). He had no hand in either goal Monday. Pascal Dupuis80 leads Washington in goals with 9 in 14.

The Capitals came into this series having won 7 of 8 playoff games, sweeping Carolina Hurricanes 4-0 and then sweeping Tampa Bay Lightning 4-0. A 4-0 loss at home in Game 2 sits in that ledger. Toronto Maple Leafs has clawed back to even.

Game 7 is Wednesday at Toronto

Washington Capitals went 29-12 on the road this regular season, the 4th-best mark in the conference. Toronto Maple Leafs went 33-8 at home, the best in the league. The Capitals scored 287 goals this season; in this series, they have 14 across 6 games.

Olaf Kolzig89 played 74 games in the regular season, 90 percent of Washington's schedule. He is 9-3 in these playoffs with a 1.69 goals-against average. Game 7 will be his 75th appearance this spring.

The series now shifts to Toronto, where the Maple Leafs have home ice and the longer rest. The winner plays Dallas Stars in the Stanley Cup Final.
