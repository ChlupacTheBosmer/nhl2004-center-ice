RECAP: Dallas 2, Toronto 1 -- 26 shots from the visitor, and the home team holds

The Stars even the Final at one game apiece. Game 3 is Tuesday in a building where the Maple Leafs lost 8 times all season.

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkMonday, 23 May 2005
ShareCopy link

Dallas Stars beat Toronto Maple Leafs 2-1 on Sunday night at the American Airlines Center to tie the Final at one game apiece. The Maple Leafs fired 26 shots at Marty Turco95 and got one past him. The Stars returned 25 at Roberto Luongo97.

The series shifts to Toronto on Tuesday, where the Maple Leafs went 33-8 this season. Dallas went 21-20 on the road. That is the big story heading into Game 3.

Turco is 13-4 with a 1.48 GAA and a .917 save percentage across 17 playoff appearances. He played 68 regular season games. Luongo sits at 13-8, 1.89, .943, and 2 shutouts this spring, carrying 45 starts from the regular schedule.

Mike Modano86 leads all scorers in this spring with 20 points (9+11) in 17 games. Brenden Morrow84 has 18 (4+14) in the same window for Dallas. On the Toronto side, Mats Sundin92 has 16 (10+6) in 21 games played and Gary Roberts87 is one behind at 15 (5+10) in 21.

Both clubs are missing men. Dallas is without Claude Lemieux76, who carries a wrist designation and has a return date of June 6. Toronto has played 21 of 23 playoff games without Marian Gaborik99, whose ankle keeps him sidelined through June 2. Gaborik produced 10 points (4+6) in the 8 games he played before going down.

The regular-season head-to-head went 2-1-0 to Toronto with a 9-5 goal margin. One of those three meetings went to overtime.

Game 3 is May 24 in Toronto.
