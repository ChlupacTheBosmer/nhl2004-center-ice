Only 28 and Only 8: Philadelphia's goaltending has no answer 16 days before the draft

The Flyers listed two goaltenders on the workload table. Neither is a starter. The draft is June 8.

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkMonday, 23 May 2005
ShareCopy link

Philadelphia Flyers went 28-35-12 and finished 12th in the Eastern Conference at 82 points. Mathematically out, well before the schedule ended. The workload table is why you can't blame defense for the 291 goals against.

Robert Esche78 played 28 games. His share of the club's schedule was 34%. Neil Little71 played 8 games, a 10% share. Those are the only goaltenders listed for Philadelphia in the workload table.

No other club in the league has fewer than 73 games covered by the goaltenders on its sheet. Philadelphia's two names cover 28 and 8. That's 54 games the Bureau has no goaltender on, in a season the Flyers finished 24 goals in the red.

Philadelphia logged 48 injury spells and 324 days lost. Dennis Seidenberg78 carries a foot designation with a return date of June 3. Sturm, Primeau, LeClair, and Gagne all had multiple spells through the spring.

The draft is June 8, 16 days from now. Free agency closes July 28. Washington's Olaf Kolzig89 played 74 games this season, and his general manager Shawn Holliday told The Tunnel's Jan Kowalczyk that the unsigned pool holds nobody: "There's nobody unsigned." If that reading is right across the league, Philadelphia can't simply phone a veteran and fill the crease in July. The fix is the draft or a trade.

Two goaltenders, 34% and 10%. The rest of the 82-game schedule has no name on it.
