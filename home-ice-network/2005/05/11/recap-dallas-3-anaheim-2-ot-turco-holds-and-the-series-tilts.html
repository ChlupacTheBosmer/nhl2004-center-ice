RECAP: Dallas 3, Anaheim 2 OT -- Turco holds, and the series tilts

The Presidents' Trophy winners edge another one-goal game in overtime and take a 2-0 lead into Game 3

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkWednesday, 11 May 2005
ShareCopy link

Dallas Stars beat Mighty Ducks of Anaheim 3-2 in overtime on May 8 at the American Airlines Center to take a 2-0 series lead in the Western Conference Final.

Marty Turco95 stopped 17 of 19 shots, lowering his postseason goals-against to 1.49. Dallas outshot Anaheim 25-19 across 65 minutes and needed the extra frame to break a game the Ducks controlled for 60.

The series has gone the way of the regular season: close, then not close. Dallas went 6-1-1 against Anaheim in 8 meetings, and the 29-19 goal differential was larger than the playoff margins suggest. Game 1 was 4-1. Game 2 was a one-goal overtime affair.

Mike Modano86 sits at 9 goals and 9 assists in 13 games this spring, the highest point total among players in this series. Brenden Morrow84 has 3 goals and 13 assists. Sergei Zubov82 contributes 5 and 8 in 13.

Anaheim's Sergei Fedorov88 has 11 goals and 5 assists in 15 games, the most goals by any player still playing this postseason. He is 39 years old. The Mighty Ducks of Anaheim lead in scoring and they sit two games from elimination.

Jean-Sebastien Giguere95 gave up 3 on 25 shots in the loss, part of a postseason line that reads 7-6 with a .872 save percentage. That number has been low all spring.

Game 3 goes to Anaheim on May 10.

Series: Dallas leads 2-0.
