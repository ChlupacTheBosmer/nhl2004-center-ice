Goalie Watch: Giguere faces elimination with the lowest save percentage left in the field

Jean-Sebastien Giguere is at .872 in 13 postseason starts. Dallas has outscored Anaheim 12-3 across three Conference Final games.

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkWednesday, 11 May 2005
ShareCopy link

Jean-Sebastien Giguere97 enters Game 4 on May 12 at Anaheim with a postseason record of 7-6, a 2.29 goals-against average, and a .872 save percentage. Of the goaltenders still active in these playoffs, his save percentage is the lowest by a wide margin.

Marty Turco95 of Dallas Stars sits at .913 in 13 starts, a 1.49 GAA, and an 11-2 record. Olaf Kolzig91 of Washington Capitals is 8-1 at 1.48 with a .937 save percentage and one shutout. Roberto Luongo97 of Toronto Maple Leafs is 9-6 at 2.17 and .937.

At .872, Giguere has stopped fewer than 9 of every 10 shots he has faced since April 8. In this series alone, Dallas has scored 12 goals on him across 3 games while Anaheim has managed 3. The Game 3 scoreline on May 10 read 5-1: Giguere surrendered 5 on 20 shots, a .750 game save percentage that belongs in a February start, not a Conference Final.

Ilja Bryzgalov79 has not offered relief: 1-1, 2.40 GAA, .852 save percentage in 2 appearances. Bryzgalov cannot be the answer because his own numbers are lower than Giguere's.

Mighty Ducks of Anaheim's offense has managed 3 goals in 3 games against Dallas this round. Sergei Fedorov90 has 16 points in 15 games and Vaclav Prospal79 has 13, but in this series the goals have not come. Three games, three goals, zero wins.

Game 4 comes down to whether the coaching staff starts the man with the .872 or turns to a backup with a .852. Dallas generated 16 shots in Game 3 and scored 5. Anaheim generated 19 and scored 1. The crease gap is the series gap, and the sweep sits one win from Dallas Stars.
