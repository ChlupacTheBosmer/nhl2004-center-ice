RECAP: Washington 2, Toronto 3 OT -- the 45-shot Maple Leafs pull one back

Toronto fires 45 shots on the road and survives in overtime to stay alive in the series, still down 2-1

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkWednesday, 11 May 2005
ShareCopy link

Toronto Maple Leafs won 3-2 in overtime at Washington Capitals on May 10, firing 45 shots on the road to stay out of a 0-3 hole in their Cup defence.

Toronto put 45 pucks on Olaf Kolzig87 across 65 minutes, and Washington managed 33. Their netminder had gone 8-1 through 9 starts. The Maple Leafs needed an extra frame and a franchise record 45 road shots to finally beat him.

Roberto Luongo94 stopped 31 of 33 shots, keeping his postseason line at 9-6, 2.17 GAA, and a .937 save percentage. He has been the steady hand through 15 postseason games, including 6 against Washington in the regular season.

Jaromir Jagr84 carries 4 goals and 14 assists in 11 playoff games, a point a game for the club now in the Eastern Conference Final against the Cup champions. Pascal Dupuis74 has 8 goals and 6 assists in 11. Robert Lang75 has 8 and 5.

Olaf Kolzig took the loss, dropping to 8-1 with a 1.48 goals-against average. He has been beaten 15 times in 9 starts on a Vezina season, and one loss does not erase that. But 45 pucks in 65 minutes is a lot to ask of anyone.

Toronto's top point producer remains Gary Roberts88 at 4 and 9 in 15 games. Mats Sundin91 has 6 and 12. Marian Gaborik98 is still out, and it shows; he has 4 and 6 in the 8 games he played before his ankle went down.

Game 4 goes May 12 at Washington.

Series: Washington leads 2-1.
