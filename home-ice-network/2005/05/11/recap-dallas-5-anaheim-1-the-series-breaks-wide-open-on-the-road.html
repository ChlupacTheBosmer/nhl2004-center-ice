RECAP: Dallas 5, Anaheim 1 -- the series breaks wide open on the road

A 5-1 blowout at the Arrowhead puts the Ducks one loss from the off-season

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkWednesday, 11 May 2005
ShareCopy link

Dallas Stars scored 5 goals on 20 shots and held Mighty Ducks of Anaheim to 1 on 16 in a 5-1 road win on May 10 that puts the Ducks one game from elimination.

The 4-goal margin is the widest of the series. Game 1 ended 4-1, Game 2 went to overtime, and Game 3 is a 4-goal difference. The regular-season head-to-head produced 6 Dallas wins, 1 Anaheim win, and 1 overtime loss on the Anaheim side, with a 29-19 goal gap. Game 3 looked like the worst version of that record.

Mike Modano86 reached 18 points on the season's playoff run, which ties him atop this series in points. Brenden Morrow84 added to his 13-assist total, and Jere Lehtinen84 has 5 goals and 8 assists in just 9 games. At that rate he is the most efficient offensive player on this roster when in the lineup.

Marty Turco95 improved to 11-2 with a 1.49 goals-against average. In 13 starts he has surrendered the league's lowest GAA among qualifying goaltenders.

Jean-Sebastien Giguere97 allowed 5 goals on 20 shots, dropping his postseason save percentage deeper into the low .800s. The team generated 16 shots against a Dallas defense that has been stingy all spring, and the 1 goal they scored came against a goaltender who has been beaten 39 times in 13 games. The Ducks managed 24 goals in their 12 games across this postseason through the first two rounds. They scored 3 in three games against Dallas.

Sergei Fedorov90 finished the night with 16 points in 15 games. His numbers are the only ones that make a case for this team being here.

Game 4 goes tomorrow, May 12, at Anaheim.

Series: Dallas leads 3-0.
