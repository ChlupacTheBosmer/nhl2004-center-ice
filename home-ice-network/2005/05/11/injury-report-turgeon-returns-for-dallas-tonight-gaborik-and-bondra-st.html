Injury Report: Turgeon returns for Dallas tonight; Gaborik and Bondra still out

The Stars get their veteran left wing back on elimination-eve for Anaheim, while two East sides wait on men who won't be back this week

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkWednesday, 11 May 2005
ShareCopy link

Pierre Turgeon is listed as returning to the lineup on May 12 for Dallas Stars's Game 4 at Mighty Ducks of Anaheim. The left wing, 75 overall, has been out since a shoulder designation with an onset between April 9 and April 12. He missed Dallas's entire Round 2 series against Phoenix and the first three games of the Conference Final.

Dallas is up 3-0 and the scoring depth is there without him: Modano 18 points, Morrow 16, Zubov 13, Lehtinen 13 in 9. But putting a 75-overall left wing into the top six on the road, with the Cup Final close enough to look at, lets the lines settle before that series starts.

Marian Gaborik99 remains on Toronto's injured list with an ankle, onset between April 26 and April 28, return targeted for June 2. He has 10 points in 8 postseason games. The Maple Leafs have played 15 without him in this run, leaning on Gary Roberts87 (13 points in 15) and Mats Sundin92 (12 in 15) to fill the gap. If the series goes 7 games, he is still unavailable through the end of the week.

Peter Bondra88 of Washington Capitals carries a knee designation with an onset between April 9 and April 12, return May 29. He has been out since the first round. Washington has gone 11 playoff games without him and their top line of Jagr, Dupuis, and Lang has produced 18, 14, and 13 points respectively.

The clubs with the deepest injury ledgers are the ones still playing. Dallas filed 59 injury spells this season, 332 days lost. Anaheim filed 63 spells, 492 days lost. Toronto filed 76, the most in the league, though only 132 days lost. Washington filed 55 spells and 355 days.
