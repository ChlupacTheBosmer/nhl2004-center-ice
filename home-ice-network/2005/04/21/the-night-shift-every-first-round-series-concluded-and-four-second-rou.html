The Night Shift: every first-round series concluded, and four second-round matchups are set

Eight eliminations in six days. Round 2 starts tomorrow.

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkThursday, 21 April 2005
ShareCopy link

Between April 15 and April 20, every first-round series ended. The second round opens Friday.

East

Washington Capitals swept Carolina Hurricanes in four. The closeout, 2-1 in Raleigh on April 15, gave Olaf Kolzig83 a 4-0 record with a 1.00 GAA for the series. Jaromir Jagr84 (0+9=9 in 4) never scored but set everything else.

Dallas Stars swept Detroit Red Wings in four. The final game, 3-1 in Detroit on April 15, capped Marty Turco95 at 4-0 and 1.17 GAA. Detroit managed 5 goals across the sweep. Steve Yzerman, who finished the series with 2 assists, went on the injured list with a groin injury sometime between April 15 and April 21 and is not back until June 26.

Pittsburgh Penguins beat New Jersey Devils 4-2. The seventh seed won Game 6, 3-2 in overtime, on the road. Five of the six games were decided by one goal. Backup Sebastien Caron76 took the Game 6 decision (1-0, 1.00 GAA, .952) after Johan Hedberg86 went 3-2 (.907) through five starts.

Tampa Bay Lightning beat Boston Bruins 4-2. Tampa Bay closed it out 4-2 in Boston on April 18. Nikolai Khabibulin92 finished 4-2, 1.99 GAA, one shutout.

Toronto Maple Leafs beat New York Islanders 4-1. The 2-1 closeout in Toronto on April 16 gave Roberto Luongo95 a series line of 4-1, 1.41 GAA, .963.

West

Mighty Ducks of Anaheim beat Nashville Predators 4-2. The Ducks scored 4 in a Game 6 win at home on April 18. Sergei Fedorov88 (4+2=6 in 6) led the way.

Edmonton Oilers beat Vancouver Canucks 4-2. Edmonton won Game 6 in Vancouver, 3-1, on April 19. Mike York76 finished with 11 points in 6.

Phoenix Coyotes beat Colorado Avalanche 4-3. The eighth seed won Game 7 in Denver, 4-3, on April 20. Sean Burke82 finished 4-2, 2.44 GAA, .909.
