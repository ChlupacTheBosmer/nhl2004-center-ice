SERIES REPORT: Washington 2, Carolina 0 -- Kolzig holds the Hurricanes to a goal a night

The Capitals take a 2-0 lead to Carolina, where Game 3 is set for April 13.

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkTuesday, 12 April 2005
ShareCopy link

Carolina Hurricanes has scored twice in two games and lost both. Washington Capitals won the opener 2-1 on April 9 and the second 4-1 on April 11, and the series moves to Carolina for Game 3 on April 13.

Olaf Kolzig87 has a 1.00 goals-against and a .952 save percentage in the two wins. Washington finished the regular season 39-26-8 with 104 points, fourth in the East. Carolina finished 30-25-14 with 100 points and fifth, and the two clubs met seven times, Washington winning five. Five of those seven were one-goal games.

Washington has not needed much at the other end. Jaromir Jagr84 has 3 assists, Robert Lang75 2 goals, Mike Grier76 a goal and an assist, Sergei Gonchar78 2 assists.

For Carolina, Radim Vrbata75 has a goal and an assist, Mathieu Dandenault74 2 assists, Tomas Kurka73 a goal and Rod Brind&#x27;Amour79 an assist.

Kevin Weekes86 has a 3.00 goals-against and a .880 save percentage in the Carolina net. The Hurricanes scored 278 goals in the regular season; Washington allowed 250.

Carolina returns home for Game 3 on April 13 and Game 4 on April 15. Washington won its last six regular-season games and has carried that into April.
