SERIES REPORT: Boston 1, Tampa Bay 1 -- Murray's 7 points carry the Bruins home

The Bruins have the series level and Game 3 tonight, with their top line producing and their goaltender not.

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkTuesday, 12 April 2005
ShareCopy link

Glen Murray81 has 7 points in two games and the first-round series between Boston Bruins and Tampa Bay Lightning is level at 1-1. Game 3 is tonight in Boston.

Tampa Bay won the opener 3-2 on April 8, and Boston answered with a 5-3 win on April 10. Murray has 3 goals and 4 assists across the two. Mike Knuble74 has 2 goals and 3 assists, Joe Thornton86 2 and 2, Nick Boynton70 one assist.

Tampa Bay's answer is Cory Stillman85, who has 4 goals in the two games. Dave Andreychuk has 3 assists, Bill Lindsay73 a goal and an assist, Dan Boyle81 2 assists.

Andrew Raycroft80 has a 3.00 goals-against and a .850 save percentage for Boston; Nikolai Khabibulin90 has a 3.50 goals-against and a .865 save percentage for Tampa Bay. Raycroft has had 10 injury spells this season.

The Lightning went 3-1-2 against Boston in the regular season, and four of the six meetings were one-goal games. Boston finished 33-29-9 with 97 points, sixth in the East; Tampa Bay finished 39-25-7 with 107 points and third.

Boston gets Game 3 tonight and Game 4 on April 14, both at home. The Bruins need the goaltending to match the top line, or the series swings back to Tampa Bay with Murray's scoring as the only thing holding it up.
