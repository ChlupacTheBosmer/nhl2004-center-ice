RECAP: Toronto 3, Islanders 1 -- Luongo turns aside 43 of 44 and the sweep sits one win away

Gaborik carries the playoff lead at 7 points while the Islanders outshot the Maple Leafs 44 to 18 and scored once

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkWednesday, 13 April 2005
ShareCopy link

The piece

Toronto leads this series 3-0 after a 3-1 win at New York Islanders on 2005-04-12. Game 4 goes at the Islanders' building on 2005-04-14, where a Toronto win ends the series.

Roberto Luongo97 faced 44 shots on goal and allowed one. The Islanders outshot Toronto Maple Leafs 44 to 18 and managed a single goal against the lowest goals-against average in these playoffs. Luongo's series line after three games: 3 wins, no losses, 1.01 GAA, .974 save percentage.

Toronto needed 18 shots to produce three goals. Marian Gaborik99 carries 2 goals and 5 assists for 7 points across three games, the highest total of any player in the postseason. Gary Roberts87 adds 1 and 5 for 6. Mats Sundin92 has 3 goals and 1 assist. Three Toronto forwards at more than a point per game against a team that has produced four points across four different men.

Rick DiPietro88 stopped 15 of 18 shots tonight. His series line is 0-3, 3.33 GAA, .839. The Islanders' scoring through three games: Jason Blake81, Chris Simon72, and Adrian Aucoin79 each with 1 goal, and Mike Peca82 with 1 assist. Four points from four players across 3 games, against 10 goals allowed.

Toronto finished 5-1-1 against New York in the regular season, outscoring them 21-12 in seven meetings. The Maple Leafs shot 45 and 25 against DiPietro in Games 5 and 6 of the regular season and allowed 24 and 20 back.

A Game 4 win on Thursday puts Toronto in the second round. A Game 4 win keeps the Islanders' season alive. The 44-18 shot gap from Tuesday night is what they have to close.
