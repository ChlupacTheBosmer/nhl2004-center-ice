Injury Report: Marchant's Third Trip Keeps Columbus's Center Out Until January 30

Two Blue Jackets went down over the weekend, and the center with three absences this fall is the one who can't be replaced tonight.

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkSunday, 20 November 2005
ShareCopy link

Todd Marchant80 is out until January 30 with a knee, his third trip to the injured list this season. The Columbus Blue Jackets center carries an 84 in the Book. The league named a body part on this spell. The two earlier absences this fall carried no designation.

Hannes Hyvonen64 went down on November 17 with a groin and returns December 6. Columbus lost the same night, 4-3 in overtime at Dallas Stars.

The Blue Jackets reshuffled the bottom of their depth chart. Adam Mair63 entered at fourth-line right wing. Kent McDonell65 moved from the fourth line to the third. Dimitri Kalinin80 slid up to the second defensive pairing. Jean-Luc Grand-Pierre73 shifted to the third pairing, and Josh Holden68 and Tyler Wright76 each moved one slot down the forward lines.

Marchant's 3 spells account for 3 of Columbus's 13 injury absences this season. The club has lost 95 days to injury, the 7th most in the league. The knee keeps him out until January 30, well past the return dates on any of the other 12 Blue Jackets absences.

Marchant and Hyvonen cover center and right wing, and their simultaneous absence thins Columbus's middle six. The Blue Jackets are 7-5-2, 24 points, and 4th in the Western Conference. The losing streak is 3, all since November 12.

Columbus hosts San Jose Sharks tonight, the 3rd meeting of a series that sits 1-0-1 to Columbus with 4 goals each side. Both prior meetings were decided by one goal. San Jose has lost 2 days to injury all season, the fewest in the league, and Evgeni Nabokov89 has started 14 of 16 games at 88 percent.
