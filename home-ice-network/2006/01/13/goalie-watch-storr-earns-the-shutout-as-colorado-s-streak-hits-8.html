Goalie Watch: Storr Earns the Shutout as Colorado's Streak Hits 8

Jamie Storr stopped all 20 Carolina shots on 2006-01-12, and the Avalanche ride the longest active winning streak in the Western Conference into a first meeting with Detroit on Saturday

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkFriday, 13 January 2006
ShareCopy link

Jamie Storr85 made 20 saves on 20 shots as Colorado Avalanche won 2-0 at Carolina Hurricanes on 2006-01-12, pushing the Avalanche's winning streak to 8 games, the longest active run in the Western Conference.

Colorado (20-12-7, 57 points) sits 4th in the West, 9 points behind Dallas Stars at 63. That is a lot of ground to make up, but the streak has turned a team that was 12-12-7 through 36 games into an upper-half club.

The goaltending workload tells part of the story. Storr has started 31 of 44 games, a 70 percent share. Trevor Kidd83 has 12 starts. Neither man is a Vezina candidate, but the club has conceded only 20 goals in its last 10 wins, a 2.0 average against. That 2.0 is structure, not a hot streak in front of the net.

The road record drives the streak. Colorado is 14-7 away from home, the best mark in the Northwest division. Before the current run, the Avalanche had been below .500 at home all season (11-12 at the time of writing) and had quietly built a playoff position on the road. Eight straight, with 4 of them coming away from the mountain, says the travel is not the problem.

Saturday's test comes in a first meeting

Detroit Red Wings visits Colorado Avalanche on 2006-01-16, the first time these clubs have met this season. Detroit is 16-18-3 with 47 points, 9th in the West, chasing the 8th-place Minnesota Wild Wild (48 points) by a single point. For Detroit, it is a must-win on the road against a team on a heater. For Colorado, it is the chance to make a top-division rival a little further away.

Detroit is 7-14 on the road. Colorado is 14-7 at home. The arithmetic favors the streak, which until Saturday has been building against opponents the Avalanche have already beaten this season.
