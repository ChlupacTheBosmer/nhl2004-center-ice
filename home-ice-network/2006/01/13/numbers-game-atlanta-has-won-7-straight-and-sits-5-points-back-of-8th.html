Numbers Game: Atlanta Has Won 7 Straight and Sits 5 Points Back of 8th

The Thrashers take a 7-game heater into tonight's game at Philadelphia, the club directly ahead of them in the Eastern standings

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkFriday, 13 January 2006
ShareCopy link

The piece

Atlanta Thrashers extended its winning streak to 7 games with a 5-4 decision at New York Islanders on 2006-01-11, and tonight it visits the one club five points ahead in the conference table.

The Thrashers are 14-19-3 with 43 points through 42 games, still 13th in the East. The 8th-place Philadelphia Flyers Flyers hold 48 points on 42 games. Five points, 40 games left. Atlanta plays Philadelphia for the third time this season tonight; the series is tied 1-1 with 7 goals apiece.

The recent form tells a different story than the season line. Over their last 10, Atlanta has outscored opponents 33-25. The wins came in every style: regulation, road, home, one-goal, three-goal. They are 10-10 at home and 10-12 on the road, which means the streak has not been built on friendly ice.

In the Southeast division, Atlanta sits at 6 wins and 5 regulation losses with 2 overtime defeats in 13 games. Divisional competition hasn't been the problem; the road losses against non-division clubs earlier in the schedule buried them.

Tonight's opponent, the Flyers, are 15-16-4 with 48 points and ride a 1-game win after beating Detroit Red Wings 3-2 in overtime on 2006-01-11. Philadelphia is 9-11 at home this season. Atlanta is 10-12 on the road. Neither side has a fortress in this series.

A win tonight puts Atlanta 3 points back with 39 to play. A loss keeps the gap at 5 with 39 still to go.

Atlanta has scored 33 goals in its last 10 games against a season total of 109 in 42. The recent form has outpaced the season line by a wide margin, and the next stretch against Philadelphia Flyers and the other clubs chasing the same spot will test whether the hot stretch holds.
