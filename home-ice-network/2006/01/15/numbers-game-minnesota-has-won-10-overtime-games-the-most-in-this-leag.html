Numbers Game: Minnesota Has Won 10 Overtime Games, the Most in This League

A plus-2 goal differential, 11 regulation wins, and 10 two-point nights in extra time. That is how the Wild sit 7th in the West, one point ahead of the cut.

Ray CallumSenior correspondent · Home Ice NetworkSunday, 15 January 2006
ShareCopy link

Minnesota Wild beat Buffalo Sabres 3-2 in overtime on 2006-01-14, their third win in a row. It was Minnesota's 10th overtime victory this season, the most of any club in the league. Buffalo is second at 9. No other team has reached double digits.

The Wild are 11-16-8 with 50 points in 45 games. That places them 7th in the Western Conference, one point ahead of Calgary Flames and the 8th-place line. Their goal differential is plus-2: 125 scored, 123 allowed. A team that scores 125 and allows 123 is not supposed to sit inside the top eight of its conference. The overtime column explains it.

Manny Fernandez95 has started 34 of 45 games, 76 percent of Minnesota's schedule. The Wild are 10-8 in overtime this season, and Fernandez is the reason they keep getting the chance.

Two men sit on the injury list. Pierre-Marc Bouchard81, a center graded 81 by the Bureau, is out until 2006-01-24 with an abdomen injury. Andrei Zyuzin77, a defenseman at 77, is out until 2006-01-18 with a neck injury. Minnesota has logged 31 injury spells and 119 days lost this season, among the lighter totals in the league.

The schedule tilts hard. Vancouver Canucks comes to town on 2006-01-16. Vancouver sits 6th, 3 points ahead of the Wild, and has beaten Minnesota 3 times in 3 meetings this season, outscoring them 9-5. A fourth loss to the same opponent would put Vancouver 5 points clear with 37 games left for both clubs.

Minnesota Wild hosts Mighty Ducks of Anaheim two nights later. Minnesota has 37 games remaining in the regular season.

The conference table does not care about the shape of a win. Two points is two points, whether a team wins 5-1 or survives 3-2 with four minutes left in the third. Minnesota has collected 10 of those in 45 tries, and it is the only reason a plus-2 differential reads like contention.
