At 22, Past the Book's Number and on $1.10M: Ilya Kovalchuk Is the Thrashers' Unsolved Problem

Potential 85. Overall 86. The first Russian ever drafted first overall has passed the projection the Bureau gave him, and the cheapest payroll in hockey has six months to find a way to keep him.

Carol BinghamFeatures writer · RinksideThursday, 12 January 2006
ShareCopy link

The piece

The Bureau gave Ilya Kovalchuk89 Potential 85 in the Book. He is 22 years old and his overall reads 86 today. He has passed the number they projected for him, and he passed it on $1.10M.

His career runs from Tver, Russia, through a shoulder that ended his rookie season, to a co-Maurice Richard Trophy, to a Lady Byng he won with 18 penalty minutes in 79 games, to a free-agent summer that the cheapest payroll in this league cannot solve.

The number that stopped him

The Development Index has him up 5. His overall of 86 is the result, and the potential of 85 sits one point below it. In the Book's language, the likelihood of development has run its course. The grade went up past the projection and the projection did not move.

Kovalchuk played 79 games last season and scored 138 points, second to Jaromir Jagr84 in the Art Ross. He shared the Richard Trophy with Jarome Iginla92 and Rick Nash85, each with 41 goals. He won the Lady Byng. He was 21 years old.

This season he is at 53 points in 41 games, a pace of 106 over 82. The Index lifted him 5 above his base, which means the Bureau's form term sees the season as better than the grade, even as the potential says there is no room left. He is already above the projection they drew at eighteen years old, when he was a kid in Tver who wore 17 for Valeri Kharlamov.

The contract

$1.10M. Zero years left. Free agency closes on 2006-07-28, 197 days from today. Atlanta Thrashers hold a 22-year-old who scored 138 points last year and is already past his projected ceiling, on a contract that costs less than the league's second-cheapest goaltender earns.

Atlanta's payroll sits at $40.44M, the lowest in the league. Revenue is $50.19M. Profit is $22.64M. They are not broke, they are just small. If they want to pay a man franchise money, the market rate for a top winger with years left is between $6.00M and $11.40M. Daniel Alfredsson93 makes $11.40M at 33. Paul Kariya89 makes $10.11M at 31. Peter Forsberg95 makes $9.90M at 32. Those men are declining or plateaued. Kovalchuk is 22 and his Index is climbing.

A contract at $8.00M or $9.00M would put him above one-fifth of the current Thrashers payroll. At $10.00M, nearly a quarter. The math is not impossible but it is not comfortable in a building that draws 14,395 a night.

The room around him

Dany Heatley92 is on the same $1.10M, same contract-year class, 24 years old, 51 points through 41 games, pace of 102. He was the second pick in 2000. Kovalchuk was the first pick in 2001. They are the two highest-drafted players on the roster and the two cheapest by actual money.

Marc Savard, at 30 and 41 points, makes $1.00M. Sami Kapanen80, 27 points in 35 games, makes $3.60M. The second-highest salary on the roster goes to a man with half Kovalchuk's production.

Atlanta's last ten games read WWWWWWLWLOTL, newest first. They have won six in a row and are at 41 standings points through 41 games. The team is turning. The question that turns with it is whether the turn is enough to make a 22-year-old who just passed his projection want to stay where the projection is still the same one.

The road

Tver to Atlanta is a long way for a man who played two seasons in the Vysshaya Liga before anyone in North America drafted him. He came over, broke his shoulder in his rookie year, missed 17 games and came back to score 63 goals. The Book saw something then, a player who could reach 85. It underestimated how fast he would get there and what the year would look like once he did: the Index 5 above his base, the potential still one point below his grade, the ceiling behind him at 22.

He is free in the summer. The cheapest club in hockey has six months to convince him that the room he built around them is the right room to finish in.
