The Gate: Phoenix books $12.34M profit on the league's smallest crowd

Dan Knights runs the 26th-ranked front office in a 30-club league and the books still balance

Ruth HalloranBusiness of hockey · The BuildingWednesday, 16 February 2005

Phoenix Coyotes draws 11,987 fans a night, the lowest average in the league, and turns $12.34M profit on a $47.81M payroll. They charge $80 a seat. Nobody in the building is pretending otherwise.

What makes a club a going concern here is the gap between payroll and revenue. Phoenix's revenue is $52.53M. Their payroll is $47.81M. The margin between them is $4.72M. That is thin but not negative.

The comparison table shows what the bigger gates buy:

Phoenix draws 3,699 fewer fans than Detroit, spends $33.08M less on payroll, and books $12.34M against Detroit's $2.30M. They draw 2,800 fewer than New York, spend $29.68M less, and book $12.34M against New York's $0.67M. The per-point line says the same thing: Phoenix pays $0.89M for every point they collect. Detroit pays $1.24M. New York pays $1.16M.

The building does not explain the gap

Phoenix's live sum sits at -16. Equipment level 2, workout level 2, practice level 1, travel level 0. The travel track is the deadest thing in the building and the Coyotes are 12-17 on the road. Their on-ice facilities reading is -0.85, which is less penalized than most because the practice penalty is halved on the overall and their equipment is not at the floor.

The building is middle of the road and the road is bad. That costs points. It does not cost money.

What Dan Knights runs

Knights sits 26th in the league's prestige standing at 330, up 2 since the last edition. The Coyotes are 22-26-6, 54 points, on a one-game winning streak. No facility upgrade has been filed at Phoenix since the record opened on December 1. No ticket change is on the record either.

The ticket is $80. Toronto charges $97. Buffalo charges $76. Phoenix is in the top third by price and at the bottom by volume. The market does not want more Coyotes at a discount. The market does not want more Coyotes at full price.

Knights has not spent to fill the building and the building does not need filling to clear money. The club works at 11,987 because the payroll is low enough that 11,987 is enough. That is what the smallest gate in the league looks like from the business side.
