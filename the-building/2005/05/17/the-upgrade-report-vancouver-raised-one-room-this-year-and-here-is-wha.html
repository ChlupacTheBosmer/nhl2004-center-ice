The Upgrade Report: Vancouver raised one room this year, and here is what moved

The lowest payroll in the league clears $40.84M in profit. The workout room is at zero. The travel budget is at zero. The one upgrade went to a room nothing reads.

Ruth HalloranBusiness of hockey · The BuildingTuesday, 17 May 2005

Vancouver Canucks moved one facility this season. Between 2004-12-08 and 2004-12-11, the scouting room went up one level. The record holds no other upgrade for this club.

One room, the one nothing reads

The scouting room carries a number in the league's tuning file. Nothing in this build reads it. The track feeds trade valuation, a figure nobody outside a club can see. The one thing Vancouver Canucks built this season, the reader cannot observe.

The tuning file reads -1 at every scouting level from 1 to 5. The upgrade moved the level number. The modifier stayed put.

The reader can see the ledger. $35.16M on the payroll, the lowest of any club. 99 points. $36M per point, the best in the league. $40.84M in profit. 14,832 a night at $78 a seat.

Vancouver Canucks runs the cheapest roster in this league and gets more per dollar than anyone else.

Two rooms at the floor, and the road that follows

The workout room sits at level 0. The tuning file puts -5 on that. The travel budget sits at level 0. The tuning file puts -6. Equipment is at 2 (-3) and the practice rink at 2 (-2). The live sum, the four tracks that actually move a player's rating, comes to -16. The facilities term averaged over Vancouver Canucks's own players reads -1.81.

Toronto Maple Leafs runs the best live sum in the league at -5. Los Angeles Kings runs the worst at -19. Vancouver Canucks sits closer to the floor.

The travel budget applies on the road. Vancouver Canucks went 28-13 at home, where the practice rink at level 2 carries a -2. They went 18-23 on the road, where the travel penalty at level 0 carries a -6.

The travel penalty is the same for all eight; the rosters are not. Tampa Bay Lightning spends $48.50M on players. Montreal Canadiens spends $45.26M. Minnesota Wild spends $37.91M.

60 spells, and a medical room that moves nothing

The injury ledger gives Vancouver Canucks 60 designated spells and 456 days lost. The medical room is one of four tracks switched off in this build. No upgrade to a medical room would change a return date.

Vancouver Canucks lost the first round to Edmonton Oilers in 6 games. Edmonton Oilers carries a $36.81M payroll and the same live sum of -16. The series ended 4-2 to the lower seed.

Emmanuel Yau sits 13th in the league's prestige standing, 493. His one move since the record opened was the scouting room, in the second week of December. The draft is 22 days away. The workout room and the travel budget are at their floor, both live tracks, both showing a negative modifier every night for every man on the roster.
