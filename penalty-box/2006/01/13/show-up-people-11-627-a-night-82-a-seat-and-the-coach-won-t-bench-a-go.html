SHOW UP, PEOPLE: 11,627 A NIGHT, $82 A SEAT, AND THE COACH WON'T BENCH A GOALLESS 31-YEAR-OLD

Phoenix draws fewer fans than any other club in this league. Tomorrow they host the second-worst team in the Pacific. Nobody is coming.

Mickey "The Mick" DonnellyColumnist, ex-goalie · The Penalty BoxFriday, 13 January 2006
ShareCopy link

11,627. That's how many butts fill the seats in Phoenix Coyotes every night. Fewer than any other building in this league. They charge $82 to watch a 13-23-4 team whose coach puts a 31-year-old with 0 points in 8 games in the lineup.

The next-worst building holds 13,131 per night. That gap closes with wins. Phoenix can't win.

The coach's lineup card tells you why. Billy Tibbetts66 is a right winger. He's played 8 games this season with 0 points. The Bureau's Development Index has him at -4, meaning his 68 overall sits below his 72 base. He's getting worse while eating a roster spot. The coach starts him anyway. Brad Ference77 carries 90 penalty minutes in 44 games, 2.05 a night. I stopped rubber for a living and I can tell you what it does to a goaltender to watch his own defenseman spend 2 minutes in the box every other shift. It gets you scored on. Every time.

The payroll math is $53.91M for 38 points. That's $1.42M per point. Revenue sits at $47.25M, the lowest in this league. You cannot build a winning club when your gate is the smallest in hockey and your product costs more per point than almost anyone else's. The front office bought a roster that doesn't produce enough to fill a building and then charged $82 for the privilege of watching it fail.

Tomorrow they host San Jose Sharks. San Jose is 13-22-8, the other 13-win team in the Pacific. Two broken clubs, the worst building in this league, $82 a seat. San Jose is 11-13 on the road and 3-17 at home, so at least they'll play better without their own fans watching. Phoenix is 11-10 at home and 6-17 away. The one environment where they're adequate is the one building that sits half empty.

The room isn't sad. It's just bad.

The Morale Scale puts Phoenix at 94.3, dead middle of this league. Morale is fine. These guys are just not good enough and the coach keeps dressing the worst of them. Tibbetts. Ference. Patrice Brisebois77 at 77 skating the third pair ahead of Radoslav Suchy74 at 74, because the coach's eye test says what the Bureau's grade told him in October.

Phoenix's road record is 6-17. That's the worst mark in the Pacific. They can't win without the building. The building doesn't want to be there. You can't blame the people who showed up. You can blame the coach who benches a 74 to dress a 68 with zero points and then tells you the process is working.

$82 buys you a seat in a building that's the worst in this league, watching a team that's the second-worst in its division, coached by a man who won't make the obvious call. Stay home. Save your money. Watch a better team on TV.
