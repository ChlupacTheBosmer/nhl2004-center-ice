$1.40 MILLION FOR 10.6 MINUTES A NIGHT: COLUMBUS HAS THE SADDEST ROOM IN HOCKEY AND THE HOT STOVE ALREADY TOLD YOU NOTHING WAS COMING

85.5 club morale. Three men at the absolute floor. Espen Knutsen collects $1.40M to play less than your fourth-line winger.

Mickey "The Mick" DonnellyColumnist, ex-goalie · The Penalty BoxWednesday, 9 February 2005
ShareCopy link

The Bureau's Morale Scale puts Columbus Blue Jackets at 85.5. Dead last among 30 rooms. Not close. Not one bad apple dragging the average. Espen Knutsen77, Tyler Wright76, and Marc Denis86 all sit at 72, the lowest individual morale number in the entire league.

Knutsen has played 22 games at 10.6 a night. He makes $1.40M. That is what Columbus pays a center to dress and then sit. Wright has played 53 games at 10.8 a night for $0.80M. Denis plays 65.1 minutes when he suits up, 21 games, $1.00M, 82 overall. The man can play and he hates it here.

The Hot Stove called this on January 10. The claim was that at least one of Denis, Wright, or Jaroslav Spacek would be off the roster by February 1. February 1 came and went. All three are still on the books. The room sits at 85.5. Nothing moved. You want to know what a locker room at 85.5 looks like? It looks like three grown men at the floor of the Morale Scale, playing 10.6, 10.8, and 65.1 minutes a night, collecting money to be miserable in a building where nobody is fixing anything.

The one-engine team

Columbus is 19-24-3 with 55 points. Geoff Sanderson85 has 93 points at 21.7 minutes a night, a 144 pace that would put him 6th in the league if he kept it up. Nobody else on the roster appears in the top 30 scorers. The other three at 72 morale watch from the bench and count paychecks. That is what 85.5 looks like on a Tuesday night.

Their last 10 is 4-5-1. Win, win, overtime loss, loss, loss, loss, win, win, loss, loss. A coin flip that keeps landing tails when it matters.

You are paying $81 for this

The ticket is $81. Only Toronto charges more. Attendance is 13,936 and the team is 14-13 at home, 12-14 on the road. The revenue is $53.75M against a $49.21M payroll, a $14.53M profit. The owner is comfortable. The room is at 85.5. The coach dresses a man for $1.40M to play 10.6 minutes. The Hot Stove said something would happen by February 1 and it didn't.

I will say what the Hot Stove should have said: nothing moves in Columbus because nobody in charge is losing money. You are. You are paying $81 to watch one good team player and three sad ones and a franchise that cannot figure out which one to sell.

The season is half over. The building has to figure it out before the seats go empty for good.
