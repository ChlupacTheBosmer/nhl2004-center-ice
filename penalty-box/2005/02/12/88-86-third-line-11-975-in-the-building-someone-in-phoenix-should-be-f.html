88. 86. THIRD LINE. 11,975 IN THE BUILDING. SOMEONE IN PHOENIX SHOULD BE FIRED.

The Coyotes have the two most wasted players in hockey, the emptiest arena in the league, and a coach who won 4 straight without asking his best men to carry anything.

Mickey "The Mick" DonnellyColumnist, ex-goalie · The Penalty BoxSaturday, 12 February 2005
ShareCopy link

Mike Sillinger88 is rated 88. He plays third-line right wing for the Phoenix Coyotes. No other mis-slotted player in this league is within 6 points of that number.

Ladislav Nagy86 is rated 86. He centers that third line. The next-worst mis-slot in hockey belongs to Aleksey Morozov75 of Pittsburgh Penguins, rated 80.

The men playing top-six at the same positions carry 81 (Nikolai Antropov81, left wing) and 78 (Jan Hrdina78, center, and Robert Reichel78, center). The coach looks at an 88 and decides a 78 can center the first line. The coach looks at an 86 and decides an 81 can wing the first line. This is not a style choice. This is a coach who has decided he knows better than a man rated twelve points above him.

And the lineup card is only half the crime.

$2.10M FOR 8.4 MINUTES

Daymond Langkow79 is a 79-rated center. He plays 39 games at 8.4 minutes a night. He collects $2.10M for the privilege. His morale reads 74, ninth-lowest in hockey. The Coyotes' club average is 90.6, ninth-worst in the league. A team whose room sits ninth-worst is a team whose best men know they are not trusted.

THE BUILDING KNOWS

Attendance at the Arrowhead Pond of Glendale is 11,975 per game. The next-emptiest building in this league belongs to New York Islanders at 13,001. The ticket is $80.

You pay $80 to sit in the coldest building in hockey and watch a coach bury an 88 and an 86 on the fourth unit while two 78s carry the top six. The Coyotes turn a $12.46M profit on a $47.81M payroll. That is not efficiency. That is a man who has decided the building stays empty and the profit stays fat and neither the customers nor the players get anything back.

THE STREAK THAT CHANGES NOTHING

The Coyotes have won 4 straight. Their last 10 reads LWLWOTLLWWWW. So the coach points at the streak and tells you it is working. Four wins with an 88 playing third-line right wing is not a system. It is a hot goaltender and a lucky bounce.

Bill Guerin89 scored 105 points at 34 for Dallas Stars. Markus Naslund85 has 104 for Vancouver Canucks on the third-cheapest payroll in the league. Those men play where they belong and get what they earn. Sillinger and Nagy play where the coach hides them and collect minutes you could fill with a healthy scratch.

The streak will end. It always does. When it does, somebody in that building should look at the lineup card before they look at anything else.
