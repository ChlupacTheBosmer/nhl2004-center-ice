42 POINTS, 87.4 MORALE, AND A $4.40 MILLION GOALIE AT THE BOTTOM OF THE ROOM

Calgary is last in hockey, the locker room is the second-saddest in the league, and the owner is $15.80 million ahead and has no reason to spend.

Mickey "The Mick" DonnellyColumnist, ex-goalie · The Penalty BoxSunday, 6 February 2005
ShareCopy link

Calgary is 16 and 29 and 6. Forty-two points. Last in a 30-club league, 8 points behind Minnesota, and the Calgary Flames locker room sits at 87.4 on the Bureau's Morale Scale, second-lowest in hockey behind only Columbus. They have 9 wins and 19 losses at home. You pay $77 for that, and 13,106 people do it every night.

The goalie at the bottom of the Morale Scale makes $4.40 million

Roman Turek83 is 72 morale, tied for the floor of the Bureau's basement list with three Columbus players and Travis Green77. He is 86 overall, 54.3 minutes a night, and signed for 3 more years at $4.40M. Calgary's top goaltender is at the absolute bottom of the league's satisfaction scale and you can watch him play it every other night. Green, a 79-overall center on $2.97M, also sits at 72 and plays 10.9 minutes a night in 36 games. The coach sat him as much as he played him. The front office gave him three more years at $2.97M. The room got sadder.

Iginla has 77 points and nobody else has an answer

Jarome Iginla92 has 77 points in 47 games at a 134-point pace, 91 overall, 21.2 minutes a night. He won the Richard Trophy last season. He is the only Calgary Flames player a season-ticket holder could name and feel warm about, and the team is 16 wins deep.

Calgary went 0 and 3 and 1 against Colorado Avalanche. They went 1 and 3 and 0 against Vancouver Canucks. They cannot beat the top of the Northwest and they scrape wins off Minnesota Wild and Edmonton Oilers to pad a standing that is still the worst in hockey.

The owner is $15.80 million ahead and the building is two-thirds full

Calgary Flames banked $51.11M in revenue against a $43.33M payroll. The cheapest roster in the Northwest and still $15.80M profit on the year. The Bureau's Morale Scale has the room at 87.4 and nobody upstairs is spending a dime to fix it. Why would they? The money comes in.

Steve Staios70 has 60 penalty minutes. That is the second-most-notable line on Calgary Flames's roster. Iginla, one goaltender at the floor of the Morale Scale on a $4.40M deal with 3 years left, and a fourth-line center collecting $2.97M to hate every shift of a 10.9-minute night. That is not a hockey team. That is a business plan with skates on.
