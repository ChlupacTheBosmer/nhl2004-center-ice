THE MAN WITH 85 POINTS PLAYS FOR THE 2ND SADDEST ROOM IN HOCKEY AND HIS OWNER BANKED $26.92 MILLION

Daniel Alfredsson does 21.7 minutes a night. Bryan Smolinski collects $2.40M for 11.3 minutes and hates every shift of it. Nobody in Ottawa has to fix a thing.

Mickey "The Mick" DonnellyColumnist, ex-goalie · The Penalty BoxMonday, 21 February 2005
ShareCopy link

Daniel Alfredsson90 has 85 points in 61 games. His locker room is at 85.8 morale, second-lowest in the league. His club made $26.92 million last season and has no reason on earth to spend it on making him better off.

Alfredsson is 32 years old and plays 21.7 minutes a night. 46 goals, 39 assists. The 133rd pick of the 1994 draft, 5th round, and he has clawed everything this franchise has ever given him out of nothing. He is the only Ottawa Senators name on the top 30 scorers. The men around him are not helping.

Bryan Smolinski78 sits at 74 morale, one of the 15 lowest individual numbers in hockey. 11.3 minutes a night, $2.40 million for that privilege. The Bureau's Book grades him 74 overall, and at 11 minutes a night and $2.40 million, nobody's arguing with it. He's taken 61 games of this. The coach puts him out for a shift-and-a-half and the man goes back to a room sitting at 85.8.

Peter Schaefer76 sits at 74 morale too, 11.3 mpg, $0.60 million. Same minutes, same mood, a quarter of the salary. Smolinski's number is four times Schaefer's and the ice time is identical. The coach has looked at both and found nothing that justifies the gap.

The business is the story. Revenue: $66.31 million. Payroll: $42.80 million. Profit: $26.92 million. That money does not go to the roster. It goes to the owner. The 14,898 people in the building pay $80 a head to watch a team that is 20 and 28 and 8, outscored 28 to 24 over its last 10 games, 14 and 14 at home, 11 and 22 on the road.

Compare Toronto Maple Leafs. The Maple Leafs sit at 97.1 morale, the happiest room in the league. They have won the Stanley Cup. They draw 17,681 at $97 a ticket. Their profit is $24.90 million. They chose hockey and the money followed. Ottawa chose the ledger and the rot followed.

The last 10 results read OTLWWLOTLLLWLOTL. Seven games below .500 in the most recent stretch, including a 4-game skid in the middle. Alfredsson plays 21.7 minutes of that. Smolinski plays 11.3 and sits at 74 morale on $2.40 million. The 14,898 keep coming. The $26.92 million keeps arriving. Nothing changes because nothing has to.
