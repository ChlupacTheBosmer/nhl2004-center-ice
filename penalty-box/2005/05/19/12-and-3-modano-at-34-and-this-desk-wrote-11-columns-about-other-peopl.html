12 AND 3, MODANO AT 34, AND THIS DESK WROTE 11 COLUMNS ABOUT OTHER PEOPLE

The Presidents' Trophy team swept Detroit, buried Phoenix, and pushed Anaheim to three overtimes. Nobody at this outlet noticed until it was over.

Mickey "The Mick" DonnellyColumnist, ex-goalie · The Penalty BoxThursday, 19 May 2005
ShareCopy link

Dallas beat Anaheim 4 games to 1 and is in the Stanley Cup Final. The Stars are 12-3 this spring with 44 goals for and 24 against. I wrote about everyone else.

The Dallas Stars Stars won the Presidents' Trophy last season, reached the conference final, and came home. The desk noticed. The desk noticed Washington Capitals, Toronto Maple Leafs, Pittsburgh Penguins, Phoenix Coyotes, every club with a narrative. Nobody sat down and wrote about the 42-win team from the Pacific that swept the most expensive roster in hockey 4-0, beat the 8th seed 4-2, and then put 5 goals on Anaheim in Game 3 and won Game 5 in overtime on the road.

Mike Modano84 is 34. He put up 125 points in 78 games this regular season. Bill Guerin played 61 games and scored 108, a pace of 145 over a full 82. The room sits at 92.7 on the Bureau's morale scale. Nobody in that building is crying, nobody is demanding a trade, and nobody has been writing about them either.

The business of winning quietly

$67.99M payroll against $97.56M in revenue. The profit line reads $22.87M. The per-point cost is $0.62M, which is neither the cheapest in hockey nor the most wasteful. They are paying for production and getting it. Dave Tippett won the Jack Adams last spring and his club is one series from a Cup.

The attendance sits at 16,067 a night, $80 a ticket. That is a full building paying a fair price and getting a Presidents' Trophy club that swept three opponents on its way to the Final. I hate writing a sentence that sounds like a press release. That one is true anyway.

The column I owe

This desk has written more pieces with Toronto's name in the lede than with any other club this spring. Washington comes next. Anaheim, Detroit, Phoenix, New Jersey, Tampa Bay, Nashville, Florida, all of them got columns because the desk chased drama. Dallas was quietly 12-3. Three of five games against Anaheim went to overtime. They won two of those on the road. That is not a team that needs a column about its feelings. That is a team that needs a column about what it is doing.

Roberto Luongo97 will be in the other crease. He has a 1.98 GAA and a .942 save percentage this spring. The Toronto Maple Leafs room is at 98.6 morale, the highest in the league. Dallas Stars sits at 92.7. The profit lines say $46.70M for Toronto and $22.87M for Dallas. Both clubs have money. Both clubs have rosters that look built for June.

The regular season record against Anaheim was 7-1-2. The Stars beat a good team and then did it to three more. The Final is set. I have been wrong for two weeks about who deserved the attention, and Modano at 34 does not owe me an apology for that.
