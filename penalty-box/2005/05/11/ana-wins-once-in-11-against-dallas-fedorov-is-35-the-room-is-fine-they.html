ANA WINS ONCE IN 11 AGAINST DALLAS. FEDOROV IS 35. THE ROOM IS FINE. THEY'RE GOING HOME.

The cheapest roster in the playoffs has 16 points from its aging center and four goals in three games. Game 4 is tomorrow.

Mickey "The Mick" DonnellyColumnist, ex-goalie · The Penalty BoxWednesday, 11 May 2005
ShareCopy link

Mighty Ducks of Anaheim is the cheapest club left in these playoffs at $41.01 million. They got here on 15 overtime losses, the most in the league, and the arm of a 35-year-old. Dallas Stars has outscored them 12-4 in three conference final games and Game 4 is tomorrow at Anaheim.

Across 11 meetings this season, Anaheim has won once. The season goals: 23 for, 41 against. This has been the same since October.

Sergei Fedorov90 has 11 goals and 5 assists in 15 playoff games. Vaclav Prospal79 sits second at 4+9=13. Andy McDonald74 has 9 in 12. Nobody else on the Mighty Ducks of Anaheim roster carries a point total above 7. The Dallas Stars side has Mike Modano86 at 9+9=18, Brenden Morrow84 at 3+13=16, Sergei Zubov82 at 5+8=13, and Jere Lehtinen84 at 5+8=13. Four Dallas players have more playoff points than anyone except Fedorov and Prospal.

Jean-Sebastien Giguere97 sits at .872 through 13 postseason decisions. Ilja Bryzgalov79 is at .852 in 2 games. Marty Turco95 in the Dallas Stars crease has a 1.49 GAA across 11 wins and 2 losses. The goaltending gap is the series. Giguere is a good starter; Turco is a wall with a Presidents' Trophy on the mantel.

The room is fine. At 91.4 club morale, Mighty Ducks of Anaheim sits 13th in hockey. Nobody in that building is throwing helmets. The profit sheet says $43.60M, the third-highest in the league. The owner made more this season than the roster costs. That is a model. It does not win Cups.

Fedorov is 35. He scored 71 goals in the regular season, 25 more than anyone else on this roster. He carries 16 playoff points and a 0-3 deficit. Tomorrow Dallas Stars closes this out or the conference final gets ugly. I've been wrong 21 times this year on predictions, but I've never watched a 0-3 series come back when the outscored club scored 4 goals in 3 games.

I predict Dallas Stars wins Game 4 and reaches the Cup Final.
