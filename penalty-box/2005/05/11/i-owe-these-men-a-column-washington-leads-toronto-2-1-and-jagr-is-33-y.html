I OWE THESE MEN A COLUMN: WASHINGTON LEADS TORONTO 2-1 AND JAGR IS 33 YEARS OLD

Four Capitals with double-digit playoff points. Kolzig at 1.48. Game 4 is tomorrow in Washington's building.

Mickey "The Mick" DonnellyColumnist, ex-goalie · The Penalty BoxWednesday, 11 May 2005
ShareCopy link

On April 12 I wrote that Washington Capitals was the best team nobody covered and that I owed them a column. Game 4 of the conference final is tomorrow and they lead the best record in hockey two games to one. Jaromir Jagr90 has 4 goals and 14 assists in 11 games at 33 years old.

Washington Capitals's payroll sits at $55.92M against Toronto Maple Leafs's $60.04M. The Capitals came in as the fourth seed with 104 points. Toronto won 133, with 381 goals for and 190 against, the best differential in hockey. The number-one seed has a .937 save rating from Roberto Luongo97 and a 2.17 GAA through 15 playoff games.

None of that matters tonight. What matters is that Washington has four players with double-digit playoff points: Jagr at 18, Pascal Dupuis79 at 14, Robert Lang at 13, and Sergei Gonchar85 at 13. Toronto Maple Leafs's leader is Gary Roberts87 at 4+9=13 in 15, a defenseman. Mats Sundin92 has 12 in 15. Their third man is Alexander Mogilny89 at 9 in 15.

Olaf Kolzig91 has a 1.48 GAA across 11 games and a .937 save rating. His only loss came in an overtime game. Josh Harding83, 20 years old and sitting +5 on the Development Index, is 2-0 with a 1.00 GAA in 2 appearances. Between them the Capitals have allowed one goal a night in 11 postseason games. He won the Vezina last spring. It wasn't a one-year thing.

The room tells the rest. Washington sits at 96.8 club morale, second in hockey behind Toronto's 98.6. Toronto's room is the fullest because they won 59 games. Washington's is nearly as full because they believe what they have. Jagr at 33 with 18 points in 11 games. Kolzig with a 1.48. Dupuis with 8 goals in 11 games.

Game 4 is in Washington's building. The Capitals went 19-22 at home all season, a record that made no sense in the regular season and makes less sense now. They swept Carolina Hurricanes 4-0 and swept Tampa Bay Lightning 4-0. At home across those two rounds they went 4-0, scoring 13 and allowing 5.

Toronto Maple Leafs won Game 3 in overtime at Washington to make it 2-1 instead of a sweep. The Leafs are 31-10 on the road all season and believe that number is enough to close this out in Washington's building. I don't believe it. I believe Jagr believes it.

I predict Washington wins Game 4 and reaches the Cup Final. I've been wrong 21 times this year. I keep coming back to a 33-year-old with 18 playoff points and a goaltender who allows one a night.
