138. 131. 86.6. ATLANTA'S ROOM IS BROKEN AND THE COACH OWNS IT

Two men scored 138 and 131 points. The team won 31 games. The locker room sits second-worst in the league and nobody in that building seems surprised.

Mickey "The Mick" DonnellyColumnist, ex-goalie · The Penalty BoxWednesday, 25 May 2005
ShareCopy link

Ilya Kovalchuk86 put up 138 points at 22 years old. Dany Heatley85 scored 131 at 24. Those are the second and fourth best seasons in the league, ahead of Peter Forsberg95, ahead of Jarome Iginla86, ahead of everyone except Jagr and Naslund and Todd Bertuzzi89. Atlanta Thrashers went 31-37-6 and went home in April.

The 8th seed in the East had 84 points. Atlanta had 84 points. One of those tickets was won on a tiebreak nobody outside that building could explain, and the other one was a beach vacation.

The club average on the Bureau's Morale Scale is 86.6. Only Edmonton sits lower at 86.3. Boston lost in the first round and scored higher. Florida lost 11 straight to close the season and scored higher. Atlanta, with two men who outscored entire teams between them, is second from the bottom in how the players feel about the room they share.

Byron Dafoe79 played 68 games at 57.5 minutes a night, the man in the net, and he sits at 72 morale making $2.40M. Magnus Arvedson74 played all 82 games at 11.6 minutes a night, the checking winger who kills penalties, and he sits at 74 making $2.49M. The coach leaned on those two. The coach lost them.

Kovalchuk is 22. He scored 63 goals and he does not need a pep talk. Heatley is 24 and he scored 65. They will sign extensions and go to dinner after games and be fine. The 33-year-old backup who played 68 games because nobody else could does not get a dinner. He gets to sit in a room where the checking winger is making $2.49M to play 11.6 minutes a night and the coach has drawn the same neutral-zone trap for four straight years and nobody has won a playoff series.

The money is there. The will is not.

Atlanta spent $36.79M and earned $70.29M in revenue. The profit line reads $25.99M. That is more money than Pittsburgh took home, more than Boston, more than Buffalo. The building holds 14,430 people per game at $78 a ticket. The gate is fine. The owner is not losing sleep. There is no excuse.

You cannot draft culture. The draft is 14 days away and Atlanta will pick high and they will add another scorer to a team that already has two of the top four. The 138 and the 131 did not carry this club to 84 points because the other 78 men in the building could not keep up, and the other 78 men could not keep up because the room at 86.6 drags everyone down.

Doug Reimer will come at this with a Development Index. Fine. What I need is one name on the bench who will look at Dafoe at 72 and Arvedson at 74 and say something. The coach did not say something. The coach watched the room rot while two 20-somethings scored on everybody and lost 7 of their last 10, counting overtimes, and sat at 84 points and called it a season.

The coach lost the room. The room lost the season.
