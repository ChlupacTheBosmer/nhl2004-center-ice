ONE LOSS FROM THE BEACH: $67.99M, 2-5 all year, and Dallas plays for its life tomorrow

They got here four games fresher than the other side. It hasn't mattered. Game 7 is at Dallas tomorrow night.

Mickey "The Mick" DonnellyColumnist, ex-goalie · The Penalty BoxTuesday, 31 May 2005
ShareCopy link

Mike Modano84 is 34 years old. He has 24 points in 21 playoff games. He carried Dallas Stars through three rounds on the freshest legs in the conference and now trails this series 4-2, one loss from a vacation he did not plan.

Dallas Stars played 21 playoff games to Toronto Maple Leafs's 25. Four fewer nights of hockey. A rest advantage that should have meant speed in the sixth week of the postseason. Instead Toronto has outscored Dallas 14-8 in this series and 34-19 across every meeting this season.

The front office spent $67.99M. They won the Presidents' Trophy, seeded second in the West at 109 points, and posted a $0.62M cost per point. Toronto spent $60.04M at $0.45M per point. Dallas spent more money, lost the regular season series 2-5, and now stands one loss from the beach with a $28.10M profit sitting in the bank.

The goaltending is not your problem

Marty Turco93 has a 1.65 GAA across 14 wins and 7 losses this spring. Roberto Luongo97 has a 1.81 GAA across 16 and 9, with a .943 save percentage to Turco's .921. Both numbers say the net is covered. Neither man has lost this series.

What lost it: January. February. The 7 regular-season meetings where Dallas scored 11 goals and allowed 20. That is not a roster built to beat this Toronto team. You knew it before the trade deadline came and went with nothing to show.

16,067 tickets, one more night?

The building fills at 16,067 per night. The ticket is $80. $102.84M in revenue, 30-11 at home all year, a morale score of 92.7 that nobody will lose sleep over. The room is comfortable. Toronto Maple Leafs's room sits at 98.6 and those men believe something the Dallas locker room has only half-decided.

Tomorrow night, Marty Turco faces Roberto Luongo for the 13th time this season. The season series is 9-3 Toronto, 34-19 on goals. Modano has 24 points this spring and a birthday he'd rather not spend on a beach.

One loss and it's over. Three rounds of efficient hockey, a rested roster, the Presidents' Trophy on the mantle, and $67.99M to show for a Game 7 that starts with them behind.
