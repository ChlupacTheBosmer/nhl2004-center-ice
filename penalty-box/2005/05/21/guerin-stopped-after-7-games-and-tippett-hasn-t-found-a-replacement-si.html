GUERIN STOPPED AFTER 7 GAMES AND TIPPETT HASN'T FOUND A REPLACEMENT SINCE

Dallas was shut out in Game 1 of the Cup Final. Their second-leading scorer has 13 points in 7 playoff games and hasn't played a shift since the second round.

Mickey "The Mick" DonnellyColumnist, ex-goalie · The Penalty BoxSaturday, 21 May 2005
ShareCopy link

The Dallas Stars offense went quiet in Game 1 of the Stanley Cup Final. 2-0, at home, no goals, no answers. Roberto Luongo94 covered the puck like a man putting his kids to bed, and the Dallas scoresheet told you who wasn't there.

Bill Guerin87 scored 108 points in 61 regular season games, a pace of 145, at 34 years old. He added 13 in his 7 playoff appearances. Dallas has played 16 playoff games. Guerin played 7 of them. He stopped after game 3 of the second round and has not been back. Dave Tippett has not replaced him. Game 2 is tomorrow in the same building.

The top end stops at three

Mike Modano88 is doing everything he can. He has 20 points in 16 playoff games, the most by any player in this Final. Brenden Morrow84 has 18. Sergei Zubov83 has 15. Three men are producing and the fourth stopped showing up nine games ago.

Toronto Maple Leafs has four men with double-digit playoff scoring and none of them stopped showing up. Marian Gaborik has 10 in 8, and he is their fourth-best offensive player this spring. Sundin, Gary Roberts, Alexander Mogilny, and then the 23-year-old who put up 99 points in 82 games during the regular season and is still not their best playoff option.

The cheaper roster is also the better roster

Tippett won the Jack Adams on a $67.99M payroll that produced 109 points, at $0.62M per point. Toronto ran 133 points on $60.04M, at $0.45M per point. The cheaper club has more bodies who can score when yours is gone.

Toronto went 2-0-0 against Dallas in the regular season, outscoring them 8-3. Then Luongo shut them out 2-0 in Game 1. Luongo carries a 1.88 GAA and a .944 save percentage this spring. Marty Turco94 has his own numbers: 1.51 GAA, .913, a 12-4 record. Both goaltenders have been outstanding. Only one of them has four players putting pucks behind the other one.

Dallas has 0 Cups and a prestige figure of 501. Toronto has 1 Cup and 647 prestige points. The Dallas Stars crowd has paid $80 a ticket to watch Modano carry a playoff run on a 34-year-old back and they deserve better than what they got on Friday. Game 2 is tomorrow. If Guerin's spot is still empty, and Modano can't find someone beside him, the hole at home becomes 0-2 in the Stanley Cup Final. Tippett drew up a Jack Adams season. Now he needs to draw up a goal.
