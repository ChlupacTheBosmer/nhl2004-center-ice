THEY CAN'T WIN TWO IN A ROW AND THEY'RE THE 6TH HAPPIEST ROOM IN HOCKEY

12 and 26 and 3. $1.45M per point. 94.4 morale. St. Louis has solved for comfort instead of wins and you pay $78 to watch it.

Mickey "The Mick" DonnellyColumnist, ex-goalie · The Penalty BoxWednesday, 26 January 2005
ShareCopy link

The last 10 games went: W, L, W, L, W, L, W, L, W, L. Five and five. Twenty-five goals for, twenty-seven against. The St. Louis Blues Blues have not managed a two-game win streak in 10 nights, and the room is the 6th happiest in hockey, and the coach owns it.

You pay $78 a seat. The building draws 13800. The payroll reads $56.63M, eighth in the league. The per-point number is $1.45M, worst in hockey. The record is 12 and 26 and 3 with 146 goals for and 192 against. They have never made the playoffs. Zero Cups. Zero playoff runs. And the profit is $13.87M because apparently people keep showing up.

A coin flip is more watchable than this

The Bureau's Morale Scale ranks 30 clubs. STL sits above 24 of them. Ahead of Detroit Red Wings, Carolina Hurricanes, Buffalo Sabres, Colorado Avalanche, Philadelphia Flyers, Columbus Blue Jackets. Ahead of every team grinding for 55 points and wondering why the room won't lift. STL's guys are at 94.4, content, warm, happy to win one, lose one, and do it again next week while you drive home in traffic wondering what you just watched.

Barret Jackman83 is +5 on the Index at 23, a defenseman with 25 points in 47 games on a roster that can't score. What is the bench doing with him? Not changing lines. Not pressing. Just letting the alternation continue like a broken slot machine.

Bryce Salvador73 has 57 penalty minutes in 47 games, 1.21 per night. The bench isn't touching the power play, isn't rotating goalies, and hasn't installed a defensive scheme worth writing home about. A defenseman hitting people is the most interesting stat line in St. Louis this season.

No STL player sits in the top 30 in scoring. No one in the top 30 wears the note. The team outscored nobody in their last 10 by more than 2 goals in either direction. GF 25, GA 27. They are a team so mediocre that the goal differential in a 10-game window is two.

The only answer is an angry one

The front office looks at $13.87M in profit, a full enough building, nobody fired, the alternation continuing, a fine room, and calls it good.

I see a 23-year-old defenseman improving by 5 points on the Index who deserves a coach wanting 26 minutes of him instead of 23. The ticket price is $78. The attendance is 13800. The per-point is $1.45M. The morale is 94.4. None of those numbers move unless somebody in that building gets angry at the right things.

Barret Jackman will be 24 in August. The alternation will still be there. So will the $78.
