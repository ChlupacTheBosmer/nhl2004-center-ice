YOU'RE PAYING $77 TO WATCH A TEAM THAT WON'T BUY A BUS

Stefaan Bullard says the travel budget is zero. The Canadiens banked $18.96 million. The road record is 6 and 20.

Mickey "The Mick" DonnellyColumnist, ex-goalie · The Penalty BoxMonday, 31 January 2005
ShareCopy link

Montreal Canadiens GM Stefaan Bullard told The Tunnel's Marcus Bell this week that the travel budget is zero. He said it twice. "Not low. Zero. The worst in the league." Then he looked at the road record, 6 wins and 20 losses, and named the budget the reason.

I believe him. I also believe the $18.96 million in profit. You cannot stand in front of a camera, on the record, and tell me this organization cannot find money for a bus while it pockets $18.96 million off a team that has won 14 games in 52. You are paying $77 a ticket to watch men whose front office will not fly them to away games. The money is in the building. Somebody upstairs decided you do not deserve a team that travels.

The Canadiens sit at $53.20 million on payroll and $60.22 million in revenue. That is $1.27 million per point. They have scored 169 goals in 52 games. They finished last in the Northeast at 42 points. The Bureau's Morale Scale puts them at 88.5, the 5th-lowest room average in hockey. The players know what the budget sheet says. You can find it in the standings.

Bullard owned it, and give him credit. "The ticket price is what it is, the money comes in, and I've got to put a product on the ice that justifies it. Right now we don't. I own that." That is one honest sentence from a man in a dishonest building.

The one thing this building got right is Mike Komisarek83, a 23-year-old defenseman the Development Index has up 6. He has 59 points, which is absurd production from a man on a 42-point team. Bullard said young players in this building "don't develop. He survives." If Komisarek's numbers are surviving, put him on a club that can afford a charter and show me what thriving looks like.

Patrice Brisebois77 is a 71 playing a 32LD slot above Stephane Quintal72 at a 68. Small potatoes next to a zero travel budget, but it is the same building making the same choices. Do not ask the coach to win on the road when the front office will not pay for the trip.

Payroll $53.20 million. Revenue $60.22 million. Profit $18.96 million. Attendance 15,563 a night at $77 a seat. The room is 5th-worst on the morale index. The bus has no money. The owner has plenty.
