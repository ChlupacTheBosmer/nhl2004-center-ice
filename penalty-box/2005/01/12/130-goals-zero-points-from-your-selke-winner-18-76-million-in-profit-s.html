130 GOALS, ZERO POINTS FROM YOUR SELKE WINNER, $18.76 MILLION IN PROFIT: SAN JOSE HAS SOLVED THE WRONG PROBLEM

The Sharks have the fewest goals in the league, the reigning Selke Trophy winner can't be found on a scoresheet, and Ron Wilson already told you why.

Mickey "The Mick" DonnellyColumnist, ex-goalie · The Penalty BoxWednesday, 12 January 2005
ShareCopy link

San Jose Sharks has scored 130 goals in 42 games. That is fewer than any other club in this league. The Phoenix Coyotes Coyotes have 132 in 43. St. Louis Blues has 132 in 41. Nobody else is under 134.

You pay $78 a ticket to watch that. The building pulls 13,195 a night and the owners cleared $18.76 million in profit. The product is the worst offense in hockey. The business is fine.

The Selke Trophy winner who isn't there

Jonathan Cheechoo73 won the Selke last season. He holds the league's award for best defensive forward. This year his name does not appear anywhere on the San Jose Sharks scoring ledger. Not in the top 30. Not in the bottom 30. The man who earned the checking trophy last June is a ghost in the lineup card this January.

Ron Wilson said the quiet part to The Tunnel's Jan Kowalczyk after the Sharks' 6-4 win over Chicago on January 2: "The honest part is we've got depth issues. You know it, I know it. You look at our third and fourth lines." He is right. Those lines are where offense goes to die.

Marleau is the whole thing

Patrick Marleau88 has 64 points in 42 games. He sits 29th in the league in scoring and carries an 86 overall. The San Jose Sharks Sharks' entire roster produced 130 goals. Marleau's 35 are the single biggest block of any single man on the roster, and the Bureau's mis-slot report shows Aaron Ward73 rated 73 on the third pair above Mike Mottau65 at 65 on the first pair. The coach is running the wrong men in the wrong spots and the offense is paying for it.

San Jose is on a three-game losing streak at 15-20-4, outscored 38 to 31 over its last 10.

The goalie is next

Wilson also said, in the same interview: "Every coach watches the mileage. I watch it every morning with my coffee. But you don't start messing with a starting goalie in January because a number is getting high. Evgeni's our guy." He's running Nabokokov into the ground because Vesa Toskala has 11 starts and the plan is to "be smarter, not to panic."

Panic is what you do when your goals-against is 148 in 42 and your offense is dead. San Jose Sharks isn't panicking. Why would it? The profit line says $18.76 million. The attendance line says 13,195. The ticket line says $78.

The room is aware. Morale sits at 89.8, tenth-worst in the league. Not a mutiny. Just the slow nod of a dressing room that knows the building would rather count money than score goals.

Doug Reimer and his spreadsheet will tell you San Jose's Development Index is fine. The Book has Marleau at 86, Nabokokov holding steady. The Index is green. The numbers are tidy.

You know what's not tidy? 130 goals in 42 games. A Selke Trophy winner with zero on the scoresheet. A coach admitting in public that his bottom six is a wasteland. A $42.78 million payroll that buys you the worst offense in hockey and still pockets $18.76 million because the people who buy $78 tickets don't look at the scoresheet either.
