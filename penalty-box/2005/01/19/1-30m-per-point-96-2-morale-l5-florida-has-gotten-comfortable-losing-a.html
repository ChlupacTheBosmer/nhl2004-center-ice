$1.30M PER POINT, 96.2 MORALE, L5: FLORIDA HAS GOTTEN COMFORTABLE LOSING AND YOU PAY $79 TO WATCH IT

The Panthers' room is the second happiest in hockey. They have scored 147 goals in 45 games. The Bureau's Morale Scale says nobody in that building cares.

Mickey "The Mick" DonnellyColumnist, ex-goalie · The Penalty BoxWednesday, 19 January 2005
ShareCopy link

The piece

The Florida Panthers Panthers sit at 96.2 on the League Scouting Bureau's Morale Scale. Only Tampa Bay Lightning is higher, and Tampa Bay is 26-12-3. Florida is 16-23-4, on a five-game losing streak, and the room does not care.

You are paying $79 a seat to watch a team that has put up 147 goals in 45 games. The payroll is $52.17M, tenth in the league. The cost per point is $1.30M. The profit is $12.80M because 15,124 people a night keep showing up to a product they cannot be proud of.

Home record: 8-14. Away: 10-13. The Panthers are worse where they are supposed to be better. Somewhere between Game 20 and Game 30, this group made peace with 40 points, and the Bureau's Morale Scale is the evidence.

Three men in free fall, one riser with nothing to show

The Bureau's fallers list carries three Panthers. Mikael Samuelsson70 has 2 points in 2 games. Jaroslav Bednar69 has 0 points in 2 games. Pavel Trnka70 has 7 points in 39 games and the Index has him three below his base.

The one Panther the Development Index has climbing is Vaclav Nedorost78. Up six on the Index. Overall 72. Twelve points in 45 games. The Bureau says his form is rising. The scoresheet says he is a fourth-line center with no production to back the grade.

No Florida player cracked the Bureau's top 30 in scoring. You do not fix a room that has made peace with losing by telling everybody to smile wider.

The comfort is the indictment

Tampa Bay sits at 97.5 on the Morale Scale. Fredrik Modin82 is playing 19.1 minutes, Vincent Lecavalier90 is at 90 points, and the Lightning have 63 standings points.

Florida sits at 96.2 with 40 points. No top-30 scorer. Three men on the fallers list. An 8-14 home record. The building prints $12.80M and nobody upstairs asks a question.

That is what comfortable losers look like. They never fire the coach. They never move a pick. The room stays happy because nobody in it is losing money. The one person losing is you, holding a $79 stub, watching a team that has scored 147 goals in 45 and feeling fine about it.

96.2 at 16 and 23. Not a team that wants to win.
