HE PICKED COLUMBUS. YOU PAY $81 TO SIT ANYWHERE ELSE.

Geoff Sanderson is third in the league at 80 points. He chose this city at 30. You won't fill the building.

Mickey "The Mick" DonnellyColumnist, ex-goalie · The Penalty BoxSaturday, 8 January 2005
ShareCopy link

The piece

Geoff Sanderson86 has 80 points in 40 games for Columbus Blue Jackets, third in the league behind Bill Guerin and Jaromir Jagr90. He is 32. He skates 22.0 minutes a night. His pace is 164 points per 82 games.

The building holds 13,778 people. They pay $81 each.

Only Toronto Maple Leafs charges more, at $97. Detroit Red Wings sits at the same $81 and draws 15,722. Columbus fields the smallest crowd in the Central Division, and you still pay Detroit prices to watch a 14-17-2 team that went out in the first round last spring.

The room works. The building doesn't.

Columbus Blue Jackets allows 132 goals against, one of the best defensive marks in hockey. The room sits at 90.3 morale, middle of the pack. The goaltender, Pascal Leclaire87, is 22, rated 88 overall, up four on the Bureau's Development Index. A franchise building something real, and the attendance line says nobody in this city knows it.

Sanderson told The Tunnel's Marcus Bell this week that he has been moved so many times he stopped feeling like a man with a home: "You get moved enough times and you stop being a guy who belongs somewhere. You're just a guy with a suitcase." Then the part that should sting every season-ticket holder in Columbus: "They asked me to be a captain. Before I'd even signed, before the ink. Somebody said, we want you here to hold this together. I've never had that. Thirty years old, twelve seasons in, and nobody had ever stood in front of me and said, you're the one."

He chose Columbus. At 30, after twelve seasons of letting other men decide where he slept, he picked a city. You pay $81 to stay away.

Jaroslav Spacek73 is the dark mark. He's a 74-rated defenseman on $1.50M with 15 games and 10.6 minutes a night. His morale sits at 72, tied for the lowest in the league. That is a coach's problem. The 13,778 is yours.
