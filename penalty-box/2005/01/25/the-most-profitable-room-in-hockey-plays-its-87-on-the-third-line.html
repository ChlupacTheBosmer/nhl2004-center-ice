THE MOST PROFITABLE ROOM IN HOCKEY PLAYS ITS 87 ON THE THIRD LINE

The Devils print $34.40M more than anyone. Their coach has decided Jamie Langenbrunner belongs on the third line. You pay $80 for it.

Mickey "The Mick" DonnellyColumnist, ex-goalie · The Penalty BoxTuesday, 25 January 2005
ShareCopy link

Jamie Langenbrunner86 is rated 87. He skates L3RW. He plays the second power play unit. He takes a shift on the fourth shorthanded group.

Turner Stevenson72 is rated 73. He skates L1RW. Grant Marshall77 is rated 78 and skates L2RW. Brian Gionta75 is rated 76 and skates L4RW.

You pay $80 a seat to watch a coach make this call.

$34.40M in profit means nobody upstairs asks questions

The New Jersey Devils Devils made $34.40M in profit this season. No franchise in the league has printed more money. Their payroll is $44.98M against 60 points, which works out to $0.75M per point. Their 17,495 in attendance is second only to Toronto. The Morale Scale puts NJ at 90.9.

So the coach sits his best right winger on the third line and keeps his job. The business is fine, the hockey is the problem.

The last 10 say the lineup is broken

NJ is 22-14-4 with 60 points and leads the Atlantic. Fine. Their last 10 games: five wins, five losses, 21 goals for, 33 against.

Your first-line winger is rated 73. Your third-line winger is rated 87. The coach is playing it wrong.

Doug Reimer will produce a spreadsheet showing Langenbrunner's point totals don't scream first line. He always does. The point totals are a product of the minutes and the matchups, not a reason to leave him there. An 87 on the third line gets third-line zone starts, third-line defensive assignments, and third-line scoring chances. Thirty-three goals against in 10 games is what you get when the top of the lineup isn't the top.

The New Jersey Devils coach has a comfort problem. A 73 on the first line won't make him look foolish when the game gets tight. An 87 on the third line gives him a body to blame when they're outscored. He protects his job and wastes his roster.

The Devils make $34.40M. The coach keeps his seat. And 17,495 people pay $80 to watch the third line do the first line's job.
