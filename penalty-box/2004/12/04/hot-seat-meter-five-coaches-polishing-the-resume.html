HOT SEAT METER: FIVE COACHES POLISHING THE RÉSUMÉ

Losing streaks, buried paychecks, and goalies who can't look at you. The blame sheet says these 5 benches are about to get uncomfortable.

Mickey "The Mick" Donnelly
Columnist, ex-goalie · The Penalty BoxSaturday, 4 December 2004

Last week I told you Detroit Red Wings was the most overpaid team in hockey. You thanked me. Now I'm widening the net. 5 coaches across this league are presiding over disasters, and the sheet tells you exactly how — in minutes, in morale, and in money wasted on the bench.

1. Minnesota, 7-16-1, L10. You want the definition of a dead locker room? Dwayne Roloson78 and Manny Fernandez79 both sit at 68 morale. Both goalies. That's the lowest pair in the league and it belongs on a team that just dropped 10 straight. They allowed 50 goals over that stretch. The front office made $31.47M in profit this season while the product on ice is a car crash. The coach's job is to stop a freefall. This one isn't stopped.

2. Atlanta, 5-16-1, 13 points. The worst record in this league. Byron Dafoe82 is 70 morale, 82 overall, and losing 45 goals in 10 games. Magnus Arvedson77 is $2.49M to play 10.3 minutes a night and score 1 point in 23 games. 10 minutes! You could fill those minutes with a fan who bought a $78 ticket and get a better return. The only reason Ilya Kovalchuk90 hasn't demanded a trade is that he's too busy putting up 39 points to notice his own team.

3. Philadelphia, 9-10-3, L3. Cliff Ronning78 is $2.77M a year for 8.7 minutes. 8.7! Donald Brashear76 is $2.10M for 9.3 minutes. Two guys making $2.77M and $2.10M respectively to play bottom-six hockey. Meanwhile the team gives up 48 goals in the last 10. You're paying for two enforcers and one power-play unit and the results show it: 25 points in 24 games.

4. Detroit, 8-9-4, L5, $3.11M per point. Still the most expensive waste in hockey. Tomas Holmstrom74 is $1.30M to play 8.9 minutes. He's 74 overall — not a star, but not a healthy scratch either. The coach keeps him in a fourth-line box while the team can't buy a win. 5 straight. The payroll is $80.89M and the bench keeps hiding pieces.

5. Pittsburgh, 7-11-3, L4. Martin Straka82 is having a fine season — 44 points in 23 games, 157-pace. None of it matters because the team is 4 straight losses and gave up 48 goals in the last 10. You can't coach when your defense is a sieve and nobody behind the bench has figured out why.

Here's the pattern, nerds: it's not just the roster. It's who plays and who sits. 5 coaches are asking their most expensive players to do the least and wondering why the wins aren't coming. The Development Index doesn't lie. Neither does a 10-game losing streak.

Reimer and his spreadsheet will tell you it's "variance." I'm telling you it's the man with the clipboard.
