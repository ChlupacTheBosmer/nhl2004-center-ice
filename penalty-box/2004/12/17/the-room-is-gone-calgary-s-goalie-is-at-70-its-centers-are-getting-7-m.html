THE ROOM IS GONE: Calgary's goalie is at 70, its centers are getting 7 minutes, and the coach keeps doing the same thing

Four men in the league's morale basement, 6 straight losses, 31 goals for and 47 against in the last 10. The feeling isn't the problem. The bench is.

Mickey "The Mick" DonnellyColumnist, ex-goalie · The Penalty BoxFriday, 17 December 2004
ShareCopy link

Calgary Flames is 9-19-2 and 22 points into a hole that nobody in that building wants to point at a single person for. The Bureau's morale scale has the club at 85.9, tied with Chicago Blackhawks for the lowest average in hockey. Four Calgary players sit in the bottom 15. The losing streak is 6. In the last 10 games the team scored 31 and allowed 47.

Everybody talks about the room. I've stopped. The room is a feeling you get when the numbers behind you are falling apart and the people in charge would rather talk about culture than talk about the lineup card.

The goalie is the number.

Roman Turek86 is 86 on the Book. He plays 52.4 minutes a night over 25 games because there is no one else in that building with a mask. His morale reads 70, the single lowest on the sheet. A goalie who plays 52 minutes every night and watches his team allow 47 goals in 10 games is not in a slump. He's been left alone, and the alone is showing.

The centers are getting buried.

Travis Green73 is 79 on the Book, on a $2.97M deal for 3 years. He gets 11.3 minutes a night over 17 games. His morale is 74. A 79-overall center on a 3-year contract who plays 11 minutes is not a development project. He's a man the coach doesn't know what to do with, so he sits.

Mika Sulku71 is 71, on $0.94M, getting 7.4 minutes. Stephane Yelle72 is 72, on $1.10M, getting 7.3 minutes over 31 games. Both read 75 on the morale scale. Both are on 1-year deals. Both are watching Green sit and thinking the same thing I'm thinking: if the 79 is getting 11, what am I supposed to be doing out there for 7?

The math doesn't save you.

$43.33M in payroll buys 22 points. That is $1.97M per point, which makes Calgary the 5th worst value in the league. Behind only Philadelphia Flyers at $2.56M, St. Louis Blues at $2.36M, Montreal Canadiens at $2.31M, and Detroit Red Wings at $2.25M. The Red Wings and the Canadiens can afford to be that bad because they're buying futures. Calgary is buying 3-year deals for centers who play 11 minutes and a goalie who has no backup.

The team is 5-10 at home and 5-11 on the road. There is no place this club wins. That is not a morale problem. That is a construction problem, and the man holding the blueprint is the only one in the building who hasn't been let go.

The fix isn't a team meeting. The fix is Green on the second line at 18 minutes, a second goalie who can play 20 minutes so Turek can breathe, and a coach who stops treating 7-minute shifts as a management tool. Until then, the room stays at 85.9 and the streak keeps going.
