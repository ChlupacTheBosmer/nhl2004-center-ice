NINE GAMES, TWO 34-YEAR-OLDS, AND A STREAK THAT IS BORROWED TIME

The Presidents' Trophy team is 9 straight deep and the engine is two men who turned 34. The pace says 184 and 182. The bodies say something else.

Mickey "The Mick" DonnellyColumnist, ex-goalie · The Penalty BoxFriday, 17 December 2004
ShareCopy link

Dallas Stars is 9 games into a winning streak. 55 goals for, 29 against, in the last 10. The Presidents' Trophy team is doing what Presidents' Trophy teams do in December: they are winning, the building is 15983 deep, and the ticket is $80.

The problem is the engine.

Bill Guerin86 is 34. He has 36 goals and 36 assists for 72 points in 32 games. That is the best in the league. 184 pace. 20.3 minutes a night. Overall grade 89.

Mike Modano86 is 34. He has 23 goals and 39 assists for 62 points in 28 games. 182 pace. 21.0 minutes a night. Overall grade 90.

Two men. Both 34. Together they have 59 of the team's 143 goals. The rest of the room is there, the rest of the room is fine. But the identity, the thing that makes the building fill up, the thing that makes the 9-game streak possible, is two men who will be 35 in a matter of months.

The pace column says 184 and 182. That is what a 34-year-old body does when it is healthy, motivated, and playing on a team that is 19-9-1 and feeding the puck to you because the whole city is behind you. The pace is a projection. It assumes the body holds. It assumes the legs do not quit in the third period of the 50th game.

I have been a goalie. I know what 34 feels like in the third period. I know what it feels like when your knee goes a half-second late on the rebound and the puck is in your net before you can get to it. I know what it feels like when you come off the ice and your body is telling you the next shift is going to hurt more than the last one did. I am 41 now and I still feel it in my left shoulder when it rains.

The morale is 92.3. That is fine. That is a happy room. But morale does not hold up a knee. Morale does not hold up a hip. The room is 92.3 because they are winning 9 straight. What is the room when the streak is over and one of those two men is sitting in the penalty box with a bad groin?

The payroll is $66.88 million. You are paying $66.88 million for a team whose identity is two 34-year-olds. The question is not whether they are good. 72 and 62 points at 34 is good. The question is what happens in February, in March, when the bodies start to tell the truth that the pace column does not know yet. The 184 and the 182 are numbers. The knees are not.
